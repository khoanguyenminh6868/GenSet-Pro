Introduction
Huadong Diesel Engine Co,.Ltd is a young company assembling middle-sized diesel engines, which locates in Weifang City, Shandong Province of China. It has advanced facilities include an accurate testing system, and quality control facility. We have more than 40 professional mechanical engineers, about 300 skilled workers, therefore we have strong ability in development and research of the technology of diesel engine. Our product has been recognized widely by customers. The main products are: 495, 4100, R4105,R6105, 6126 and HD51 series diesel engines which have endured thousands of verifications. With output ranging from 30kw-700kw, this series diesel engine are the ideal power for engineering machinery, agricultural machinery, heavy duty trucks, ships, generating sets, stationary power and some special machinery. Our company is among the first to receive Certification of ISO 9000 Quality Assurance Standards in the industry. We are dedicated to the technological development of diesel engine industry. Leveraging on the advanced technology, high capabilities, proven quality and comprehensive services, the company established great reputation in both the domestic and international markets.
