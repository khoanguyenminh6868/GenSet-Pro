Fujian GOOALL Power Technology A Trusted Generator Manufacturer Since 1996
Founded in 1996, Fujian GOOALL Power Technology Co., Ltd. has developed into a professional manufacturer specializing in alternators and diesel generator sets. With over two decades of industry experience, the company has continuously expanded its production capacity and improved its product quality.
Located in Fu'an City, Fujian Province, GOOALL Power now operates a modern facility of 4,500 square meters and serves customers across global markets including Europe, America, Southeast Asia, and Africa.
Today, the company continues to focus on innovation, quality improvement, and global expansion.
