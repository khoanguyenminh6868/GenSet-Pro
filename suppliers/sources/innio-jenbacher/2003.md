JENBACHER TYPE 4

An efficiency milestone

Based on the proven design concepts of Types 3 and 6, the modern

Jenbacher Type 4 engines in the 800 to 1,560 kW power range are

characterized by a high-power density and outstanding efficiency.

The enhanced control and monitoring provide easy preventive

maintenance, high reliability and availability.
