Containerized Generator Set Solutions
Integrated Power Solutions.
Jenbacher offers containerized generator sets based on our Type 2, 3, 4, and 6 gas engines. Drawing on decades of experience, these genset containers deliver strong performance across various site conditions. By maintaining high-quality standards for both the engine and the complete containerized power plant package, we help ensure reliable power and heat generation.
Our dedicated Jenbacher experts are available to provide personal consultation.

Compact, easily transportable format
Our generator containers for Type 2, 3, and 4 gas engines have a standard 40-foot length. Width and height depend on engine type, the application (power generation only or CHP) and ambient conditions.
Customized sizes to accommodate special project requirements are available on request.

Features and options
Sound attenuation
The overall package (gas engine, container, and auxiliary components) is designed to meet 65 dB(A) in 10m (surface sound pressure level according to DIN 45635). Lower sound pressure levels are available on request.
Ambient temperature
The standard ventilation is designed for ambient temperatures from -25°C to +34°C.
All genset containers are also available with an option for -40°C and most variants can be upgraded for +45°C.
Options
A broad range of options, both mechanical and electrical, are available to ensure that our containers meet the project requirements. For example, we offer several heat recovery configurations and exhaust after treatment systems. Our containers can be designed to meet different country-specific regulations, earthquake safety requirements and various ambient conditions.

Features and options
Sound attenuation
The overall package (gas engine, container, and auxiliary components) is available for 65, 70, 75 and 80 dB(A) in 10m (surface sound pressure level according to DIN 45635). Lower sound pressure levels are available on request.
Ambient temperature
The standard ventilation is designed for ambient temperatures from -25°C to +40°C. The genset containers can be equipped with several additional features to enable operation at lower temperatures on request.
Options
Type 6 containers are available with a similarly broad range of options that we offer for the Type 2 / 3 / 4 containers. These include several heat recovery configurations, exhaust after treatment systems, compliance with country-specific regulations, earthquake safety requirements and various ambient conditions. Additionally, several Type 6—specific options are available, such as the integration of pre-chamber gas compressors and medium voltage equipment.

Frequently Asked Questions
What is a containerized genset or packaged generator set?
A containerized genset or packaged generator set is a power generation system installed in a transportable container with key auxiliary systems pre-integrated. Jenbacher containerized solutions are designed to reduce onsite installation work and support faster project execution. A gas genset container, also called a gas generator container, packages the gas engine and key auxiliary systems together for fast deployment.
What does a containerized power generation solution include?
A containerized power generation solution packages the engine, generator, and key auxiliary systems into a single pre-engineered unit, ready for onsite installation. Jenbacher and its regional experts or channel partners can scope this to your project’s location and requirements. Options include natural gas generator package and package generator set configurations, sometimes called a packaged gas engine generator set, engineered for industrial and commercial deployments.
What are the benefits of a container power plant?
In addition to the benefits of an onsite power generation, a container power plants reduce onsite installation work, save space, and provide a pre-engineered package matched to project requirements. Jenbacher highlights turnkey engineering, auxiliary-system integration, compact footprint, and tuning for site-specific conditions as key benefits.
Are containerized generator sets suitable for fast deployment?
Yes, containerized generator sets are often suitable for faster deployment because major components are pre-installed and pre-engineered. Actual schedule still depends on permitting, grid connection, civil works, fuel supply, and country-specific requirements.
Can containerised generators run on hydrogen?
Yes, containerised generators can be configured for hydrogen applications where the engine type, safety concept, hydrogen supply, and local regulations allow it. Jenbacher presents containerized energy systems linked to its hydrogen-capable engine portfolio. A containerized gas generator configured this way can be engineered for hydrogen blends as well.
Are containerized generators noisy?
Containerized generator sets are available with different sound-attenuation options to meet site-specific requirements. For certain configurations, a standard sound pressure level of around 65 dB(A) at 10 meters is available, with quieter configurations available for noise-sensitive locations. Actual sound levels depend on the engine type, package design and selected configuration.
