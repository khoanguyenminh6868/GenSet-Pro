About Starlight
Jiangsu Xingguang Power Generation Equipment Co., Ltd. (Jiangsu Xingguang Power Group) was founded in 1974 and is one of the earliest manufacturers of generators and diesel generator sets in China. The company currently has a registered capital of 218.88 million yuan, covers an area of 86,000 square meters, and has a building area of 55,000 square meters. It currently employs 328 people and has established 46 directly affiliated sales and service outlets across the country.
The main products include
Diesel engines, generator sets, and diesel generators, providing users with one-stop services including design, supply, commissioning, and maintenance.

Our Journey & Achievements
Since 1982, our company has been a qualified manufacturer of generator sets, having passed inspections conducted by the "National Internal Combustion Engine Quality Supervision and Inspection Center," and has obtained a self-operated import and export qualification certificate. Over the years, Xingguang's generator sets have been widely used in various industries and have been selected as qualified suppliers by large state-owned and private enterprises such as Muyuan Group, East Hope, China National Nuclear Corporation, China Huadian Corporation, China State Construction Engineering Corporation, China Communications Construction Company, China Railway Group, PetroChina, Sinopec, China Unicom, Evergrande, and Country Garden. Through the unremitting efforts of Xingguang's team, our small spark has grown into a prairie fire.
The company boasts a management team that is forward-thinking, energetic, and proficient in business operations. With the establishment of a high-tech innovative enterprise as its starting point, it adheres to the principles of "high starting point, high technology, and excellent service", actively collecting and absorbing advanced technologies and products from both domestic and international sources, and continuously upgrading its products. Over the years, Xingguang has maintained close cooperation with Jiangsu University, Changzhou University, and Yangzhou University, while also collaborating with world-renowned engine manufacturers such as Shangchai, Weichai, Cummins, MTU Benz, DAEWO Daewoo Heavy Industries, UK's Perkins, Guangxi Yuchai, Sweden's Volvo, as well as generator manufacturers like Delco, LS Lilei Senma, Stamford, and Marathon, and controller manufacturers like Zhongzhi and Deep Sea. It has become an (OEM) supporting factory and technology center, establishing long-term and stable strategic partnerships with them. Through continuous improvement and upgrading of its industrial system, it has enhanced its research and development capabilities and after-sales service support, laying a solid foundation for expanding into the international market.
