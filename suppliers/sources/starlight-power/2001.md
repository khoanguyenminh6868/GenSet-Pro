Starlight Electricity founded 1974
Jiangsu Xingguang Power Generation Equipment Co., Ltd. (Jiangsu Xingguang Power Group) was founded in 1974 and is one of the earliest manufacturers of generators and diesel generator sets in China.
Our main products include diesel engines, generator sets, and diesel generators. We are always ready to provide users with a one-stop service that encompasses design, supply, commissioning, and maintenance. Xingguang has always adhered to the principle of employing top-notch talents, building a first-class enterprise, manufacturing top-quality products, and delivering exceptional service, striving to create a domestic top-tier enterprise.
