The TCG 3020:
Successful deployment.
Krikato BVBA, Belgium
The TCG 3020 V20 is the second MWM genset for tomato producer Krikato BVBA
in Belgium. In 2012, they decided to use the MWM brand – at that time, a
TCG 2020 V12 – for the construction of a CHP. They once again selected an MWM genset
for the extension of their greenhouse. Since June 2020, the two gensets together have
been generating 3.5 MW of electrical and 4.2 MW of thermal power and reliably supply the
greenhouse, which has been expanded from 1.2 to 1.7 hectares, with electricity and heat.
By using SCR catalysts, the carbon dioxide in the exhaust gas released by the natural
­gas-powered MWM gas gensets can be used for organic carbon fertilization of the plants
after proper treatment, which has a positive effect on growth and yield.
