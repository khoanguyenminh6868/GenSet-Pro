Twining-planta-CHP Facts & Figures
| Genset / gas engine: | 1x TCG 3016 V12 | 1x TCG 3016 V08
| Segment / gas type: | Natural Gas
| Customer: | Tea Manufacturer R. Twining, Poland
| Electrical output: | 1 x 600 kW | 1x 399 kW
| Thermical output: | 1x 680 kW | 1x 443 kW
| Commissioning: | 2023
R. Twining & Co Limited (“Twinings”), the world’s third-largest tea manufacturer, has optimized the energy supply of its Polish factory with a modern combined cooling, heat, and power (CCHP) plant. Powered by two MWM engines, the CCHP plant makes the production more energy-efficient and reduces emissions.
