Energy from Portugal to the World
With almost half a century of history, Grupel has the experience and technical knowledge to meet any challenge. We produce and sell power generators up to 4200kVA, portable lighting towers and customized energy solutions for any application.
We are in over 70 countries, responding to the requirements and needs of our customers with commitment and dedication.​

Portable
Portable, gasoline-powered generators, that can supply energy to low-power equipment and be used by professionals or non-professionals.

Smart
Diesel generators for use in applications in your home and/or business. Available with automatic start, responding to mains failures.

Generator sets designed to act as the main or emergency source of energy in installations where there is no other source or where the existing one is weak.
