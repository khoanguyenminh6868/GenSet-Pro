Product Application
Field of use
Nonferrous metal smelting: in nonferrous metal smelting, it is required that the fuel heat and mass be stable, no furnace products and no pollution, and LPG has these conditions. After heating and gasification, LPG can be easily introduced into smelting furnace for combustion. Shandong Jinsheng Nonferrous Metals Group Co., Ltd. has successfully used LPG in the copper smelting process of German Krupp smelting furnace, replacing the original gas combustion process, reducing the harm of sulfur, phosphorus and other impurities, and improving the quality of copper.
Main uses
It can be used as raw material for petrochemical industry, solvent for low temperature extraction of subcritical biotechnology and fuel.
Liquefied petroleum gas (LPG) is mainly used as petrochemical raw materials for cracking hydrocarbons to ethylene or steam reforming to syngas. It can be used as industrial, civil and internal combustion engine fuel. The main quality control indexes are evaporation residue, sulfur content and sometimes olefin content.
