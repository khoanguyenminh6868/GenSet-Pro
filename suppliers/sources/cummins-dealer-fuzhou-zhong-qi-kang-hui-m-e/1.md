### Service Definitions

- #### Full service:


Engine and component rebuilds and all warranty repairs (subject to Pre-Authorization Requirements) as well as all service provided at the Repair and Maintenance Service levels. This also includes parts sales.

- #### Repair service:


Warranty repairs on all external engine components including fuel system components (subject to Pre-Authorization Requirements) as well as all service provided at the Maintenance service level. Cannot remove/replace cylinder heads or any internal rotating components without prior approval from Distributor. This also includes parts sales.
