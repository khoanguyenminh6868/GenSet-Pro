Weifang Huayuan Diesel Engine Manufacture CO.,LTD is established in the year 1999 . We specialize in manufacture and develop Ricardo series , Steyr series diesel engine from 26.5KW to 280 KW , Generator Set from 8KW to 1000KW and Water Pump Set .
We mainly produce two cylinders , four cylinders and six cylinders diesel engine , our brand is " Huafa" . Most of the engines are used for assemble the generator set , air compressor , water pump , others engines as the power of wheel loader , corn harvester and other machine .
Weifang Huayuan Diesel Engine Manufacture CO.,LTD has widely cooperated with international and domestic famous diesel engine and alternator manufacture , such as Cummins , Perkins , Deutz , Lovol , Shanghai , Wudong , Yangdong , Quanchai , Laidong so on . Alternator like Stamford , Leroy Somer , Lan Dian , Farady , XN and DG... With the convenience operation , advanced technology , low noise , low vibration , easy starting , creditable capability advantage , our products can be widely used in factories , mines , banks , hospital , supermarket , telecommunication , oil filed , construction filed so on . Our products have passed the CE , ISO certificate , such high quality engine , generator sets , water pump sets have been exported to more than 30 countries and regions , some of our clients become our agency in local market .
Our company tenets is : Good quality is the key to survive !Good credit standing is the key to develop !
