Cummins marine generator set
1. Overview of Cummings marine generator sets:
Cummins Marine Generator Unit uses the marine engine produced by Dongfeng Cummins and Chongqing Cummins. It has the advantages of high quality, low fuel consumption, low noise, high output power and reliable performance. Generators use SIEMENS, Landian and other marine generators. Advanced structure, excellent performance and reliable operation. The unit is equipped with optional monitor, parallel car module, self start function and four protection function.
Two. Features of Cummings marine generator set:
Dongfeng Mings marine generator set is of high quality, low fuel consumption, low noise, high output power and reliable performance. Its reliable stability, economy, dynamism, durability and environmental safety are welcomed by users at home and abroad. It is guaranteed by three guarantees all over the world. Sino-US joint venture Dongfeng Cummins and Chongqing Cummins generators are small in size, light in weight, low in fuel consumption, high in power, reliable in operation, and convenient in parts supply and maintenance. Adopting electronic governor, it has protection functions such as high cooling water temperature, low oil pressure, alarm over speed and automatic stop.
