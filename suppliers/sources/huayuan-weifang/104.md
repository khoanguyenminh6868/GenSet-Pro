Marine generating set
1. Summary of the products of 135 diesel generating sets.
Shangchai 135 marine generator set adopts Dongfeng 135 and G128 series marine engines produced by Shanghai Diesel Engine Generator Co., Ltd. It has excellent power performance and reliability, simple operation, convenient maintenance and good service network, so that users can really avoid the worries of spare parts supply and maintenance services. The generator is selected by Siemens, Landian, Shanghai and other marine generators of Sino-German joint venture, with advanced performance and stable and reliable operation. The unit can be equipped with side monitor, parallel module, self-starting function and four protection functions. Users can choose CCS, ZC, BV, AK ship inspection.
Two. The characteristics of the 135 generation marine generator set are:
1. The diesel generating set of Shanghai diesel engine is made of 135 SC series diesel engine.
(2) Adapt the famous-brand generators and control panels such as Marathon (Sino-US joint venture), Stamford (British-owned), Leyson Ma (French brand), etc.
3. Generating units adopt a new type of reducing combustion chamber to achieve environmental protection targets.
4. Good dynamic performance, large torque reserve, low fuel consumption and low fuel consumption.
5. Flexible configuration, good stability and meeting the different requirements of users.
