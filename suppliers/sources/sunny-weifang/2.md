Weifang Sunny Power Machinery Co., Ltd. is located in China Power Capital - Weifang City, owning Chinese leader and world famous diesel engine manufacturers.
Having professional and experienced workers, supported by strong technology and advanced equipments, we successfully formed strict and perfect production procedure from design, production, testing, to after-service, which guarantee the stable quality and technology innovation. We have been approved by ISO9001 and CE.
Owing to good quality and stable performance, we have been working with clients in long term worldwide. Our main markets cover East European countries, American counties, Asia, Africa, Australia and so on.
We hope to work with any client; Hope to win bilateral benefits.
main products
To meet customers' demands fully and help to organize the relative machinery and spare parts regular in use, our company builds three branch factories, with main products as following:
01/
Professionally producing diesel engine 15-500KW, diesel generator 5-2000KW, functioned with intelligent CTRL system, remote CTRL/test/message communication, even off duty care. Optional equipped with wheel trailer, sound proof case, weather proof canopy, container and so on;
02/
Professionally producing high quality diesel water pump, fire fighting water pump, mining pump, high pressure washing pump station, piston mud pump, sand pump and marine use water pump, tractor PTO water pump and generator, one engine driving water pump and generator for double application, and relative pump equipment;
03/
Professionally producing diesel engines 15-400KW, diesel generators 15-2000KW as well as diesel engine driven machinery;
04/
Professionally assembling diesel engine intelligent CTRL system, which can fulfill automatic control, remote CTRL, remote test, remote message communication, even off duty care;
05/
Professionally producing wheel trailer, skid mounting base, fuel tank, sound proof case, weather proof case and spare parts for diesel engines, diesel generators and diesel water pumps.
06/
Dumpers for mining industry, covering 1-20tons, working at land or underground tunnel, have diesel engine driving 3-wheel, 4-wheel, 6-wheel models and electric motor driving models.
