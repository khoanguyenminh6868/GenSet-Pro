Fuan Acepow Equipment Co.,Ltd is located in fuan,fujian,China. It’s special at manufacturing diesel generator set, diesel alternator and motor product. Now our factory, covers an area of 50000 square meters, and have gotten ISO9001 and ISO4001 certificate. Our diesel genset have passed CE, SASO, SONCAP certificate.
Now our mainly brand for generator set is Cummins, Perkins, Deutz, Isuzu, Weichai, Shangchai, Yangdong, Quanchai and so on. And our alternator mainly brand conclude Stamford, Leroy somer, Marathon, Mecc alte.
Our mainly exporting market is South-east Asia,South-America,MID-east,South-Asia,Africa and so on. Acepow will be your best power supplier!

Established in 1978, the original name of Aepow is MINDONG MOTOR SPARE PARTS CO.,LTD
Development: In 1985, Our first diesel generator set was born, and Acepow is the fifth manufacturer of silent diesel generator set in Fuan city,Fujian province.
In 2000, Acepow got our own power of exporting and start to export diesel generator set, electric motor, water pump to many area, including south-east Asia, Europe, Africa and so on.
In 2002, Acepow set up own design team, bring to advance equipment.
In 2007, Our branch company is established in Fuzhou city, Acepow sales team is becoming stronger day by day.
In 2013, In order to expend production line furher, Acepow start to construct a new plant in Gantang.
In 2016, Acepow develop a new product - natural gas generator.
In 2017, Acepow Sales over 9,000 sets.
In 2018, In order to improve production capacity, purchase a hi-tech canopy making equipment and additional extend assembling workshop.
In 2020, Acepow annual sales over 16,000,000USD.
In2021, Acepow established the second sales office in Fuzhou city, Fujian province.
Systems Application Engineering
Our engineers can help you in planning, design and implementation of Powercity Generators and associated equipment. They are equipped with tools and experience in power generation and with broad knowledge on industry standards in generator design and installation. Our generator product comes with two (2) years or 2,000 run hour warranty. If you wish to extend this warranty, avail of our design, supply, installation and maintenance package and we can extend this warranty to five (5) years or 10,000 run hours whichever comes first, subject to terms and conditions.
Product Installation
You can avail of our product installation services in your premises wherever you are in the Philippines. Our installation will give your assurance on proper set up of your back up power for its best performance, safety and that will not compromise our product warranty.
Electrical and Mechanical Service.
We provide consultancy and labor for your back up power requirement. Inquire from our sales account officers.
Diesel Fuel System Design and Installation
For extended operation of generators, we design, supply and install diesel fuel tanks. Our fuel tanks are made of carbon steel based on standards of the American Petroleum Institute (API) for aboveground diesel fuel tanks. We use black iron (BI) for fuel piping in accordance to the International Mechanical Code and EGSA Standards. Fuel tank bund or spill containment is always part of our standard design in compliance with EGSA Standards, the National Fire Protection Association (NFPA) USA and the Bureau of Fire Protection (BFP) Philippines.
Product Maintenance and Repair
We provide after sales support to our products nationwide. Sign up in our Periodic Maintenance Service (PMS) for your Powercity Generator and Automatic Transfer Switch. Having your Powercity equipment serviced by us will ensure original parts and longer equipment service life.
Product Rental
For a short term use of a generator or if you don’t want to purchase one, you can rent our generator (subject to availability). Inquire from our sales account officers.
Product Training
We provide operations and maintenance training to our customers. The training will equipped you with familiarization in using our products and skills in maintenance. Our training is held at our main office where you will be comfortable in our air-conditioned training room and mock up facility.
Generator Set and Equipment Lifting and Hauling Services
We maintain a fleet of boom trucks and a mobile crane in hauling and lifting our products to your premises. You can also rent them. Please book at least five (5) working days before your date of hauling or lifting. Inquire from our sales account officers.
