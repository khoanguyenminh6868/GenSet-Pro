Our History
YANGDONG CO., LTD was established in 1964, after 50 years development, has become one of the largest multi-cylinder diesel production base of small, multi-cylinder diesel engine in China, we recombined with YTO Group on 2010 year.
Our Factory
The company covers an area of 370 acres, plant area of 268000 square meters, have around 780 workers and staff, there are two plants, one is diesel engine plant, the other is casting. The company has from the casting, machining, assembly to test complete diesel engine production capacity.
Our Product
We have 18 kinds of basic types of multi-cylinder diesel engine, bore from 80mm to 110mm, displacement from 1.3 to 4.5L, power cover 10kw to 105kW.
Product Application
Products are suitable for passenger cars, commercial vehicles, low-speed trucks, tractors, generator set, fire fighting pump, water pump, combines, construction machinery, marine units, air conditioning units and other products.
Our Certificate
ISO/TS16949, CE, E-MARK, EPA.
Production Equipment
Enterprises with advanced production equipment and complete inspection equipment, most of these equipment are imported form German, USA, Japan and South Korean.
Production Market
Our diesel engines are exported to more than 100 countries around the world, including the United States, Germany, Spain, Turkey, Italy, Brazil, Pakistan, etc.
Our Service
We offer a two-year warranty on our export products, during which we will provide replacement parts free of charge due to damage to parts caused by diesel engine quality problems.
