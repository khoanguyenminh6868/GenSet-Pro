ENDRESS EZG - PTO GENERATORS
Reliable emergency power supply for agriculture and dairies
Secure energy during power outages – animal welfare and operational continuity guaranteed
A power failure can have serious consequences for farms and dairies: milking robots come to a standstill, cooling systems fail, ventilation systems stop. This not only jeopardizes the supply chain, but also animal welfare – a legally enshrined aspect in Germany.
With the ENDRESS EZG series PTO generators , you are well prepared. Our generators use the power of your tractor and deliver reliable power immediately – flexibly, mobile, and cost-efficiently.
Your advantages with ENDRESS EZG
Ensuring animal welfare: The Animal Welfare Livestock Farming Ordinance stipulates an emergency power supply for feeding, water, and ventilation. With EZG generators, you can meet these requirements and ensure optimal care for your animals—even in the event of a crisis.
Keeping supply chains stable: Dairies and processors depend on continuous milk deliveries. A power outage must not lead to a halt in production. Our PTO generators keep your operations running reliably—from milking robots to cooling systems.
Flexible and economical: No separate motor, no high maintenance costs. You can use your existing vehicle fleet and be ready to go in just a few minutes: Attach – set the speed – use the power.
Technology that protects: Modern voltage regulation and monitoring systems ensure maximum safety for people and machines. Even sensitive devices such as milking robots or climate control systems are reliably supplied.
For farmyards and fields: Whether as a backup power supply in the barn or as a mobile power source in the field, the EZG series offers solutions for all areas of application.
Performance range:
From 40 kVA to over 100 kVA – suitable for your tractor and your individual requirements.
ENDRESS EZG – Keeping your business running when others are left in the dark. Find out more now and increase your security of supply!
All advantages at a glance
Mobile pump solutions from ENDRESS
In addition to PTO generators, ENDRESS also offers powerful mobile pumps. These are suitable for a wide range of applications in agriculture, municipal tasks, or disaster control. The pumps are robust, durable, and easy to operate—ideal for use in demanding conditions.
