When every second counts – mobile and stationary energy supply for fire departments, disaster control, and emergency services
EMERGENCY POWER SUPPLY
DIN POWER GENERATORS
ENERGY STORAGE SYSTEMS
In emergencies, major disasters, or widespread power outages, a reliable power supply is crucial. ENDRESS DIN power generators, stationary power supply systems with IT/TN operating mode switching, and mobile lighting systems offer a robust, powerful, and immediately available solution for emergency services. They secure the power supply exactly where it is most urgently needed – independently of the public power grid.
Thanks to their DIN-compliant design and practical features, ENDRESS mobile power generators are specially tailored to the requirements of fire departments, disaster control units, and technical rescue services . They enable the reliable operation of modern consumers such as radio technology, thermal imaging cameras, medical devices, or lighting systems – even under extreme conditions.
Reliable energy supply – support for other critical infrastructure sectors
ENDRESS solutions not only make an effective contribution to resilience in the area of authorities and organizations with security tasks, but also support other critical infrastructure sectors:
Healthcare: Power supply for medical facilities, mobile treatment stations, and laboratory technology.
Information and communication technology (ICT): Operation of radio equipment, servers, and emergency communication facilities.
Water and wastewater: Supply of pumping stations and emergency wells to ensure drinking water supply.
Transportation and traffic: Lighting of emergency sites, supply of traffic management systems, and charging infrastructure.
Government and administration: Maintaining the operational capability of authorities, control centers, and emergency communications.
Food and logistics: Ensuring the cold chain and supplying distribution centers in the event of a power failure.
The optional integrated IT/TN switch allows for secure power supply to building networks and significantly increases flexibility in use. This means that emergency shelters, treatment areas, or command centers can be fully supplied with power even in the event of a power failure.
ENDRESS also sets standards in the field of mobile lighting technology : the mobile lighting systems with pneumatically extendable masts and powerful LED light heads provide large-area, glare-free illumination – ideal for night-time operations, accident sites, or temporary infrastructure.
Future-proof technology – developed for use
The demands placed on mobile power supplies are constantly increasing – due to new technologies, higher performance requirements, and more complex application scenarios. That is why ENDRESS is continuously developing its product lines. The focus is on reliability, ease of use, and adaptability . Close cooperation with emergency services and technical users results in solutions that meet the requirements of today and tomorrow.
With ENDRESS, emergency services organizations rely on certified quality, robust technology, and maximum operational reliability – for a stable power supply when every second counts.
