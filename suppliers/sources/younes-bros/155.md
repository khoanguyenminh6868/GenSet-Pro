Description
Caterpillar 3306B DITA Industrial Generator Set. Rated at 250kW / 313kVA Standby, 60hz, 208v, 1800rpm, 0.8PF, 3 Phase, 446 Frame. Year 2004, less than 10760 hours since new. Complete with Caterpillar SR4B Generator End, Air Inlet system, Cooling system, Exhaust, EMPCII Control Panels, Hydra-Mechanical Governor, 24v Starter, Block Heater, New Silencer, Flywheel 11.0 and Flywheel Housing, Heat rejection System and Lube System. Unit is Skid Mounted, UL1446 Recognized with class H Insulation and EPA Certified.
