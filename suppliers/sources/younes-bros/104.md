Description
New Surplus MAK 8M25 HFO Marine Generator Set. Rated at 2530kw, 60hz, 690v, 720rpm, 0.9PF, 4 Stroke, 8 Cylinder, 2385Amps, Tier 1. Year 2009, units have zero hours since new. Complete with Hyundai HFJ7 716-14E Synchronous Generator End, Silencer, Spark Arrester, Expansion Joints for Exhaust Pipes, Exhaust Gas Temperature Monitoring System, Cooling Water Preheating Control, Preheaters, Stand by Pump, Turbocharger, Control Panel, Electronic Speed Governor, Air Intake System, Exhaust System. Units are Skid Mounted and IMO Compliant. Units can be upgraded to Tier 2 at additional cost.
