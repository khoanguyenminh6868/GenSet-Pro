Power Rating Explanations
PRP (Prime Power): Available for unlimited annual hours in variable load applications according to GB/T2820-97 standards. Includes 10% overload capability for 1 hour within 12-hour operation period.
ESP (Standby Power): Suitable for emergency power supply during utility interruptions. No overload, utility parallel, or negotiated outage operation capability available at this rating.
