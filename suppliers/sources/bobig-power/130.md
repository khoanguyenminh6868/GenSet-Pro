Company Profile
Established in 2004, Fujian Bobig Electric Machinery Co., Ltd. is an electrical machinery manufacturer specializing in technological research, production and sales. With a total factory area of 10,000 square meters, including 1,000sqm standard construction area, we have 160 employees, including 20 technicians.
Our main products include air-cooled gasoline generators, air-cooled diesel generators, air-cooled welding & generating sets and water-cooled diesel generator sets.
