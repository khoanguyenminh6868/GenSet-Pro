Product Description
ISUZU Diesel Generator 10KW/13KVA - Open/Silent Type
Factory-priced ISUZU diesel generator available in both open and silent configurations with water cooling system. This reliable 10KW/13KVA power supply unit delivers consistent performance for various industrial applications with advanced features and robust construction.
Key Features
Premium brushless alternator brands: Leroy Somer, Marathon, BOBIG, Mecc Alte
50°C radiator with fan for optimal engine cooling during extended operation
Advanced control systems including generator set control, ATS system, remote control, and parallel operation
High-quality steel canopy construction (2MM to 6MM thickness)
High-density sound-absorbing material for effective sound insulation and fireproofing
12V/24V DC battery with charger and connecting wires
10-12 hours fuel tank with fuel indicator for continuous operation
High protection class control box (IP55) with waterproofing, electric leakage protection, and breaker
Turn-back type air inflow & outlet design reduces noise and improves engine efficiency
Practical design with bottom holes for forklift, water outlet, and oil outlet for easy maintenance
Double open doors on both sides for comprehensive engine and alternator access
Available in silent, super silent, soundproof, trailer type, and container type configurations
Compliance with major standards: GB/T2820, ISO8528, IEC34, CE, EPA Tier4
