PRP - Prime Power Rating
Prime power is available for an unlimited number of annual hours in variable load application, in accordance with GB/T2820-97. A 10% overload capability is available for a period of 1 hour within a 12-hour operation cycle.
ESP - Emergency Standby Power
The standby power rating is applicable for supplying emergency power for the duration of a utility power interruption. No overload, utility parallel or negotiated outage operation capability is available at this rating.
