SDEC SC13E500D2 Diesel Generator Set
Our SDEC SC13E500D2 Diesel Generator is a high-performance, heavy-duty generator set designed for reliable prime and standby power. Engineered with the renowned SDEC engine, this 300kW unit is encased in a robust soundproof canopy, making it ideal for applications requiring low noise operation without compromising on power and durability. Perfect for construction, industrial, commercial, and emergency backup applications.
