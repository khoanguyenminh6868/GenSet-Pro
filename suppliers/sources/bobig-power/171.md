CE Standby Commercial Diesel Generator
High-performance 15kw, 20kw, 25kw, 30kW diesel generator sets with electric start and super silent frame design. Available in 50/60Hz configurations with SDEC power technology.
Product Overview
The SDEC 4Z3.2-G21 Diesel Generator is a heavy-duty power solution designed for reliable prime and standby applications. Engineered with the renowned SDEC engine, this 25kw unit features a robust soundproof canopy for low-noise operation, making it ideal for construction, industrial, commercial, and emergency backup use.
