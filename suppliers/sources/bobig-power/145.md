280KW Silent Diesel Genset - Industrial Standby Power
Our YTO YM6S9L-DB Diesel Generator is a high-performance, heavy-duty diesel generator set designed for reliable prime and standby power. Engineered with the renowned YTO engine, this 280kW unit is encased in a robust soundproof canopy, making it an ideal solution for applications requiring low noise operation without compromising on power and durability. Perfect for construction, industrial, commercial, and emergency backup applications.

Power Rating Definitions
PRP (Prime Power): Available for unlimited annual hours in variable load applications according to GB/T2820-97 standards. Includes 10% overload capability for 1 hour within 12 hours of operation.
ESP (Standby Power): Applicable for supplying emergency power during utility power interruptions. No overload, utility parallel, or negotiated outage operation capability available at this rating.
