Baudouin FAW Genset Diesel Generator
Our Baudouin 4M10G110/5 Diesel Generator is a high-performance, heavy-duty generator set designed for reliable prime and standby power applications. Engineered with the renowned Baudouin engine, this 80kW unit features a robust soundproof canopy, making it ideal for applications requiring low noise operation without compromising on power and durability.
Perfect for construction, industrial, commercial, and emergency backup power needs.

Power Rating Definitions
PRP (Prime Power): Available for unlimited annual hours in variable load applications according to GB/T2820-97 standards. Includes 10% overload capability for 1 hour within 12 hours of operation.
ESP (Standby Power): Applicable for supplying emergency power during utility power interruptions. No overload, utility parallel, or negotiated outage operation capability available at this rating.
