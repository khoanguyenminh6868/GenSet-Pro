280kW Diesel Generator - Super Silent 350kVA 3 Phase 50Hz
Our YTO YM6S9L-DB Diesel Generator is a high-performance, heavy-duty generator set designed for reliable prime and standby power applications. Engineered with the renowned YTO engine, this 280kW unit features a robust soundproof canopy, making it ideal for applications requiring low noise operation without compromising power and durability. Perfect for construction, industrial, commercial, and emergency backup use.
