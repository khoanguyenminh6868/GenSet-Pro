Quality guarantee:
1. Material guarantee
The stator and the rotor use 800 grade silicon steel sheet from WISCO.
The copper wires are all made of Superior Essex.
The insulating material is of German brand Elantas, then is painted resin glue, vacuum insulating paint, and mildew resistant paint, and the final products are respectively the Elantas highest level of epoxy E300, epoxy EIP5070 and solvent-based alkyd resin paint VA42.
2. First-class insulation process
Through CNC vacuum pressure impregnated with varnish equipment(VPI), the six process including preheating, 100KPa vacuum maintaining pressure to eliminate bubbles, 100KPa vacuum maintaining pressure for primary insulating painting, 450KPa positive maintaining pressure for secondary deep insulation, 160 degree constant temperature baking are conducted to ensure the insulation level up to 500M&Omega;-&infin;.
3. Strict factory test
With large scale feedback motor load testing center, each operation index of the alternator are fully record before delivery. And to strictly control the key factor—main winding temperature rise, every alternator equips load testing to guarantee the product of 100% ex-factory pass rate under strict requirement.
SHYAN has passed ISO9001 and ISO14001 quality system certification with our products having obtained many patents certification, thus our product has the feature of reliable quality and durability.
