Application:
Gloria brand GR180 brushless alternator can be cooperate with diesel engine to work as diesel generator set, which serve for industrial appliances like oil field, mining, railway, coal, etc., or commercial appliances like communication, real estate, shopping mall, etc.
The Gloria series engines have a power ranging from 12.5KVA to 37.5KVA, voltage ranging from 110V to 13800V, and the frequency including 50 HZ and 60HZ, with the protection level up to IP21-IP25, thus the specific power demand of the local customers are meted.

Quality guarantee:
1. Material guarantee
The stator and the rotor use WISCO 800 grade silicon steel sheet.
The copper wires are all of Superior Essex wire.
The German brand Elantas insulating material is painted resin glue, vacuum insulating paint, and mildew resistant paint, then the final products are respectively the Elantas highest level of epoxy E300, epoxy EIP5070 and solvent-based alkyd resin paint VA42.
2. First-class insulation process
Our factory owns CNC vacuum pressure impregnated with varnish equipment(VPI) for preheating, 100KPa vacuum maintaining pressure to eliminate bubbles, 100KPa vacuum maintaining pressure for primary insulating painting, 450KPa positive maintaining pressure for secondary deep insulation, 160 degree constant temperature baking and other six processes so as to make the insulation level be 500M&Omega;-&infin;.
3. Strict factory test
Owning large scale feedback motor load testing center, the operation index of the alternator will be fully record before delivery. And each alternator is equipped with load testing to strictly control winding temperature rise so that the product are of 100% ex-factory pass rate under strict requirement.
SHYAN has passed ISO9001 and ISO14001 quality system certification with our products having obtained many patents certification, thus our product has the features of reliable quality and durability.
