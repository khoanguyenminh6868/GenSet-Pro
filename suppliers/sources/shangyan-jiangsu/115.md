Power: 8.1KVA-20.0KVA

Application:
Gloria brand GR160 brushless alternator can be assembled with diesel engine, working as diesel generator set to serve for industrial appliances like oil field, mining, railway, coal, etc., or commercial appliances like communication, real estate, shopping mall, etc.
The power ranges from 8.1KVA to 20.0KVA, the voltage is 110V to 13800V, and the frequency includes 50 HZ and 60HZ, with the protection level up to IP21-IP25, thus the specific power demand of the local customers are satisfied.

Quality guarantee:
1. Material guarantee
The stator and the rotor adopt WISCO 800 grade silicon steel sheet.
All the copper wires are from Superior Essex.
The insulating material is of German brand Elantas, which is painted resin glue, vacuum insulating paint, and mildew resistant paint to form the final products. They are respectively the Elantas highest level of epoxy E300, epoxy EIP5070 and solvent-based alkyd resin paint VA42.
2. First-class insulation process
By using CNC vacuum pressure impregnated with varnish equipment(VPI), the preheating, 100KPa vacuum maintaining pressure to eliminate bubbles, 100KPa vacuum maintaining pressure for primary insulating painting, 450KPa positive maintaining pressure for secondary deep insulation, 160 degree constant temperature baking and other six processes are conducted to make the insulation level reached 500M&Omega;-&infin;.
3. Strict factory test
Equipped with large scale feedback motor load testing center, the operation index of the alternator will be fully record before delivery. And each alternator is equipped with load testing to strictly control winding temperature rise so that the product are of 100% ex-factory pass rate under strict requirement.
SHYAN has passed ISO9001 and ISO14001 quality system certification with our products having obtained many patents certification, thus our product has the feature of reliable quality and durability.
