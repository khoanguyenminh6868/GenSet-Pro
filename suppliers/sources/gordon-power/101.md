How to install Sound Insulated Air Cooled Diesel Generator For Homes
Choose a suitable location for the generator, ensuring it is away from windows and doors to minimize noise and exhaust fumes.
Install a concrete pad or base to provide a stable and level surface for the generator to sit on.
Connect the generator to the home's electrical system through a transfer switch, which will safely isolate the generator from the utility power grid.
Install an air intake and exhaust system to ensure proper ventilation and prevent overheating. This may include installing ductwork and an exhaust pipe to direct the air and fumes away from the home.
Test the generator to ensure it is functioning properly and providing power to the home during a power outage. Regular maintenance and servicing should also be scheduled to keep the generator in good working condition.
