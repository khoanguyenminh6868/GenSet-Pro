产品概述
Designed for continuous power supply and frequent start-stop scenarios in low-to-medium power ranges, it is suitable for primary or backup power requirements in industrial warehousing, temporary construction power for real estate, and small-to-medium commercial facilities.
产品优势
High Reliability: Reinforced design and fatigue validation of the crankshaft, connecting rod, and cylinder block ensure stable operation under all load conditions
High Economy: Matched with high-pressure ratio turbochargers to increase air intake, resulting in a flat fuel consumption curve across the full load range for low fuel consumption
Intelligent Control: Supports CAN bus communication and is compatible with mainstream ATS and remote cloud monitoring platforms; standard features include remote start/stop, parameter monitoring, and fault alarming
High Adaptability: Capable of reliable operation in complex environments, including high temperatures, extreme cold, high altitudes, high humidity, and dusty conditions, while maintaining performance with varying fuel qualities.
