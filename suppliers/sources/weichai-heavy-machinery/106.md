产品概述
The 6M33 series land-based generator sets are Weichais new premium inline 6-cylinder generator sets, featuring stable power, high reliability, excellent fuel economy, and a wide range of applications; The 6M31 series land-based generator sets offer a power output range of 500-800 kW.
产品优势
High Reliability: Derived from over a century of European engine technology, the generator sets have undergone more than thirty durability and performance tests, ensuring long-lasting durability and superior reliability
Intelligent Control: Equipped with a microcomputer-based intelligent controller and international brand circuit breakers to achieve automated genset control
High-Efficiency Power: Features a proprietary ECU and specialized transient response calibration for robust power performance
High Economy: Matched with high-pressure ratio turbochargers and intercooling to increase air intake and optimize combustion, delivering significant fuel savings
