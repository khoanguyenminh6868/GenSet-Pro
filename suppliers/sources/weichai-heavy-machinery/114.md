产品概述
The 15 kVA-63 kVA series land-use standard generator sets are developed for low-power and high-portability needs, such as rural backup power, small shops, and outdoor camping, offering high economic efficiency and robust reliability.
产品优势
Superior Performance: Features industry-leading product performance indicators and an advanced unit security system
Multi-scenario Application: Professional generator set application integration capability, suitable for various application scenarios
Extended Maintenance Cycle: Exclusive triple-filter design and large-capacity fuel tanks ensure long-lasting endurance
