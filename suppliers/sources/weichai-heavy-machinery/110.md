产品概述
The 63-110 kVA series is equipped with the new-generation Weichai WP4.1 engine, featuring high power density, a stable power output of 50-100 kW, superior fuel efficiency, extended oil change intervals, and excellent economic performance.
产品优势
Fuel System: High injection pressure ensures high engine power and excellent fuel atomization for superior fuel economy
Air Intake System: High-efficiency turbocharger for optimized air intake and more efficient combustion
Superior NVH Performance: Utilizes pilot injection technology and a high-precision meshing gear train to achieve low engine vibration and noise
