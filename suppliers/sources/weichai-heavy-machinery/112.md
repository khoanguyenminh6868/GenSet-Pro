产品概述
The 16M33NG series is a new generation of high power density engines independently developed by Weichai, featuring a compact structure and superior economy; The 16M33 gas engine has a power range of 900–1280 kWm, suitable for 858–1231 kWe generator sets.
产品优势
Combustion System: Adopts multiple technical routes, providing high engine thermal efficiency and compatibility with various emission standards and associated petroleum gas requirements for versatile applications
Air Intake System: High-efficiency turbocharger for optimized air intake and more efficient combustion
Superior NVH Performance: Utilizes a high-precision meshing gear train to achieve low engine vibration and noise
