Gas Generator Sets
Inquiry Form
In addition to our line of water-cooled and air-cooled diesel generators, we also produce a line of gas generator sets. Our BFM1015GCP gas engines are similar in design to the diesel models except for a redesigned piston, turbocharger, cylinder head and intake manifold among other components. These generators have been calibrated to achieve the same level of electricity output as diesel engines. They can be used in vehicles and construction machinery among other applications.
