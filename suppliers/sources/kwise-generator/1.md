ABOUT US
FUJIAN KWISE GENERATOR CO.,LTD
As one of wholly-controlled company of ADDSINO CO.,LTD, FUJIAN KWISE GENERATOR CO.,LTD is an integrated professional factory committed to research ,development ,sale and after-sale and to be one of earliest factory to produce synchronous alternators. Since 1955, KWISE began to produce alternators and from 1963 worked with various Chinese armies supplying a big amount of alternators to cooperate with diesel generator sets, trailer and containerized power stations. KWISE generators were selected by the main powers of China three largest satellite launching centers, China Zhongshan station, and series of Shenzhou spaceships. Widely used in military, communication, oil, railway, ship, industry and mining, hospital, refrigerated container and real estate and other important fields with 30,000 square meters of factory area and many advantage equipments.
OUR MILESTONE
We started to produce the first generator, and was one of the first companies to produce generators in China.
1955
We were coupling with diesel generator sets ,trailer generator sets and containerized power stations for various China armies.
1964
We were coupling with diesel generator sets to be main powers of the three satellite launching centers.
1980

1993
