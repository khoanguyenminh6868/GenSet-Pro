Shandong Wokang Power Technology Co., Ltd.
Located in Weifang, China's "Power City", Shandong Wokang Power Technology Co., Ltd. is a high-tech manufacturer integrating R&D, production, sales, and after-sales of diesel/gas generator sets. With independent import and export rights, it delivers full-scenario one-stop power solutions to global clients.
I. Seven Years of Dedication, Forging Industry Benchmark
Since 2019, the company has accumulated 7 years of professional experience in diesel engine and generator set manufacturing, achieving leapfrog development:
2019-2021: Focused on core technology R&D, established standardized production, and obtained ISO9001 certification;
2022-2023: Expanded to gas generator sets, reached 10, 000+ units/year capacity, earned CE/SONCAP certifications, and entered overseas markets;
2024-2026: Built a 26, 000 square meters intelligent production base, boosted capacity to 15, 000 units/year, and exported to 80+ countries/regions with a global service network.
II. Full-Chain Strength for Diverse Needs
The company offers a full range of generator sets (open-type, silent-type, container-type, trailer-mounted) with power coverage from 2kW to 3500kW, compatible with international/domestic engines (Cummins, Volvo, Perkins, Weichai, Yuchai, Weifang Ricardo). It provides personalized customization (automatic control, noise reduction, mobile integration) and supporting equipment (water pumps, sand pumps). All products meet ISO9001/CE standards, pass authoritative certifications, and serve industrial/agricultural production, engineering, emergency power, commercial facilities, data centers, and medical security.
Backed by the 26, 000 square meters base, the company features a professional R&D team, intelligent production equipment, and full-process testing. Its global after-sales network covers Southeast Asia, Africa, the Middle East, and Europe, offering rapid spare parts delivery, 24/7 technical guidance, and remote troubleshooting, ensuring stable operation during the warranty period.
III. Business Performance & Industry Status
Based on industry averages and production capacity, the estimated annual turnover is 850-950 million RMB (calculated by 2025 average price of 62, 500 RMB/unit, 70%-75% capacity utilization, 19%-21% gross profit margin). Adhering to "Quality as Foundation, Service as Core", it has long-term cooperation with global clients, holding a key market share in small/medium generator set exports, with growing penetration in Southeast Asia, Africa, and other emerging markets.
IV. Future Strategy
In the next 3-5 years, the company focuses on three core directions:
Technological Upgrading: Increase R&D in intelligent generator sets, break through IoT integration, AI predictive maintenance, and National IV+ emission compliance, aiming for 60%+ intelligent models;
Market Expansion: Deepen existing overseas markets, tap high-end European markets and domestic "East Data West Computing" related sectors, expand high-value scenarios (data centers, new energy peak shaving), targeting 8%-10% annual compound growth;
Ecosystem Construction: Launch "Equipment + Service + Digitalization" integrated solutions, build a global after-sales cloud platform, expand long-term O&M services, and explore "Solar + Energy Storage + Diesel" hybrid systems. Committed to becoming a globally trusted comprehensive power solution provider, it delivers more efficient, stable, and low-carbon services.
