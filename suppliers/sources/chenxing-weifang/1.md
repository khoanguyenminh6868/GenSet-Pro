About Us
MORE>>
Weifang Chenxing Power Equipment, situated in Weifang, Shandong, a beautiful city of kites worldwide, is a comprehensive enterprise that combines production, sales, import and export trade. Weifang Chenxing Power Equipment, with a commitment to providing the generating set in power machinery industry, is a service provider offering complete equipment manufacturing, sales, installation and related supporting engineering services for diesel and gas generator sets. This company has a marketing consulting team and after-sales service team.
Weifang Chenxing Power Equipment has a modern production, processing and assembly workshop for diesel generating set, and it mainly offers production and service of high-quality diesel engines, diesel and gas generator sets and diesel water pump units. The power of the diesel generating sets sold is from 10KW to 2000KW; The power of gas generating set ranges from 8KW to 800KW; Diesel engine power from 20KW to 308KW; The power of diesel water pump units ranges from 5KW to 500KW, and it covers various purposes like diesel generating sets and diesel engines. Now the leading products are: The multi-cylinder diesel engines mainly fall into fixed power type, power generation type, construction machinery type, agricultural machinery matching type, etc. There are over 100 models and varieties. The main models of diesel generating sets are: over 500 models and varieties of various mobile power stations like fixed, ordinary, mobile, and trailer types. It can also produce low-noise, automated, diesel generating sets for diesel engine generators like Weichai, Yuchai, Cummins, SDEC, Volvo, Daewoo, Perkins, etc.
