About Yingtai
Yingtai Group Co., Ltd. is a large-scale generator manufacturer, located in Yangzhou City, Jiangsu Province, China. We are the main drafting and revision unit of national standards for diesel generator sets.
Our company owns an annual output of over 1 billion CNY , and has a modern production base with an area of over 240,000 square meters . Complete industrial production line from components to final products. Our main products include generator sets (diesel, gas, heavy oil, clean energy), alternators, high and low voltage switchgear and related accessories. The generator sets cover a single power range from 3kW to 3000kW, including low-voltage, high-voltage, silent, canopy, container and trailer types etc.
We have established long-term and in-depth cooperation with world-renowned engine manufacturers such as Cummins, Volvo, MTU, Perkins, Yuchai, Weichai, etc. Yingtai Group always upholds the core philosophy of "always starting a business and striving for excellence". With craftsmanship we build dreams, and through dedication we forge ahead!
