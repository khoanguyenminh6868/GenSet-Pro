Parallel Operation Feature

(Standard feature for DCA-125 to 800.)

From time to time, at a construction site, mine site or in other situations, a large

temporary power supply is required for a particular job. To meet this requirement,

Denyo‘s DCA Series generators incorporate a built-in manual parallel operation drive

system as standard, allowing you to create a large capacity generating plant on-site

without the need to procure any other equipment. See page 21 for optional automatic

parallel operation devices.

Voltage System

For companies that operate internationally or have motors that require power at different voltages, a different generator is usually

required for each voltage setting. However, the DCA Series generators are equipped with a dual voltage system or multi voltage

system, so one generator can be used to power motors with different voltage settings.

Dual Voltage System

(Details are as per specification table.)

The 3-phase 4-wire voltage can be switched between 200V/400V class depending

on the application.

Multi Voltage System

(Details are as per specification table.)

3-phase 400V/3-phase 200V/1-phase 100/200V class can be switched with a single

touch. One generator can be used for a variety of purposes.

Transportability

The skid base is designed for forklift handling, while the overall unit is engineered for

single-point lifting. The frame is optimized for load distribution and rigidity, with reliability

proven through years of operation. A lifting hook (lug) is positioned on the roof at the

balance point, allowing safe and smooth transportation by crane. All models feature a

modular design, enabling generators to be stacked for efficient use of storage space.

can maintain a constant RPM regardless of the amount of load applied (isochronous control*1).

control the speed using switches in a control box. *2
