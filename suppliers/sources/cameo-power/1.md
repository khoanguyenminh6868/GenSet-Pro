Chongqing Camel Power Machinery Co., Ltd., located in Beibei, Chongqing, is a renowned manufacturer of small diesel engines and related products. Formerly a large state-owned enterprise known as Chongqing Diesel Engine Factory, it was reorganized and established in 2002 through a merger with Hongyan Internal Combustion Engine Factory, another major domestic producer of internal combustion engines. As one of the early bases for diesel engine research, development, and production in China, Camel Power Machinery holds a significant place in the history of China's mechanical industry.
To further enhance its market competitiveness and expand its market share, Camel Power Machinery successfully merged into Senci Electric (stock code: SH 603109) in 2023. This strategic move not only brought valuable capital support but also opened a new chapter of development for the company. Backed by the strong foundation of Senci Group, Camel Power Machinery is dedicated to achieving greater breakthroughs and advancements in technological innovation, product development, and market expansion.
Currently, as a production base under Senci Group, Camel Power Machinery focuses on the manufacturing of small-power water-cooled and air-cooled diesel engines, generator sets, and welding generator equipment, continuously providing customers with high-quality products and services. Through continuous technological innovation and management optimization, the company is steadily advancing towards its goal of becoming an industry leader.
