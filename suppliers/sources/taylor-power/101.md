About the Agricultural Generators
Taylor Power Systems Agricultural Generators are built to meet the demanding needs of today's farms, offering reliable standby power when it matters most. Designed, engineered, and manufactured in Clinton, Mississippi, these generators feature heavy-duty industrial diesel engines and brushless rotating field alternators with Class "H" insulation for long-lasting performance. Units rated 80kW and above come standard with PMG excitation for enhanced motor-starting capability. Each generator includes a rugged steel base with integral vibration isolators, oil and coolant pre-filled, and is factory-tested prior to shipment to ensure dependable operation in the field.
An analog control panel offers clear readouts of AC frequency, load percentage, and hours of operation, along with a manual/auto/off selector switch. Critical engine diagnostics are easily accessible with separate oil pressure and water temperature gauges, and a manual key override ensures operation even in the event of control failure. High ambient unit-mounted radiators provide reliable cooling in extreme temperatures, and standard features such as an engine block heater, spark arrestor muffler, flexible fuel lines, and a battery charger are all included for quick deployment and smooth operation.
With a two-year standard warranty and full support from Taylor Sudden Service's national network of factory-trained technicians, these agricultural generator sets are built for years of dependable service. Optional accessories and multiple engine configurations allow for tailored solutions to meet the unique needs of every farm. When downtime isn't an option, trust Taylor Power Systems Agricultural Generators to keep you running.

How do I choose the right generator size for my farm?
Generator size depends on the total power requirements of your agricultural equipment-such as irrigation pumps, fans, feeders, and milking systems. A professional load assessment is the best way to determine the appropriate kilowatt (kW) rating to keep your operation running smoothly during outages.
What is the difference between standby and portable generators for farm use?
Standby generators are permanently installed and automatically engage during a power outage, making them ideal for farms that require uninterrupted power. Portable generators are best for smaller, short-term applications but may not support critical systems like irrigation or livestock climate control.
How long can a standby generator run on a farm?
Standby generators can run continuously as long as there is sufficient fuel. Diesel models depend on the tank size and refueling schedule. Many agricultural operations install larger or sub-base tanks to ensure extended runtime during prolonged outages.
Do I need a transfer switch for my agricultural generator?
Yes. An automatic transfer switch (ATS) is essential for safely switching from utility to generator power. It prevents backfeeding and ensures your equipment stays powered without manual intervention-critical for operations like poultry houses and dairy parlors.
How often should I service my agricultural generator?
Routine maintenance-including oil and filter changes, coolant checks, battery inspections, and load testing-should be done at least annually or according to the manufacturer's guidelines. More frequent servicing may be necessary for high-use applications.
What type of fuel do Taylor agricultural generators use?
The Agricultural Series uses diesel fuel, which is ideal for rural and remote locations due to its availability, energy density, and long shelf life.
Will the generator automatically start during an outage?
Yes. When paired with an ATS, the generator will detect a power loss and automatically start within seconds to maintain operation of critical systems.
Can I monitor my farm generator remotely?
Many Taylor generators can be equipped with remote monitoring systems that provide real-time status updates, alerts, and maintenance notifications-especially useful for large farms or operators managing multiple systems.
How long is the warranty on agricultural models?
All Taylor Power Agricultural Generators include a standard two-year warranty on components and workmanship, with optional extended coverage available for added peace of mind.
What makes an agricultural generator different from a commercial model?
Agricultural generators are built to power specific systems used in farming, including irrigation, ventilation, milking machines, and feed lines. They're engineered for high durability in outdoor, remote, and often dirty environments where uptime is critical to avoid loss of livestock or crops.
