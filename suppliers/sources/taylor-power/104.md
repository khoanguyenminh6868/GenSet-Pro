About the Select Series
The Select Series generator features a digital control panel for enhanced reliability and optimum performance, powered by a microprocessor-based system with digital metering. The heavy-duty skid base is built with integral vibration isolation, eliminating the need for additional vibration spring isolators, making servicing easier. The generator set includes a reliable, fuel-efficient 4-cycle industrial engine, ensuring years of trouble-free operation. Additionally, each unit is equipped with a high ambient cooling system, designed to handle full-rated output in temperatures up to 50°C (122°F), perfect for challenging environments. This series comes with industry-standard features such as a heavy-duty steel base, vibration isolators, a high ambient unit-mounted radiator, and a two-year standard warranty. Optional features include transfer switches, custom enclosures, and a sub-base fuel tank for diesel models, among others, allowing full customization to meet your needs. The generator's combination of standard, optional, and custom features makes it versatile for a wide range of applications, delivering both performance and reliability.

How do I choose the right generator size for my needs?
Generator size depends on the total power requirements of the equipment you need to run during an outage. A load assessment by a professional can help determine the appropriate kilowatt (kW) rating to ensure uninterrupted power.
What is the difference between standby and portable generators?
Standby generators are permanently installed, automatically start during an outage, and provide continuous power. Portable generators are manually operated, typically run on gasoline, and are best suited for temporary or limited use.
How long can a standby generator run continuously?
A standby generator can run indefinitely as long as it has a sufficient fuel supply. Natural gas units can run as long as gas service is available, while diesel models depend on the size of the fuel tank and refueling intervals.
Do I need a transfer switch for my standby generator?
Yes. A transfer switch safely connects the generator to your electrical system, preventing dangerous backfeeding and ensuring a seamless transition from utility to generator power.
How often should I maintain my generator?
Routine maintenance, including oil and filter changes, battery checks, and load testing, should be performed at least once a year or as recommended by the manufacturer. Frequent testing ensures reliability in an emergency.
Are these generators suitable for extreme temperatures?
Yes. Both the Select and Streamline Series feature high ambient cooling systems, designed to operate in temperatures up to 50°C (122°F), making them ideal for demanding environments.
Can I monitor my generator remotely?
Yes. Many standby generators support remote monitoring, allowing you to check status, run time, and maintenance alerts via a mobile app or connected system.
What type of fuel do these generators use?
Select Series models are available in diesel and natural gas configurations, while the Streamline Series is designed for natural gas and LP (liquid propane) applications.
Will a standby generator automatically start during an outage?
Yes. When paired with an automatic transfer switch (ATS), the generator detects a power loss and starts automatically, restoring power within seconds.
How long is the warranty on these generators?
Both the Select and Streamline Series come with a standard two-year warranty, with options to extend coverage for added peace of mind.
