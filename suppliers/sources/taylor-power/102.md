About the Trailer Mounted Generators
TM Series Trailer Mounted Mobile Generators are designed for fast, flexible, and dependable power delivery wherever it's needed. These towable generators offer a wide range of output capacities from 25kW to 1000kW, making them ideal for everything from small job sites to large-scale operations. Built with rugged Tier 4 Final diesel engines and engineered for easy mobility, each unit comes equipped with a sound-attenuated enclosure, tandem axle trailer, and dual containment fuel cell for safe, quiet, and reliable operation in virtually any environment.
Features like multi-voltage selector switches, color-coded camlocks, and busbar output connections make the TM Series highly adaptable to various applications. Whether you're a rental company, municipality, first responder, or telecom provider, Taylor's mobile power solutions deliver the performance and convenience you need without the hassle of permanent installation or crane transport. Backed by nationwide service support from Taylor Sudden Service, the TM Series keeps your operations moving even when the grid doesn't.

What sizes are available in the TM Series of trailer mounted generators?
The TM Series ranges from 25kW to 1000kW, offering a broad selection to meet small, mid-size, and large-scale power demands. Models include trusted Tier 4 Final engines from Isuzu, Kohler, FPT, and Volvo.
What industries commonly use trailer mounted generators?
These generators are used by equipment rental companies, municipalities, emergency response teams, telecom providers, construction firms, utilities, and event organizers who require immediate, portable power.
Do I need a crane or special equipment to transport these generators?
No. Each TM Series generator comes mounted on a tandem axle trailer, making it towable with a standard heavy-duty truck-no crane required for deployment or relocation.
Are the TM Series generators weatherproof and sound-attenuated?
Yes. Each unit includes a sound-attenuated, powder-coated enclosure designed to perform in all types of weather while minimizing operating noise in sensitive environments.
Can I choose between different voltages?
Yes. All TM Series units come with a multi-voltage selector switch and standard output connections including camlocks and busbar terminals for versatile setup options.
How long can these generators run before refueling?
Run time depends on the model and load, but each unit is equipped with a large dual-containment fuel cell to support extended operation and comply with fuel spill prevention standards.
What type of fuel do TM Series generators use?
All TM Series models use ultra-low sulfur diesel (ULSD) and are compliant with EPA Tier 4 Final emission standards.
Are these generators compliant with Tier 4 Final regulations?
Yes. Every TM Series generator is built with Tier 4 Final-certified engines, ensuring compliance with the latest EPA emissions standards for mobile power equipment.
Is technical support or service available for these generators?
Yes. Taylor Sudden Service provides parts, service, and on-site support across the country. Our factory-trained technicians are equipped to handle all TM Series models and major generator brands.
Can I use a TM Series generator as a backup for critical infrastructure?
Absolutely. These units are suitable for both prime and standby applications, including hospitals, communication systems, data centers, and other critical services.
