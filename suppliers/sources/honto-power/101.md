Our Service Process
From requirement to delivery, quality assured at every stage
1
Requirement Analysis
▲ (click to collapse)
Technical feasibility and economic rationality evaluation.
Requirements collection: Analyzes and determines the general model, specifications, technical parameters, and usage environment of the required transformer based on the power system requirements.
Assessment of Requirements: Evaluate the collected requirements to ensure technical feasibility and economic rationality.
2
Technical Exchange
▼ (click to expand)
Understanding capabilities and finalizing solutions.
Technical communications: Conduct technical exchanges with us, provide detailed requirements, and understand our production capabilities, technical strength, and past project experience.
Determination of Solution: Both parties reach an agreement on technical details, delivery time, price, etc., forming a preliminary technical proposal and quotation.
3
Contract Signing
▼ (click to expand)
Clear terms and formal agreements.
Contract negotiation: After both parties reach an agreement on the technical proposal and quotation, the contract negotiation stage begins, clarifying terms such as delivery time, quality standards, payment methods, and after-sales service.
Formal signing: Sign a formal procurement contract to ensure the rights and interests of both parties are protected.
4
Final Design & Production Preparation
▼ (click to expand)
Detailed engineering and pre-production planning.
Detailed design: According to the contract requirements, carry out detailed design of the power transformer, including drawings and dimensional design of components such as coils, insulation parts, iron cores, and oil tanks.
Material procurement: Procure high-quality raw materials such as silicon steel, copper/aluminum wires, insulation materials, etc. to ensure product quality.
Production preparation: Preparation work before production, including equipment debugging, personnel training, production plan formulation, etc.
5
Manufacturing Process
▼ (click to expand)
Precision manufacturing with strict quality control.
Insulation accessories: Cut the insulation material into required shape and size, perform moisture-proof treatment and other processes.
Oil tank: Cut steel plates into various parts of the oil tank and oil storage components, and perform welding, rust removal, painting and other processes.
Iron core production: Stack the silicon steel sheets into an iron core, install a pull plate and shield, and perform iron core testing and other processes.
Coil production: According to the drawing requirements, select appropriate wire specifications and turns, make the high voltage and low voltage coil windings, and perform wire welding, insulation, coil testing and other processes.
6
Factory Testing
▼ (click to expand)
Comprehensive testing to ensure product quality.
Assembly and testing: Assemble the iron cores, coils, and other insulation components together for semi-finished product testing, body drying, after that, the active parts will be put into oil tank with oil injection, and leakage testing, hot oil circulation, and static discharge will be proceeded.
Finally, perform a factory test, normally routine test and optional type test and special test according to requirements listed in contract to ensure the product performance meets the requirements.
7
Delivery
▼ (click to expand)
Safe packaging and professional shipping.
Packaging and Transportation: Packaging the product to prevent damage during transportation. Generally, we fix the main body of power transformer in wooden case and for transportation to ensure safe arrival at the destination. For large size main body of power transformers, we have professional solutions for transportation too.
8
After-Sales Service
▼ (click to expand)
24-month warranty and ongoing support.
Warranty & Support: Our product warranty lasts up to 24 months. After delivering the product to the customer, we will provide necessary technical support of installation guidance. Emails, phone calls, video guide, and on-site guidance are all optional.

Frequently Asked Questions
What types of transformers do you manufacture?
We offer a wide range of diesel generator sets, including open type generators, silent generators, trailer-mounted generators, containerized power stations, and gas generators for industrial, commercial, agricultural, and emergency backup applications.
What power range are your generators available in?
Our generator sets are available from 5kW to 3000kW, suitable for residential backup power, construction sites, factories, hospitals, mining projects, and utility applications.
Which engine brands are available?
We provide flexible engine options based on customer requirements, including Cummins, Perkins, Deutz, Weichai, Yuchai, SDEC, Ricardo, Baudouin, and other reliable brands.
Can you provide silent diesel generators?
Yes. We manufacture silent type diesel generators with low-noise canopies, weatherproof enclosures, and efficient cooling systems, ideal for urban areas, hospitals, hotels, and commercial buildings.
Do you support OEM and customized solutions?
Yes. We support OEM & ODM services, including custom voltage, frequency, controller brands, canopy colors, logo branding, ATS systems, trailer configurations, and containerized solutions.
What voltage and frequency options can you provide?
Our generators can be customized for different markets, including:
50Hz / 60Hz
220V / 380V / 400V / 415V / 440V
Single-phase and three-phase power systems
Are your generators tested before shipment?
Yes. Every generator set undergoes strict quality inspection and load testing before delivery, including voltage stability testing, frequency testing, noise testing, and operational performance checks.
What certifications do your generators have?
Depending on project and market requirements, we can provide ISO, CE, factory test reports, and other related certifications and inspection documents.
How long is the production lead time?
Standard models are usually available within 7–15 working days. Customized generator solutions may require additional production time depending on specifications.
Which countries do you export to?
Our generators have been exported to customers in Africa, Southeast Asia, the Middle East, South America, and other global markets for industrial, commercial, agricultural, and emergency power projects.
Can your generators be used for prime power and standby power?
Yes. We provide both prime power generators for continuous operation and standby generators for emergency backup applications.
How can I get a quotation quickly?
Simply send us your required power range, voltage, frequency, and application details. Our team will provide a professional quotation and technical recommendation within 24 hours.
