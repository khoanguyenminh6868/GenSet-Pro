The so-called mobile diesel generator set is to add "mobile dragging equipment" to the diesel generator set.
1. With movable hook: 180* turntable, flexible steering, Easy to operate.
2. Brake: At the same time, it has a reliable air brake intertace and manual brake system to ensure safety during driving.

★ Product Feature
The minimum thickness of the generator top cover is 2.0mm, and 2.5mm for special orders. The canopy adopts an overall disassembly structure design, and the door is large for easy inspection and maintenance.
The generator is constructed from a heavy-duty fabricated steel base frame that also includes a built-in fuel tank for at least 8 hours of continuous operation.
For the Australian market, the environmentally friendly fully enclosed base tank ensures no oil or coolant spills to the ground.
The canopy and underframe are shot blasted, high quality outdoor electrostatic powder coated, and oven heated at 200°C to provide excellent protection against rust, corrosion and wear.
To reduce noise levels, the generator is constructed with 4cm thick silent foam sound absorbing material, with a 5cm high density rock wool option available on special order request.
For specific regions such as Southeast Asia, Africa, and the tropics, the generator can be equipped with a 50°C radiator to ensure optimal performance in hot climates.
In cold weather countries, generators include water heaters and oil heaters that are coolant tested to ensure reliable operation at low temperatures.
The entire generator is mounted on a solid base and equipped with anti-vibration devices to minimize noise and vibration during operation.
