An LPG generator set is a clean energy power generation device fueled by liquefied petroleum gas. Different from traditional diesel and gasoline generator sets, it converts the chemical energy of fuel into mechanical energy via a gas internal combustion engine to drive the generator and output electric power, serving as an energy-saving, environmentally friendly, and cost-effective distributed power generation solution. Powered by a mixture of propane and butane, LPG features stable storage and convenient access. It is ideal for scenarios without fixed gas pipelines or stable mains power and areas with strict environmental regulations, acting as an optimal alternative to conventional fuel generator sets.
An LPG generator set mainly consists of a gas engine, generator, intelligent control system, fuel supply system, cooling system and safety protection system. During operation, liquefied petroleum gas undergoes pressure stabilization, gasification and filtration before entering the engine cylinder. Fully mixed with air and ignited, the gas pushes the piston to do work, converting thermal energy into mechanical energy and driving the generator rotor to rotate. Electric power is then output based on the electromagnetic induction principle. The supporting intelligent control system monitors core parameters in real time, including unit speed, voltage, current, oil temperature and water pressure, and automatically adjusts output power according to load changes to ensure stable, safe and efficient operation of the unit.
