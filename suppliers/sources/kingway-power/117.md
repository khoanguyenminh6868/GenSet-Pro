Biogas generator set is a power generation device that uses biogas as fuel and gas internal combustion engine as prime mover to drive AC generators, converting mechanical energy into electric and thermal energy. It is mainly composed of gas engine, synchronous AC generator and control system, featuring high efficiency, cost-effectiveness and environmental friendliness.
Our biogas generator sets are applicable to biogas derived from straw and mushroom residue, sewage biogas produced from sewage sludge, livestock manure and kitchen waste, as well as landfill gas. For detailed fuel information, please refer to biogas introduction.
Classification of Biogas Generator Sets
By gas source: Biological biogas generator set, sewage biogas generator set and landfill gas generator set.
By engine speed: High-speed biogas generator set with rotating speed over 1000 r/min, medium-speed biogas generator set with rotating speed ranging from 500 to 1000 r/min.
Advantages of Medium-speed Generator Sets
Compared with high-speed units, medium-speed gas generator sets possess prominent merits:
Longer service life and higher reliability. Low rotating speed effectively reduces engine abrasion and extends component lifespan. Less wear cuts maintenance costs and improves economic benefits. The overhaul cycle of medium-speed units exceeds 60,000 hours, while that of high-speed units is less than 15,000 hours. Turbochargers and intercoolers of high-speed equipment also suffer higher failure rates.
Superior thermal efficiency. Lower speed prolongs the mixing and combustion duration of gas and air inside cylinders for sufficient combustion, resulting in lower exhaust temperature.
