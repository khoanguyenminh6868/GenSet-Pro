Product Introduction
The 350KW three-phase silent natural gas generator is a high-performance industrial power supply solution, integrating efficiency, environmental protection and low-noise operation. Tailored for large-scale facilities requiring uninterrupted and stable power, it leverages the cleanliness of natural gas and advanced noise reduction technology, making it ideal for both emergency and regular power supply across multiple industries.
Core Specifications:
Rated power of 350KW, three-phase power supply configuration compatible with industrial standard voltage ratings; Fueled by natural gas, it offers cost-effective energy consumption and meets strict emission standards, aligning with environmental regulations; Equipped with a high-efficiency generator core, it delivers superior power generation efficiency and stable voltage output, ensuring the safe operation of precision equipment.
Key Advantages:
Silent operation is a standout feature-integrating professional sound insulation enclosures, shock absorption structures and noise-reducing air ducts, its operating noise is significantly lower than traditional generators, enabling use in noise-sensitive areas such as residential complexes, hospitals and office buildings; Natural gas is easily accessible and cost-efficient, with combustion producing no black smoke and minimal pollutants, achieving a balance between economy and environmental friendliness; The compact design facilitates easy installation, with automatic start/stop functions for simplified operation and maintenance, ensuring high reliability.
Application Scenarios :
Suitable for factory workshops, commercial malls, data centers, hospitals, large stadiums, real estate projects and other venues. It serves as a reliable emergency backup power source to handle sudden grid outages, and can also function as a regular power supply to guarantee uninterrupted power for production, business operations and daily activities.
