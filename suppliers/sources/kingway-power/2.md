About Us
Reliable Power Solutions Expert
KINGWAY GROUP has been deeply engaged in the power generation sector for over 20 years, standing as a high-tech enterprise integrating production, R&D, overseas sales, and after-sales services. The company boasts 18 patents covering various core technologies and has obtained more than 10 product and production certifications, including ISO, CE, ATEX, ROHS, DNV, BV, SGS, Lloyd's Register, CSC, and CCS, etc. KINGWAY's main product portfolio comprises: Gas generator sets, explosion-proof series products, Rig Safe series products, high-end diesel generator sets, load banks, lighting towers, etc. Adhering unswervingly to the development philosophy of "Innovation as the Guide, Quality as the Foundation, and After-sales as the Priority", KINGWAY continuously delivers premium products and services to global clients.
