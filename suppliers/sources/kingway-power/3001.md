Product Introduction
Diesel driven generator sets for applications in Hazardous Area ( Zone 2 ) mounted in the DNV certified 2.7-1 Skid frame enclosure producing 60KVA @ 0.8 pf,400VAC, 3 Phase +Neutral, 50HZ Output
Quality & Compliance:
The Ex product will follow the ATEX standard EN 60079 series and EN 1834 series ,and will give a《DECLARATION OF CONFORMITY》which is signed by the manufacturer according to the ATEX Standard EN 1834-1.
The 60KVA Zone 2 Hazardous Area Diesel Generator is a safety-focused power solution for environments with occasional flammable gases/vapors (e.g., mining sites, petrochemical auxiliaries, high-risk workshops). It meets ATEX/IECEx explosion-proof standards, with sealed critical parts and insulated exhausts to avoid spark/high-temperature ignition risks.​
With 60KVA capacity (48KW active power, 0.8 PF) and 400VAC 3-phase output, it suits industrial heavy loads. We offer tailored solutions: mine dust-proof designs, petrochemical gas-monitoring setups, and emergency power systems for workshops. Customization covers voltage/frequency adjustments, high-altitude adaptations, and add-ons like remote control. After-sales includes on-site surveys and maintenance to ensure reliable, compliant operation.
