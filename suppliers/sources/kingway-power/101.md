Product Introduction
The 500KW Natural Gas Generator is a medium-to-large power generation unit that takes natural gas as the core fuel, featuring high efficiency, environmental friendliness and stable operation. It is widely used in industrial and commercial backup power supply, distributed energy systems, and areas with unstable grid power.
Core Features:
1.Power Performance
Rated output power reaches 500KW, with stable voltage and frequency output, which can meet the power demand of large factories, commercial complexes, data centers, hospitals and other places. It supports both single-unit operation and parallel operation with the grid to realize peak shaving and valley filling.
2.Fuel & Environmental Protection Advantages
Taking clean energy natural gas as fuel, it has the characteristics of low carbon emission, low noise and low pollutant discharge. Compared with diesel generators, it has no black smoke emission, and the content of sulfur dioxide and particulate matter in exhaust gas is close to zero, which is in line with strict environmental protection standards.
3.Reliability & Durability
The engine is usually equipped with a high-performance spark ignition system, with strong load-bearing capacity and long service life. It supports 24-hour continuous operation and has a low failure rate, which is suitable for long-term power supply scenarios. In addition, it has multiple protection functions, such as overvoltage, overcurrent, overheating and oil shortage protection, to ensure safe operation.
4.Operation & Maintenance
The unit is equipped with an intelligent control system, which can realize automatic start-stop, remote monitoring and fault alarm, reducing manual operation intensity. The structure design is simple, the number of wearing parts is less than that of diesel generators, and the later maintenance cost is low.
Typical Application Scenarios:
Industrial manufacturing: As a backup power supply for factories to avoid production interruption caused by grid power failure.
Commercial and public facilities: Power guarantee for shopping malls, hotels, hospitals and data centers.
Distributed energy projects: Combined heat and power (CHP) operation to realize cascade utilization of energy and improve energy efficiency.
Remote areas: Power supply for areas where grid connection is difficult, relying on natural gas pipelines or LNG gas sources.
