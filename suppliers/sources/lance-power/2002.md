When emergency power is required, speed, reliability and availability are everything. This week we dispatched a 250kVA diesel generator in under 16 hours, supporting an urgent requirement from YESSS Electrical.
We are equipped to respond quickly to time-sensitive enquiries across commercial, industrial and infrastructure sectors.
The Challenge: Urgent Standby Power Requirement
At 14:00 on a Thursday, our team received an urgent enquiry for a 250kVA standby diesel generator. The requirement called for immediate availability, rapid preparation, and full compliance with industry standards.
For many projects, sourcing a generator at short notice can lead to delays due to manufacturing lead times, testing requirements, and logistical challenges. The key objective was to supply a fully tested, ready-to-deploy generator within an exceptionally tight timeframe to avoid disruption to site operations.
The Solution: Stock Availability & Fast-Track Deployment
Thanks to our stock holding of diesel generators in the UK, we were was able to allocate a suitable unit immediately and prioritise it for fast-track preparation.
The generator supplied was a 250kVA soundproof diesel generator , built using a Cummins 6LTAA 8.9-G2, paired with a Leroy-Somer alternator and controlled via a DSE7320 controller. This combination ensures high performance, fuel efficiency, and reliable standby power for demanding applications.
Our engineers carried out full generator testing, commissioning checks and Pre-Delivery Inspection (PDI) overnight. This ensured the unit met all operational and quality standards before leaving our facility.
The Result: Generator Dispatched in Under 16 Hours
By 07:30 the following morning, the generator was fully tested, inspected, and ready for collection – achieving a complete enquiry-to-dispatch turnaround in less than 16 hours.
This rapid deployment allowed the customer to maintain continuity and avoid costly downtime. Reinforcing the importance of working with a supplier that specialises in emergency generator supply and rapid power solutions.
At Lance Power, we understand that backup power is critical to operations. Whether supporting data centres, healthcare facilities, construction or industrial sites, we deliver reliable and responsive solutions tailored to your requirements.
Our ability to supply diesel generators quickly, combined with technical expertise and strong stock availability, ensures we can meet even the most demanding project timelines.
