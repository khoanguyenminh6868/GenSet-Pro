COMPANY PROFILE
LONGEN POWER, founded in 2006, is a leading generator manufacturer and specialized in the design, manufacture, sales, installation and services of diesel generator sets. Our generators power range from 5kVA to 3300kVA, equipped with Perkins, Cummins, Doosan, FPT, Mitsubishi, MTU, Volvo, Yanmar and Kubota engines and coupled with Stamford, Leroy Somer and Meccalte alternators.
