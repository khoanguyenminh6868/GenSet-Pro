Company Profile
Mindong Shengyuan Electromechanical Co., Ltd (FOR SHORT "MINSUN" ) was founded in 2000, loacated in Fu'an city, Fujian province. Minsun is a hightech enterpries which integrateds the research, development, assembly , production, sales and service of diesel generator set and alternators.
Our partners are all from world-famous and Chinese famous engine manufactuers such as Cummins, Perkins, Isuzu, Fawde, Ricardo, Weichai, Yuchai,Shangchai, Yangdong etc. Meanwhile we also have the production line for brushless alternator and brush alternator, such as ST/STC SERIERS, MINSUN STAMFORD.
We are providing comprehensive stable and timely solutions for elecricity power supplying systerm in allapplication fields (i.e. Railway, Mining, Petroleum, Petrifaction, Communication, Rental, Government, Military, Factories and Real Estate etc.). Till now, our products and solutions have been normally operated in more than 30 regions and countries all over the world.

Quality Control
Full-Link Quality Control Process
Multi-Stage Inspection, Defects Under Control
From raw material intake to finished product shipment, every generator set undergoes a rigorous full-link quality inspection. We implement four critical checkpoints: incoming material inspection, in-process assembly monitoring, full-load performance testing, and final pre-delivery inspection. This ensures stable performance, reliable delivery, controllable defect rates, and full traceability.
Incoming Material Inspection
All key components (engines, alternators, controllers, etc.) are sampled or 100% inspected before warehousing. Non-conforming items are rejected immediately.
