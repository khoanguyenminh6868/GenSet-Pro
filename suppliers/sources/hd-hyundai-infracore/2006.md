Overview
P158LE
A 15-liter engine (maximum output: 458 kWm) for a V-type, 8-cylinder, turbo/intercooler generator
DP Engine
The P series is our basic lineup of mid/large-sized generators. Our engines are mounted on emergency/commercial generators and are recognized for their high output and durability, so they are supplied all over the world. They are characterized by convenience in maintenance, high performance, and high fuel efficiency, meeting customers’ requirements.
Handiness of maintenance and high performance
User convenience - high fuel efficiency and being easy to install
