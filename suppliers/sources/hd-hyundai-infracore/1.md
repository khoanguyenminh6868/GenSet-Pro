Transparent Bucket
DEVELON has introduced a transparent bucket wheel loader that allows the driver to see even blind spots in front of the bucket for the first time in the world. With the transparent bucket function, the driver can easily check the blind spot in front of the bucket with a monitor to prevent safety accidents.

DEVELON Fleet Mgmt.
DEVELON Fleet Mgmt. enables the remote monitoring of the location,
operational status, and major part status of construction equipment
such as excavators and articulated dump trucks. To date,
the solution has been applied to 65,000 nits of HD CONSTRUCTION EQUIPMENT
construction equipment around the world in order to improve
operational efficiency and user convenience, using an IoT system
based on advanced ICT (Information and Communications Technology).
