Introduction
KEMA is a leading manufacturer of gasoline and diesel engine, generator, water pump, generating and welding machine, high pressure washer. We started making these products in 2001. We have two factory: Zhejiang Kema Engine and Machinery Co., LTD. and Chongqing Kema Industrial and Trading Co., LTD.
Zhejiang Kema is located in Taizhou, Zhejiang, China, near the East China Sea. It is 200 km far from Ningbo port. The company covers an area of 27,000 square meters with 500 staffs and 2 assembling lines. The production capacity is 30,000 pcs per month.
Chongqing Kema is located in Jiangjin, Chongqing, a mountain city in China. The company covers an area of 50,000 square meters with 500 staffs and 2 assembling lines. The production capacity is 30,000 pcs per month. Chongqing Kema is still in building new workshops.
After more than ten years' development, KEMA has grown into an advanced and modern enterprise with effective management. Believing in knowledge and continual development is power for a company, we continually innovate our products. Our products are exported to more than 100 countries and have already obtained a respectable reputation.
The quality of the products is our consistent goal. We always try our best to improve the quality and to reduce the cost.
Besides certified by ISO9001:2008 international quality management system, we also obtained some other international certificates such as GS, CE.
Face to the future development, KEMA will always maintain a close contact with customers, commit to developing high quality products to provide optimized service, realizing the harmonious development between KEMA and the society.
