Description
The 500KVA Perkins 2506C-E15TAG1 Super Silent Type diesel generator set is a premium low-noise industrial power solution engineered for environments where ultra-quiet operation is critical. As a genuine Perkins generator set, this reliable 500KVA Perkins generator is powered by the durable Perkins 2506C-E15TAG1 engine, a heavy-duty 6-cylinder inline diesel from the globally trusted Perkins 2500 series. Designed explicitly as a high-performance super silent generator, it delivers 500KVA prime power and 550KVA standby power while maintaining extremely low noise levels, making this 500KVA Perkins diesel generator ideal for hospitals, data centers, hotels, residential areas, and commercial buildings where noise pollution must be minimized. Whether used for continuous prime power or emergency standby power, the Perkins 2506C-E15TAG1 generator serves as an ultra-quiet, stable, and highly efficient heavy-duty industrial generator around the clock.
At the core of this 500KVA Perkins diesel generator lies the Perkins 2506C-E15TAG1 engine, a 6-cylinder, inline, turbocharged and air-to-air intercooled diesel engine with a displacement of 15.2 liters. Built for reliable heavy-duty power generation, this Perkins 2506C-E15TAG1 diesel generator runs at 1500 RPM (50 Hz) and delivers 360kW (450KVA) prime power and 400kW (500KVA) standby power at 0.8 power factor. The engine features electronic unit injection (EUI) and G3-level speed governing, ensuring precise fuel delivery, rapid load response, and stable voltage output for the 500KVA Perkins generator system. With turbocharging and air-to-air intercooling, this Perkins 2506C-E15TAG1 generator achieves high combustion efficiency, lower fuel consumption, and reduced emissions, complying with EU Stage II and EPA Tier 2 emission standards, making this super silent generator a globally compliant and premium low-noise industrial power solution for any critical application.

Core product advantages
The most outstanding feature of this 500KVA generator is its super silent performance. Unlike standard silent generators (75–85 dB(A)@7m), this super silent generator adopts a multi-layer composite noise reduction system, including high-density sound-absorbing cotton, damping layers, impedance composite mufflers, and labyrinth-type air intake and exhaust channels. The fully enclosed heavy-duty canopy is made of thick galvanized steel with internal acoustic insulation, achieving an ultra-low noise level of 60–68 dB(A)@7m—equivalent to a quiet office environment and well within the limits for hospitals, schools, and residential areas. The integrated intelligent ventilation system automatically adjusts fan speed according to temperature, ensuring full-load operation at 50°C ambient temperature while maintaining low noise.
The alternator matched to this 500KVA Perkins generator is a high-quality Stamford / Leroy-Somer brushless synchronous alternator with IP23 protection grade, providing excellent dust and water resistance. It delivers stable 400V/50Hz three-phase AC power, with voltage regulation within ±1% under variable loads and total harmonic distortion (THD) <1.5%, protecting sensitive equipment such as servers, medical devices, and precision instruments. The intelligent control system uses Deep Sea DSE7320 / EMCP4.4 controller, integrating auto start-stop, ATS automatic transfer, real-time monitoring, fault alarm, and remote communication (4G/Wi-Fi/Ethernet). Safety protections include overload, over-temperature, low oil pressure, over-speed, and short-circuit shutdown, ensuring the Perkins 2506C-E15TAG1 super silent generator operates safely in all environments.
Built with a high-strength steel base and heavy-duty shock absorbers, the 500KVA Perkins generator minimizes vibration transmission, extending component life. All key parts are original Perkins genuine parts, with strict quality control during manufacturing, ensuring low failure rate and long service life. The integrated large fuel tank supports 8–10 hours full-load continuous operation, reducing frequent refueling. The canopy is designed with quick-open access panels for easy maintenance, while the rainproof, dustproof, and corrosion-resistant coating ensures reliable performance outdoors.
Widely used as prime power for commercial complexes, industrial parks, and luxury residential areas, and as emergency standby power for hospitals, data centers, banks, and telecommunications base stations, the 500KVA Perkins 2506C-E15TAG1 Super Silent Type diesel generator is your ultimate choice for high power + ultra-quiet + high reliability applications.
