Description
Remote infrastructure operators — managing telecoms towers, off-grid relay stations, and unmanned substations across rural regions and island networks — require standby generators that activate without human intervention and sustain autonomous runtime across extended service cycles. The JIUAN Power 330KVA Cummins diesel generator — Model CDC330KVA, powered by the Cummins 6LTAA9.5-G1 9.5L engine from DCEC's ISO 9001-certified Wuhan manufacturing base — delivers 256KW/320KVA prime and 264KW/330KVA standby, purpose-built for infrastructure deployments demanding reliable unmanned operation. Every 320KVA silent Cummins genset leaves the factory ISO 8528-verified, with full pre-shipment acceptance records included as standard.
The Cummins 6LTAA9.5-G1 engine at the core of this soundproof diesel generator is produced at DCEC Dongfeng Cummins' ISO 9001-certified Wuhan facility — an inline six-cylinder, 9.5L platform with a 114mm bore, 145mm stroke, and 16.6:1 compression ratio. A Holset turbocharger with air-to-air aftercooler and Bosch P7100 high-pressure mechanical fuel injection — no ECU in the fuel circuit — maintain dependable combustion at remote sites where electronics servicing is unavailable. The electronic governor holds speed deviation to ≤3%, ensuring telecoms rectifier and battery-charging loads receive stable frequency across all duty points. Rated at 197–207 g/kW·h BSFC and a 12,000-hour overhaul interval, this platform supports long maintenance cycles between scheduled site visits.
The weatherproof shell of this 330KVA Cummins diesel generator is assembled from ≥2.0mm cold-rolled steel panels, finished with a 120°C high-temperature electrostatic enamel coat, and lined internally with 40 kg/m³ flame-retardant rock wool — measured acoustic output at 7 metres is 65–68 dB(A), within regulatory limits for residential-adjacent tower sites. A 650-litre integral skid-frame fuel tank provides over 7 hours of full-load autonomous runtime between resupply visits. Each 320KVA silent Cummins genset is pre-fitted with a DeepSea DSE7320 controller monitoring voltage, current, frequency, lube-oil pressure, coolant temperature, and operating hours; integrated ATS logic starts the generator within 3–6 seconds of mains failure and shuts down automatically on supply restoration.
JIUAN Power ships every CDC330KVA 330KVA Cummins diesel generator with load bank acceptance footage covering six duty-cycle stages from 0% to 110% and a PDF output certificate confirming ISO 8528 compliance. Documentation includes 3D engineering assembly drawings, macro-photography stills of key components for parts authentication, and a PICC insurance-backed 5-year warranty policy citing the Cummins 6LTAA9.5-G1 engine serial number for infrastructure asset registers. A 150-page field service reference in five language editions (English, Arabic, French, Spanish, Portuguese), electrical wiring diagrams, a 3D disassembly animation CD, and complimentary service wrenches with testing meters complete the package. Steel panel gauge is checked by digital calliper before despatch; warranty spares reach any major port within 3–5 working days, covering the full 5-year warranty period.

C5-M anti-corrosion coating (ISO 12944) is available for coastal island tower deployments; cold-region kits — jacket water heaters, fuel preheaters, and arctic lubricant packages — ensure the 330KVA Cummins diesel generator starts reliably at -30°C for high-latitude and mountainous infrastructure sites.

FAQ
Q: Can this 330KVA Cummins diesel generator operate unattended at a remote telecoms tower site?
A: Yes. The DSE7320 controller detects mains failure, starts the generator, and transfers the load within 3–6 seconds — then shuts down automatically on supply restoration — without human involvement. The 650-litre tank provides over 7 hours of full-load autonomous runtime, and optional 4G cloud monitoring supports remote start-stop and parameter checking via smartphone or PC.
Q: What cold-weather performance does this 320KVA silent Cummins genset offer?
A: The Cummins 6LTAA9.5-G1 engine starts reliably at -12°C without preheat. JIUAN Power supplies factory-fitted jacket water heaters, fuel preheaters, and arctic lubricant packages as an integrated cold-region kit, extending dependable cold-start to -30°C for high-latitude tower sites and northern infrastructure deployments.
