Who We Are
JIUAN Power is a leading direct generator manufacturer and exporter of premium diesel generator sets based in Guangzhou, China. With over 18 years of expertise , we specialize in providing reliable power solutions for global markets including Africa, South America, Southeast Asia and Europe.
We guarantee 100% brand-new machines assembled with only top-tier components — Cummins, Perkins, Weichai, Baudouin, Stamford and Leroy-Somer — covering every application from project backup power to large-scale industrial power plants. OEM / ODM customization is welcome.
All production runs under strict quality control with major international certifications (CE, ISO, BV, SASO and more), and we deliver one-stop service from selection and design to installation guidance and after-sales support.

Our Quality & Service Commitment.
What every JIUAN unit delivers — before and after it reaches your site.
01
Extended warranty, PICC-backed
Up to 2 years or 2,000 hours of full-unit warranty (whichever comes first), underwritten with PICC (People’s Insurance Company of China). The freight forwarder’s insurance policy is available for your reference. OEM extended cover: up to 3 years on Perkins engines and 2 years on Stamford S-series alternators.
02
3–5 day global parts express
Guaranteed expedited air shipping: core warranty components arrive anywhere in the world in just 3 to 5 days, keeping your downtime to a minimum.
03
100% new & individually traceable
Every unit is brand new with a unique ESN (Engine Serial Number) . Each generator undergoes staged load tests at 0%–25%–50%–75%–100% and 110% overload before shipment — the test video and PDF load analysis report are delivered with every unit.
04
Heavy-duty canopy engineering
Soundproof canopies use cold-rolled steel ≥ 2.0 mm , finished with 120°C electrostatic high-gloss powder coating for outstanding rust and scratch resistance. High-density flame-retardant rock wool (40 kg/m³) keeps noise down to 65–68 dB at 7 m .
05
Ready for extreme environments
Cold-region kit: engine jacket water heater, fuel heater and automatic louver system for Siberia and Northern Europe. Coastal kit: C5-M grade anti-corrosion painting for salt-mist islands and offshore sites.
06
Support that lowers every barrier
Free service wrench & testing meters; a 150-page maintenance manual in 5 languages (CN/EN/ES/FR/RU) with wiring diagrams and 3D disassembly animation; 3D exploded views & anti-counterfeit close-ups for CAD-like inspection; and one-stop customs clearance certificate service.
