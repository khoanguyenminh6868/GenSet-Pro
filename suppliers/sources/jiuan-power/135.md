Product Overview
The heavy-duty 900kVA diesel generator (720kW/900kVA standby, 640kW/800kVA prime) is an uncompromising, industrial-grade power generation system engineered for mission-critical applications, continuous base-load operations, and demanding emergency standby scenarios. At the heart of this high-capacity 900kVA diesel generator lies the world-renowned Perkins 4006-23TAG3A turbocharged and air-to-air charge-cooled (ATAAC) diesel engine, precision-coupled with an authentic industrial-class Stamford S6L1D-C41 brushless synchronous alternator.
To deliver seamless multi-unit synchronization, grid paralleling, and proportional load sharing, the genset incorporates the advanced SmartGen HGM9510N intelligent digital parallel controller. Built to satisfy international manufacturing and testing standards including ISO 8528, ISO 3046, IEC 60034, and CE directives , this 900kVA diesel generator provides superior transient step-load response, optimal frequency stability, high thermal efficiency, and an exceptional total cost of ownership (TCO) across its operational lifecycle.
Whether deployed across Tier III/IV data centers, hospital intensive care units, high-load manufacturing facilities, high-altitude mining sites, or municipal infrastructure, this Perkins-powered 900kVA diesel generator ensures uncompromising electrical stability when grid downtime is not an option.
Key Engineering Advantages & Competitive Selling Points
Heavy-Duty Powertrain: Perkins 4006-23TAG3A Engine
High Displacement & Robust Power Density — Configured with 6 inline cylinders and individual 4-valve cylinder heads to optimize airflow dynamics, promote complete fuel combustion, and sustain severe ISO 8528 G3 block-load additions.
Advanced Turbocharging & ATAAC — Heavy-duty exhaust-driven turbochargers coupled with an air-to-air charge air cooling (ATAAC) system substantially lower intake manifold air temperatures, ensuring resilient continuous power output even in high-ambient climates (up to 50°C ).
Individual Cylinder Head Architecture — Simplifies major top-end overhauls, improves cylinder bore circularity and thermal dissipation, and dramatically cuts downtime costs during scheduled service maintenance.
Unit Injector Fuel Delivery — Mechanically actuated unit fuel injectors deliver high injection pressures and ultra-fine fuel atomization, minimizing specific fuel consumption (SFC) while suppressing particulate emissions.
Superior Alternator Technology: Genuine Stamford S6L1D-C41
Brushless PMG Excitation with MX321 AVR — Outfitted with a Permanent Magnet Generator (PMG) and Stamford MX321 AVR, this setup ensures clear separation between main stator harmonics and excitation circuits, boasting an impressive 300% short-circuit current ride-through capacity for up to 10 seconds .
2/3 Pitch Winding Configuration — Effectively suppresses 3rd, 9th, and 15th harmonic wave distortions, providing pristine sinusoidal electrical waveforms fully compatible with sensitive non-linear loads, such as large data center UPS banks and variable frequency drives (VFDs).
Class H Insulation & Vacuum Pressure Impregnation (VPI) — Industrial-grade Class H insulation combined with high-grade VPI treatment delivers superior dielectric strength, withstands high thermal rise, and resists moisture, dust, and corrosive coastal salt mist.
Digital Synchronization & Load Sharing: SmartGen HGM9510N
Automated Paralleling & Load Allocation — Engineered for automatic synchronization and proportional real/reactive load sharing between multiple gensets of equal or disparate capacities, as well as automatic mains paralleling and peak-lopping.
Comprehensive Engine & Electrical Protections — Continuous surveillance of 3-phase bus/generator voltage, current, frequency, reverse power, overcurrent, loss of excitation, earth fault, oil pressure, coolant temperature, and overspeed.
Telemetry & BMS Integration — Onboard opto-isolated RS485 communication port running standard Modbus RTU protocol, facilitating seamless SCADA and Building Management System (BMS) integration.
Structural Rigid Skid & Acoustic Soundproofing
Anti-Vibration Base Frame — Fabricated from heavy-gauge structural steel with high-deflection elastomeric vibration isolators to eliminate harmonic vibration transmission to concrete foundations.
Acoustic Silent Canopy (Optional) — Formed from 2.0mm–2.5mm cold-rolled galvanized steel panels lined with high-density, fireproof acoustic rock wool, keeping operating noise levels down to 75–78 dB(A) @ 7 meters .
