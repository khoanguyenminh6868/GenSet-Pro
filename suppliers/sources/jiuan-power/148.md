64KW Supermarket Cold Chain and Food Retail ATS Standby — Weichai WP4.1D80E200 Compact Engine
The CDW88KVA is Jiuan Generator's 88KVA Weichai diesel generator for supermarket cold chain protection, food retail outlets, small offices, clinics, and community commercial premises where ATS-triggered emergency standby must auto-start within seconds of grid loss. Powered by the Weichai WP4.1D80E200 four-cylinder turbocharged diesel, the CDW88KVA delivers 64KW/80KVA ISO8528 PRP prime and 70KW/88KVA standby — in a compact 2600×1100×1600mm, 1700kg footprint with ≤72dB(A) silent canopy.
The Weichai WP4.1D80E200 genset engine is an inline four-cylinder, turbocharged water-cooled diesel with 4.088L displacement, 105mm × 118mm bore and stroke, and 17.5:1 compression ratio. The high compression ratio supports reliable cold start in un-air-conditioned generator rooms across tropical and subtropical markets. Electronic fuel injection holds specific fuel consumption at ≤205g/kWh across the prime load range. The 13L oil sump supports 500-hour planned maintenance intervals aligned with typical commercial building service cycles.
The 64KW compact prime generator CDW88KVA brushless synchronous alternator is wound with all-copper conductors, H-class insulated, and delivers stable 400V/50Hz three-phase supply with steady-state voltage regulation within ±1%. The DSE7320 or SmartGen controller triggers ATS automatic start within 3-6 seconds of grid loss — a transfer speed critical for maintaining refrigeration circuit temperature and protecting food stock from spoilage at supermarkets and cold-chain retail facilities during grid outages.
The 88KVA retail backup power unit CDW88KVA silent canopy is fabricated from 2.2mm cold-rolled steel with multi-layer anti-rust coating and 50mm flame-retardant acoustic insulation, achieving ≤72dB(A) at 7m — within the noise limits for urban commercial zones. The integrated base fuel tank supports 8 hours of continuous full prime load operation on a single fill, covering a full overnight period at unmanned retail outlets and food storage facilities without manual intervention.
The Weichai WP4 series generator CDW88KVA draws on Weichai Power's parts and service network in Africa, the Middle East, Southeast Asia, and South Asia. WP4.1 series spare parts carry approximately 32% lower procurement cost versus equivalent Perkins or Cummins units — a budget advantage for small business owners and community facility operators. Jiuan Generator provides an 18-month or 2,000-hour warranty on each CDW88KVA unit.
