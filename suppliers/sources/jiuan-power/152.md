Overview
80KW Construction Site Prime and Township Community Grid — 41% Power Reserve, ESN-Traceable, PICC-Insured
The CDW110KVA is Jiuan Generator's 110KVA Weichai diesel generator built for commercial construction site prime power, remote township community grid replacement, and small industrial park standby — applications where an 80KW continuous prime load combines with unpredictable peak demand. Driven by the Weichai WP4.1D113E200 four-cylinder turbocharged diesel rated at 113kW gross, the CDW110KVA delivers 80KW/100KVA ISO8528 PRP prime and 88KW/110KVA standby with a 41% gross-to-prime power reserve built into every operating cycle.
The Weichai WP4.1D113E200 genset engine is an inline four-cylinder turbocharged and intercooled water-cooled diesel with 4.088L displacement, 105×118mm bore and stroke, 17.5:1 compression ratio, and ECU-controlled electronic fuel injection holding specific fuel consumption at ≤205g/kWh. The 11L oil sump and 24V DC electrical start support reliable operation in tropical, dusty, and remote construction environments; the Weichai WP4.1 global spare parts network covers all major market regions in Africa, Southeast Asia, and the Middle East.
The 80KW prime power generator CDW110KVA all-copper brushless synchronous alternator carries H-class insulation with ±1% steady-state and ±15% transient voltage regulation — protecting electronic equipment on construction sites and in community buildings from instantaneous surge during load switching. The 41% gross-to-prime power reserve means the WP4.1D113E200 engine runs at comfortable thermal loading even at full prime output throughout extended daily operating cycles in hot ambient conditions.
The 110KVA construction site generator CDW110KVA canopy is fabricated from 2.5mm cold-rolled steel with 50mm flame-retardant sound insulation and seven high-damping rubber shock absorbers, achieving ≤72dB(A) at 7m — within EU residential noise limits for urban site installations. Warranty coverage is underwritten by PICC (People's Insurance Company of China), with air-freight warranty parts delivered worldwide in 3-5 days for rapid repair turnaround at active construction and community project sites.
The Weichai WP4.1 silent generator CDW110KVA undergoes full pre-shipment verification: each unit has a unique traceable ESN (Engine Serial Number) and is load-bank tested at staged steps — 0%-25%-50%-75%-full load and 110% overload — with test video and PDF load analysis report delivered with every unit. Jiuan Generator provides an 18-month or 2,000-hour warranty. The 5-language 150-page maintenance manual and 3D disassembly animation CD serve non-professional local technicians at overseas project sites.

5 Core Competitive Advantages
1. 41% Gross-to-Prime Power Reserve — 113kW Engine Absorbs Peak Load Surges at Construction Sites
The 80KW prime power generator CDW110KVA WP4.1D113E200 113kW gross engine provides 33KW of reserve capacity above the 80KW rated prime load — 41% headroom that absorbs morning AC startup surges in community compounds, welding inrush at construction sites, and short-term overload during simultaneous load switches without dropping frequency or voltage.
2. ESN Traceability + Staged Load Test — PICC-Backed Documentation for Project Procurement

4. 2.5mm Steel Canopy + 8-Hour Integrated Tank — Weatherproof Outdoor Prime for Site Operations

The integrated 8-hour fuel tank supports a full work shift without refueling, and the optional ATS starts the engine automatically when grid power fails at occupied community buildings.
5. 5-Language Manual + 3D Animation CD — Zero Language Barrier for Overseas Installation Teams
The Weichai WP4.1 silent generator CDW110KVA ships with a 150-page maintenance manual in five languages (Chinese, English, Spanish, French, Russian), electrical wiring diagrams, a 3D disassembly animation CD, and 3D rendered exploded views of all spare parts — giving overseas construction engineers and community facility technicians the documentation to install, commission, and maintain the unit without specialist support.
