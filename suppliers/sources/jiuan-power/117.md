Overview
160KW Hotel, Campus, and Civic Building ATS Standby — HPCR ≤198g/kWh, ESN-Traceable, PICC-Insured
The CDW200KVA is Jiuan Generator's 200KVA Weichai diesel generator for hotel, university campus, and civic building ATS emergency standby — applications where municipal grid provides normal supply and the generator exists to cover outage events. The Weichai WP10D200E200 six-cylinder high-pressure common rail diesel delivers 160KW/200KVA ISO8528 PRP prime and 176KW/220KVA standby, with ATS auto-start within 3-5 seconds restoring lifts, HVAC, and emergency lighting without manual intervention.
The Weichai WP10D200E200 genset engine is an inline six-cylinder turbocharged and intercooled diesel with 9.726L displacement, 126×130mm bore and stroke, 17:1 compression ratio, and high-pressure common rail injection with ECU speed control. Specific fuel consumption is ≤198g/kWh at full prime load — reducing fuel cost per standby event for hotel and campus facilities where the generator accumulates dozens of annual running hours across grid outage incidents.
The 160KW standby power generator CDW200KVA all-copper brushless synchronous alternator is H-class insulated with ±1% steady-state and ±15% transient voltage regulation, protecting hotel HVAC compressors, hospital imaging equipment, and campus server rooms from switching transients during ATS transfer. The SmartGen or DSE ATS controller detects grid failure within seconds and restores full building supply before elevator safety systems trigger an emergency halt.
The 200KVA hotel backup generator CDW200KVA canopy is fabricated from 3.0mm cold-rolled steel with 60mm flame-retardant sound insulation and eight high-damping rubber shock absorbers, achieving ≤72dB(A) at 7m — within EU residential noise limits for hotel-adjacent and urban campus installations. Each CDW200KVA carries warranty coverage underwritten by PICC (People's Insurance Company of China), with air-freight warranty parts delivered worldwide in 3-5 days for rapid resolution at active building standby installations.
The Weichai WP10 silent generator CDW200KVA undergoes full pre-shipment verification: each unit carries a unique traceable ESN (Engine Serial Number) and is load-bank tested at staged steps — 0%-25%-50%-75%-full load and 110% overload — with test video and PDF load analysis report delivered with every shipment. Jiuan Generator provides an 18-month or 2,000-hour warranty. The 5-language 150-page maintenance manual and 3D disassembly animation CD reduce installation barriers for facility staff at overseas hotel and campus sites.

4. ATS 3-5 Second Auto-Start — Hotel Lifts and HVAC Restored Before Occupants Notice the Outage
