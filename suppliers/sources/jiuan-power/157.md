Description
The 400KVA Perkins 2206C-E13TAG2 diesel generator set is a high-performance industrial power solution built for prime power and standby power applications. As a genuine Perkins generator, this 400KVA generator is powered by the world-renowned Perkins 2206C-E13TAG2 engine, a robust 6-cylinder inline diesel engine from Perkins' trusted 2200 series. Whether you need a 400KVA Perkins generator for daily continuous operation or emergency backup during blackouts, the Perkins 2206C-E13TAG2 generator delivers outstanding reliability, high efficiency, and long service life across all working conditions. It is widely trusted by data centers, hospitals, manufacturing plants, and commercial buildings globally.
At the heart of this 400KVA generator lies the Perkins 2206C-E13TAG2 engine, a 6-cylinder, inline, turbocharged and air-to-air intercooled diesel engine with a displacement of 12.5 liters. Designed for heavy-duty power generation, the 2206C-E13TAG2 runs at 1500 RPM (50 Hz) and produces a prime power of 350KVA (280kW) and a standby power of 400KVA (320kW) at 0.8 power factor. The engine adopts electronic unit injection (EUI) and G3-level speed governing, ensuring precise fuel delivery, rapid load response, and stable voltage output. Thanks to turbocharging + air-to-air intercooling, the Perkins 2206C-E13TAG2 achieves high combustion efficiency, lower fuel consumption, and reduced exhaust emissions, fully complying with EU Stage II and EPA Tier 2 emission standards. Its durable cast iron block and premium components guarantee exceptional longevity and minimal maintenance requirements.
The alternator matched to this 400KVA Perkins generator is a high-quality synchronous alternator (Stamford / Leroy-Somer) with IP23 protection grade, providing excellent dust and water resistance. It delivers stable 400V/50Hz three-phase AC power, with voltage regulation within ±1% under variable loads, protecting sensitive equipment such as servers, medical devices, and precision instruments. The intelligent control system (Deep Sea / EMCP4.4) integrates auto start-stop, ATS automatic transfer, real-time monitoring, fault alarm, and remote communication, allowing operators to monitor the 400KVA generator anytime, anywhere. Safety protections include overload, over-temperature, low oil pressure, over-speed, and short-circuit shutdown, ensuring the Perkins 2206C-E13TAG2 generator runs safely in all environments. Additionally, the soundproof canopy option reduces noise levels significantly, making it ideal for urban installations.
