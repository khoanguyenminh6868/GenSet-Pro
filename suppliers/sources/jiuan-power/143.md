Description
Internet data centres and colocation facilities operate on a simple rule — no power means no revenue. Server racks drawing continuous load, UPS arrays managing battery state of charge, and precision in-row cooling cannot tolerate a transfer gap exceeding the UPS hold-time budget. The JIUAN Power 440KVA Cummins diesel generator — Model CDC440KVA, driven by the Cummins 6ZTAA13-G2 13L engine from DCEC's ISO 9001-certified Wuhan manufacturing base — provides 320KW/400KVA prime and 352KW/440KVA standby, purpose-engineered for data centre standby duty. Every 400KVA silent Cummins genset leaves the factory ISO 8528-verified, with pre-shipment test documentation as standard.
The Cummins 6ZTAA13-G2 engine at the core of this soundproof diesel generator is manufactured at DCEC Dongfeng Cummins' ISO 9001-certified Wuhan facility — an inline six-cylinder, 13L platform with a 130mm bore, 163mm stroke, and 17:1 compression ratio. A Holset turbocharger with air-to-air intercooler and high-pressure mechanical fuel injection with electronic GAC governor hold frequency deviation to ≤3% from cold-start through 110% transient, ensuring UPS input circuits and PDU metering systems receive stable power through every load-step pickup. Rated BSFC of 192–194 g/kW·h and a 15,000-hour overhaul interval minimise per-rack fuel cost and scheduled downtime over the data centre's depreciation horizon.
The weatherproof shell of this 440KVA Cummins diesel generator is fabricated from ≥2.0mm cold-rolled steel panels, finished with a 120°C high-temperature electrostatic enamel coat, and lined with 40 kg/m³ flame-retardant rock wool — measured acoustic output at 7 metres is 65–68 dB(A), within campus noise ordinances at suburban data centre sites. A 950-litre integral skid-frame fuel tank provides over 8 hours of full-load autonomous runtime during planned grid maintenance windows or utility outages. Each 400KVA silent Cummins genset is shipped pre-fitted with a DeepSea DSE7320 controller; integrated ATS logic completes mains-to-generator transfer within 3–6 seconds — within standard UPS hold-time windows — and returns the load automatically on utility restoration.
JIUAN Power ships every CDC440KVA 440KVA Cummins diesel generator with factory-documented step-load acceptance results — six incremental duty bands from 0% through 110% — and a PDF performance certificate for Tier III audit documentation. Documentation includes 3D engineering assembly drawings, magnified anti-counterfeiting component photographs, and a PICC insurance-backed 5-year warranty policy recording the Cummins 6ZTAA13-G2 engine serial number for data centre DCIM asset registers. A 150-page generator operations manual in five language editions (English, Arabic, French, Spanish, Portuguese), electrical schematics, a 3D disassembly animation CD, and service wrenches with testing meters complete the delivery. Generator enclosure panel gauge is checked by precision digital calliper before despatch; warranty spares reach any major port within 3–5 working days, valid for the full 5-year warranty period.

FAQ
Q: How does this 440KVA Cummins diesel generator fit a Tier III data centre's N+1 backup power plan?
A: At 320KW prime, the CDC440KVA fills the single-genset slot in a Tier III N+1 configuration protecting a 0.8-power-factor rack load of up to 256KW. The DSE7320 ATS transfers within 3–6 seconds — inside the typical 10-second UPS hold-time window — and the 950-litre tank sustains over 8 hours of full-load runtime, covering planned utility maintenance windows without refuelling.
Q: What is the fuel consumption of this 400KVA silent Cummins genset at full load?
A: The Cummins 6ZTAA13-G2 engine achieves 192–194 g/kW·h BSFC at rated output — roughly 62–67 litres per hour at 320KW prime load. Over the 15,000-hour overhaul interval, this places the CDC440KVA among the most fuel-efficient options in the 400KW standby class.
