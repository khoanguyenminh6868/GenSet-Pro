285KW Commercial and Hospitality Prime Power — Weichai WP13D490E310 HPCR Engine
The CDW356KVA is Jiuan Generator's 356KVA Weichai diesel generator for large hotels, shopping malls, office complexes, hospitals, and commercial developments requiring 285KW ISO8528 PRP continuous prime power throughout operational hours. Powered by the Weichai WP13D490E310 with high-pressure common rail injection and electronic governing, the CDW356KVA delivers 285KW/356KVA prime and 314KW/392KVA standby — housed in a 3850×1720×2200mm, 3800kg silent canopy at ≤72dB(A) measured 7 metres from the enclosure.
The Weichai WP13D490E310 genset engine is an inline six-cylinder, turbocharged and intercooled diesel with 12.54L displacement, 127mm × 165mm bore and stroke, and 15:1 compression ratio. The high-pressure common rail (HPCR) electronic fuel injection system holds specific fuel consumption at ≤198g/kWh — delivering measurably lower fuel cost per prime power hour versus mechanically injected engines at the same output rating. The 36L oil sump capacity supports 500-hour planned service intervals between oil changes.
The 285KW prime power generator set CDW356KVA brushless synchronous alternator is wound with all-copper conductors, H-class insulated, and delivers stable 400V/50Hz three-phase supply with steady-state voltage regulation within ±1%. The DSE7320 or SmartGen controller executes ATS automatic start within 3-7 seconds of grid loss — a transfer speed that meets life-safety load requirements at hospitals, hotels, and commercial buildings operating in regions with frequent or extended grid outages.
The 356KVA commercial backup power unit CDW356KVA silent canopy is fabricated from 2.5mm cold-rolled steel with multi-layer anti-rust coating and 50mm acoustic insulation lining, achieving ≤72dB(A) at 7m — within noise limits for urban commercial zones in most jurisdictions. The integrated base fuel tank supports 8 hours of continuous full prime load operation on a single fill, and the canopy incorporates access panels on all sides for oil filling, filter replacement, and routine inspection without removing the housing.
The Weichai WP13 silent generator CDW356KVA draws on Weichai Power's authorized service and parts network, providing in-country procurement for engine components across Africa, the Middle East, Southeast Asia, and South Asia. Compared with equivalent Perkins or Cummins models, WP13 series spare parts carry 25-30% lower procurement cost and shorter in-country lead time — a measurable TCO advantage across a 5-10 year commercial operational lifespan. Jiuan Generator provides an 18-month or 2,000-hour warranty on each CDW356KVA unit.

5 Core Competitive Advantages
1. HPCR Fuel Injection — ≤198g/kWh for 24/7 Commercial Prime Operations
The 285KW prime power generator set CDW356KVA WP13D490E310 HPCR common-rail injection maintains ≤198g/kWh specific fuel consumption across the prime load range — reducing daily fuel cost for commercial buildings and hospitality operations where the genset runs as primary power during extended grid-unavailable periods or planned prime operation shifts.
2. 490KW Gross Engine Output — Deep Mechanical Reserve Above Prime Rating
The Weichai WP13D490E310 genset engine produces 490KW gross mechanical output at 1500RPM — significantly above the 285KW electrical prime rating. This mechanical reserve provides thermal and load headroom during continuous prime operation, reducing exhaust temperatures and bearing wear rates compared with engines running close to their rated ceiling at equivalent electrical output.
3. 10.2% Standby Headroom — Load Surge Buffer Above Prime Rating
The 356KVA Weichai diesel generator CDW356KVA 314KW/392KVA standby rating provides 10.2% power headroom above the 285KW prime. This buffer absorbs starting inrush from HVAC compressors, lift motors, and cycling commercial loads at hotels and malls without voltage sag that disrupts building management systems or trips motor protection relays.
4. ≤72dB(A) @ 7m — Urban-Rated Noise Level for Commercial Zone Installation
The 356KVA commercial backup power unit CDW356KVA ≤72dB(A) at 7m meets noise thresholds for urban commercial zones in most jurisdictions. Combined 50mm acoustic lining, secondary exhaust silencer, and anti-vibration mounts achieve this noise floor without reducing cooling airflow at full prime load — enabling installation in hotel plant rooms and commercial basement generator rooms.
5. In-Country Weichai Parts Sourcing — Lower TCO vs Imported Genset Brands
The Weichai WP13 silent generator CDW356KVA uses parts available through in-country Weichai distributors across Africa, the Middle East, Southeast Asia, and South Asia. In-country procurement cuts lead time and parts cost — a measurable TCO benefit compared with Perkins or Cummins equivalents where imported parts carry higher landed cost and longer sourcing delays.
