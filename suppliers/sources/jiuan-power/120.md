600KW Marine Terminal and Shipyard Continuous Prime Power — Cummins KTA38-G2 V12, 731kW Certified, PICC-Insured
The CDC825KVA is Jiuan Generator's 825KVA Cummins diesel generator for offshore marine terminals, large commercial shipyards, and port dry dock facilities requiring 24-hour continuous prime power. Powered by the genuine CCEC Chongqing Cummins KTA38-G2 engine, the unit delivers 600KW/750KVA ISO8528 PRP prime and 660KW/825KVA standby, with CE and ISO8528 certification for global project procurement and export compliance.
The Cummins KTA38-G2 genset is a 60° V-type 12-cylinder turbocharged and intercooled diesel with 37.8L displacement, 159×159mm bore and stroke, 14.5:1 compression ratio, Holset dual turbocharger, and PT high-pressure fuel injection with EFC electronic governing at 1500RPM. Engine certified standby output reaches 731KW — sustaining the 600KW prime rating with a 22% power reserve for ship-to-shore crane DOL motor starting surges and dry dock pump load restoration. Specific fuel consumption: 198–205g/kWh at full prime load.
The 660KW standby power generator CDC825KVA canopy is manufactured from 4mm cold-rolled steel with double-layer epoxy zinc-rich anti-rust coating for long-term outdoor coastal and marine environment placement. Multi-layer flame-retardant acoustic foam, impedance composite muffler duct, and an extra-large industrial compound silencer reduce operating noise to ≤72dB(A) at 7m — meeting EU commercial port zone noise regulations for open-quay installation adjacent to occupied dock facilities.
The 825KVA industrial generator CDC825KVA all-copper brushless H-class alternator delivers ±1% steady-state voltage regulation and THD <3%, protecting port crane inverter drives, shipyard welding rectifiers, dry dock pump control panels, and BMS precision systems from voltage disturbances. The DeepSea DSE7320 controller provides auto-start within 3-5 seconds of grid failure with reserved ATS installation space for seamless switchover between shore power and genset power.
The Cummins KTA38 heavy duty genset CDC825KVA carries a factory-registered ESN (Engine Serial Number) and is load-bank tested at 0%-25%-50%-75%-full prime load and 110% overload before shipment, with test video and PDF load report shipped with every unit. Warranty is underwritten by PICC (People's Insurance Company of China), with air-freight core parts in 3-5 days via the worldwide official Cummins after-sales service network.
