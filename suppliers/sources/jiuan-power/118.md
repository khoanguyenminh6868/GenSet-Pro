280KW ATS Emergency Standby for Office Towers and Commercial Complexes — Weichai WP10D320E200, CE+ISO8528, PICC-Insured
The CDW350KVA is Jiuan Generator's 350KVA Weichai diesel generator for grade-A office towers, large commercial complexes, and civic buildings requiring ATS emergency standby with strict urban noise compliance. The Weichai WP10D320E200 delivers 250KW/312KVA ISO8528 PRP prime and 280KW/350KVA standby, with CE and ISO8528 certification covering Africa, the Middle East, and Southeast Asian commercial property markets.
The Weichai WP10D320E200 genset is a six-cylinder turbocharged and intercooled inline diesel with 9.726L displacement, 126×130mm bore and stroke, 17:1 compression ratio, and HPCR injection with ECU governing. Engine gross output is 320kW at 1500RPM — providing a 28% power reserve above the 250KW prime rating to handle elevator DOL motor starting, HVAC compressor surges, and full-building load restoration events. Specific fuel consumption is ≤197g/kWh at full prime load.
The 280KW standby power generator CDW350KVA canopy is built from 3mm cold-rolled steel with four-layer anti-corrosion treatment — acid descaling, zinc-phosphate conversion, cathodic electrophoresis, and electrostatic powder coating. The 60mm flame-retardant acoustic foam and eight rubber shock absorbers reduce operating noise to ≤72dB(A) at 7m, enabling rooftop and basement plant room installation within CBD commercial zones and residential-adjacent business parks.
The 350KVA office generator CDW350KVA all-copper brushless H-class alternator delivers ±1% steady-state voltage regulation and ±15% transient protection, guarding building BMS systems, UPS arrays, and precision HVAC drives from voltage disturbances during load switching. The SmartGen or DSE ATS controller provides auto-start within 3-6 seconds of grid failure, with automatic cooling-run shutdown after grid restoration supporting unattended commercial building power management.
The Weichai WP10 silent generator CDW350KVA carries a factory-registered ESN (Engine Serial Number) and is load-bank tested at 0%-25%-50%-75%-full prime load and 110% overload before shipment, with test video and PDF load report shipped with every unit. Warranty is underwritten by PICC (People's Insurance Company of China), with air-freight parts worldwide in 3-5 days. Jiuan Generator's 5-language 150-page maintenance manual, wiring diagrams, and 3D animation CD support overseas commercial building commissioning teams.
