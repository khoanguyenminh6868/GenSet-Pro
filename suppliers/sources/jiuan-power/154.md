Description
Selecting a heavy-duty power generation asset for large industrial or critical-facility deployment involves more than matching nameplate kilowatts to building load schedules. The CDS440KVA 400KVA SDEC diesel generator from JIUAN Power is factory-engineered to address both peak standby capacity and continuous prime-duty demands in a single certificated unit, rated at 290KW/363KVA prime power and 320KW/400KVA standby power. Verified under ISO8528 prime power and emergency standby test protocols, this unit delivers output at 400/230V, 50Hz, 0.8 lagging power factor.
Facility engineers sourcing hospital backup power for critical-care campuses or selecting standby generation for IDC data centres typically face a trade-off between fast mains-failure response and clean output waveform. The CDS440KVA meets both requirements: the SDEC SC12E480D2 genset engine starts within 3–6 seconds of mains fault detection via the DSE7320 controller's automated transfer logic, while the Class H alternator's ≤±1.0% voltage regulation and THD below 2.8% protect downstream medical diagnostic and server infrastructure from surge or waveform distortion.
Industrial park developers and EPC contractors sourcing prime-duty units for long-running captive power plants choose the CDS440KVA for its 363KVA ISO8528 prime power rating because the SDEC SC12E480D2 genset logs 192–202g/kW·h specific fuel consumption across 80–100% continuous loading — a figure that directly governs weekly diesel procurement costs. JIUAN Power's graded load aging test protocol certifies every unit from 25% to 110% load before factory gate inspection, providing buyers with a documented performance baseline rather than relying on nameplate figures alone.
The factory-integrated enclosed noise reduction canopy transforms the CDS440KVA into a self-contained installation package: 3.5mm hot-rolled steel outer shell with triple-layer anti-corrosion coating, 850L base fuel tank for over 12 continuous full-load operating hours, and high-temperature closed-circuit cooling. JIUAN Power delivers every 400KVA diesel generator fully assembled and function-tested; on-site commissioning is limited to the diesel supply connection and main power cable, removing the cost and lead time of a separate reinforced generator room.

Five Core Product Advantages
① SDEC SC12E480D2 Six-Cylinder Engine — 11.8L Displacement, Fractured-Forged Crankshaft, B10 Over 15,000 Hours
The SDEC SC12E480D2 genset drives an in-line six-cylinder four-stroke water-cooled architecture: bore×stroke 128×153mm, 11.8L displacement, 17:1 compression ratio, turbocharger plus air-to-air intercooler delivering enhanced combustion efficiency at altitude and in high-ambient-temperature environments. High-pressure direct injection with an electronic governor maintains stable frequency output under rapid load fluctuation. A fractured-forged crankshaft and roller-follower single-overhead-camshaft valve gear reduce mechanical vibration at rated 1500RPM compared with pushrod-equipped engines in this displacement class. Wet replaceable cylinder liners and a Fleetguard triple-filter service kit with 500-hour maintenance intervals support a B10 service life exceeding 15,000 operating hours. The optional dual-circuit water jacket and engine oil preheating kit extends reliable cold-start from the standard -15°C rating to -30°C for Arctic-zone mining and high-latitude construction projects.
② Dual PRP/ESP Rating — Continuous Prime Power and Emergency Standby Duty in One Unit
JIUAN Power certifies the CDS440KVA under two internationally defined operating modes. The 363KVA ISO8528 prime power (PRP) rating sustains continuous full-load operation as a permanent on-site captive power plant for industrial facilities running two or three production shifts per day. The 400KVA standby power (ESP) rating delivers certified hospital backup power and emergency industrial standby with a 10% short-time overload margin to absorb motor-start inrush currents from centrifugal chillers, mine jaw crushers, and large industrial air compressors. Factory-calibrated 60Hz/440V export variants are available for North American, Middle Eastern, and African grid requirements.
③ ≤72dB(A) Enclosed Noise Reduction Canopy for Coastal and Urban Site Deployment
The enclosed noise reduction canopy on this 400KVA SDEC diesel generator is fabricated from 3.5mm hot-rolled steel with triple-layer epoxy anti-rust coating for all-weather outdoor placement in coastal salt-fog ports, tropical monsoon zones, and desert installations. Multi-grade high-density flame-retardant acoustic panels and a multi-stage impedance composite muffler duct reduce broadband noise to ≤72dB(A) at 7 metres. Twin full-open maintenance doors and a tempered glass observation window enable complete engine and alternator servicing without removing any canopy section; the 850L base fuel tank with external dual quick-fill ports permits daily fuelling without entering the cabinet.
④ All-Copper Class H Alternator — Voltage Regulation ≤±1.0%, THD <2.8%
The single-bearing vacuum-impregnated Class H full-copper brushless synchronous alternator on the CDS440KVA delivers voltage regulation ≤±1.0% and total harmonic distortion below 2.8% under rated operating conditions — meeting the power quality thresholds of Grade-A hospital MRI systems, IDC server room PDUs, and automated production line PLC controllers with tight power-quality input tolerances. IP23 dust and moisture protection maintains stable output in coastal high-humidity environments, underground plant rooms, and high-temperature mining deployments. An integrated moulded-case circuit breaker covers overload, short-circuit, under-voltage, overspeed, and overtemperature protection.
⑤ Deepsea DSE7320 Controller with Built-in ATS Bay and Optional 4G Cloud Monitoring
JIUAN Power installs the Deepsea DSE7320 bilingual LCD controller on every 400KVA standby power unit, providing real-time monitoring of output power, lube oil pressure, coolant temperature, cumulative runtime, and fault codes. Automated start on mains failure, delayed cool-down shutdown on supply restoration, and fault alarm stop are built-in standard functions. The control cabinet includes a dedicated ATS installation bay supporting direct integration for millisecond-range mains-to-genset transfer switching. An optional 4G cloud monitoring module connects the SDEC SC12E480D2 genset to remote management via mobile app or PC, enabling 24-hour unattended power house supervision and remote fault diagnosis.

FAQ
Q: What graded load test does JIUAN Power perform on each CDS440KVA before shipment?
A: JIUAN Power, as a specialist industrial genset supplier, subjects every 400KVA diesel generator to a factory aging test at 25%, 50%, 75%, 100%, and 110% of rated standby load, with voltage output, frequency, coolant temperature, oil pressure, and exhaust data logged at each stage. This validates the SDEC SC12E480D2 genset engine and alternator interaction under thermal conditions representative of real operating sites. Graded load test documentation ships with each unit alongside CE, ISO8528, and SGS certificates.
Q: How does the enclosed noise reduction canopy perform in coastal salt-fog environments?
A: The canopy shell carries triple-layer epoxy zinc-rich primer and a corrosion-resistant outer topcoat rated for sustained salt-fog and high-humidity exposure. JIUAN Power has shipped this 400KVA diesel generator to port logistics operations, coastal industrial zones, and island facility projects across Southeast Asia, the Middle East, and West Africa. Full coating specification documentation is available from JIUAN Power's export team on request.
