Overview
Air-to-Air Aftercooling: Thermal Margin for 24/7 Commercial Prime Power
The CDP200KVA is Jiuan Generator's 200KVA Perkins diesel generator designed for commercial properties — hotels, resorts, retail centers, mid-sized manufacturing plants, and office complexes — that require continuous prime power at 144KW during business operations and reliable standby backup when the grid fails. The Perkins 1106A-70TAG3 engine's air-to-air aftercooler cools the turbocharged charge air before it enters the combustion chamber, reducing charge air temperature from approximately 150°C to 50°C — increasing charge density, improving combustion efficiency, and reducing thermal loading on pistons, cylinder head, and exhaust valves compared with non-aftercooled turbocharged configurations. The result is a 180KVA prime power generator rating at 144KW that the TAG3 engine sustains continuously without power derating, at a 500-hour planned service interval.
The Perkins 1106A-70TAG3 genset engine is a six-cylinder, inline, four-stroke, water-cooled unit with 7.01L displacement, 105mm × 135mm bore and stroke, and a 16:1 compression ratio. Running at 1500 RPM (50Hz), it produces 144KW/180KVA prime power for continuous commercial use and 160KW/200KVA standby power for emergency backup. The TAG3 configuration combines a single turbocharger to compress intake air for increased charge density with a downstream air-to-air aftercooler that dissipates the heat of compression before the charge reaches the intake manifold — the thermal margin that allows sustained full-load prime operation in ambient conditions up to 40°C. Cold-start capability is rated to -10°C unassisted. The engine complies with EU Stage II emission standards, supporting commercial project import, planning authority submissions, and environmental compliance documentation in export markets. Fuel consumption: 41.6 L/h at 100% prime load, 31.8 L/h at 75% prime load, 20.1 L/h at 50% prime load.
ISO8528 certification covers two operating classifications for the CDP200KVA. The PRP classification (144KW/180KVA) certifies the unit as a 180KVA prime power generator for unlimited continuous operation as a facility's primary power source, provided the average load factor remains below 70% over each 24-hour period — the classification that applies to hotels and commercial buildings using the CDP200KVA as main supply during grid-unavailability or in off-grid locations. The ESP classification (160KW/200KVA) certifies the unit as a 200KVA standby generator set for grid-failure emergency backup, with a short-duration overload margin within any 12-hour standby window for inrush current at startup. CE certification documentation covering ISO8528, alternator insulation class, and EU Stage II engine compliance is provided with each unit for international commercial procurement.
Three enclosure types meet all commercial installation needs. The open-frame design fits industrial and commercial generator rooms prioritizing easy maintenance and heat dissipation. The super silent canopy limits noise to ≤75dB(A) at 7 meters via double-layer acoustic insulation and a heavy-duty muffler, complying with urban noise standards and hotel construction acoustic limits. The 20ft weatherproof containerized version with IP54-grade protection suits retail, temporary and uncompleted building projects. Its 400L integrated tank enables 8–9 hours of full-load continuous operation, supporting a full day’s commercial work with one refuel.
The brushless synchronous alternator uses H-class insulation with IP23 base protection (IP55 optional), vacuum-impregnated full-copper windings, and a digital AVR holding steady-state voltage within ±1% and THD below 5% — the power quality specification required by medical-grade HVAC systems, commercial electronic POS terminals, server infrastructure, and kitchen equipment. For the Perkins 1100 TAG3 generator , Jiuan Generator installs a Deep Sea DSE6120 or SmartGen HGM6120 controller, providing automatic mains failure (AMF) detection, auto start-stop, ATS transfer in under 10 seconds, and real-time monitoring of phase voltages, currents, frequency, power factor, oil pressure, coolant temperature, and accumulated run hours. RS485 communication is standard; optional 4G remote monitoring enables status access from any networked location. Fault protections include high coolant temperature, low oil pressure, overspeed, undervoltage, short-circuit, and overload shutdowns.

5 Core Competitive Advantages
1. Air-to-Air Aftercooling — Thermal Margin at Full Prime Load for 24/7 Commercial Operations
The "TAG3" designation confirms air-to-air aftercooled induction on the Perkins 1106A-70TAG3 genset : the aftercooler dissipates turbocharger compression heat before the charge enters the cylinders, lowering charge air temperature by approximately 100°C compared with non-aftercooled turbocharged configurations. Cooler combustion temperatures reduce thermal stress on the cylinder head, piston crown, and exhaust valves — extending TBO (time between overhaul) for generators operating continuous prime duty at commercial facilities where 8–16 hours of daily running is standard. This thermal reserve is the engineering reason the TAG3 achieves 144KW from 7.01L versus the 108KW limit of the non-aftercooled 1106A-70TG1 from the same displacement.
2. EU Stage II Emission Certification — International Commercial Project Compliance
The Perkins 1106A-70TAG3 engine meets EU Stage II non-road mobile machinery emission limits for particulate matter and nitrogen oxides, supporting import certification, environmental impact assessment submissions, and local authority planning documentation for the 200KVA Perkins diesel generator in commercial property developments across the EU, Africa, Southeast Asia, and the Middle East. Jiuan Generator supplies the relevant engine emission certificates with each unit for inclusion in project compliance packages.
3. 144KW ISO8528 PRP — Full Prime Duty Without Hour-Limited Derating
ISO8528 PRP (144KW/180KVA) certifies the 180KVA prime power generator for unlimited continuous operation at the full prime rating — unlike ESP ratings, which carry 12-hour operational limits. For hotels, commercial centers, and manufacturing plants using the CDP200KVA as their main supply during extended outages or in off-grid locations, PRP certification confirms that the 144KW output is sustainable continuously without a derating schedule or runtime cap.
5. DSE6120 AMF Auto-Start — Seamless Transfer for Critical Commercial Loads
The Deep Sea DSE6120 controller's automatic mains failure (AMF) function detects grid undervoltage or failure and automatically starts the 200KVA standby generator set , completing power transfer in under 10 seconds — the transfer time required by most commercial UPS bypass and critical load protection sequences for hotel CCTV, electronic access control, POS systems, refrigeration, and IT rooms. The DSE6120 also monitors individual phase voltages and currents, enabling load imbalance detection across three-phase distribution boards in multi-tenanted commercial buildings.

Office towers and commercial centers — standby power for common area systems, lifts, building management systems, and IT infrastructure rooms
Medium-scale manufacturing and light industrial plants — prime or standby power for production lines, air compressors, and material handling equipment
Healthcare and educational facilities — standby power with AMF auto-start for life-safety, critical care, and laboratory systems
Cold-chain and food processing facilities — prime or standby power for refrigeration lines and controlled-atmosphere storage
Telecom towers and remote data relay stations — prime power in off-grid locations or standby in grid-connected zones with high uptime requirements
