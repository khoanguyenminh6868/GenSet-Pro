Description
The 250KVA Perkins generator (200kW) Prime / 275KVA (220kW) Standby Perkins 1206A-E70TTAG3 super silent generator diesel generator set is a high-power, ultra-quiet industrial power solution designed for noise-sensitive environments such as urban commercial areas, hospitals, hotels, residential complexes, schools, and data centers. As a genuine Perkins super silent generator, this 250KVA Perkins generator is powered by the Perkins 1206A-E70TTAG3 engine—a 6-cylinder, inline, turbocharged, air-to-air aftercooled diesel from the advanced Perkins 1200 series, delivering exceptional power output, superior fuel efficiency, and industry-leading low-noise operation. Rated at 250KVA (200kW) prime power (0.8 power factor) and 275KVA (220kW) standby power, this Perkins 1206A-E70TTAG3 super silent generator diesel generator set provides stable, continuous power for both daily prime use and emergency backup, while maintaining ultra-low noise levels that comply with strict urban environmental regulations. Whether deployed as a primary power source for off-grid commercial facilities or a critical backup power supply for grid-connected noise-sensitive sites, this heavy-duty 250KVA Perkins generator super silent generator ensures reliable performance, minimal noise pollution, and long-term cost-effective operation as a premier industrial power solution in the most demanding acoustic and industrial environments.
At the core of this 250KVA Perkins generator super silent generator lies the high-performance Perkins 1206A-E70TTAG3 engine, a 6-cylinder, vertical inline, 4-stroke, water-cooled diesel engine with a displacement of 7.01 liters. Engineered for high power density and quiet operation, the Perkins 1206A-E70TTAG3 operates at 1500 RPM (50Hz) and delivers 200kW (250KVA) prime power for continuous use and 220kW (275KVA) standby power for emergency peak loads. The engine features series turbocharging with air-to-air aftercooling, electronic common-rail fuel injection, and an ECM electronic governor, ensuring precise fuel control, rapid load response, and smooth, low-vibration power delivery. With a 15.8:1 compression ratio, this Perkins 1206A-E70TTAG3 engine provides excellent cold-start capability down to -12°C and complies with EU Stage II emission standards, making the entire diesel generator set environmentally friendly and globally compliant. Fuel efficiency for this industrial power solution is outstanding: 56.9 L/h at 100% prime load, 41.5 L/h at 75% load, and 28.1 L/h at 50% load, significantly reducing long-term operational costs.
The defining feature of this 250KVA Perkins generator is its super silent generator canopy system, engineered with 7-layer noise reduction technology to achieve ultra-low noise levels of 62–65 dB(A)@7m—far quieter than standard silent generators and fully compliant with ISO 3744 and CE noise regulations. The canopy is constructed with a 1.8mm thick galvanized steel outer layer, a 2mm damping steel inner layer, and 50mm high-density nano-porous sound-absorbing cotton filling the entire interior, effectively blocking and absorbing mechanical, combustion, and airflow noise. The labyrinth-style air intake and exhaust channels are equipped with high-efficiency silencers, reducing airflow noise by 12–15 dB while maintaining optimal ventilation for engine cooling. A residential-grade heavy-duty muffler with a rain cap is integrated into the exhaust system, further minimizing exhaust noise and protecting the diesel generator set against harsh weather. The entire unit is mounted on a reinforced steel base frame with heavy-duty rubber shock absorbers, reducing vibration transmission by 95% and eliminating structure-borne noise to establish a true low-noise industrial power solution.
The alternator paired with this 250KVA Perkins generator super silent generator is a premium brushless synchronous alternator from Stamford or Leroy-Somer, featuring IP23 protection grade and H-class insulation. It delivers stable 400V/50Hz three-phase AC power, with voltage regulation within ±1% and THD <5%, supplying clean, distortion-free electricity for sensitive equipment such as medical devices, IT servers, precision electronics, and HVAC systems. The intelligent control system adopts a Deep Sea DSE7320 or SmartGen HGM6120 controller, integrating auto start-stop, ATS transfer, real-time monitoring, fault diagnosis, and remote communication. Comprehensive safety protections include overload, over-temperature, low oil pressure, over-speed, short-circuit, and under-voltage shutdown, fully safeguarding the Perkins 1206A-E70TTAG3 diesel generator set and critical loads.
