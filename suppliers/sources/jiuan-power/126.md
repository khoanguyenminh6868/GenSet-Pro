Overview
Three-Cylinder Compact Power for Small Business and Remote Community Applications
The CDP50KVA is Jiuan Generator's 50KVA Perkins diesel generator designed for space-constrained small business installations, rural community facilities, and remote off-grid sites where a large six-cylinder unit would exceed both the power requirement and the available installation footprint. At 2250mm × 1000mm in plan and 1200kg assembled weight, the CDP50KVA passes through a standard commercial doorway and sits within the structural load capacity of most ground-floor concrete slabs — practical for retrofitting backup power into existing buildings where dedicated generator rooms were not part of the original construction. ISO8528 PRP rated at 36KW/45KVA for continuous prime use; ESP rated at 40KW/50KVA for standby backup.
The 1103A-33TG1 diesel genset engine is a three-cylinder, four-stroke, water-cooled unit with 3.3L displacement, 105mm × 127mm bore and stroke, and a 17.25:1 compression ratio. Running at 1500 RPM (50Hz), it produces 36KW/45KVA prime power for continuous operation and 40KW/50KVA standby power for emergency grid-failure backup. The TG1 designation confirms turbocharged aspiration with a mechanical governor — direct, load-responsive speed regulation without reliance on electronic control units or wiring harnesses that can be compromised by dust, moisture, or vibration in remote field conditions. The 17.25:1 compression ratio supports reliable cold starts at -20°C without electrical preheating, covering cold-climate remote sites across Eastern Europe, Central Asia, and high-altitude locations where winter temperatures routinely drop below -15°C.
The Perkins 1103A-33TG1 engine holds both EPA Tier 2 (North American) and EU Stage II (international) emission certifications — dual-market compliance at the 36KW power class that supports the 45KVA prime power generator across US, Canadian, European, African, and Southeast Asian markets from a single product configuration. For project engineers specifying generator sets for international contractor fleets or multi-country infrastructure programmes, dual EPA/EU certification avoids the complexity of maintaining region-specific SKU variants. CE documentation covering ISO8528 ratings and emission compliance is supplied with each unit for project submission packages.
Three enclosure configurations are available for the CDP50KVA 50KVA standby power unit . The open-frame format suits purpose-built generator rooms in small commercial buildings where full maintenance access is the priority. The super silent canopy uses dual-layer sound-absorbing panels and an oversized industrial exhaust muffler, reducing radiated noise to ≤75dB(A) at 7 meters for retail, hospitality, and urban residential-adjacent installations where local noise limits apply. The containerized option seals the unit against dust, rainfall, and corrosion for construction site, agricultural, and field deployment without a fixed generator room. The integrated base fuel tank supports 8–10 continuous hours at full prime load — enough to cover a full business day, an overnight shift, or a weekend period without on-site refuelling attendance.
The brushless synchronous alternator uses H-class insulation with IP23 protection (IP55 optional), vacuum-impregnated full-copper windings, and a digital AVR holding steady-state voltage within ±1% and THD below 5% — the power quality standard for protecting electronic POS terminals, CCTV recording equipment, office networking hardware, and light process control panels from voltage fluctuations. For the Perkins 1103 series generator , Jiuan Generator installs a Deep Sea or SmartGen controller with AMF auto-start, ATS transfer switching, and real-time monitoring of voltage, current, frequency, oil pressure, coolant temperature, and run hours, plus fault shutdown protection for overload, low oil pressure, high coolant temperature, and overspeed conditions. An optional 4G remote monitoring module enables start-stop control and status access from mobile devices — useful for unattended rural and rooftop installations.

5 Core Competitive Advantages
1. Three-Cylinder Compact Footprint — Retrofit Installation in Space-Constrained Buildings
At 2250mm × 1000mm and 1200kg, the CDP50KVA 50KVA Perkins diesel generator passes through a standard commercial doorway, fits within the load capacity of most existing ground-floor slabs, and can be positioned in a domestic garage or small plant room without structural reinforcement — a practical advantage for retrofitting backup power into existing retail units, guesthouses, and community buildings where space and structural margins are limited.
2. -20°C Unassisted Cold Start — Remote and Cold-Climate Site Reliability
The 17.25:1 compression ratio of the 1103A-33TG1 diesel genset supports reliable cold starts at -20°C without intake preheating or starting aids — covering the full range of winter ambient conditions across northern-latitude remote sites, high-altitude rural locations, and cold-storage facilities where generator non-start in cold weather is an unacceptable operational risk and electrical preheating systems add complexity and failure points.
3. EPA Tier 2 + EU Stage II Dual Certification — One Unit for Multi-Market Projects
The Perkins 1103A-33TG1 holds both EPA Tier 2 and EU Stage II emission certifications at the 36KW power class — dual-market compliance that enables the 45KVA prime power generator CDP50KVA to serve US, Canadian, European, African, and Southeast Asian project destinations from a single product configuration, removing the need for region-specific inventory variants in international contractor fleets and multi-country infrastructure programmes.
4. Mechanical Governor + Direct Injection — Field-Serviceable Without Specialist Diagnostic Tools
The TG1 mechanical governor and direct-injection fuel system operate without an ECU, proprietary diagnostic software, or OBD interface. Any qualified diesel mechanic with standard hand tools can adjust governor speed droop, inspect injector spray patterns, and complete fuel system maintenance on the 50KVA standby power unit CDP50KVA in field conditions — reducing service dependency on manufacturer representatives in remote locations where specialist access is limited or expensive.
5. 8–10 Hours Fuel Autonomy — Full Business Day on a Single Tank
The integrated base fuel tank covers 8–10 continuous hours at full prime load — a standard business operating day, a full overnight shift, or a weekend period without on-site refuelling. For the Perkins 1103 series generator installed at unattended rural relay stations, small retail units, and off-grid community facilities, this autonomy directly reduces the cost and logistical burden of daily fuel attendance while maintaining continuous power coverage during extended grid outages.
