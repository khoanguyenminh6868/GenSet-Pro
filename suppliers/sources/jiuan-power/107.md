Description
The Perkins 2506C-E15TAG2 generator installed on this 500kVA diesel generator is an inline 6-cylinder diesel engine built with turbocharging and air-to-air intercooling technology. This advanced structure greatly enhances air intake efficiency, fully burns diesel fuel and maximizes the power output of the 500kVA power generator. Running at a rated speed of 1500 RPM, this robust 500kVA diesel generator achieves a prime power of 500kVA (400kW) and a standby power of 550kVA (440kW) at a power factor of 0.8, fully meeting the power demand of large-scale equipment and complete facilities. Equipped with an electronic unit injector fuel system, the Perkins 2506C-E15TAG2 generator realizes precise fuel delivery and excellent speed regulation performance up to G3 level. This design not only ensures the 500kVA power generator runs steadily with low vibration and low noise, but also effectively cuts down fuel consumption, making this Perkins 3-phase generator more cost-effective in long-term operation. In terms of emission performance, the 500kVA diesel generator powered by the Perkins 2506C-E15TAG2 generator engine complies with EU Stage II and EPA Tier 2 emission standards, so it can be legally used in most regions with strict environmental requirements without pollution concerns.
When it comes to the alternator part of this 500kVA power generator, we select top-brand synchronous alternators such as Stamford and Leroy-Somer, which are perfectly matched with the Perkins 2506C-E15TAG2 generator engine. The alternator features IP23 protection grade, strong dustproof and waterproof capacity, and can output a stable 400V, 50Hz AC supply from this Perkins 3-phase generator. The voltage output of this 500kVA diesel generator stays consistent even under frequent load changes, which can well protect sensitive electrical equipment like servers, medical devices and precision industrial instruments. The intelligent control system is another highlight of this Perkins 3-phase generator. It is fitted with a Deep Sea or EMCP4.4 controller, supporting automatic start and stop, ATS automatic switching, real-time data monitoring, fault alarm and remote monitoring functions. Operators can check the running status of the 500kVA power generator anytime and anywhere, and the system will trigger an emergency shutdown once abnormal water temperature, low oil pressure, overload and other faults occur, providing all-round safety protection for the Perkins 2506C-E15TAG2 generator and the whole power system.
