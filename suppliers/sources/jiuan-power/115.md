Overview
≤68dB Super Silent — Indoor Data Centre and CBD Hotel Grade, 172KW Prime, WP10D238E200 Triple-Layer Canopy
The CDW215KVA is Jiuan Generator's 215KVA Weichai diesel generator for noise-critical applications — indoor data centres, CBD luxury hotels, private hospitals, and urban high-density residential buildings where standard ≤72dB silent generators exceed local environmental noise ordinances. The Weichai WP10D238E200 six-cylinder HPCR engine delivers 172KW/215KVA ISO8528 PRP prime and 194KW/242KVA standby; the triple-layer super silent canopy holds running noise to ≤68dB(A) at 7m — 4dB below standard-series silent performance.
The Weichai WP10D238E200 genset engine is a six-cylinder turbocharged and intercooled inline diesel with 9.726L displacement, 126×130mm bore and stroke, 17:1 compression ratio, and high-pressure common rail injection with ECU control. Engine gross output is 238kW — 38% above the 172KW prime rating — keeping the engine at 72% of rated capacity under continuous prime load. This derating margin lowers combustion pressure and exhaust turbulence, directly contributing to the ≤68dB(A) noise figure alongside the super silent canopy. Specific fuel consumption is ≤195g/kWh at full prime load.
The 172KW super silent generator CDW215KVA canopy is fabricated from 3.2mm cold-rolled steel with four-layer corrosion treatment — pickling, phosphating, electrophoresis, and high-temperature powder coating. Interior sound attenuation uses a triple-layer composite: 60mm flame-retardant cotton, 30mm rubber damping sheet, and a perforated inner absorption panel, combined with a tertiary exhaust silencer and nine rubber shock isolators — achieving ≤68dB(A) at 7m to satisfy EU residential and data centre plant room noise limits.
The 215KVA ultra silent genset CDW215KVA all-copper brushless synchronous alternator is H-class insulated with ±1% steady-state and ±15% transient voltage regulation, protecting server UPS inputs, MRI scanners, and building automation systems from switching transients during ATS transfer. The SmartGen or DSE ATS controller detects grid failure and restores supply within 3-5 seconds — reconnecting data hall cooling, hotel lifts, and hospital critical loads before battery reserves reach cutoff thresholds.
The Weichai WP10 data center generator CDW215KVA carries a factory-registered ESN (Engine Serial Number) and is load-bank tested at 0%-25%-50%-75%-full prime load and 110% overload before shipment, with test video and PDF load report delivered with every unit. Warranty is underwritten by PICC (People's Insurance Company of China), with air-freight parts worldwide in 3-5 days. The 5-language 150-page maintenance manual, wiring diagrams, and 3D disassembly animation CD support non-professional facility teams at overseas data centre and hotel sites.
