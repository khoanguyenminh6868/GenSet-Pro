Description
The 1000KVA Cummins KTA38-G5 diesel generator set is a high-power, industrial-grade power supply system. This robust 1000KVA industrial diesel generator is equipped with an original Chongqing Cummins KTA38-G5 dedicated generator engine, matched with a high-precision brushless synchronous alternator and a fully automatic intelligent control system.
Engineered as a heavy-duty 1000KVA/1100KVA Cummins generator set, this unit features a prime power of 1000KVA (800KW) and a standby peak power of 1100KVA (880KW). It is specially designed for high-power, high-stability, and uninterrupted power supply scenarios, making it an ideal heavy duty power generator for large-scale industrial production, infrastructure construction, commercial complexes, mines, oil fields, data centers, and municipal engineering.
As a reliable high power main power system and responsive emergency standby power supply, this 1000KVA Cummins KTA38-G5 diesel generator set possesses the core advantages of continuous full-load operation capability, strong environmental adaptability, and an ultra-low failure rate. This premium 1000KVA industrial diesel generator perfectly solves various power supply problems such as unstable municipal power, sudden power outages, or the complete absence of grid power in field areas, ensuring 24-hour stable operation of your core equipment.

Core Product Advantages
Original Cummins Power, Stable and Powerful Performance
The unit adopts authentic Chongqing Cummins KTA38-G5 engine with 12-cylinder V-type structure and 37.8L large displacement. Equipped with mature turbocharging and air-to-air intercooling technology, it delivers strong and stable continuous power output, completely avoiding power drop and frequency loss under high-power load. The exclusive Cummins PT fuel injection system ensures accurate and uniform fuel supply and high combustion efficiency. With a standard rated speed of 1500RPM, it fully matches the national 50Hz power distribution standard. Tested by millions of working conditions, the KTA38-G5 engine features durable parts, low power loss and no power attenuation after long-term full-load operation, making it a highly reputable power core in the 1000KVA power range.
Dual Power Mode, Full Coverage of Working Scenarios
The unit is equipped with dual power modes: 1000KVA (800KW) continuous prime power and 1100KVA (880KW) peak standby power. The prime power supports 24-hour all-weather continuous full-load stable operation, meeting long-term power demand for factory production and mining operations. The standby power can start quickly in case of sudden municipal power failure and instantaneous load surge, realizing short-term overload output to cope with peak equipment startup power and sudden power increment, effectively avoiding production stagnation and equipment damage caused by power outage. The rated 400/230V three-phase four-wire output is compatible with global universal power distribution standards.
High Efficiency and Low Consumption, Lower Operating Costs
Adopting optimized fuel injection and air intake technology, the Cummins KTA38-G5 engine achieves sufficient fuel combustion and far lower fuel consumption than competing units in the same power range. The unit is fitted with a large-capacity customized fuel tank, supporting long-term continuous operation after a single refueling and reducing labor costs and downtime caused by frequent refueling. Meanwhile, the triple precision filtration system for air, fuel and engine oil filters impurities effectively, reduces wear of core components, extends the replacement cycle of vulnerable parts, and greatly reduces long-term operation and maintenance costs.
High-Quality Power Generation Core, Pure and Safe Power Supply
Matched with industrial-grade brushless self-excited synchronous alternator, the unit adopts Class H super-high insulation and IP23 professional protection grade, which is dust-proof, moisture-proof and anti-corrosive, adapting to harsh outdoor working conditions such as mines and oil fields. The brushless structure eliminates carbon brush wear problems, with low operating noise, small vibration and ultra-low failure rate, realizing maintenance-free operation for a long time. The unit’s voltage steady-state regulation rate is ≤±1% with tiny frequency fluctuation and pure output waveform, which can effectively protect precision instruments, industrial control equipment and large motors. Multiple intelligent protection mechanisms including overload, short circuit, under-voltage and overspeed protection ensure safe and stable operation of the unit.
Intelligent Automatic Control, Unattended Operation
Equipped with famous Deep Sea and Smartgen intelligent LCD controllers with full Chinese and English visual operation interface, the unit can real-time monitor core data such as voltage, current, frequency, water temperature, oil temperature, oil pressure, speed and running time. It supports full-automatic start-stop, automatic switching during power failure, automatic shutdown after power recovery, automatic fault alarm and fault code memory, realizing 24-hour unattended operation. Optional ATS dual-power automatic switching device and remote monitoring module support remote start-stop, data viewing and fault early warning, meeting the needs of modern unattended power supply scenarios.
Durable Structure, Strong Environmental Adaptability
The unit is welded with thickened high-strength integral steel plate base and integrated shock absorption structure, which effectively offsets vibration generated by high-power operation and keeps the fuselage stable. Multiple customized models including open frame, silent type, container type and trailer type are available. The silent version has low operating noise, meeting urban environmental protection noise reduction standards. The unit can operate stably in complex and harsh working conditions such as high and low temperature from -25℃ to +50℃, high altitude, dust and humidity, with excellent weather resistance and long service life.
