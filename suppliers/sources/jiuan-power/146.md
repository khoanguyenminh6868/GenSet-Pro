Overview
320KW Food Processing Park and Cold-Chain Logistics Prime Power — Cummins 6ZTAA13-G2, 192g/kWh, PICC-Insured
The CDC450KVA is Jiuan Generator's 450KVA Cummins diesel generator for large food processing industrial parks, cold-chain logistics hubs, and regional distribution centers requiring 24-hour continuous prime power. Powered by the genuine DCEC Dongfeng Cummins 6ZTAA13-G2 engine, the unit delivers 320KW/400KVA ISO8528 PRP prime and 360KW/450KVA standby, with CE and ISO8528 certification for global food industry procurement and export compliance.
The Cummins 6ZTAA13-G2 genset is an inline six-cylinder turbocharged and intercooled diesel with 13L displacement, 130×163mm bore and stroke, 17:1 compression ratio, Holset turbocharger, and PD high-pressure mechanical fuel pump with GAC electronic governing at 1500RPM. Engine certified standby output reaches 365KW. With a full-load specific fuel consumption of 192-194g/kWh, the 6ZTAA13-G2 achieves class-leading fuel economy for 24-hour food plant continuous prime duty cycles.
The 360KW standby power generator CDC450KVA canopy is manufactured from 3mm cold-rolled steel with double-layer epoxy zinc-rich anti-rust coating for long-term outdoor placement at coastal and tropical food processing facilities. Multi-layer flame-retardant acoustic foam, impedance composite muffler duct, and an industrial compound silencer reduce operating noise to ≤72dB(A) at 7m. The 950L integrated base fuel tank delivers over 8 continuous full-load running hours before refuelling.
The 450KVA silent diesel generator CDC450KVA all-copper brushless H-class alternator delivers ±1% steady-state voltage regulation and THD <3%, protecting food plant PLC systems, refrigeration compressor VFD drives, HVAC inverters, and precision temperature control equipment from voltage disturbances during load switching. The DeepSea DSE7320 controller provides auto-start within 3-6 seconds of grid failure with reserved ATS installation space for seamless mains-to-genset switchover.
The Cummins 13L diesel genset CDC450KVA carries a factory-registered ESN (Engine Serial Number) and is load-bank tested at 0%-25%-50%-75%-full prime load and 110% overload before shipment, with test video and PDF load report shipped with every unit. Warranty is underwritten by PICC (People's Insurance Company of China), with air-freight core parts in 3-5 days via the worldwide official Cummins after-sales service network.
