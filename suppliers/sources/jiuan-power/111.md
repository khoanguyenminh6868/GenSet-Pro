Description
Getting the power rating right matters more than most buyers expect. Size too far above actual site demand and the capital cost and footprint consume budget better spent on production equipment; size too small and the unit runs in chronic overload, accelerating insulation degradation and shortening alternator service life. The CDS55KVA 55KVA diesel generator sits at the practical optimum for light-commercial and township site demand in Southeast Asia, the Middle East, and Sub-Saharan Africa: 40KW/50KVA for extended daily prime-power running and 44KW/55KVA for mains-failure standby response. JIUAN Power builds this capacity range around a verified SDEC 4ZT3.5-G21 engine and a Class H all-copper alternator in a compact, factory-complete soundproof package that requires no civil construction before use.
JIUAN Power's quality process on this unit goes further than standard import-assemble practice. Each CDS55KVA is constructed as an ISO8528 certified genset — the SDEC 4ZT3.5-G21 engine, Deepsea DSE6120 controller, and single-bearing full-copper alternator are matched by specification and sequentially load-aged at 25%, 50%, 75%, and 110% of the 55KVA standby rating before leaving the factory floor. The test report covering output voltage accuracy, temperature rise under load, measured fuel consumption, and structural vibration ships inside the unit documentation package. Facility procurement officers at township hospitals, school campuses, and small factory parks cite this traceability as a primary reason for specifying JIUAN Power over comparable alternatives.
When township retailers and SME site managers evaluate running cost for a small business backup power installation, the fuel line almost always dominates the annual operating budget. A generator running 250 days a year at seven hours per shift burns diesel in volumes that quickly surpass the original purchase price of the unit. The SDEC 4ZT3.5-G21 engine's full-load specific fuel consumption of 202g/kW·h is the decisive technical specification — it sits at the leading edge for turbocharged four-cylinder units in the 40–60KVA bracket, delivering consistent per-shift savings across a multi-year deployment. The 220L integrated base tank covers a full day of continuous running without a refuelling stop.
JIUAN Power configures the CDS55KVA as a soundproof prime power generator that operators can put to work immediately on arrival. The complete package — SDEC engine, alternator, canopy, Deepsea controller, and base fuel tank — lands on a standard shipping pallet, factory-tested and integrated. The site team connects the fuel line and routes the output cable to the switchboard distribution panel. There is no concrete generator room to schedule, no external acoustic enclosure to source, no day tank to plumb. That streamlined commissioning model has made the 55KVA diesel generator a repeatable specification for residential estate developers and township contractors operating across three continents.

Five Core Product Advantages
① SDEC 4ZT3.5-G21 Engine — Generator-Dedicated Architecture, Wet Liner Rebuild Design, B10 Life Beyond 12,000 Hours
The SDEC 4ZT3.5-G21 is a generator-dedicated engine — not a converted automotive block adapted to stationary duty. Its 105×115mm bore×stroke gives 3.5L displacement running at 1500RPM with an 18:1 compression ratio, turbocharger plus water-to-water intercooler, high-pressure direct injection fuel system, and electronic governor calibrated for stable frequency across variable load steps. Thick cast-iron cylinder block with individually replaceable wet cylinder liners means each major overhaul is a bolt-in rebuild rather than a full engine swap — a significant cost difference over a 10-year service cycle. Cold-start works to −12°C standard; optional dual preheater extends reliable ignition to −25°C. Genuine Fleetguard triple-filter kit with 500-hour oil change intervals, B10 design life exceeding 12,000 hours, and 202g/kW·h full-load fuel consumption.
② ISO8528 Dual Rating — 50KVA Continuous Prime for Daily Operation, 55KVA Standby for Emergency Response
JIUAN Power validates each unit as an ISO8528 certified genset across both internationally defined load categories. The 50KVA prime power (PRP) rating authorises unrestricted continuous full-load running — the configuration used by convenience stores, small repair workshops, and community clinics operating on variable grid schedules for weeks at a time. The 55KVA standby (ESP) rating activates exclusively on mains failure, with a 10% short-duration overload allowance that handles the current inrush from water pump motors, small air conditioners, and cold-storage units starting simultaneously at the transfer event. Three-phase 400/230V output at 50Hz, 0.8PF; factory-calibrated 60Hz/440V export variants cover regional market requirements.
③ Steel-Body Soundproof Canopy — Zinc-Rich Epoxy Primer, ≤72dB(A) at 7m, Nine-Hour Tank Autonomy
JIUAN Power fabricates the CDS55KVA enclosure from 2.8mm cold-rolled steel panels, applying the double-layer zinc-rich epoxy anti-rust primer before canopy assembly — not as a post-coat on a formed shell. This build sequence improves corrosion resistance at panel edges and weld joints, extending outdoor service life in coastal salt-fog and monsoon-zone deployments. Inside, graduated-density flame-retardant acoustic panels work alongside a composite muffler duct and compact industrial silencer calibrated to the engine's exhaust frequency, holding measured broadband noise at ≤72dB(A) at 7 metres — within residential and commercial noise ordinance thresholds for most township and suburban sites. The 220L base tank provides over eight continuous hours of full-load autonomy; a canopy-side quick-fill port handles daily diesel top-up without opening any panel.
④ Vacuum-Impregnated All-Copper Alternator — ≤±1.2% Voltage Regulation, THD Below 3%, IP23 Moisture Protection
The single-bearing all-copper brushless alternator uses vacuum-impregnated windings in a Class H insulation package with IP23 moisture and dust protection — designed to perform in the damp basements, coastal installations, and high-humidity tropical environments where this generator size is routinely deployed. Voltage regulation at ≤±1.2% and total harmonic distortion below 3% satisfy the power quality demands of township clinic diagnostic instruments, networked monitoring workstations, and small-cell telecom installations. A factory-fitted moulded-case main circuit breaker provides overload, short-circuit, under-voltage, overspeed, and overtemperature protection as an integrated system, without any additional field-installed switchgear.
⑤ Deepsea DSE6120 Controller — Pre-Configured Auto-Start Logic, ATS Bay, Optional 4G Cloud Dashboard
Every CDS55KVA soundproof prime power generator ships with a Deepsea DSE6120 bilingual LCD controller installed and pre-configured at the factory. The panel displays live readings for voltage, current, oil pressure, coolant temperature, accumulated runtime hours, and active fault codes — enough information for a non-specialist operator to assess unit condition without calling a technician. Auto-start on mains loss, timed cool-down shutdown on supply restoration, and fault alarm stop are firmware-standard, not field-programmed additions. The cabinet's dedicated ATS bay keeps mains-to-genset transfer switch wiring self-contained; the optional 4G module links the unit to a cloud monitoring portal accessible from a smartphone — a practical arrangement for distributed small business backup power installations managed by a single remote team.
Application Scope
JIUAN Power supplies the CDS55KVA 55KVA diesel generator as small business backup power to township health clinics, street-level retail stores, small hardware fabrication shops, villa residential power distribution boards, municipal road project site offices, rural mobile base stations, and distributed agricultural cold-chain facilities. Consistent export volume flows from Southeast Asian village retailers, African processing micro-businesses, and Middle East suburban project sites that require a compact soundproof prime power generator ready for immediate outdoor deployment with minimum civil preparation.
