Description
Industrial plant managers running 24-hour production lines need prime-power generators with quantifiable fuel economy, traceable build quality, and a dependable service network. The JIUAN Power 500KVA Cummins diesel generator — Model CDC500KVA, powered by the Cummins 6ZTAA13-G2 13L engine from DCEC's ISO 9001-certified Wuhan facility — is rated 438KVA prime (350KW) and 500KVA standby (400KW), purpose-configured for factories, industrial parks, and mining sites requiring uninterrupted power across multiple production shifts. Every 500KVA silent Cummins genset leaves the factory ISO 8528-tested, with step-load bank recordings and full technical documentation included at no added cost.
The Cummins 6ZTAA13-G2 at the core of this soundproof diesel generator is a 13-litre, inline six-cylinder platform with a bore of 130mm, stroke of 163mm, and a 17:1 compression ratio — engineered for sustained high-output cycles at a rated full-load BSFC of ≤197 g/kW·h, equivalent to approximately 89–93 litres per hour under full prime load, placing it 5–8% ahead of competing units in the same power class on fuel efficiency. An electronic governor holds engine speed variation to ≤3%, suppressing voltage and frequency drift under the sudden load swings characteristic of heavy manufacturing start-ups. This 13-litre platform incorporates 30% fewer components than class rivals, reducing potential failure points and cutting spare-parts inventory requirements for plant maintenance departments.
The weatherproof enclosure panels of this 500KVA Cummins diesel generator are cold-rolled from ≥2.0mm steel sheet, then kiln-cured with a 120°C powder enamel topcoat; 40 kg/m³ flame-retardant rock-wool acoustic lining reduces radiated noise to 65–68 dB(A) measured at 7 metres, meeting industrial estate and residential-adjacent zone regulations. A 720-litre integral base-frame fuel tank sustains over 7.5 hours of unattended full-load operation per fill, eliminating mid-shift refuelling interruptions. Each 500KVA silent Cummins genset ships with a DeepSea DSE7320 control panel as standard, logging voltage, current, frequency, lube-oil pressure, coolant temperature, and operating hours; a pre-wired ATS terminal bay delivers automatic mains-to-generator changeover within seconds of supply interruption.
JIUAN Power ships every CDC500KVA 500KVA Cummins diesel generator with pre-shipment step-load recordings at six duty points — 0% to 110% overload — and a PDF performance certificate confirming ISO 8528 output. Documentation includes 3D engineering assembly diagrams, macro-lens component shots for counterfeit-parts screening, and a PICC-underwritten full-unit 5-year warranty certificate recording the Cummins 6ZTAA13-G2 engine serial number for the plant's fixed-asset register. A 150-page operations and maintenance reference in five language editions (English, Arabic, French, Spanish, Portuguese), electrical wiring diagrams, a 3D disassembly animation CD, and complimentary service wrenches with testing meters complete the shipment package. Panel plate thickness is confirmed by digital vernier gauge before despatch; express air delivery routes warranty components to any major port within 3–5 working days, covering the full 5-year warranty period.
