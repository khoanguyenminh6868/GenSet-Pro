200KW Manufacturing Plant Prime Power — Weichai WP10D264E200 32% Engine Reserve for Industrial Motor Loads
The CDW250KVA is Jiuan Generator's 250KVA Weichai diesel generator for manufacturing plant prime power — metal fabrication shops, plastic injection lines, compressor-driven processing facilities, and industrial parks requiring continuous 200KW three-phase supply with sufficient starting reserve for large inductive motor loads. Powered by the Weichai WP10D264E200 six-cylinder turbocharged diesel, the CDW250KVA delivers 200KW/250KVA ISO8528 PRP prime and 220KW/275KVA standby, in a 3800×1400×2100mm, 2700kg heavy-duty silent canopy.
The Weichai WP10D264E200 genset engine is an inline six-cylinder, turbocharged and intercooled water-cooled diesel with 9.726L displacement, 126mm × 130mm bore and stroke, 17:1 compression ratio, and high-pressure common rail (HPCR) electronic injection with ECU speed regulation. Rated gross output is 264kW@1500RPM — 32% above the 200KW prime rating — providing sustained starting reserve for DOL air compressors, CNC spindle drives, and centrifugal pump motors on manufacturing production lines. Specific fuel consumption is ≤197g/kWh.
The 200KW industrial prime generator CDW250KVA all-copper wound brushless synchronous alternator is H-class insulated, IP23 protected, with steady-state voltage regulation within ±1% and transient regulation within ±15%. The DSE7320 or SmartGen controller monitors voltage, frequency, current, coolant temperature, and oil pressure in real time, with auto-shutdown on overspeed, overheat, and low oil pressure. ATS auto-start transfers load within 3-6 seconds of grid failure — keeping CNC programs, PLC systems, and industrial refrigeration running without interruption.
The 250KVA manufacturing power generator CDW250KVA silent canopy uses 3mm cold-rolled steel with four-stage anti-corrosion treatment — pickling, phosphating, electrophoresis, and powder coating — and 60mm flame-retardant acoustic insulation. Eight high-damping rubber shock absorbers isolate the drivetrain from the chassis, achieving ≤72dB(A) at 7m. Multiple removable access panels allow engine oil, filter, and inspection servicing without disassembling the canopy, supporting 24/7 prime power maintenance schedules at production facilities.
The Weichai WP10 silent generator CDW250KVA reduces initial procurement cost by approximately 30% versus equivalent Perkins or Cummins 250KVA units. Weichai WP10 spare parts are available through in-country distributors in Africa, Southeast Asia, and the Middle East — lowering parts cost and reducing maintenance lead times at overseas manufacturing sites. Jiuan Generator provides an 18-month or 2,000-hour global warranty with technical support and parts delivery within 48 hours of fault reporting.
