Description
Procurement decisions for long-service generator installations — data centres, pharmaceutical production facilities, public infrastructure — hinge on more than nameplate output rating. For buyers building a 10-to-15-year power continuity plan around a 500KVA Cummins diesel generator, the pivotal differentiators are fuel economy over cumulative runtime, overhaul interval, and the supplier accountability structure behind the warranty. JIUAN Power's 438KVA silent Cummins genset addresses all three criteria, anchored by the Cummins QSZ13-G2 engine platform's published 18,000-hour between-overhauls rating.
The Cummins QSZ13-G2 in this soundproof diesel generator is a 13L six-cylinder turbocharged and aftercooled unit paired with the XPI 2400-bar ultra-high-pressure electronic injection system. At 1500RPM on 50Hz duty, it delivers 350KW/438KVA continuous prime power and 400KW/500KVA standby output at 0.8 power factor. Full-load brake specific fuel consumption of 186g/kW·h sits approximately 6% below the 197g/kW·h benchmark of comparable turbocharged platforms — a gap that compounds across tens of thousands of operating hours into measurable fuel cost and CO₂ reductions. Electronic governing holds speed deviation within ±0.25%, and cold-start performance extends to -15°C without preheating; an optional water jacket heater covers -30°C northern hemisphere deployment zones.
JIUAN Power backs each 438KVA silent Cummins genset with a 5-year warranty (or 2,000 operating hours, whichever comes first) underwritten by PICC (People's Insurance Company of China). This 5-year warranty converts a supplier pledge into a third-party insurance document that buyers can review before placing an order. Every Cummins QSZ13-G2 engine carries a unique traceable ESN linking the unit to Cummins' global manufacturing record. Each unit undergoes staged load testing at 0%, 25%, 50%, 75%, 100%, and 110% of rated prime output before shipment; the full test video and PDF load analysis report are co-shipped as standard deliverables. Warranty replacement components reach buyers via air freight within 3–5 business days globally.
The soundproof canopy of this 500KVA Cummins diesel generator uses 2.0mm minimum cold-rolled steel sheet, processed through 120°C high-temperature electrostatic powder coating with high-gloss paint for outstanding rust and scratch resistance. Interior acoustic treatment combines 40kg/m³ high-density flame-retardant rock wool with a secondary industrial exhaust muffler, holding measured noise to 65–68dB at 7m — within commercial and urban site noise ordinance limits. Pure copper stator windings are sectioned for visual weight verification at handover; panel steel thickness is confirmed by vernier caliper as JIUAN Power's standard pre-acceptance protocol.
