Description
Star hotels and full-service resort complexes cannot sustain any interruption in supply — guest elevators stop, HVAC zones drop, kitchen refrigeration warm-trips, and property management system servers lose live reservation data mid-check-in cycle. The JIUAN Power 275KVA Cummins diesel generator — Model CDC275KVA, powered by the Cummins 6LTAA8.9-G2 8.9L engine from DCEC's ISO 9001-certified Wuhan manufacturing base — delivers 200KW/250KVA prime and 220KW/275KVA standby, purpose-built for hospitality facilities demanding seamless, low-noise backup power. Every 250KVA silent Cummins genset leaves the factory ISO 8528-verified, with complete pre-shipment acceptance documentation as standard.
The Cummins 6LTAA8.9-G2 engine at the centre of this soundproof diesel generator is produced at DCEC Dongfeng Cummins' ISO 9001-certified Wuhan plant — an inline six-cylinder, 8.9L platform with a 114mm bore, 145mm stroke, and 16.6:1 compression ratio. A Holset turbocharger with air-to-air aftercooler and Bosch P7100 high-pressure mechanical fuel injection — no ECU in the fuel circuit — delivers dependable combustion performance across the 15–20 year service horizon of hotel fixed-asset plans. The electronic governor holds frequency deviation to ≤3%, ensuring HVAC compressor soft-starters, elevator drive controllers, and kitchen induction equipment receive stable frequency from no-load to full draw. Rated BSFC of 202–207 g/kW·h and a 12,000-hour overhaul interval keep planned maintenance practical for hotel engineering teams.
The weatherproof enclosure of this 275KVA Cummins diesel generator is fabricated from ≥2.0mm cold-rolled steel panels, finished with a 120°C high-temperature electrostatic enamel coat, and lined with 40 kg/m³ flame-retardant rock wool — acoustic emission at 7 metres is 65–68 dB(A), compliant with noise-sensitive plantroom siting adjacent to guestroom floors. A 580-litre integral skid-frame fuel tank sustains over 7 hours of continuous full-load runtime through overnight outage events. Each 250KVA silent Cummins genset arrives fitted with a DeepSea DSE7320 controller monitoring voltage, current, frequency, lube-oil pressure, coolant temperature, and operating hours; integrated ATS logic restores hotel power within 3–6 seconds of mains failure and shuts the set down automatically on supply restoration.

Three Core Product Advantages
6LTAA8.9-G2 P7100 Mechanical Injection: Long-Cycle Reliability for Hotel Fixed-Asset Service Life
The Cummins 6LTAA8.9-G2 engine in this 275KVA Cummins diesel generator uses Bosch P7100 high-pressure mechanical fuel injection — no ECU dependency in the injection circuit — maintaining calibrated combustion accuracy across the hotel property's 15–20 year fixed-asset service horizon without firmware updates or sensor management. JIUAN Power films and supplies a progressive load test acceptance video for each unit, confirming ISO 8528 output at all six sequential test bands before despatch. Rated BSFC of 202–207 g/kW·h and a 12,000-hour overhaul interval support predictable annual maintenance budgeting for hotel engineering departments.
PICC Full-Unit Warranty, ESN Traceability, and Steel Canopy Gauge Certification
Each CDC275KVA carries a PICC-underwritten 5-year warranty policy with the Cummins engine serial number registered on the policy certificate — providing auditable evidence for hotel brand-owner asset registers and project handover packages. JIUAN Power confirms canopy steel gauge by precision digital calliper and delivers 3D assembly reference drawings alongside magnified anti-counterfeiting component photographs, allowing hotel procurement teams to inspect this soundproof diesel generator's build quality remotely. Warranty spares are air-freighted to any international port within 3–5 working days via JIUAN Power's global Cummins network.
±1% Voltage Stability, C5-M Resort Protection, and Mountain-Site Cold-Start Packages
The Stamford or Leroy-Somer Class H brushless synchronous alternator in this 250KVA silent Cummins genset — IP23-rated, vacuum-impregnated all-copper windings — sustains voltage regulation within ±1% and total harmonic distortion below 3%, safeguarding hotel property management system servers, CCTV and access-control networks, fire detection panel control loops, and kitchen induction cooking lines from power-quality events. C5-M anti-corrosion coating (ISO 12944) protects the 275KVA Cummins diesel generator at coastal island and beachfront resort locations; cold-region kits — jacket water heaters, fuel preheaters, and arctic lubricant packages — ensure dependable cold-starts at -30°C for mountain and highland hotel properties.
Application Scope
The CDC275KVA 275KVA Cummins diesel generator provides prime and standby power for 4- and 5-star hotels, resort complexes, commercial mixed-use towers, campus medical centres, boutique hospitals, and logistics cold-chain warehouses across Southeast Asia, the Middle East, East Africa, and South Asia. JIUAN Power pre-assembles every soundproof diesel generator for transport — site installation requires only fuel connection and power-cable termination.
