Overview
600KW Remote Mine and Chemical Plant Prime — Weichai 6M33D725E310 19.6L, 130+ Country Parts Network, PICC-Insured
The CDW825KVA is Jiuan Generator's 825KVA Weichai diesel generator for remote mineral mines, chemical production parks, and large port facilities where the municipal grid is absent or unreliable. The Weichai 6M33D725E310 heavy-duty diesel delivers 600KW/750KVA ISO8528 PRP prime and 660KW/825KVA standby, with 19.6L six-cylinder Baudouin-heritage architecture and HPCR injection for stable 24/7 industrial prime power across Africa, the Middle East, and Southeast Asia project markets.
The Weichai 6M33D725E310 genset engine is a six-cylinder turbocharged and intercooled inline diesel with 19.6L displacement, 150×185mm bore and stroke, 15:1 compression ratio, and high-pressure common rail injection with ECU speed governing. Engine max output is 725kW with a rated continuous capacity of 675kW — sustaining the 600KW prime rating through continuous mine crushing cycles and chemical plant production shifts. Specific fuel consumption is ≤210g/kWh at full prime load; cold start is rated to -25°C with CI-4 15W-40 lubricant.
The 600KW prime power generator CDW825KVA industrial canopy is fabricated from 3.5mm carbon steel — 40% heavier-gauge than residential-grade silent enclosures — with four-layer corrosion protection: pickling, phosphating, electrophoresis, and high-temperature powder coating. The 70mm flame-retardant sound insulation layer and twelve rubber shock absorbers achieve ≤72dB(A) at 7m, allowing installation at industrial zone perimeters adjacent to site offices, residential blocks, and community boundaries without exceeding local noise ordinances.
The 825KVA industrial generator CDW825KVA all-copper brushless synchronous alternator is H-class insulated with ±1% steady-state voltage regulation and ±12% transient fluctuation protection, guarding chemical process control systems, pharmaceutical HVAC, and data centre UPS inputs from switching transients. The SmartGen or DSE ATS controller delivers auto-start within 3-6 seconds of grid failure and an automatic cooling-run shutdown after grid recovery, supporting unmanned industrial park and remote mine camp deployment.
The Weichai 6M33 heavy duty generator CDW825KVA carries a factory-registered ESN (Engine Serial Number) and is load-bank tested at 0%-25%-50%-75%-full prime load and 110% overload before shipment, with test video and PDF load report shipped with every unit. Warranty is underwritten by PICC (People's Insurance Company of China), with air-freight parts worldwide in 3-5 days. The 5-language 150-page maintenance manual, wiring diagrams, and 3D animation CD support remote-site engineering teams at overseas mine and chemical plant installations.
