Overview
250KW Port Cold Chain Prime Power — WP10D320E200 320kW Gross, 28% Reserve for Reefer Compressor Start
The CDW350KVA is Jiuan Generator's 350KVA Weichai diesel generator for port terminal cold chain and refrigerated container reefer power supply — dockside container yards, cold logistics hubs, and export processing facilities running 50 or more reefer sockets on round-the-clock prime power. The Weichai WP10D320E200 six-cylinder turbocharged intercooled diesel delivers 320kW gross output — 28% above the 250KW/312KVA ISO8528 PRP prime rating — absorbing compressor motor direct-on-line start currents without bus voltage dip. 3500kg; 3800×1400×2100mm.
The Weichai WP10D320E200 genset engine is an inline six-cylinder, turbocharged and charge-air-cooled diesel with 9.726L displacement, 126mm × 130mm bore and stroke, and 17:1 compression ratio. High-Pressure Common Rail injection with ECU speed control holds fuel consumption at ≤197g/kWh across full and partial-load port cycles. The 24L oil sump and 500-hour service intervals reduce scheduled maintenance stoppages at port terminals operating around the clock.
The 312KVA prime power generator CDW350KVA all-copper brushless synchronous alternator is H-class insulated, delivering stable 400V/50Hz three-phase supply with ±1% steady-state and ±15% transient voltage regulation at reefer load switching events. The DSE7320 or SmartGen controller provides ATS auto-start within 3-6 seconds — delivering continuous prime supply to dockside reefer distribution panels, cold store loading docks, container crane auxiliaries, and port authority office buildings simultaneously.
The 350KVA reefer power generator CDW350KVA silent canopy is fabricated from 3mm cold-rolled steel treated with pickling, phosphating, electrophoresis, and electrostatic powder coating — four-layer anti-corrosion for sustained resistance to salt air, coastal humidity, and dockside chemical exposure. The 60mm flame-retardant acoustic insulation and eight high-damping rubber shock absorbers control running noise to ≤72dB(A) at 7m, meeting port zone noise compliance at coastal industrial facilities.
The Weichai 350KVA silent genset CDW350KVA carries approximately 28-30% lower initial procurement cost versus equivalent Perkins or Cummins 350KVA units. Weichai WP10D320E200 parts are available at distributor hubs in Singapore, Dubai, Mombasa, and Santos — covering the key port city corridors where CDW350KVA installations are concentrated. Jiuan Generator provides an 18-month or 2,000-hour warranty with technical support and parts dispatch within 48 hours.

Q: What makes the CDW350KVA suitable for long-term deployment at coastal port installations?

Q: How does HPCR injection reduce total operating cost for 24/7 port prime use?

For a port running prime power 20+ hours daily, this efficiency translates directly to lower annual diesel cost versus conventional mechanical-injection gensets of equivalent output.
