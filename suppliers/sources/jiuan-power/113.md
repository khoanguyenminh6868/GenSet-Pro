Description
Township processing halls, community health posts, and mid-scale construction sites across Southeast Asia and Africa share a recurring challenge: grid supply is inconsistent, and even a short blackout mid-production run or during a patient procedure carries real financial and operational cost. JIUAN Power engineered the CDS65KVA 65KVA diesel generator for exactly this operating environment — rated 52KW/65KVA prime power and 56.8KW/71KVA standby, it pairs the SDEC 4ZT4.1-G21 prime power genset with a full-copper Class H alternator inside a factory-assembled soundproof canopy that holds ambient noise at ≤72dB(A) at 7 metres. Dependable silent industrial backup power, deployable without a dedicated equipment room.
What distinguishes JIUAN Power's approach from standard import-assemble operations is the pre-delivery validation chain. Each CDS65KVA ships as an ISO8528 certified diesel generator: the SDEC 4ZT4.1-G21 engine, Deepsea DSE6120 controller, and single-bearing full-copper alternator are matched as a system, then aged under graded loads from 25% up to 110% of the 71KVA standby rating. JIUAN Power documents output voltage, temperature rise, fuel consumption, and vibration at every load step, bundling the signed test report with each unit — giving procurement officers at township hospitals and industrial park operations the traceable commissioning record required for facility compliance sign-off. This township hospital power solution documentation package is a standard delivery inclusion, not an optional add-on.
Site managers who evaluate a fuel-efficient 65KVA generator for continuous auxiliary duty typically track one operating cost metric above all others: fuel volume per shift. The SDEC 4ZT4.1-G21 prime power genset records 208–215g/kW·h full-load specific fuel consumption — within the leading tier for turbocharged four-cylinder units in the 55–75KVA bracket. Running eight hours at 65KVA prime load draws roughly 74–80 litres from the 260L integrated base tank, leaving a full day's reserve before refuelling. For distributed field sites where fuel logistics are a genuine planning constraint, that autonomy translates directly into lower total operating cost over a 3–5 year deployment.
JIUAN Power ships every CDS65KVA 65KVA diesel generator fully factory-assembled — engine, alternator, canopy, controller, and base tank are integrated, load-tested, and palletised as a single unit. On-site commissioning comes down to running the diesel supply line and connecting the output cable to the main switchboard. No concrete generator room, no external acoustic liner, and no day tank plumbing. This pre-commissioned format delivers meaningful savings on civil construction cost for township contractors and distributed operators across Sub-Saharan Africa, the Middle East, and emerging Southeast Asian industrial zones.

Five Core Product Advantages
① SDEC 4ZT4.1-G21 Engine — 4.1L Turbocharged Inline Four-Cylinder, Wet Cylinder Liner, B10 Life Over 13,000 Hours
JIUAN Power sources the SDEC 4ZT4.1-G21 prime power genset directly from Shanghai SDEC's certified supply chain. The in-line four-cylinder water-cooled architecture delivers bore×stroke 105×118mm, 4.1L total displacement, 18:1 compression ratio, turbocharged direct injection, and electronic governor at 1500RPM. Replaceable wet cylinder liners and a forged crankshaft keep mechanical vibration low and deformation resistance high through extended full-load cycles. Cold-start capability reaches down to −13°C without pre-heating; optional dual jacket and oil preheater supports reliable ignition at −28°C for highland and cold-zone deployment. Genuine Fleetguard filter service kits at 500-hour change intervals underpin a B10 design life exceeding 13,000 operating hours — making the CDS65KVA a genuinely fuel-efficient 65KVA generator for multi-year field deployments.
② Dual PRP/ESP Certified Ratings — 65KVA Prime Power and 71KVA Emergency Standby
JIUAN Power validates the CDS65KVA as an ISO8528 certified diesel generator under two internationally defined load modes. The 65KVA prime power (PRP) rating supports continuous full-load operation as the preferred township hospital power solution for medium hardware workshops, township commercial centres, and small community hospitals running extended daily shifts. The 71KVA standby (ESP) rating activates on mains failure with a 10% short-time overload margin to absorb the inrush of air compressors, cold-room compressors, and small electric motors starting simultaneously. Standard three-phase output: 400/230V, 50Hz, 0.8PF; factory-configured 60Hz/440V export variants cover North American and African grid requirements.
③ ≤72dB(A) All-Weather Soundproof Canopy — 2.9mm Cold-Rolled Steel, 260L Integrated Base Tank
The CDS65KVA canopy is pressed from 2.9mm cold-rolled steel with a double-layer epoxy zinc-rich primer for sustained corrosion resistance in coastal salt-fog and tropical monsoon environments. Multi-density flame-retardant acoustic panels combined with an impedance muffler duct and large-bore industrial silencer hold broadband noise to ≤72dB(A) at 7 metres — making this unit viable as silent industrial backup power adjacent to township hospitals and residential commercial streets without breaching local noise ordinances. The 260L integrated base tank delivers over nine continuous full-load hours; a canopy-mounted quick-fill port makes daily fuelling practical without opening any panel enclosure. A widened single-side maintenance door and tempered observation window allow full filter and service access without canopy removal.
④ Full-Copper Class H Alternator — Voltage Regulation ≤±1.1%, THD Below 3%
The CDS65KVA pairs a single-bearing full-copper brushless self-excited synchronous alternator with Class H insulation, vacuum-impregnated windings, and IP23 moisture protection. Voltage regulation holds at ≤±1.1% and total harmonic distortion stays below 3% under rated steady-state conditions — the output quality demanded by small PLC-controlled production lines, township clinic diagnostic instruments, and micro telecom base stations sensitive to supply waveform distortion. A factory-fitted moulded-case main circuit breaker provides layered overload, short-circuit, under-voltage, overspeed, and overtemperature protection as standard, without requiring additional field-installed protection devices.
⑤ Deepsea DSE6120 Bilingual Controller — Built-in ATS Bay, Optional 4G Remote Monitoring
JIUAN Power installs the Deepsea DSE6120 bilingual LCD controller as standard on every unit in this range. Real-time display of voltage, current, lube oil pressure, coolant temperature, runtime hours, and fault codes gives operators complete situational awareness from a single panel. Automated start on mains failure, timed cool-down shutdown, and fault-triggered alarm stop are hard-wired standard functions requiring no additional programming. An internal ATS bay accommodates a direct mains-to-genset transfer switch without external wiring changes; an optional 4G cloud monitoring module extends remote start-stop and live parameter access via smartphone or desktop browser — particularly useful for distributed unmanned field sites managed by a single maintenance team.
Application Scope
JIUAN Power exports the CDS65KVA as a fuel-efficient 65KVA generator and trusted silent industrial backup power solution to township public hospitals, small and mid-scale hardware and food processing factories, commercial retail blocks, high-density residential estate distribution nodes, construction sites for municipal road and bridge projects, rural telecom base stations, and distributed logistics yards. Consistent demand flows from Southeast Asian village industrial parks, African processing workshop clusters, and Middle East suburban infrastructure projects that need a reliable township hospital power solution with a compact outdoor-deployable footprint and minimal civil preparation.
