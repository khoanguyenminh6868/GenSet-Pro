Description
Facilities making a generator procurement decision at the 200kW output level face a constrained shortlist. Among credible platforms in the 200–220kW working range, the Perkins 1206A-E70TTAG3 engine is the dominant specification for sites that prioritise multi-continent service coverage and verified spare parts supply. JIUAN Power builds the CDP275KVA 275KVA Perkins diesel generator around this engine: 200KW/250KVA continuous prime power for unrestricted daily running and 220KW/275KVA standby for mains-failure response, calibrated to 50Hz/1500RPM for European, African, and Asian grid standards with 60Hz/1800RPM available for North American and Pacific Rim market requirements.
The Perkins 1206A-E70TTAG3 engine powering this unit is a 7.01L six-cylinder turbocharged and aftercooled diesel built exclusively for generator-set duty. Its dual turbocharger arrangement maintains boost pressure through the low-load-to-full-load transition — the operating phase responsible for the most disruptive frequency excursions in standby generator service. At 105×135mm bore×stroke with 15.8:1 compression, the engine starts reliably to −15°C without supplementary aids. Fuel consumption measures 56.9L/h at prime full load and 28.1L/h at 50% duty — positioning this industrial prime power generator among the most fuel-efficient standby generator options in the 250KVA bracket.
The brushless synchronous alternator — Stamford, Leroy-Somer, or compatible Class H — is matched by kVA to the 1206A-E70TTAG3 engine output curve, not simply mounted on a shared frame. Vacuum-impregnated windings deliver voltage regulation within ±1% and total harmonic distortion below 5%, satisfying the power-quality thresholds of variable-frequency drives, medical imaging equipment, and multi-rack server rooms. JIUAN Power fits every CDP275KVA with a Deep Sea DSE7320 controller as standard equipment, providing auto-start, ATS bus transfer, RS-485 Modbus telemetry, and 14-channel fault protection without requiring additional field-installed control panels.
As an ISO 9001 generator supplier, JIUAN Power mounts each CDP275KVA on a reinforced welded-steel sub-base with four anti-vibration mounts per certified assembly specification, reducing transmitted oscillation to the structural slab and extending coolant pump and alternator bearing service intervals. A 400L integrated belly tank delivers 7–9 hours of uninterrupted continuous operation at prime load — sufficient for a standard production shift or the duration of most utility grid-restoration events without a mid-run refuelling stop. Each CDP275KVA 275KVA diesel generator exits the production line with a sequential factory load-test certificate.

FAQ
Q: What is the practical difference between the 250KVA prime (PRP) rating and the 275KVA standby (ESP) rating for daily site operation?
A: The 250KVA prime rating authorises unrestricted continuous full-load running — the correct specification for 24/7 prime-power installations such as off-grid processing facilities or primary supply sites. The 275KVA standby rating permits a 10% overload ceiling, activated only during mains-failure events and automatically released when grid supply is restored. Continuous operation at ESP level shortens the 1206A-E70TTAG3 engine service intervals; JIUAN Power sizes every industrial prime power generator installation against confirmed prime demand, not the standby ceiling.
Q: What documentation is supplied with each CDP275KVA 275KVA Perkins diesel generator?
A: As an ISO 9001 generator supplier, JIUAN Power includes a complete sequential factory load-test certificate documenting measured output voltage, current, frequency stability, oil pressure, coolant temperature, and fuel consumption at 25%, 50%, 75%, and 100% of rated 250KVA prime power. This record supports insurance underwriting, facility commissioning sign-off, and regulatory compliance documentation in jurisdictions that require certified test evidence for installed standby power equipment.
