350KW Mine Camp and Mineral Processing Plant Continuous Prime Power — Cummins 6ZTAA13-G2, 720L Tank, PICC-Insured
The CDC500KVA is Jiuan Generator's 500KVA Cummins diesel generator for large open-pit mine camps, quarry extraction sites, and mineral processing plants requiring 24-hour continuous prime power in remote and high-altitude locations. Powered by the genuine DCEC Dongfeng Cummins 6ZTAA13-G2 engine, the unit delivers 350KW/438KVA ISO8528 PRP prime and 400KW/500KVA standby, with CE and ISO8528 certification for global mining and minerals sector procurement compliance.
The Cummins 6ZTAA13-G2 genset CDC500KVA delivers inline six-cylinder turbocharged and intercooled power with 13L displacement, 130×163mm bore and stroke, and 17:1 compression ratio. PD high-pressure mechanical fuel pump with GAC electronic governor maintains speed regulation ≤3% under sudden load changes from mine site motor starts. The Holset turbocharger sustains output at elevated mine-site altitudes and +50°C desert environments across Africa, the Middle East, and Australia.
The 400KW standby power generator CDC500KVA canopy is built from 3mm cold-rolled steel with double-layer epoxy zinc-rich anti-rust coating and multi-layer flame-retardant acoustic foam, rated ≤72dB(A) at 7m — meeting noise limits for mine camp residential zones. The 720L integrated base fuel tank delivers approximately 7.5 continuous full-load running hours, reducing fuel resupply requirements at remote open-pit mine and quarry installations.
The 500KVA silent generator CDC500KVA all-copper brushless H-class alternator delivers ±1% steady-state voltage regulation and THD <3%, protecting mine-site PLC systems, crusher drive VFDs, submersible pump soft-starters, ventilation fan inverters, and precision survey instruments from voltage disturbances during DOL motor starting cycles. The DeepSea DSE7320 controller provides auto-start within 3-6 seconds of grid failure, with reserved ATS installation space for seamless mains-to-genset switchover.
The 350KW prime power generator CDC500KVA carries a factory-registered ESN (Engine Serial Number) and is load-bank tested at 0%-25%-50%-75%-full prime load and 110% overload before shipment. Test video and PDF load report accompany every unit. Warranty is underwritten by PICC (People's Insurance Company of China), providing verified quality assurance for overseas mine procurement teams, with air-freight core parts delivered worldwide in 3-5 days.
