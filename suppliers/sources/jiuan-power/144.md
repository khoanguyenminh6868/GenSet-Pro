Description
Open-cast mines and mineral processing plants cannot absorb gaps in electrical supply — ore crushers stall, conveyor drives lose sequence, dewatering pumps fail, and access lighting goes dark. The JIUAN Power 650KVA Cummins diesel generator — Model CDC650KVA, driven by the Cummins KTA19-G8 19L engine manufactured at CCEC Chongqing Cummins' ISO 9001-certified facility — delivers 480KW/600KVA prime and 520KW/650KVA standby for mine-site prime and emergency duty. Every 600KVA silent Cummins genset leaves the factory ISO 8528-compliant, with documented acceptance test results included as standard.
The Cummins KTA19-G8 engine at the centre of this soundproof diesel generator is an inline six-cylinder, 19L platform — 159mm bore, 159mm stroke, 13.9:1 compression ratio — running a PT high-pressure fuel system with EFC mechanical governor and Holset turbocharging with air-to-air intercooling. The PT fuel circuit operates without complex ECU modules, making it serviceable by mine-site mechanical crews at remote locations where specialist engine-management diagnostics are unavailable. Rated BSFC of 196–202 g/kW·h across the prime operating range, combined with an 18,000-hour overhaul interval, align with the 15–20 year fixed-asset lifecycle of a mine's generating plant.
The weatherproof canopy of this 650KVA Cummins diesel generator is fabricated from ≥2.0mm cold-rolled steel panels, finished with a 120°C electrostatic enamel coat, and lined with 40 kg/m³ flame-retardant rock wool — measured acoustic output at 7 metres is 65–68 dB(A), within statutory permit limits at mine-site accommodation camp boundaries. A 1,200-litre integral skid-frame fuel tank supports over 9 hours of full-load autonomous operation, covering a complete three-shift mine cycle without refuelling interruption. Each 600KVA silent Cummins genset is shipped pre-fitted with a DeepSea DSE7320 controller; ATS logic completes mains-to-generator transfer within 3–6 seconds and returns the load automatically on utility restoration.
JIUAN Power ships every CDC650KVA 650KVA Cummins diesel generator with factory-filmed staged load trials — six acceptance runs from 0% through 110% — and a PDF load report for mine procurement files. Documentation includes 3D engineering assembly drawings, magnified anti-counterfeiting component photographs, and a PICC insurance-backed 5-year warranty policy recording the Cummins KTA19-G8 engine serial number for mine asset registers. A 150-page installation and service handbook in five language editions (English, Arabic, French, Spanish, Portuguese), electrical schematics, a 3D disassembly animation CD, and service wrenches with testing meters complete each delivery. Enclosure panel gauge is confirmed by digital calliper before despatch; air-freight delivery gets warranty components to any destination port within 3–5 working days, covering the full 5-year warranty period.

FAQ
Q: How does the KTA19-G8 PT fuel system benefit remote mine-site maintenance?
A: Unlike ECU-controlled common-rail platforms, the Cummins KTA19-G8 in this 650KVA Cummins diesel generator uses a PT injection circuit with a mechanical EFC governor. Mine-site crews holding standard diesel-engine competency can service the fuel system without specialist electronic diagnostic equipment, reducing mean time to repair at locations far from Cummins service centres.
Q: What is the fuel tank capacity and runtime of this 600KVA silent Cummins genset?
A: The integral 1,200-litre base tank sustains over 9 hours of full-load operation — enough to cover a full three-shift mine working cycle. The Cummins KTA19-G8 engine consumes approximately 110–120 litres per hour at 480KW prime load, depending on site altitude and ambient temperature.
