Description
This 1100KVA Containerized Cummins KTA38-G5 diesel generator set delivers 800KW/1000KVA prime continuous power and 880KW/1100KVA emergency standby power, fully pre-installed inside a standard 20GP marine shipping container. As a high-capacity 1100KVA container Cummins genset, it is powered by a genuine CCEC Chongqing Cummins KTA38-G5 V12 dedicated generator engine, matched with a Class H all-copper brushless alternator, a Deepsea intelligent control panel, and an integrated container cooling & silencing system. As a mainstream heavy-duty industrial diesel genset, this robust containerized diesel generator features the reliable Cummins KTA38-G5 engine and is manufactured per ISO8528 & GB/T2820 international standards.
Under its 1100KVA standby rating, this durable backup power unit runs non-stop full-load as a prime power source for 24-hour daily operation. Once the mains power cuts off, the 1100KVA standby power generator automatically starts within 3~7 seconds for emergency backup.
The sealed enclosure of this rugged containerized diesel generator features excellent anti-corrosion & weatherproof performance, perfectly suited for coastal salt-mist mines, desert oilfields, remote construction camps, petrochemical plants, and off-grid mining sites without a fixed power house. Thanks to the robust 37.8L large-displacement design of the KTA38-G5 engine, this high-performance 1100KVA container Cummins genset needs no on-site workshop construction after delivery. It is ready for operation once the fuel and cables are connected, making this premium industrial diesel genset the top tender choice for overseas EPC projects across the Middle East, Africa, and South America.

Seven Core Competitive Advantages
Genuine CCEC KTA38-G5 Engine with Abundant Power Reserve
Power core adopts authentic CCEC KTA38-G5 diesel engine with 60° V-type 12-cylinder four-stroke layout, total displacement 37.8L, bore & stroke 159mm×159mm, equipped with Holset turbocharger + air-to-air intercooler plus original Cummins PT-EFC precision fuel injection system, rated speed 1500RPM, enough power surplus for 800KW prime and 880KW standby rating without voltage/frequency drop under sudden full load spike. Wet replaceable cylinder liner and upgraded combustion chamber realize outstanding high-temperature & abrasion resistance; customized high-altitude cooling kit keeps full 1100KVA output at 3500m elevation or 50℃ ambient temperature. Global Cummins service network ensures worldwide spare parts availability, overhaul lifespan over 20000 working hours to cut long-term downtime cost.
Pre-assembled Container Design for Fast Delivery & Installation
Whole genset is fully fitted inside standard 20ft shipping container at factory, all mechanical fixing, wiring, silencing and piping finished before shipment; end users only connect fuel pipe and power cable to put into service, saving huge cost of civil power house construction. Standard container dimension fits container vessel and flatbed truck for standardized sea & land transportation, greatly simplifying customs clearance and inland delivery for global remote projects. Container shell is made of thick weather-proof steel with epoxy zinc-rich anti-rust coating to resist rain, sand and coastal salt spray for long-term open-air placement.
Dual Rated Power for Prime & Standby Dual Working Mode
Prime Rating 1000KVA(800KW): Comply with PRP continuous power standard, stable operation under 80%~100% full load for daily mining & factory production; Standby Rating 1100KVA(880KW): ESP emergency rating, instant startup upon blackout with 10% short-time overload capacity to offset large motor inrush current and avoid production shutdown loss. Standard 400/230V three-phase four-wire output PF0.8 lagging, customized 60Hz/440V version available for Middle East, Africa and Southeast Asia local grid standard.
Optimized Fuel System for Lower Full-Cycle Consumption
Upgraded fuel injection structure of KTA38-G5 controls full-load specific fuel consumption ≤207g/kW·h, 7% more fuel-saving than same-class domestic gensets. Oversized integrated base fuel tank inside container supports over 8 continuous running hours at full load, external quick refuel port on container side for easy oil filling without opening main door. Original Fleetguard triple filter system effectively blocks dust and impurity, extending filter replacement cycle and reducing field maintenance expense.
Integrated Noise Reduction & Ventilation for Stable Internal Temperature
Inner container is fitted with industrial composite silencer and optimized air intake/exhaust duct; dust-proof louver for air inlet and high-efficiency exhaust muffler lower noise ≤72dB(A)@7m to meet urban and port environmental requirement. Dual large-capacity reinforced radiator plus forced air duct enhance inner air circulation, preventing engine overheating power drop under high ambient temperature. Independent maintenance door and control cabinet door enable easy daily inspection without container disassembly.
Premium All-Copper Brushless Alternator Supplies Clean Stable Power
Industrial single-bearing all-copper brushless synchronous alternator with Class H insulation & IP23 protection grade, vacuum impregnated full-copper winding features superior moisture & salt-spray resistance for coastal petrochemical and seaside mining sites. Steady voltage regulation ≤±1%, THD ＜ 3% guarantees smooth power supply for PLC, precision instrument and server load; built-in molded case circuit breaker provides five safety protections: overload, short-circuit, under-voltage, overspeed and high coolant temperature to avoid accidental unit burnout.
Independent Intelligent Control Cabin with Optional ATS & Parallel Function
Separate insulated control compartment inside container pre-installed Deepsea DSE7320 LCD controller (Smartgen/ComAp optional) with bilingual English-Chinese screen, real-time monitoring of voltage, current, coolant/oil temp, oil pressure, rotate speed and fault code, auto start/stop logic for mains failure & recovery. Reserved mounting space for ATS automatic transfer switch, optional remote cloud monitoring and multi-unit parallel control system realize centralized management via mobile phone or PC, ideal for large-scale centralized power station projects.

The 1100KVA Containerized Cummins KTA38-G5 diesel genset is widely applied in overseas open-pit non-ferrous metal mine, Middle East desert oilfield, coastal petrochemical plant, large seaport terminal, cross-border EPC infrastructure project, remote off-grid mining camp, IDC backup power station and offshore drilling platform, becoming mainstream container power equipment for global engineering procurement.
