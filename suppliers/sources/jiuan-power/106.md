Description
This Weichai silent diesel generator with 1000kVA prime power and 1100kVA standby power is powered by a genuine Weichai 12M26D968E200 V-type 12-cylinder diesel engine. It belongs to the heavy-duty silent genset series specially customized for industrial continuous power supply and critical industrial backup power projects. As core heavy-duty power equipment, this 1000kva Weichai silent diesel generator strictly follows ISO8528, CE, and GB/T2820 international manufacturing standards. The complete unit integrates a rugged soundproof enclosure, a brushless synchronous alternator, an intelligent auto-start control system, and an integrated fuel tank into one compact silent genset setup. This makes it widely applicable for mining projects, large factories, data centers, hospitals, commercial complexes, oilfield construction, and remote area prime power lacking reliable municipal grid access.
The rated prime power of this Weichai 12M26D968E200 Weichai silent diesel generator is 800kW/1000KVA (COSΦ=0.8 lagging), and its standby emergency power reaches 880kW/1100KVA to ensure uncompromising industrial backup power readiness. The standard configuration of this silent genset runs at 1500RPM, 50Hz, with a 400/230V three-phase four-wire output, matching the global mainstream power grid standards of Africa, the Middle East, Southeast Asia, and Latin America markets.
Different from an open-frame diesel genset, the whole machine of this specialized Weichai silent diesel generator is wrapped by a thickened soundproof canopy, effectively controlling running noise below 72dB(A) at 7 meters away. This perfectly solves noise pollution problems for downtown construction, residential area backup power, and noise-restricted industrial park installations where a quiet silent genset is mandatory.
Our factory owns 18 years of OEM production experience for the Weichai silent diesel generator. All spare parts adopt original Weichai genuine accessories, and every finished silent genset passes a strict SGS factory inspection before delivery. We fully support customized upgrades to maximize your industrial backup power efficiency, including a super silent canopy, an ATS auto transfer switch, a parallel cabinet, and an external large-capacity fuel tank according to your specific site requirements.

Core Product Advantages
Genuine Weichai 12M26D968E200 Engine, Stable Dynamic Performance
The core power source of this 1000kva prime 1100kva standby silent diesel generator is original Weichai 12M26D968E200 four-stroke V12 diesel engine, 150mm×150mm bore & stroke, total displacement 38.1L, turbocharged intercooled air intake structure, electronic speed governor, closed-cycle water cooling design, maximum engine gross power 968kW, fully covering 800kW prime load demand with abundant power reserve. Under 100% full load working condition, fuel consumption is controlled at 210g/kW·h, low fuel consumption effectively cuts long-term operation cost for end users; engine adopts high-strength alloy cast iron cylinder block, forged integral crankshaft with surface quenching treatment, wear-resistant four-valve cylinder head structure, strong anti-high temperature, anti-vibration ability, can run continuously for long hours under high temperature, high altitude and dusty harsh working environment like Africa gold mine and Saudi desert oilfield. Engine lube oil total capacity 113L, adapts CF grade 15W-40 diesel engine oil and common 0# light diesel fuel, global aftermarket spare parts supply system of Weichai ensures quick parts replacement and convenient local maintenance in overseas project site.
High-efficiency Silent Canopy, Low Noise & Weatherproof
The customized soundproof canopy for Weichai 12M26D968E200 silent diesel generator is made of 3mm thick cold-rolled steel plate, going through pickling, phosphating, electrophoresis and high-temperature powder coating anti-corrosion treatment, anti-rust and anti-rain, suitable for outdoor long-term open-air placement without extra rainproof warehouse. Inner canopy pastes 50mm thick flame-retardant high-density acoustic cotton, matched with two-stage industrial exhaust muffler and heat insulation wrapping on exhaust pipeline, plus four groups high-damping rubber shock absorbers installed between base chassis and engine-alternator assembly, comprehensively reduce mechanical vibration noise and exhaust noise, final noise index ≤72dB(A)@7m, meeting EU environmental noise limitation standard. The canopy reserves multi-side removable maintenance access door with 304 stainless steel lock and sealing rubber strip, operators can finish daily oil filling, filter replacement and routine overhaul without dismantling the whole enclosure, greatly improve maintenance efficiency of this 1000kva silent diesel generator.
Copper Wound Brushless Alternator, Stable Voltage Output
Matching alternator of the 1100kva standby Weichai silent diesel generator is all-copper wire wound brushless synchronous alternator, H class insulation, IP23 protection grade, equipped built-in AVR automatic voltage regulator device, steady-state voltage regulation rate ≤±1%, transient voltage fluctuation controlled within ±15%, no voltage drop or surge during sudden load increase or decrease, effectively protect precision equipment such as server, medical instrument and industrial PLC from unstable current damage. No carbon brush design avoids regular carbon brush replacement trouble, reduce daily maintenance workload and fault probability, alternator adapts continuous 24h average 70% load running as PRP power definition, allows 10% overload operation for 1 hour within every 12 hours when using standby power, fully satisfy sudden peak power demand during municipal power cut.
Intelligent Control System & Complete Safety Protection
Standard fitted Deepsea/SmartGen digital auto-start control panel for this Weichai silent diesel generator, real-time display of voltage, frequency, current, water temperature, oil pressure, running hour data on color screen, with over-speed, over-temperature, low oil pressure, over-current, short-circuit, under-voltage automatic shutdown protection function, once abnormal parameter appears, genset cuts off power supply automatically to avoid core component burnout. Optional ATS auto transfer switch cabinet can realize unattended automatic startup and shutdown: when city grid fails, genset starts within 3-8 seconds and switches load automatically; after mains recovers, genset cools down and stops by itself, ideal for unmanned factory, remote base station backup power solution. Integrated base fuel tank inside silent chassis holds enough diesel for 8-hour continuous full-load running, external fuel inlet reserved for connecting large bulk fuel tank for long-term uninterrupted prime power use.
Wide Application & Cost-effective After-sales Support
This 1000KVA prime 1100KVA standby Weichai silent diesel generator covers multiple application scenarios: prime continuous power for Africa mining plant, remote island factory, desert construction site; emergency standby power for hospital, shopping mall, data center, hotel, government building, chemical factory; temporary construction power for large engineering project and outdoor exhibition.
