Overview
Dual-Turbocharged Power Density for Uninterruptible Prime Applications
Data centers, pharmaceutical cold-chain warehouses, large hotel complexes, and continuous manufacturing lines share a common power requirement: the facility cannot accept load interruptions during a generator transition, and the generator must sustain full rated output across extended operating periods without thermal derating. These requirements point toward a specific engine class — dual-turbocharged, intercooled, with verified emission compliance for international project documentation. The CDP275KVA 275KVA Perkins diesel generator is built around the Perkins 1206A-E70TTAG3, a dual-turbocharged, aftercooled engine that positions this 250KVA prime power generator for applications where continuous rated output, EU Stage II emission compliance, and 12V DC fast-start reliability all have to co-exist in a single packaged system.
The 1206A-E70TTAG3 diesel genset engine is an inline six-cylinder, four-stroke, water-cooled unit with 7.01L displacement, 105mm × 135mm bore and stroke. The defining technology on the 1206A versus single-turbo platforms is dual sequential turbocharged aspiration combined with an air-to-air aftercooler — a configuration that compresses intake air in two sequential stages and then cools the compressed charge before entry to the combustion chamber, increasing air density without increasing displacement. The result is 220KW standby output from the same 7.01L block that produces 120KW in single-turbo form — an engineering approach that maintains compact engine dimensions while achieving output levels previously requiring 8–12L displacement. Compression ratio is 15.8:1, calibrated for the high-density charged air environment. Fuel consumption is 56.9L/h at full prime load, 41.5L/h at 75% load, and 28.1L/h at 50% load. Service interval is 500 hours; the electronic governor maintains load response within ±0.5% frequency deviation under step-load events.
ISO8528 certification covers two operating modes for the CDP275KVA. The 250KVA prime power generator PRP rating (200KW/250KVA) certifies unlimited continuous operation as a facility's primary power source, subject to the 24-hour average load staying below 70% of prime capacity. The 275KVA standby power supply ESP rating (220KW/275KVA) covers emergency grid-failure backup with a short-duration overload margin in any 12-hour emergency window for startup inrush from HVAC compressors, lifts, and three-phase industrial motors. Standard output: 50Hz/1500RPM, 400/230V three-phase four-wire, power factor 0.8 lagging; a 60Hz/440V configuration is available for US and non-European markets.
Three enclosure configurations are available. The silent canopy enclosure reduces operating noise to ≤75dB(A) at 7 meters through high-density acoustic insulation, reinforced galvanized steel panels, and a large-bore industrial silencer. The open-frame configuration provides full-perimeter access for maintenance in dedicated generator rooms. The containerized configuration offers IP54-equivalent dust and rain protection for construction sites, mining facilities, and remote off-grid installations where weather and airborne particulates are operational constraints. The 400L integrated base fuel tank supports 7–9 continuous hours at full prime load, with a low-fuel alarm and external refuel port as standard across all enclosure types.
The brushless synchronous alternator uses H-class insulation with IP23 standard protection (IP55 optional), vacuum-impregnated full-copper windings, and a digital AVR holding steady-state voltage within ±1% and waveform distortion below 5% — a requirement for IT server loads, UPS systems, and medical imaging equipment. For the 275KVA Perkins diesel generator, Jiuan Generator installs either a Deep Sea DSE7320 or SmartGen HGM6120 controller, both providing real-time monitoring of voltage, current, frequency, oil pressure, coolant temperature, and run hours, with automatic mains-failure detection, timed cool-down before shutdown, and multi-channel fault alarm protection. ATS terminal blocks are factory-wired; optional 4G remote monitoring enables start-stop commands and parameter access via mobile or PC.

5 Core Competitive Advantages
1. Dual Sequential Turbocharging + Air-to-Air Aftercooler — 220KW from 7.01L
The 1206A-E70TTAG3 diesel genset 's dual sequential turbocharger system charges intake air through two compression stages before the aftercooler reduces intake temperature and increases charge density entering the combustion chamber. This technology allows the 1206A to deliver 220KW standby output from the same 7.01L displacement used in single-turbo form — producing an output level that previously required larger-displacement platforms, while retaining the compact engine footprint and 12V DC starting system of the 1106A family. Step-load voltage recovery is within two cycles, meeting the transient response requirements of UPS bypass systems and industrial motor drives.
2. EU Stage II + EPA Tier 2 — Export-Ready Emission Compliance
The CDP275KVA meets EU Stage II and EPA Tier 2 exhaust emission standards — certification that covers generator import approval in European Union member states, the United States, and markets that align domestic standards with these frameworks. For the 275KVA standby power supply operating in West African, Southeast Asian, and Middle Eastern project sites with EU or US destination contracts, Jiuan Generator supplies the original Perkins CE Declaration of Conformity and emission test documentation for project submission, customs clearance, and site commissioning approval.
3. 400L Integrated Fuel Tank — 7–9 Hours Full-Load Autonomous Runtime
The 400L base tank allows 7–9 hours of continuous operation at full prime load — covering overnight operating shifts and extended grid outages without daily refueling. A low-fuel alarm and external refuel port are standard across all enclosure configurations. For installations requiring 24-hour or longer autonomous runtime, optional day tank piping systems connect external storage without interrupting operation. The fuel system uses a primary filter plus secondary fine filter arrangement to protect the injection system against particulate contamination.
4. Deep Sea DSE7320 + Factory ATS Pre-Wiring — Grid-Failover Ready on Delivery
The Deep Sea DSE7320 controller integrates mains-sensing, generator start sequencing, ATS transfer timing, and multi-channel fault alarm into a single unit factory-configured for the CDP275KVA's rated load. The Perkins 1200 series generator CDP275KVA arrives with ATS terminal blocks wired and labeled — reducing site integration from days to hours. The DSE7320's RS485 Modbus interface supports BMS/SCADA integration for data centers and building management systems requiring centralized power monitoring.
5. ISO9001 Steel Frame + Anti-Vibration Mounts — Long-Term Structural Integrity
The heavy-duty base frame, fabricated to ISO9001 tolerances from structural steel and hot-dip treated against corrosion, carries four-corner heavy-duty rubber vibration mounts that isolate engine-alternator operational frequencies from the building slab. This isolation protects both the generator's internal components and the host facility's floor structure over multi-year continuous prime operation. The 250KVA prime power generator CDP275KVA frame is rated for tropical coastal and high-humidity industrial environments where untreated steel would show surface oxidation within 18–24 months.
