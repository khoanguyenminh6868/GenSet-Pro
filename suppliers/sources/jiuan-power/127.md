Overview
400KW Hospital and Pharmaceutical Plant Prime — Weichai WP13D510E311 12.54L, ≤198g/kWh, PICC-Insured
The CDW550KVA is Jiuan Generator's 550KVA Weichai diesel generator for large urban hospitals, pharmaceutical production facilities, and commercial complexes requiring continuous prime power with strict noise compliance. The Weichai WP13D510E311 delivers 400KW/500KVA ISO8528 PRP prime and 440KW/550KVA standby, with 12.54L four-valve turbocharged architecture and HPCR injection for stable 24/7 institutional power supply across Africa, the Middle East, and Southeast Asia markets.
The Weichai WP13D510E311 genset engine is a six-cylinder turbocharged and intercooled inline diesel with 12.54L displacement, 127×165mm bore and stroke, 16:1 compression ratio, and HPCR injection with ECU speed governing. Maximum output is 510kW with prime rated capacity at 400KW — maintaining a reliable power margin through hospital ward HVAC, surgical theatre lighting, and pharmaceutical cleanroom production cycles. Specific fuel consumption is ≤198g/kWh at full prime load.
The 400KW prime power generator CDW550KVA silent canopy is built from 3.2mm cold-rolled steel with four-layer anti-corrosion treatment — pickling, phosphating, electrophoresis, and powder coating — resisting the chemical vapour and high-humidity environments of pharmaceutical production floors and hospital mechanical plant rooms. The 65mm flame-retardant sound insulation cotton and ten rubber shock absorbers achieve ≤72dB(A) at 7m, complying with EU and WHO noise limits for healthcare facility perimeter installations.
The 550KVA silent hospital generator CDW550KVA all-copper brushless alternator is H-class insulated with ±1% steady-state voltage regulation and ±13% transient protection during load switching, safeguarding MRI scanners, ICU monitoring systems, pharmaceutical HPLC instruments, and cleanroom HVAC drives from voltage transients. The SmartGen or DSE ATS controller provides six automatic shutdown protections and delivers auto-start within 3-5 seconds of grid failure, supporting unattended hospital and pharmaceutical facility power management.
The Weichai WP13 industrial generator CDW550KVA carries a factory-registered ESN (Engine Serial Number) and is load-bank tested at 0%-25%-50%-75%-full prime load and 110% overload before shipment, with test video and PDF load report delivered with every unit. Warranty is underwritten by PICC (People's Insurance Company of China), with air-freight parts worldwide in 3-5 days. Jiuan Generator has 18 years of WP13 series OEM experience; the 5-language 150-page manual and 3D animation CD support overseas hospital and pharmaceutical engineering teams.
