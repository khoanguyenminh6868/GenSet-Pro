Overview
36KW Telecom BTS, Clinic, and Off-Grid School Standby — ESN-Traceable, PICC-Insured, 1100kg Single-Hoist
The CDW50KVA is Jiuan Generator's 50KVA Weichai diesel generator for telecom BTS, rural clinic, and off-grid school standby power — compact sites requiring 1100kg single-hoist installation, fast ATS auto-start, and verifiable unit quality for overseas procurement. The Weichai WP2.3D48E200 four-cylinder turbocharged diesel delivers 36KW/45KVA ISO8528 PRP prime and 40KW/50KVA standby in a 2200×1000×1600mm footprint designed for BTS generator rooms, clinic basements, and remote community power installations.
The Weichai WP2.3D48E200 genset engine is an inline four-cylinder, turbocharged and intercooled water-cooled diesel with 2.289L displacement, 89mm × 92mm bore and stroke, and 17.5:1 compression ratio. Electronic fuel injection with ECU speed control holds specific fuel consumption at ≤205g/kWh. The 8L oil sump and 5L coolant circuit support 500-hour service intervals — reducing scheduled maintenance cost and parts volume at telecom tower base and rural site installations.
The 36KW compact standby generator CDW50KVA all-copper brushless synchronous alternator is H-class insulated, delivering 400V/50Hz three-phase supply with ±1% steady-state and ±15% transient voltage regulation. The SmartGen or DSE controller provides ATS auto-start within 3-5 seconds — bridging the telecom BTS battery gap during grid outage, and maintaining supply to clinic vaccine refrigerators, diagnostic equipment, and off-grid school lighting and computer lab loads without manual intervention.
The 50KVA telecom backup generator CDW50KVA canopy is fabricated from 2.5mm cold-rolled steel with 50mm flame-retardant sound insulation and six high-damping rubber shock absorbers, achieving ≤72dB(A) at 7m for residential-area BTS noise compliance. Each CDW50KVA unit carries warranty coverage underwritten by PICC (People's Insurance Company of China), with air-freight warranty parts delivered worldwide in 3-5 days — critical response time for active telecom and clinic standby installations far from service centres.
The Weichai WP2.3 silent generator CDW50KVA delivers full pre-shipment verification: each unit has a unique traceable ESN (Engine Serial Number) and undergoes staged load testing at 0%-25%-50%-75%-full load and 110% overload on a load bank, with test video and PDF load analysis report supplied with every shipment. Jiuan Generator provides an 18-month or 2,000-hour warranty. The 5-language 150-page maintenance manual and 3D disassembly animation CD reduce installation barriers for non-professional local technicians at overseas telecom and clinic sites.
