Overview
64KW Island Resort and Eco-Lodge Off-Grid Prime Power — Weichai WP4.1D80E200 1700kg Compact Transportable
The CDW80KVA is Jiuan Generator's 80KVA Weichai diesel generator for island resort and eco-lodge off-grid prime power — remote ecotourism camps, island community facilities, and off-grid sites where no grid connection exists and reliable continuous power must be generated on-site. Powered by the Weichai WP4.1D80E200 four-cylinder turbocharged diesel, the CDW80KVA delivers 64KW/80KVA ISO8528 PRP prime and 70KW/88KVA standby — in a 1700kg, 2600×1100×1600mm compact footprint transportable by standard truck or medium barge to remote island installations.
The Weichai WP4.1D80E200 genset engine is an inline four-cylinder, turbocharged water-cooled diesel with 4.088L displacement, 105mm × 118mm bore and stroke, and 17.5:1 compression ratio. The high compression ratio supports reliable self-start in tropical island generator rooms where ambient temperatures regularly exceed 35°C and relative humidity exceeds 90%. Electronic fuel injection holds specific fuel consumption at ≤205g/kWh, and the 13L oil sump supports 500-hour service intervals — reducing the frequency of parts shipments to remote island locations.
The 64KW off-grid prime generator CDW80KVA all-copper wound brushless synchronous alternator is H-class insulated and delivers stable 400V/50Hz three-phase supply with voltage regulation within ±1% and transient regulation within ±15%. The DSE7320 or SmartGen controller provides ATS auto-start within 3-6 seconds, enabling seamless switching between solar generation and diesel prime at off-grid island resorts — covering guestroom air conditioning, restaurant refrigeration, water pumping, and pathway lighting loads simultaneously.
The 80KVA island resort generator CDW80KVA silent canopy is fabricated from 2.2mm cold-rolled steel with multi-layer anti-rust coating and 50mm flame-retardant acoustic insulation, achieving ≤72dB(A) at 7m. For island eco-lodge guests, this noise level is inaudible at typical guestroom-to-generator distances of 30-50m. The integrated base fuel tank supports 8 hours of continuous prime operation on a single fill — covering a full overnight cycle without manual refueling or staff intervention.
The Weichai WP4 compact generator CDW80KVA carries approximately 32% lower initial procurement cost versus equivalent Perkins or Cummins 80KVA units. Weichai WP4.1 parts are available through in-country distributors across Southeast Asia, Indonesia, the Philippines, and the Pacific Islands — keeping maintenance cost low and parts lead time short at island and ecotourism generator installations. Jiuan Generator provides an 18-month or 2,000-hour warranty on each CDW80KVA unit.

FAQ
Q: What does the ISO8528 PRP prime rating mean for an island resort without a grid connection?
A: The 80KVA Weichai diesel generator CDW80KVA is rated 64KW/80KVA ISO8528 PRP prime — certified for continuous unrestricted-hour operation at ≤70% average load factor. This makes it suitable as the primary electricity source at an island resort operating 12-24 hours daily for guestroom air conditioning, lighting, refrigeration, and water pumping without any grid connection.
Q: Can the CDW80KVA be used in a solar-diesel hybrid system at an eco-lodge?
A: Yes. The 80KVA island resort generator CDW80KVA DSE7320 controller supports solar-diesel hybrid ATS switching — the genset auto-starts when solar battery reserves fall below a set threshold and shuts down after the battery bank is recharged. This load-following operation reduces daily diesel consumption at eco-lodges with solar panels while maintaining uninterrupted power to guests.
Q: What makes the CDW80KVA practical for transport to a remote island site?
A: The Weichai WP4 compact generator CDW80KVA weighs 1700kg and measures 2600×1100×1600mm — within the payload and bed-width limits of a standard flatbed truck or medium cargo barge. No crane is needed for loading or offloading. Weichai WP4.1 spare parts are available through distributors across Southeast Asian island chains and Pacific coastal markets, reducing supply chain risk at remote installations.
