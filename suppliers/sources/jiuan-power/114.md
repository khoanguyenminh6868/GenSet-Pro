Description
The CDS55KVA 55KVA SDEC diesel generator solves one of the most persistent challenges for small and medium enterprises in markets with inconsistent grid supply: sustaining business operations through unplanned outages without inflating site costs or violating local noise codes. Built on the proven SDEC Z-series generator set platform, JIUAN Power configures this low-noise commercial diesel generator for operators who need a low-maintenance, space-efficient power asset deployable directly beside a retail unit, behind a community clinic, or inside a small manufacturing workshop — all while remaining within municipal noise limits.
At the core of this 55KVA SDEC diesel generator is the genuine Shanghai SDEC 4ZT3.5-G21 genset engine, a turbocharged 3.5-litre in-line four-cylinder water-cooled unit rated at 1500RPM. The SDEC 4ZT3.5-G21 generator achieves a factory-verified 54KW standby bench output, providing JIUAN Power engineers with the working headroom to certify the 50KVA/40KW prime rating and 55KVA/44KW standby rating, qualifying the CDS55KVA as ISO8528 certified backup power under internationally recognized test protocols. Its turbocharger and water-to-water intercooler assembly holds full-load specific fuel consumption at a measured 202g/kW·h, delivering tangible annual running-cost savings compared with naturally-aspirated alternatives in the same power band.
JIUAN Power pairs every silent diesel genset 55KVA with a single-bearing all-copper brushless synchronous alternator (Class H insulation, IP23 rating) and a Deepsea DSE6120 controller that tracks 14 live operating parameters including lube oil pressure, coolant temperature, voltage and cumulative runtime. The complete 1100kg assembly leaves JIUAN Power's facility fully built and load-aged: end users connect power cables and a diesel supply line — this 50KVA soundproof generator enters service immediately, with no plant-room construction or on-site commissioning labour required.

FAQ
Q: What is the difference between the 50KVA prime rating and the 55KVA standby rating of the CDS55KVA?
A: The 50KVA/40KW prime rating (PRP) is certified for unlimited annual running hours as the main power source — for example, as daily power for a convenience store or small workshop. The 55KVA/44KW standby rating (ESP) applies exclusively during grid failures with a capped annual usage limit; it carries a 10% short overload margin to absorb motor-start inrush currents from water pumps and small compressors. JIUAN Power tests every silent diesel genset 55KVA to deliver both ratings as ISO8528 certified backup power within voltage and frequency tolerances before shipment.
Q: Can the SDEC 4ZT3.5-G21 genset start reliably in cold-climate deployments?
A: The standard SDEC 4ZT3.5-G21 genset engine starts unaided at ambient temperatures down to -12°C. For the compact silent diesel generator deployed in colder environments, JIUAN Power supplies an optional engine oil pre-heater that extends reliable cold-start capability to -25°C, making this low-noise commercial diesel generator suitable for highland and northern-latitude infrastructure projects.
Q: Does JIUAN Power offer OEM and private-label packaging for the SDEC Z-series generator set?
A: Yes. JIUAN Power provides custom model plates, canopy colour options and full documentation packages for the CE-compliant industrial generator line including the CDS55KVA. Distributors marketing the SDEC Z-series generator set under a regional brand can request white-label configurations with a minimum order quantity; contact JIUAN Power's export sales team for OEM programme details.
