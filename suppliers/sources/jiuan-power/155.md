Description
Street shops, township clinics, and small workshops scattered across emerging markets share a real vulnerability: a single unplanned power outage can erase an entire day's revenue. JIUAN Power engineered the CDS44KVA 44KVA SDEC diesel generator to address this at the right scale — rated 32KW/40KVA prime power and 35.2KW/44KVA standby power, this compact silent generator auto-starts in 3–5 seconds and delivers stable 400/230V three-phase output as reliable silent commercial backup power without requiring a dedicated machine room.
Unlike budget soundproof units built from mixed-brand components, the CDS44KVA pairs a certified SDEC 4ZT3.2-G11 engine with a Class H all-copper alternator and a Deepsea DSE6120 controller — three purpose-matched components jointly validated under ISO8528 and GB/T2820 test protocols. JIUAN Power runs every shipped ISO8528 certified genset through graded load aging from 25% up to 110% of rated standby load before handover, supplying traceable output, temperature, and fuel log documentation with every delivery.
Small business operators and project site managers who specify a fuel-efficient prime power generator for daily auxiliary or peak-shaving duty evaluate one metric first: fuel cost per operating shift. The SDEC 4ZT3.2-G11 engine records 212–222g/kW·h full-load specific fuel consumption — within the leading tier for turbocharged units in the 30–50KVA bracket. Running eight hours at 40KVA prime load draws roughly 67–72 litres from the 200L integrated base tank, keeping generous reserve for overnight coverage without mid-shift refuelling.
JIUAN Power ships every CDS44KVA silent diesel genset fully assembled with engine, alternator, canopy, controller, and base fuel tank integrated. The only field tasks are the diesel supply connection and main power cable — no concrete generator room, no additional acoustic lining, no separate day tank. This plug-and-go packaging has made the 44KVA diesel generator from JIUAN Power a dependable answer to small business backup power requirements across Southeast Asian village retailers and Middle East infrastructure contractors alike.

Five Core Product Advantages
① SDEC 4ZT3.2-G11 Four-Cylinder Engine — 3.2L Turbocharged, Wet Cylinder Liner, B10 Over 11,500 Hours
JIUAN Power sources the SDEC 4ZT3.2-G11 dedicated generator engine directly from Shanghai SDEC, the same certified supply chain used across JIUAN Power's full SDEC genset range. The in-line four-cylinder four-stroke water-cooled architecture delivers bore×stroke 98×105mm, 3.2L total displacement, 18:1 compression ratio, turbocharged direct injection with mechanical governor at 1500RPM. Wet replaceable cylinder liners lower overhaul cost at major service intervals; standard cold-start covers down to -12°C, with an optional oil preheater rated to -25°C for winter-zone and highland field sites. Genuine Fleetguard service kit with 500-hour cycles supports a B10 service life exceeding 11,500 operating hours, confirming the CDS44KVA as a fuel-efficient prime power generator built for long-haul deployment.
② Certified Dual PRP/ESP Ratings for Continuous Prime and Emergency Standby Duty
JIUAN Power validates the CDS44KVA as an ISO8528 certified genset under two internationally defined operating modes. The 40KVA prime power (PRP) rating supports continuous full-load daily silent commercial backup power for convenience stores, township clinics, and mini workshops running extended operating shifts. The 44KVA standby power (ESP) rating activates on mains failure with a 10% short-time overload capacity to handle motor-start inrush from small pumps and air-handling equipment. Standard output is 400/230V three-phase four-wire, 50Hz, 0.8PF; factory-calibrated 60Hz/440V export variants cover North American and African grid specifications.
③ ≤69dB(A) All-Weather Soundproof Canopy — Residential and Commercial Noise Compliant
The soundproof canopy of this 44KVA SDEC diesel generator is pressed from 2.7mm cold-rolled steel with double-layer anti-rust epoxy primer for sustained outdoor deployment in salt-fog coastal zones, tropical monsoon regions, and dusty inland construction yards. Multi-grade flame-retardant acoustic panels, a composite muffler duct, and a purpose-sized industrial silencer bring broadband noise to ≤69dB(A) at 7 metres — meeting the noise ordinances of residential estates and commercial high streets. A widened single-side maintenance door and glass observation window allow full servicing without canopy panel removal.
④ Full-Copper Class H Alternator — Voltage Regulation ≤±1.2%, THD Below 3%
The vacuum-impregnated Class H single-bearing full-copper brushless synchronous alternator on the CDS44KVA maintains voltage regulation ≤±1.2% and total harmonic distortion below 3% under rated conditions — matching the power quality requirements of clinic diagnostic instruments, small monitoring server rooms, and micro telecom base stations. IP23 dust and moisture protection maintains stable output in damp basement installations and coastal environments. A factory-fitted moulded-case circuit breaker provides layered protection against overload, short-circuit, under-voltage, overspeed, and overtemperature.
⑤ Deepsea DSE6120 Controller with Built-in ATS Bay and Optional 4G Remote Monitoring
JIUAN Power installs the Deepsea DSE6120 bilingual LCD controller as standard on every silent diesel genset in this range, giving operators real-time visibility of voltage, current, lube oil pressure, coolant temperature, runtime hours, and fault codes from a single panel. Automated start on mains loss, timed cool-down shutdown on supply restoration, and fault alarm stop are hard-wired standard functions. The dedicated ATS bay inside the control cabinet accommodates a transfer switch for direct mains-to-genset switching; an optional 4G module enables mobile-phone remote monitoring for distributed unmanned field sites.

FAQ
Q: What load profile can the CDS44KVA handle continuously in a small clinic or pharmacy?
A: Clinics typically operate a combination of refrigeration compressors, diagnostic lighting, centrifuge motors, and networked computers. The CDS44KVA's 40KVA prime rating supports this combined load at 75–80% of capacity for all-day continuous duty, while the SDEC 4ZT3.2-G11 engine's 3–5 second auto-start via DSE6120 keeps service interruption short during unexpected outages. JIUAN Power recommends confirming total connected load with a site electrical assessment before final unit sizing.
Q: Is an external generator room required for pavement or courtyard installation?
A: No. The sealed canopy of this ISO8528 certified genset provides IP23 protection and ≤69dB(A) noise output, making pavement and open-courtyard placement of this compact silent generator viable under most commercial and residential noise regulations. The 200L base tank and canopy wall quick-fill port allow daily fuelling in place. Anchoring the unit to a concrete base plinth is the only site preparation needed for long-term stable outdoor deployment.
