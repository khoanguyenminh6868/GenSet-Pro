Overview
280KW Quarry and Industrial Prime Power — Perkins 2206C-E13TAG2 EUI G3 Electronic Governing
The CDP350KVA is Jiuan Generator's 350KVA Perkins diesel generator for quarry operations, aggregate processing, large manufacturing complexes, and industrial parks where 280KW ISO8528 PRP prime power must run continuously across production shifts. Powered by the Perkins 2206C-E13TAG2 with G3-class electronic governing and EUI fuel injection, the CDP350KVA delivers 280KW/350KVA prime and 320KW/400KVA standby — housed in a 4300×1600×2250mm, 3500kg installation for dedicated generator room and containerized site deployment.
The Perkins 2206C-E13TAG2 genset engine is a six-cylinder, turbocharged and air-to-air intercooled diesel with 12.5L displacement, 130mm × 157mm bore and stroke, and 16.3:1 compression ratio. Electronic unit injection (EUI) with G3-class electronic governing provides steady-state frequency regulation within ±0.25% — a tighter tolerance than mechanical governor systems — and transient frequency recovery in under 10 seconds following a 60% step load change. This frequency precision protects variable-speed drives and motor protection relays at quarry crushing and screening circuits from nuisance trips during production cycles.
The 280KW industrial prime generator CDP350KVA brushless synchronous alternator delivers stable 400V/50Hz three-phase supply with voltage regulation within ±1% and THD below 1.5% — within the harmonic tolerance of crusher drives, PLC control panels, and power factor correction banks. The alternator's transient load capability handles the inrush currents from direct-on-line crusher motor starts — a high-frequency operational event at quarry and aggregate sites during shift start cycles. EU Stage II / EPA Tier 2 certified.
The 350KVA quarry power generator CDP350KVA is available in open-frame, super silent canopy (≤75dB(A) at 7m), and weatherproof containerized configurations. The containerized option delivers rainproof, dustproof, and corrosion-resistant enclosure performance for open quarry faces, aggregate stockpile areas, and remote off-grid mineral processing locations. The large-capacity integrated fuel tank supports 10-12 hours of continuous full prime load operation — covering a complete production shift without mid-shift refueling interruption.
The Perkins 2200 series genset CDP350KVA 2206C-E13TAG2 engine is supported by Perkins-authorized service centres in Africa, the Middle East, South Asia, Southeast Asia, and Latin America — the primary quarry and mineral extraction regions. Planned maintenance follows 500-hour oil and filter service intervals, aligned with the periodic shutdown cycles at aggregate plant and quarry sites. Anti-vibration mounts on the heavy-duty steel base frame reduce structural transmission. Jiuan Generator provides an 18-month or 2,000-hour warranty on each CDP350KVA unit.

5 Core Competitive Advantages
1. G3 Electronic Governing — ±0.25% Steady-State Frequency for Industrial VFD Circuits
The 280KW industrial prime generator CDP350KVA Perkins 2206C-E13TAG2 G3 electronic governor holds steady-state frequency within ±0.25% and recovers from transient deviation in under 10 seconds following a 60% step load — protecting variable-speed drives on conveyor feeds, crusher circuits, and industrial processing equipment from frequency excursions that trigger overcurrent protection.
2. EUI Fuel Injection — Precise Load Response Across Variable Industrial Loads
The Perkins 2206C-E13TAG2 genset EUI (Electronic Unit Injection) system precisely meters fuel delivery according to real-time engine load and speed demand — reducing smoke transients at crusher motor starts and improving fuel economy during partial-load intervals between production cycles, compared to mechanically governed engines of equivalent displacement.
3. 14.3% Standby Headroom — Inrush Buffer Above Prime Rating
The 350KVA Perkins diesel generator CDP350KVA 320KW/400KVA standby rating provides 14.3% power headroom above the 280KW prime rating — sufficient to absorb the inrush current of a direct-on-line crusher or large compressor motor start without underfrequency tripping or voltage sag that interrupts other running loads at the quarry site.
4. 10-12 Hour Tank Autonomy — Full Production-Shift Coverage Without Refueling
The 350KVA quarry power generator CDP350KVA large-capacity integrated fuel tank covers a full production shift on a single fill — eliminating mid-shift refueling stops at remote quarry faces, aggregate plants, and off-grid mineral processing sites where tanker access during operating hours disrupts production.
5. 500-Hour Service Cycle — Aligned with Quarry Maintenance Schedules
The Perkins 2200 series genset CDP350KVA 2206C-E13TAG2 engine follows 500-hour oil and filter service intervals — a cadence that integrates with the periodic maintenance shutdowns at quarry, mine, and aggregate plant sites. Genuine Perkins 2200 series parts are globally stocked, minimizing lead time for both scheduled and emergency maintenance.

FAQ
Q: What is the difference between the CDP350KVA prime and standby ratings?
A: The 350KVA Perkins diesel generator CDP350KVA is rated 280KW/350KVA ISO8528 PRP prime — certified for continuous operation with no annual hour restriction, making it the correct class for quarry and industrial prime power. The 320KW/400KVA standby rating adds 14.3% headroom for motor inrush events, but standby mode is reserved for emergency grid-backup with restricted annual running hours.
Q: Can the CDP350KVA handle a direct-on-line crusher motor start?
A: The 350KVA quarry power generator CDP350KVA 320KW/400KVA standby headroom and G3 electronic governor provide frequency and voltage stability during direct-on-line motor starts. For a 90-110KW crusher motor starting DOL, the genset absorbs the 5-7× inrush current within its transient capability without underfrequency tripping or disrupting other equipment running at the quarry site.
Q: Where can Perkins 2206C-E13TAG2 service parts be sourced at remote quarry sites?
A: The Perkins 2200 series genset CDP350KVA uses parts available at Perkins-authorized distributors in Africa, the Middle East, South Asia, and Southeast Asia — the primary quarry and mineral extraction regions. Injectors, belts, filters, and cooling components are locally stocked, supporting timely maintenance without extended supply-chain delays at remote operational sites.
