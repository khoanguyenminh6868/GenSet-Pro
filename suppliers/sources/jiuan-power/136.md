Overview
Critical Facility Emergency Standby Power with 357KVA Inrush Headroom
The CDP357KVA is Jiuan Generator's 357KVA Perkins diesel generator configured for critical facility emergency standby — hospitals, high-rise commercial buildings, data centres, and industrial plants where grid failure triggers an immediate ATS transfer across a full facility load. Rated at 285KW/357KVA ISO8528 ESP standby and 245KW/306KVA prime, the CDP357KVA provides a 16% standby headroom above its prime rating — a margin specifically sized for the combined inrush current demands of elevator drives, AHU fans, chiller compressors, and fire pump motors that energise simultaneously during emergency transfer switching.
The Perkins 1706A-E93TAG1 genset engine is a six-cylinder, four-stroke, water-cooled unit with 9.3L displacement, 115mm × 149mm bore and stroke, and a 16.5:1 compression ratio. It uses electronic high-pressure common-rail fuel injection with an electronic governor, delivering fast load-step response during ATS transfer events. Fuel consumption at 1500 RPM (50Hz): 58.5 L/h at 100% standby load, 42.8 L/h at 75% load, 29.3 L/h at 50% load. Cold start: -15°C unassisted. EU Stage II / EPA Tier 2 certified for global market deployment.
The brushless synchronous alternator holds ±1% voltage regulation and THD below 5% — power quality critical for protecting medical imaging equipment, IT server infrastructure, and BMS building management systems from voltage transients during load transfer. For the 285KW standby generator set CDP357KVA, Jiuan Generator fits a Deep Sea DSE7320 or SmartGen HGM6120 controller with AMF auto-start, ATS transfer switching, per-phase voltage and current monitoring, and comprehensive fault protection covering overload, low oil pressure, high coolant temperature, overspeed, and undervoltage. Optional 4G remote monitoring enables centralised alarm management across multi-building estate installations.
Three enclosure formats are available for the 357KVA emergency power generator CDP357KVA. The open-frame format with reinforced steel base and anti-vibration mounts suits dedicated generator rooms in commercial buildings and facilities with structural plant rooms. The super silent canopy reduces radiated noise to ≤75dB(A) at 7 meters for urban hospitals, campus installations, and office buildings where local noise standards apply. The containerized format provides weatherproof protection for rooftop, basement external plant, or car park deck deployment without a fixed generator building. The 500L integrated fuel tank supports 8+ hours of standby operation without manual refuelling.
The Perkins 1700 TAG1 generator 1706A-E93TAG1 engine is rated for 500-hour planned oil service intervals — an extended cycle that suits critical facility standby generators which typically operate only during weekly test exercises and actual grid-failure events. Genuine Perkins 1706A-E93TAG1 parts — filter kits, belts, and injectors — are available through Perkins' global distribution network in over 180 countries. Jiuan Generator assembles each unit to ISO 9001 procedures and conducts a full-load factory burn-in test before dispatch; 18-month or 2,000-hour warranty is included.

5 Core Competitive Advantages
1. 16% Standby Headroom Above Prime — Motor Inrush Absorption at ATS Transfer
The 357KVA Perkins diesel generator CDP357KVA standby rating (285KW/357KVA) is 16.3% above its prime rating (245KW/306KVA). This proportional margin provides the kVA capacity to absorb simultaneous motor inrush currents from elevator drives, AHU fans, and fire pump motors at the moment of ATS transfer switching — each drawing 5–7× their running current at start — without frequency collapse or overload trip.
2. Common-Rail Electronic Injection — Fast Load-Step Recovery for Full Facility Transfer
The Perkins 1706A-E93TAG1 genset engine uses common-rail high-pressure injection with an electronic governor. When the ATS transfers full facility load in a single step, common-rail's sub-cycle fuel delivery response minimises voltage dip duration and speed recovery time — critical for medical equipment and IT infrastructure that are sensitive to supply voltage transients during emergency transfer events.
3. DSE7320 AMF Controller — Under-10-Second Emergency Auto-Start
The Deep Sea DSE7320 controller on the 285KW standby generator set CDP357KVA monitors grid voltage and frequency independently on each phase. On grid loss, AMF auto-start initiates and the generator reaches operating speed and completes ATS transfer within 10 seconds — meeting the emergency power start-time requirements for hospitals, high-rise fire safety systems, and data centre generator-backed UPS installations.
4. 500L Tank — Extended Autonomy for Multi-Event Grid Failures
The 357KVA emergency power generator CDP357KVA carries a 500L integrated fuel tank. At 42.8 L/h at 75% standby load (a typical commercial building emergency load level), this covers approximately 11.7 hours of standby operation per fill — sufficient for an overnight grid-failure event without manual refuelling intervention at the site.
5. 500-Hour Service Interval — Efficient Maintenance for Standby-Duty Generators
The Perkins 1700 TAG1 generator 1706A-E93TAG1 engine's 500-hour service interval means that a critical facility standby generator running 2 hours per week on test exercises reaches its first planned service after approximately 5 years of normal standby duty — significantly reducing the maintenance cost of keeping a large standby generator set in certified operational readiness.
