Overview
320KW Hospital and Medical Campus Critical ATS Emergency Standby — Cummins 6ZTAA13-G2, 192g/kWh, PICC-Insured
The CDC400KVA is Jiuan Generator's 400KVA Cummins diesel generator for large general hospitals, specialist medical centers, and university medical campus complexes requiring sub-10-second ATS emergency standby power for operating theaters, ICU life support, and medical imaging equipment. Powered by the genuine DCEC Dongfeng Cummins 6ZTAA13-G2 engine, the unit delivers 320KW/400KVA ISO8528 PRP prime and 352KW/440KVA standby, with CE and ISO8528 certification for international healthcare facility procurement compliance.
The Cummins 6ZTAA13-G2 genset CDC400KVA delivers inline six-cylinder turbocharged and intercooled power with 13L displacement, 130×163mm bore and stroke, 17:1 compression ratio, Holset turbocharger, and PD high-pressure mechanical pump with GAC electronic governing. Engine certified standby output reaches 365KW at 1500RPM. Fuel consumption of 192-194g/kWh at full prime load delivers class-leading economy for hospital generator periodic load exercise cycles and planned commissioning tests.
The 352KW standby power generator CDC400KVA canopy is built from 3mm cold-rolled steel with double-layer epoxy zinc-rich anti-rust coating and multi-layer flame-retardant acoustic foam, rated ≤72dB(A) at 7m — meeting hospital campus and residential zone environmental regulations. The 950L integrated base fuel tank supports over 8 continuous full-load running hours during extended grid outages, and Fleetguard triple-filter assembly extends the 400-hour maintenance interval for hospital planned shutdown schedules.
The 400KVA silent diesel generator CDC400KVA all-copper brushless H-class alternator delivers ±1% steady-state voltage regulation and THD <3%, providing medical-grade clean power for hospital MRI and CT scanning rooms, ICU ventilators and patient monitoring systems, operating theater precision instruments, and pharmacy automation equipment sensitive to voltage transients. The DeepSea DSE7320 controller provides auto-start within 3-6 seconds of grid failure — meeting international hospital backup power response requirements for life-critical equipment.
The Cummins 400KVA industrial genset CDC400KVA carries a factory-registered ESN (Engine Serial Number) and is load-bank tested at 0%-25%-50%-75%-full prime load and 110% overload before shipment. Test video and PDF load report accompany every unit. Warranty is underwritten by PICC (People's Insurance Company of China), providing certified procurement documentation for hospital authority approval, with air-freight core parts delivered worldwide in 3-5 days via the official Cummins after-sales network.
