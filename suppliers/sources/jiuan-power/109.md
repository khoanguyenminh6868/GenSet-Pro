Description
This 710KVA Cummins KTAA19-G6A diesel generator set features 568KW/710KVA prime power and 640KW/800KVA standby power, driven by an original CCEC Chongqing Cummins KTAA19-G6A inline six-cylinder engine, matched with a Class H insulation brushless synchronous alternator and a Deepsea intelligent control panel.
As a premier industrial diesel generator, the Cummins KTAA19-G6A generator set complies with the original Cummins factory power calibration standard. It serves as a highly reliable heavy duty power generator, capable of running continuously at full load for 24 hours as a prime power supply, or starting up instantly as an emergency standby power unit once utility power fails.
Widely deployed in mining sites, heavy industry factories, commercial complexes, grade-A hospitals, oil & gas stations, IDC data rooms, infrastructure construction, and remote off-grid mining areas worldwide, this industrial power supply unit delivers uncompromising performance. Benefiting from a mature KTAA19-G6A engine design, this 710KVA Cummins KTAA19-G6A diesel generator set stands out for its low fuel consumption, outstanding durability, and ultra-low failure rate. Whether you need a continuous prime power generator or a responsive emergency standby power system, this model has become a top choice for global industrial projects and overseas engineering procurement of Cummins diesel generators.

Core Six Advantages
Genuine CCEC KTAA19-G6A Engine with Ample Power Reserve
Power core adopts authentic CCEC Cummins KTAA19-G6A, inline 6-cylinder four-stroke with 18.9L large displacement, equipped with Holset turbocharger & air-to-air intercooler plus exclusive Cummins PT-EFC precision fuel injection system, rated at 1500RPM, with sufficient power margin for 568KW prime and 640KW standby output. No frequency drop or voltage dip under sudden heavy load spike after millions of global field tests. Spare parts of KTAA19-G6A are globally available for convenient aftersales maintenance. 13.9:1 compression ratio and wet replaceable cylinder liner ensure excellent heat resistance and abrasion resistance, maintaining full 710KVA rated output under 50℃ high ambient temperature or high altitude conditions.
Dual-Rated 710KVA Design for Prime & Standby Dual Usage
Prime Rating 710KVA(568KW): Comply with GB/T2820 & ISO8528 standard for non-stop full-load daily operation, ideal for long-term production power of factory and continuous mining operation; Standby Rating 800KVA(640KW): Fast startup upon blackout with 10% short-time overload capacity to handle inrush starting current of large equipment and avoid production loss from power cut. Standard 400/230V three-phase four-wire, 50Hz, PF0.8, customized 60Hz version available to fit power grid standard of Middle East, Africa, Southeast Asia and European countries.
Low Fuel Consumption to Cut Long-Term Operation Cost
Optimized combustion chamber of KTAA19-G6A reduces full-load fuel consumption down to ≤212g/kW·h, 6%~9% more fuel-saving than same-class domestic competitors. Integral base large fuel tank supports over 7 continuous running hours at full load to cut frequent refueling labor cost. Original Fleetguard triple filter system (air/fuel/lube oil) blocks impurities effectively and extends filter replacement cycle by 30%, greatly reducing regular maintenance expenditure.
Premium Brushless Alternator Delivers Clean & Safe Power Output
Single-bearing brushless self-excited synchronous alternator with Class H insulation & IP23 protection grade, dustproof, moisture-proof and anti-corrosion for open-air mine and coastal oilfield working environment. Steady voltage regulation ≤±1%, transient voltage deviation ≤±15%, THD ＜ 3% to safeguard precision medical device, PLC control system and server equipment. Standard molded case circuit breaker integrates overload, short-circuit, under-voltage and overspeed multi-protection to prevent unexpected unit burnout.
Intelligent Auto Control for 24H Unattended Operation
Factory-fitted Deepsea7220 LCD controller (Smartgen/ComAp optional) with bilingual English-Chinese display, real-time monitoring of voltage, current, frequency, coolant/oil temp, oil pressure, rotate speed, runtime and fault code. Functions include auto start/stop, auto switch when mains loss, auto shutdown after power recovery and fault alarm. Optional ATS automatic transfer switch and remote cloud monitoring module enable remote start-stop & data check via PC or mobile phone, perfect for modern unattended power station project.
Multiple Customized Structure for All Harsh Working Conditions
Heavy-duty steel welded skid with integrated vibration dampers to minimize runtime vibration; four optional types: open frame, silent canopy, weather-proof containerized, mobile trailer mounted. Silent version noise ≤75dB(A)@7m to meet urban noise limit for hospital & residential area. Wide working ambient from -25℃ to +50℃; customized high-altitude kit including enlarged radiator & high-efficiency air filter available for sites up to 4000m elevation.
