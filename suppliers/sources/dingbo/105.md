Introduction:
The 32kW silent diesel generator set from Dingbo Power is a compact yet powerful energy solution engineered for applications where noise control and power reliability are equally critical. Designed with a robust 2mm galvanized steel soundproof enclosure and advanced acoustic insulation, this generator delivers prime power output at whisper-quiet operation levels — as low as 75 dB(A) measured from 7 meters away.
As a professional diesel generator set manufacturer with nearly 20 years of production experience, Dingbo Power is an authorized OEM partner for globally renowned engine and alternator brands including Cummins, Perkins, Deutz, Weichai, Shangchai, Yuchai, Stamford, and Leroy-Somer. We offer fully customizable solutions tailored to your specific application scenarios and budget requirements.
Advantages:
1. Ultra-Quiet Operation — The 32kw 40kva silent genset features a fully enclosed soundproof canopy lined with 5cm high-density sound-absorbing foam, effectively reducing noise to ≤75 dB at 7 meters. This makes it ideal for residential neighborhoods, hospitals, hotels, schools, and other noise-sensitive environments.
2. Durable Galvanized Steel Enclosure — Constructed from 2mm galvanized steel, the enclosure offers superior resistance to corrosion, rust, and fading. The heavy-duty design ensures long service life even in harsh outdoor conditions, including high humidity, dust, and rain exposure.
3. Extended Runtime with 8-Hour Fuel Tank — The integrated base fuel tank provides up to 8 hours of continuous operation at full load, minimizing downtime and reducing the need for frequent refueling during extended power outages or remote operations.
4. Multiple Engine & Alternator Options — As an authorized OEM partner for well-known engines and alternators such as Cummins, Perkins , Deutz, Weichai, Shanghai, Yuchai, Stamford , and Leroy-Somer , Dingbo offers flexible configuration options to suit different performance requirements and budgets-.
5. User-Friendly Maintenance — The canopy features wide-opening access doors for easy inspection, servicing, and parts replacement. All service points are conveniently located for routine maintenance access.
