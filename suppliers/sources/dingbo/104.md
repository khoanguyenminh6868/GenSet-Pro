Dingbo's set type DB-32GF pairs a DCEC Cummins 4BT3.9-G1 engine with a 40 kVA alternator, giving a continuous electrical output of 32 kW at the standard 0.8 power factor, 50 Hz, 400/230 V three-phase. It sits in the smaller end of Dingbo's Cummins-powered range , between the 25 kVA and 62.5 kVA models, and is typically specified for shops, telecom cabins, small workshops and single-building backup where a 32 kW running load is a reasonable fit.
The Dingbo DB-32GF 40 kVA Cummins Diesel Generator is powered by a DCEC Cummins 4BT3.9-G1 diesel engine and delivers a published electrical output of 32kW/40kVA at 50Hz, 400/230V and 0.8 power factor. This four-cylinder, turbocharged diesel generator is available in open-type and soundproof configurations for commercial backup power, small industrial equipment and site power applications.

Generator Output vs. Cummins Engine Power
The DB-32GF's published generator output is 32kW/40kVA. The Cummins 4BT3.9-G1 engine has separate mechanical ratings of 36kW prime and 40kW standby. These engine ratings must not be interpreted as the complete generator set's electrical standby output.
For standby applications, request confirmation of the complete generator set's applicable emergency standby rating. Engine reserve power and alternator short-term overload capability do not independently determine whether a generator can start a large electric motor. Motor starting performance must be evaluated using the starting current, starting method, allowable voltage dip and alternator characteristics.
Is a 40 kVA Cummins Generator Suitable for Your Load?
A 40kVA generator rated at 0.8 power factor provides 32kW of published electrical output. Before ordering, check the connected running load, starting demand and operating duty rather than selecting solely by the total equipment nameplate capacity.
| | Selection Factor | What to Check | Recommendation
| Running Load | Total simultaneous kW and kVA demand | Confirm that the operating load remains within the applicable generator rating.
| Prime or Standby Duty | Regular power generation or emergency backup operation | Specify the intended duty so the correct genset PRP or ESP rating can be confirmed.
| Motor Starting | Starting current, starting method and permitted voltage dip | Provide motor nameplate data for pumps, compressors, fans and other high-inrush equipment.
| Voltage and Frequency | Required voltage, 50Hz/60Hz and phase | Confirm compatibility with the electrical equipment and destination market.
| Operating Environment | Altitude, ambient temperature, ventilation and installation location | Check cooling conditions and potential generator derating.
| Future Expansion | Planned additional equipment and future load growth | Consider a higher-capacity model if the expected demand approaches the generator's usable rating.

40 kVA Cummins Diesel Generator Purchasing FAQs
1. Is the DB-32GF a 32kW or 40kW generator?
The complete generator set has a published electrical output of 32kW/40kVA at 0.8 power factor. The Cummins 4BT3.9-G1 engine has separate mechanical prime and standby ratings of 36kW and 40kW. These engine figures are not the complete generator set's electrical output ratings.
2. What is the standby output of this 40kVA Cummins generator?
The published generator set output is 32kW/40kVA. Its engine standby power is stated as 40kW, but a separate complete generator set ESP rating must be confirmed in the final technical datasheet. Buyers should specify whether the generator is intended for prime or emergency standby service before ordering.
3. Can this generator start a pump, compressor or other electric motor?
Motor starting suitability depends on starting current, starting method, permitted voltage dip, alternator excitation and engine response. Provide the motor power, starting arrangement and other loads operating simultaneously. Dingbo can review whether the DB-32GF is suitable or whether a larger generator or different starting system is required.
4. Can I order the DB-32GF in 60Hz or a different voltage?
Yes, compatible alternative electrical configurations can be specified. Available options include 60Hz and voltage arrangements such as 380/220V, 415/240V and 440/254V. The final engine speed, alternator selection and electrical output must be confirmed for the requested configuration. The published 50Hz data should not be assumed to remain unchanged.
5. Can I choose the alternator brand?
Yes. Dingbo lists Leroy-Somer, Stamford, Siemens, Marathon, ENGGA and KEPU among its available alternator options. Confirm your preferred brand and model during quotation. Different alternators may have different electrical characteristics, dimensions, excitation systems and prices.
6. What is the difference between the open and soundproof versions?
The published open version measures 1600 × 750 × 1200mm and weighs approximately 700kg. The soundproof version measures 1860 × 760 × 1300mm and weighs approximately 900kg. The soundproof configuration adds an enclosure, but its final noise performance should be confirmed against the required acoustic specifications and measurement conditions.
7. Can I request a custom steel enclosure and powder-coated finish?
Yes. Custom metal enclosure materials, panel thicknesses, protective coatings, colors and structural layouts can be specified. Provide the required material, coating performance, site exposure conditions and any drawings. Final dimensions and coating details will be confirmed during technical review.
8. Can the fuel tank support eight hours of operation?
An extended-runtime fuel tank can be configured according to the required operating hours and expected load. The published engine fuel consumption is 10L/h at 100% prime load and 5.9L/h at 50% prime load. The final tank capacity and usable runtime must account for the actual load, usable fuel volume and operating conditions. An eight-hour runtime is not a fixed standard specification without a confirmed tank arrangement.
9. Can I order the generator with ATS and remote monitoring?
Yes. Automatic control, ATS, remote operation and paralleling options are available. Provide the electrical system information and required control functions so compatible equipment can be selected. These options are not automatically included in every standard DB-32GF configuration.
10. What is the MOQ and delivery time for this model?
MOQ and delivery time depend on quantity, component selection and customization requirements. Provide your required quantity, destination country, preferred configuration and target delivery date. The applicable production schedule and commercial terms will be confirmed in the quotation.
11. Can Dingbo provide certificates and factory test documents?
Buyers can request available technical documentation, applicable certificate details and factory inspection information for the selected generator. Required destination-country compliance documents and special acceptance tests should be specified before order confirmation. The availability and scope of documents must be verified for the exact configuration being supplied.
