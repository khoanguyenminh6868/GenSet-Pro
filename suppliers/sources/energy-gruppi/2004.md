Self-supporting frame made of 2 mm electro-welded pickled steel combined with 1.5 mm soundproof casing to ensure structural strength.
The RAL6017 textured polyester powder coating (70–80 microns) ensures protection and aesthetic quality. 250-liter internal fuel tank with spill containment basin.
Sound-absorbing, water-repellent, and oil-resistant polyurethane lining compliant with EN13501-1 Class B. Integrated muffler with noise reduction of up to 35 dB(A).
Four padlockable doors, a side-mounted electrical panel with a viewing window, efficient ventilation, two eye bolts, and mounting feet complete the system.

CONTROL UNIT
COMAP AMF8
The AMF8 InteliLite is ideal for automating generator sets in both emergency and continuous operation.
The 128x64-pixel backlit graphic display allows for quick parameter management.
It operates at 8–36 VDC and can handle short-term power outages.
It precisely controls three-phase voltage and current.
It tracks operating hours and logged events, which are essential for maintenance.
The built-in protections trigger alarms and automatically shut down the generator set in the event of a fault.
The control unit can be expanded using additional plug-in modules.
ELECTRICAL PANEL
AUTOMATIC
Designed for the INDUSTRIAL line, the EY-150P is synonymous with reliability and durability even in challenging environments. The IP44-certified automatic control panel, made of painted steel, features a door with a viewing window and a lock for enhanced security. The ComAp AMF8 control unit offers advanced control, while the terminal block and 4-pole circuit breaker ensure high electrical efficiency. The system is completed by a battery charger and an emergency button for comprehensive protection.
