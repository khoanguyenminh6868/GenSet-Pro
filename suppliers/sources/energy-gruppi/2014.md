The structure features a self-supporting frame made of electro-welded carbon steel sheet with a minimum thickness of 6 mm and a soundproof steel enclosure with a thickness ranging from 2 to 9 mm, ensuring high structural strength and long-term reliability.
The RAL6017 exterior polyester powder coating with a textured finish (70–80 microns) ensures protection against the elements and a professional appearance. The 400-liter internal fuel tank, complete with a containment basin, guarantees ample range and safety in fuel management.
The interior is lined with sound-absorbing polyurethane featuring a water- and oil-repellent finish, compliant with EN13501-1 Class B standards. The residential-style muffler, mounted externally on the housing, reduces noise by up to 35 dB(A).
Four doors with padlockable locks provide convenient and secure access, while the electrical panel, located on the long side, is protected by a dedicated door with a viewing window. The ventilation system manages the intake of ambient air and the exhaust of hot air; lifting is facilitated by four eyebolts, and the pre-installed feet allow for stable anchoring to the ground.

CONTROL UNIT
COMAP AMF25
The ComAp AMF-25 is a control unit designed for automatic generator sets, with a front panel measuring 210x150 mm. It features front-panel buttons for simple and intuitive navigation. The backlit LCD display ensures clear and immediate readability, while the 12 or 24 VDC power supply is protected against polarity reversal, ensuring reliability.
The control unit monitors key parameters such as voltage (L1, L2, L3), frequency, apparent power, current, engine RPM, battery voltage, etc. It stores up to 350 events and allows for the management of maintenance intervals, facilitating diagnostics and operational planning.
It includes alarms for high engine temperature, low oil pressure, low fuel level, voltage or frequency anomalies, failed startup, exceeding maximum current, etc. Each alarm can be configured to trigger engine shutdown.
Up to two optional modules for remote control can be added, increasing the device’s versatility.
