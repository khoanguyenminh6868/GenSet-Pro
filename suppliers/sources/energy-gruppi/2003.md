Robust construction thanks to a 2.5 mm electro-welded pickled steel self-supporting frame and 1.5 mm soundproof enclosure.
The RAL6017 polyester powder coating (70–80 microns) protects against the elements and ensures a professional appearance. 120-liter fuel tank with spill containment basin.
Internal sound-absorbing polyurethane insulation compliant with EN13501-1 Class B. Integrated muffler with noise reduction of up to 35 dB(A).
Three padlockable doors, an electrical panel on the long side with a viewing window, ventilation, two eye bolts, and pre-installed feet complete the configuration.

CONTROL UNIT
COMAP AMF8
The ComAp AMF8 control unit is designed for safe and continuous control of generators, even under critical conditions.
It features a clear, backlit 128x64-pixel display.
It operates on an 8 to 36 VDC power supply and can withstand brief power outages.
It performs accurate monitoring of three-phase parameters for both the generator and the grid.
It records operating hours and event history, improving maintenance.
With active alarms for overvoltage, abnormal frequencies, and low fuel, it triggers automatic shutdown.
Its structure is modular and customizable.
ELECTRICAL PANEL
AUTOMATIC
The EY-100F model, part of the INDUSTRIAL Line, was developed to offer durability and reliability in demanding working conditions. The IP44-certified painted steel enclosure features a door with a viewing window and a lock for added protection. The ComAp AMF8 control unit, integrated into the automatic system, provides advanced control, while the high-capacity terminal block and 4-pole circuit breaker ensure safe electrical operation. A battery charger and an emergency stop button complete the system, offering total protection.
