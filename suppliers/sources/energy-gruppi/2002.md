Robust structure with a self-supporting frame made of 2.5 mm electro-welded pickled steel and a 1.5 mm soundproof enclosure.
The exterior RAL6017 finish, made of textured polyester powder coating (70–80 microns), ensures durability and aesthetic quality. 100-liter fuel tank with a spill containment basin.
Internal sound-absorbing polyurethane insulation compliant with EN13501-1 Class B, water-repellent, and oil-resistant. Integrated muffler with a noise reduction of 35 dB(A).
Two padlockable doors, an electrical panel with a viewing window, optimized ventilation, eyebolts, and mounting feet ensure practicality and safety.

CONTROL UNIT
COMAP AMF8
The ComAp AMF8 control unit is a complete solution for controlling generator sets, whether in backup or continuous operation.
The 128x64-pixel backlit display clearly shows key data.
It supports voltages from 8 to 36 VDC, with tolerance for brief dropouts of up to 100 ms.
It accurately monitors voltage and current on the three phases of the grid and the generator.
It offers logging functions, including event logs and operating hours.
It features built-in protections against electrical faults and low fuel levels, triggering automatic shutdown.
Modular via plug-ins, it easily adapts to different configurations.

AUTOMATIC

CASE HISTORY
INDUSTRIAL EY-60P-SA Generator Set for Powering a Crane at a Construction Site in the Historic District
Energy supplied an INDUSTRIAL EY-60P-SA generator set configured with dedicated outlets and a super-immunized ground-fault circuit interrupter (GFCI) to power a crane at a construction site in the historic district. Thanks to this configuration, the generator set ensures a reliable power supply and reduces the risk of outages caused by electrical disturbances, ensuring greater operational continuity and minimizing downtime during construction activities.
ENERGY recommends
These generating sets are designed to power any type of equipment, within the generator’s power limits.
Although the soundproof enclosure is suitable for outdoor use, for fixed installations, we recommend installing a canopy if the generating set is not placed in a dedicated room.
The 1,500 rpm models are suitable for continuous operation. For extended use lasting several days or more, we recommend consulting our technical staff to ensure you select the appropriate power rating.
