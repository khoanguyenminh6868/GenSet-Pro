The unit features a self-supporting frame made of 2.5 mm electro-welded pickled steel and a soundproof enclosure made of 1.5 mm steel, ensuring durability and long-term reliability.
The exterior finish consists of a textured RAL6017 polyester powder coating (70–80 microns), which protects against the elements and enhances the unit’s aesthetic appeal. The 100-liter tank with a spill containment basin ensures operational safety.
Inside, the sound-absorbing polyurethane—treated to be water- and oil-repellent—is certified to EN13501-1 Class B. The integrated muffler reduces noise by up to 35 dB(A).
Two doors with padlockable locks provide convenient access, while the side-mounted electrical panel is protected by a door with a viewing window. An optimized ventilation system, lifting eyes, and mounting feet complete the system.

CONTROL UNIT
LOVATO RGK420SA
The Lovato RGK420SA is a compact control unit with a 110x110 mm front panel, ideal for stand-alone generator sets. It features 3 front buttons and a 3-position mechanical key (OFF-ON-REM), which can be removed in the OFF and REM positions. The backlit LCD display ensures clear readability, while the 12- or 24-VDC power supply is protected against reverse polarity.
The control unit monitors voltage (L1, L2, L3), frequency, apparent power, current, engine RPM, and battery voltage. It stores the last 40 events and manages maintenance intervals.
It includes alarms for high engine temperature, low oil pressure, low fuel level, voltage or frequency anomalies, failed start, and maximum current exceeded, with automatic engine shutdown for protection.

CONTROL UNIT
COMAP AMF8
Designed for automation and safety, the InteliLite AMF8 control unit provides reliable control of generators, whether in standby or continuous mode.
The 128x64-pixel graphic display is backlit, ensuring easy readability in all conditions.
It accepts a power supply between 8 and 36 VDC and provides protection against brief power interruptions (up to 100 ms).
It offers accurate monitoring of three-phase voltage and current for both the utility grid and the generator set.
It logs significant events and operating hours, simplifying maintenance management.
In the event of electrical or operational anomalies, it triggers alarms and automatically shuts down the generator set.
Thanks to plug-in expansion modules, it easily adapts to complex systems.
ELECTRICAL PANEL
MANUAL
The EY-30P model, part of the INDUSTRIAL line, was developed to deliver reliable performance thanks to a sturdy, IP44-certified painted sheet metal control panel. The Lovato RGK420SA control unit, integrated into the manual control panel, enables constant monitoring of the system. The CEE outlets and protective devices, such as circuit breakers and fuses, ensure a high level of electrical safety, with the emergency stop button ready for rapid intervention.
