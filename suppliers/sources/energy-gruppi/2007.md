Extremely durable construction thanks to the self-supporting frame made of electro-welded carbon steel with a minimum thickness of 4 mm and a soundproof enclosure with a thickness of 2–9 mm.
The RAL6017 finish, applied with textured polyester powder coating (70–80 microns), ensures protection against external elements and a professional appearance. The 400-liter fuel tank with a spill containment basin ensures safety and operational autonomy.
Sound-absorbing polyurethane interior lining compliant with EN13501-1 Class B. Integrated muffler with noise reduction of up to 35 dB(A).
Four padlockable doors, a side-mounted electrical panel with a viewing window, ventilation, four eye bolts, and mounting feet complete the unit.

CONTROL UNIT
COMAP AMF25
The ComAp AMF-25 is a control unit designed for automatic generator sets, with a front panel measuring 210x150 mm. It features front-panel buttons for simple and intuitive navigation. The backlit LCD display ensures clear and immediate readability, while the 12 or 24 VDC power supply is protected against polarity reversal, ensuring reliability.
The control unit monitors key parameters such as voltage (L1, L2, L3), frequency, apparent power, current, engine RPM, battery voltage, etc. It stores up to 350 events and allows for the management of maintenance intervals, facilitating diagnostics and operational planning.
It includes alarms for high engine temperature, low oil pressure, low fuel level, voltage or frequency anomalies, failed startup, exceeding maximum current, etc. Each alarm can be configured to trigger engine shutdown.
Up to two optional modules for remote control can be added, increasing the device’s versatility.
ELECTRICAL PANEL
AUTOMATIC
The EY-350VO from the INDUSTRIAL line is the perfect choice for demanding conditions, featuring an automatic control panel made of painted sheet metal and certified to IP44. Efficiency is ensured by the high-capacity terminal block and the 4-pole circuit breaker, complemented by a battery charger and an emergency stop button.
