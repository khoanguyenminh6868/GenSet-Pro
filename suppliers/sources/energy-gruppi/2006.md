Robust structure with a self-supporting carbon steel frame of at least 3 mm and a soundproof enclosure with a thickness of 1.5–5.5 mm.
The RAL6017 exterior finish, applied with polyester powder coating (70–80 microns), ensures durability and aesthetic quality. 250-liter fuel tank with a spill containment basin.
Sound-absorbing polyurethane lining compliant with EN13501-1 Class B. Integrated muffler with a noise reduction of 35 dB(A).

CONTROL UNIT
COMAP AMF8
The InteliLite AMF8 is a compact and powerful automatic control unit, ideal for controlling generator sets in emergency or continuous mode.
Its 128x64-pixel backlit display ensures quick and intuitive viewing of parameters.
It supports power supplies between 8 and 36 VDC and protects the system from power dips lasting up to 100 ms.
It measures current and voltage with a maximum error of 2% on all phases, both on the utility side and the generator side.
It automatically logs operating hours and recorded events.
Equipped with a comprehensive alarm system, it ensures protection of the generator set in the event of electrical faults or insufficient fuel, with automatic shutdown.
Thanks to its modular design, the control unit can be customized with plug-ins to meet any industrial need.
ELECTRICAL PANEL
AUTOMATIC
Designed for the INDUSTRIAL Line, the EY-250S offers robust performance and safety in demanding environments. The IP44-certified automatic steel control panel, with a door featuring a viewing window and lock, ensures excellent protection. The ComAp AMF8 control unit enables precise control, while the 4-pole circuit breaker and terminal block ensure comprehensive protection. The battery charger and emergency stop button further enhance the system’s safety.
