Silent Diesel Generator
20–3000kVA · 70–75 dB(A) at 7m · Weatherproof soundproof canopy
A silent diesel generator is a diesel genset built inside a sound-attenuating canopy, so it can run close to people without breaking noise limits. Wiseke Power manufactures soundproof canopy gensets from 20kVA to 3000kVA, powered by Cummins, Perkins, Weichai, Yuchai, SDEC, Baudouin and MTU engines. Every unit is factory load-tested before shipment, supplied with a weatherproof enclosure rated for permanent outdoor installation, and every unit ships with a load test report. All sets meet ISO 9001 and CE standards. Typical sound pressure is 70–75 dB(A) at 7 metres, against 95 to 105 dB(A) at 1 metre for the same engine on an open skid.
Three Canopy Families: Small, Large and Containerised
Small Power Silent Gensets, 20–100kVA
Compact single or three-phase units for shops, telecom shelters, clinics and small factories. A 20kVA set fits inside a 1.8 metre canopy and can be moved by forklift, while a 100kVA unit still clears a standard 2.2 metre doorway. Engine options include Perkins 1103A, Weichai WP2.3 and SDEC, with a base fuel tank for 8 hours of running at 100% load.
Large Power Silent Gensets, 120–1000kVA
The workhorse range for hospitals, hotels, factories and standby power. Common platforms are the Cummins 6C and 6L engines, Perkins 1106 and 2806 series , Weichai and Yuchai blocks, coupled to brushless alternators and Deepsea, Smartgen or ComAp control panels. All units come with auto start, remote start contacts and an optional ATS for mains failure changeover. Sound pressure stays at 70–75 dB(A) at 7 metres across the whole range.
Containerised Silent Gensets, 500–3000kVA
For 500kVA and above, a 20ft or 40ft container gives you a walk-in service aisle, external fuel connections and better heat rejection than a skinned canopy. The container wall carries the same acoustic lining, so noise stays at 70 dB(A) at 7 metres, and the unit can be shipped as a finished power house. See MTU Series 2000 and 4000 or Baudouin engines for this power band, or compare all enclosure formats on the open type diesel generator page.
What Makes a Genset Silent?
The canopy is not a box with sound-absorbing cotton stuffed inside. It does four jobs at the same time. High-density flame-retardant acoustic cotton absorbs mid and high frequency noise. The air intake and exhaust paths turn at least twice, so sound cannot escape in a straight line. An industrial-grade muffler sits inside the canopy, with flexible bellows that stop engine vibration from reaching the steel panels. Finally, anti-vibration mounts under the engine and alternator cut the low frequency rumble that thin enclosures cannot absorb. Lockable access doors with compression seals and a 2mm powder-coated steel shell complete an enclosure for outdoor use.
Silent Genset Noise Levels: What dB to Expect
| Enclosure type | Genset size | Sound Pressure | Typical application
| Open type, no canopy | 20–3000kVA | 95–105 dB(A) @ 1m | Plant room, mining places, remote site
| Standard silent canopy | 20–1000kVA | 70–75 dB(A) @ 7m | Factory, warehouse, construction site
| Super-silent canopy | 20–600kVA | 65–69 dB(A) @ 7m | Hospital, hotel, residential area, office
| Containerised soundproof | 500–3000kVA | 70 dB(A) @ 7m | Data centre, large outdoor standby power
A dB figure means nothing without three things: the distance, the load condition and the measurement standard. Sound pressure drops roughly 6 dB every time you double the distance outdoors, and the dB scale is logarithmic, so each 10 dB step sounds about twice as loud to the human ear. Noise figures on this page are typical values for each enclosure type,taken at 7 metres in free field at 75% load. Every set is load-tested before shipment and ships with its own test report. Where a project needs certified data, a third-party noise test to ISO 8528-10 can bearranged before delivery.

Options That Change the Noise Number
Four options are worth deciding before you order. A residential silencer instead of the standard muffler typically drops 5 to 10 dB(A). An acoustic enclosure room built around the canopy takes another 10 dB, but needs airflow designed by an engineer. Anti-vibration spring mounts help most where the genset sits above living space or on a steel deck. And an ATS adds no noise, but decides whether the set starts by itself, so check the ATS selection guide for uninterrupted operation before you size the panel.
