Weichai Power Diesel Generator
18–3300kW
Description
These sets use Weichai’s WP and M series diesel engines. The WP range covers the small end — the 2.3-litre WP2 up to the 12.5-litre WP13 — and the M range takes over above 500 kW with 26, 33 and 55-litre blocks, six to twenty cylinders.
The tables below list 28 models at 50 Hz from 22 to 1,500 kVA, and 42 models at 60 Hz from 30 to 4,125 kVA. Every row shows both kVA and kW, so you can work in whichever unit your tender uses. Model numbers ending W1 are 50 Hz, W2 are 60 Hz.
Open skid, silent canopy and trailer versions are quoted on the same engine. See the genset range for the three build types, or the silent genset page for canopy dimensions and noise figures.
Engine series

60 Hz models use the same series with a W2 suffix. kVA shown at 0.8 power factor.
The block decides the duty. At the same output, a larger block runs at lower load per litre. For continuous prime duty, or a site with sustained high ambient temperature, quote the larger displacement at the same rating.
WP12 lists a single model. It exists for the buyer who wants 375 kVA on a larger block than the WP10 offers at that point.
Features
Global warranty, because the engine is genuine. Weichai factory warranty is one year or 1,500 working hours from the shipment date, whichever comes first, and it follows the engine serial number. The serial is written on your order confirmation, so a claim can be verified at any Weichai service point.
Load-tested to 110%. Every set runs through 50%, 75%, 100% and 110% load before it ships, and the test sheet travels with the unit.
Alternator options. Stamford, Marathon, Leroy Somer or a China-built alternator, quoted as one package with the engine, control panel and canopy.
Parts through the Weichai network. Filters, injectors and gaskets are stocked through the same commercial vehicle network that services the engines on the road, so replenishment does not depend on a single factory order.
ISO 9001 and CE. Sets are built and tested to ISO 9001 and CE, with IEC test data available on request.
