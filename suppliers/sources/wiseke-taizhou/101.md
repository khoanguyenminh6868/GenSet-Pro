Diesel Generator Powered by CCEC Cummins (220–1,650 kW)
Description
Each set is built around M, N, K and Q series diesel engines from Chongqing Cummins. CCEC supplies the engine; the alternator, control panel, skid and canopy are matched to it and confirmed on the order. Alternators are Stamford, Marathon or Leroy Somer,etc.
CCEC states a power range of 200 kW to 1,600 kW, and lists buildings, telecommunications, ships, construction and industrial power among its main applications. Chongqing Cummins is described by Cummins as the starting point for its local production in China and its largest plant for heavy-load, high-horsepower engines.
Cummins states a service network of more than 500 distributors and 5,200 dealers across 190 countries.
The tables below list 40 models at 50 Hz from 220 to 1,650 kW and 26 models at 60 Hz from 220 to 1,500 kW.
Below 220 kW the DCEC range takes over, from 18 kW.
Features
One rating, two or three blocks. From 280 kW upward the same output appears on more than one engine. WDL-330C1 is built on the NTA855 at 14 litres, the M15 at 15 litres, and the KTA19 at 19 litres.
The block decides the duty. The NTA855 has been in service longest, so parts coverage is widest. The KTA19 delivers the same output from 19 litres instead of 14, so each litre carries less load — the safer quote for continuous prime duty or for sites with sustained high ambient temperature.
60 Hz trims the top. The KTA50 reaches 1,650 kW at 50 Hz but 1,500 kW at 60 Hz.
Global warranty, because the engine is genuine. Cummins factory warranty follows the engine serial number, not the invoice. Any authorized Cummins service point, in any country, can look up that serial and honour the claim. A grey-market seller cannot make that promise, because the serial would give them away.
Every engine is new, and you can prove it. The engine carries a factory plate and a QR code. Scan it, match the serial against the number on your order confirmation, and the unit is confirmed as factory-new. No rebuilt units, no grey-market stock.
Container planning. The 1,500 and 1,650 kW sets measure 6,200 mm on the longest side, which will not fit a 20 ft container.
We keep the serial on file. Every unit we ship is logged by engine serial number. A warranty question three years later can be traced to the build and the delivery date, without your customer digging through paperwork.
