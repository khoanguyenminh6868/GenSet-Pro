Why Choose Cummins Diesel Generators?
Cummins is our widest-coverage engine brand, and the only one that supports both 50Hz and 60Hz across its entire power range — with identical engine production lead times for both frequencies, so a 60Hz order delivers no slower than a 50Hz one. From 16kW to 1,340kW, a single brand can match almost any standby or prime-power requirement.
Coverage comes from two Cummins product lines: DCEC (Dongfeng Cummins) for 16–400kW, and CCEC (Chongqing Cummins) for 200–1,340kW. Proven industrial engine series — B, C, L, Z plus N855, K19, K38 and K50 — keep parts readily available worldwide. Recommended oil: SAE 15W-40, API CH-4 / CI-4.
Every H&C Cummins generator set is built with a genuine Cummins engine paired with Marathon or Stamford alternators and Deepsea controllers. Available in 50Hz (1500 rpm) and 60Hz (1800 rpm) configurations, in open skid, soundproof canopy, and ISO container enclosures. Whether you need a 24kW standby unit for a telecom site or a 350kW prime power station, we configure every generator to your voltage, frequency, and site conditions.

Frequently Asked Questions — Cummins Generators
How much does a Cummins diesel generator cost? + The price of a Cummins diesel generator depends on the power rating, configuration (open skid, soundproof canopy , or containerized), frequency (50Hz/60Hz), and options such as ATS and alternator brand. Larger CCEC-powered sets (200–1,340kW) cost more than smaller DCEC-powered sets (16–400kW). Send the required kW, voltage and destination and we will return a Cummins factory-direct quotation within 24 hours.
What is the fuel consumption of a Cummins 100kW generator? + The Cummins 6BTA5.9-G2 (R-DC113D5/S, 100kW prime) consumes approximately 18.8 L/h at 75% load; at 100% load it rises to about 25.0 L/h. The 6BTA 5.9L engine achieves 211 g/kW·h — competitive for its class. See the full fuel-consumption table in the power range above.
What are the advantages of a Cummins diesel generator? + H&C builds Cummins generators from 16kW to 1,340kW in both 50Hz and 60Hz, using genuine DCEC and CCEC engines. H&C can also supply imported Cummins engines produced in non-China Cummins factories (such as the USA and Japan ), which meets tender documents that restrict the engine's country of origin. Every set is full-load tested before shipment, and we configure the voltage, frequency, and controls to your project.
What's the difference between DCEC and CCEC Cummins engines? + DCEC (Dongfeng Cummins) is a joint venture in Xiangyang, Hubei, producing B/C/L/Z series engines from 16kW to 400kW — the most common choice for small-to-medium generator sets. CCEC (Chongqing Cummins) produces the larger N855, K19, K38, and K50 engines from 200kW to 1,340kW. Both are genuine Cummins products with worldwide parts and service support. H&C integrates both engine families — tell us your power requirement and we'll recommend the right one.
What is the delivery time for a Cummins generator set? + Delivery is based on the engine. DCEC engines (16–400kW) typically ship within 4 weeks of order confirmation, and CCEC engines (200–1,340kW) within 4–6 weeks . Imported Cummins engines depend on stock availability in China; high-demand data-center ratings such as 1,600kW can take 6–8 months . Tell us the engine model and quantity and we will confirm the DCEC, CCEC or imported-Cummins lead time for your project.
