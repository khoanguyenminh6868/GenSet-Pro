How much does a 600kW diesel generator cost? + A 600kW diesel generator is priced by engine platform, configuration and alternator choice. The price moves with the enclosure (open skid, soundproof canopy or containerized), the alternator (Stamford S5L1D-H4 or Leroy-Somer TAL-A49-B for the R-CC750D5/S), the controller and options such as ATS, and with the destination country and freight terms.
