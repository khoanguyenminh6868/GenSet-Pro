Frequently Asked Questions — 120kW Diesel Generator
How much does a 120kW diesel generator cost? + A 120kW diesel generator is priced by engine platform, configuration and alternator choice. At this rating H&C builds 2 engine platforms (Cummins R-DC150D5/S , Perkins R-P150D5/S ), rated 120 kW / 150 kVA prime and 132 kW / 165 kVA standby at 50Hz (1500 rpm). The price moves with the enclosure (open skid, soundproof canopy or containerized), the alternator (Stamford UCI274F or Leroy-Somer TAL-A44-J for the R-DC150D5/S), the controller and options such as ATS, and with the destination country and freight terms. Send your voltage, frequency and destination country and we will confirm the R-DC150D5/S / R-P150D5/S price within 24 hours .
What is the fuel consumption of a 120kW diesel generator? + Fuel consumption for a 120kW diesel generator is 21.6- 21.8 L/h at 75% load and 28.9- 29.0 L/h at 100% load across the 2 platforms: Cummins R-DC150D5/S 21.8 / 29.0 L/h; Perkins R-P150D5/S 21.6 / 28.9 L/h . Measured efficiency is 202- 203 g/kW·h per ISO 3046-1, and actual consumption varies about ± 5% with ambient conditions, load profile and fuel quality. At a diesel price of $1.0/L, 75% load costs roughly $22-22 per hour.
