Frequently Asked Questions
Is H&C a factory-direct manufacturer or a trading company? + H&C is a factory-direct manufacturer, not a trading company. We own and operate our manufacturing facility in Jiangsu, China — manufacturing diesel generator sets since 1983 — and build every set in-house, from bases, enclosures, and control panels to final assembly and full-load testing. You buy directly from the people who build your generator.
What does H&C manufacture? + H&C manufactures diesel generator sets from 30kW to 2000kW, available with Cummins, Perkins, MTU, or Mitsubishi engines. Popular ratings include 100kW, 200kW, 500kW, and 1000kW.
Are your diesel generators certified and tested? + Every set is ISO 8528-5 certified and CE marked, and every unit undergoes a full-load test before shipment. We operate under ISO 9001 (quality) and ISO 14001 (environmental) management systems, and each generator ships with a signed factory test report.
Do you export worldwide? + H&C has been exporting diesel generator sets since 2003 and has delivered projects to 30+ countries across Asia, the Middle East, Africa, and beyond. We handle full export logistics — documentation, customs clearance, and ocean freight.
