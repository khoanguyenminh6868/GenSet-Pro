Frequently Asked Questions — 200kW Diesel Generator
How much does a 200kW diesel generator cost? + A 200kW diesel generator is priced by engine platform, configuration and alternator choice. At this rating H&C builds 2 engine platforms (Cummins R-CC250D5/S , Perkins R-P250D5/S ), rated 200 kW / 250 kVA prime and 220 kW / 275 kVA standby at 50Hz (1500 rpm). The price moves with the enclosure (open skid, soundproof canopy or containerized), the alternator (Stamford UCDI274K or Leroy-Somer TAL-A46-D for the R-CC250D5/S), the controller and options such as ATS, and with the destination country and freight terms. Send your voltage, frequency and destination country and we will confirm the R-CC250D5/S / R-P250D5/S price within 24 hours .
What is the fuel consumption of a 200kW diesel generator? + Fuel consumption for a 200kW diesel generator is 34.2- 36.8 L/h at 75% load and 45.7- 49.0 L/h at 100% load across the 2 platforms: Cummins R-CC250D5/S 34.2 / 45.7 L/h ; Perkins R-P250D5/S 36.8 / 49.0 L/h. Measured efficiency is 191.8- 206 g/kW·h per ISO 3046-1, and actual consumption varies about ± 5% with ambient conditions, load profile and fuel quality. At a diesel price of $1.0/L, 75% load costs roughly $34-37 per hour.
What can a 200kW diesel generator power? + A 200kW set is used for commercial and industrial buildings: hotels, schools, mid-size manufacturing plants, water and sewage pumping and office towers. Which engine brands and configurations are available for a 200kW generator? + The 200kW rating is available on 2 engine platforms: Cummins R-CC250D5/S ( NT855-GA , 191.8 g/kW·h ), Perkins R-P250D5/S ( 1206A-E70TTAG3 , 206 g/kW·h ).
