Why Choose Mitsubishi Diesel Generators?
Mitsubishi Heavy Industries (MHI) engines deliver Japanese precision engineering with a low failure rate — MHI builds its own fuel pump, turbocharger and injectors. The S6R, S12R and S16R engine families are purpose-built for power generation, with an operating range of -30°C to 50°C.
H&C offers both the R-SM (SME) and R-JM (JM) Mitsubishi-powered series — from the S6R2 6-cylinder (520kW) to the S16R2 16-cylinder (1,800kW). JM is built in Japan; SM is built in the China joint venture with a "-C" model suffix — same models and technology. Each genset is paired with a Leroy-Somer or Stamford alternator and Deep Sea 7320 controller, assembled and load-tested at our manufacturing facility in Jiangsu, China.
For sites in the Middle East, Africa, and high-temperature regions, Mitsubishi is engineered for harsh conditions: 10-second start, 100% one-step load acceptance, and 300% momentary overload — specified for data-center designs targeting Uptime Institute Tier III/IV.

Frequently Asked Questions — Mitsubishi Generators
How much does a Mitsubishi diesel generator cost? + A Mitsubishi generator set is priced by power rating, engine series and configuration. H&C builds Mitsubishi-powered sets from 520kW to 1,800kW in two series: R-SM , using engines built by Shanghai MHI Engine (SME) , MHI's engine joint venture in China, and R-JM , using imported Japan-built engines. Because the two series come from different production sources, their prices differ — R-JM carries Japanese production and import costs, while R-SM is the more economical route for projects that do not specify a Japanese origin. Alternator choice (Stamford S5–S7 or Leroy-Somer TAL/LSA), enclosure (open skid, soundproof canopy or containerized) and controller options ( Deep Sea DSE 8610 or DSE 7320 standard; ComAp and SmartGen optional) also affect the final price. Tell us the required kW, voltage and destination country, and we will confirm the R-SM or R-JM price within 24 hours .
What is the fuel consumption of a Mitsubishi generator? + Mitsubishi generator fuel consumption is 196–221 g/kW·h at 75% load depending on engine family: the S6R2 series uses 196–203 g/kW·h, the S12R series 199–221 g/kW·h, and the S16R/S16R2 series 200–209 g/kW·h. The most efficient engine in the lineup is the S6R2-PTA at 196 g/kW·h , used in the R-JM (imported) series. In litres, Mitsubishi sets consume about 72.9 L/h at 520kW , 170.4 L/h at 1,200kW and 269.0 L/h at 1,800kW at 75% load. Figures are measured per ISO 3046-1 and vary about ±5% with ambient conditions and load profile; the power range table above lists L/h at 75% and 100% for every model.
Where are Mitsubishi generator engines manufactured? + Mitsubishi generator engines reach H&C from two production sources. The R-JM series uses imported Mitsubishi engines built in Japan — the model code has no suffix (for example S6R2-PTA). The R-SM series uses engines built by Shanghai MHI Engine (SME) , Mitsubishi Heavy Industries' engine joint venture in China — the model code carries the "-C" suffix (for example S6R2-PTA-C). Both are MHI-licensed designs on the same S6R, S12R and S16R architecture, with parts interchangeability above 85%, and both cover 520kW to 1,800kW . Tell us whether your project specifies a Japanese or a Chinese production origin and we will quote the matching series.
Which Mitsubishi series is right for my project — R-SM or R-JM? + Choose by origin requirement, lead time and price . Specify R-JM when the tender or the end user requires engines built in Japan; specify R-SM when the project allows Chinese production and cost is the priority — the engines are the same MHI design, built by SME in China with the "-C" suffix. Both series cover 520–1,800kW and are matched with Stamford or Leroy-Somer alternators and Deep Sea controllers. H&C also supplies Cummins , Perkins and MTU , so we can compare platforms for your load profile — send your kW, load profile and country.
Are Mitsubishi generators suitable for hot climates and harsh sites? + Mitsubishi engines are factory-rated to 50°C ambient at ISO conditions, with a documented operating range of -30°C to 50°C — so no performance penalty applies in Middle East, African and tropical installations. The S6R, S12R and S16R designs use mechanical fuel injection , so there is no common-rail electronics to fail in high-temperature, high-humidity or high-dust conditions, and they accept wider variation in fuel quality. Starting performance is 10 seconds to full load with 100% single-step load acceptance. Tell us the site ambient temperature, altitude and fuel quality and we will confirm the derating curve for your configuration.
What is the delivery time for a Mitsubishi generator set? + The lead time for a Mitsubishi generator set depends on which series you choose , the engine model , the quantity and the factory's current schedule . R-SM (China-built) and R-JM (imported) engines come from different production sources, so their lead times differ and are confirmed against the factory's current schedule rather than quoted as a fixed figure. Send the engine model and the quantity you need and we will confirm the current factory lead time for your order.
