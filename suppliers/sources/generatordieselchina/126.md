Why Choose MTU Diesel Generators?
MTU, a brand of Rolls-Royce Power Systems, builds its 2000 Series and 4000 Series in a Sino-German joint-venture factory in China — combining German MTU technology with China-made price and delivery advantages. Both series are the standard for mission-critical power generation, trusted by data centers, hospitals, and industrial facilities worldwide.
H&C integrates the complete MTU R-M and R-MYC series into our generator sets — from the 12V2000G25 (520kW) to the 20V4000G63LF (2,400kW). Every genset is paired with a Leroy-Somer or Stamford alternator and Deep Sea 7320 controller — assembled and load-tested at our manufacturing facility in Jiangsu, China per ISO 8528 G3 standards.
For data center operators, MTU reaches full load in about 10–15 seconds and offers high power density in a smaller footprint. Series 4000 supports continuous 100% load with 10% overload (DCCP rating) for Uptime Institute Tier III/IV sites — with fuel consumption from 188 g/kW·h at 75% load and long overhaul intervals.

Frequently Asked Questions — MTU Generators
How much does an MTU diesel generator cost? + An MTU generator set is priced by power rating, engine series and configuration. H&C builds MTU-powered sets from 520kW to 2,400kW: the 2000 Series covers 520–1,000kW and the 4000 Series 1,000–2,400kW. Alternator choice ( Stamford S5–S7 or Leroy-Somer TAL/LSA ), enclosure (open skid, soundproof canopy or containerized), controller and ATS options all affect the final price, and a DCCP-rated 4000 Series unit for a data center carries a higher specification than a standby set. Give us the required kW, voltage and destination country and we will come back with a factory-direct MTU price within 24 hours .
What is the fuel consumption of an MTU generator? + MTU generator fuel consumption is 188–203 g/kW·h at 75% load depending on series: the 2000 Series (520–1,000kW) uses 198–203 g/kW·h and the 4000 Series (1,000–2,400kW) 188–193 g/kW·h. The most efficient model is the 16V4000G23F at 1,600kW with 188 g/kW·h . In litres, that is about 94.1 L/h at 520kW , 162.0 L/h at 1,200kW and 274.3 L/h at 2,000kW at 75% load. Figures are derived from g/kW·h values per ISO 3046-1 and vary about ±5% with ambient conditions and load profile; the power range table above lists L/h at 75% and 100% for every model.
Which MTU series is right for my project — 2000 or 4000? + The choice between the 2000 and 4000 Series comes down to power rating and duty cycle. The 2000 Series (12–18 cylinder, 520–1,000kW) suits standby and baseload projects where cost is the priority. The 4000 Series (12–20 cylinder, 1,000–2,400kW) is the choice for mission-critical sites — data centers, hospitals and large industrial plants — with G3 voltage regulation, a DCCP continuous rating and models up to 4,000kVA at 50Hz . H&C also supplies Cummins , Perkins and Mitsubishi engines — send your kW, load profile and country and we will recommend the right platform.
Are MTU generators suitable for data centers? + MTU's 4000 Series is specified for data-center designs targeting Uptime Institute Tier III/IV . It provides G3 voltage and frequency regulation, a DCCP continuous rating with 10% overload, single-step load acceptance of 60% or more of rated power, and reaches full load in about 10–15 seconds . H&C supplies MTU-powered sets with Deep Sea DSE 7320 controllers (ComAp optional) for ATS and BMS integration, and load-bank test reports are available for design reviews.
Where are MTU engines manufactured? + MTU power-generation engines are built in Europe and, through the MTU Yuchai Power joint venture , in China. That joint venture is a 50/50 company formed by MTU Friedrichshafen and Guangxi Yuchai Machinery , and it has produced the 4000 Series at its Yulin plant since 2018 — more than 3,000 units to date. The 2000 Series is produced at the joint venture's Suzhou plant , which began as MTU's wholly-owned Suzhou operation in 1996 ; the first locally built 2000 Series engines came off the line in 2025 , and the joint venture's series production of the 2000 Series is authorized from 2026 . The 1600 Series is not part of the China localization and is supplied from India . European-built 2000 and 4000 Series engines remain available on special custom orders , and the German plant is currently fully booked with a production lead time of 6 months or more . Tell us your required series and any country-of-origin requirement and we will confirm what can be supplied.
What is the delivery time for an MTU generator set? + Delivery depends on the engine model , the quantity and the current factory schedule. Because demand for the MTU 2000 and 4000 Series is currently very high, the lead time has to be confirmed with the factory in advance — we cannot confirm a firm delivery date immediately. Engines produced in Germany are custom orders with a lead time of 6 months or more . Send the engine model and quantity and we will confirm the current factory slot for your order.
