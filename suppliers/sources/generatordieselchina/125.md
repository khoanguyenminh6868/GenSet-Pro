Why Choose Perkins Diesel Generators?
Perkins Engines Company Limited, headquartered in Peterborough, UK, is a subsidiary of Caterpillar Inc. with manufacturing facilities in China, India, the USA and the UK — a global footprint that gives buyers with engine-origin restrictions an extra choice. Perkins engines power generator sets from 7kW to 1,800kW.
H&C integrates the complete Perkins R-P series into our generator sets: from the compact 400 Series (7kW) to the industrial 4000 Series (1,800kW). Every genset is paired with a Stamford or Leroy-Somer alternator and Deep Sea / ComAp controller — assembled and load-tested at our manufacturing facility in Jiangsu, China per ISO 8528 standards.
For buyers, Perkins is engineered for low lifetime maintenance: the 2000 Series is built in the same factory as Caterpillar, and engines across families share common components with long service intervals. The 1100 Series uses mechanical injection with a 500-hour service interval — fast on-site servicing, less labour, and less downtime.

Frequently Asked Questions — Perkins Generators
How much does a Perkins diesel generator cost? + A Perkins diesel generator is priced mainly by its power rating and the engine series behind it. H&C builds Perkins sets from 7kW to 1,800kW: the 400 Series (403D/404D) covers 7–20kW, the 1100 Series (1103/1104/1106A) covers 24–280kW, and the 2800/4000 Series (2806–4016) covers 320–1,800kW. Configuration also moves the price — open skid, soundproof canopy or containerized enclosure, Stamford or Leroy-Somer alternator, Deep Sea, ComAp or SmartGen controller, and options such as ATS. Tell us the required kW, voltage and destination country and we will quote the matching Perkins series within 24 hours .
What is the fuel consumption of a Perkins generator? + Perkins generator fuel consumption is 196–264 g/kW·h depending on engine series. The 400 Series (403–404D) uses 250–264 g/kW·h, the 1100 Series (1103–1106A) 197–237 g/kW·h, and the 2800/4000 Series (2806–4016) 196–216 g/kW·h; the most efficient models are the 2506C-E15TAG2 at 400kW and the 2806A-E18TAG2 at 520kW, both at 196 g/kW·h . In litres, a Perkins set consumes about 2.4 L/h at 10kW , 20.1 L/h at 108kW and 89.6 L/h at 480kW at 75% load. Figures are measured to ISO 3046-1 and vary about ±5% with ambient conditions and load profile; the power range table above lists L/h at 75% and 100% for every model.
Which Perkins engine series is right for my application? + Choose the Perkins series by power rating and duty cycle. The 400 Series (403D/404D, 7–20kW) is a compact 3-4 cylinder for light commercial and residential standby. The 1100 Series (1103/1104/1106A, 24–280kW) is the most widely used platform for industrial standby and light prime power, with mechanical injection and a 500-hour service interval. The 1200/1500 Series covers commercial prime power and the 2200/2500 Series heavy-duty standby. The 2800/4000 Series (2806–4016, 320–1,800kW) uses V8, V12 and V16 engines for large-scale prime and baseload duty. H&C supplies both Perkins and Cummins engines — send your kW, load profile and country and we will recommend which fits.
What is the delivery time for a Perkins generator set? + The delivery time depends on the engine model. Perkins engines built in China typically ship in 4–6 weeks after order confirmation. For the 2000 Series manufactured in the USA and the 4000 Series manufactured in India , the lead time depends on the engine's stock availability in China. Every set is assembled and load-tested at our manufacturing facility in Jiangsu, China to ISO 8528 before shipment. Send your engine model and required quantity and we will confirm the current engine lead time with Perkins.
