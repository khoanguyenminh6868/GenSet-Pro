About Fuzhou H&C
Fuzhou H&C imp&exp Co., Ltd, established in 2018, is a factory-direct diesel generator manufacturer. We produce generator sets in our own self-owned factory that has been manufacturing since 1983, and we have been exporting them to EPC contractors, project developers, and end users across 30+ countries since 2003. From banking data centers and telecom infrastructure to power plants and hotel backup systems, our generators power critical facilities where reliability is non-negotiable.
We are not a trading company. We own and operate our production facility, fabricating bases, enclosures, and control panels in-house. This means your generator is built to specification — not assembled from catalog parts — and you deal directly with the people who build it. Every unit undergoes full-load testing before shipment, and our engineering team supports you from bid preparation through commissioning. ISO 8528-5 certified, CE marked, and built for projects where failure is not an option.
