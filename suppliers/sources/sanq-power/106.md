Description

About Perkins: for more than 80 years, UK Perkins has been the world's leading supplier of diesel and gas engines in the 4 - 2,000 kW (5 - 2,800 hp) market. Perkins key strength is its ability to tailor engines precisely to meet customers' requirements, which is why its engine solutions are trusted by more than 1,000 leading manufacturers in the industrial, construction, agricultural, materials handling and electrical power generation markets.
Perkins global product support is provided by 4,000 distribution, parts and service centers. Perkins distributor network provides support wherever needed around the world and an extremely high standard is set to ensure the distribution network provides an outstanding service to all customers.
