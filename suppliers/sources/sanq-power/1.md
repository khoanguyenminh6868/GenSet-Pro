Guangzhou Sanq Power EquipmentCo.,Ltd. (SANQ POWER for short) Founded in 2004, headquarter is located in
Guangzhou PanyuDonghuan Industrial Zone . The company is specialized in manufacturing diesel generators, integrating assem bling, sales , maintenance and repair service. It is Cummins Authorized de aler for repair service in Guangdong territory. We have over 200 employees and an annual sales of over USD 30million. Its prod ucts are mainly used as backup power or emergency power in many important fields, such as, hotels, mines, buildings, airports, hospitals, factories all over China and now aiming at all over the world.
