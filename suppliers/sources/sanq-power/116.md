Power Pack Position: Home &raquo; Products &raquo; Special type &raquo; Power Pack
The power pack is specifically designed for rail application or shipping or stationary terminal. The power pack supplies electrical power for container refrigeration systems. The power pack is powered by water-cooled, heavy-duty, industrial type CUMMINS diesel engine that is closed couple via SAE flexible disk/flange coupling to the Stamford brush-less, synchronous, self-regulated alternator. The engine/alternator/radiator/control panel is mounted on the steel base frame with four lifting attachments for lifting. The generator set frame is insulated from the container floor by anti-vibration mountings. The safety shutdown system and the electricity measuring data are provided with Deepsea Controller.
