► About Cummins Diesel Engines
Cummins is one of world top famous engines brands and widely acceptable all over the world. Cummins Inc., a global power leader, a corporation of complementary business units that design, manufacture, distribute and service engines and related technologies, including fuel systems, controls, air handling, filtration, emission solutions and electrical power generating systems. Headquartered in Columbus, Indiana, (USA) Cummins serves customers in approximately 190 countries and territories through a network of more than 600 company-owned and independent distributor locations and approximately 5,200 dealer locations. Cummins reported a net income of $1.99 billion in 2010(click cummins.com to read more). ► About Ettes Cummins Series Diesel Generators
Ettes Power Cummins diesel-engine-generator-set are ranging from 20kw up to 1500kw, which are driven by engines series of 4B3.9, 6B5.9, 6C8.3, 6L8.9, QSZ13, NT855, QSNT, MT11, KT19, QSK19, KT38, QSK38 and KT50 etc . Cummins diesel power generators/genset are vastly used in various fields like manufacturing plant, transportation sector, buildings, telecom, schools, banks, hospitals, ships, petrolube oil fields and industrial areas etc, which can act as main power or backup generator (standby generator or emergency generator). With Cummins diesel generators reliability, durability, flexibility, energy-saving, environment protection and World wide after-service networks, Ettes Brand Cummins diesel engine generating set play a fundamental role in global engines & generators industry.
