Ettes Power is the leading manufacturer and packager for complete diesel & gas generators from 20kW upto 3000kW. Ettes power generations enjoy hot markets in more than 70 countries all over the world including Latin America, Africa, Middle East, Europe and Asia markets.
► About MTU Diesel Engines
MTU history can be traced back to the 18th century. MTU is the core brand of Rolls-Royce Power Systems AG, which is a world-leading provider of high- and medium-speed diesel and gas engines, complete drive systems, distributed energy systems and fuel injection systems for the most demanding requirements. Especially within the shipping sector the company has established a long and successful partnership with some ten thousands of engines in operation around the globe on all seas.
Based on its innovative capabilities, its reliability and system competence, MTU combines unique drive system know-how and a large range of products of excellent quality. MTU Engines are the ideal motors to drive diesel power plant.
