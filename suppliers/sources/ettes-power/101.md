► About MAN Engines
MAN is a global leader in fields of diesel & gas engines cogeneration in Germany, MAN Engines offers low-pollution gas engines for CCHP plants in the power range from 37 kW to 800 kW with natural gas, and from 68 kW to 800 kW with special gas (biogas, landfill gas, sewage gas). The MAN engines developed especially for the application with natural and special gas excel through their ultimate reliability and highest energy efficiency. Through their optimum combustion process, they achieve best overall efficiencies in excess of 90 percent.
MAN gas engines have proven themselves through their ruggedness, combined with high operation in peak-load and continuous operation, in the agricultural and municipality sectors, in hotels and hospitals, as well as in numerous other industries. The long service intervals and high service life of MAN gas engines ensure low total cost of ownership.
► About MAN Gas Engine Generators
60kW~800kW, Compact Design, Powerful, Max efficient, High Reliable, Low Emissions, Long Service Life
As a leading manufacturer and assembler for whole gas engine generator set & CHPs in China, ETTES mainly adopt MAN engines of E0834, E0836, E2676, E3262, E3268 and E3872 series etc, with features of Proven and fully developed, Flexibility and individuality, Robust and compact, High Efficient and Low emissions. In addition to standard package of gas fired generator, client’s special requirements can be tailored such as CHP (combined heat and power), Cogeneration, Trigeneration ,combined cold heat and power (CCHP), ATS, Parallel operation system, Remote monitor & Control, Modularized soundproof & weatherproof container.
