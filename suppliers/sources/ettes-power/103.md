► About MWM Engines global
MWM is a global leader in fields of diesel & gas engines generation, with more than 150 years history, by development strategy of energy, efficiency and ecology. With merits of high reliable, highest efficiency, best control concept, wide range of application, low emissions and long life-span. Thanks for highest efficient electricity and heat generation, total CHP efficiency can reach 90%. MWM gas engines play a fundamental role in decentralized energy supply (cogeneration plant or CHP plant), which enjoy high reputation all over the world.
► About MWM Gas Engine Generators
400kW~2000kW, Optimum Efficiency, Full Turbopower, Long Life-span, High Reliable, More Profits
As a leading manufacturer and assembler for whole gas fired generator set & CHPs in China, Ettes mainly adopt MWM engines of CG132 (TCG3016), CG170 (TCG2020) and CG260 (TCG2032) at engine type of V8, V12, V16 and V20 etc, with features of high reliable, quick installation, easy operation and low maintenance cost. In addition to standard package of electrical generation, client’s special requirements can be tailored such as CHP(combined heat and power), Cogeneration, trigeneration ,combined cold heat and power (CCHP), ATS, Parallel operation system, Remote monitor & Control, Modularized soundproof & weatherproof container.
