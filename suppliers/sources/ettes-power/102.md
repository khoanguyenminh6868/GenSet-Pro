► About CNPC Engines
CNPC Series gas engines are developed by cooperation with AVL from Germany and Austria. The engine assembling plant is affiliated to CNPC (China National Petroleum & Oil Corporation), which is the first and the largest engine manufacture in China since 1953. CNPC engine generator performance is “Leading China” and “First class Global”, which work widely in 80% of China Oil & Gas Fields, Coalmines, Vessels and other areas under harsh working conditions, enjoy high reputation in global.
► About Ettes-CNPC Gas Engine Generators
400kW~1200kW, Powerful, Big Displacement, Strong loads-starting-ability, Long Serice Life
Ettes CNPC series gas fired generators & CHPs are driven by engines series of 6140, 12V190, G12V190 and H16V190 etc, with merits of big displacement, sufficient reserve-power, strong capability against loads-surge, easy for installation & maintenance, long overhaul, widely used in oil & gas fields, coalmines and wild construction etc. It is the ideal solution for electrical generation to drive loads under harsh conditions for island operation.
Besides standard package of electrical generator, Ettes can satisfy client’s special requirements and make tailored design like CHP, CCHP/Trigeneration, Cogeneration, ATS, Parallel operation system, Remote monitor & Control, Modularized soundproof & weatherproof container, which can realize the most efficient and comprehensive use of energy and generate electricity, hot water, cooled water, hot steam or hot oil etc.
