About Ettes Power
Ettes Power, one of the leading group company in China for assembling and manufaturing complete generator set, power ranging from 20kW upto 3000kW, to be driven by world top engine brands of Cummins, Perkins, MAN, MWM MTU and CNPC 190 series. Transformed from China state owned enterprise and founded in 1998, Ettes Group has total three production bases & assembling plants for diesel gensets, methane gas generators & CHPs with fixed assets of USD 80 million and total area of 86,000 square meters working shops at production ability of 4000 units per year, backed by more than 330 outstanding employees...
Our Culture
Business Philosophy: Integrity & Honesty, Customers Satisfaction is Our Priority Pursuit
Corporate Vision: Acting as the Leading Role in Fields of Diesel & Gas Engines Gensets & CHPs
Corporate Mission: Superior Engine Generation, Drive a Better World
Core Values of Products: High Reliability, Environmental-Protection, Energy-saving, All-around Service
Our Quality
China leading and World Class, each Ettes brand genset will be an elaborate works. With more than two decades experience in the R&D and manufacturing of generator sets in strict compliance with international standards. All Ettes brand gensets are certified by ISO9001:2008, ISO14001 and CE, complying with standards of GB2820, ISO8528, ISO3046, BS5000PT99, AS1359 and IEC34 etc and supply special national or regional conforms like UL, EPA, CSA, and SONCAP etc.
Our Markets
Based in China with global visions, up to being now, more than 80,000 units of high quality gensets from Ettes Power have been working smoothly in various fields and sectors like commercial buildings, telecoms, manufacturing plants, farms, oil & gas fields, mines, railways, airports, seaports, military bases, farms, sewage gas plants, landfill gas plants and Waste to Energy projects like MSW gasification etc. Ettes brands engine-generators enjoy hot markets in more than 70 countries all over the world including Latin America, Africa, Middle East, East Europe and Southeast Asia etc.
