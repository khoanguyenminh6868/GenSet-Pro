Xiamen Aosif Engineering Ltd.
Since its founding in 2001, AOSIF ENGINEERING LTD. has specialized in the research, development, and manufacturing of generator sets and alternators, accumulating extensive manufacturing and service experience in this field. Continuous breakthroughs in this specialized field have led to the continuous expansion and upgrading of its brand production base. The company's core manufacturing base, located in the Changtai Industrial Park of the Changtai Economic Development Zone in Fujian Province, covers a total area of 33,333 square meters and has a total construction area of 21,703 square meters. It was completed and put into operation in 2015. Construction is about to begin on the new manufacturing base in Haicang District, which will occupy 30,666 square meters and have a total construction area of 86,400 square meters. It is expected to be completed and put into operation by the end of 2027, further strengthening the company's position as a top international brand in power generation.
All product manufacturing and services adhere to ISO8525 and GB/T2820-97 standards. Our management system is ISO9001 and ISO14001 certified, and our entire product line is CE certified, ensuring the high quality of our products and services. Aosif has been awarded the Xiamen Famous Trademark, Fujian Province Famous Trademark, Xiamen Quality Brand, and Fujian Famous Brand Product designations, as well as dozens of patents and designation as a National High-Tech Enterprise. Our products are now exported worldwide and have received widespread acclaim and recognition from users.As a professional generator set manufacturer, AOSIF focuses on product R&D innovation, process quality, and service. It has established long-term strategic partnerships with internationally renowned engine and generator manufacturers, including Cummins, Perkins, Hyundai, Leroysomer, and Stamford, as well as renowned domestic engine manufacturers. Its product range includes diesel generator sets ranging from 10kVA to 5000kVA, gas generator sets ranging from 25kVA to 2500kVA, alternators ranging from 10kVA to 2500kVA, silent containerized generator set, trailers, vehicle-mounted mobile power stations, light towers, and hybrid power station. control systems include automatic start-stop control systems, paralleling systems, and automatic transfer systems (ATS). Furthermore, AOSIF offers customized products and complete power station solutions based on standard products to best meet market demand. Focusing on the global market, building a top international brand, setting an industry benchmark, and continuously creating value for customers are the unwavering commitment and eternal pursuit of all AOSIF employees.
