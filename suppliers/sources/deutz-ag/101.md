DEUTZ G 2.2/2.9 - GAS/GASOLINE ENGINES
Low emission
meets
top performance
Water-cooled gas and gasoline engines from DEUTZ combine maximum performance, environmental friendliness and versatility. Based on the successful 2.2/2.9 series, they offer outstanding performance and efficiency for demanding industrial applications.
New standard for demanding applications
The engines are equipped with a fully electronically controlled gas/gasoline system that ensures stoichiometric combustion. Thanks to lambda control and knock sensors, the gas/gasoline engines achieve the lowest emissions and best performance at the same time.
KEY FEATURES
Robust engine design for demanding industrial applications based on the successful 2.9 series .
Full electonic controlled LPG gas system , stoichiometric combustion, closed loop lambda control and knocking sensors for lowest emissions and best performance at the same time .
Open EAT system (3 way catalyst) means 100% machine availability even under lowest load duty cycles.
Same foot print and major installation interfaces as diesel engines TCD 2.2/2.9 for ease of installation in diesel and gas/gasoline powered machines.
Up to 1000 h oil change intervals, maintenance-free valve train and optionally service-friendly multi-V belt drive result in low maintenance cost and increased machine availability.
The extremely compact engine design and customer friendly accessories reduce the installation cost and increase the number of applications.
100% power take-off at flywheel and front end; additional side PTO drive with up to 120 Nm for the mounting of hydraulic pumps.
Compact 3-way catalyst for EU Stage V and EPA Tier 2 compliance.

The same connection dimensions and main installation interfaces as the DEUTZ 2.2/2.9 series diesel engines enable easy installation in diesel and gas/gasoline-powered equipment.
Our quality processes ensure that every product meets the high DEUTZ standards and that customers receive reliable, world-class solutions.
Our gas/gasoline engines focus on robustness and ease of maintenance. With oil change intervals of up to 1000 hours, a maintenance-free valve train and optional poly-V-belt drive, they offer low maintenance costs and high device availability.
Experience a motor design that combines compactness with modularity. Our customer-oriented system of optional attachments reduces installation costs and expands applications. With 100% power take-off on the flywheel, the front end and a side power take-off, our motors offer maximum flexibility.
