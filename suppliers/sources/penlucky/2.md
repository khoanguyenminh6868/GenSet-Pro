COMPANY PROFILE
Established in 2007, Penlucky today is one of the Chinese leading manufacturers of the highest quality diesel generating sets and complete power generation systems. We have a wide power range from 9kVA to 3450kVA with world famous engine and alternator brand. Our business is based on procuring, producing, distributing, trading and selling Diesel Generator and related products all over the world.
We are specializing in producing soundproof and open type diesel Generating Sets. We already built long term business relationship with MTU, CUMMINS, PERKINS, BAUDOUIN and MITSUBISHI engines and world famous STAMFORD, Leroy Somer and Mecc Alte alternator. We also use WoodWard, Deep Sea and ComAp control module which are professional engine controllers as our core control module. Also we own abundant skills for mechanical & control system design which can fulfill the individual requirements fully.
As the core products of the production, our soundproof generating sets are widely used in construction, communication, finance, mineral exploitation, leasehold and other situations with their superexcellent performance. As what you are looking forward to, we uphold more secure, more reliable and cleaner with our generating sets to meet your individual needs as our design principles. Our generating set can work without damage to the environment, and according to its low noise level, energy saving & reliable performance, it already became the strong backing of many businesses.
We believe that industry responsibility and accountability are essential for our company growth. Our fast growth trajectory reflects our concept & passion to pursue premium product development. We expect with you a long term business partnership based on mutual understanding and profit.
Penlucky Power is located in Weifang High-Tech Zone, Shandong, China, founded in 2007, specializing in the design, manufacture, and sales of mobile lighting towers, standby generator sets, and continuous operation power stations. China generator set manufacturer.
