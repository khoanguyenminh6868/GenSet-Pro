Why Choose Penlucky Gas Generator Sets ？
With the decreasing amount of oil storage and the increasing requirement of environmental protection emission, a kind of power generation equipment instead of oil is needed. Gas generator sets are developed in response to the world’s environmental protection requirements and the new market environment, and gas gensets have more advantages in terms of usage, maintenance and price cost. In the future, gas genset will gradually replace other fossil fuel units to produce clean energy, while continuously improving the global ecosystem.
High power generation efficiency. Power generation efficiency between 33%~40%, such as the use of combined heat and power supply, the efficiency can reach more than 80%.
Low emission, more environmental protection. Natural gas is a clean and high quality energy source, with 60%~80% less NOX and CO2 emissions, almost free of SO2, dust and other harmful substances.
Low cost.The cost of using gas is 30%-50% lower than that of fuel oil to generate the same power of electricity.
Wide range of application, low requirement for gas source. Applicable to various industries, natural gas, biogas, wellhead associated gas, coal bed methane, LPG and other combustible gases can be used.
High safety performance. Equipped with high efficiency engine with strong power, advanced control system, thin combustion technology with high air-fuel ratio, equipped with various sensors, control valves and explosion-proof valves to integrate detection and safety functions.
Long service life. Low thermal load, equipped with advanced ignition control system, more equipped with water air cooler, two-way water tank, research and development of spark plug water cooling technology, etc. to make the unit and accessories longer service life

The Development Trend of Gaseous Generators
Gas generator sets have been gaining popularity in recent years due to their low emissions, cost-effectiveness, and reliability.
Increased Use of Natural Gas
It is the most common fuel used in gas generators due to its low cost, high availability, and low emissions. As the world shifts towards renewable energy sources, natural gas is also being viewed as a transitional fuel that can help reduce carbon emissions while renewable energy sources continue to be developed.
Improved Efficiency
Gas generators are becoming more efficient due to advances in technology such as lean burn combustion, variable valve timing, and electronic control systems. These advancements result in lower fuel consumption and reduced emissions.
Increased Automation
Automation and remote monitoring technologies are being integrated into gas generator sets, allowing for real-time monitoring and control of the system. This reduces the need for on-site personnel and improves overall system reliability.
Energy Storage Integration
Gas generator sets are being integrated with energy storage systems such as batteries, allowing for better management of peak demand periods and increased system stability.
Consideration When Buying Gaseous Generators
When buying gaseous generator sets, there are several important considerations to keep in mind to ensure that you choose the best generator for your needs.
Fuel Type: The primary factor to weigh when purchasing a gaseous generator set revolves around the fuel it utilizes. Gaseous generators are adaptable, operating on various fuels like natural gas, propane, and biogas. Your selection should align with factors such as fuel availability, cost-effectiveness, and environmental impact.
Power Output: Another critical factor to consider is the generator’s power output. It’s essential to assess your specific power needs for your intended application and select a generator that can deliver the necessary amount of electricity. This ensures that your generator can effectively meet your demands and power your equipment or facility efficiently.
Efficiency: Generator efficiency is a pivotal factor to contemplate. Opting for a more efficient generator means it will consume less fuel while delivering the same power output, resulting in long-term cost savings. Prioritizing efficiency ensures you get the most out of your fuel investment and minimizes operational expenses.
Noise Level: The generator’s noise level is a crucial aspect to account for, particularly when intended for use in residential or urban settings. Seek out generators specifically designed to operate quietly, ensuring minimal disruption to your surroundings. This consideration is vital for maintaining a peaceful environment while benefiting from reliable power generation.
Reliability: Prioritize generators crafted by well-established manufacturers known for their track record of dependability. Additionally, explore options that come with warranties or comprehensive service plans, providing you with peace of mind and assurance that your generator will perform reliably when you need it most.
