6CTA8.3-GM FEATURES
Engine Specifications
Configuration: In-line, 6-cylinder
Bore x Stroke: 114 x 135 mm
Displacement: 8.3 L
Compression Ratio: 17.3 : 1
Emission: CCS IMO
Aspiration: Turbocharged / Aftercooled
Product Portfolio

Fuel System
Bosch in-line fuel pump operates at high injection pressures for cleaner combustion and lower emissions.
Cooling System
Single loop keel cooling or engine mounted heat exchanger available for simplified installation. Optional fan drive for radiator applications.
Air System
Cummins turbocharger optimized for marine applications with wastegate for exceptional transient response. Marine grade air filter.
Engine Design
Robust in-line six cylinder designed for high hour marine applications. Single piece crossflow cylinder head for maximum durability and optimized combustion. Replaceable wet cylinders for longer life and lower rebuild cost.
Lubrication System
Standard capacity (18.9 L [20 qt.]) marine grade oil pan. Spin-on Cummins filters available.
Certifications
Complies with IMO Tier II emissions regulations. Certificates of compliance are available from Lloyd’s Register of Shipping. Consult your local Cummins professional for a complete listing of current marine agency approvals for this engine.
Electrical System
24v standard electrical system with 12V option available.
Exhaust System
SOLAS compliant, wet exhaust manifold with shielding or water cooled design available for lower engine surface temperatures and improved emissions.
