公司简介
Xiamen Kangwo mechanical and Electrical Equipment Co., Ltd. has many years of industry experience and is located in 32D, building 2, Xiangjiang garden, No. 106, Xiahe Road, Siming District, Xiamen city. Since its establishment, the company has made unremitting efforts. The company has always been rigorous production process, advanced equipment, strong technical strength, and the spirit of professional service as always, which is the fundamental guarantee for us to provide high-quality products and engineering services. The company is a technology development oriented enterprise specializing in the production of generator sets and related supporting products. The company has developed three series of diesel generator sets, namely, land-based, marine and fire-fighting And passed ISO9001:2000, ISO14001:2004 quality and environment system certification. The product quality conforms to the international standard gb-2820-97.
The company produces complete sets of generator sets, including ordinary type, mobile type, anti voice type, self starting generator set, three remote generator set, ATS automatic switching screen, full-automatic parallel screen and other intelligent generator sets. The unit adopts British deep sea and Guilin Guangming production Chinese LCD module, full number display, with automatic protection system of overspeed, oil temperature, water temperature and oil pressure. The company sells diesel generator sets, which are widely used in real estate buildings, business buildings, hotels, hospitals, military forces, airports, railways, communications, petrochemical, factories and many other fields, and exported to Southeast Asia, the Middle East, Africa and many other countries and regions. The product quality and service are highly appreciated and favored by customers at home and abroad.
The company's technical service personnel can provide more accurate and effective pre-sales and after-sales technical services to users everywhere at any time. 24-hour service telephone: 400-1600-779 our company always adheres to the business philosophy of "one center", "two promises" and "eight trumps". That is to say, we take "providing customers with the most reasonable price and the most reliable quality of new generator set" as the center; we take "Three Guarantees of quality" and "nanny after-sales service" as the commitment. To create value for customers with specialty. In the generator industry, we are No. 1 in terms of industrial scale and number of employees, not the largest; but we should strive to create "three firsts and five firsts" in terms of honesty and trustworthiness, professional technology, price quality, after-sales service and operation management. Exceed customer expectations.
Relying on good business reputation and development potential, the company has established long-term friendly cooperative relations with famous companies at home and abroad, such as Shandong Weichai, Shanghai Dongfeng, Wuxi Wandi, Benz, Cummins, Daewoo, Rolls Royce, Volvo, lileisenma, abb, Stanford, marathon, etc., and become their super maintenance point. The company adheres to the business philosophy of "taking technological innovation as the driving force and constantly exceeding the market demand for products and services", and provides more valuable power solutions and value-added services for the majority of users.

106 号香江大花园 2 号楼 32D。公司自成立以来经过不懈的奋斗努力。该公司一

精神，是我们提供优质产品、工程服务的根本保证，公司是以技术开发为主、
专业生产发电机组及相关配套产品的企业，公司先后开发了陆用、船用、消防
三大系列柴油发电机组，并通过了 ISO9001:2000、ISO14001:2004 质量环境体
系认证。产品质量符合国际 GB-2820-97 标准。
本公司生产成套发电机组有普通型、移动型、防音型、自启动发电机组、三

酒店、医院、部队、机场、铁路、通讯、石化、工厂等诸多领域；并远销东南
亚、中东、非洲等多个国家和地区，产品质量和服务在国内外均受到客户的高
度赞赏和青睐。
