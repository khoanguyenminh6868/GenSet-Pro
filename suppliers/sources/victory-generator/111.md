Victory diesel generator sets, equipped with Volvo Penta engines, deliver high performance, reliability, and fuel efficiency for prime and standby power applications. These gensets feature turbocharged and intercooled engines, ensuring optimal power output with low fuel consumption.
Designed for low emissions, they comply with EU Stage V, EPA Tier 4, and IMO Tier III standards, utilizing SCR (Selective Catalytic Reduction) and DOCs (Diesel Oxidation Catalysts) to minimize environmental impact.
With advanced electronic control (EVC), the generator enables real-time monitoring, remote diagnostics, and easy maintenance, reducing downtime. The low-noise design makes it suitable for urban and noise-sensitive environments.
Supported by Volvo Penta's global service network, Victory’s gensets guarantee durability, efficiency, and uninterrupted power supply for industrial, commercial, and marine applications.
Volvo Penta's diesel engines for generator sets are renowned for their reliability, efficiency, and low emissions, making them ideal for prime and standby power applications.
1. High Performance & Durability
Built with robust materials and advanced engineering, these engines deliver consistent power output even under heavy loads. Features like turbocharging and optimized combustion ensure high fuel efficiency and long service life.
2. Low Emissions & Eco-Friendly
Compliant with EU Stage V, EPA Tier 4, and IMO Tier III standards, Volvo Penta engines incorporate SCR (Selective Catalytic Reduction) and DOCs (Diesel Oxidation Catalysts) to minimize NOx and particulate emissions without compromising performance.
3. Smart Control & Easy Maintenance
Equipped with Electronic Vessel Control (EVC) and remote monitoring, these engines provide real-time diagnostics for proactive maintenance. Simplified service points reduce downtime.
4. Quiet Operation
Advanced noise reduction technology ensures low vibration and sound levels , making them suitable for urban and sensitive environments.
5. Global Support
Backed by Volvo Penta’s worldwide service network, these engines guarantee quick parts availability and expert technical support .
