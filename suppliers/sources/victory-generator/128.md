Victory Mobile Trailer Lighting Tower is widely used in road repair, disaster relief, rescue and other large construction sites. It has high brightness, but would not dazzle people. The angle of illumination is 360°, so it is working without shadow, ensuring the safety of drivers.
Main Features:
The lamp panel of our Trailer-mounted Lighting Tower has four high efficient gaseous discharge lamps, with large scale illumination, high brightness, long distance irradiation and long service time. On the trailer, the lamp has perfect shockproof function and it's particularly suitable for the outdoor operation lighting.
Lighting Tower Advantages:
As long as 52 hours lighting time with wide fuel tank displacement.
Durable for any kind of air condition, full protection against to external
factor with elektrostatic colored body.
Galvanized coating tower durable against to corrosion, tower earth
connection, jacks and supported front wheel.
Easy balanceable specification with 4 units jacks at the rough terrain and
windy weather.
For external needs, 2 units 220VAC plugs with 10A and 16A safety fuse.
Separate oriented control panel; informing to user with warning and attitude
indicator during first working and attitude indicator during first working and
using.
High intensity of illumination with 4 units metal halide lamps.
Heated spark plug system during working without problem at the cold
weather.
Technical Specifications:
Frequency 50HZ, 1500RPM, Rating voltage 380V/220V, 400V/230V, 415V/240V
Frequency 60HZ, 1800RPM, Rating voltage 208V/120V, 220V/127V, 480V/277V
Power Factor 0.8(lagging)
Protection Standard: IP23
Model of Connection: 3 phases 4 wires, or single phase two wires
Insulation Grade: H
Light Tower Specifications:
1. Mast type(Up/Down): Pneumatic.
2. Light tower unused, the mast placed on the unit at the top.
3. Start mast must first by hand crank.
The mast can be erected light poles from the horizontal into the vertical
direction, by hand cranks.
4. Motorized Faders, refers to a device installed in the poles, just adjust
the lamp up and down angle (90°).
5. 360 ° rotating light pole, manual rotation, only need to rotate the light
pole.
6. The manual light pole by raising or lowering the hand crank to make light
pole.
7. Through the compressor, the pneumatic poles can be raised or lowered.
8. Light house refers to the height, ground to a lamp distance, including the
units and telescopic legs.

Protection Function: High temperature, Over speed, Low Speed, Low Oil
Pressure, Over voltage, Over current, Undervoltage.
3. Synchronous A.C. Alternator Self Excitation System, with AVR delivering
non fluctuating power
4. Extemal recessed emergency stop button for extra safety.
5. Radiator with engine driven fan mounted on skid
6. Good quality MCCB breaker mounted
7. Anti-vibration pads fixed between engine/alternator and base frame to
ensure the compete genset running stable
8. 12V DC electric start system
9. Free maintenance lead-acid starting battery and its cables
10. Weatherproof and soundproof enclosure high grade steel protected by
powder coat painting
11. Industrial primary silencer with flexible connection elbows
11. Fuel tank for at least 8~10 hours continuous running under full load,
with fuel gauge integrated on base frame
13. Test report, Set of drawings and Operation&Maintenance English
manuals
