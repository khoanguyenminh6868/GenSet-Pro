Perkins is a famous engine manufacturing company, which acquired the world’s
leading brand Rolls-Royce in nineties of last century. Since the engines
produced are used in the assembly of Rolls-Royce cars, it is world-renowned.
Perkins engines have excellent stability, reliability and durability, being
widely used in the communication, industry, outdoor works, mining, the rescue,
military and other fields.
Victory Perkins generator set adopt Perkins engine originally manufactured by
China Perkins in Wuxi City and UK Perkins Company. The engine adopts the latest
technology and high quality grind-proof materials to make sure first level
products, furthermore is coupled with Mecc Alte, Leory Somer or Stamford brand
alternator. After strict running testings before delivery, our superior quality
reach the international standards ISO8528. It has special features of low fuel
consumption, stable performance, easy maintenance, low operation cost, low
emission, some models can reach EUIIII Emission standard, which is an ideal
power system of prime and standby usage.
Perkins diesel gensets offered by Victory Power Generation are manufactured
strictly accord to the ISO9001:2000 international quality management system. Our
company has commanded the design of diesel gensets well with the overseas
companies' great support and the years' experience. Besides the design of
industry workshop, our company also has good experience with the design for the
monitors in intellectualized building, including connection, remote device,
engine room without on duty, soundproof design and installation. Up till now,
there are thousands gensets with control monitor provided by Victory Power
Generation, which proves the high preponderant situation of Victory Power
Generation.
Victory generator sets compliance with all main standards, such as GB/T2820,
GB1105, YD/T502, ISO8525, ISO 8528, ISO14000, ISO3046, GB755, BS5000, VDE0530,
ISO3046, IEC34-1.
Diesel Generator Set Features:
1. With fine cooling capability, the engine can work well at an environmental
temperature of 40 to 50 Centigrade.
2. The engine can be easily started without the help of pre-heater at the
temperature of -10 C; And with the cold start aid at the temperature of -25
C.
3. Starting system: 12V or 24V DC starting motor and battery charger.
4. Fuel system: 12V or 24V DC electromagnetic valve fuel cutoff, two-layer
rotating paper fuel filter;
5. Filter system: Dry core air filter with state indicator. Rotating and
replaceable paper full flow filter. Rotating and replaceable paper diesel
filter.
6. Big enough free maintenance battery, world famous brand VARTA.
7. Packing with Strong and thick enough WOOD CASE to protect the gensets
during long transporation.
8. Every spare parts suppliers are choosed extremely stricted, to control the
quality well.
9. From engine to whole genset, we test every link of each part with testing
machines in our test center, to make sure the generator will run very well at
the client's site.
10. A part of spare parts are produced by ourselves, which could save cost
for customers greatly.
11. Following ISO Standards and procedures to run all the business, and carry
on International Standards of ISO8528 to make the generators;
12.We have more than 20 engineers and technicians to provide reliable after
sales service to all of our generator sets.
