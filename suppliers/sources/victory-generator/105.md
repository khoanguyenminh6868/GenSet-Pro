YANMAR(Japan) was founded in 1912 and developed the world's first
commercially viable small diesel engine in 1933. The diesel engine has continued
as Yanmar core business ever since, Yanmar key tool in the rationalization and
modernization of agriculture, fishing and industry. They have brought the world
ever more energy-efficient and economic engines.
VICTORY Yanmar(original Japan brand) Super Silent Diesel Generator scores a
point over other models like Kubota as it is less noisy due to its noise
reduction cover (patented); Easy to start which is tension free due to the
auto-return decompression and recoil starter features and has cooling systems
like other models the 4-cycle, vertical, air-cooled diesel engines. Yanmar
Diesel Generator also scores in efficiency for diesel generators. It uses
minimum resources to give maximum output; A tank gives continuous power for
13-15 hours. This is possible due to the newly incorporated feature-injection
feature. Yanmar Diesel Generator have service centres and their spare parts are
available in the market. It is compliant with EU and EPA emission standard. As
small power genset, it is widely used in the area of industry, railway,
construction and so on.
