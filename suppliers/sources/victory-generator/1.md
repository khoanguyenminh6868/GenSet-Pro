Victory Power Technology Co., Limited
Is a professional manufacturer since 1999, specializing in production, design, sales, installation, maintenance and service of generator sets with CUMMINS, PERKINS, DEUTZ, LOVOL, YANMAR, MTU, ISUZU, DOOSAN DAEWOO, Quanchai, Shangcai(SDEC), Weichai, Yuchai, etc. Series world famous diesel engine and STAMFORD, LEROY SOMER, MARATHON series AC alternators. Victory can make generator from 1kVA-2500kVA.
We believe that it is our assurance of quality and exceptional communication skills in generation set field as well as our competitive prices and professional service that will always mean your best choice.
Our strength: Customers' satisfaction, our target!
With great efforts and developments, o ur company was registered in UK, at 7 Copperfield Road, Coventry, West Midlands, England, CV2 4AQ, United Kingdom , to do the design, research and technical service. The China branch as production base not only mainly produce the complete generator sets, but also do some generator projects in domestic market and responsible for international trade. Also, our own brand alternators are being made and exported to overseas countries at hundreds of thousands of units each year.
Comply strictly with ISO9001: 2000 Quality System, our products have passed quite a lot of Certifications, including USA EPA, Europe CE, Argentina IRAM, Egypt CIQ, Nigeria SONCAP, Kenya CoC, Tanzania CoC, Angola CNCA, Saudi Arabia SASO, etc. We always maintain the high quality of transmission and the standard of reliability, safety, durability as well as economy and environment-friendliness. And it has formed a completed pre-sale, sale and after-sale service system.
As a big group in China, a professional generator enterprise is developing toward its objectives enthusiastically ---win customers with quality; Stand in the market with credit; Redound upon society with value; Call on innovation and humanism, try our best to serve customers based on novel design and integrated service!
