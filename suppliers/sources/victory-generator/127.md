The 493 series engine is one of the main products for a wide range of applications. Victory Isuzu series diesel gensets offered by Victory Power Generation are manufactured strictly accord to the ISO9001: 2000 international quality management system. Our company has commanded the design of diesel gensets well with the overseas companies' great support and the years' experience. Up till now, there are thousands gensets with control monitor provided by Victory Power Generation, which proves the high preponderant situation of Victory Power Generation.
