Victory exports a lot of compact gasoline generators ranging in
capacity from 0.6 – 7.0 kVA to Middle East, South-Central Asia, North America,
Europe, Africa, and other countries around the world every year.These gasoline
generators are widely used for a diverse range of needs, such as an emergency
power source in the event of a power failure, outdoor construction work, outdoor
leisure activities and for disasters and other emergencies. Since the company
was found in 1999, we always do the best to improve the quality in order to meet
the requirements of various customers. Therefore, Victory LT series engines
emerged as required, which have high reliability, high quality, low emissions,
and compact design. LT gasoline engine series of generators that incorporated a
super light weight and compact constraction offering low fuel consumption and
low noise. Vitroy LT engine adopted the design of overhead valve (OHV) mechanism
and inclined cylinder, which comes from Honda company, and it's very popular in
the world.
Features:
1. OHV design permits low fuel consumption and noise
2. Classical combustion-chamber shape and excellent suction exhaust effect
which to improve combustion efficiency
3. Auto decompressing makes starting easy
4. Oil alert automatically shut off the engine
5. Reliable transistorized magneto-ignition system
6. Speedy start, smooth operation and low vibration
7. With the recoil starter which is easy started by a woman
8. With transistor magnet ignition device to ensure the stable perfomance of
ignition & low fuel consumption
