Since the Victory twin-cylinder generators were launched in recent years, this kind of generator has become the most popular product in our air-cooled generators. Victory twin-cylinder inherits the excellent performance & OHV structure of Victory LT engines, which have high reliability, high quality, low emissions, and compact design. Twin-cylinder engine series of generators that incorporated a super light weight and compact constraction offering low fuel consumption and low noise, and providing more power than LT single-cylinder engines, which can reach to 12KW. Victory twin-cylinder generators include gasoline generator and diesel generator. And both generators include two types: Open Type & silent type. The open skid mounted generator can have optional wheels and hand kits, electric start; the silent type with waterproof canopy ensures protection against ingress of dust and water, and at same time, it can effectively reduce the noise. Up to now, Victory twin-cylinder generators have been sold to Middle East, South-Central Asia, North America, Europe, Africa other countries around the world.
