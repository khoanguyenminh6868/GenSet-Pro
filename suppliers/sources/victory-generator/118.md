Victory sales a lot of compact gas portable generators ranging in capacity from 1.0kVA – 10 kVA to Middle East, South-Central Asia, North America, Europe, Africa and other countries around the world every year. These LPG generators are widely used for a diverse range of needs, such as an emergency power source in the event of a power failure, outdoor construction work, outdoor leisure activities and for disasters and other emergencies. Since the company was found in 1999, we always do the best to improve the quality in order to meet the requirements of various customers. Therefore, Victory LPG series engines emerged as required. It inherits the excellent performance of Victory gasoline engines, which have high reliability, high quality, low emissions, and compact design. LPG engine series of generators that incorporated a super light weight and compact constraction offering low fuel consumption and low noise.Because of its stable performance, energy efficiency and environmental protection, more and more people prefer to use this kind of generator.
Features:
1. OHV design permits low fuel consumption and noise
2. Classical combustion-chamber shape and excellent suction exhaust effect
which to improve combustion efficiency
3. Auto decompressing makes starting easy
4. Oil alert automatically shut off the engine
5. Reliable transistorized magneto-ignition system
6. Speedy start, smooth operation and low vibration
7. With the recoil starter which is easy started by a woman
8. With transistor magnet ignition device to ensure the stable perfomance of
ignition & low fuel consumption.
