GOOGOL ENGINE-TECH CO., LTD. CHONGQING, CHINA
The core team of Chongqing Googol Engine-Tech Co.,Ltd. entered the diesel generator set industry in 1991 and initiated the technical research and development of high-horsepower engines in January 2003. In 2006, we made a strategic investment and established a manufacturing base in Yongchuan, Chongqing, formerly known as “Chongqing Cork Engine Technology Co., Ltd.” Due to the development needs of the company, it was renamed “Chongqing Googol Engine-Tech Co.,Ltd.”in 2021.
Googol's product line includes diesel engines, gas engines, and gas generator sets. These products are based on a global mainstream design and power calibration, which has undergone extensive research and development. The use of modular design allows for two different bore and stroke sizes, with cylinder counts ranging from 6 to 20. This results in standby output power ranging from 490 to 3600 kW at 1500 rpm. The company offers dozens of different power engine matrices, ensuring a wide selection to meet diverse customer needs. The system has a maximum output of 3,600 kW at 1,500 rpm and has constructed a matrix of dozens of engines with different power levels. Googol's engines are characterized by low specific power output , high reliability, low exhaust temperature, low fuel consumption, clean emissions, and low operation and maintenance cost. This is due to the company's sharing of supply chain and lean manufacturing with global mainstream engine manufacturers.
Googol has maintained a commitment to technological innovation for many years, consistently introducing competitive engine products to the market. The company has obtained 14 invention patents, applied for trademark registration in 66 countries worldwide, and has passed the ISO9001-2015 quality management system certification. In 2012, it was recognized as the Chongqing Municipal Enterprise Technology Center. In 2013, it became a National High-tech Enterprise, and in 2022, it was designated a National Intellectual Property Advantageous Enterprise. We have been honored with the following distinctions: China Excellent Patent Award, National Intellectual Property Advantageous Enterprise,National Little Giant Enterprise,Chongqing Specialized, Refined, Unique and Innovative Enterprise , Chongqing Outstanding Private Enterprise, Chongqing Top 100 Private Enterprises, and Chongqing Intellectual Property Advantageous Enterprise.
Googol is a global leader in the field, specializing in the development and manufacture of high-speed, high-horsepower diesel and gas engines. These engines provide reliable power to a diverse range of customers, including those in advanced manufacturing, semiconductor, infrastructure, communications, data centers, energy and chemical industries, healthcare, and cultural and educational institutions.
This base is located within the 108-mu municipal green factory park of Googol Power in the Phoenix Lake Industry of Yongchuan High-tech Zone.The site is strategically located in close proximity to major industrial entities, including the Great Wall Motor Chongqing Plant, Liebherr Yongchuan Base, and Yongchuan Free Trade Zone. This proximity fosters a vibrant manufacturing ecosystem, characterized by a strong industrial atmosphere. The property is strategically situated on the south side of Fenglong Avenue, in close proximity to the Great Wall No.1 Gate Station of Bus Line 306 and Yongchuan South Railway Station, offering seamless access to high-speed rail, bus, and self-driving services.

Driven by the vision "Technology Empowering Humanity", we deliver reliable, high-efficiency megawatt-class diesel power solutions worldwide.
We are one of the few global manufacturers with end-to-end capabilities in clean high-power engine. We specialize in R&D, production, sales, and service of advanced engines that set new standards for efficiency and sustainability.
