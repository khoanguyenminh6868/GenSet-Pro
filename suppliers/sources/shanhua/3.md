ABOUT HUALI
Founded in 1999, Shandong Huali Electromechanical Co., Ltd. (Huali Power) manufactures 10–4,000 kVA industrial diesel generator sets , dust suppression fog cannons , and automated guided vehicle (AGV) systems. Its 269,300 m&sup2; manufacturing site in Shandong, China, supports generator assembly and testing, with Cummins, Perkins, Weichai, and Yuchai engine options.
Huali maintains an ISO-certified management system and CE documentation for applicable generator and fog cannon models. Its products have reached customers in over 70 countries, with configurations and technical documentation tailored to project requirements.
Manufacturing Capability: Six diesel generator assembly lines.
Quality & Compliance: ISO management system certification and model-specific CE documentation.
Engineering & Testing: 20 MW generator testing center.
Export Experience: Products supplied to over 70 countries.
