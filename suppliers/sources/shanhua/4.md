About Us
Shandong Huali Electromechanical Co., Ltd
Shandong Huali Electromechanical Co., Ltd., founded in 1999, is a professional manufacturer of diesel generator set , fog cannon , and intelligent AGV systems. With a 269300 m&sup2; modern factory and a certified ISO & CE quality system, we deliver reliable power solutions for industrial, mining, construction, and energy environments.
Our production capability includes 6 assembly lines for diesel generator sets, 7 AGV lines, and a 20MW national-standard testing center ensuring stable performance and fast delivery. Huali’s products are exported to 70+ countries and trusted globally for durability, low maintenance costs, and consistent output.
We provide customized engineering design, rapid shipment, and professional technical support to help customers secure continuous power, improve workplace safety, and reduce operational risks.
