Our Foundation & Scale

Frequently Ask Questions
Q: What are the main advantages of buying a generator directly from ShanHua?
A: By partnering directly with us, you benefit from factory-direct pricing, rigorous quality control at every stage, and the flexibility of full OEM/ODM customization. We pass the savings and quality assurance directly to you.
Q: Can you provide a generator for my specific industry or application?
A: Absolutely. We specialize in creating tailored power solutions. Our engineering team works closely with you to understand your unique operational needs, ensuring we deliver the perfect generator for any application, from industrial manufacturing to critical backup power.
Q: How do I get a price or a formal quote for a generator?
A: Simply use the “Get a Quote” button on our website or email our sales team directly. Provide us with your project details, and our experts will promptly respond with a comprehensive, competitive, and no-obligation quote.
Q: What quality standards do your generators meet?
A: Quality is paramount at ShanHua. Our manufacturing processes are ISO 9001 certified, and our products meet major international standards, including CE and CCC. Every unit is thoroughly tested in our national standard testing center before shipment.
Q: Do you ship and provide support for international clients?
A: Yes. We are a global supplier with extensive experience in shipping our generators worldwide. We manage the entire export process and provide our international clients with comprehensive after-sales and technical support.
Q: What kind of support can I expect after my purchase?
A: Our partnership continues long after the sale. We offer expert technical support from our engineering team to guide you through installation, operation, and best maintenance practices, ensuring the longevity and performance of your generator.
