Other informations
The generator is a key component of any Cummins Diesel Generator, responsible for converting kinetic energy into electrical energy. Two critical factors that determine the performance of a Cummins Generator are the materials used in its construction and the excitation method it employs.
Excitation Method:
All HP series Cummins Diesel Generator models, regardless of size or output, come equipped with a high-performance brushless self-excitation system. This system is designed for low heat generation, zero electric sparks, and lifetime maintenance-free operation — making it ideal for demanding environments.
Brushless motor: The only friction part in the motor is dust. Safer (lifetime maintenance-free)
Materials:
Our HP Cummins Generator series uses top-grade silicon steel sheets sourced from Shanghai Baowu Steel (formerly Baosteel), ensuring superior magnetic performance and durability. In addition, the internal copper windings are manufactured with high-quality copper wire from Guancheng Datong, a reputable publicly listed company known for electrical-grade materials.
Compared to the national standard, the HP generator's internal copper wire content is increased by 20%, which significantly reduces thermal attenuation and enhances energy conversion efficiency. Under the same power output, Cummins Diesel Generator units equipped with HP generators exert less resistance torque on the engine, allowing the diesel engine to operate more efficiently and with less fuel consumption.
As a result, Cummins Diesel Generator for Sale options fitted with HP generators provide excellent load response, greater resistance to sudden load changes, and stable output in terms of current, voltage, and frequency.

Water Tank
Our company uses copper water tanks, not aluminum water tanks. Copper water tanks (cost 50% higher than aluminum water tanks) are corrosion-resistant, have good thermal conductivity, and good ductility; they have a long service life and are maintenance-free for life; they can ensure that the diesel engine does not overheat under extreme conditions, and that full-load operation is more stable. The life of a copper water tank is twice that of an aluminum water tank. Once used frequently, the copper water tank can extend the efficiency of the machine a little bit further under use.
Chassis
The base is an important structural component of a generator set, and the strength of the body depends entirely on the chassis of the unit.
Our company's standard units without boxes are all channel steel or I-beams. The channel steel used by our company is a one-piece hot-formed material with high structural strength and thickened bends; it has better stability, is more solid, and has better load-bearing capacity, ensuring that the unit still maintains the factory installation accuracy after frequent handling.
The shock-absorbing pads installed on the chassis are all high-quality stainless steel bowl-shaped shock-absorbing pads. The force-bearing area is larger. The center of the installation hole is not easy to offset, the service life is longer, and the load-bearing range is configured according to the dynamic load of the unit, which is 3 times the weight of the engine, ensuring that the installation hole of the unit is accurate and stable throughout the life cycle without offset.
