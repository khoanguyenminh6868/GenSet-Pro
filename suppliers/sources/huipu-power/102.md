Materials:
The silicon steel sheets used in our GOOGOL Generator are sourced exclusively from Shanghai Baowu Steel (formerly Shanghai Baosteel), ensuring strong magnetic properties and durability. Accessories
Controller
HG Smart（HGM) / UK DSE
All electronic components used in our control cabinets are ABB or Schneider brands, ensuring the stability of the unit's switching power supply and control circuit, and preventing the machine from false alarms or shutdowns due to circuit breaker relay failures.
Water Tank
Our company uses copper water tanks, not aluminum water tanks. Copper water tanks (cost 50% higher than aluminum water tanks) are corrosion-resistant, have good thermal conductivity, and good ductility; they have a long service life and are maintenance-free for life; they can ensure that the diesel engine does not overheat under extreme conditions, and that full-load operation is more stable. The life of a copper water tank is twice that of an aluminum water tank. Once used frequently, the copper water tank can extend the efficiency of the machine a little bit further under use.
Chassis
The base is an important structural component of a generator set, and the strength of the body depends entirely on the chassis of the unit.
Our company's standard units without boxes are all channel steel or I-beams. The channel steel used by our company is a one-piece hot-formed material with high structural strength and thickened bends; it has better stability, is more solid, and has better load-bearing capacity, ensuring that the unit still maintains the factory installation accuracy after frequent handling.
The shock-absorbing pads installed on the chassis are all high-quality stainless steel bowl-shaped shock-absorbing pads. The force-bearing area is larger. The center of the installation hole is not easy to offset, the service life is longer, and the load-bearing range is configured according to the dynamic load of the unit, which is 3 times the weight of the engine, ensuring that the installation hole of the unit is accurate and stable throughout the life cycle without offset.
