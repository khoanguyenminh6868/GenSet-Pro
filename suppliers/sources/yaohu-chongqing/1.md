Introduction
Chongqing Yaohu Power Machine Co., Ltd. is a leading professional manufacturer of gasoline engines, diesel engines, gasoline generators, diesel generators, water pumps, welding generators, mini tilling machines, high pressure washers, and other power machineries and equipments in China. Our brand ''''YAOHOO'''' enjoys high reputation both home and abroad, and we have established a long term business relationship with customers from all over the world.
Yaohu is located in Chongqing-the heavy industrial city and manufacturing base of power machinery and equipment of Southwest of China. Kohler and Honda have established Joint-venture factory in Chongqing and we share the vendors resources. Yaohu workshop is established in the Xiyong Industrial Park and occupies 20,000 square meters. We employ more than 300 members of staff and R&D staff is more than 20%, who work on three automatic assembly lines and two advanced testing lines.
Yaohu runs in accordance with the ISO9001 quality system. Under strict control, we can guarantee the quality of all the products with our managerial system and high-tech inspecting equipment. The production capacity of our factory is 300,000 sets annually. Yaohu products are widely used in a new line of industrial energy, water resources, transportation, construction, and other areas.
With continuous improvements, we are trying to keep our technology innovation and service in pace with the world''''s most advanced standard to meet the ever-growing demands of our customers. We can offer our customer not only products but also custom-fit power solution service. We strive to offer our valuable customers the best quality products, the most competitive prices and on-time-delivery as well as the enthusiastic pre-sale and post-sale services.
We extend a warm welcome to new customers worldwide, and sincerely hope to establish cooperative business relationships with serious clients who are seeking for the quality products in China.
