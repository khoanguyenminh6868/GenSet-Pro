Diesel Engine Power Unit (SEP series)
Diesel Engine Power Unit (SEP series) is specially designed for underground water pumping system in Saudi Arabian market.
SEP series is produced by Japanese factory with Japanese high quality control system.
Engines can be operated continuously with high reliability and durability under tropical condition.
