Company Profile
Jiangsu Jianghao Generator Co.,Ltd.
Jiangsu Jianghao Generator Set Co., Ltd. is a professional manufacturer of diesel generator sets in China which has been established for 14 years. The total sales of the company exceeded 550 million RMB in 2021. The modern standard workshop has 20,000 square meters, nearly 200 employees, with an annual output of more than 5,000 sets of diesel generator sets of different power sizes. The company has a standardized diesel generator set testing center to ensure that customers' products can be used properly, and the parts warehouse has parts to match all machines. The perfect service center to meet the customer 7 days 24 hours a day, problem communication.
Our products include seven large diesel generator sets: ordinary open frame type, silent type, mobile power station, automatic type, multi-machine interconnection type, unattended, and computerized centralized remote monitoring. The diesel engine part is selected from domestic and imported engine brands according to customers' needs, equipped with high-quality all-copper alternators (can also have customer-specified brands) to provide customers with the most professional power solutions. The power range of fixed diesel generators is 3-3000kw, and mobile power stations are 5-2000kw.
