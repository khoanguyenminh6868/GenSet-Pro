Introduction
The FG Wilson P450-3 diesel generator set delivers 400kVA/320kW prime power and 450kVA/360kW standby power. Powered by a Perkins 2206A-E13TAG3 six-cylinder diesel engine and equipped with an FG Wilson FG29A320 alternator, the generator set provides reliable power for commercial, industrial, construction and standby applications.
Designed for 50Hz operation, the FG Wilson P450-3 features a DSE7420 control panel, ABB main circuit breaker and an 888-litre fuel tank, providing a practical solution for applications requiring dependable and continuous electrical power.
