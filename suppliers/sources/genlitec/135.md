Introduction
The FG Wilson P400-3 diesel generator set delivers 350kVA/280kW prime power and 400kVA/320kW standby power. Powered by a Perkins 2206A-E13TAG2 six-cylinder diesel engine and equipped with an FG Wilson FG29A280 alternator, the generator set provides reliable power for commercial, industrial, construction and standby applications.
Designed for 50Hz operation, the FG Wilson P400-3 features a DSE7420 control panel, ABB main circuit breaker and an 888-litre fuel tank. The sound-attenuated enclosure is fully weatherproof and includes an internal exhaust silencer, providing a practical solution for outdoor and noise-sensitive installations.
