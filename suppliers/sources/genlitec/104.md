Introduction
The GENLITEC POWER® GPC28D5 open type and GPC28S5 silent type diesel generator sets provide 22kW/28kVA prime power and 24kW/30kVA standby power. Powered by a Cummins 4B3.9-G2 four-cylinder water-cooled diesel engine and coupled with a brushless copper alternator, they are designed to deliver stable and reliable power for commercial, industrial, construction and backup applications.
Designed for 50Hz operation at 1500RPM, the generator sets provide three-phase, four-wire power with flexible voltage configurations of 220/380V, 230/400V and 240/415V. The generator package features a 24V DC electric starting system, anti-vibration mountings, engine protection functions and a digital control panel for dependable operation and convenient monitoring.
The GPC28D5 open type is suitable for installations where easy access for inspection and maintenance is important. The GPC28S5 silent type features a weatherproof sound-attenuated enclosure with an internal exhaust silencer, making it suitable for applications where reduced operating noise and environmental protection are required.
