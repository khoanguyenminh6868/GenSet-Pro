Introduction
The FG Wilson P65-5 diesel generator set delivers 60kVA/48kW prime power and 65kVA/52kW standby power. Powered by a Perkins 1103A-33TG2 three-cylinder turbocharged diesel engine and equipped with an FG Wilson FGL20080 alternator, the generator set provides reliable power for commercial, industrial, construction and standby applications.
Designed for 50Hz operation, the FG Wilson P65-5 features a DSE4520 control panel, ABB main circuit breaker and a 145-litre fuel tank. The sound-attenuated enclosure is fully weatherproof and includes an internal exhaust silencer, providing a practical solution for outdoor and noise-sensitive installations.
