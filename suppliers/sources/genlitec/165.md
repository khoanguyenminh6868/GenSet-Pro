Introduction
The GENLITEC POWER® GPC20D5 open type and GPC20S5 silent type diesel generator sets deliver 16kW/20kVA prime power and 17.6kW/22kVA standby power. Powered by a reliable Cummins 4B3.9-G11 water-cooled diesel engine and matched with a brushless copper alternator, these generator sets provide stable and dependable power for commercial, industrial, construction and standby applications.
Designed for 50Hz operation at 1500RPM, the generator sets provide three-phase, four-wire output with voltage options including 220/230V, 230/400V and 240/415V. The GPC20S5 silent type features a weatherproof sound-attenuated enclosure with internal exhaust silencer, while the GPC20D5 open type provides convenient access for installation, inspection and maintenance. Optional ATS, trailer, extended fuel tank and power sockets are available according to application requirements.
