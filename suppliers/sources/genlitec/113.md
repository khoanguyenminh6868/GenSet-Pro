Introduction
The GENLITEC POWER® GYM12D6 open type and GYM12S6 silent type diesel generator sets deliver 9.6kW/12kVA prime power and 10.6kW/13.2kVA standby power. Powered by a YANMAR 3TNV82A-GGE three-cylinder, water-cooled diesel engine and matched with a brushless copper alternator, these 12kVA diesel generator sets provide reliable power for commercial, industrial, construction, agricultural, telecommunications and backup applications.
Designed for 60Hz operation at 1800RPM, the GYM12D6 and GYM12S6 provide three-phase, four-wire power with multiple voltage configurations, including 120/208V, 127/220V, 220/380V and 254/440V. The generator package features a 12V DC electric starting system, mechanical governor, water-cooled engine, anti-vibration mountings and a control panel for convenient operation and monitoring.
The GYM12D6 open type generator set is suitable for installations where ventilation, service access and maintenance convenience are priorities. The GYM12S6 silent type generator set is equipped with a weatherproof sound-attenuated enclosure and internal exhaust silencer, making it suitable for outdoor applications where reduced operating noise and additional environmental protection are required.
