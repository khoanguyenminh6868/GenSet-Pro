Introduction
The GENLITEC POWER® GYM9D6 open type and GYM9S6 silent type diesel generator sets provide 7.2kW/9kVA prime power and 8kW/10kVA standby power. Powered by a YANMAR 3TNV76-GGE three-cylinder, water-cooled diesel engine and coupled with a brushless copper alternator, these generator sets are designed to provide stable and dependable power for commercial, industrial, agricultural, construction and backup applications.
Designed for 60Hz operation at 1800RPM, the generator sets provide three-phase, four-wire power with flexible voltage configurations of 120/208V, 127/220V, 220/380V and 254/440V. The generator package features a DC 12V electric starting system, water-cooled engine, anti-vibration mountings and a control panel for reliable operation and convenient monitoring.
The GYM9D6 open type is suitable for installations where ventilation, service access and convenient maintenance are important. The GYM9S6 silent type can be equipped with a weatherproof sound-attenuated enclosure with an internal exhaust silencer, making it suitable for applications requiring reduced operating noise and improved protection in outdoor environments.
