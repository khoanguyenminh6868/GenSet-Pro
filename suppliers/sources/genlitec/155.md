Introduction
The GENLITEC POWER GPD30D5 / GPD30S5 diesel generator set is a compact and reliable power solution designed for prime, standby and backup power applications.
Powered by a DEUTZ BFM3T G1 diesel engine, the generator set delivers 24kW/30kVA prime power and 26.4kW/33kVA standby power at 50Hz and 1500RPM. Multiple voltage configurations are available, including 220/380V, 230/400V and 240/415V, making it suitable for a wide range of commercial, industrial, telecommunications and backup power applications.

FAQ
Q: What is the rated power of the GPD30D5 / GPD30S5 generator set?
A: The generator set provides 24kW/30kVA prime power and 26.4kW/33kVA standby power.
Q: Which diesel engine is used in the GPD30D5 / GPD30S5?
A: The generator set is powered by a DEUTZ BFM3T G1 four-cylinder, 2.72L, naturally aspirated diesel engine with an in-line pump fuel system and electronic governing.
Q: What voltage configurations are available?
A: The available rated voltage configurations are 220/380V, 230/400V and 240/415V.
Q: What is the difference between the open and silent type generator sets?
A: The GPD30D5 is the open type model, while the GPD30S5 is equipped with a sound-attenuated enclosure for applications where lower noise levels and weather protection are required.
Q: What is the frequency and rated speed of the generator set?
A: The generator set operates at 50Hz with a rated speed of 1500 RPM.
Q: Is remote monitoring available for the generator set?
A: Yes. A Remote Monitoring System is available as an optional configuration for remote status monitoring and generator set management.
Q: What is the warranty period for the GPD30D5 / GPD30S5?
A: GENLITEC POWER provides a standard warranty of 12 months or 1000 working hours, whichever comes first.
