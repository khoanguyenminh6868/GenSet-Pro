Introduction
The GENLITEC POWER GPK8D5 and GPK8S5 are compact 8.4kVA diesel generator sets powered by a reliable Kubota D1105-E2BG-CHN diesel engine. Designed for stable and efficient power generation, this model provides 6.7kW/8.4kVA prime power and 7.4kW/9.2kVA standby power at 50Hz.
Available in open and silent configurations, the GPK8 series combines a compact design, water-cooled diesel engine and reliable alternator technology, making it suitable for backup power, commercial facilities, small industrial applications, construction sites and other locations requiring dependable three-phase power.

Frequently Asked Questions
Q1. What is the rated power of the GPK8D5 and GPK8S5?
A: The generator set provides 6.7kW/8.4kVA prime power and 7.4kW/9.2kVA standby power at 50Hz.
Q2. Which engine is used in the GPK8 series?
A: The GPK8D5 and GPK8S5 are powered by the Kubota D1105-E2BG-CHN three-cylinder diesel engine.
Q3. What is the rated engine power?
A: The Kubota D1105-E2BG-CHN engine has a rated power of 8.4kW and standby power of 9.5kW at 1500 RPM.
Q4. What is the fuel tank capacity and fuel consumption?
A: The standard fuel tank capacity is 50L. Fuel consumption is 2.5L/H at 100% prime load and 1.9L/H at 75% prime load.
Q5. What voltage and frequency does the generator provide?
A: The generator is designed for 50Hz operation with a rated voltage range of 380~415V and a three-phase, four-wire configuration.
Q6. What is the difference between GPK8D5 and GPK8S5?
A: GPK8D5 is the open type model, while GPK8S5 is the silent type model with a sound attenuated enclosure for reduced noise and outdoor use.
Q7. Is the GPK8 suitable for prime power applications?
A: Yes. The GPK8 provides 6.7kW/8.4kVA prime power and can be used for continuous-duty power applications within its rated load range.
Q8. Can the GPK8 be equipped with an automatic transfer switch?
A: Yes. An ATS can be supplied as an optional configuration for automatic mains failure and standby power applications.
Q9. Where can the GPK8 diesel generator be used?
A: It is suitable for commercial buildings, construction sites, workshops, agricultural applications, telecom sites, remote locations and emergency backup power.
Q10. What is the warranty period?
A: GENLITEC POWER provides a standard warranty of 12 months or 1000 working hours, whichever comes first.
