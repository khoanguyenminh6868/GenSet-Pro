Introduction
The GENLITEC POWER GPP18D6 / GPP18S6 diesel generator set is a compact and reliable power solution designed for prime, standby and backup power applications.
Powered by a Perkins 403A-15G2 diesel engine, the generator set delivers 14kW/18kVA prime power and 16kW/20kVA standby power at 60Hz and 1800RPM, with multiple voltage configurations available for different applications.

FAQ
Q: What is the rated power of the GPP18D6 / GPP18S6 generator set?
A: The generator set provides 14kW/18kVA prime power and 16kW/20kVA standby power.
Q: Which diesel engine is used in the GPP18D6 / GPP18S6?
A: The generator set is powered by a Perkins 403A-15G2 3-cylinder, 1.496L, naturally aspirated diesel engine.
Q: What voltage configurations are available?
A: The available rated voltage configurations are 120/208V, 127/220V, 220/380V and 254/440V.
Q: What is the difference between the open and silent type generator sets?
A: The GPP18D6 is the open type model, while the GPP18S6 is equipped with a sound-attenuated enclosure for applications where lower noise levels and weather protection are required.
Q: What is the frequency and rated speed of the generator set?
A: The generator set operates at 60Hz with a rated speed of 1800 RPM.
Q: Can remote monitoring and a water-jacket heater be supplied?
A: Yes. A Remote Monitoring System and Water-Jacket Heater are available as optional equipment.
Q: What is the warranty period for the GPP18D6 / GPP18S6?
A: GENLITEC POWER provides a standard warranty of 12 months or 1000 working hours, whichever comes first.
