Introduction
The GENLITEC POWER GPP20D5 / GPP20S5 diesel generator set is a compact and reliable power solution designed for prime, standby and backup power applications.
Powered by a Perkins 404A-22G1 diesel engine, the generator set delivers 16KW/20KVA prime power and 17.6KW/22KVA standby power at 50Hz, with multiple voltage configurations available for different applications.
