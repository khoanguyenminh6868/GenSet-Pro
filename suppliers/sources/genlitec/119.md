Introduction
The GENLITEC POWER® GSC20D6-II open type and GSC20S6-II silent type diesel generator sets provide 16kW/20kVA prime power and 18kW/22.5kVA standby power. Powered by an SDEC 4Z2.3-G21 four-cylinder, 2.3L water-cooled diesel engine and coupled with a brushless copper alternator, these generator sets are designed to provide stable and dependable power for commercial, industrial, agricultural, construction and backup applications.
Designed for 60Hz operation at 1800RPM, the generator sets provide three-phase, four-wire power with flexible voltage configurations of 120/208V, 127/220V, 220/380V and 254/440V. The generator package features a DC 12V electric starting system, water-cooled engine, electronic governor, anti-vibration mountings and a control panel for reliable operation and convenient monitoring.
The GSC20D6-II open type is suitable for installations where ventilation, service access and convenient maintenance are important. The GSC20S6-II silent type can be equipped with a weatherproof sound-attenuated enclosure with an internal exhaust silencer, making it suitable for applications requiring reduced operating noise and improved protection in outdoor environments.
