Introduction
The FG Wilson P715-3 diesel generator set delivers 650kVA/520kW prime power and 715kVA/572kW standby power. Powered by a Perkins 2806A-E18TAG2 six-cylinder diesel engine and equipped with an FG Wilson FG33A560 alternator, the generator set provides reliable power for commercial, industrial, construction and standby applications.
Designed for 50Hz operation, the FG Wilson P715-3 features a DSE7420 control panel, ABB main circuit breaker and a 1132-litre fuel tank. The sound-attenuated enclosure is fully weatherproof and includes an internal exhaust silencer, providing a practical solution for outdoor and noise-sensitive installations.
