Introduction
The FG Wilson P850-1 diesel generator set delivers 770kVA/616kW prime power and 850kVA/680kW standby power. Powered by a Perkins 2806A-E18TTAG5 six-cylinder diesel engine and equipped with a Leroy Somer LL7224L alternator, the generator set provides reliable power for commercial, industrial, construction and standby applications.
Designed for 50Hz operation, the FG Wilson P850-1 features a DSE7420 control panel, ABB main circuit breaker and a 1702-litre fuel tank. The sound-attenuated enclosure is fully weatherproof and includes an internal exhaust silencer, providing a practical solution for demanding outdoor and noise-sensitive installations.
