Introduction
The FG Wilson P33-3 diesel generator set delivers 30kVA/24kW prime power and 33kVA/26.4kW standby power. Powered by a Perkins 1103A-33G1 three-cylinder diesel engine and equipped with an FG Wilson FGL20030 alternator, the generator set provides reliable power for commercial, industrial, construction and standby applications.
Designed for 50Hz operation, the FG Wilson P33-3 features a DSE4520 control panel, ABB main circuit breaker and a 71-litre fuel tank. The sound-attenuated enclosure is fully weatherproof and includes an internal exhaust silencer, providing a practical solution for outdoor and noise-sensitive installations.
