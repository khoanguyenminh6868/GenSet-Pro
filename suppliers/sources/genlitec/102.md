Introduction
The GENLITEC POWER® GPC150D5 open type and GPC150S5 silent type diesel generator sets provide 120kW/150kVA prime power and 132kW/165kVA standby power. Powered by a Cummins 6BTAA5.9-G12 six-cylinder water-cooled diesel engine and coupled with a brushless copper alternator, they are designed to deliver stable and reliable power for commercial, industrial, construction and backup applications.
Designed for 50Hz operation at 1500RPM, the generator sets provide three-phase, four-wire power with flexible voltage configurations of 220/380V, 230/400V and 240/415V. The package features a 24V DC electric starting system, engine-driven cooling system, anti-vibration mountings and a digital control system for dependable operation and convenient monitoring.

Frequently Asked Questions
Q: What is the rated power of the GPC150D5 and GPC150S5 diesel generator sets?
A: The generator sets provide 120kW/150kVA prime power and 132kW/165kVA standby power.
Q: What diesel engine is used in this generator set?
A: The generator set is powered by a Cummins 6BTAA5.9-G12 six-cylinder, water-cooled diesel engine with turbocharged air-to-air cooling.
Q: What voltage configurations are available?
A: Available voltage configurations include 220/380V, 230/400V and 240/415V, subject to the selected generator configuration.
Q: Is a silent type version available?

Q: Can the generator set be supplied with an ATS?
A: Yes. An ATS can be supplied as an option for automatic generator start and stop when the utility power fails or returns.
