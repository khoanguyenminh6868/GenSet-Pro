Introduction
The GENLITEC POWER GPK11D5/GPK11S5 is a compact 50Hz diesel generator set rated at 9kW/11.3kVA prime power and 10kW/12.5kVA standby power. It is powered by a reliable Kubota V1505-E2BG-CHN diesel engine and designed for dependable standby and prime power applications.
The GPK11D5 is the open type version, while the GPK11S5 features a sound attenuated enclosure for applications requiring reduced operating noise and weather protection. With a 50L fuel tank and compact configuration, the GPK11 series is suitable for commercial, residential, agricultural and remote power applications.

Frequently Asked Questions
Q: What is the rated power of the GPK11D5/GPK11S5 diesel generator?
A: The GPK11D5/GPK11S5 provides 9kW/11.3kVA prime power and 10kW/12.5kVA standby power at 50Hz.
Q: Which diesel engine is used in the GPK11 series?
A: The generator set is powered by a Kubota V1505-E2BG-CHN diesel engine with a 1.498L displacement and 1500 RPM rated speed.
Q: What is the engine power of the Kubota V1505-E2BG-CHN?
A: The Kubota V1505-E2BG-CHN engine has a rated power of 11.1kW and a standby power of 12.5kW.
Q: What is the fuel tank capacity and fuel consumption?
A: The standard fuel tank capacity is 50L. Fuel consumption is approximately 3.28L/H at 100% prime load, 2.42L/H at 75% load and 1.61L/H at 50% load.
Q: What voltage and frequency does the GPK11 generator provide?
A: The GPK11 series operates at 50Hz with a rated voltage range of 380~415V and a three-phase, four-wire configuration.
Q: What is the difference between the GPK11D5 and GPK11S5?
A: The GPK11D5 is the open type model, while the GPK11S5 is equipped with a sound attenuated enclosure for reduced noise and outdoor protection.
Q: Can the GPK11 be used as a prime power generator?
A: Yes. The GPK11D5/GPK11S5 is rated for 9kW/11.3kVA prime power and can be used for applications requiring continuous or regular power generation within its rated capacity.
Q: Can an ATS be installed with the GPK11 generator?
A: Yes. An ATS is available as an option for automatic transfer between the utility power supply and the generator set.
Q: Where can the GPK11D5/GPK11S5 be used?
A: The GPK11 series is suitable for residential and commercial backup power, construction sites, workshops, agricultural applications, telecom sites, remote locations and emergency power.
Q: What is the warranty for the GPK11D5/GPK11S5?
A: GENLITEC POWER provides a standard warranty of 12 months or 1000 working hours, whichever comes first, covering manufacturing defects under normal operation and maintenance.
