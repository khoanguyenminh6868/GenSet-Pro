Introduction
The GENLITEC POWER® GPK7D5 and GPK7S5 are 7kVA Kubota diesel generator sets designed to provide reliable power for residential, commercial, construction, agricultural, backup and other small-scale power applications. The generator sets provide 5.6kW/7kVA prime power and 6kW/7.5kVA standby power at 50Hz.
Powered by the Kubota D905-E2BG-CHN three-cylinder, 0.898-liter water-cooled diesel engine, the GPK7 series combines compact dimensions, efficient operation and dependable power output. The engine features natural aspiration, E-TVCS combustion technology, a Bosch Type PFR MD fuel injection pump and a 22:1 compression ratio.
The GPK7 generator set is available in both open and silent configurations. The GPK7D5 open type is suitable for generator rooms and installations where ventilation and easy maintenance are required, while the GPK7S5 silent type provides additional noise attenuation and protection for outdoor or noise-sensitive applications. A 50L fuel tank is provided for extended operating periods.

Frequently Asked Questions
Q: What is the rated power of the GPK7 Kubota diesel generator set?
A: The GPK7D5 open type and GPK7S5 silent type are rated at 5.6kW/7kVA for prime power and 6kW/7.5kVA for standby power at 50Hz.
Q: Which Kubota engine is used in the 7kVA generator?
A: The GPK7 series is powered by the Kubota D905-E2BG-CHN, a three-cylinder 0.898L water-cooled diesel engine with natural aspiration and E-TVCS combustion technology.
Q: What is the Kubota D905-E2BG-CHN engine output?
A: The Kubota D905-E2BG-CHN provides 6.6kW rated engine power and 7.4kW standby engine power at 1500 RPM.
Q: What is the fuel tank capacity and fuel consumption?
A: The standard GPK7 configuration is equipped with a 50L fuel tank. Fuel consumption is approximately 2.1L/H at 100% prime load and 1.6L/H at 75% prime load. Fuel consumption data at 50% and 25% load is not provided.
Q: What voltage and frequency can the GPK7 generator provide?
A: The GPK7 generator set operates at 50Hz with three-phase, four-wire output. The rated voltage range is 380~415V.
Q: What is the difference between the GPK7D5 and GPK7S5?
A: Both models use the same Kubota D905-E2BG-CHN engine and have the same generator rating. The GPK7D5 is an open type generator set, while the GPK7S5 is equipped with a sound-attenuated enclosure for reduced noise and outdoor installation.
Q: Is the GPK7 suitable for prime power applications?
A: Yes. The GPK7 is rated at 5.6kW/7kVA prime power and can be used for suitable continuous or long-duration power applications when operated within the specified load and environmental conditions.
Q: Can the GPK7 be configured for automatic operation?
A: Yes. ATS, automatic start and stop, remote monitoring and other options can be configured according to the project requirements.
Q: Where can the 7kVA Kubota generator be used?
A: The GPK7 series is suitable for residential buildings, small commercial facilities, farms, construction sites, telecommunications, remote facilities and other applications requiring compact and reliable backup or prime power.
Q: What warranty is provided for the GPK7 series?
A: The standard warranty is 12 months or 1000 working hours, whichever comes first.
