Introduction
The GENLITEC POWER® GPC50D6 open type and GPC50S6 silent type diesel generator sets deliver 40kW/50kVA prime power and 44kW/55kVA standby power. Powered by a reliable CUMMINS 4BT3.9-G2 four-cylinder, turbocharged diesel engine and matched with a brushless copper alternator, these generator sets provide stable and dependable power for commercial, industrial, construction and standby applications.
Designed for 60Hz operation at 1800RPM, the generator sets provide three-phase, four-wire output with voltage options including 120/208V, 127/220V, 220/380V and 254/440V. The GPC50S6 silent type features a weatherproof sound-attenuated enclosure with internal exhaust silencer, while the GPC50D6 open type provides convenient access for installation, inspection and maintenance. Optional ATS, trailer, extended fuel tank and power sockets are available according to application requirements.
