Introduction
The GENLITEC POWER GPD50D5 / GPD50S5 diesel generator set is a compact and reliable power solution designed for prime power, standby power and backup power applications.
Powered by a DEUTZ BFM3C G1 diesel engine with turbocharging and charge-air cooling, the generator set delivers a specified prime output of 40kW/50kVA at 50Hz and 1500RPM. Featuring a 75L fuel tank, a four-cylinder inline engine and multiple voltage configurations, this diesel generator is suitable for commercial facilities, industrial applications, construction sites, agricultural operations and telecommunications installations. The GPD50D5 is the open-type model, while the GPD50S5 features a sound-attenuated enclosure.

FAQ
Q: What is the rated power of the GPD50D5 / GPD50S5 generator set?
A: The specified prime power output is 40kW/50kVA at 50Hz and 1500RPM. The final standby rating should be confirmed against the approved generator set datasheet.
Q: Which diesel engine is used in the GPD50D5 / GPD50S5?
A: The generator set uses the DEUTZ BFM3C G1 four-cylinder, 2.72L diesel engine with turbocharging and charge-air cooling.
Q: What is the fuel tank capacity?
A: The specified fuel tank capacity is 75L. Actual operating time depends on generator load and usable fuel capacity.
Q: What is the fuel consumption of the DEUTZ BFM3C G1 engine?
A: According to the supplied engine datasheet, fuel consumption is approximately 3.3L/h at 25% load, 6.2L/h at 50% load, 9.0L/h at 75% load and 11.6L/h at 100% load. Actual generator set consumption may vary.
Q: What voltage configurations are available?
A: Configurations such as 220/380V, 230/400V and 240/415V may be available, subject to the selected alternator winding and final generator set configuration.
Q: What is the difference between the open and silent type generator sets?
A: The GPD50D5 is the open-type model, while the GPD50S5 features a sound-attenuated enclosure for applications requiring reduced noise and additional weather protection.
Q: What is the warranty period for the GPD50D5 / GPD50S5?
A: GENLITEC POWER provides a standard warranty of 12 months or 1000 working hours, whichever comes first.
