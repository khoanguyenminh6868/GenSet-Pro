Introduction
The GENLITEC POWER® GYM10D5 open type and GYM10S5 silent type diesel generator sets provide 8kW/10kVA prime power and 8.8kW/11kVA standby power. Powered by a YANMAR 3TNV82A-GGE three-cylinder, water-cooled diesel engine and coupled with a brushless copper alternator, these compact generator sets are designed to provide stable and dependable power for commercial, industrial, construction, agricultural and backup applications.
Designed for 50Hz operation at 1500RPM, the generator sets provide three-phase, four-wire power with flexible voltage configurations of 220/380V, 230/400V and 240/415V. The generator package features a 12V DC electric starting system, water-cooled engine, anti-vibration mountings and a control panel for reliable operation and convenient monitoring.
The GYM10D5 open type is suitable for installations where ventilation, service access and convenient maintenance are important. The GYM10S5 silent type can be equipped with a weatherproof sound-attenuated enclosure with an internal exhaust silencer, making it suitable for applications requiring reduced operating noise and improved protection in outdoor environments.
