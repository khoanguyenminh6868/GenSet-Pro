Introduction
The GENLITEC POWER GPD40D5 / GPD40S5 diesel generator set is a compact and reliable power solution designed for prime power, standby power and backup power applications.
Powered by a DEUTZ BFM3T G2 turbocharged diesel engine, the generator set delivers 32kW/40kVA at 50Hz and 1500RPM. Featuring a 60L fuel tank, a four-cylinder inline engine and multiple voltage configurations, this diesel generator is suitable for commercial facilities, industrial applications, construction sites, agricultural operations and telecommunications installations. The GPD40D5 is the open-type model, while the GPD40S5 features a sound-attenuated enclosure.

FAQ
Q: What is the rated power of the GPD40D5 / GPD40S5 generator set?
A: The specified prime power output is 32kW/40kVA at 50Hz and 1500RPM. Please confirm the final standby rating against the approved generator set datasheet.
Q: Which diesel engine is used in the GPD40D5 / GPD40S5?
A: The generator set uses the DEUTZ BFM3T G2 four-cylinder, 2.72L, turbocharged diesel engine with an in-line fuel injection pump.
Q: What is the fuel tank capacity?
A: The standard fuel tank capacity specified for this model is 60L. Actual operating time depends on generator load and usable fuel capacity.
Q: What is the fuel consumption of the DEUTZ BFM3T G2 engine?
A: According to the supplied engine datasheet, fuel consumption is approximately 3.0L/h at 25% load, 5.4L/h at 50% load, 7.9L/h at 75% load and 10.1L/h at 100% load. Actual generator set consumption may vary.
Q: What voltage configurations are available?
A: Configurations such as 220/380V, 230/400V and 240/415V may be available, subject to the selected alternator winding and final generator set configuration.
Q: What is the difference between the open and silent type generator sets?
A: The GPD40D5 is the open-type model, while the GPD40S5 features a sound-attenuated enclosure for applications requiring reduced noise and additional weather protection.
Q: What is the warranty period for the GPD40D5 / GPD40S5?
A: GENLITEC POWER provides a standard warranty of 12 months or 1000 working hours, whichever comes first.
