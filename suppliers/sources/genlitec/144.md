Introduction
The GENLITEC POWER® GPK8D6 and GPK8S6 are 8kVA Kubota diesel generator sets designed to provide reliable power for residential, commercial, construction, agricultural, backup and other small-scale power applications. The generator sets provide 6.4kW/8kVA prime power and 7kW/8.8kVA standby power at 60Hz.
Powered by the Kubota D905-E2BG-CHN three-cylinder, 0.898-liter water-cooled diesel engine, the GPK8 series combines compact dimensions, efficient operation and dependable power output. The engine provides 7.8kW prime power and 8.9kW standby power at 1800 RPM, and features natural aspiration, E-TVCS combustion technology, a Bosch Type PFR MD fuel injection pump and a 22:1 compression ratio.
The GPK8 generator set is available in both open and silent configurations. The GPK8D6 open type is suitable for generator rooms and installations where ventilation and easy maintenance are required, while the GPK8S6 silent type provides additional noise attenuation and protection for outdoor or noise-sensitive applications. A 45L fuel tank is provided for extended operating periods.

FAQ
Q: What is the rated power of the GPK8 Kubota diesel generator set?
A: The GPK8D6 open type and GPK8S6 silent type provide 6.4kW/8kVA prime power and 7kW/8.8kVA standby power at 60Hz.
Q: Which Kubota engine is used in the 8kVA generator?
A: The GPK8 series is powered by the Kubota D905-E2BG-CHN, a three-cylinder 0.898L water-cooled diesel engine with natural aspiration and E-TVCS combustion technology.
Q: What is the Kubota D905-E2BG-CHN engine output?
A: The engine provides 7.8kW prime power and 8.9kW standby power at 1800 RPM.
Q: What is the fuel tank capacity and fuel consumption?
A: The standard fuel tank is 45L. Fuel consumption is approximately 2.5L/H at 100% prime load.
Q: What voltage and frequency can the GPK8 generator provide?
A: The GPK8 generator set operates at 60Hz with three-phase, four-wire output. Available rated voltages are 120/208V, 127/220V and 220/380V.
Q: What is the difference between GPK8D6 and GPK8S6?
A: Both use the same Kubota engine and ratings. GPK8D6 is open type, while GPK8S6 has a sound-attenuated enclosure for reduced noise and outdoor installation.
Q: Is the GPK8 suitable for prime power applications?
A: Yes. It is rated at 6.4kW/8kVA prime power and can be used for suitable continuous or long-duration applications when operated within specified load and environmental conditions.
Q: Can the GPK8 be configured for automatic operation?

Q: Where can the 8kVA Kubota generator be used?
A: It is suitable for residential buildings, small commercial facilities, farms, construction sites, telecommunications, remote facilities and other compact reliable backup or prime power applications.
