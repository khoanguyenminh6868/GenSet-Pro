Introduction
The FG Wilson P780-1 diesel generator set delivers 700kVA/560kW prime power and 780kVA/624kW standby power. Powered by a Perkins 2806A-E18TTAG4 six-cylinder diesel engine and equipped with a Leroy Somer LL7224J alternator, the generator set provides reliable power for commercial, industrial, construction and standby applications.
Designed for 50Hz operation, the FG Wilson P780-1 features a DSE7420 control panel, ABB main circuit breaker and a 1702-litre fuel tank. The sound-attenuated enclosure is fully weatherproof and includes an internal exhaust silencer, providing a practical solution for outdoor and noise-sensitive installations.
