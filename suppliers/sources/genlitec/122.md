The FG Wilson P900-1 is a high-performance diesel generator set designed to provide reliable prime and standby power for demanding industrial and commercial applications. Powered by a robust Perkins 4006-23TAG3A diesel engine and equipped with a FG Wilson FGL70030 alternator, this generator delivers dependable power output, excellent durability and stable performance for continuous and emergency power requirements.
The P900-1 generator set provides 800kVA/640kW prime power and 892kVA/713.6kW standby power at 50Hz, with 380/400/415V three-phase output. It features a DSE7420 digital controller, original ABB main circuit breaker, engine-driven radiator and a large 1494-litre fuel tank. The sound-attenuated enclosure and heavy-duty construction make the FG Wilson P900-1 suitable for industrial plants, construction projects, data centres, infrastructure facilities and other applications requiring dependable high-capacity power generation.
