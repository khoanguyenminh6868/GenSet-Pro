Introduction
The GENLITEC POWER® GPC30D5 open type and GPC30S5 silent type diesel generator sets provide 24kW/30kVA prime power and 26kW/33kVA standby power. Powered by a Cummins 4B3.9-G12 four-cylinder water-cooled diesel engine and coupled with a brushless copper alternator, they are designed to deliver stable and reliable power for commercial, industrial, construction and backup applications.
Designed for 50Hz operation at 1500RPM, the generator sets provide three-phase, four-wire power with flexible voltage configurations of 220/380V, 230/400V and 240/415V. The package includes a 24V DC electric starting system, engine-driven cooling system, anti-vibration mountings and a digital control system for dependable operation and convenient monitoring.
The GPC30D5 open type is suitable for installations where ventilation and service access are priorities, while the GPC30S5 silent type features a weatherproof sound-attenuated enclosure with an internal exhaust silencer, making it suitable for applications where reduced operating noise and environmental protection are required.

Frequently Asked Questions
Q: What is the rated power of the GPC30D5 and GPC30S5 diesel generator sets?
