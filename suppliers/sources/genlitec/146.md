Introduction
The GENLITEC POWER® GPK10D6 open type and GPK10S6 s4ilent type diesel generator sets deliver 8kW/10kVA prime power and 8.8kW/11kVA standby power. Powered by a reliable Kubota D1105-E2BG-CHN water-cooled three-cylinder diesel engine and matched with a high-quality brushless alternator, these generator sets provide stable and dependable power for commercial, industrial, construction, agricultural and standby applications.
Designed for 60Hz operation at 1800RPM, the generator sets provide three-phase, four-wire output with voltage options including 120/208V, 127/220V and 220/380V. The GPK10S6 silent type features a weatherproof sound-attenuated enclosure with internal exhaust silencer, while the GPK10D6 open type provides convenient access for installation, inspection and maintenance. Optional ATS, trailer, extended fuel tank and power sockets are available according to application requirements.

Frequently Asked Questions
Q: What is the rated power of the GPK10D6 and GPK10S6 diesel generator sets?
A: The generator sets provide 8kW/10kVA prime power and 8.8kW/11kVA standby power.
Q: What diesel engine is used in this generator set?
A: The generator set is powered by a Kubota D1105-E2BG-CHN three-cylinder, water-cooled diesel engine.
Q: What frequency does the GPK10D6/GPK10S6 generator set use?
A: The generator set is designed for 60Hz operation at a rated speed of 1800RPM.
Q: What voltage configurations are available?
A: Available voltage configurations include 120/208V, 127/220V and 220/380V, subject to the selected generator configuration.
Q: Is a silent type version available?
A: Yes. The GPK10S6 is the silent type version equipped with a weatherproof sound-attenuated enclosure and internal exhaust silencer.
Q: What is the fuel tank capacity of the GPK10D6/GPK10S6?
A: The standard fuel tank capacity is 50 liters.
Q: How much fuel does the generator consume at full prime load?
A: The fuel consumption at 100% prime load is approximately 3.0L/H.
Q: What type of alternator is used?
A: The generator set is equipped with a three-phase, four-pole alternator with self-excited excitation and an A.V.R. voltage regulator.
Q: Can the GPK10D6/GPK10S6 be supplied with an ATS?
A: Yes. An ATS can be supplied as an option for automatic generator start and stop when the utility power fails or returns.
Q: What warranty is provided for the GPK10D6/GPK10S6?
A: The standard warranty is 12 months or 1000 working hours, whichever comes first.
