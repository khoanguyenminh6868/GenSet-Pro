WE ARE WORLDWIDE
Founded in 2016, GENLITEC POWER specializes in the manufacturing and international export of diesel generator sets and mobile lighting towers. Serving customers and distributors in more than 50 countries, we provide reliable power equipment for construction, mining, oil and gas, infrastructure, telecommunications, commercial, industrial and other applications.
Our 66,000-square-meter production facility includes more than 7,000 square meters of dedicated generator and lighting tower assembly workshops. With experienced engineering and production teams, GENLITEC POWER manufactures approximately 2,500 diesel generator sets and mobile lighting towers annually, supporting both standard and customized power equipment requirements.

10+ YEARS OF EXPERIENCE
With over 10 years of experience in diesel generator manufacturing and export, GENLITEC POWER understands the practical needs of customers in different international markets. We work with distributors, contractors and end users, supplying diesel generator sets and mobile lighting towers for a wide range of applications.
Our experience covers product selection, power configuration, production, testing, packing and shipment. We understand different requirements for power rating, voltage, frequency, engine, alternator and control systems.
With practical experience in different projects and markets, our team can communicate efficiently, recommend suitable configurations and provide technical and spare parts support when customers need assistance.
FACTORY DIRECT PRICING
As a manufacturer and exporter, GENLITEC POWER offers competitive factory-direct prices for diesel generator sets and mobile lighting towers. Working directly with customers and distributors helps reduce unnecessary middleman costs and keeps the purchasing process simple.
We offer different engine, alternator and control system options to meet various power requirements and budgets. Our team recommends practical configurations based on the actual application, helping customers get the right equipment without paying for unnecessary features.
For distributors and project customers, we can also provide quotations based on specific quantities and requirements, offering a straightforward purchasing experience from factory to delivery.
TESTED BEFORE DELIVERY
Every GENLITEC POWER generator set is carefully inspected and tested before shipment. Our production team checks the engine, alternator, control system and other key components to make sure the equipment meets the required specifications.
Depending on the project requirements, testing can include starting performance, voltage, frequency, protection functions and load operation. This helps us identify potential issues before the generator leaves our factory.
By testing the equipment before delivery, we aim to make sure each generator arrives ready for installation and operation, giving customers greater confidence in their purchase.
PRACTICAL TECHNICAL SUPPORT
We understand that reliable support is important when customers operate equipment far from the factory. Our team provides practical technical assistance for diesel generator sets and mobile lighting towers throughout the service process.
When technical questions arise, we can provide wiring information, troubleshooting guidance, operating instructions and other technical support to help customers identify and solve problems efficiently.
We also provide spare parts assistance for the equipment we supply, helping distributors, contractors and end users keep their generators running reliably and reduce unnecessary downtime.
GLOBAL MARKET EXPERIENCE
GENLITEC POWER supplies diesel generator sets and mobile lighting towers to customers in different international markets. We understand that power requirements can vary from one country, application and project to another.
Our team can work with customers to select suitable power ratings, voltage, frequency, engines, alternators and control systems according to their specific requirements. We also understand different expectations for equipment configuration, documentation and delivery.
With experience in different markets and applications, we aim to make international purchasing easier by providing practical configurations, clear communication and reliable equipment for each project.
