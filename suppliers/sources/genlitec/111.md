Introduction
The GENLITEC POWER GPP13D5-I / GPP13S5-I is a 10KW/12.5KVA prime diesel generator set with 11KW/14KVA standby power, designed for reliable prime, standby and backup power applications. It operates at 50Hz and 1500 RPM, with rated voltage configurations of 220/380V, 230/400V and 240/415V.
Powered by a Perkins 403A-15G1 3-cylinder diesel engine and matched with a 10.8KW/13.5KVA alternator, the GPP13D5-I open type and GPP13S5-I silent type generator sets provide a practical power solution for commercial buildings, industrial facilities, construction sites, agricultural applications, telecommunications and emergency backup power systems.
