Introduction
The GENLITEC POWER GPP9D5 / GPP9S5 diesel generator set is a compact and reliable power solution designed for prime, standby and backup power applications.
Powered by a Perkins 403A-11G1 diesel engine, the generator set delivers 7KW/9KVA prime power and 8KW/10KVA standby power at 50Hz, with multiple voltage configurations available for different applications.
