Weifang Yidaneng Power Co., Ltd. (http://www.yidaneng.com) was established in 2009 with 36,000㎡ .The company has a registered capital of 10 million yuan, a high-tech enterprise. The company has registered the "YDN POWER" trademark brand. YDN is located in Shandong Province. It is mainly engaged in the production and operation of diesel generator sets, gas engines and gas generator sets, and the investment and operation of gas power stations.
The company has passed ISO9001, CE for many years. The company has intensively cultivated industry segments, and at the same time increased its investment in innovation, and started in the field of gas power generation. In order to respond to energy conservation and emission reduction, new environmental protection products are developed. YDN brand natural gas generator sets, biogas generator sets, liquefied petroleum gas generator sets, biomass gas generator sets, etc. have been introduced.
Natural gas generator sets provide main and backup power sources for natural gas, gas stations and oil fields; biogas generator sets are equipped with waste heat recovery equipment to provide power and heating for farms and biogas digesters. It can not only make full use of resources, save costs, but also save energy and protect the environment. The main customers are Sinopec, CNPC Gas, etc in domestic market. At the same time, the company has self-support import and export rights, and its products are exported to foreign countries.
Weifang Yidaneng Power Co., Ltd. has always been adhering to the corporate tenet of customer first and service first. The company pays attention to customer satisfaction and after-sales service. Promoting product sales with after-sales is the company's corporate sales philosophy. With the trust of customers and the support of international brands, Yidaneng Power Co., Ltd. will expand and strengthen gas generator sets and diesel generator sets persistently.
