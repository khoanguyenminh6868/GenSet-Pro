Product Description
TFX series compound excitation a.c. synchronous alternator generator
Description :
TFX series three-phase compound excitation synchronous generators by introducing and developing on foreign advanced technology of well-known generators, are our newly developed products. The generators are self-excited by compound excitation, have high reliability, easy maintenance. excellent dynamic characteristics when load or unload suddenly.The characteristic of generators see as the followings:
1. The generators are made as per standard JB/T3320.2-2000. And in conformity to IEC34-1.
2. The construction of the generators are salient-pole rotating field , Insulation is class B or F, drip-proof type is IP22.
3. Tight in construction for steel frame, beautiful appearance.
4. Unique construction for ventilating to windings.
5. High quality of AVRs have excellent characteristics .
6. Excellent dynamic characteristics when load or unload suddenly;Strong starting system, even the motors only with its 70% capacity volume can be directly started and the switch will not trip.
7. Generally the generators are two-bearing construction, also can produce for single-bearing construction.
8. The units can be connected for single-phase, but rated power only is 1/3 of three-phase units.
