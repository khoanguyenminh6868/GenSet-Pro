Why choose our CHP?
- With CE certification.
- Comprehensive heat insulation to minimize heat loss and with high overall efficiency
- CHP inner pipelines are made of full stainless steel material.
- Maximum lower the noise level ensure the CHP is available to run in office building and residence site.
- Fully open design, maintenance friendly thanks to easy access.

- Water-cooled 4-cylinders engine suitable for 24hours continuous running and low speed lower noise level.

- Recover heat from engine jacket water and exhaust
- Sevenpower exhaust heat exchanger made of full stainless steel material which is with longer service life
