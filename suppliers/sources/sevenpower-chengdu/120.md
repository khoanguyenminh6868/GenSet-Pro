FAQ:
Q1: What is Natural Gas CHP?
A1: Natural Gas CHP, short for Combined Heat and Power, is a SevenPower product manufactured in China. It's a type of cogeneration system that captures the heat generated during electricity generation to provide heating and hot water.
Q2: What are the benefits of Natural Gas CHP?
A2: Natural Gas CHP provides many benefits, such as improved operational efficiency, reduced energy costs, and reduced emissions.
Q3: What type of fuel is used for Natural Gas CHP?
A3: Natural Gas CHP uses natural gas as its fuel source. LPG or Biogas is available on request.
Q4: What is the purpose of a Natural Gas CHP system?
A4: The purpose of a Natural Gas CHP system is to generate electricity and capture the heat generated during the process for use in heating and hot water.
Q5: What is the efficiency of a Natural Gas CHP system?
A5: The efficiency of a Natural Gas CHP system is typically between 85%-90%. This means that up to 90% of the energy in the fuel is converted to useful energy.
