Product description
Cummins is the world’s largest designer, manufacturer, and seller of diesel generators and compressed natural gas engines. In China, Cummins has invested over US$140 million, encompassing Dongfeng Cummins and Chongqing Cummins diesel generator sets. As the largest foreign‑invested player in China’s engine industry, Cummins operates eight joint‑venture and wholly owned manufacturing facilities in the country, with Dongfeng Cummins producing B-, C‑, and L‑series diesel engines, and Chongqing Cummins manufacturing M‑, N‑, and K‑series diesel engines.
