MAMO POWER set up in 2004 belongs to Bubugao Electronics Industry Co., Ltd. The production base covers an area of 37000 square meters. We have obtained CE certification, passed ISO9001, ISO14001, OHSAS1800 certification and obtained many invention patents.As a professional generator sets manufacturer, MAMO POWER work on R & D, manufacture, sales and service, Mamo strategy has always been positioned at the power system solution provider. Mamo power can customize overall power solution personalized according to customers’ personalized demand. Relying on the strong R & D team and technical advantages, Mamo products can be specially designed and developed according to the different needs of different customers, and continue to provide customers with product upgrading, function transformation and other follow-up improvement services based on customer needs that formed a unique Mamo business model. The design ability of personalized power system solution is the foundation of core competitiveness and high added value. According to the different needs of customers, the intelligent function, noise reduction ability, high temperature resistance, frost resistance, corrosion resistance and seismic function modules are combined and integrated to realize the continuous improvement of the added value of the products, without relying on upstream suppliers and outsourcing manufacturers.
