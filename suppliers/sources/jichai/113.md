Specification of 4000 Series
In recent years, with the improvement and promotion of H16V series gensets, The market share of Jichai gas generator sets in the gas power generation market, especially in Shanxi, has been increasing year by year, and the market share of 1000kW low-concentration gas generator sets has reached more than 90%. However, At present, Jichai does not meet the market requirements within the power range of 1000kW~2000kW. It is necessary to develop 190 series gas generator sets covering the power segment of 1000kW~2000kW, so as to maintain the cost advantage over foreign gensets, expand the power coverage, continue to improve the competitiveness of 190 series gas generator sets and maintain the competitive advantage of providing firewood.
The single power of the long-stroke 20V190 gas engine covers the power segment of 1000kW~2000kW, and the gas type can take into account natural gas, gas and biogas, which has a broad market demand prospect.
