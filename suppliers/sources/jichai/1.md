CNPC JICHAI POWER
Originated in Jinan China, started in 1920
China National Petroleum Corporation (CNPC) Jichai Power Company Limited founded in 1920, is the only power equipment manufacturer affiliated to CNPC and the world's mainstream drilling power service provider.
The main business of Jichai is R & D and manufacturing of internal-combustion engine, compressor and relative power equipment. The main technical index of leading products approaches or reaches the world`s advanced level, Jichai market share is in the forefront of the same industry in China...
