Provide professional one-stop power solutions
ASTRA (FUAN) POWER TECHNOLOGY CO.,LTD., founded in 2014, registered in 2024,located in Fuan city,Fujian Province,China.
We’re a modern factory,occupying a total space of 6000M2,specializing in R&D and manufacturing of diesel generator sets, AC synchronous alternators and related accessories with a history of 10 years.
Relying on scientific management and advanced technique, we adopt the numerical-control process and strict quality inspection for ensuring the high performance of our production also economic. Our products have obtained CE and ISO9001 certificate, could meet the modern requirements on appearance design,moreover, have excellent performance.
We have a team of professional technicians to provide technical support for our business development. Not only to release more new products, but also to offer final solution to our users, which help our customers to win more market share.
We warmly welcome you joining with us and hope that will bring mutual benefit to both parties in the upcoming future.
