Our STAMFORD and AvK alternators are produced in five manufacturing facilities across the globe: Xinrong (China), Xiangjiang (China), Craiova (Romania), Ahilyanagar (formerly Ahmednagar) (India) and Ranjangaon (India), and world-class alternator technical testing and development services in Stamford (United Kingdom).
China
The first STAMFORD brand alternators manufactured in China began back in 1996 in Wuxi city, Jiangsu Province where two manufacturing facilities exist.

Romania
The STAMFORD | AvK plant in Craiova was founded in 1999 and has been distributing alternators all over the world from the heart of Europe.

India
The first STAMFORD factory was built in Ahilyanagar (formerly Ahmednagar) in 1992 with a second to follow in Ranjangaon in 2007 and production has risen ever since.

United Kingdom
Headquartered in the UK, STAMFORD | AvK's heritage dates back to 1904 in the United Kingdom. Our Stamford facility based in Lincolnshire, UK, delivers world-class alternator technical testing and development services, with compliance to industry standards.

Our STAMFORD and AvK alternators are produced in five manufacturing facilities across the globe: Xinrong (China), Xiangjiang (China), Craiova (Romania), Ahilyanagar (formerly Ahmednagar) (India) and Ranjangaon (India), and world-class alternator technical testing and development services in Stamford (United Kingdom).
China - Xinrong Plant, Wuxi
Founded in 1996, our Wuxi facilities represent the first STAMFORD brand alternators manufactured in China. Located in Jiangsu Province, this facility has been instrumental in our growth across the Asia-Pacific region.
Read More
China - Xiangjiang Plant, Wuxi
Our second manufacturing facility in Wuxi, expanding our production capacity in China. This modern facility incorporates advanced manufacturing technologies and quality control systems for enhanced efficiency.
Read More
Romania - Craiova Plant, Craiova
Founded in 1999, our Craiova facility has been distributing alternators all over the world from the heart of Europe. This strategic location provides excellent access to European and global markets.
Read More
India - Ahilyanagar Plant
The first STAMFORD factory built in India in 1992. This facility has been a cornerstone of our Indian operations, serving the growing South Asian market with high-quality alternators.
Read More
India - Ranjangaon Plant
Opened in 2007 as Cummins' first truly "green" facility. This state-of-the-art plant incorporates sustainable features that minimize environmental impact while maintaining exceptional production standards.
Read More
United Kingdom - Stamford, UK
Located in Stamford, UK, where our heritage began in 1904. This facility delivers world-class alternator technical testing and development services with compliance to industry standards.
