China Manufacturing
Powering innovation since 1996 with world-class manufacturing excellence in Wuxi, Jiangsu Province
China
Leading power generation solutions in the Asia-Pacific region.
STAMFORD | AvK (Cummins Generator Technologies) (China) Co., Ltd. was founded in 1996 in Wuxi City, Jiangsu Province, with a total investment of more than $40 million USD. We are the only authorised company that manufactures and sells STAMFORD and AvK brand alternators in China.
Our Wuxi facilities have helped the company grow together with employees, customers and communities while focusing on developing our global technologies and network within a region full of commercial viability. Through strategic partnerships and continuous innovation, we've established ourselves as a cornerstone of power generation excellence in China.
With nearly three decades of experience in the Chinese market, we continue to expand our capabilities and strengthen our commitment to delivering reliable, efficient power solutions that meet the evolving needs of our customers across diverse industries.
