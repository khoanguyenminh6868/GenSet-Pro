CDLO250PSOA | Industrial Generator Set
CCEC CDLO250PSOA Industrial Generator uses the QSNT-G1 generator drive engine,the engine is V Type 12-Cylinder, 14 liters (855 in3) Four-Strokes Engine with 140 mm (5.5 in) Bore and 152 mm (6.0 in) Stroke.
QSNT-G1 Generator Drive Engine Equipped with Direct Injection CMI PT Fuel System, Comes with Turbocharged and Charge Air Cooled.The Standby Power for 50 Hz Generator Set (1500 RPM) of CCEC QSNT-G1 Generator Drive Engine is 525 HP (392 kW). The Prime Power for 50 Hz Generator Set (1500 RPM) of CCEC QSNT-G1 Generator Drive Engine is 480 HP ( 358 kW). The Continuous Power for 50 Hz Generator Set (1500 RPM) of CCEC QSNT-G1 Generator Drive Engine is 430 HP ( 321 kW).
As Part of EMAC, SinoGen Also Provide Complete Power Supply Solutions that Powered by CCEC, Our Ranges of Products Including Open Type Generator Set, Silence Type Generator Set, Super Silence Type Generator Set, Mobile Trailer, Complete Power Truck and Complete Power Car for Railways Applications.
We Provide Full Life Cycle Services for All Customers, From Design to Power System Supply, from Installation to Commissioning, from After-Sales Service Training to Spare Parts Supply, From Trouble Shooting to Overhaul Technical Support.
Advantages of CCEC CDLO250SA Industrial Generator
Cummins unique PT with fixture and Step Timing control for precise fuel injection make the fuel injection pressure above 120 Mpa, which leads to an excellent fuel consumption rate and emission performance. Check valve in fuel tube improve reliability.
Great flux gear pump, lube oil filter and by-pass oil filter can separate impurity above 10 um effectively. Keep lubrication system in good state and make the engine more reliability.
Gear water pump and great flux channel design provide effective cooling. Spin-in water filter makes the coolant clean and keeps it in normal acidity.
Advanced Holset turbocharger, pulse exhaust manifold reduce exhaust temperature, provide excellence fuel consumption and emission performance.
