Elitech Power supplies genuine Mecc Alte alternators from 5kVA to 2,500kVA, including the ECO, ECP, UniStar, and SPA series from Mecc Alte S.p.A. — Italy’s leading alternator manufacturer with over 60 years of production experience.
Mecc Alte alternators are widely specified across Europe, the Middle East, and Asia for their high efficiency, compact dimensions, and exceptional quality-to-price ratio. Available as standard or optional equipment on Elitech Power’s full range of diesel generators, Mecc Alte alternators feature a brushless, self-excited design with the DECS digital AVR system for precise voltage regulation. Their stamped steel lamination core and Class H vacuum pressure impregnated windings deliver industry-leading thermal performance and long service life even in high ambient temperature environments.
The ECO series covers 5kVA to 650kVA in compact, lightweight frames ideal for genset OEM applications and rental fleet generators. The ECP series extends to 2,500kVA for large industrial and power plant applications. UniStar series units offer a unique single-bearing design with extended shaft options for flexible engine coupling configurations.
