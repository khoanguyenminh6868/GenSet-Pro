Elitech Power supplies Acelite brushless alternators from 8kVA to 4000kVA — a reliable, cost-effective alternator solution manufactured to international quality standards for industrial diesel generator applications.
Acelite alternators offer an outstanding price-performance ratio, making them the standard alternator specification for many of Elitech Power’s budget-tier and mid-range generator sets targeting emerging markets in Africa, Southeast Asia, and the Middle East.
Acelite alternators feature a brushless, self-excited design with a digital AVR for stable voltage output, 100% copper windings with Class H vacuum pressure impregnated insulation, and heavy-duty cast iron end shields for robust mechanical performance. The 2/3 pitch winding configuration minimises harmonic distortion and ensures compatibility with sensitive electronic loads.
All Acelite alternators are manufactured at an ISO 9001-certified facility and comply with IEC 60034 and ISO 8528-3 standards. SAE bell housing options ensure direct compatibility with Cummins, Perkins, Yuchai, SDEC, Ricardo, and other popular diesel engine brands. Available in single-phase and three-phase configurations across 50Hz and 60Hz frequencies. Ideal for wholesalers, rental companies, and project developers seeking a reliable, internationally certified alternator at a competitive cost.
