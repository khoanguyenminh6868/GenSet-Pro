The 15kVA Quanchai Silent Generator is a 15kVA / 12kW diesel generator set powered by the Quanchai QC385D engine, operating at 50Hz. Quanchai is a publicly listed Chinese engine manufacturer specialising in compact diesel engines for the 13kVA to 150kVA range, widely used in rural electrification and light commercial applications.
This generator set is purpose-built for residential backup power, retail shops, clinics, telecom shelters, and light commercial use , providing dependable standby and prime power with low fuel consumption and minimal maintenance requirements.
