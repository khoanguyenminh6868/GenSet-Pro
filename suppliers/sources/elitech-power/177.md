The 200kVA Yunnei Diesel Generator is a 200kVA / 160kW diesel generator set powered by the Yunnei YN6L280 engine, operating at 50Hz. Yunnei Power (Kunming) specialises in compact, lightweight diesel engines for tropical and subtropical climates, widely deployed in Southeast Asia, South Asia, and East Africa in the 13kVA to 200kVA range.
This generator set is purpose-built for hospitals, data centers, manufacturing plants, and medium-scale commercial developments , providing dependable standby and prime power with low fuel consumption and minimal maintenance requirements. CE certified, ISO 9001:2015 manufactured. SGS inspection reports available. Manufactured at Elitech Power’s ISO 9001-certified facility in Fuzhou, China.
