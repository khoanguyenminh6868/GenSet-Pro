Marathon Generators has been a trusted name in reliable power generation for over 60 years. Whether you’re looking for a reliable emergency backup generator or a high-performance power solution for daily operations, Marathon Generators has you covered.
Our generators are designed for rugged durability and tailored to meet a wide range of applications. With power output options ranging from 5 kW to 4000 kW, We provide solutions for both small and large-scale power needs.
Elitech Power supplies genuine Marathon Electric alternators from 5kVA to 3,000kVA, including the Marathon 4P series — one of the most widely used alternator families in the North American and global industrial generator market.
Marathon Electric, a brand of Regal Rexnord Corporation (USA), has supplied alternators to the power generation industry for over 60 years, with an established reputation for rugged construction and dependable performance in the most demanding environments.
Marathon Electric alternators are available as optional equipment on Elitech Power’s Cummins, Perkins, and SDEC generator sets, particularly for projects destined for North American markets or where UL listing compliance is required. Their brushless, self-excited design with the DVR2000E digital AVR delivers ±0.5% voltage regulation under steady-state conditions. The 2/3 pitch copper winding minimises harmonic distortion, and Class H insulation with vacuum pressure impregnation ensures outstanding thermal performance. All units are manufactured to IEEE 115, NEMA MG1, and IEC 60034 standards. SAE bell housing configurations ensure compatibility with all major diesel engine brands. Single-bearing and double-bearing options are available across the full power range.
