Description
The R38 is the latest addition to FPT's innovative portfolio, representing a major advancement in the low-displacement power generation field. This four-cylinder G-Drive engine, designed with a compact footprint, integrates the engine, radiator, and air filter into a compact package. Single-side servicing facilitates and speeds up maintenance activities to ensure maximum uptime.
Featuring a simple and reliable mechanical pump, the 3.8-liter engine is equipped with turbocharger, and it covers power outputs from 50 kVA to 60 kVA. The R38 is fully compliant with EU standards and seamlessly integrates with the rest of FPT's ecosystem.
