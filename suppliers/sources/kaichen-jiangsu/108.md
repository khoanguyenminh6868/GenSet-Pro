Product Introduction
Products Description
The 320kW diesel generator set is designed to deliver stable and continuous power output for industrial and project-based applications. Built with a high-performance Cummins diesel engine, the unit ensures reliable operation under heavy load conditions and supports long working hours in demanding environments.
This model is widely used for factory backup power, construction projects, and infrastructure applications, where consistent performance and fuel efficiency are essential.
Core Configuration
Engine: Cummins NTAA855-G7
Alternator: KCC-375GF
Controller: Automatic digital control system
Optional: ATS, silent canopy, remote monitoring
Key Features
1. Reliable Power Output for Continuous Operation
The NTAA855-G7 engine provides sufficient power margin to ensure the generator can deliver stable output during long-term operation, supporting continuous industrial use.
2. High Efficiency Fuel Performance
Cummins engine technology ensures optimized fuel consumption under load, helping reduce operating costs in long-hour applications.
3. Stable Performance Under Variable Load
Designed to handle load fluctuations, suitable for equipment with high starting current and dynamic power demand.
4. Flexible Alternator Options
Standard configuration includes a reliable industrial alternator, with optional brands available to meet different project requirements.
5. Industrial Durability
Heavy-duty structure with water-cooled system ensures long service life and stable performance in harsh environments.

FAQ
Q: Is 320kW suitable for industrial use?
A: Yes, this model is designed for medium to large-scale industrial applications and continuous operation.
Q: Can the alternator brand be customized?
A: Yes, different alternator brands can be selected based on project requirements.
Q: Is it suitable for high starting load equipment?
A: Yes, it can handle load fluctuations and high starting current applications.
Q: Can this generator be used for prime power instead of standby?
A: Yes, the generator can be configured for both standby and prime power applications. For continuous operation, the load should be within the prime power rating to ensure stable performance and longer service life.
Q: What type of fuel consumption should I expect in real operation?
A: Fuel consumption depends on load conditions. At around 70–80% load, the generator operates at optimal efficiency. Actual consumption may vary based on environment and usage.
Q: Is this generator suitable for parallel operation?
A: Yes, it can be equipped with a parallel control system to operate with multiple generator sets, providing higher total power and improved system reliability.
