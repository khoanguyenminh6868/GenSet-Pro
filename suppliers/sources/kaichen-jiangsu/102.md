20kw Silent Diesel Generator
The 20kW Silent Diesel Generator from our company is designed for small commercial, residential, and remote applications where low noise and consistent power are essential.
Send Inquiry
Product Introduction
The 20kW silent diesel generator is designed for users who require reliable backup or primary power while maintaining low noise levels. Compared with open-type generators, this model features a soundproof enclosure that helps reduce operating noise and protects internal components from outdoor conditions.
It is commonly used in residential communities, small businesses, offices, telecom stations, construction sites, farms, and temporary power projects where noise control is important.
Available in fixed installation and trailer-mounted options.
Why Choose a Silent Generator?
Open generators may create excessive noise in urban areas, commercial zones, or residential projects.
This silent generator offers:
Lower operating noise
Weather protection
Safer enclosed structure
Easier outdoor installation
Better appearance for commercial environments
It is suitable for areas with strict noise restrictions.
Silent Canopy Features
The silent enclosure is built for durability and noise reduction.
Heavy-duty steel canopy structure
Powder-coated anti-corrosion surface
Internal sound-absorbing materials
Industrial exhaust muffler
Ventilation system for heat dissipation
Maintenance access doors
Anti-vibration base design
Noise levels can be customized based on project requirements.
