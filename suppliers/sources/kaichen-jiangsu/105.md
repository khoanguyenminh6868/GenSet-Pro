Product Introduction
This 200kW diesel generator set is designed to deliver reliable and efficient power for industrial, commercial, and backup applications. Built with proven engine technology from brands such as Volvo Penta and Cummins, the unit provides stable voltage output, strong load handling capability, and consistent performance under varying operating conditions.
Engineered for long-hour operation and easy maintenance, it is an ideal solution for factories, construction sites, and facilities requiring dependable power supply.
Key Performance Highlights
Stable Voltage Output
Equipped with AVR-controlled alternator, maintaining voltage regulation within ±1%, ensuring safe operation of sensitive equipment.
Strong Load Handling Capacity
Supports high load impact (up to 60–70%), suitable for equipment with high starting current.
Fuel-Efficient Operation
Optimized combustion system reduces fuel consumption to approximately 50–55 L/h at full load .
Continuous Running Capability
Industrial-grade cooling and lubrication systems support long-hour operation.

FAQ
Q1: What factors should I consider when choosing a 200kW generator?
A: Key factors include total load demand, starting current of equipment, operating hours (prime or standby), installation environment, and future expansion requirements.
Q2: Can this generator handle equipment with high starting current?
A: Yes, it is designed to support high load impact, making it suitable for motors, compressors, and HVAC systems with high starting current.
Q3: What type of fuel consumption can I expect?
A: At full load, fuel consumption is typically around 50–55 L/h, depending on engine brand and operating conditions.
Q4: Is this generator suitable for continuous industrial use?
A: Yes, it can operate as a prime power source for long hours, provided proper maintenance and cooling conditions are maintained.
Q5: What customization options are available for bulk orders?
A: We offer flexible configurations including silent type, containerized units, extended fuel tanks, and control system upgrades.
