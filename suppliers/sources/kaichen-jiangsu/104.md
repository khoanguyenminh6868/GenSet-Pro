75kW diesel generator designed for commercial buildings, construction sites, farms, telecom stations, and medium-duty backup power applications.

Product Introduction
Product Overview
The 75kW diesel generator is commonly used for medium-sized backup and primary power applications where stable output is required for multiple electrical systems.
Compared with smaller generators, this model can support higher starting loads such as pumps, compressors, elevators, workshop equipment, and commercial HVAC systems.
It is widely used in factories, construction sites, commercial buildings, farms, telecom stations, and rental power projects.
Available in open type, silent type, trailer-mounted, and containerized configurations.
