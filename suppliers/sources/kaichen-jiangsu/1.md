About Kaichen Power
Delivering Reliable Power Solutions Worldwide
Diesel Generators
Efficient and reliable power generation systems designed for industrial, commercial, and emergency applications.
Jiangsu Kaichen Electric Power Equipment Co., Ltd. is a professional manufacturer specializing in diesel generator sets and integrated power solutions. With over 20 years of industry experience, we have developed strong expertise in engineering, manufacturing, and delivering dependable power systems to global customers.
We operate a modern 24,000㎡ production facility with an annual output of over 6,000 generator sets. Supported by a team of more than 200 professionals, we ensure strict quality control and consistent product performance at every stage.
Performance
Engineered for durability, fuel efficiency, low noise operation, and stable output under demanding working conditions.
Our products are widely used in construction, telecommunications, healthcare, energy, and infrastructure projects. We are committed to providing long-term value through high-quality products, professional service, and continuous innovation.

We welcome global customers to visit our factory and explore our production and quality control.
