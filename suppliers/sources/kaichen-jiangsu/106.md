Product Introduction
Product Overview
The 370KW Perkins generator set is designed for large-scale industrial and commercial power applications that require dependable three-phase output and long operating hours.
Powered by a water-cooled Perkins diesel engine and matched with a high-efficiency brushless alternator, this generator provides stable performance for facilities where uninterrupted electricity is critical.
It is commonly used for manufacturing plants, hospitals, infrastructure projects, data centers, construction sites, and backup power systems.
The generator can be supplied in open type, silent canopy, containerized, or trailer-mounted configurations.
Why Choose Perkins Power
Perkins has been manufacturing diesel engines since 1932 and is widely recognized for reliable performance in global power generation markets.
Key advantages include:
Strong continuous power output
Long engine service life
Low fuel consumption
Stable performance under variable loads
Worldwide spare parts availability
Mature global service network
Perkins engines are widely used in industrial, commercial, agricultural, and infrastructure applications.
