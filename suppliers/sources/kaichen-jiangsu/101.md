Product Introduction
Product Overview
The 300kW industrial diesel generator is designed for continuous and standby power supply in demanding environments such as factories, construction sites, and infrastructure projects. Powered by a Chongqing Cummins engine, this unit delivers stable output, strong load capacity, and reliable performance under heavy-duty operation.
It is suitable for users who require dependable power for critical equipment and long-hour operation, especially where grid power is unstable or unavailable.
Core Configuration
Engine: Chongqing Cummins NTA855-G2A
Alternator: KCC-350GF
Controller: Smartgen HGM6110
Optional: ATS (Automatic Transfer Switch)
Key Features
Cummins Engine with Proven Reliability
Equipped with NTA855-G2A engine manufactured by Chongqing Cummins, offering stable performance across a wide power range and suitable for industrial applications.
Designed for Continuous Heavy Load Operation
4-stroke, water-cooled diesel engine ensures durability and consistent output during long working hours.
Strong Transient Response
Quick response to sudden load changes, suitable for equipment with high starting current.
Matched Alternator for Stable Output
Youkai alternator ensures reliable voltage regulation and efficient power generation.
Flexible Power Supply Options
Supports multiple voltage outputs and frequency configurations to meet different project requirements.
Industrial-Grade Control System
Smartgen controller provides real-time monitoring, protection, and easy operation.
