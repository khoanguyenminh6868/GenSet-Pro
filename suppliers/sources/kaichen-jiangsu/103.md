Product Introduction
Product Overview
The 100kW diesel generator set is designed for commercial and industrial users that require dependable power for daily operations or emergency backup. It offers a practical balance between output capacity, fuel efficiency, and installation flexibility.
This power range is commonly used for facilities operating multiple electrical systems at the same time, including machinery, HVAC systems, pumps, compressors, lighting systems, and control equipment.
It is widely used in factories, warehouses, farms, construction sites, commercial buildings, and telecom facilities.
Available in open type, silent type, trailer-mounted, and containerized configurations.
When Is a 100kW Generator the Right Choice?
A 100kW generator is often selected by businesses that need more capacity than small backup generators but do not require large industrial power stations.
It is suitable for:
Manufacturing workshops
Warehouses
Construction projects
Commercial buildings
Agricultural operations
Telecom stations
Backup power systems
This model can also support future business expansion by handling growing electrical loads.
