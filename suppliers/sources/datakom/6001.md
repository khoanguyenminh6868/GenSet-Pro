Announcement Regarding The Construction of New Factory ( 08.03.2022 )
Datakom started building its new factory in Istanbul.
The new factory will be 4500m2 big. It will be more than triple the current factory building.
With increased production and processing spaces, Datakom will be able to supply its products in higher quantities.
The new factory opening is planned for Q4-2022.
