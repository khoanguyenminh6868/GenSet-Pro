Hybrid Energy System
Tide Power Hybrid Energy System integrates diesel generator sets, solar panels, battery energy storage system, and hybrid inverter into a single unit, designed to provide reliable prime or backup power for off-grid and weak-grid areas. Supporting self-consumption, rate arbitrage, and island operation, the system features a visualized intelligent control system and efficient liquid cooling for low-noise, zero-emission operation, making it ideal for residential, commercial, and microgrid applications.

Diesel Power Solution
Tide Power diesel generator sets cover a wide power range of 6–5625kVA, available in open, soundproof, and containerized configurations with globally renowned engine brands such as Cummins, Perkins, and Lister Petter. Renowned for optimal fuel economy, low operating costs, and high reliability, they serve diverse applications including rental, mining, construction, data centers, power plants, and telecommunications, meeting both prime and standby power requirements.
View More
Gas Power Solution
Tide Power gas generator sets feature MWM, Lister Petter, and other internationally renowned engine brands, compatible with natural gas, biogas, and various gaseous fuels across a power range of 22–4500kVA. Characterized by high efficiency, low emissions, and stable operation, they serve sewage treatment plants, landfill sites, distributed energy projects, and industrial power stations, delivering clean and economical power solutions.
View More
General Machinery
Tide Power general machinery products focus on pump units, covering a full range of diesel-driven pumps including self-prime trash pumps, dry suction pumps, multi-stage pumps, and long shaft deep well pumps, widely used in mine drainage, construction dewatering, agricultural irrigation, and emergency flood control. The product line also includes mini excavators, skid steer loaders, and other construction machinery, offering customers a one-stop power solution from dewatering to earthmoving operations.
