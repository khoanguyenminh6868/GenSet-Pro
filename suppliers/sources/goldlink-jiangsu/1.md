Introduction
Goldlink Power Jiangsu Co.,Ltd. (hereinafter referred to as "GOLDLINK power") is a high-tech generator manufacturer integrating research and development, production, sale and service.It is also the Jiangsu famous brand.
The main products of our company are GOLDLINK brand open type diesel generator sets,silent type diesel generator sets,containerized type diesel generator sets, mobile power stations and GOLDLINK brand three phase brushless alternators.
GOLDLINK power had devoted to newest technology application of domestic and international power generation equipment industry since it was established.GOLDLINK power guarantees to provide customers with optimal power solutions.The company has perfect quality management system,certification system and a team set up by professional engineers and technicians.So the individualized power solutions can be offered according to different requirements of our customers at home and abroad.
GOLDLINK generator sets are coupled with world famous brand diesel engines ,such as Cummins from US ,Perkins from UK,Deutz from Germany, Volvo from Sweden ,Iveco from Italy, Doosan from Korea,Benz from Germany and ShangChai, WeiChai,YuChai,FAW,Yangdong and etc Chinese major brand engines,coupled with Stamford alternators from UK and GOLDLINK alternators from China.All these ensure that 8-2000KVA gensets of GOLDLINK power could supply our customers nonstop electric power with reliability and durability and also could guarantee electric power supply for severe environment and plateau area.
