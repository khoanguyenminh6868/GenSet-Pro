Perfect House Pvt. Limited
Perfect House Private Limited, founded in 1988 , is an ISO 9001:2015 certified organization that is engaged in manufacturing and supplying Generator & Water Pumps. Our product range comprises of Diesel Pump Sets, Portable Diesel Generators, Eicher Engine Generator and much more . The offered products are demanded by our clients owing to their durability, easy installation, fuel efficiency, robust design and high tensile strength. These products are used in commercial, residential and industrial sectors for dealing with power shortages. Our firm also offers R epair & Maintenance Services for increasing the service life of our products.
Our infrastructural facility is spread over a vast stretch of land and is divided into various departments. We have a modernized manufacturing facility for meeting the bulk orders within the stipulated time. Our raw materials are specifically procured from an authentic vendor base and the track records of the vendors are tallied for maintaining quality. We have a well-maintained quality control facility, which is loaded with numerous instruments for checking the quality of the manufactured products. Our warehousing facility has enabled us to store bulk orders conveniently. We have a major transportation facility for meeting the deliveries within the stipulated time period. Our professionals are the biggest asset for our company. They are dedicated and talented. Through their committed efforts, we have been able to cater to the requirements of a wide clientele.
Our mentor, Mr. Neil Ghelani, has empowered us to carve a niche for ourselves in the competitive market. His sharp business acumen has earned us a reputed name.

Our Infrastructure
