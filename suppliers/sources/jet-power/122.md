DIESEL ENGINE
Powered by Cummins Engine, Perkins Engine, Deutz , LOVOL , Lister Petter , Doosan , Yanmar , Kubota, Shangchai, SDEC, Weichai, MTU, Ricardo, Jichai.
• Brand new engine 4-Strokes,Water cooled, direct injection engine
• Rated Speed: 1500rpm / 1800rpm
• Start type: 12V or 24V DC Electrical Start
• Governing control: Mechanical or Electrical
• Radiator standard: 50°environment temperature
• Altitude above sea level: ≤1000 meters
• Ambient air temperature: ≤40°C
A.C ALTERNATOR
Self-regulation is obtained through a digital electronic regulator that is fed by an auxiliary winding.The windings have a 2/3 pitch in order to reduce the harmonic content of voltage.
• Generator Phase and connecting: 3 phase, 4wire.
• Rated frequency: 50Hz@1500rpm / 60Hz@1800rpm.
• Alternator excited type: Brushless, self excited or PMG optional
• Alternator Insulation: Class H， Protection: IP21 ~ IP23
• Voltage regulation: AVR automatic control 1%
• Altitude above sea level: ≤1000 meters
• Ambient air temperature: ≤40°
• Relative air humidity: ≤ 90%
Complies with the most common specifications such as CEI 2-3, IEC 34-1, EN 60034-1, VDE 0530, BS 4999-5000, CAN/ CSA-C22.2 No14-95- No100-95.
