PRODUCT DESCRIPTION
Portable Silent Air-cooled Diesel Generator Set
Safety – Stability – Economy - Reliability Power
01. Use commercial-grade，Vertical, four-stroke, direct injection, air-cooled single-cylinder diesel engine.
02. Equipped with 100% copper reliable 2-pole AC alternator with brush
03. The advanced air duct design ensures better heat dissipation performance, guarantee long time working
04. Equipped with advanced generator set control system, ATS system, remote control system
05. Generator set use High-quality steel are thickness frame and canopy --2MM and 3MM
06. Equipped with high density sound-absorbing material -- sound insulation, fireproofing
07. Generator equipped with 12V DC battery with charger, battery connects wire.
08. Generator equipped with fuel tank with fuel indicator.
09. Generator equipped with sockets and plugs
10. Generator equipped with high-quality muffler

DIESEL ENGINE
Powered by Air Cooled Diesel Engine.
• Brand new engine 4-Strokes,Air cooled, direct injection engine
• Rated Speed: 3000rpm / 3600rpm
• Start type: Manual start or 12V DC Electrical Start
• Governing control: Mechanical
• Design standard: 50°environment temperature
• Altitude above sea level: ≤1000 meters
• Ambient air temperature: ≤40°C
Self-regulation is obtained through a digital electronic regulator that is fed by an auxiliary winding.The windings have a 2/3 pitch in order to reduce the harmonic content of voltage.
• Generator Phase and connecting: 2 Phase 2 wire, 2 phase 3wire,3 phase, 4wire.
• Rated frequency: 50Hz@3000rpm / 60Hz@3600rpm.
• Alternator excited type: With Brush, self excited with AVR
• Alternator Insulation: Class H， Protection: IP21 ~ IP23
• Voltage regulation: AVR automatic control 1%
• Altitude above sea level: ≤1000 meters
• Ambient air temperature: ≤40°
• Relative air humidity: ≤ 90%
Complies with the most common specifications such as CEI 2-3, IEC 34-1, EN 60034-1, VDE 0530, BS 4999-5000, CAN/ CSA-C22.2 No14-95- No100-95.
