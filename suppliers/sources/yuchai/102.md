Product Advantages
Fuel economy
Application of Yuchai's patented efficient combustion technology, achieving a minimum fuel consumption of 194 g/kwh
Road spectrum collection and fine calibration according to market segment and applications, ensuring fuel economy
Use of an intelligent fuel-saving switch: The position switch is adjusted according to the real-time load, allowing the vehicle to operate in the economical range all the time, thus reducing the fuel consumption by at least 10%
Lighter dead weight (5% lighter than competitors) and more fuel-efficient
Comfort
Use of a Bosch common rail system, achieving multiple injections and accurate control, and reducing combustion noise
Reduction soft meshing starter, ensuring smooth start
Damping silicone oil damper
Application of a grooved cylinder head cover and a noise-reduction cylinder head cover gasket, effectively reducing noise transmission
Worry-free
More sufficient combustion, reducing pollution of oil by carbon and other harmful gases
Optimized piston material and lubricating oil passage, avoiding any impact of ultra-high temperature on the service life of lubricating oil
Use of an oil filter, achieving higher filtration efficiency and dust holding capacity, lower flow resistance, and stronger breakage resistance of filter paper
Reliability
Passed the road adaptability test of about 3 million km (including high-temperature test, alpine-cold test and plateau test)
Passed the reliability bench test of about 10,000 hours
High-altitude adaptability: no power loss at altitudes less than 2,000 m, suitable for applications at altitudes of 4,800 m
Cold starting performance: smooth starting at an ambient temperature of -35°C
Strong power
