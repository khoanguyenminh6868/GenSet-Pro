Why choose Everllence for your marine operations?
With over a century of innovation and engineering excellence , Everllence is a global leader in providing integrated, future-proof propulsion and power solutions for the marine industry . From state-of-the-art engines to propellers, turbochargers and a comprehensive portfolio of digital services, Everllence empowers shipowners and operators to achieve top-tier performance, efficiency, compliance, and sustainability.
Choosing Everllence means partnering with a trusted technology leader that’s committed to shaping the future of maritime transport.
Your key benefits of choosing Everllence
Proven expertise : Decades of experience and leadership in designing reliable two-stroke and four-stroke marine engines.
Efficiency & performance : High-efficiency propulsion and auxiliary systems, turbochargers and propellers that reduce fuel consumption and operational costs.
Sustainability : Advanced solutions supporting alternative fuels (e.g., LNG, methanol, ammonia) and decarbonization strategies.
Digital advantage : Smart technologies for real-time monitoring, diagnostics, and engine- and fleet optimization.
Global service network : Comprehensive after-sales support and service hubs worldwide.
Modular flexibility : Scalable systems tailored for various vessel types and applications, from container ships to cruise liners, offshore platforms or fishing vessels.
Compliance ready : Future-proof designs that meet or exceed IMO regulations and environmental standards.

Scalable energy transition
Everllence B&W ME-LGIM
Our ME-LGIM is a dual-fuel engine characterized by its high efficiency and capability of operating on methanol. With a proven record of more than 600,000 operating hours on methanol alone, the engine concept provides a high degree of reliability. When fuelled by green methanol, our ME-LGIM engine can offer truly carbon-neutral propulsion , while reducing sulfur and particle matter emissions significantly at the same time.
Engine speed: 56 – 125 rpm
Output: 6,950 – 82,440 kW at L1

B&W ME-GI
