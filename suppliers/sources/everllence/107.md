Bulk carrier propulsion engineered for low-emission global trade
Smarter, future-ready propulsion for the next generation of bulk carriers
Bulk carriers dominate international waters, making up over 40% of the total world fleet operating today. This important transport segment continues to grow along with increasing global market volumes, but the sector also needs to adapt to more stringent requirements .
Today’s bulk carrier owners need cost-effective propulsion systems to make their operations profitable. Since bulk carriers often sail in environmentally sensitive areas, their engines need to meet strict limits for NOx, SOx , and particulate emissions . With stricter IMO restrictions, finding high-efficiency propulsion systems with low fuel consumption is key. Operators need the best two-stroke engines that meet low rpm demands to achieve ideal ship designs with highly efficient large propellers.
Power for your bulker: Everllence B&W two-stroke engines
Our two-stroke engines offer the best solution for the propulsion of all kinds of merchant vessels, including bulk carriers.
Our electronically controlled Everllence B&W ME-C engines offer countless advantages such as optimized fuel and lube oil consumption , as well as improved low-load operation – based on the many decades of success enjoyed by their mechanically controlled predecessors.
The ME-GI is a highly efficient, flexible propulsion solution. With its introduction, we began a new chapter in diesel history, from which your bulker vessel stands to benefit. Thanks to the ME-GI engine, we give vessel owners the option of using either fuel oil or methane – depending on market prices and availability.
The ME-LGIM engine is the world’s first two-stroke dual-fuel methanol engine. It came into service in 2016, and has already reached an impressive track record of running hours on methanol. It is well-suited for the application on bulk carriers, either as newbuilding project, or as retrofit.
Our four-stroke GenSets: A trusted source for flexible onboard power
Everllence four-stroke auxiliary GenSets are widely recognized for their reliable power solutions, having served the global maritime industry for decades. These engines are the preferred option for onboard electricity generation, providing essential energy for various operational needs including lighting, refrigeration, and other essential functions. With a broad power range and flexible fuel compatibilities, Everllence offers cost-effective solutions tailored to meet the requirements of today’s bulk carriers.

Compact design and retrofittable

B&W ME-C
Our ME-C engines with electronic controls serve up a host of advantages. You can look forward to superior performance parameters, compactness, fully integrated electronic control as well as the necessary fuel injection pressure and rate shaping at any load. Plus, the ME-C engine can be retrofitted for alternative fuels such as methanol, LPG, methane or ethane as well as ammonia estimated for 2026.

Low methane slip emissions
Everllence
B&W ME-GI
The ME-GI engine impresses with extremely lowest methane slip and very high fuel efficiency. It is the most environmentally friendly methane-fuelled engine on the market. By the optional use of fuel oil or LNG/bio-methane/SNG, it represents a highly efficient, flexible, propulsion-plant solution.
Engine speed: rpm to 56 – 167
Output: 4,350 – 82,440 kW at L1
Explore the ME-GI
Scalable energy transition
Everllence B&W ME-LGIM
Our ME-LGIM is a dual-fuel engine characterized by its high efficiency and capability of operating on methanol. With a proven record of more than 600,000 operating hours on methanol alone, the engine concept provides a high degree of reliability. When fuelled by green methanol, our ME-LGIM engine can offer truly carbon-neutral propulsion , while reducing sulfur and particle matter emissions significantly at the same time.
Engine speed: 56 – 125 rpm
Output: 6,950 – 82,440 kW at L1
