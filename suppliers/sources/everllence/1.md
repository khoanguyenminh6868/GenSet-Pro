Dual fuel engines: built for flexibility
Unlocking fuel flexibility for reliable power generation
Everllence (former MAN Energy Solutions) offers a range of reliable dual fuel engines to give operators the flexibility they need to react to changing environmental regulations and fuel availability. And whichever fuel may be available, our dual fuel engines deliver consistent high performance and efficiency.
The main advantage of dual fuel technology is fuel flexibility . This lets you take advantage of the low cost and superior environmental benefits offered by e-methane . If the supply of gas is interrupted, the engine can continue operating using liquid fuel . In addition to gaseous fuels, our dual fuel engines can also run on liquid biofuels such as bioethanol, fatty acid methyl ester (FAME), and hydrotreated vegetable oil (HVO) or distillates like marine diesel oil (MDO), marine gas oil (MGO), and heavy fuel oil (HFO).
Dual fuel engines from Everllence are known for their reliability, efficiency, performance, and the ability to switch between fuel sources. Additionally, if you already have one of our liquid fuel engines, it's easy to upgrade to dual fuel capability to meet changing needs.

Everllence commissions three dual-fuel power plants in Indonesia
Ten gensets with a total of 140 MW guarantee the energy supply for East and West Nusa Tenggara and employment of over 130 local employees.
The dual fuel engines deployed offer complete fuel flexibility, thereby ensuring the sustainability of the power plants as they can operate on low-emission LNG at any time.
