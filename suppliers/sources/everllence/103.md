Modularity to rely on: Everllence B&W ME-C two-stroke engines
Reliable ME-C engine performance – today and tomorrow
When you are investing in newbuilding project and there by a large two-stroke engine, you can look to both the past and the future: Go for a proven solution that is highly efficient and reliable, or choose one that is adaptable to future fuel and emission regulations. With the Everllence B&W ME-C engine (former MAN B&W ME-C engine), you get both – high performance and long-term flexibility .
The ME-C engine range delivers superior performance in a compact form , supported by fully integrated electronic control as well as the necessary fuel injection and rate shaping at any load.
Thanks to our wide portfolio of dual-fuel engines – capable of running on alternative fuels such as methane, methanol, LPG and soon also ammonia – existing ME-C engines can be retrofitted to dual-fuel engines. This makes the ME-C engine a smart and flexible choice for shipowners preparing for the future.
A two-stroke platform ready for every vessel type
The Everllence B&W ME-C engine is the most flexible and future-proof solution for the propulsion of all types of ocean-going merchant vessels. Whether powering tankers, bulk carriers, or large and ultra-large container ships, the ME-C engine delivers the performance, efficiency, and reliability required for demanding global operations. Built on decades of experience with two-stroke engines , this platform continues to set the standard for heavy-duty marine propulsion.

Modular design for long-term adaptability
The ME-C engine features a modular design that makes it particularly suitable for upgrading . This design enables shipowners to future-proof their vessels for evolving economic and regulatory conditions – for example, by adding dual-fuel capability for methanol.
As the only two stroke engine designer in the world, we have successfully completed retrofit projects to dual-fuel operation , and we now have an ammonia retrofit option in development as well. With these proof points, the ME-C engine has a proven track record as a platform that evolves with market and environmental demands.
The ability to convert an existing ME-C engine to operate on alternative fuels such as methanol, methane, LPG or ethane gives ship operators valuable fuel flexibility and a pathway to decarbonization .

The optimal choice for ethane carriers
Everllence B&W ME-GIE
The technology of the ME-GIE is based on our successful ME-GI engine. This engine can be operated with liquefied ethane gas (LEG) as well as fuel oil – which makes it the standard choice for ethane carriers. This engine offers very competitive fuel oil and gas consumption, plus operational stability and efficiency due to the diesel principle.
Engine speed: 72 – 100 rpm
Output: 8,600 – 21,440 kW at L1
Scalable energy transition
Everllence B&W ME-LGIM
Our ME-LGIM is a dual-fuel engine characterized by its high efficiency and capability of operating on methanol. With a proven record of more than 600,000 operating hours on methanol alone, the engine concept provides a high degree of reliability. When fuelled by green methanol, our ME-LGIM engine can offer truly carbon-neutral propulsion , while reducing sulfur and particle matter emissions significantly at the same time.
Engine speed: 56 – 125 rpm
Output: 6,950 – 82,440 kW at L1

Proven performance, tailored to your operation
Fuel efficiency and reliability are at the core of the ME-C engine’s performance promise. The range includes engines with outputs from 4,350 to 82,440 kW, bore sizes from 35 to 95 cm, and 5 to 12 cylinders – with speeds ranging from 56 to 167 rpm.
All ME-C engines feature fully integrated electronic control systems that deliver the required fuel injection pressure and rate shaping at any load condition. Recent innovations, such as the introduction of the G50 10.7 variant, reflect our ongoing efforts to reduce fuel consumption and emissions across all engine configurations.

Optimize your investment at every stage
Smart investment decisions require both long-term flexibility and reliable performance from day one. The ME-C engine is designed to support your business case – from initial CAPEX planning to optimized OPEX and future-ready retrofit options.
Invest in what you need
From a CAPEX perspective, the ME-C engine offers a scalable solution tailored to your vessel’s requirements. With its modular design and proven platform, you only invest in what you need today – while keeping the flexibility to adapt to future demands. Thanks to its established retrofitability , the ME-C engine protects long-term asset value and ensures a smooth upgrade path when fuel strategies or regulatory frameworks evolve.
Maximize your profitability
Operationally, the ME-C engine is built for low OPEX . It can be fine-tuned to deliver industry-leading thermal efficiency helping to reduce fuel consumption, lube oil use, and emissions across a wide range of operating conditions. Especially in volatile fuel markets with fluctuating fuel prices, the ME-C engine gives operators a critical edge in managing long-term profitability.
Retrofit with confidence
Retrofit solutions from Everllence PrimeServ make it easy to extend the lifecycle and upgrade the capabilities of your ME-C engine. Whether converting to dual-fuel operation or implementing performance-enhancing modifications, every solution is engineered to safeguard engine integrity and align with your operational goals. The result: flexible, future-ready propulsion .
