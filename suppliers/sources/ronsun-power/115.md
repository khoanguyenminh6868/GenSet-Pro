Description
Product Overview:
Our 80kW (100kVA) Syngas Generator Set is engineered to convert synthetic gas (syngas) derived from biomass gasification, coal gasification, or waste-to-energy processes into stable electrical power. As a professional manufacturer, we offer full OEM/ODM manufacturing services, catering to global distributors and wholesalers.
Key Features & Advantages:
Specialized Mixing System: Equipped with an advanced gas-air mixing system specifically calibrated for the lower calorific value of syngas, ensuring stable combustion and high efficiency.
Robust Engine Design: Features reinforced engine components (valves, seats, and pistons) to withstand the impurities often found in syngas, prolonging the unit's operational lifespan.
Electronic Control System: Integrated high-precision governor system including ECU, ignition controller, and speed governor for precise load management.
Economical Operation: Offers low operating costs by utilizing renewable biomass or industrial by-product gases, providing a quick return on investment.
Customization Available: Capable of soundproof canopy customization, remote monitoring integration, and parallel operation (synchronization) configuration.
Application Scenarios:
Ideal for wood processing plants, rice mills, agricultural farms, and industrial facilities utilizing gasifiers to generate power from waste materials.
