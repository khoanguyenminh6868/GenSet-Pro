Description
Our 100kW (125kVA) LPG Generator Set is engineered to provide stable and efficient power generation using Liquefied Petroleum Gas (LPG). As a leading manufacturer conducting R&D and production globally, we offer this unit for wholesale, OEM, ODM, and custom branding needs.
Key Features & Advantages:
Eco-Friendly Power: LPG combustion produces significantly lower emissions compared to diesel, making it an environmentally responsible choice for power generation.
Cost-Effective Operation: High thermal efficiency and lower fuel costs contribute to reduced operational expenses over the generator's lifespan.
Reliable Performance: Equipped with a robust engine and high-quality alternator, ensuring stable voltage output and quick response to load changes.
Durable Construction: Designed for harsh environments with a heavy-duty chassis and anti-vibration mounts.
Versatile Applications: Ideal for use in remote areas, oil fields, commercial buildings, data centers, and industrial facilities requiring continuous or backup power.
Service & Partnership:
We are actively looking for global distributors, wholesalers, and agents. We support full customization to meet local market standards and branding requirements.
