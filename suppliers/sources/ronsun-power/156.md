Description
This 60kW / 75kVA Diesel Generator Set is engineered to deliver robust and continuous power for a variety of industrial and commercial applications. Built with a focus on durability and efficiency, this unit is ideal for factories, construction sites, hospitals, and telecommunication stations requiring reliable backup or prime power.
Key Features & Advantages:
Reliable Power Output: Provides a steady 60kW rated power with high load-bearing capacity to handle heavy machinery and sensitive electronics.
Fuel Efficiency: Equipped with an advanced injection system that optimizes fuel combustion, reducing operational costs over long running hours.
Durable Construction: Features a heavy-duty steel chassis and a weather-resistant canopy, making it suitable for harsh outdoor environments.
Easy Maintenance: Designed with accessible service points for oil, fuel, and air filters to minimize downtime.
Smart Control System: Includes a user-friendly digital control panel for real-time monitoring of voltage, frequency, and engine status.
