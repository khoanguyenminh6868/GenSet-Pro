Description
Our 350kW (437.5kVA) Biogas Generator Set is a premium power generation solution engineered for efficiency, durability, and broad adaptability. As a professional manufacturer, we offer comprehensive services including OEM, ODM, and customization to meet global standards.
Key Features & Advantages:
High Efficiency: Optimized gas mixing technology ensures fuller combustion and lower fuel consumption, maximizing data center or industrial uptime.
Eco-Friendly: Designed to convert methane from biogas into clean electricity, significantly reducing greenhouse gas emissions and operational costs.
Robust Design: Features a heavy-duty engine block and enhanced cooling system, making it suitable for continuous operation in harsh industrial environments.
Intelligent Control: Equipped with advanced digital control panels for real-time monitoring, auto-start capabilities, and grid-connected operations.
Cost-Effective: Lower maintenance requirements and extended service intervals reduce the Total Cost of Ownership (TCO).
Applications: This generator set is ideal for livestock farms (pig/dairy), landfills, wastewater treatment plants, food processing factories, and biomass power plants. We welcome inquiries from distributors, wholesalers, and agents worldwide looking for reliable gas power solutions.
