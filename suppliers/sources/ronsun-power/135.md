Description
This 120kW / 150kVA Syngas Generator Set is engineered to convert biomass synthesis gas (such as wood gas, straw gas, or other gasified biomass) into reliable electricity. As a leading manufacturer, we provide robust power solutions suitable for various industrial and commercial applications.
Key Features & Advantages:
Specialized Design: Specifically calibrated air-fuel ratio mixers and ignition systems designed to handle the variable calorific value of Syngas.
Cost-Effective: Significantly lowers operational costs by utilizing renewable biomass waste or synthesis gas instead of expensive diesel or grid power.
Durability: Built with high-strength alloy materials to resist the corrosive elements often found in biomass gases, ensuring a longer service life.
Advanced Control: Equipped with a smart digital control panel for real-time monitoring of voltage, frequency, and engine parameters, ensuring safe operation.
OEM/ODM Support: We offer comprehensive customization options including soundproof canopies, mobile trailers, and ATS integration tailored to your specific project needs.
Application Scenarios: Ideal for biomass gasification power plants, wood processing factories, agricultural farms utilizing crop waste, and remote off-grid locations seeking sustainable energy independence.
