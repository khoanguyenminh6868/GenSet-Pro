Description
The 100kW / 125kVA Syngas Generator Set is an advanced power generation solution engineered specifically to run on low-calorific value gases such as syngas, biomass gas, wood gas, and coal gasification byproducts. This unit transforms waste into valuable energy, making it an ideal choice for renewable energy projects and industrial applications seeking sustainability.
Key Features & Advantages:
Specialized Gas Mixer: Equipped with an advanced air-fuel ratio control system designed to handle the variable composition of synthetic gases.
Robust Engine Design: Modified combustion chambers and hardened valves ensure durability against the impurities often found in syngas.
High Efficiency: Optimized thermodynamic cycle delivers excellent thermal efficiency, maximizing power output from limited gas resources.
Stable Operation: Features a high-performance governor for stable speed control and load response, ensuring consistent electricity output.
Safety & Protection: Comprehensive protection systems including high water temperature, low oil pressure, over-speed, and over-current alarms.
Application Scenarios:
Biomass Gasification Power Plants (Wood chips, straw, rice husks).
Coal Gasification Projects.
Waste-to-Energy Facilities.
Remote Off-Grid Industrial Sites.
