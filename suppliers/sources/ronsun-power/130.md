Description
Product Overview:
Our 250kW (312.5kVA) Biogas Generator Set is a premium power generation solution engineered for reliability and efficiency. Designed to convert biomass fuels into electricity, this unit is perfect for landfills, wastewater treatment plants, and agricultural facilities looking to harness renewable energy. As a leading manufacturer, we offer comprehensive OEM, ODM, and customization services to meet specific project requirements.
Key Features & Advantages:
High Efficiency: Optimized for biogas combustion, ensuring maximum power output with minimal fuel consumption.
Durability: Built with a robust structure and high-quality components to withstand continuous operation in harsh environments.
Advanced Control System: Equipped with a smart control panel for real-time monitoring, auto-start/stop, and fault protection.
Eco-Friendly: Significantly reduces carbon footprint by utilizing waste gas, meeting international emission standards.
Grid Synchronization: Capable of parallel operation with the main grid or other generator sets.

Livestock Husbandry & Poultry Farms
