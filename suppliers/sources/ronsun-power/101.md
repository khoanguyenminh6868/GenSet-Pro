Description
Product Overview:
Our 150kW (187.5kVA) Natural Gas Generator Set is designed for high efficiency and long-term durability. Utilizing clean energy sources like natural gas, LNG, CNG, or biogas, this unit significantly reduces operating costs and environmental impact compared to traditional diesel generators. We specialize in manufacturing, OEM, and ODM services, offering tailored solutions for global wholesalers, distributors, and agents.
Key Features & Advantages:
Eco-Friendly Power: Low carbon emissions and reduced noise operation, ideal for strict environmental regulation zones.
High Efficiency: Optimized gas mixing technology ensures complete combustion and lower fuel consumption (&le;0.30 m&sup3;/kW·h).
Robust Design: Features a heavy-duty industrial engine and high-grade alternator suitable for continuous 24/7 operation.
Advanced Control System: Equipped with a digital controller for auto-start monitoring, grid synchronization, and remote management.
Cost-Effective: Significantly lower fuel and maintenance costs over the lifecycle of the product.
Applications:
Ideal for oil & gas fields, data centers, hospitals, manufacturing plants, livestock farms (biogas), and commercial buildings requiring reliable standby or prime power.
