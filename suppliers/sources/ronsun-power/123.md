Description
This 30kW / 38kVA Diesel Generator Set is engineered to provide stable and reliable power for a wide range of applications. Whether serving as a prime power source for construction sites and remote locations or as a critical backup for hospitals, data centers, and commercial buildings, this genset delivers superior performance.
Key Features & Advantages:
Robust Diesel Engine: Equipped with a heavy-duty 4-cycle industrial diesel engine tailored for longevity and fuel efficiency.
High-Efficiency Alternator: Features a brushless, self-excited alternator with IP23 protection and Class H insulation, ensuring stable voltage output.
Advanced Control System: Integrated with a user-friendly digital controller for automatic start/stop and real-time parameter monitoring.
Silent Operation: Available with an optional sound-attenuated canopy to minimize noise levels, making it suitable for residential areas.
OEM/ODM Support: We offer full manufacturing customization, including branding, color schemes, and configuration adjustments to suit your specific market needs.
Designed for the global market, we welcome inquiries from wholesalers, distributors, agent, and traders looking for high-quality, cost-effective power generation solutions.
