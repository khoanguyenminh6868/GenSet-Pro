Description
Professional 50kW Syngas Power Solution
Our 50kW (62.5kVA) Syngas Generator Set is engineered to convert biomass-derived synthetic gas (wood gas, straw gas, etc.) into reliable electricity. As a leading manufacturer with global reach, we provide flexible OEM, ODM, and wholesale solutions.
Key Features & Advantages:
Specialized Design: The engine is specifically calibrated for low-calorific value gases like syngas, ensuring stable combustion and prevents engine knocking.
High Durability: Robust components withstand the impurities often found in biomass gasification, extending the overhaul interval and reducing maintenance costs.
Advanced Control: Integrated electronic governor and gas mixing system optimize the air-fuel ratio for maximum efficiency and lower emissions.
Cost-Effective Ops: Utilizing waste biomass or wood chips significantly reduces fuel costs compared to diesel or natural gas.
Application Scenarios:
Biomass Gasification Power Plants
Wood Processing Factories & Sawmills
Agricultural Farms (Rice Husk, Straw utilization)
Rural Electrification & Off-Grid Communities
