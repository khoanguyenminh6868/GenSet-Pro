Description
Product Overview:
Our 30kW (37.5kVA) Syngas Generator Set is engineered to convert synthetic gas (produced from biomass gasification, coal gasification, or industrial by-products) into stable electrical power. As a specialized manufacturer, we offer comprehensive OEM, ODM, and customization services to meet specific project requirements globally.
Key Features & Advantages:
Specialized Gas Mixer: Designed specifically for low-heat value gases like syngas, ensuring efficient combustion and easy startup.
Durability: Reinforced engine components to handle the impurities often found in synthetic gases, resulting in longer service intervals.
Cost-Effective: Significantly lowers electricity costs by utilizing waste biomass or industrial gas by-products.
Global Standards: Manufactured to approximate international ISO standards for emission and safety.
Automatic Control: Features an advanced digital control panel for monitoring voltage, frequency, and engine health in real-time.
Application Scenarios:
Biomass power plants (wood chips, straw, rice husk gasification).
Rural electrification projects using agricultural waste.
Industrial factories with coal gasification systems.
Remote off-grid locations requiring sustainable power solutions.
We are actively seeking distributors, wholesalers, and trade agents worldwide to expand our network of green energy solutions.
