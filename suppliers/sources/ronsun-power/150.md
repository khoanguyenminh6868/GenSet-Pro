Description
Our 60kW (75kVA) Syngas Generator Set is engineered specifically to handle the unique properties of low-calorific synthetic gas derived from biomass gasification, coal gasification, or wood chips. As a professional manufacturer, we offer comprehensive OEM and ODM services to global agents and distributors.
Key Features & Advantages:
Specialized Gas Mixer: Equipped with an advanced air-fuel ratio control system designed to handle the fluctuating caloric value of Syngas efficiently.
Robust Engine Design: Features hardened valves and seats to resist corrosion and wear often associated with syngas impurities.
High Cost-Efficiency: Converts waste by-products into electricity, significantly reducing operational costs for biomass plants.
Stable Performance: Digital ignition system ensures smooth startup and stable operation under continuous load.
Primary Applications:
Biomass Gasification Power Plants
Wood Processing Factories (using wood gas)
Rice Mills (using rice husk gas)
Rural Electrification in Off-Grid Areas
Industrial Waste-to-Energy Projects
