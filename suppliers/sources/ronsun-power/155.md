Description
Our 300kW / 375kVA Diesel Generator Set is designed to meet the rigorous demands of global industrial and commercial sectors. As a leading manufacturer offering OEM, ODM, and wholesale services, we ensure this unit provides stable, continuous power with excellent fuel efficiency.
Key Features & Advantages:
High Reliability: Powered by a heavy-duty industrial diesel engine, ensuring long service intervals and durability under load.
Advanced Control System: Equipped with a digital controller for auto-start, monitoring, and comprehensive protection against over-current, low oil pressure, and high temperature.
Superior Performance: Features a brushless alternator with AVR (Automatic Voltage Regulator) for stable voltage output, protecting sensitive equipment.
Flexible Customization: Available in open type or soundproof/weatherproof canopy options suitable for outdoor installation.
Application Scenarios:
This generator is ideal for use in mining sites, manufacturing plants, data centers, hospitals, and large construction projects. We actively welcome distributors, agents, and wholesalers to partner with us for global distribution.
