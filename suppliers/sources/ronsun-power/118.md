Our 250kW / 313kVA Diesel Generator Set is designed to deliver reliable power in the most demanding environments. Built by a professional manufacturer serving the global market, this unit combines durability with high efficiency.

High Reliability: Equipped with a heavy-duty industrial diesel engine ensuring continuous operation capacity and long service life.
