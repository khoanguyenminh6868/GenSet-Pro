Description
Our 50kW (62.5kVA) LPG and Gas Generator Set represents the pinnacle of clean energy power generation technology. Designed and manufactured by industry experts, this unit is engineered for durability, efficiency, and lower environmental impact compared to traditional diesel counterparts.
Key Features & Advantages:
Eco-Friendly Performance: Operates on LPG (Liquefied Petroleum Gas) or Natural Gas, significantly reducing carbon emissions and exhaust smoke.
Cost-Effective Operation: Lower fuel costs and reduced maintenance requirements result in a highly competitive Total Cost of Ownership (TCO).
Reliable Power Output: Equipped with a robust engine and premium alternator to ensure stable voltage and frequency, safe for sensitive electronics.
Advanced Control System: Features a smart digital controller with auto-start/stop functionality, comprehensive protection (over-load, over-temp, low oil pressure), and remote monitoring capabilities.
Quiet Operation: Optional soundproof canopy design minimizes noise pollution, making it ideal for residential areas and noise-sensitive commercial zones.
Application Scenarios:
Residential Backup: Reliable standby power for large homes and estates during grid outages.
Commercial & Retail: Continuous power supply for restaurants, small hotels, clinics, and retail stores.
Industrial & Agricultural: Powering pumps, small machinery, and farm operations in off-grid locations.
Telecom Base Stations: Stable prime power for remote communication infrastructure.
