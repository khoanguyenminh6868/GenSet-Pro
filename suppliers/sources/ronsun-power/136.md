Description
Product Overview:
The 50kW / 62.5kVA Biomass and Gas Generator Set is a robust power solution engineered for reliability and environmental sustainability. Designed to run efficiently on biogas, natural gas, and other renewable gas sources, this unit helps industries and agricultural facilities convert waste into usable energy.
Key Features & Advantages:
1. High Efficiency: Optimized engine design ensures maximum fuel combustion and power output with minimal gas consumption.
2. Durability: Built with high-strength materials and reinforced components to withstand continuous operation in harsh environments.
3. Eco-Friendly: Low nitrogen oxide and particulate emissions, contributing to a greener energy footprint.
4. Advanced Control System: Equipped with an intelligent digital controller for real-time monitoring of voltage, frequency, and engine health.
5. Flexible Customization: We offer comprehensive OEM/ODM services, including branding, canopy design (open/silent type), and specific alternator choices.
Applications:
- Biogas Plants: Farms, wastewater treatment plants, and landfills converting methane to electricity.
- Oil & Gas Fields: Utilizing associated gas for on-site power.
- Remote Industrial Sites: Providing continuous or backup power where gas pipelines are accessible.
