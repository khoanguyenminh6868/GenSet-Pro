Description
Our 350kW (437.5kVA) Natural Gas Generator Set is a robust power solution designed for versatility and durability in the global market. As a specialized manufacturer, we provide comprehensive OEM, ODM, and customization services to meet specific project requirements.
Key Advantages:
Eco-Friendly Power: Utilizes cleaner natural gas (CNG/LNG) or biogas, significantly reducing carbon emissions compared to diesel counterparts.
Cost-Effective Operation: Combines low fuel consumption with reduced maintenance costs for a lower Total Cost of Ownership (TCO).
Stable Performance: Equipped with a high-performance engine and a brushless alternator with AVR, ensuring stable voltage output for sensitive industrial equipment.
Adaptable Design: Suitable for diverse environments, ranging from extreme cold to tropical heat, with optional silent canopies for noise-sensitive areas.
Smart Control: Integrated digital control panel for automatic start/stop and real-time monitoring of engine parameters (oil pressure, water temperature, speed).
Applications:
Industrial factories and manufacturing plants requiring continuous power.
Commercial buildings, hotels, and data centers needing reliable backup power.
Oil and gas fields for on-site power generation.
Remote mining operations and rural electrification projects.
