Description
Reliable 150kW LPG Power Generation Solutions
Our 150kW (187.5kVA) LPG Generator Set delivers robust and cleaner energy, making it an excellent choice for industrial applications, commercial buildings, and remote locations requiring substantial power. Designed for durability and low operating costs, this unit utilizes Liquid Petroleum Gas (LPG) to provide stable electricity with lower emissions compared to diesel counterparts.
Key Features & Advantages:
Cost-Effective Operation: High thermal efficiency and economical fuel consumption reduce long-term operational costs.
Eco-Friendly Performance: Clean combustion technology ensures lower NOx and particulate emissions, meeting environmental standards.
Advanced Control System: Equipped with an intelligent digital controller for automatic start/stop, data monitoring, and safety protection (over-speed, low oil pressure, high water temperature).
Flexible Customization: We offer soundproof canopies, mobile trailers, and ATS (Automatic Transfer Switch) integration. As a direct manufacturer, we support OEM/ODM branding services.
Reliable Engine: Features a heavy-duty, industrial-grade gas engine optimized for continuous running and heavy load acceptance.
Application Scenarios:
Ideal for factories, shopping malls, hospitals, data centers, farms (biogas potential), and oil fields. Perfect for markets seeking wholesale, distribution, or customized power solutions.
