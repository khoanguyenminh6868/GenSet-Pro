Description
The 120kW / 150kVA Diesel Generator Set is a robust power solution designed to meet the rigorous demands of global markets. We are a professional manufacturer specializing in generator R&D and production, offering flexible cooperation models including Wholesale, OEM, and ODM services for distributors and agents worldwide.
Key Features & Advantages:
Reliable Power Output: Delivers a steady 120kW prime power, ensuring your critical operations continue without interruption during grid failures.
High Efficiency: Equipped with a turbocharged, intercooled engine that optimizes fuel consumption and reduces operating costs.
Durable Construction: Built with a heavy-duty chassis and high-quality components to withstand harsh environments and long operating hours.
Advanced Control: Includes a user-friendly digital control panel for automatic start/stop functionality and real-time monitoring of engine parameters (oil pressure, temperature, voltage).
Versatile Application: Ideal for construction sites, hospitals, data centers, factories, mining operations, and commercial buildings requiring 3-phase standby power.
We welcome specific customization requests regarding engine brands, alternator types, and enclosure options (silent/open type) to suit local market requirements.
