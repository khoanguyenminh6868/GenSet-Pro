Description
This 720kW / 900kVA Diesel Generator Set is engineered to provide robust and reliable power solutions for large-scale industrial and commercial applications. Built with a heavy-duty diesel engine and a high-efficiency alternator, this unit ensures stable performance under demanding load conditions.
Key Features & Advantages:
High Power Output: Capable of delivering a prime power of 720kW, capable of supporting heavy machinery, data centers, and critical infrastructure.
Durability: Rugged structural design withstands harsh environments, ensuring a long service life with minimal maintenance requirements.
Fuel Efficiency: Optimized electronic fuel injection system reduces fuel consumption while minimizing emissions.
Safe Operation: Equipped with a comprehensive control panel featuring automatic shutdowns for low oil pressure, high water temperature, and over-speed protection.
Customization (OEM/ODM): We offer tailored solutions including silent canopies, trailer mounts, and specific color branding for wholesalers and distributors.
Applications: Ideal for hospitals, mining sites, construction projects, factories, shopping malls, and telecommunication base stations requiring dependable continuous or emergency standby power.
