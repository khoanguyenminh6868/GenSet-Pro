Description
This 120kW (150kVA) LPG Generator Set is expertly engineered to provide robust power solutions for global markets. Utilizing clean-burning Liquefied Petroleum Gas (LPG), this unit significantly reduces emissions and maintenance costs compared to traditional diesel generators. It is ideal for continuous operation in oil fields, industrial parks, hotels, or as a reliable backup power source for critical facilities.
Key Features & Advantages:
Eco-Friendly Performance: Low carbon emissions and minimal particulate matter.
Cost-Effective Operation: Lower fuel costs and extended service intervals reduce Total Cost of Ownership (TCO).
Advanced Control System: Equipped with a digital controller for automatic start/stop and comprehensive engine protection.
Superior Durability: Rugged design capable of withstanding harsh environmental conditions.
Customization Available: We offer full OEM/ODM services, including silent canopy, trailer mounting, and specific component selection to meet distinct distributor and wholesaler needs.
