Description
Reliable 80kW LPG Power Generation Solution
Our 80kW (100kVA) LPG Generator Set is engineered for superior performance and durability, utilizing Liquefied Petroleum Gas for a cleaner, cost-effective energy source. Designed for the global market, this unit is perfect for areas with established LPG infrastructure, offering a dependable alternative to diesel.
Key Features & Advantages:
Eco-Friendly Operation: Effectively reduces harmful emissions and carbon footprint compared to diesel gensets.
Cost-Efficiency: Lower operating costs due to the competitive pricing of LPG fuel and reduced maintenance requirements.
Durability: Built with a robust chassis and high-quality components to withstand continuous operation in harsh environments.
Advanced Control: Equipped with a smart control panel for real-time monitoring of voltage, current, frequency, and engine status.
Flexible Manufacturing: We offer full OEM/ODM services, allowing for custom branding, color schemes, and component selection to suit specific distributor needs.
Application Scenarios:
Ideal for diverse applications including residential backup, small factories, shopping malls, telecommunications base stations, and poultry farms. We are actively seeking global distributors and agents to expand our reach.
