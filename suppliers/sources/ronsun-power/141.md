Description
This 30kW (37.5kVA) Biomass/Gas Generator Set is a robust power solution designed for maximum efficiency and longevity. Engineered to operate on various fuel sources including Biogas, Natural Gas, LPG, and biomass-derived syngas, this unit is perfectly suited for small-to-medium-sized industrial applications, farms, and distributed energy projects.
Key Features & Advantages:
Versatile Fuel Compatibility: Specialized gas mixer and ignition system ensure stable operation on variable gas qualities.
High Efficiency: Optimized engine design delivers superior thermal efficiency, reducing operational costs.
Durability: Built with high-grade components to withstand continuous operation under heavy loads.
Advanced Control System: Equipped with an intelligent digital controller for real-time monitoring and protection.
Customization (OEM/ODM): As a direct manufacturer, we offer full customization services including soundproof canopies, mobile trailers, and CHP (Combined Heat and Power) integration.
Application Scenarios: Ideal for livestock farms (biogas), landfills, sewage treatment plants, food processing factories, and off-grid locations requiring reliable backup or prime power. We welcome inquiries from global distributors, wholesalers, and agents looking for competitive, high-quality gas power generation equipment.
