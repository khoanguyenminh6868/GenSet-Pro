Description
Product Overview:
Our 350kW / 438kVA Diesel Generator Set is a robust power solution engineered for stability and durability. As a professional manufacturer serving the global market, we offer this high-efficiency unit for wholesale, OEM, and ODM customization. It fits perfectly into power grids requiring consistent prime power or critical standby backup.
Key Features & Advantages:
High Reliability: Powered by a premium water-cooled diesel engine with turbocharged aspiration for optimal load acceptance.
Stable Output: Equipped with a self-exciting, brushless alternator and advanced Automatic Voltage Regulation (AVR) to ensure clean power for sensitive equipment.
Cost-Effective: Designed for fuel efficiency and low maintenance costs over a long service life.
Global Standards: Manufactured to meet international ISO and industrial safety standards.
Application Scenarios:
This generator is ideal for industrial factories, construction sites, mining operations, oil fields, hospitals, data centers, and commercial real estate needing reliable 3-phase power.
