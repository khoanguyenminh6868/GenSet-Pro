Description
Professional 30kW Biogas Generator Set for Global Operations
As a premier manufacturer specializing in gas power generation, we offer this robust 30kW (37.5kVA) Biogas Generator Set designed to convert waste gas into continuous electricity. This unit is built for durability and efficiency, making it an excellent choice for a wide range of applications.
Key Features & Advantages:
High Efficiency: Optimized gas mixing technology ensures maximum combustion efficiency and stable power output even with varying biogas methane content.
Cost-Effective Operation: Significantly reduces electricity costs by utilizing locally available biogas sources from livestock farms, landfills, or wastewater treatment.
Resilient Design: Engineered to withstand the specific properties of biogas, featuring corrosion-resistant components and reinforced engine structures.
Smart Control: Equipped with advanced digital controllers for automatic start/stop, grid synchronization (optional), and comprehensive engine protection.
Eco-Friendly: Low emission levels supporting green energy initiatives and carbon footprint reduction.
Applications:
Livestock and Poultry Farms (Piggery, Dairy, etc.)
Sewage and Wastewater Treatment Plants
Food Processing Factories
Landfill Gas Projects
We provide complete OEM/ODM services, including wholesale manufacturing, rebranding, and customized specifications to meet local market needs. We are actively seeking global distributors, agents, and trade partners.
