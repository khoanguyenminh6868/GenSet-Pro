Description
Product Overview:
Our 200kW / 250kVA Biomass Generator Set is a robust renewable energy solution designed for converting biomass fuels (such as wood chips, straw, rice particles, and other agricultural waste gasification products) into stable electricity. As a specialized manufacturer, we offer full OEM/ODM services, tailoring the unit to specific site requirements.
Key Features & Advantages:
Specialized Gas Engine: Designed with a high-performance ignition system and optimized air-fuel ratio control specifically for low-heat value biomass gases.
High Efficiency: Innovative mixer technology ensures thorough combustion, reducing fuel consumption and operational costs.
Durability: Reinforced engine components withstand the impurities often found in biomass gas, ensuring a longer service life.
Safety Systems: Includes comprehensive protection against over-speed, low oil pressure, high water temperature, and ignition failure.
Smart Control: Equipped with an intelligent LCD controller for real-time monitoring and optional remote management capability.
Application Scenarios:
This generator is ideal for wood processing plants, rice mills, agricultural farms, landfills, and remote areas utilizing gasification technology for power generation. It supports both island operation and grid-connected modes.
Global Partnership:
We are actively seeking distributors, wholesalers, and agents worldwide. We provide competitive wholesale pricing, custom branding (support for white-labeling), and dedicated technical support.
