Gas Generator Set
We are a premier manufacturer of high-efficiency Gas Generator Sets, specializing in Natural Gas and Biogas power solutions for the global market. Our products offer reliable, clean energy for industrial and residential use, backed by comprehensive OEM/ODM services for wholesalers and distributors worldwide.
Diesel Generator Set
We are a leading global manufacturer specializing in high-performance Diesel Generator Sets for industrial and commercial applications. About Ronsunpower
business
Advanced Gas Power Technology
Weifang Ronsun Power Technology Co., Ltd. specializes in the R&D and manufacturing of gas generator sets and gas engines for more than 10 years. Its gas-fired power generation technology has been in the international leading position. The unit adopts the world's advanced lean combustion technology and ignition control system, imported gas mixing system and reduction. The pressure system has the characteristics of high efficiency, easy start, explosion-proof vibration and full intelligent operation. And international famous brand engines such as Cummins, PERKINS, DEUTZ, WEICHAI, STAMFORD, LEROY SOMER, MARATHON Generator manufacturers have established long-term and stable strategic partnerships.

One-Stop Power Solutions
Ronsun Power provides a complete range of gas generator sets covering biogas, natural gas, coalbed methane, coking gas, straw gas, biomass gas, and other gas fuels, with calorific values ranging from 800 to 18,000 kcal. The power output of a single generator set ranges from 10kW to 1000kW, fully meeting the needs of small, medium, and large-scale power generation projects. From pre-sales planning and system design to installation guidance and after-sales service, Ronsun Power delivers comprehensive one-stop gas power generation solutions. Its products are widely used in agriculture, food processing, energy, and industrial sectors, and have been successfully exported to Australia, Italy, France, Southeast Asia, South America, Africa, and other regions worldwide.
