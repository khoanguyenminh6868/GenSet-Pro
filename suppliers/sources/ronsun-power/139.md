Description
Professional 40kW 50kVA Biogas Power Solution
Designed for sustainability and efficiency, this 40kW Biogas Generator Set converts biomass waste into reliable electrical power. As a leading manufacturer, we provide robust engines specifically calibrated for biogas, natural gas, and LPG applications. This unit is ideal for agricultural farms, landfill sites, sewage treatment plants, and industrial distributed energy projects.
Key Features:
- High Efficiency: Optimized gas mixing system ensures complete combustion and low gas consumption.
- Durability: Rugged engine design withstands the impurities found in biogas.
- Cost-Effective: Significantly lowers operational costs by utilizing waste gas for power.
- Customization: We offer OEM/ODM services, including soundproof canopies, mobile trailers, and ATS integration.
- Control System: Equipped with SmartGen/DeepSea controllers for intelligent monitoring and protection.
