Description
High-Efficiency 100kW Biomass & Gas Generator Set
Our 100kW (125kVA) generator set is a versatile power solution designed for maximum efficiency in utilizing renewable energy sources. Whether fueled by natural gas, biogas, or biomass, this unit delivers stable and continuous power, making it an excellent choice for a variety of sectors.
Key Features & Advantages:
Fuel Flexibility: Specifically engineered to operate on Natural Gas (NG), Biogas, LPG, and Biomass gas, reducing dependency on diesel and lowering operational costs.
Eco-Friendly Operation: Features low emissions and clean combustion technology, supporting green energy initiatives and environmental compliance.
Robust Design: Equipped with a heavy-duty industrial gas engine and a premium brushless alternator for long service life and high reliability.
Smart Control System: Includes an advanced digital control panel with auto-start/stop functionality, comprehensive monitoring, and safety protection against over-speed, low oil pressure, and high temperature.
Cost-Effective: Lower fuel consumption and reduced maintenance requirements provide an excellent return on investment (ROI).
Application Scenarios:
Ideal for livestock farms (biogas), landfills, waste treatment plants, oil & gas fields, industrial factories, and commercial buildings requiring standby or prime power. We welcome global distributors, wholesalers, and agents for OEM/ODM cooperation.
