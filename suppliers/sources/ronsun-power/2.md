Weifang Ronsun Power Technology Co., Ltd.
About Us
Weifang Ronsun Power Technology Co., Ltd. was founded with a clear focus on gas power generation technology. For more than 10 years, the company has been dedicated to the research, development, and manufacturing of gas generator sets and gas engines, continuously improving product reliability and performance through technological innovation. With a strong engineering background and a commitment to quality, Ronsun Power has gradually grown into a professional gas power solution provider, serving customers in multiple industries and international markets.
business
Advanced Gas Power Technology
Ronsun Power adopts internationally advanced lean combustion technology, intelligent ignition control systems, and high-quality imported gas mixing and pressure reduction systems. These technologies ensure high efficiency, stable operation, easy starting, explosion-proof vibration resistance, and intelligent control of the generator sets. The company has established long-term and stable strategic partnerships with world-renowned engine and alternator brands, including Cummins, Perkins, Deutz, Weichai, Stamford, Leroy Somer, and Marathon, ensuring reliable performance and international-standard product quality.
list_alt
One-Stop Power Solutions
Ronsun Power provides a complete range of gas generator sets covering biogas, natural gas, coalbed methane, coking gas, straw gas, biomass gas, and other gas fuels, with calorific values ranging from 800 to 18,000 kcal. The power output of a single generator set ranges from 10kW to 1000kW, fully meeting the needs of small, medium, and large-scale power generation projects. From pre-sales planning and system design to installation guidance and after-sales service, Ronsun Power delivers comprehensive one-stop gas power generation solutions. Its products are widely used in agriculture, food processing, energy, and industrial sectors, and have been successfully exported to Australia, Italy, France, Southeast Asia, South America, Africa, and other regions worldwide.
