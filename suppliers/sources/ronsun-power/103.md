Description
Reliable and Eco-Friendly 200kW Natural Gas Power Generation
Our 200kW (250kVA) Natural Gas Generator Set offers a robust electricity solution for varied industrial applications. As a professional manufacturer, we specialize in R&D and production of gas-powered gensets, providing custom OEM/ODM services for global markets. This unit combines high efficiency with lower operating costs compared to diesel alternatives.
Key Features & Advantages:
Clean Energy: Utilizes natural gas, biogas, or LPG, significantly reducing carbon footprint and exhaust emissions.
Cost-Effective Operation: Lower fuel costs and longer maintenance intervals ensure a high return on investment.
Advanced Control System: Equipped with intelligent controls for automatic start/stop and real-time monitoring of voltage, frequency, and load.
Durable Design: Built with a heavy-duty chassis and high-grade components to withstand continuous operation in harsh environments.
Customization: We offer open-type, silent canopy, and containerized designs tailored to your site requirements.
Applications:
Ideal for oil fields, mining sites, hospitals, data centers, factories, and remote areas requiring stable primary or backup power. We are actively seeking global distributors, agents, and wholesalers to join our network.
