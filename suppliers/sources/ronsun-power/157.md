Description
Our 200kW (250kVA) Biogas Generator Set is engineered for high performance and durability, utilizing renewable energy sources such as biogas, methane, and landfill gas. As a professional manufacturer, we offer complete OEM, ODM, and customization services for global wholesalers and distributors.
Key Features & Advantages:
Eco-Friendly Power: Specifically tuned for biogas combustion, reducing greenhouse gas emissions and converting waste into reliable energy.
High Efficiency: Equipped with an advanced gas engine featuring an optimized air-fuel ratio control system for maximum electrical efficiency.
Durable Construction: Heavy-duty chassis and industrial-grade components ensure stable operation under continuous load (COP) conditions.
Advanced Control: Integrated digital control panel with monitoring functions for voltage, frequency, and safety shutdowns.
Cost-Effective: Low operational and maintenance costs compared to traditional diesel gensets.
Application Scenarios:
Livestock and Poultry Farms (converting manure to energy).
Food Processing Plants.
Wastewater Treatment Facilities.
Landfill Gas Recovery Projects.
Remote off-grid power generation.
