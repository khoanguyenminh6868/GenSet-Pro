Description
This 280kW / 350kVA Diesel Generator Set is engineered for optimal reliability and durability, making it an excellent choice for a wide range of power generation needs. As a professional manufacturer, we offer comprehensive OEM/ODM services, supporting wholesale distribution worldwide.

Robust Power: Capable of delivering continuous prime power and reliable standby power for critical applications.
