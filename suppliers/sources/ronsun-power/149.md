Description
Product Overview:
This 500kW (625kVA) Biogas Generator Set is a robust power solution specifically engineered for the conversion of biomass gas into electricity. Designed by a professional manufacturer with extensive R&D capabilities, this unit offers high thermal efficiency and stable performance for continuous operation.
Key Features & Advantages:
- High Efficiency: Optimized air-fuel ratio control system ensures complete combustion and maximum power output from variable biogas sources.
- Durability: Rugged engine design withstands the impurities often found in biogas, reducing maintenance frequency.
- Advanced Control: Equipped with a smart control panel for real-time monitoring of voltage, current, frequency, and engine health.
- Cost-Effective: Significantly lowers electricity costs for businesses by utilizing waste gas (methane) for power generation.
- Eco-Friendly: Reduces greenhouse gas emissions by burning methane effectively.
Application Scenarios:
Ideal for Landfill Gas Plants, Wastewater Treatment Plants (Sewage Gas), Livestock Farms (Pig/Cow Manure Digesters), and Food Waste Processing Facilities.
