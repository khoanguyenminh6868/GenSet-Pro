Reliable 50kW / 63kVA Diesel Generator Set for Global Applications
As a leading manufacturer specializing in power generation solutions, we offer this high-performance 50kW (63kVA) diesel generator set. Built for resilience and efficiency, this unit is engineered to meet the demands of distributors, wholesalers, and project contractors worldwide. We support OEM, ODM, and custom manufacturing to suit specific brand requirements.
Key Features & Advantages:
Robust Power Output: Delivers a stable 50kW rated power, capable of handling heavy electrical loads with ease.
Fuel Efficiency: Equipped with an advanced injection system that optimizes fuel consumption, reducing long-term operational costs.
Durability: Features a reinforced steel chassis and high-quality components designed to withstand harsh operating environments.
Easy Maintenance: Designed with accessible service points to simplify routine checks and maintenance tasks.
Global Compatibility: Available in 50Hz or 60Hz configurations to match regional grid requirements.
Application Scenarios:
Construction Sites: Providing temporary power for heavy machinery and tools.
Commercial Buildings: Emergency backup for offices, hotels, and retail centers during grid failures.
Small Industries: Prime power source for workshops and small-scale manufacturing units.
Telecom Towers: Ensuring uninterrupted communication networks in remote locations.
