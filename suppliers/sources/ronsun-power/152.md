Description
Product Overview:
Our 2000kW / 2500kVA Diesel Generator Set is a robust power generation unit designed to meet the rigorous demands of industrial, commercial, and utility sectors. Built for longevity and high efficiency, this genset is an ideal solution for continuous prime power or reliable standby backup.
Key Features:
High Reliability: Equipped with a world-renowned engine and high-efficiency alternator, ensuring stable voltage and frequency output.
Advanced Control System: Features a digital control panel with auto-start, auto-stop, and comprehensive monitoring of engine parameters (oil pressure, water temperature, speed, etc.).
Durability: Rugged base frame and vibration dampeners reduce wear and tear, extending the lifespan of the unit.
Global Compatibility: Available in 50Hz and 60Hz configurations to suit international power grid standards.
Customization available: We offer open-type, silent canopy, and containerized options depending on noise requirements and environmental conditions.
Applications:
This 2.5MVA generator is perfect for data centers, large manufacturing plants, mining sites, hospitals, shopping malls, construction sites, and remote oil fields requiring dependable heavy-duty power.
