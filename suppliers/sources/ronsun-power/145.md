Description
This 80kW (100kVA) Biomass and Gas Generator Set represents the cutting edge of renewable energy technology. Designed for versatility and durability, it excels in converting various gas sources—including biomass, natural gas, biogas, and coal bed gas—into stable electricity.
Key Features & Advantages:
High Efficiency: Features a specialized gas engine with an advanced ignition system and electronic governor, ensuring optimal fuel combustion and lower operational costs.
Durability: Built with high-strength components to withstand the rigorous demands of continuous industrial operation.
Eco-Friendly: Significantly reduces carbon emissions compared to traditional diesel generators, supporting green energy initiatives.
Customization: We offer comprehensive OEM/ODM services, including soundproof canopies, containerized solutions, and ATS (Automatic Transfer Switch) integration.
Applications:
Ideal for animal husbandry farms (biogas), landfills, waste-to-energy plants, rural electrification, and industrial facilities seeking to reduce energy costs by utilizing available gas resources. As a leading manufacturer, we invite global distributors and agents to partner with us.
