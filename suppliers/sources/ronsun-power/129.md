Description
Our 150kW (187.5kVA) Biogas Generator Set is engineered for high efficiency and durability, transforming waste gases into reliable electricity. Manufactured by industry experts in gas power generation, this unit is optimised for continuous operation in demanding environments.
Key Features & Advantages:
High Efficiency Engine: Specially designed for low-calorific value gases like biogas, ensuring stable combustion and maximum power output.
Cost-Effective Operation: Significantly lowers operational costs by utilizing renewable methane from landfills, wastewater treatment plants, or agricultural digesters.
Advanced Control System: Equipped with an intelligent digital controller for automatic monitoring of speed, temperature, and oil pressure, ensuring safety and ease of use.
Robust Alternator: Features a premium brushless alternator with strong anti-interference capabilities and stable voltage output.
Customizable Solutions: We offer comprehensive OEM/ODM services, including silent canopy options, containerized solutions, and Combined Heat and Power (CHP) integration.
Applications: Ideally suited for livestock farms, food processing plants, landfills, and sewage treatment facilities looking to implement green energy solutions. We are actively seeking global distributors, wholesalers, and agents.
