Description
This 120kW (150kVA) Biogas Generator Set is engineered for high-efficiency renewable energy power generation. Designed to operate on biogas derived from landfills, livestock manure (pig/cow farms), wastewater treatment plants, and food waste biomass, it transforms waste gases into stable electricity.
Key Features & Advantages:
High Efficiency: Features an advanced ignition system and optimized air-fuel ratio control (electronic governor) to maximize combustion efficiency and reduce fuel consumption.
Durability: Rugged engine design withstands the impurities often found in biogas, ensuring a longer service life and reduced maintenance intervals.
Eco-Friendly: Low emission levels supporting green energy initiatives.
Control System: Equipped with a smart control panel (Deepsea/ComAp) for automatic start/stop, grid synchronization, and remote monitoring capabilities.
Versatile Application: Ideal for continuous running or standby power in agricultural farms, breweries, sewage plants, and chemical industries.
As a leading manufacturer, we offer comprehensive OEM/ODM services, allowing for customization of canopies (silent type), trailers, and CHP (Combined Heat and Power) systems. We are actively seeking global distributors and wholesale partners.
