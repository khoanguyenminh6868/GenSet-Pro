Description
Product Overview:
This 300kW (375kVA) Biogas Generator Set is a robust power solution designed to convert organic waste gas into reliable electricity. Ideal for farms, wastewater treatment plants, and landfills, this unit maximizes energy efficiency while reducing greenhouse gas emissions.
Key Features & Advantages:
High Efficiency: Optimized lean-burn technology ensures maximum power output with minimal fuel consumption.
Durability: Built with hardened components to withstand the corrosive nature of biogas impurities.
intelligent Control: Equipped with an advanced ComAp or DeepSea control panel for real-time monitoring, auto-synchronization, and safety protection.
Versatile Application: Suitable for continuous base load operation or standby emergency power.
Global Compliance: Manufactured to meet international ISO and CE standards.
