Description
This 1400kW / 1750kVA Diesel Generator Set is engineered for superior performance and reliability in demanding industrial environments. As a professional manufacturer serving the global market, we offer this unit manufactured to strict quality standards, available for OEM, ODM, and wholesale distribution.
Key Features & Advantages:
High Power Output: Delivers a consistent rated power of 1400kW, making it ideal for large-scale operations.
Robust Engine: Powered by a heavy-duty, industrial-grade diesel engine with turbocharging and intercooling technology for optimal fuel efficiency and load response.
Advanced Alternator: Features a high-efficiency brushless alternator with AVR for stable voltage output, ensuring safety for sensitive electronic equipment.
Durable Construction: Built on a rigid steel base frame with vibration isolators to minimize noise and wear during operation.
Smart Control: Equipped with a user-friendly digital control panel providing comprehensive monitoring, protection, and auto-start capabilities.
Application Scenarios:
This generator is the perfect prime or standby power solution for:
Large manufacturing plants and factories.
Data centers and telecommunication hubs.
Hospitals and healthcare facilities.
Mining sites and construction projects.
Commercial buildings, hotels, and shopping malls.
