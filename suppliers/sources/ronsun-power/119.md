Description
Product Overview:
Our 1000kW (1250kVA) Biogas & Gas Generator Set is a heavy-duty power solution specifically designed for the conversion of biogas and natural gas into reliable electricity. As a leading manufacturer, we offer comprehensive OEM, ODM, and custom branded solutions for global distributors and project contractors. This 1MW unit combines robust engineering with advanced control systems to maximize energy output while minimizing operational costs.
Key Features & Advantages:
High Electrical Efficiency: Optimized ignition timing and air-fuel ratio control ensure maximum power generation from every cubic meter of gas.
Fuel Flexibility: Perfectly adaptable for biogas (from landfills, sewage, or biomass) and natural gas pipelines.
Durable Construction: Built to withstand continuous 24/7 operation in demanding industrial environments.
Advanced Control System: Integrated with Deepsea or ComAp controllers for automatic synchronization, remote monitoring, and grid protection.
Low Emissions: Designed to meet strict environmental standards with efficient combustion technology.
Applications:
Ideal for Livestock Farms (Biogas), Landfills, Waste Water Treatment Plants, Natural Gas Power Stations, Industrial Factories, and Oil & Gas Fields requiring off-grid or grid-parallel power.
