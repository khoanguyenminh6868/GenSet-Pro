Description
Product Overview:
Our 150kW (187.5kVA) Syngas Generator Set is specifically designed to convert synthetic gas (produced from biomass gasification, coal gasification, or industrial waste processing) into stable electrical power. Built for durability and continuous operation, this unit is ideal for renewable energy plants and industrial facilities looking to reduce energy costs.
Key Features & Advantages:
Specialized Gas Engine: The engine is optimized with a specific air-fuel ratio control and ignition system to handle the varying calorific values and composition of syngas effectively.
High Efficiency: Utilizes advanced lean-burn technology to maximize power output while maintaining fuel efficiency.
Robust Construction: Designed to withstand the rigors of continuous base-load operation with high-quality components ensuring a long service life.
Comprehensive Protection: Equipped with an intelligent control panel monitoring oil pressure, water temperature, speed, and electrical parameters to prevent damage.
Environmentally Friendly: Promotes waste-to-energy conversion, significantly reducing carbon footprint compared to traditional diesel generators.
