Description
This 30kW (37.5kVA) LPG Generator Set is engineered for durability and efficiency, providing a reliable power solution for a wide range of applications. Designed by a professional manufacturer specializing in gas power generation, this unit utilizes Liquid Petroleum Gas (LPG) to deliver clean, stable electricity with lower emissions compared to traditional diesel generators.
Key Features & Advantages:
Eco-Friendly Power: LPG combustion results in significantly reduced carbon footprint and exhaust pollutants.
High Cost-Efficiency: Lower operational costs due to the availability and pricing of LPG in many regions.
Stable Performance: Equipped with a high-performance engine and AVR (Automatic Voltage Regulator) for stable voltage output, safe for sensitive electronics.
Easy Maintenance: Simplified engine design ensures longer service intervals and easier access for routine maintenance.
Global OEM/ODM Service: We provide full customization options (color, logo, canopy design) for wholesalers, distributors, and agents worldwide.
Application Scenarios: Ideal for use in restaurants, small factories, residential backup, telecommunication base stations, and poultry farms where continuous or reliable standby power is essential.
