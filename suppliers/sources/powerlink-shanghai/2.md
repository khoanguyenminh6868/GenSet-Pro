What types of applications does PowerLink provide gensets for?
PowerLink delivers versatile diesel power generation solutions ranging from 5kVA to 3250kVA. Our reliable industrial power equipment is designed to meet diverse commercial needs. They feature manual or automatic starting capabilities and are available in open, silent canopy, containerized, and trailer mounted configurations.
We primarily engineer our diesel generator sets for two core application types:

Continuous Prime Power: Acting as a reliable continuous energy source for remote mining sites, manufacturing plants, and industrial facilities lacking stable national grid access.
To determine the perfect customized power solution based on your specific installation location and operational requirements, please contact your local PowerLink dealer for expert assistance.
How do I choose the right diesel generator for my project?
Selecting the correct power generation equipment depends on essential factors like required power output, delivery time, operating environment, and total installation cost. Our modular diesel generators offer highly competitive setup costs and exceptional power generation efficiency. To ensure you select the optimal capacity, we strongly recommend a thorough load analysis. Please contact our technical team for a customized power solution based on your specific site conditions.
What standard components are included in your generator sets?
Our comprehensive diesel generator sets come fully equipped for immediate operation. Standard components include a premium diesel engine, reliable alternator, intelligent PLC control system for parallel operations, main circuit breaker, cooling radiator, advanced filtration systems, structural steel chassis, silencer, battery, and a daily fuel tank. We also offer extensive customized accessories to meet your specific project requirements.
What are the fuel tank options for your diesel generators?
We provide flexible fuel storage options depending on the structural design of the genset. Open type units generally feature a single layer base fuel tank integrated directly into the chassis. Silent canopy models typically include an independent fuel tank housed safely within the chassis structure. Containerized power units are equipped with standard single layer daily fuel tanks, and double layer customized fuel tanks are readily available upon request.
Do you provide automatic transfer switches and power distribution equipment?
Yes, PowerLink supplies high quality automatic transfer switches and comprehensive power distribution systems. A transfer switch is essential for safely connecting the standby generator to your site distribution panel, preventing dangerous electrical feedback into the main utility grid. We can customize all distribution equipment to precisely match your project specifications.
How do your silent diesel generators reduce operational noise?
We engineer our silent diesel generators using three primary noise reduction techniques. First, we install specialized exhaust silencers to minimize engine combustion noise. Second, we utilize advanced cooling fan designs to significantly reduce axial airflow noise. Finally, we apply premium sound insulation materials within the canopy or container and utilize vibration isolation mounts to ensure quiet operation in sensitive environments.
Are your diesel generators suitable for harsh weather conditions?
PowerLink continuous power generators are robustly engineered to perform reliably in extreme weather and demanding industrial environments. While our equipment operates efficiently in temperatures ranging from negative 25 degrees Celsius to 50 degrees Celsius, we highly recommend proper sheltering to extend the lifespan of the internal components, prevent rust, and avoid electrical shorts during severe weather events.
How is the AC alternator protected from overloads and faults?
We secure our AC alternators using advanced electrical protection mechanisms. These include thermal magnetic protection to prevent damage from overloads and short circuits, leakage protection switches to safeguard personnel from electrical shocks, and insulation protection relays to monitor system resistance. These integrated safety features ensure our power equipment operates securely under all demanding load conditions.

Every PowerLink product includes a detailed maintenance schedule based on operating hours and environmental conditions. We advise conducting visual inspections every two to three months to ensure optimal performance. What are the starting modes of diesel engines?
The common starting methods of diesel engines are divided into manual starting (hand crank), electric starting (lead battery), gas starting and hydraulic starting motors. Among them, the electric starting mode is the most popular device of diesel engine, which is widely accepted by the market because of easy use, simple maintenance and stable work, and the complete electric starting system needs: electric motor, battery, control components, cables, chargers and so on. For large power diesel generators usually use compressed air to start, divided into compressed air and air motor start.
