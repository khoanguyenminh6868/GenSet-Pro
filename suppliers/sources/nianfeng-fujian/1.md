NIANFENG (FUJIAN) MOTOR CO., LTD. founded in July 2018, is located in Fu'an City, Fujian Province. The company covers an area of 40,000 square meters and has more than 200 employees. It is a professional manufacturer for the ac generator, brushless alternator and high-voltage generator. The power covers 8kVA to 6250kVA, the production capacity is over 60000 pieces per year.
