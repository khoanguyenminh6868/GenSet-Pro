BISON / Factory
BISON machinery factory
BISON is a machinery manufacturer based in Taizhou, Zhejiang, China.
Contact us Our services
ZheJiang BISON Machinery Co., Ltd.
Our factory covers 30,000 square meters and has about 340 employees. More than four production lines, automated equipment and our technical team support the manufacture of more than 2,000 product series.
Our range covers generators, water pumps, small engines, cleaning equipment, garden power tools, power tools, parts and accessories. For cleaning, we offer pressure washers, floor scrubbers, industrial sweepers and wet & dry vacuum cleaners.
Product design and quality control
We focus on practical designs and continue to improve our products. Our own test laboratory and dedicated testing equipment support this work. We check components and finished machines before packing.
Millions of BISON machines have been sold in China and overseas. Our products have been exported to 203 countries and regions worldwide.
