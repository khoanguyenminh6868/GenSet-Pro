What is a diesel generator?
Diesel generator is a device that converts this fuel into electrical energy, thanks to the combination of the two main components, diesel engine and generator. A diesel engine is a device that uses fuel to work. The engine and the generator are connected by a crankshaft, and the crankshaft drives the generator to rotate to convert mechanical energy into electrical energy.
How does a diesel generator work?
Compared with gasoline generators, the difference lies in the type of ignition of the engine. Gasoline engines use spark ignition, while diesel engines use compression ignition to ignite the fuel. This causes diesel engines to reach much higher temperatures than gasoline engines. Because diesel engines only compress air, the compression ratio can be much higher. Generally, the air compression ratio of a diesel engine is between 14:1 and 25:1, while the compression ratio of a gasoline engine is between 8:1 and 12:1. At this temperature and pressure, the diesel fuel entering the engine can be fully burned.
The diesel engine drives the crankshaft of the generator to rotate. In a generator, when the rotor rotates at a high speed, the conductor cuts the magnetic field, so current is generated.
Are diesel generators better than gasoline generators?
Diesel generators have higher power output and higher efficiency and are more suitable for high demand or remote applications than gasoline. In terms of price, diesel generators tend to cost more, but diesel generators require less overall maintenance.
