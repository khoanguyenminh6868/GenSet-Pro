7KW diesel engine generator Faq
Q: What is diesel engine generator?
A: A diesel generator is used to generate electric energy by using a diesel engine along with an electric generator. A diesel generator can be used as an emergency power supply in case of power cuts or in places where there is no connection with the power grid.
Q: Are diesel engine generators efficient?
A: The main pros of diesel generators compared to petrol generators: Diesel engines are more fuel efficient that petrol engines. This means longer running time when running at the same capacity. Some diesel engines consume up to half as much fuel as compatible petrol engines.
