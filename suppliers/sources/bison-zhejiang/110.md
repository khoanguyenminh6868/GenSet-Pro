10 kva domestic diesel generator details
BISON, China's leading diesel generator manufacturer, is proud to introduce our 10kVA domestic diesel generator. Our product line, including the BS6500, BS7500 and BS11000SE models, offer a variety of maximum and rated outputs, delivering up to 10kVA for seamless power delivery. Customized to your specifications, you can choose single-phase or three-phase operation to easily adapt to your electrical configuration.
One of the main advantages of this generator is its powerful 10kVA output. This capacity is ideal for powering a range of home appliances and systems, from heating and cooling to kitchen appliances, even during widespread power outages. It ensures that family life can continue as usual with minimal disruption.
The BISON 10kVA generator is designed for domestic use and offers two unique advantages. First, it provides reliable, stable power that is perfectly suited to the needs of modern households. Secondly, its compact design and ease of installation make it an unobtrusive addition to any property.
Not only are these BISON generators available in a variety of output and phase options, they are also available in a wide range of AC voltages. A self-excited brushed alternator optimizes power generation, while an electric starting system improves ease of use. Additionally, a 12V/8.3A DC output is available for auxiliary power needs.
Our generators have fuel tanks with a capacity of 14.5 to 25 liters and are designed for long periods of operation without the need for frequent refueling. These generators are carefully manufactured in our state-of-the-art facility with strict quality control measures to ensure each generator meets the highest industry standards, reflecting our commitment to excellence and customer satisfaction.
