What is a gasoline generator?
A gasoline generator is a portable power source that uses gasoline as a fuel to generate electricity. Gasoline generators are used in many applications and are popular worldwide. And are commonly found in a variety of applications, including construction, emergency, recreation, and camping.
Gasoline generators are low cost and easy to use. They are available in a variety of sizes, from small units capable of powering a single device to large units capable of powering an entire house. At full capacity, they tend to be noisy and emit toxic fumes such as carbon monoxide (CO).
How long will gasoline run a generator?
These numbers obviously depend on the type and size of the engine in the generator, as well as how efficiently it converts gasoline into energy. You can find your engine's efficiency rating in its manual or online.
You can estimate fuel consumption by knowing two things: the load on the generator (in watts) and the amount of fuel it burns (in gallons per hour).
A portable generator with a 5-gallon fuel tank can last about 8 hours at 50% load, which is enough to run a few refrigerators and a few lights. At 100% load, however, it only takes 4 hours to use up all the gas.

Gasoline generator maintenance
In all types of generators, good maintenance is essential to ensure the normal operation of the equipment and prolong its service life. If the gasoline generator is operating under a lot of dust or high temperature, it must be maintained more frequently.
Maintenance plan:
Engine oil: Check the oil level before each use. Change the oil every 20 hours for the first and second time. The oil is changed every 100 hours thereafter.
Oil filter: check and clean every 50 hours. Replace it when it looks bad.
Spark plug: Clean and adjust the electrode every 100 hours. Replace when damaged.
Fuel valve filter: clean every 300 hours or 1 year.
Engine valve: adjusted every 500 hours.
Combustion chamber: clean every 500 hours.
Fuel tank: Clean every 500 hours.
Fuel hose: Replace every two years or when damaged.
