4.5KW silent genset details
BISON 4.5KW silent genset combines a variety of measures to prevent sound from leaking to the outside, so they produce almost no noise during operation. The noise level is limited to 65-69 decibels (A), which greatly reduces the impact of noise on users.
A high-performance shock absorber is used between the diesel engine and the generator to reduce the noise caused by vibration;
The sound insulation cover, which is made of four to five layers of sound-absorbing materials, has obvious sound insulation effect;
The air inlet muffler, the labyrinth-type air inlet muffler with three layers of sound-absorbing materials, not only guarantees enough fresh air to enter, but also effectively seals the noise;
Exhaust muffler greatly reduces the noise when exhaust gas is discharged;
The complete combination of diesel engine, 4.5KW silent diesel generator, and various accessory devices (such as base, cover, muffler, control system, circuit breaker, jacketed water heater, and starting system) is called "generator set".
The diesel silent genset can be used on ships to provide reliable and efficient services under extreme conditions. Our marine diesel genset combine proven design and manufacturing methods with the latest technologies such as advanced control to provide greater power and maintain low noise.
4.5KW silent genset, sometimes not only to provide auxiliary power for lights, fans, winches, etc. but also indirectly to provide auxiliary power for main propulsion. With electric propulsion, the 4.5KW silent genset can be placed in a convenient location to carry more goods.
