Electrical and mechanical data
TAL 049
The best of performance
The Leroy-SomerTM TAL049 alternator has been designed to offer you the best power generation
performances. With its meticulous design and optimized architecture, the TAL 049 strikes the perfect
balance between compactness, reliability, performance and longevity.
Whatever your application, the Leroy-SomerTM TAL049 alternator will meet your needs and will adapt
to all situations.
Standards
The Leroy-SomerTM TAL049 alternator meets all key international standards and regulations such as
IEC 60034, NEMA MG 1.32-33, ISO 8528-3, CSA C22.2 n°100-14, UL 1446, UL 1004-1 and UL 1004-4.
EC, UKCA, CMIM, UL 1446, UL recognized and UL listed declarations and certifications are available
for the TAL 049.
The standards IEC 61000-6-2, IEC 61000-6-3, IEC 61000-6-4, VDE 0875G, VDE 0875N and EN 55011
allow compliance with group 1 class A for the European zone.
The Leroy-SomerTM TAL049 alternator is designed, manufactured and marketed in an ISO 9001 and
ISO 14001 quality assurance environment.
