LSA 44.3
The best of performance
The Leroy-SomerTM LSA44.3 alternator has been designed to offer you the best power generation performances.
With its meticulous design and optimized architecture, the LSA 44.3 strikes the perfect balance between compactness, reliability,
performance and longevity.
Whatever your application, the Leroy-SomerTM LSA44.3 alternator will meet your needs and will adapt to all situations.
Standards
The Leroy-SomerTM LSA44.3 alternator meets all key international standards and regulations such as IEC 60034,
NEMA MG 1.32-33, ISO 8528-3, CSA C22.2 n°100-14, UL 1446, UL 1004-1 and UL 1004-4.
EC, UKCA, CMIM, CSA, UL 1446, UL recognized and UL listed declarations and certifications are available for the LSA 44.3.
The standards IEC 61000-6-2, IEC 61000-6-3, IEC 61000-6-4, VDE 0875G, VDE 0875N and EN 55011 allow compliance with
group 1 class A for the European zone.
The Leroy-SomerTM LSA44.3 alternator is designed, manufactured and marketed in an ISO 9001 and ISO 14001 quality
assurance environment.
