CORE80
Overview
SMART POWER FOR VERSATILE WORK
Work smart AND hard
The biggest of the future-proof CORE engine family, CORE80 offers 15% more torque and 12% more power than the award-winning CORE75. It’s robust structure and intelligent design make it perfect for jobs that require both muscle and brain. It is the first engine in the CORE family to feature a fuel-saving engine brake, which improves machine uptime and fuel economy.
High response with superior torque
Extremely responsive, the AGCO Power CORE80 has a wide torque range of 1680 Nm available within a useful operating range of 1200–1400 rpm, with excellent dynamics. A Variable Geometry Turbocharger (VGT) enables a braking performance of 90 kW and superior fuel economy. The engine is extremely robust while having 25% fewer parts. It is also EGR-free, making it more reliable and easier to service.
Sustainable power
AGCO Power CORE80 meets the most stringent emission standards and the platform is prepared to fulfill future emission requirements. Fuel efficiency, long engine lifetime and HVO fuel (up to 90% less GHG emissions) compatibility guarantee sustainable performance without compromise. Today and tomorrow.

Built Tough. Works Clever.
The biggest of the CORE family, the 6-cylinder, 8-liter CORE80 offers responsive dynamics with its 252 kW of power and 1680 Nm of torque. But besides working harder, CORE80 works smarter. Its over-the-air monitoring, updates and diagnostics capability gives you more security and control while the all-new Variable Geometry Turbocharger (VGT) enables high torque, excellent response and higher braking power. AGCO Power CORE80 offers you smart, sustainable and future-proof performance.
