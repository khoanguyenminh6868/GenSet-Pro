AGCO Power CORE is an exceptional diesel engine family with its
excellent fuel economy and a streamlined design that enables higher
quality with fewer parts. Rigorously tested and crafted, AGCO Power

versatile work
High response with superior torque
A wide range of useful torque with excellent
dynamics makes the engine extremely
responsive. AGCO Power CORE80 has a
torque of 1680 Nm available in a useful
range of 1200–1400 rpm.
VGT for improved braking power
and fuel economy
AGCO Power CORE80 features a Variable
Geometry Turbocharger (VGT) that enables a
braking performance of 90 kW and an excellent
fuel economy.
Robust and reliable structure
AGCO Power CORE80 has been designed
from the ground up to be extremely robust
while having 25% fewer parts. It is also EGR-
free, making the engine more reliable and
easier to service.
Wireless engine monitoring
AGCO Power CORE80 features over-the-
air (OTA) connection to its engine control
unit, enabling remote diagnostics, effortless
monitoring and simpler updates. This improves
reliability and makes fleet management easier.
Sustainable power
AGCO Power CORE80 meets the most stringent
emission standards and the platform is prepared
to fulfill future emission requirements. Fuel
efficiency, long engine lifetime and HVO (up
to 90% less GHG emissions) compatibility
guarantee sustainable performance without
compromise. Today and tomorrow.

The heritage of reliable power
Founded in 1942 as an aviation engine repair workshop, the
AGCO Power factory in Linnavuori, Finland produced its first
own diesel engine designs already in 1946. Since then, the AGCO
Power engine hallmarks have been long-lived basic constructions,
wide spare part interchangeability and extreme reliability.
Today, AGCO Power has personnel of over a 1000 with
factories in four different countries, producing some 30 000
engines every year for off-road machinery, generators and
marine use as well as transmission components and auxiliary
power units. Building on the heritage of reliable power, AGCO
Power engines combine the tradition of precision engineering
with the most modern engine and emission control technology.
