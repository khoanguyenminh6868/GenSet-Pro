CORE Engine Platform
AGCO Power CORE is a future-proof engine platform offering benchmark performance, excellent response and superior torque. Multi-award-winning CORE engines provide industry-leading fuel efficiency, as proven by the DLG PowerMix tests. The robust and reliable structure is designed for economical use and optimal total value of ownership. Fully HVO-compatible CORE platform meets the most stringent emission standards, and it’s ready for tomorrow’s work with alternative fuels in mind. Times change. Power remains.
