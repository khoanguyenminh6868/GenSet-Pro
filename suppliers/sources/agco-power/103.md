CORE75
Overview
Power for the changing work
Robust, reliable and economical
Versatile CORE75 has a huge potential in various off-road, forestry and agricultural machinery and the engine has already proven its power in the award-winning 6th generation Fendt 700 tractor series. CORE75 has been designed from the ground up to be extremely robust, with 25% fewer parts and no EGR, making the engine more reliable and easier to service. It is perfect for economical long-term use and has excellent fuel efficiency. Superb engine integration and fewer parts mean long life and lower maintenance costs.
Superior torque, response and fuel economy
A wide range of useful torque with excellent dynamics. It provides a torque of 1450 Nm, the highest in its power class*. Maximum torque is achieved at 1300 rpm instead of the more typical 1500 rpm. It offers the best fuel economy on the market today (188 g/kWh). Lower noise level.
* Less than 250 kW.
Sustainable diesel engine
AGCO Power CORE75 meets the most stringent emission standards and the platform is prepared to fulfill future emission requirements. Fuel efficiency, long engine lifetime and HVO (up to 90% less GHG emissions) compatibility guarantee sustainable performance without compromise. Today and tomorrow, on our journey towards more carbon-neutral engines.

VERSATILE POWER. CORE75 DIESEL ENGINE.
AGCO Power CORE75 is a next generation 250 kW 7.5-liter diesel engine with excellent torque, fuel economy and reliability. Optimized for off-road use for tractors and heavy machinery, the engine meets today’s and tomorrow’s tough requirements for efficiency, power generation and sustainability. Its features ensure versatile power – such as excellent torque in low RPM, responsive dynamics and an advanced emission control system.
