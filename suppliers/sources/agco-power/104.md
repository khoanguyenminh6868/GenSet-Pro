CORE50
Overview
FUTURE-PROOF PERFORMANCE
Reliable and economical for long term use
AGCO Power CORE50 has been designed from the ground up to be extremely robust. The simplified, EGR-free structure with less moving parts makes the engine more reliable and easier to service. The future-ready diesel engine is made for economical long-term use. It has excellent fuel efficiency, while the superb engine integration and fewer parts enable long engine lifetime and lower maintenance costs.
Excellent response, torque and fuel economy
Extremely responsive with a wide torque range and excellent dynamics. It provides 950 Nm of torque, one of the highest figures in its power class*. Great fuel efficiency (190 g/kWh) ensures that you get more done with less fuel. Its excellent mechanical design and CO₂/NOx-optimized combustion result in a low cost of ownership.
* Less than 165 kW.
Sustainable power
Saving fuel is both economic and environmental. The AGCO Power CORE50 meets the most stringent emission standards. The platform is also prepared to fulfill future emission requirements. Fuel efficiency, long service life and HVO compatibility (up to 95% less GHG emissions) guarantee sustainable performance without compromise.
