Baudouin 1000kVA Diesel Generator - GFE1100BSC
Call Now
ENQUIRE
The 1000kVA GFE1100BSC Diesel Generator is a high-output power solution designed for demanding commercial and industrial applications. Powered by a robust Baudouin engine and paired with a Stamford alternator, this generator delivers reliable, stable power capable of supporting substantial electrical loads.
Unlike the containerised models in this range, the GFE1100BSC is supplied in a heavy-duty acoustic canopy, making it ideal for sites that require flexibility, faster installation, and effective noise reduction. Its impressive power output makes it well suited for manufacturing facilities, utilities, hospitals, and standby power for critical infrastructure.
