1.Superior Quality Backed by Strong Collaboration
Shanghai Mitsubishi Heavy Engine Co., Ltd., a joint venture with Japan’s Mitsubishi Heavy Industries Co., Ltd., strictly adheres to Mitsubishi’s rigorous technical, production, and quality standards. This ensures every engine meets the highest global benchmarks for performance and durability.
2.Wide Power Range for Versatile Needs
The Kaihua Mitsubishi series covers a high power spectrum from 750 kVA to 2500 kVA, providing a flexible solution to meet diverse power requirements across different scenarios.
3.Cost-Effective with Low Capital Expenditure
With low initial capital expenditure, the series stands as a cost-efficient option for businesses in search of reliable and efficient power generation solutions, balancing performance and economic benefits.
4.Easy Access to Spare Parts for Minimal Downtime
A key advantage lies in the easy availability of spare parts and components. This allows customers to minimize equipment downtime, maximize operational uptime, and ensure uninterrupted power supply for critical operations (e.g., hospitals, data centers).
5.Multi-Scenario Adaptability
Tailored for project applications, the series meets both standby power needs (providing reliable backup for emergencies) and the demands of prime/continuous power stations. Its engines are designed for continuous, efficient operation, delivering stable and reliable power over extended periods.
