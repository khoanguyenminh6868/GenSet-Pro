About Kaihua Diesel Generator
Kaihua marine diesel generators produce from 12kW to 1500kW, while remaining compliant with the latest emission requirements.All the marine generators have been EIAPP(Nox),CCS,BV approved.
Advantages of Marine Diesel Generator
1.Powered by original marine diesel engines
2.Excellent performance
3.24h after-service
4.Environmental-friendly
5.International warranty service
6.CCS emission standard
7.Spare parts are easy to get from worldwide market with competitive price.

3.Marine standard control system
