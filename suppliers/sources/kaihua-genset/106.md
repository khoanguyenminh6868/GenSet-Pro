DEUTZ diesel engine is based on German thchnique,combined the characteristics of compact structure,big power, economic, environmental adaptability and high reliability together.
Now there are more than 400 thousand DUETZ diesel generator sets running reliably all over the world, won the trust by the most users.The outatanding characteristics of DEUTZ diesel generator are low fuel consumption, small size,light weight, high Reliability and durability, slight vibration,low-noise, convenient using and maintenance, which is a successful example of Sino-German cooperation.
About Deutz Series Diesel Generator
DEUTZ was founded in 1864 in Cologne, Germany.
There’re two factories that manufacture Deutz engine in China: DaLian Deutz with power range from 50kva to 200kva; HuaChai Deutz with power range from 225kva to 750kva; We have established long term business cooperation with all these two Deutz factories in China.
Advantages of Deutz Diesel Generator
1. International Warranty Service
2. Water cooled engine
3. With ISO9001&CE certification
4. Spare parts are easy to get from worldwide market with much cheaper price
5. Coupled with Stamford alternator, Marathon alternator or Kaihua alternator
6. Perfect after-service network
7. Power range from 50kva to 750kva, 50hz &60hz
8. Rigorous test including 50% load, 75% load, 100% load and 110% load
9. Very short delivery time
