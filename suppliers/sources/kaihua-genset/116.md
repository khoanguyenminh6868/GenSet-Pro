INTRODUCTION
GAS
The gas generator unit fueled mainly by natural gas, casing-head gas and LPG, is widely used inregions or areas with abundant oil field and natural gas. The unit starts quickly, be operated easily, with complete set of safety system, and can be as power device and electricity device for oil field and local companies.
The main functions:
(1) Drive oil pumping unit;
(2) Generate power by surplus natural gas;
(3) Drive water injector and mixed pumps by separate gas;
(4) In gas station, drive compressor or as substituted power;
(5) For hotel, standby power supply;
(6) In cool-hot power supply system, improve efficiency and lower the cost;
(7) Establish small and medium power plant.
