TAIZHOU KAIHUA DIESEL GENERATOR SETS CO., LTD
Taizhou Kaihua Diesel Generator Sets Co., Ltd. was established in 2003 and is located in Taizhou City, Jiangsu Province. With 22 years of manufacturing experience, the company has an annual production capacity of over 3,000 units. As a high-tech enterprise specializing in the production of generator sets, Kaihua's products have undergone rigorous technical verification and have obtained ISO9001-2015 quality management system certification, as well as several other certifications, including CE, TLC, CCS, and BV.
Kaihua serves as an OEM for renowned brands such as Cummins, Stamford, Marathon, and Weichai, and maintains good cooperation with international brands like MTU, Volvo, and Perkins. The company offers a range of products, including mobile power stations from 15 to 750 KVA, land use generator sets from 3 to 3,750 KVA, and marine generator sets from 12 to 1,250 KVA, capable of meeting diverse needs for voltage, frequency, soundproofing, multi-parallel operation, and gas power generation.
