About PERKINS Series Diesel Generator
KAIHUA PERKINS Series Diesel Generators are powered by PERKINS engines,which have the advantages of high stability,reliability,durability and long life with a wide range of power output.
KAIHUA has been cooperated with PERKINS engine several years,and praised from the customers around the world,eg communication,industry,projects,mining,rescuing,military and other industry preferences.
Advantages of PERKINS Diesel Generator
1.Small size
2.Excellent performance
3.24h after-service
4.Environmental-friendly
5.International warranty service
6.EU emission standard
7.Spare parts are easy to get from worldwide market with competitive price.
Standard Configuration of Perkins Open Type:
1.Perkins engine
2.Stamford/Marathon/Kaihua alternator
3.DSE6020, Smartgen control system
4.Base fuel tank for option
5.ABB,Schneider, Chint breaker
6.Battery and battery switch
7.Industrial silencer and flexible exhaust hose
