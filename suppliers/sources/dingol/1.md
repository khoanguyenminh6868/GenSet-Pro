展商风采 | 顶格电力：行业领先的发电机定制厂家
由中国内燃机工业协会、中国内燃机工业协会应急动力装备专委会、中国电器工业协会内燃发电设备分会、隆高展览（上海）有限公司联合主办的“第24届上海国际动力设备及发电机组展览会”（简称“GPOWER动力展）将于2025年6月11-13日在上海新国际博览中心召开。
届时， 顶格电力设备江阴有限公司 将重磅亮相展会， 展位号：N2-265 ，诚邀莅临展位参观洽谈。
顶格电力设备江阴有限公司
展位号：N2-265
DINGOL 顶格电机 ，更擅长定制，致力于为全球国际一线品牌电站制造商、发动机主机厂、各类行业和系统用户、国内外工程机械企业集团提供高可靠性之卓越性能产品，涵盖船舶、军工、移动通讯、海陆风电、工程机械配套、冷链物流、工业应急及商业租赁等等。
DINGOL 顶格TEAM19年来与全球伙伴共同成就梦想，已经从国际一线品牌发电机的追随者成长为拥有行业领先专业设备、产品配备CCS、CSA、CE证书，持多项独立开发专利技术的差异化产品定制领航者，并广受全球战略合作伙伴和国际一线品牌企业的信赖和支持。
DINGOL Alternators excels in customization, committed to providing high-reliability and superior-performance products for global international first-tier brand power station manufacturers, engine OEMs, various industrial and system users, as well as domestic and foreign construction machinery enterprise groups. Its products cover ships, military industries, mobile communications, onshore and offshore wind power, construction machinery supporting, cold-chain logistics, industrial emergency, commercial leasing, etc.
For 19 years, the DINGOL TEAM has collaborated with global partners to achieve dreams. From being a follower of international first-tier brand Alternators, it has grown into a leader in differentiated product customization. It possesses industry-leading professional equipment；Products equipped with CCS, CSA, and CE certificates, holds multiple independently developed patented technologies, and is widely trusted and supported by global strategic partners and international first-tier brand enterprises.
