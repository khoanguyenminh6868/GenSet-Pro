ENERGY SOLUTIONS FOR VARIOUS DOMAIN
Industrial Generator Sets: Enhance your business operations with Voltgent’s durable indus trial generators. Our carefully designed generator sets, equipped with soundproofing, cater to the varied needs of industries, ensuring r eliable power supply when it is most crucial.
Voltgent’s gene rator sets are versatile and can be utilized across various fields, including rental , data centers , oilfields, coastal areas, tropical regions, and cold climates.
Rental Sector: Voltgent offer tailored rental solutions that meet customer demands, such as mobile units on trucks or trailers, remote management and control options, and high-emission standard-compliant units like T4F and Stage V .
Data Centers : Voltgent deliver customized energy solutions for data centers, designed to meet on-site requirements with layouts offering high starting and load acceptance capabilities .
Oilfields : Voltgent provides generator sets tailored for oilfield applications, meeting end-user requirements with DNV certification, safety compliance options, and solutions with high starting and load acceptance capabilities.
Coastal Areas : Voltgent offers specialized high-corrosion-resistant solutions tailored for coastal applications. Our offerings include Marine C5-grade or stainless enclosures and container corrosion protection generators, ensuring optimal performance in highly corrosive environments. Additionally, we provide internal anti-moisture and anti-corrosion solutions to safeguard the generator set components from the harsh coastal conditions.
Tropical Regions : Voltgent offer solutions designed to withstand hot, tropical environments. In regions with high temperatures, the air becomes thinner, which can significantly affect the performance and efficiency of equipment. To address these challenges, Voltgent provides highly customized cooling solutions tailored specifically for hot climates.
Cold Regions : Voltgent deliver solutions specifically designed for cold weather conditions. In frigid regions, low temperatures can pose significant challenges to equipment functionality. Voltgent offers customized heating solutions tailored for cold climates, ensuring that generator sets can start and operate efficiently in any environment.
