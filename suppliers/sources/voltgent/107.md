SCANIA DIESEL GENERATOR SETS
Voltgent Scania-powered diesel generator sets are available in 50 Hz and 60 Hz for prime and standby applications.
Built around Scania's DC09, DC13 and DC16 power generation engine series , they combine strong torque, high power density and fuel-efficient performance. Service-friendly engine design also helps simplify maintenance, making them well suited for industrial, rental, construction and critical power applications.
