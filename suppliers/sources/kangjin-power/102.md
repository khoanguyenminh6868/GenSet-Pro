Description
Additional information
The QSB6.7 diesel engine is a major performance upgrade over the QSB5.9 platform.
With a displacement of 6.7 liters , an inline 6-cylinder configuration, and a sophisticated advanced electronic control architecture, the QSB6.7 integrates Bosch high-pressure common rail (HPCR) technology , delivering precise fuel injection, optimized combustion efficiency, and exceptional power output while ensuring superior fuel economy and lower emissions.
It has become the preferred engine choice for high-end construction machinery, agricultural equipment, and special-purpose vehicles.
Key Advantages
Significantly Higher Power Output Rated power typically ranges from 99 to 194 kW (135-264 HP) , far exceeding the QSB5.9 platform.
Strong Low-Speed Torque Maximum torque of 580-990 N·m , ideal for heavy-duty and high-load applications.
High Power Density Compact size with outstanding power-to-weight ratio.
Low Operating Cost Optimized combustion and electronic control reduce fuel consumption and extend maintenance intervals.
Core Applications
High-End Construction Machinery Medium to large excavators, cranes, air compressors
Generator Sets Prime and standby power compliant power stations
Agricultural Machinery High-horsepower tractors and combine harvesters
Marine Applications Main or auxiliary engines for vessels requiring high emission standards
Special Vehicles Fire trucks, airport ground support equipment
