Company Profile
Shandong Pulita New Energy Technology Co.,ltd
PULITA ENERGY is a manufacturer specializing in continuous and emergency power generation equipment that established in 2010. The company integrates design, research and development, and manufacturing.
We have an excellent technical team that can customize diversified power solutions for global customers based on customer needs and application scenarios, and provide stable, reliable, intelligent, and environmentally friendly generator sets and supporting power equipment.
