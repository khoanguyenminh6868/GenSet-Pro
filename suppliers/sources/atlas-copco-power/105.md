TDV ( adiabatic) dry coolers series
The TDV is a closed-loop cooling unit, which can be either an adiabatic or dry fluid cooler. It is specifically engineered to efficiently reject heat from water-cooled compressors. These cooling systems ensure perfect temperature regulation, enhancing compressor efficiency, longevity, and overall system reliability.

The TDV is an advanced cooler that makes use of ambient air to effectively cool the water circuit of water-cooled equipment. This innovative system ensures that the desired temperature is consistently maintained.
Additionally, the TDV cooler offers an adiabatic option, which allows it to achieve water temperatures lower than the ambient air temperature. This is made possible through the evaporative effect and the high efficiency of the adiabatic pads. By leveraging these features, the TDV cooler not only guarantees optimal temperature regulation but also enhances the overall efficiency and performance of the water-cooled equipment.
