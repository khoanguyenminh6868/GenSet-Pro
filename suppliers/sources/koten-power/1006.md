Prime power
According to ISO8528-1, prime power is the maximum power available during a variable power sequence, which may be run for
an unlimited number of hours per year, between stated maintenance intervals.The permissible average power output during at 24 hours
period shall not exceed 80% of the prime power. 10% overload available for governing purposes only.
Standby power
According to ISO 8528-1, It is defined as the maximum power available, under the agreed operating conditions, for which the
generating set is capable of delivering for up to 500 hours of operation per year (of which no more than 300 hours for continuative use)
with the maintenance intervals and procedures being carried out as prescribed by the manufacturers. No overload capability is available.
