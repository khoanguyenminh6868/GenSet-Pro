1250kVA Generator for Indonesia Palm Oil Plant
Author: Site Editor Publish Time: 2026-06-12 Origin: Site
Inquire
Project Overview
A client in Indonesia has a palm oil processing plant located in a remote area with insufficient grid coverage and frequent power outages. A reliable backup power solution was required. Powerzoo provided a 1250kVA diesel generator to ensure stable plant operation during mains power outages.
Client Requirements
Reliable power supply, low fuel consumption, ease of maintenance, automatic start-up, remote monitoring, and fault warning.
Challenges
The plant is located in a hot and humid area with humidity levels of 80%–100%, temperatures consistently above 30°C, frequent heavy rainfall, and high levels of palm fiber dust. This project needed to maintain reliable performance under local operating conditions while protecting the palm oil processing plant's critical operations from downtime.
Powerzoo Solution
Powerzoo provided a customized 1250kVA generator set equipped with an intelligent control system, high-quality alternator options, and professional technical support.

Project Achievements:
Customized anti-corrosion, moisture-proof, and dust-proof design for the special operating environment of the factory, preventing radiator blockage and generator winding moisture, reducing downtime, improving production efficiency, and minimizing losses.
