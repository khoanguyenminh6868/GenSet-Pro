Core Technical Advantages of SDEC Diesel Generator Sets
Shanghai Diesel Engine Genset utilizes original SDEC engines as the power core. SDEC (Shanghai Diesel Engine Co., Ltd.), established in 1947 and now affiliated with SAIC Motor Corporation, ranks among China's top three diesel engine manufacturers. Its engine portfolio covers a power range from 8kW to 2176kW, serving applications in trucks, buses, construction machinery, agricultural equipment, and power generation systems. Yangzhou Kangcheng Power Equipment Co., Ltd., as a professional SDEC Diesel Generator manufacturer, precisely matches SDEC engines with alternators from internationally renowned brands including Stamford, Marathon, and Siemens, ensuring that each generator set achieves industry-leading performance in power output, fuel economy, and operational stability.
SDEC engines feature an integral crankshaft, gantry-type body, flat-cut connecting rod, and short piston design, delivering a compact and rational structure. The newly designed tapered combustion chamber technology effectively increases fuel injection pressure and optimizes the combustion process, enabling emission compliance with environmental standards. The D series engines were jointly developed by SDEC and AVL of Austria, with reinforcement design and four-valve upgrades completed in collaboration with the Southwest Research Institute (SwRI) of the United States in 2005. All components are designed and verified to 295kW standards, providing substantial reliability margins within the applicable power range of 185-225kW. The E series engines adopt the Bosch electronically controlled high-pressure common rail system with injection pressures reaching 1800bar, achieving multiple injections and minimum fuel consumption rates as low as 192g/kW·h, making them suitable for high and medium-end generator sets in the 236-495kW range.

Environmental Performance and Low-Temperature Capabilities
SDEC engines demonstrate outstanding environmental performance. Both D series and E series engines comply with China Nonroad Stage III and Stage IV emission standards, with select models meeting US EPA Tier and EU Stage emission requirements. The E series employs Bosch electronically controlled high-pressure common rail systems with 1800bar injection pressure, achieving uniform combustion through multiple injection technology and significantly reducing particulate matter and nitrogen oxide emissions. The H series engines offer torque reserves of 30%-36%, covering power ratings from 60-162kW, and also meet nonroad China III and China IV emission standards.
In terms of low-temperature starting performance, SDEC engines excel. Without air intake heating assistance, the minimum starting temperature reaches -10 degrees Celsius. With preheating assistance, the minimum starting temperature can drop to -30 degrees Celsius. Select models maintain excellent cold start capabilities at -40 degrees Celsius, meeting GJB-235B certification standards for 5000-meter altitude operation, with units below 55kW capable of operating at 3500 meters without power loss. Silent-type generator sets operate at noise levels below 70 decibels under full load, while open-type units maintain noise control within 86 decibels, achieving passenger vehicle-grade vibration and noise standards.

How do SDEC engines compare with Cummins engines
SDEC engines are comparable in quality to Cummins QSB series and 8.3L, 8.9L series engines. As a state-owned enterprise with over 70 years of engine manufacturing experience, SDEC products are recognized for high reliability, excellent performance, and competitive pricing. The D series and E series engines integrate international advanced technology with local market requirements, delivering outstanding performance in fuel economy, low-temperature starting, and emission control, making them a highly cost-effective power solution.
How to choose between silent type and open type generator sets
Silent-type units feature soundproof enclosures with noise levels below 70 decibels, making them suitable for hospitals, schools, residential areas, and other noise-sensitive environments, though they are relatively larger and heavier. Open-type units offer compact structure, superior heat dissipation, and easier maintenance access, with noise levels around 86 decibels, making them ideal for factory workshops, mining sites, and outdoor construction projects where noise restrictions are less stringent. Yangzhou Kangcheng Power Equipment Co., Ltd. can provide professional selection recommendations based on site conditions.
Can the fuel tank capacity be customized
Standard SDEC diesel generator sets are equipped with base fuel tanks supporting 8 hours of operation. Yangzhou Kangcheng Power Equipment Co., Ltd. offers customized fuel tank solutions based on customer requirements, including enlarged base tanks, external daily fuel tanks, and fuel level sensors, to meet the needs of extended continuous operation or unmanned operation scenarios. Specific customization plans can be discussed with the company's sales team.
What voltage and frequency specifications do SDEC generator sets support
SDEC diesel generator sets produced by Yangzhou Kangcheng Power Equipment Co., Ltd. support both 50Hz and 60Hz frequencies, with voltage specifications including 220/127V, 380/220V, 400/230V, 415/240V, 440/254V, 460/266V, and 480/277V, accommodating both single-phase and three-phase power requirements. Customers can select the appropriate specifications based on their country or regional grid standards, and the company also provides non-standard voltage customization services.
How to verify the authenticity of an SDEC engine
Genuine SDEC engines can be verified through the following methods: first, inspect the engine appearance, color, and overall color difference against factory standards; second, check that brand identification stickers on the engine body are clear and intact; third, verify the engine serial number on the nameplate and locate corresponding codes on the cylinder block and fuel pump.
