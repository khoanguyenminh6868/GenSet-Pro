About Us
Yangzhou Kangcheng Power Equipment Co., Ltd. was founded in 2003 and is located in Jiangdu District, Yangzhou, a famous historical and cultural city. It borders the Beijing-Shanghai Expressway to the east, the Nanjing-Nantong Expressway to the south, and the North Jiangdu Exit of the Yangzhou-Liyang Expressway to the north, enjoying convenient transportation.
Specializing in the production of generators and diesel generator sets, Yangzhou Kangcheng Power Generation Equipment Co., Ltd. has been a qualified manufacturer inspected and certified by the National Internal Combustion Engine Generator Set Quality Supervision and Inspection Center since its establishment.
Our Kangcheng diesel generator sets ranging from 7KW to 2500KW adopt world-renowned engines including MAN, MTU, Perkins, Cummins, IVECO and VOLVO, as well as domestic leading engines such as Shanghai Diesel Engine, Weichai Power and Yuchai Machinery.
They are matched with alternators from Stamford, Marathon, Siemens and other well-known domestic brands.
Our products have passed the inspection of the National Internal Combustion Engine Generator Set Quality Supervision and Inspection Center, and obtained certifications including ISO9001 International Quality Management System and ISO14000 Environmental Management System.
The company also holds independent import and export rights, network access qualifications issued by the Ministry of Industry and Information Technology and the National Radio, Film and Television Administration.
Through the joint efforts of all staff and sustainable development, the company has grown into an integrated enterprise integrating engineering design, marketing, equipment installation, environmental noise reduction and technical services.
Adhering to the principle of mutual benefit and win-win results for many years, we have maintained trade and cooperative relations with customers at home and abroad.
Convenient transportation networks, advanced production equipment and sound management systems guarantee product quality and drive the rapid and sound development of the company.
Our company designs and manufactures land-use diesel generator sets of various specifications and models.
Our products are widely applied in highways, railways, mining operations, enterprises, airports and other fields, serving as primary power supply or emergency backup power supply for users in various industries.
Our nationwide sales network and after-sales service team cover the whole country. We are committed to providing customers with high-quality products, strict management and thoughtful services.
