Core Technology of Chongqing Pangu Power Engines
The core team of Chongqing Pangu Power Technology Co., Ltd. entered the diesel generator set industry as early as 1991. In January 2003, they commenced research and development of high-power engines. In 2006, they invested in establishing a manufacturing base in Yongchuan, Chongqing, and in 2021, the company was officially renamed Chongqing Pangu Power Technology Co., Ltd. The company has pioneered numerous industry firsts. As early as 2012, Pangu independently designed and manufactured China's first V20 high-speed diesel engine with a cylinder bore of 170mm, becoming the third company globally capable of researching and manufacturing V20-cylinder high-power high-speed engines. This technological breakthrough positions the Pangu Diesel Generator as a competitive contender in the high-power segment alongside international brands.
The core technical characteristics of Pangu series engines include low power output per liter, high reliability, low exhaust temperature, low fuel consumption, clean emissions, and low operation and maintenance costs. Through collaboration with global mainstream engine manufacturers, shared supply chains, and efficient lean manufacturing processes, the Pangu Industrial Genset achieves an excellent balance between performance and economy. The engines adopt a V-type multi-cylinder layout with turbocharging and intercooling systems, and feature direct injection fuel systems to ensure complete combustion and smooth power delivery.
