260KW Chongqing cummins generator set
Product Advantages
Durability:
The 260KW Chongqing Cummins generator set features a high-strength integrated design for the engine block and cylinder head, effectively reducing operational failures. Its robust structure ensures long-term continuous operation, delivering stable and reliable quality with an excellent market reputation.
High Reliability:
Equipped with Cummins’ advanced PT fuel system, it includes a unique overspeed protection feature. The low-pressure fuel supply design reduces fuel lines and simplifies system structure, significantly lowering the probability of faults. Based on Cummins’ mature overall power system design philosophy, the equipment maintains stable operation even under complex working conditions, demonstrating outstanding reliability.
Excellent Performance:
It adopts an efficient Cummins exhaust gas turbocharger for better air intake and higher combustion efficiency. Coupled with pressure-type pulse exhaust technology, it fully recovers exhaust energy to enhance combustion efficiency. Even under low-load conditions, it maintains high output, effectively reducing specific fuel consumption.
The generator set delivers stable power output, ample torque, strong power, and fast transient response, with excellent load adaptability and torque reserve performance.
Easy Maintenance:
The overall modular structure design integrates components in blocks with a compact layout. Parts are easy to disassemble and reuse. With standardized design across the platform, spare parts compatibility is high, maintenance costs are low, and repair efficiency is improved, significantly reducing downtime.
Strong Adaptability:
The generator set provides stable power output and fuel efficiency, adapting to various complex operating conditions. It tolerates a wide range of fuel qualities and demonstrates excellent environmental adaptability, suitable for high-temperature, high-humidity, high-altitude, dusty, and other challenging application scenarios.
