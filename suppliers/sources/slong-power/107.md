what is tri fuel ?
| | |
Gasoline: This is the most common fuel source for generators. Gasoline is widely available and easy to store. It is the default fuel for many generators, especially in portable and emergency backup applications.
Propane: Also known as liquefied petroleum gas (LPG), propane is a versatile and clean-burning fuel. Propane tanks are easily transportable, making them a convenient option for various applications.
Natural Gas: Natural gas is a common utility-supplied fuel. It is often more readily available in urban and suburban areas. Generators that can run on natural gas are often connected directly to a natural gas line.
