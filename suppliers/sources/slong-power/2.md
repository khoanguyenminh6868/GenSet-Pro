Company Profile
Founded in 2007, YANCHENG SLONG MACHINERY & ELECTRIC CO., LTD. is situated in Changzhou Hi-Tech Industrial Park, Dafeng District of Yancheng City, Jiangsu Province, China. As a professional manufacturer, we specialize in gas and gasoline generators, integrating the entire industrial chain from research and development, production and sales to after-sales service.
With the support of a team consisting of experienced engineers and skilled technical personnel, SLONG is dedicated to delivering high-performance, reliable and innovative power solutions to both domestic and global markets. Our well-established sales and service network ensures smooth and all-round support, effectively safeguarding customer satisfaction at every stage of cooperation.
Guided by our core philosophy – "Focus on segmented markets with continuous innovation" – SLONG has developed a comprehensive product portfolio tailored to meet the diverse needs of residential and commercial users. Our main products include: Gasoline & LPG Dual Fuel Generators (3-20kW), LPG & Natural Gas Generators (5-20kW), Natural Gas Generators (5-20kW), Triple Fuel Generators (Gasoline, LPG & Natural Gas) (5-20kW), and Super Silent Residential Standby Generators.
Renowned for their excellent durability, low noise operation, high fuel efficiency and cost-effectiveness, our products have become a trusted choice for backup power in various application scenarios. Looking forward, SLONG will continue to focus on technological innovation and the advancement of energy solutions, striving to empower end-users and promote the common development of our global partners.
