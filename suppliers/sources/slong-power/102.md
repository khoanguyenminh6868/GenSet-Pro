Air Cooling Low Noise CNG NG 18KW Natural Gas Generator For Home Standby
This silent tri-fuel generator is specially engineered for residential use, supporting natural gas, LPG and gasoline to meet daily household electricity needs and provide reliable backup power during unexpected power outages.
Adopting an optimized four-stroke silent engine and professional fuel adaptive system, the device realizes low-noise operation without disturbing families and neighbors. It converts combustion energy into stable AC power via an alternator. The built-in pressure regulator adapts to three fuels automatically: gaseous fuel features eco-friendly and cost-efficient performance for long-time daily use, while gasoline delivers higher output for heavy-load household equipment.
Equipped with an intelligent CO detection and auto-shutdown safety system, this generator effectively prevents potential safety hazards. With simple operation, flexible fuel switching and compact structure, it is a safe, quiet and economical power backup solution for modern homes.
