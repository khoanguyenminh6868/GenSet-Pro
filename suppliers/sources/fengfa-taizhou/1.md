About Us
Who We Are
Taizhou Fengfa Power Equipment Co., Ltd. is a well-established company in the production of diesel generator sets in China. Feng Fa Power mainly produces and sells seven types of diesel generators: general type, automatic type, multi-machine parallel type, low noise type, mobile power station type, unmanned type, and remote monitoring type. Fengfa's products are widely used in various fields such as communication, factories, mines, ports, highways, ships, railways, airports, power plants, hotels, and military forces, serving as an ideal emergency power supply device and gaining trust and support from all walks of life. It has established a modern standard diesel generator set testing center and a well-stocked spare parts to ensure the use of customer products for more than 20 years.

APPLICATION
Product Type
Company has 25 years of experience with providing wide area of specialty services works listed below.
Open Genset
Open-frame diesel generator sets are novel in design and reasonable in structure. Open-frame diesel generator sets are mainly composed of four parts: diesel engine,alternator, control system and bottom frame. The diesel generator chassis adopts steel plate bending or channel beam welding to form a frame structure, which is of high strength and good rigidity, and can be customized to the size of the fuel tank. Control system cabinet can also be customized
Silent Genset
The main features of the silent generator set are low noise and strong mobility. Can adapt to different environments and weather. It has the following advantages: first, it can save the cost of building a machine room; second, it is the first choice for field operations; third, the silent box-type generator set is more suitable for leasing.
Marine Genset
Fengfa Power produces marine generator sets with a power range of 37.5KVA-1250KVA 30kW-1000kW 50Hz/1500RPM
Cummins diesel engines can be equipped with Marathon alternators, Stamford alternators, and other China brand altenator, depending on customer requirements.
Diesel Engine International Air Pollution Prevention Certificate
CCS, BV ship inspection
Control System
The control system is the brain of the diesel generator set and plays an extremely important role. It commands the generator set to start, stop, and monitor the generator set. Fengfa can provide customers with parallel systems and remote monitoring systems ect.. The controller brands include Smartgen, Deepsea, Com Ap, etc., which can be customized according to customer requirements.
