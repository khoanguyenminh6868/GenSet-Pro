Our History Jiangsu Mingbang Intelligent Technology Co., Ltd (MBKL) has been focusing on independently developing and producing different types of diesel generator sets, brushless generators, generator sets control panel system etc, for over 30 years. With great reputation, high quality and competitive price. In the past 30 years, our factory experienced a rapid development. We have exported our gensets to more than 30 countries. Moreover, In China we are the designated supplier of CRCC (China Railway Construction Corporation Limited), Bank of China, China Weapon Industry Corporation and many other government units. Additionally, MBKL factory built 5 branches companies in China: Changsha MBKL Mechanical Electrical Co., Ltd; HONGKONG Stamford Company Limited; Wuhan Mingbang Mechanical Electrical Co., Ltd; Shengyang Mingbang Technology Co., Ltd.
Our Factory Jiangsu Mingbang Intelligent Technology Co.,Ltd (MBKL) factory occupies an area of 9042 square meters, located in Yangzhou City, Jiangsu Province, which is the central part of manufacturing industrial zone in Southeast China near Shanghai. MBKL as a machinery factory mainly manufactures alternators, and installing diesel generator sets. MBKL is also an integrated high-tech enterprise involved the areas of research $ development, production,and sales. Moreover, we also manufacture OEM generators powered by various brands with foreign brands as Cummins, Perkins, Deutz, Volvo, MTU, Doosan, and Chinese local brands Shangchai ,Yuchai ,Weichai,Jichai engines with Stamford, Marathon, Neroy - Somer , or our own manufactured alternators.
Our Product MBKL diesel generator sets range from 2.5kVA to 3500kVA, 50Hz or 60Hz frequency, can be open type gensets, silent type gensets, trailer type gensets or container type gensets with the features of light weight, small size, delicate appearance, strong reliability. MBKL regards sciences and technology first, quality first, customer first, openness basing on sincerity’ as the management aims. In addition, we have a complete test process, strictly according to GB/T2820-1997 standard production, the MB series three-phase ac brushless synchronous alternators which are independent developed, designed, manufactured by our company, have met a criterion of IEC (International Electric Technical Commission), GB755 standard and so on.
With convincing quality, our products endue generator made in China with a new implication of European quality with competitive price. Additionally, our electric performance is definitely close to the international level!
