Deutz (Dalian) Engine Co., Ltd. is located in Dalian, China and it is a subsidiary of German engine manufacturer Deutz AG. and operates as a joint venture between Deutz AG and Chinese partners. The company combines Deutz’s advanced engine technology and expertise with local manufacturing capabilities to produce engines specifically designed for the Chinese market. Deutz engines are known for their high quality, performance, and reliability, and are widely used in various industries and applications. mainly includes water cooled engines BFM3, BF4M, BF6M series.It enjoy great popularity all over the world. There are also air cooled Deutz engines such as F2L912, F3L912, F4L912, F4L913, F6L912, F6L913.
