Cummins introduction
Cummins is a leading global engine manufacturer that designs, manufactures, distributes, and services engines and related technologies, including fuel systems, control systems, air handling, filtration, exhaust treatment, and electrical systems. Headquartered in Columbus, Indiana, USA, Cummins serves customers through 550 distribution facilities and over 5,000 dealer locations in more than 160 countries and regions. For the generator application engines, Cummins operates two major factories in China: Dongfeng Cummins Engine Co., Ltd. (DCEC) and Chongqing Cummins Engine Co., Ltd. (CCEC).
Dongfeng Cummins Engine Co., Ltd. (DCEC):
DCEC, established in 1996, is a joint venture between Cummins and Dongfeng Motor Corporation. Located in Xiangyang, Hubei Province, DCEC is one of the largest engine manufacturers in China. DCEC’s product range includes engines from the 4B3.9, 6B5.9, B6.7, 6C8.3, 6L8.9, 6L9.5, and 6Z13 QSZ13 series from 20KW to 463KW.
Chongqing Cummins Engine Co., Ltd. (CCEC):
CCEC, founded in 1995, is a joint venture between Cummins and Chongqing Machinery and Electric Co., Ltd. Located in Chongqing, CCEC is a leading manufacturer of high horsepower engines for heavy-duty applications.It includes engines from N855, K19, QSK19, K38, K50 series from 240KW to 1760KW.
The advantages of Cummins generator
1. Basic Design Features
The diesel generator set boasts a durable cylinder block design with low vibration and noise. Its four-stroke engine provides smooth operation and high efficiency. Replaceable wet cylinder liners offer long life and easy maintenance. Two cylinders per head, four valves per cylinder, provide ample air intake, and forced water cooling minimizes heat radiation for superior performance.
2. Fuel System
The Cummins patented PT fuel system features a unique overspeed protection device. Low-pressure fuel lines minimize piping, reduce failure rates, and enhance reliability. High-pressure injection ensures complete combustion. Fuel supply and return check valves ensure safe and reliable operation.
3. Air Intake System
The Cummins diesel generator set is equipped with a dry-type air filter and air resistance indicator. It uses an exhaust turbocharger for ample air intake and guaranteed performance.
4. Exhaust System
Cummins diesel generator sets use a pulsed dry exhaust system, effectively utilizing exhaust gas energy and maximizing engine performance.
5. Cooling System
Cummins diesel generator sets utilize a geared centrifugal water pump for forced water cooling. The high-flow water channel design provides excellent cooling, effectively reducing heat radiation and noise. A unique spin-on water filter prevents rust and corrosion, controls acidity, and removes impurities.
6. Lubrication System
A variable-flow oil pump with a main oil channel signal line adjusts pumping volume based on main oil channel oil pressure, optimizing the amount of oil entering the engine. Low oil pressure (241-345 kPa) effectively reduces pumping power loss, improves power, and enhances engine economy.
7. Ultra-Low Fuel Consumption
Utilizing the Cummins XPI ultra-high-pressure common rail fuel injection system and CTT high-flow turbocharger, combined with Cummins power cylinder design and electronic control systems, fuel consumption is significantly reduced, ensuring excellent fuel economy across a wide range of engine operating conditions and applications.
8. Outstanding Reliability
Utilizing engineering techniques and analytical tools, and supported by powerful sensors and electronic control systems, the engine boasts enhanced high-altitude, low-temperature, and high-load continuous operation capabilities. It can operate smoothly in temperatures ranging from -40°C to 60°C and at altitudes of 5,200 meters, while delivering full load without compromising output.
