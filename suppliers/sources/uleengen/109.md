Perkins: Perkins engine co.,ltd is a leading global engine manufacturer that produce high-quality diesel engines. Founded in 1932, Perkins has a rich history marked by continuous research, development, and innovation. Over the decades, Perkins has established itself as one of the world’s premier engine manufacturers. Perkins headquartered in the UK and it has manufacturing base in China, UK, USA and India. producing 400000 units per year;
So far, it has provided 15 million generator sets with power ranges from 4kw to 1940KW for the world; The company has set up two parts distribution centers in Manchester, UK and Singapore, and more than 3500 service outlets around the world to provide uninterrupted services to customers all over the world. As a world-famous manufacturer of Rolls Royce, Perkins is committed to product quality, environment and economy. Strictly implement ISO9001 and ISO14001 standards. The products have the characteristics of high emission standard, high economy, high stability and high reliability.
In 2008, Perkins opened their factory in Wuxi to produce a range of engines, including the 403, 404, 1104, and 1106 series. These engines are exported worldwide. Perkins diesel generators are widely used in the Telecom, Data center, Military, Mining industry, Factory, Construction and Infrastructure
