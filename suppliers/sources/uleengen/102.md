Benefits & Features
The main application of generator set 1000KVA-3750KVA
| |
Large data centers:
Data centers have high requirements for power supply continuity and quality. 1-2 MW generator sets are typically used to provide backup power for the entire data cente, ensuring uninterrupted operation of critical loads such as servers and cooling systems.
Hospitals:
These power generator sets ensure the reliable power supply to operating rooms, ICUs , emergency rooms, medical imaging equipment (such as CT and MRI), as well as lighting and ventilation systems, directly impacting the patients’ safety.
Airports and transportation hubs:
The generator sets maintain the operation of basic terminal lighting, flight information display systems, security equipment, communications systems, and critical equipment in air traffic control towers, preventing disruptions and safety incidents.
Large commercial buildings and shopping malls:
The Cummins Perkins MTU generator sets provide backup power for emergency lighting, fire protection systems, elevators, surveillance and security systems, and for key tenants (such as data center computer rooms and cold storage facilities in large supermarkets), ensuring personnel safety and minimizing property damage.
Factories and Production Lines:
Gensets providing backup power to entire factories or critical production lines, such as semiconductor manufacturing plants, chemical plants, automobile manufacturers, and food processing plants. Once production processes are interrupted, restarting them is extremely costly.
Mines and Quarries:
In remote locations or mining areas with unstable power grids, generator sets may serve as primary power sources or operate in parallel with the grid to power mining equipment, crushers, conveyor belts, lighting, and camp life.
Major Engineering Projects:
During the initial construction phase of large-scale infrastructure projects such as bridges, tunnels, and subways, construction sites are often not connected to the municipal power grid, requiring high-power Cummins/Perkins/MTU generator sets as temporary main power sources to power various heavy machinery and equipment.
Peak shaving and grid-connected power generation:
During periods of grid power shortage (such as peak summer demand), the power generator sets can be activated and connected to the grid, supplying power to the grid, thus reducing peak loads. They can also be used to supplement renewable energy sources (such as wind and solar) to ensure stable power supply during calm or cloudy days.
Oil and gas field development:
During the exploration and development of oil and gas fields, the Perkins generator sets provide power for drilling platforms, fracking operations, production equipment, and worker living quarters.
Large-scale events:
The generator sets provide primary or backup power for temporary large-scale events such as concerts, sporting events, and exhibitions, meeting the high-energy demands of stage lighting, sound systems, large screens, and catering areas.
