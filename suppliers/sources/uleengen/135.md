FAW Diesel Generator
Fawde diesel generator with power range from 15KVA to 400KVA
Powered by FAW brand new diesel engine. water cooled, electric start.
Brand new brushless self exciated single bearing alternator.
Heavy-duty steel base frame with 8-24 hours operation fuel tank.
Low noise level 68-75dBA at 7 meters.
Vibration damper between engine/alternator and base frame
Main line circuit breaker/Automatic transfer switch.
Type: Silent type, Open type, mobile trailer type
Voltage: 220/127V, 380/220V, 400/230V, 415/240V, 440/254V, 460/266V, 480/277V…
Phase: 3 Phase, Single Phase
Control panel type: manual start, auto start, remote start, synchronization panel.
