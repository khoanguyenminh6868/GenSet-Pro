Open type 10-50kva diesel generator
Open type generator has no silent canopy, accordingly shipping size is much smaller, which is more cost-effective. and it is better for cooling and ventilation. Suitable for hot areas where enclosed type generators might struggle with heat dissipation.
Silent type 10-50kva diesel generator
silent diesel generators with silent canopy, which the noise level can be 70-75DB at 7 meters. It is ideal for urban areas, hospitals, schools, offices, and residential use.
