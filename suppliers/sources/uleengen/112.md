MTU
As a supplier of engines for land, water, and rail propulsion systems, as well as power generation equipment, MTU enjoys a global reputation for its leading technology, highly reliable products, and first-class after-sales service. MTU is renowned for its high output power, high efficiency, compact size, and durability. However, high prices have historically limited MTU’s products to key applications such as warships, tanks, nuclear power plants, transport ships, etc. MTU Yuchai Power Co. Ltd. was founded in February 2017 – a joint venture between MTU and Guangxi Yuchai Machinery Company Ltd. MTU is a wholly owned subsidiary af Rolls-Royce Power Systems, is one of the most famous off-road high-power engine manufacturers, which has more than 100 years of professional knowledge and experience in manufacturing large engines. The quality and performance of MTU products are world-class. Guangxi Yuchai Machinery Company Ltd is a coresubsidiary of Guangxi Yuchai Machinery Group with 67 years of history, which is one of China’s top 500 enterprises, is the largest independent diesel engine manufacturer and one of internal combustion engine production bases with most complete product portfolio in China. It produces 4000 series MTU engine from 1205KW to 2509KW.
