Yuchai is a prominent Chinese manufacturer of diesel engines since 1951. They offer a wide range of products suitable for various applications, including standby, prime, and continuous power generation. Yuchai as one of the top three diesel engine brands in China, has large range of power from 20kVA to 3750kva with reliable quality include YC4D, YC4A, YC6A, YC6MK, YC6MJ,YC6K, YC6T, YC6TC, YC6TD, YC6TH, YC6C, YC12VTD, YC12VC, YC16VTD, YC16VC, YC20VTD series.
Yuchai engine Design advantages:
(1) Adopting the world’s advanced 4-valve structure and mid-mounted fuel injector, the oil and gas are more fully mixed.
(2) Adopt international advanced electronic injection technology, high injection pressure, full combustion, high power density and light weight.
(3) The mature supercharging and intercooling technology ensures sufficient air intake under various load conditions and wide economic fuel consumption area.
(4) Reticulated rib structure cylinder block, alloy forged crankshaft, vermicular graphite cast iron cylinder head to ensure product quality.
(5) The Yuchai engine is calibrated for plateau and alpine regions, and is suitable for use in high-altitude areas.
(6) Yuchai’s entire series of engines purchased EFI technology, which is easy to start the engine, has a good response time for sudden addition and sudden reduction, and meets the national regulation T3 emission.
(7) Plate type oil cooler, high heat exchange efficiency, the engine is not easy to be high temperature.
(8) Cast oil pan, vermicular graphite cast iron cylinder head, alloy cylinder block, prolong engine life.
(9) Integral forged steel crankshaft, light weight and high strength
(10) There is only one exhaust main pipe, which is convenient for layout and installation, especially for indoor space.
