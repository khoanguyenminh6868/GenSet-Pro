Weichai is one of the top diesel engine manufacturers in Weifang, China since 1946, during the past years Weichai consistently striving to enhance their engine performance. They have made significant advancements in engine efficiency. Initially, Weichai collaborated with Deutz, and more recently, they acquired Baudouin. This merging of technologies has undoubtedly propelled Weichai engines to a new level of excellence. Product range from 20kw to 3000KW include WP2.3D, WP4.1D, WP6D, WP10D, WP12D, WP13D, 6M33, 8M33, 12M33, 12M55, 16M55, 20M33 series.
The WP series is an advanced engine jointly developed by Weichai and Bosch of Germany. Utilizing electronically controlled high-pressure common rail technology, it is currently the mainstay and high-performance representative of Weichai’s power generation products.
Technical Features:
Electronic high-pressure common rail : Precise fuel injection, more complete combustion, low fuel consumption, and excellent emissions.
Four-valve technology : High intake efficiency, cleaner exhaust, and high power density.
Turbocharging and intercooling : Universally utilizes turbocharging and intercooling technology to increase power and torque.
Intelligent Control : Perfectly matched with the digital intelligent control system, delivering fast response.
Main Power Range: Covering 50kW to 1800kW.
WP2.3 and WP4.1: Suitable for small and medium power applications, suitable for lighthouses, small units, backup power supplies, and more.
WP6, WP10, WP12, and WP13: These generators are used in the medium-to-high power range and are the absolute mainstay for backup and primary power supply in data centers, hospitals, factories, ports, and other applications, enjoying a significant market share.
Baudouin M26 M33 series engine as main products of moteurs Baudouin company, is famous for its advantages worldwide. as reliable performance and long service life, low maintenance costs, the overhaul period is near 32,000 hours.
