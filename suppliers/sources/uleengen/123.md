SDEC is one of the top three diesel engine manufacturers in China, Shanghai New Power Automotive Technology Co., Ltd. (previously known as Shanghai Diesel Engine Co., Ltd.), was founded in 1947 and is now affiliated with SAIC Motor Corporation Limited (SAIC Motor). In 1993, it was restructured into a state-owned holding company.and it is one of the brands we highly recommend. As a state-owned enterprise with decades of experience in engine manufacturing, SDEC produces engines comparable to Cummins’ QSB, and 8.3 and 8.9 L series models. Their engines are known for their reliable quality, good performance, and competitive pricing.
