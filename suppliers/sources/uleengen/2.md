ABOUT US
QUALITY CHANGES THE WORLD
Uleen generator, who is devoted to the R&D and manufacture of the intelligent generator sets since its establishment. and it is a National Torch Plan Hi-tech enterprise providing customers with technical consultation, training, installation and maintenance. The marketing center is located in Shandong, China.
Uleen generator production are submit to GB/T2820-97 and CE certification, and quality certified by ISO9001 & ISO14001 Management System to guarantee high quality products and service. We have 20 more patents and we maintain long term strategic friendship cooperation with the famous engine and alternator such as Cummins, Perkins, Doosan, Lovol, Weichai, Yuchai, SDEC , Deutz, Baudouin, Leroy Somer, Stamford, Mecc alte and Marathon, etc. We have wide products ranges from 5kva to 2000kva for the diesel generator set; And power from 25kva to 2500kva for gas generator set; soundproof/weatherproof type, containerized type; trailer type. Regarding to the electrical control systems, it covers Auto start control panel, Synchronization panels, ATS etc. With controller such as Deepsea, Smartgen, ComAp, Deif, Harsen, etc.
With the great support of our professional overseas partners, Uleen generators are distributed to markets all over the world, including the Asia, Middle East, Africa, South America, etc.
With the concept of “Your Smart Solution”, we will strengthen cooperation and communication with domestic and overseas customers, and make effort together to develop the electric power industry of all over the world.
