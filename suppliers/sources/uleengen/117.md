Benefits & Features
Main applications of gas generators
Distributed energy stations:
The gas generator provide electricity and heating(CHP) for industrial parks, commercial building, hospitals, data centers, and more.
Backup power:
Provide emergency power in the event of a main grid failure in locations requiring high power continuity, such as hospitals, financial centers, and communication base stations.
Grid peak shaving:
Compensate for the intermittent nature of renewable energy (such as wind power and solar energy), enabling rapid startup during peak demand periods to balance grid load.
Oil and gas field associated gas utilization:
The oilfield associated gas generator directly utilize associated gas generated during oil and gas field extraction to generate electricity, avoiding the waste and pollution caused by gas venting.
Landfill gas/biogas power generation:
After treatment, this gas can also be used as fuel for gas-fired generators, realizing waste resource utilization.
