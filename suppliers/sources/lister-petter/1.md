Lister Petter Engine Company LTD launches a new range of diesel engines
Earth Max Series.
Lister Petter proudly introduces a new generation of diesel engines. Engineered to meet the demands of modern power generation while staying true to a heritage built on strength, reliability and engineering excellence. Developed for global applications, this new engine range represents a decisive step forward in performance, efficiency and scalability, delivering dependable power wherever it is needed most.
Engineered for fixed-speed 1500/1800 rpm applications where downtime is not an option, Earth Max Series delivers uncompromising performance from 1020 kW to 2220 kW, built to anchor large-scale standby and prime operations.
From the 20-cylinder LP2041EG models to the flagship LP1686EG variants exceeding 2 MW net prime output, this is a platform designed for serious power integration, supporting generator installations up to 2750 kVA.
At its core:
• Advanced high-pressure Common Rail injection
• High efficiency turbocharged aftercooled architecture
• ISO 3046 / ISO 8528 certified performance
• Optimised fuel efficiency across the load curve
• Heavy-duty cooling and lubrication systems engineered for continuous operation
