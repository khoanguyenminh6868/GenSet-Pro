Product Description:
This generator is powered by a Cummins water-cooling diesel engine, coupled with an original Stamford alternator. It offers the option of a copy of the Stamford alternator, a Leroy Somer alternator, and a Marathon alternator. Additionally, it has a standard CHINT or DELIXI circuit breaker, and an ABB breaker as an optional choice.
It is equipped with a steel base fuel tank with anti-vibration mounting. A built-in fuel tank lasting 8-10 hours ensures daily use, a 9dB muffler exhaust system provides a quieter environment, and a free-maintenance battery is included with the user's manual. For convenience, it is also equipped with flexible fuel lines, oil, fuel & water drain cocks.
The digital control module consists of Deepsea, ComAp, Smartgen, and Harsen. Lastly, the packaging method includes both shrink wrap and plywood cases.
