Introduction
Tecgen brand diesel generating set ranging from 8kVA to 3000kVA with various famous engine brand like Cummins, Perkins, Deutz, Ricardo.
Tecgen, which aims to deliver comprehensive power solutions to the people through sustainable improvement and innovation, designs high quality diesel generator sets, alternators, that exceed the expectations of the customers.
Tecgen adopts internationally and domestically reputed high-quality engines and alternators including CUMMINS, PERKINS, Deutz, SDEC, WEICHAI, YUCHAI, RICARDO ,FAW, Yangdong, Stamford, Leroy Somer, Mecc Alte and Marathon for our industrial diesel generator sets, with power range from 8kVA to 3000kVA and various types of design. We supply fast and effective after-sales technical support and maintenance services in more than 50 countries,
Tecgen has experienced 10 years of operation, continuous development and growth ,thanks to its solid experience and engineering know-how, Tecgen has been providing power solutions that can efficiently operate even under the most challenging conditions for projects such as constructions, shopping centers, hotels, residential buildings, supermarkets, sport centers, mining facilities, hospitals, educational institutions and industrial plants all around the world.
