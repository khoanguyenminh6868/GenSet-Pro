Z SERIES MEDIUM & LOW SPPED GENERATORS
Z SERIES MEDIUM & LOW SPPED GENERATORS, max. capacityof 12,500 kVA, are manufactured with adoption of stator SWSpatented technology, rotor RIS patented technology and anti-coronainsulation, and suitable for different medium-low speed engines.
The design according to the continuous operation conditions,provides efficient and integrated design solutions, and also can becustomized to customer requirements.The generators are verypowerful and manufactured to meet the most severe application andenvironmental requirements, and suitable for continuous or standbypower supply to meet the max. demand for power plant black start,distributed new energy power generation, ship power generation,mining main power supply, etc.
