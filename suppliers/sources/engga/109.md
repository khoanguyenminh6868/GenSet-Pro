N SERIES LV GENERATORS
SERIES LV GENERATORS, capacity of 6 to 3,750kVA, , are manufactured with adaption of patented technologies of stator (SWS) and rotor (RIS), and common applied in the fields of industry, marine, telecommunications, oil mining, construction and other continuous or standby power applications. N-series alternators are equipped with standard SAE spec, suitable for different types of engines.N series generators were tested with reliable performance and durable with long service life and more than 200,000 units provided power for different occasions.
