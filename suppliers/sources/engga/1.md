Brand Heritage
Founded in 1992, we have been deeply engaged in the generator
industry for more than 30 years, with over 200,000 units delivered
worldwide, covering key global application scenarios. Vision: To revitalize China’s industrial sector and become a global
leader in the generator and drive system market.
Robust R&D
We maintain substantial and continuous R&D investment, and have established an industry-university-research system in collaboration with multiple universities. With more than 100 proprietary technologies, we have achieved breakthroughs in core processes including insulation and heat dissipation, enabling products to adapt to high-altitude, high-protection and other diverse application scenarios.
Intelligent Manufacturing
We implement an EPS lean production system and intelligent production lines, with an annual capacity of over 30,000 units. Equipped with 8 major testing platforms, we carry out full-process quality control and 100% load testing. The company has been awarded authoritative certifications including "Specialized, Refined, Differential and Innovative Small and Medium-sized Enterprise" and "High-tech Enterprise".
