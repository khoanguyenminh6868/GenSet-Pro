ABOUT LEES
One Mission. One Standard. 10+ Decades Of Power Engineering.

WUXI LEES POWER COMPANY LIMITED
LEES is a global innovative application company committed to diesel power generation, gas power generation, photovoltaic power generation, energy storage system research and development, manufacturing, sales and service. In the past decades, we have provided related products and services for more than 80 countries in five continents, and are committed to delivering energy solution products to more application fields. We have always taken green energy as our own responsibility, committed to improving the development and application of new energy power generation technology, and constantly pursued advanced science and technology and innovative business management mode. To provide customers with value-added green energy solutions.
Since its establishment, the company has paid special attention to brand building and technology precipitation, and created its own "LEES" brand, which has been recognized by the market, trusted by partners and praised by customers. After years of development, the company has won the honors of "high-tech Enterprise", "Jiangsu Province Specialized and Special New Enterprise", "Jiangsu Gazelle Enterprise", "Wuxi High Performance Diesel Generator Group Engineering Technology Research Center", and has passed ISO9001, ISO14001, ISO45001 system certification.
LEES has a team of professional and technical strength, including mechanical engineers, electrical engineers, pre-sales engineers, after-sales engineers, test engineers, etc., and has reached school-enterprise cooperation with Jiangnan University.
For many years, LEES has been focusing on innovation of energy saving products. In the future, LEES will actively grasp the direction of low-carbon economy and green development. and will build a comprehensive energy solution supplier with core competitiveness and influence based on the concept of products, deep market
cultivation and service.
