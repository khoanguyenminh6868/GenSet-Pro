Tellhow Power Technology Co., Ltd. is a wholly-owned subsidiary of Tellhow Sci-Tech
Co., Ltd. with a registered capital of RMB 200 million and
international standard Tellhow Xiaolan industrial production base.
The company is committed to the application of information technology, the introduction of international advanced technology, Tsinghua high-tech technology into the Sanbo Power 50 years of design and manufacturing technology, and actively develop independent intellectual property rights technology products.
