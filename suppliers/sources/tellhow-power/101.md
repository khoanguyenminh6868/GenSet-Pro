Features and Benefits
The unit is equipped with a world-class premium engine and a high-efficiency generator, combined with control systems of various designs to ensure optimal operation. It delivers strong power and stable performance.
With a pure sine wave output, it provides stable voltage and frequency, offering strong capability to handle nonlinear loads.
Power loss during transmission is minimized, making it suitable for long-distance power delivery.
The unit is widely applied in data centers, computing power centers, metallurgy, paper manufacturing, coal, gold and copper mines, islands, and long-distance power stations, as well as other large-capacity load scenarios.

FAQ
Are you a manufacturer or a trading company?
We are a manufacturer. Tellhow owns multiple generator set production bases and provides full-cycle solutions, including pre-sales support, R&D and engineering, production and assembly, as well as after-sales installation and services.
Can you customize generator sets according to specific requirements?
Yes. We provide fully customized solutions, from open-type to soundproof canopy and containerized gensets. Voltage options include 400V, 480V, 10.5kV, and 13.8kV. Functions can be configured for single-unit operation, automatic mains failure (AMF), multiple-unit parallel operation, or grid connection.
Which engine and alternator brands do you supply?
We integrate leading global brands such as MTU, Cummins, Perkins, Volvo, Yuchai, Weichai, and Baudouin. Alternators include Leroy Somer, Stamford, Marathon, and Tellhow.
How do you ensure product quality?
All gensets are ISO9001 certified, component-inspected, and assembled under strict process control. Every unit is tested to national standards before delivery.
Can you supply spare parts in the long term?
Yes. As an OEM partner of major engine and alternator brands, we ensure long-term availability of genuine spare parts for reliable operation.
Do you provide installation and commissioning services?
Yes. Our professional team of over 50 engineers delivers on-site installation, commissioning, and technical support to ensure smooth operation.
