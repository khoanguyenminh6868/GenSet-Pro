Industrial Generator, 900kW | Diesel | 30.6L
| Built for the Toughest Demands. Generac’s 30.6 L 900 kW & 1000 kW industrial diesel generators deliver uncompromising power, performance, and durability. As the workhorse of the industry, this generator is engineered to provide the reliability and longevity required in the harshest environments. Designed and built within Generac’s vertically integrated facilities, every component—engine, alternator, controls, and enclosure—is manufactured to meet the highest standards of quality and performance.
Features & Benefits
Proven 30.6 L Perkins Engine: Turbocharged and intercooled 8-cylinder diesel engine delivers exceptional load response, efficiency, and durability for mission-critical applications.
DSE G8601 Controller: Advanced digital controller with high-resolution display, integrated PLC functionality, Modbus® communication, and on-screen system diagnostics for intuitive operation.
Modular Power System (MPS) Paralleling: Easily scale capacity and redundancy across multiple units with Generac’s MPS architecture, offering up to 99.999% reliability and simplified system expansion.
Industrial Alternator: Brushless, permanent-magnet excitation alternator with Class H insulation and <5% THD ensures clean, stable power delivery to sensitive systems.
Engineered Enclosures: Generac-built steel and aluminum enclosures combine sound attenuation, corrosion resistance, and modular service access for all-weather reliability.
Performance
Prime Power: 810 kW
Cooling System: Closed-Loop Pressurized with 50/50 Ethylene Glycol for reliability in all climates and reduced maintenance.
Fuel Type: Ultra-Low Sulfur Diesel (ASTM Specification) for a cleaner operation, regulatory compliance and equipment protection.
Sound Levels/Enclosures: Open and Weather-Protected through Level 2 Sound-Attenuated Enclosures for stringent noise requirments.
Construction: Steel or aluminum with RhinoCoat™ powder-coated finish protects against rust, UV and weather for long-term durability
Product Snapshot
Power Nodes: 900 kW | 1000 kW
Ideal For: Data centers, hospitals, industrial facilities, and utilities requiring high-output standby or prime power solutions.
Applications: Standby and modular parallel systems where uptime, scalability, and long service life are essential.
Footprint: Approx. 210–329 in L × 102–106 in W × 91–187 in H (depending on enclosure and tank configuration)
Operating Temperature: Up to 122 °F (50 °C) before derate
Certifications: EPA Certified Stationary Emergency, UL2200, NFPA 110 Compliant
