ABOUT US A
Fujian Agestar Electrical Machinery Co.,Ltd (Agestar Power) was established in 2014. Before that it is a lamination manufacturer, located in Fujian Province, the southeast China. Step by step, we had been developing into a factory that professional in design and manufacture alternator, electric motor, diesel generating set and water pump. With a rich experience in this filed, we are successfully design, manufacturing and exporting our goods to many countries. We are proud to be associated with a bank of loyal and happy customers. All this has come after rugged product testing of a quality product produced by us, after honoring our commitments of timely deliveries and round the clock after sales service support given to our esteemed customers. We invest continuously to improve our management, keep adopting international latest advanced technology so that our products will always match international standard for excellent performances and reliability. With great enthusiasm and persistence efforts and your great support, Agestar company will moving toward more and more successfully.
