Diesel Generators 3000rpm
Powered by air and water cooled engines by leading manufacturers such as Kubota, Yanmar and Kohler/Lombardini. Providing quiet and efficient power, these versatile generators are perfect for the Hire market, construction sites, event power and all other applications where mains power is not available. Diesel Generators 1500rpm
Designed to offer the ultimate in versatile silent power. EU Stage V emission compliant Kubota, Yanmar and Perkins engines provide clean and efficient energy. Mecc Alte brushless alternators have enhanced “TOTAL” winding protection as standard to ensure maximum resistance to severe environmental conditions. Panel mounted Deep Sea Electronics control systems ensure user friendly operation as well as data monitoring and event logging functions with optional telemetry. Suited to any application where mains power is unavailable or potentially unreliable (auto-start / standby). Non-emission compliant EU Stage IIIA range units also available specifically for standby use.
