                                              POWER DEFINITION

Prime Power (PRP) : Applicable for supplying power to varying electrical load for unlimited hours in accordance ISO

8528-1 under the agreed operating conditions with the maintenance intervals and procedures being carried out as

prescribed by the manufacturers.

Standby Power : An approximate overload of 10% is allowed for 1 hour in 12. This is typically used for regulation

purposes and small unexpected loading.
