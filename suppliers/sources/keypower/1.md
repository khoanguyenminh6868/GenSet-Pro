Who We Are
Fuzhou GFF KEYPOWER Equipment Co., Ltd. originating in 1998, owns the independent brand KEYPOWER. Its main products include air-cooled diesel generator sets, multi-cylinder water-cooled diesel generator sets, load banks, diesel water pumps and others. The company is located at No. 3-1, Dongling Rd, Ganzhe Street, Minhou County, Fuzhou, Fujian, China. The park covers an area of 66,000 square meters, with a diesel generator set assembly workshop of 8,000 square meters and an annual production and sales volume of 3,000-5,000 units. The company has more than ten years of rich experience in the production and R&D of diesel generator sets. It has a team of more than 100 highly qualified employees and uses modern processing equipment and advanced design concepts to create power generation products with trouble-free operation . The company has obtained multiple independent patent certificates. It has passed the ISO9001:2008 quality management system certification, and its products have obtained CE certificates. Rigorous production process control, strict product inspection procedures, and demanding procurement of raw materials and components ensure the reliable quality and stable performance of the products. High-quality products and services have been widely recognized by customers at home and abroad, agency agreements have been signed with partners in multiple countries. More than 80% of products are exported under the KEYPOWER brand. Within the industry, KEYPOWER is gradually building its reputation as a trusted premium Chinese electromechanical brand featuring reliable and honest services.
The company has been awarded honors such as National High-Tech Enterprise, Provincial High-Tech Enterprise, Provincial Specialized, Refined, Differential and Innovative Enterprise, Technology-Based Small and Medium-Sized Enterprise, and Innovative Small and Medium-Sized Enterprise. It owns 17 independent utility model patents, 3 invention patents, 8 design patents, and 6 software copyrights.
The company's main export products include air-cooled diesel generator sets, multi-cylinder water-cooled diesel generator sets, load banks, ATS switch boxes, diesel water pumps, external independent fuel tanks, and other products. They are widely used in important fields such as communication systems, industrial power supply, financial systems, hotels, engineering companies, road administration maintenance, commercial buildings, airports, customs, and hospitals.
The main export countries and regions include Australia, New Zealand, Argentina, Chile, Mongolia, Kazakhstan, Indonesia, Singapore, the Philippines, Malaysia, the United Kingdom, Norway, the Netherlands, and more than 100 countries and regions.

Frequently Asked Questions About KEYPOWER
What does KEYPOWER manufacture?
KEYPOWER manufactures diesel generator sets (5–4,000 kVA), gas generator sets (300–1,200 kW), load banks (100–3,000 kW), self-priming diesel dewatering pumps, energy storage systems and mobile lighting towers at its 66,000+ m&sup2; factory in Fuzhou, Fujian, China.
Where is the KEYPOWER factory located?
Our factory is located at No. 3, Dongling Road, Ganze Street, Minhou County, Fuzhou, Fujian, China, about one hour from Fuzhou Changle International Airport. Factory visits and online factory tours are both welcome.
Which engine brands do KEYPOWER generators use?
We are an OEM partner of Cummins, Perkins, MTU, Volvo Penta, Scania, Deutz, Hyundai, Weichai, Yuchai, Baudouin, SDEC, FAW, Kohler, Kubota, Yanmar, Isuzu and Mitsubishi, with Stamford, Leroy-Somer or KEYPOWER alternators.
What warranty does KEYPOWER provide?
All KEYPOWER products are covered by a 1-year or 1,000-running-hour warranty (whichever comes first). Easily-damaged spare parts caused by manufacturing or material defects are supplied free of charge during the warranty period.
Does KEYPOWER accept OEM/ODM orders?
Yes. OEM and ODM are core services: custom engine/alternator combinations, canopy and container designs, voltage/frequency configurations, colors and branding are all available, supported by our in-house engineering team.
