Baudouin Powergen Solutions
Since 1918, Baudouin has built a reputation for quality and dependability. Today, Baudouin is proud to offer the most comprehensive range of powergen solutions available on the market.
DC GENSETS
The Baudouin generator set range offers a selection of power ratings designed to meet data center requirements. With outputs up to 5250 kVA, these pre-approved Uptime Institute gensets provide scalable and reliable power solutions while supporting HVO diesel for enhanced sustainability. This range of powergen solutions is the hightest power in the market.
VIEW GENERATOR SETS RANGE
BAUDOUIN POWERKIT DIESEL ENGINES
Our powergen solutions include our PowerKit engines offer the widest range on the market, from 18 to 4125 kVA, a selection few manufacturers can match. They stand out for their fuel consumption, load acceptance and power density, making PowerKit engines the preferred choice. Additionally, our engines are HVO-compatible and renowned for their best-in-class lead time.
VIEW POWERKIT DIESEL ENGINE RANGE
PSI RICH BURN GAS ENGINES
PSI provides end-to-end electric power generation and large, custom-engineered, integrated electrical power generation systems. PSI’s rich burn engines are purpose-built for heavy-duty, mission-critical applications. Learn more about Power Systems International (PSI)
VIEW PSI RICH BURN GAS ENGINE RANGE
BAUDOUIN POWERKIT LEAN BURN GAS ENGINES
Baudouin gas engines offer a power range from 63 to 1750 kVA. PowerKit Gas engines will provide an opportunity for a smooth transition towards zero emissions, until large scale hydrogen and fully renewable energies are available.
