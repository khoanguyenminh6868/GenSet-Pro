Aksa Power Generation
Starting its production journey in 1968 with the electric motor factory founded by Ali Metin Kazancı, AKSA manufactured its first generator in 1984 and quickly became one of the world's leading generator manufacturers by specializing in the production of machinery and equipment for electricity supply. Aksa Power Generation reached its current structure in 1994 with the incorporation of the Aksa community under Kazancı Holding and its reorganization. As the long-standing leader of the Turkish generator market, Aksa is among Turkey's top 200 industrial enterprises and exporting companies.
Today, Aksa Power Generation is one of the leading companies in the industry, producing gasoline, diesel, and natural gas generators ranging from 1 kVA to 3,750 kVA, marine generators, lighting towers, and generator equipment at its production facilities in Türkiye, China, Spain and South Africa, as well as at its commercial centers in the USA, the Netherlands, and the UAE.

Data Center Solutions
Companies need data centers to store and secure large amounts of data. For this reason, data centers are of critical importance for businesses. The most important security measure for the safe operation of data centers is the uninterrupted supply of electrical energy. In the event of a power outage, the generator will activate to continue supplying energy safely and without interruption. Aksa Power Generation has been providing you with customized and reliable solutions when you need them most, ensuring an uninterrupted life.

Telecom Solutions
The telecommunications sector provides the communication infrastructure that forms the backbone of the modern world. Delivering uninterrupted service is vital for this sector. However, power outages or deficiencies in the energy infrastructure can disrupt telecommunications networks. Aksa Power Generators play a critical role in ensuring communication continuity by maintaining the uninterrupted operation of telecommunication infrastructure.

Construction Sector
The construction sector is highly dependent on energy and requires uninterrupted power sources. Generators are critical for ensuring the continuity of on-site operations, the uninterrupted functioning of heavy machinery, and the timely completion of projects. In areas where electrical infrastructure is absent or insufficient, generators serve as the primary power source for construction sites.
Detalis
Retail Sector
The retail sector relies heavily on energy to provide uninterrupted customer service and maintain its operations. Retail locations such as stores, shopping malls, supermarkets, and logistics warehouses are severely affected by power outages. Therefore, generators offer a critical solution to ensure energy continuity in the retail sector.
Detalis
Healthcare Sector
In healthcare facilities like hospitals, clinics, laboratories, and emergency rooms, energy continuity and safety are paramount. Therefore, uninterrupted electrical power is not just a convenience; it's a vital necessity. When a hospital experiences a power outage, critical equipment, from operating rooms to life support systems in intensive care units, is rendered inoperable. Such situations can have irreversible consequences in healthcare, where even seconds matter. Aksa Power Generators step in during such emergencies, ensuring systems continue to operate and enabling healthcare professionals to continue their lifesaving duties.
Detalis
Mining Solutions
The mining industry operates under challenging conditions, often in remote areas far from urban power grids. Therefore, reliable and uninterrupted energy is of critical importance for the continuity of mining operations. From tunnel boring machines and ventilation systems to lighting and dewatering pumps, many types of equipment run on electrical power. Power outages not only cause production losses but also pose serious health and safety risks. Aksa diesel generators ensure safe, efficient, and uninterrupted operation in mining fields.
