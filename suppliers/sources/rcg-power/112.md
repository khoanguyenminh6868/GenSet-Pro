 Product Description
Drawing on a century of French marine heritage, Baudouin engines are uniquely engineered for heavy-duty reliability and mechanical simplicity.
Designed for easy maintenance with accessible service points and a robust mechanical fuel system for reliable startup.
Cabinetry is manufactured from high-quality sheet metal with specialized anti-corrosion coatings for extreme durability.
The heavy-duty base frame is optimized for all transport modes, including cranes, forks, and long-distance drags.
Provides standard ≥8-hour fuel autonomy and high-performance cooling for sustained operation in 50°C soundproof settings.
