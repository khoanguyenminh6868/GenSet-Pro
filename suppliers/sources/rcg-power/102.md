 Product Description
Specializes in high-horsepower heavy-duty engines (NT, K, M series) renowned for their formidable torque and structural resilience under extreme loads.
Optimized for heavy-duty industrial durability with a focus on simplified serviceability to minimize operational downtime in remote sites.
Encased in a weather-sealed cabinet with high-grade waterproof and dust-proof ratings, shielding critical components from harsh environmental ingress.
Designed for logistical convenience with integrated lift points and fork pockets for effortless cranes, pull, and drag transportation.
