 Product Description
Built upon a legacy of domestic industrial strength, SDEC units offer formidable torque and high-availability startup for primary and backup power.
Engineered for simplified maintenance cycles and low vibration profiles to reduce long-term structural fatigue and noise.
Cabinetry features high-sealing performance, creating an effective shield against rain, dust, and external particulate ingress.
Designed for rapid deployment with heavy-duty base frames compatible with cranes, forks, and drag transportation.
