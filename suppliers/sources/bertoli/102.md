AIR-COOLED GENERATORS
GENERATORS - AS FAR AS ENERGY CAN GO
Since we founded the company, we have been producing High-Tech and safe generators with power ranging between 2 and 125 kVA, which run on:
petrol (up to 16kVA)
Diesel (up to 135 kVA)
available with different engines including Deutz, Kohler/Lombardini, Lister Petter, Yanmar.
Our generators are designed for emergency use and also continuous production and are suitable for many areas of application.
We design and manufacture generators in Italy and use only the best components on the market.
Even the generators are made according to our philosophy: we first start from the design according to the needs to be fulfilled. This means that the generators that we will provide you with are not mass-produced but customised machines, designed to adapt perfectly to your production cycle.
Our company never makes an exception on quality, absolute priority on the choice of materials and thorough supervision of the entire production process until the finished product is tested.
