Static UPS: static Uninterruptible Power Supplies
The ABB static UPS offer consists of a complete range of UPS in standard or modular formats:
single-phase unit power of 0.35 kVA to 10 kVA
three-phase unit power of 10 kVA to 1,500 kVA
Energy can be stored in batteries (VRLA, NiCd or Li-Ion, depending on the model).
Please contact our staff for other powers or information about the accessories for static UPS.
