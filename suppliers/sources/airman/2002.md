Product Feature Highlights
220 KVA GENERATOR: BUILT TO PERFORM IN ANY ENVIRONMENT
AIRMAN’s SDG220S-8E1, 220 kVA generator delivers 220 kVA of prime power, providing 610 amps at three-phase 208V and 50 amps x 3 in single-phase 240V. Powered by a robust 323 HP John Deere engine, this 220 kVA generator ensures reliable performance with a 23.75-hour runtime at full load. Built to excel in any environment, AIRMAN generators combine durability, efficiency, and power for demanding applications.
