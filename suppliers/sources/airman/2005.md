Product Feature Highlights
THREE PHASE GENERATOR: BUILT TO PERFORM IN ANY ENVIRONMENT
AIRMAN’s SDG40S three phase generator delivers 36 kVA of prime power with 95 amps at three-phase 208V and 108 amps in single-phase 240V, ensuring reliable performance for demanding applications. With an impressive runtime of over 36 hours at full load, this generator is built for endurance. Its generator ends feature a series star wire high-wye configuration, offering best-in-class motor starting capability. The AIRMAN SDG40S three phase generator combines efficiency and power for versatile use in any environment.
