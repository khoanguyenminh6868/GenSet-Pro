COMPACT DIESEL GENERATOR: BUILT TO PERFORM
IN ANY ENVIRONMENT
AIRMAN ® ’s compact diesel generator with 12.5kVA/10kW prime power provides 30 amps at three-phase 208V and 40 amps in single-phase 240V with 27-hour runtime at full load. Configured with the industry’s best automatic voltage regulation (+/-0.5%), providing the widest range of voltage selection, AIRMAN ® compact diesel generators out-perform in any environment.
