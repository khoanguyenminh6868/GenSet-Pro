Product Feature Highlights
400 KVA GENERATOR: BUILT TO PERFORM IN ANY ENVIRONMENT
AIRMAN’s SDG400S 400 kVA generator delivers exceptional performance with 1049 amps at three-phase 208V and 50 amps x 3 in single-phase 240V. Powered by a robust Isuzu 6WG engine, it features electronic fuel priming and air bleed to minimize downtime. With a runtime of 23.1 hours at full load, this generator ensures continuous reliability. Equipped with Delta Demand Excitation, the AIRMAN SDG400S 400 kVA generator provides the industry’s best motor starting capability and automatically adjusts to varying loads for peak efficiency.
