Product Feature Highlights
125 KVA GENERATOR: BUILT TO PERFORM IN ANY ENVIRONMENT
AIRMAN’s SDG125S 125 kVA generator delivers powerful performance, providing 328 amps at three-phase 208V and 300 amps in single-phase 240V. Powered by a robust 147.9 HP Isuzu engine, this generator ensures over 24 hours of runtime. Equipped with standard two-way auxiliary fuel tank piping, the AIRMAN SDG125S 125 kVA generator seamlessly connects to external fuel tanks for extended operation, making it ideal for remote sites and demanding applications.
