Product Feature Highlights
150 KVA GENERATOR: BUILT TO PERFORM IN ANY ENVIRONMENT
A IRMAN’s SDG150S 150 kVA generator delivers 150 kVA of prime power, providing 394 amps at three-phase 208V, 180 amps at 480V, and over 360 amps in single-phase 240V. With a runtime of over 24 hours at full load, this generator offers outstanding reliability. Operating at just 66 dBA, the AIRMAN SDG150S 150 kVA generator is one of the quietest in the industry, making it a top choice for applications requiring low noise and high performance.
