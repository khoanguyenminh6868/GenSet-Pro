From Niigata
to your jobsite
Airman USA is the American arm of Airman Corp, founded in Niigata, Japan in 1938. We bring the same engineering discipline that powered post-war reconstruction to the modern American contractor — refined for U.S. emissions, voltages, and weather.
Headquartered in Adairsville, Georgia, AUC ships through a national distributor network in partnership with ANAcorp in the United States and Canada
Where to buy.
