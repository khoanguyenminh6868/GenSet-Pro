150kW Perkins Diesel Generator Overview
A generator should not be selected from the kW figure alone. Two projects with similar running loads can require different generator capacities because their motor loads, starting sequence, load steps, operating duty and future expansion plans are different.
A 150kW-class generator may be considered for workshops, small-to-medium industrial facilities, commercial facilities, construction or temporary project power, pump and motor applications, and utility or telecom support. These are possible application categories rather than automatic sizing recommendations.
Huali manufactures the complete generator set. Perkins is the engine manufacturer. The final quotation should identify the exact Perkins engine model, generator-set prime and standby ratings, alternator, controller, voltage, frequency and selected construction so that the buyer can review one clearly defined configuration.
Buyers comparing other Perkins-powered ratings can also review the Perkins Diesel Generator range .
Is 150kW the Right Rating for Your Load?
Start with the loads that must actually operate when the generator is running.
Running and Simultaneous Loads
A connected-load total does not necessarily represent the generator duty. Some equipment may operate continuously, some intermittently, and some may never operate at the same time.
For initial sizing, identify:
the equipment that must operate on generator power;
the normal running load of each item;
which loads can operate simultaneously;
loads that can be delayed or shed;
equipment planned for future expansion.
The resulting simultaneous load is more useful than simply adding every nameplate rating in the facility.
A 150kW generator should not be selected simply because the calculated running load is below 150kW. Motor starting, transient load response, duty rating and site conditions may change the required generator capacity.
If your load information is incomplete, send Huali a load list with equipment names, quantities and available nameplate data for an initial sizing review. You can use our Diesel Generator Load List Template to organize the project information.
Large Motor Starting Can Change Generator Sizing
Motors, pumps, fans and compressors can impose a much higher transient demand during starting than during normal operation.
For the largest motor, provide its rated kW, kVA, HP or current together with the starting method. Direct-on-line starting, star-delta starting, soft starters and variable-frequency drives can create different generator requirements.
The starting sequence also matters. A motor that starts after other loads are already operating presents a different condition from the same motor starting on an unloaded generator.
Where several motors are involved, identify whether they can start together or whether the control system allows staged starting.
Huali can then review the proposed generator against the running load and starting sequence. A specific motor size should not be declared suitable for a 150kW generator until the motor and project data have been checked.
For projects containing pumps, compressors or other significant motor loads, see Diesel Generator Sizing for Motors, Pumps and Compressors .
Prime or Standby Operation?
Prime and standby ratings are not interchangeable labels.
Standby applications normally involve generator operation when the normal utility supply is unavailable. Prime applications involve a different operating profile and must be evaluated against the applicable generator-set and engine rating definitions.
Tell Huali how the generator will be used, including expected operating hours and load pattern. The quotation and technical datasheet should state the applicable rating for the selected configuration.
For a more detailed explanation of generator ratings, see Prime, Standby and Continuous Power Ratings .
Perkins Engine and Complete Generator Configuration
The Perkins engine is one part of the complete generating set. The final package also includes the alternator, control system, cooling system, base structure, starting system, protection equipment and other items defined in the quotation.
For procurement comparison, ask suppliers to identify the exact component models rather than comparing only the engine brand.
The approved Huali quotation should identify:
the exact Perkins engine model;
generator-set prime and standby ratings;
alternator manufacturer and model;
controller manufacturer and model;
rated voltage and frequency;
phase and connection;
included protection and breaker arrangement;
standard equipment and project-specific options.
This makes it easier to compare technical offers on the same basis and prevents optional equipment from being mistaken for standard scope.
Voltage, Frequency and ATS Requirements
Confirm the project electrical system before the generator is ordered.
Provide the required line voltage, frequency and phase. These requirements affect the alternator configuration, rated current, protection and downstream electrical interface.
Rated current also changes with voltage. A current value specified for one three-phase voltage should not be treated as a universal value for every project.
If the generator is intended to start automatically after a utility failure, an automatic transfer system may also need to be evaluated. The ATS rating and arrangement should be matched to the actual electrical system and project operating sequence.
Do not specify an ATS solely from the generator kW rating. Supply arrangement, voltage, current, poles, transfer logic and project requirements should also be confirmed.
Open or Silent Generator Configuration
An open generator set can be considered where the equipment will be installed in a suitably protected generator room or other engineered indoor location.
The room still requires appropriate cooling airflow, hot-air discharge, exhaust routing, fuel arrangements and maintenance access.
A silent enclosure may be considered when outdoor installation, environmental exposure or project noise requirements make an enclosed configuration preferable.
The required noise criterion should be stated by the buyer where it is important. A fixed sound level should not be assumed without a defined enclosure configuration, operating condition and measurement method.
Buyers evaluating an enclosed configuration can also review Huali's Silent Diesel Generator range.
Installation, Ventilation and Maintenance Access
Generator performance depends on more than the engine and alternator.
Before finalizing the installation, review:
ambient temperature and altitude;
cooling-air inlet;
radiator hot-air discharge;
exhaust routing;
fuel supply and required runtime;
foundation and equipment access;
space for routine inspection and maintenance;
route for equipment installation or future removal.
For indoor installations, avoid placing the generator in a room first and treating ventilation as a later issue. Cooling airflow and exhaust arrangements should be considered during the layout stage.
>Site altitude and ambient temperature should also be provided because environmental conditions can affect the available generator and engine performance.
For additional project planning information, read How Altitude, Temperature and Site Conditions Affect Diesel Generator Sizing .
Factory Integration and Project Verification

Testing requirements should be stated specifically rather than replaced by a general claim such as "fully tested" or "100% reliable."
For projects requiring formal acceptance records, identify the required documents and witness requirements before manufacture.

FAQ About 150kW Generator Selection
How do I know whether a 150kW generator is large enough?
Compare the required simultaneous running load with motor-starting requirements, load steps, operating duty, future expansion and site conditions. The running-load total alone is not sufficient for final sizing.
Can a 150kW generator start a large motor?
That cannot be determined from the generator and motor kW ratings alone. Provide the motor rating, starting method, starting current or manufacturer data, and the other loads already operating during the start.
Should I choose an open or silent generator?
The choice depends on installation location, weather exposure, ventilation arrangement, project noise requirements, maintenance access and other site conditions. An open set may be appropriate for a properly designed generator room, while an enclosed configuration may be evaluated for outdoor or noise-sensitive installations.
Do I need an ATS?
An ATS may be required where automatic transfer between the normal supply and generator supply is part of the project. Its electrical rating and transfer arrangement should be selected from the actual system requirements rather than the generator kW figure alone.
What should I send before requesting a quotation?
At minimum, provide the required power or load information, voltage, frequency, application and destination country. For motor loads, also provide the largest motor and starting method.
