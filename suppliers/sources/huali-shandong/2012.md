Is 1200kW / 1500kVA Enough for Your Project?
Not from running kW alone. A 1200kW generator must be checked against the loads operating at the same time, the largest motor, starting method, load steps, future expansion, site conditions and required redundancy.
Start with the project load schedule rather than the total connected equipment rating. Many installations contain loads that do not operate simultaneously, while essential loads may need to remain online throughout generator operation.
For each major load, identify:
running kW or kVA;
rated voltage;
operating sequence;
whether it runs continuously or intermittently;
whether it starts while other major loads are already online;
whether its start can be delayed.
If the confirmed duty is substantially below the 1200kW range, the 800kW Perkins Diesel Generator Set can be reviewed as a lower-output reference.
Where the project requires more capacity, additional growth margin or another system architecture, the 1500kW Perkins Diesel Generator Set provides a higher-output comparison.
Final selection should follow the real load profile, not simply the nameplate total of all connected equipment.
Large Motors and Load Steps
Large motors are one of the main reasons a generator that appears adequate on running kW may still require further sizing review.
For the largest motors, provide:
motor kW or HP;
rated voltage;
starting method;
starting-current data where available;
mechanical load during starting;
loads already connected when the motor starts.
Direct-on-line, star-delta, soft-start and variable-frequency-drive starting can impose different transient demands on the generator.
Load sequence matters as much as total load. Starting one large motor while the generator already carries substantial process load is different from starting the same motor immediately after generator startup.
Where several large loads are involved, identify the required restoration sequence. This allows the generator, control system and switchgear strategy to be reviewed against actual operating conditions instead of a theoretical connected-load total.
Future expansion should also be identified at this stage. Reserve capacity is most useful when it reflects a defined expansion plan or operating requirement rather than an arbitrary percentage.
Prime or Standby Duty?
The confirmed HL1200GF ratings are:
Prime: 1200 kW / 1500 kVA
Standby: 1320 kW / 1650 kVA
These are different operating ratings and should not be treated as interchangeable.
Prime duty applies where the generator operates as a regular source of power under the applicable variable-load rating conditions. Standby duty applies when the generator supplies the installation following loss of the normal power source.
Prime power should not be described as unrestricted continuous power.
For a prime-power project, provide the expected operating hours, normal load range and major load variations.
For a standby installation, define which loads transfer after utility failure, which loads are restored first and whether large motors or other major loads are introduced in stages.
Perkins 4012-46TAG2A Engine
The confirmed 50 Hz HL1200GF configuration uses the Perkins 4012-46TAG2A operating at 1500 rpm.
The Perkins 4012 platform uses a 12-cylinder V arrangement with approximately 45.8 L displacement and a 160 × 190 mm bore and stroke. The engine family uses turbocharging with charge-air cooling, direct injection and liquid cooling.
Engine mechanical output and complete generator-set electrical output are different ratings. Engine power data should therefore not be substituted directly for the 1200 kW / 1500 kVA electrical rating of the complete generator set.
Huali integrates the engine with the alternator, cooling system, baseframe, controls and other equipment required by the confirmed generator-set configuration.

How Much Current Does a 1500kVA Generator Produce at 400V?
The confirmed rated current of this 1500kVA, 400V HL1200GF configuration is 2165 A.
The three-phase relationship is:
I ≈ kVA × 1000 / (&radic;3 × V)
For 1500 kVA at 400 V:
I ≈ 1500 × 1000 / (&radic;3 × 400) ≈ 2165 A
The calculation and the approved rated current therefore align.
This current level has direct consequences for the project electrical interface. Generator terminals, breaker arrangement, busbar or parallel cable routing, switchboard termination space and installation access become major design items.
This is one of the reasons a 1200kW-class low-voltage generator should not be evaluated from kW alone.
Final breaker, conductor and busbar design still requires the complete protection study, installation method and applicable project standards.
Breaker, Busbar and Switchgear Interface
At 2165 A rated current , physical electrical connection is a major part of the project.
For the initial engineering review, provide:
required system voltage;
generator-to-switchboard distance;
cable or busbar preference;
conductor material where already specified;
existing switchgear data;
termination-space constraints;
protection and interlocking requirements.
A large low-voltage generator may use multiple conductors per phase, busbar or another project-specific connection arrangement. The correct solution depends on the approved electrical design.
No single cable cross-section, breaker frame size or busbar rating should be specified from the 1200kW figure alone.
One 1200kW Generator or Parallel Generators?
A single HL1200GF can be appropriate when it satisfies the required load, reserve and availability target. Parallel generators become relevant when the system needs redundancy, staged capacity or greater operating flexibility.
Evaluate a parallel arrangement when the project has:
a defined N+1 or other redundancy philosophy;
substantial future expansion;
large staged loads;
a wide operating load range;
maintenance requirements that favour multiple generating sources.
Parallel operation adds equipment and control requirements, including synchronizing, generator breakers, switchgear, communications and protection logic.
Do not choose a parallel architecture simply by dividing 1200kW into two smaller generator ratings. The decision should follow the required operating philosophy and load profile.
If paralleling is being considered, provide the single-line diagram, expected load profile, required redundancy and proposed number of generator sources during the quotation stage.
When Should LV, Transformer or MV Distribution Be Compared?
A 1200kW generator does not automatically require medium voltage.
The confirmed 1500kVA / 400V HL1200GF configuration has a rated current of 2165 A . At this current level, low-voltage breakers, busbars, cables and switchboard interfaces can become physically significant.
For some projects, direct 400V distribution remains appropriate.
Other installations may already use transformers or higher-voltage plant distribution. A transformer-connected or medium-voltage arrangement can therefore become relevant where current, distribution distance, existing infrastructure or plant architecture makes low-voltage distribution less practical.
If LV, transformer-connected or MV alternatives are being compared, provide:
the project single-line diagram;
required distribution voltage;
approximate generator-to-load distance;
existing or proposed transformer information;
existing switchgear information;
grounding philosophy where already defined.
Do not select medium voltage solely because the generator is rated 1200kW.
Synchronizing, Protection and Transfer Equipment
Control and protection requirements depend on how the HL1200GF connects to the power system.
A utility-to-generator standby arrangement has different requirements from a generator-to-generator parallel system or another multiple-source architecture.
Where synchronizing is required, define:
which sources operate in parallel;
operating sequence;
generator breaker interfaces;
transfer logic;
protection requirements;
required communications with project switchgear or supervisory systems.
An ATS is appropriate for some standby arrangements, but it should not automatically be treated as a substitute for full switchgear engineering on every 1200kW project.
Only controller functions, synchronizing functions, remote monitoring and other features included in the approved Huali scope should be described as supplied equipment.
Cooling, Fuel and Installation Conditions
The final cooling and fuel arrangement depends on the installation environment and operating duty.
For an indoor generator room, provide:
maximum ambient temperature;
altitude;
room layout;
fresh-air intake arrangement;
radiator-discharge arrangement;
proposed exhaust route.
The installation must provide adequate cooling airflow while limiting hot-air recirculation and excessive restriction.
Fuel planning starts with required runtime. A standby generator required to operate for a defined emergency period can need a different fuel arrangement from a generator used regularly for prime power.
Provide the required fuel autonomy and any site restrictions affecting bulk fuel storage, day tanks or refilling.
An open generator can be installed in a properly engineered generator room. Projects requiring weather protection or defined acoustic treatment can also review a silent diesel generator or containerized diesel generator configuration.
Fuel-consumption figures, acoustic performance, dimensions and weight should always correspond to the actual ordered configuration.
Transport and Maintenance Access
A large generator package must be able to reach its final installation position and remain serviceable after commissioning.
Before the configuration is finalized, check:
road and site access;
gates and building openings;
crane or lifting access;
route from unloading point to foundation;
maintenance clearance around major components.
For an indoor installation, confirm that the complete generator package can physically enter the generator room.
Final dimensions, lifting points and weight should come from the approved Huali general arrangement drawing for the ordered configuration.
Maintenance access should allow work on the Perkins engine, Shanhua alternator, cooling equipment, electrical terminals and control system, including future removal of major components where required.
Factory Acceptance Testing and Project Documentation
For a 1200kW-class generator project, inspection and factory-testing requirements are best agreed before production is completed.
Depending on the project specification, the acceptance scope can include:
component identification;
electrical checks;
controller and protection verification;
operating tests;
load-bank testing;
buyer or consultant witness points.
Where witness FAT is required, define the test scope and acceptance criteria before the test date.
Project documentation can include approved technical datasheets, general arrangement drawings, electrical drawings, component information and factory test records. The documents supplied with the generator should correspond to the actual HL1200GF configuration delivered for the project.

FAQ About the 1200kW Perkins Generator
Is 1200kW / 1500kVA enough for my project?
Not from running load alone. Check simultaneous demand, the largest motor, starting method, load steps, future expansion, site conditions and redundancy before finalizing the generator rating.
How much current does a 1500kVA generator produce at 400V?
The confirmed rated current of the 1500kVA / 400V HL1200GF is 2165 A . This current directly affects breaker, busbar, cable, termination and switchgear design.
Can the HL1200GF start my largest motor?
Generator kW alone cannot determine this. Provide the motor rating, voltage, starting method, starting-current data and the load already connected when the motor starts.
Should I use one 1200kW generator or parallel generators?
Use a single unit when it meets the required capacity and availability target. Evaluate parallel generators where redundancy, future expansion, staged capacity or maintenance flexibility justifies the additional system complexity.
Does a 1200kW generator need medium voltage?
No. The confirmed 400V configuration is a valid low-voltage arrangement. Compare LV, transformer-connected and MV systems when current, distribution distance, switchgear or existing plant architecture makes the distribution method important.
What information should I send for a technical quotation?
Send the load schedule, voltage/frequency, operating duty, largest motors, starting methods, load sequence, redundancy requirement, installation conditions and destination country. Include the single-line diagram for projects involving switchgear, parallel generators or transformers.
