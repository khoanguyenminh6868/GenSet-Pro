Italian power generators and generator sets
| 120 Employees | 300 Units produced/month | 41.200 m² Business area | 8000 Customers
Revolutions and innovations: 40 years of Italian success
Since 1980, CGM has embodied the passion and Italian brillance in the design and production of power generators. Each GenSet manufactured at the Vicenza plant is not just a product but the result of a commitment to innovation and quality. Solutions are provided that not only power machinery but also initiate projects, passions, and dreams in various applications worldwide.

History of CGM
From a simple idea to a global market leader
Founded by Giorgio Chilese, CGM has turned small projects into a successful industrial business, establishing its position as a manufacturer of high-quality Generating Sets. From a small local distributor near Vicenza, the company has grown to reach customers all over the world, thanks to its extreme dedication to quality and innovation.

Today
Innovation and development in recent years:
Evolution of production machinery
From 2022 to 2024, CGM has invested in a significant upgrade of its production infrastructure: a completely renovated area for the production of electrical panels, a dedicated warehouse for engines and alternators, and an in-house metal work, as well as the expansion of production lines and the establishment of a new testing room.
Expansion of the professional team
Alongside the upgrading of machinery, CGM has strengthened its technical department with the addition of electrical and mechanical designers, as well as electrical engineers. A new team of technicians has also been dedicated exclusively to after-sales support, to ensure increasingly efficient and responsive service.
Expansion into international markets
With a constant eye on global expansion, the company has intensified its export activities, reaching new markets in Europe, Africa, America and Southeast Asia. This step further consolidates CGM's presence at an international level, bringing innovations and the reliability of power generators beyond national borders.
