Safety and Sustainable Innovation in the energy sector
Why choose
CGM?
Trust Your Power represents the company's philosophy:
believing in your own abilities and in the strength of CGM products. Safety, power, and reliability are the qualities that have enabled a small Italian company to become a global benchmark in the power generator sector.
Our promise:
excellence and sustainability
Learn more about our
ECO POWER
powered by CGM
CGM's mission is to boost innovation and support development through the creation of high-quality power generators that combine Italian engineering expertise with sustainable and reliable solutions. We are committed to transforming energy into a force that powers tomorrow's possibilities, providing our customers with powerful, efficient and, adaptable tools.
Through continuous investment in research and development, CGM aims to lead change in the energy sector, elevating standards of production, safety, and environmental respect to build a brighter and more sustainable future.
A continuous commitment
to cleaner energy
Sustainability and efficiency are key elements of the company's philosophy. CGM has always been committed to implementing meaningful solutions that reflect both its dedication to environmental responsibility and to quality. The installation of 40 kW solar panels significantly reduces the environmental impact of production, providing clean and renewable energy for every daily operation.
Additionally, to ensure the high quality and reliability of the GenSets, the instruments used in the testing room undergo regular calibrations; a process that ensures the proper functioning of each piece of equipment with the precision that has always distinguished the company.
