Main parameter
Agricultural Equipment are widely used in 24hp to 100hp tractor and 88hp to 120hp harvester.
These engines boast the advantages of being environmentally friendly, economical, durable, powerful, comfortable, and easy to install.
Environmental Friendliness: The coomon rail fuel system accurately controls the timing or fuel quantity according to the working conditions.
Economy: The engine delivers direct injection with a multi-injection common rail system for efficient combustion and low fuel use.
Durability: This durable engine features an alloy cast iron block, famous brand piston rings, and a reinforced bearing system for proven reliability.
Power: Long stroke, sufficient torque reserve and low speed with high torque.
Comfort: The unique structural design of the body and cylinder head has high rigidity and little deformation; optimized combustion organization, low noise and small vibration.
Ease of Installation: Different kinds of interface type, wide range of adaptation,compact structure and esay to install.
