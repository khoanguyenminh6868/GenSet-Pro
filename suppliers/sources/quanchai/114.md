Product features
Main parameter
This engine boasts exceptional power, superb performance, low fuel consumption, premium configuration, long service life, and outstanding reliability.
Environmental Friendliness: The common rail fuel system accurately controls the timing or fuel quantity according to the working conditions, which is clean and environmentally friendly.
Economy: The engine delivers direct injection with a multi-injection common rail system for efficient combustion and low fuel use; it also features optimized friction-pair matching for reduced oil consumption.
Durability: This durable engine features an alloy cast iron block, joint-venture piston rings, and a reinforced gear train with PTO interface for proven reliability.
Power: Long stroke,sufficient torque reserve,low speed with high torque and strong idle speed.
Comfort: The unique structural design of the base and cylinder head has high rigidity and little deformation;optimized combustion organization,low noise and little vibration.
Ease of Installation: Platform design, good interface versatility,compact structure,easy to stall.
