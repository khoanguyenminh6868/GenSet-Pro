INTELLIGENT ENERGY ARBITRATION
How Dual-Power Synergy Works
Smart bidirectional energy dispatch autonomously balancing battery state-of-charge (SOC) and diesel engine peak efficiency.
01
Silent Pure-Battery Supply
Under low-to-medium loads and during nighttime hours, the high-capacity LFP battery bank powers the load through the hybrid inverter/PCS with zero noise, zero emissions, and pure sine-wave stability.
02
Intelligent Auto-Genset Start
When battery SOC drops below preset thresholds or high surging loads occur, the Yanmar/Perkins engine starts automatically, locking into its maximum thermal efficiency sweet spot.
Heavy-Duty Diesel Genset + LiFePO4 Storage BESS
03
High-Efficiency Fast Recharging
Surplus energy generated by the diesel engine is simultaneously channeled into the LFP battery bank at high charging rates, ensuring rapid replenishment without wasteful idling.
04
Seamless 0ms Power Synchronization
Advanced DSE7320 / AGC150 microgrid controllers manage millisecond synchronization and changeover, eliminating voltage spikes and brownouts for critical operations.
