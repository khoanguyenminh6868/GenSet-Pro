Diesel Generator Sets | 60Hz
■ Open-type ■ Containerized-type
The MPMC MC2500-2 is a heavy-duty 60Hz diesel generator powered by the original Cummins QSK78-G7 engine. It delivers 3125kVA standby power and 2500kVA (2000kW) prime power, providing stable, high-efficiency and reliable power output.
Compliant with 60Hz grid standards, this genset perfectly fits UK, European and North American market demands. Equipped with strong load capacity, low fuel consumption and complete intelligent protection systems, it serves as an ideal continuous main power and emergency standby power solution for large factories, data centers, mines and commercial facilities.
