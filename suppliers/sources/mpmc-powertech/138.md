The MPMC MS365-1 is a high-performance diesel generator set engineered for excellence and environmental compliance. Powered by the world-renowned SCANIA DC13 071A 02-01 engine meeting Stage IIIA emission levels, this unit delivers a Standby Power of 365kVA (292kW) and a Prime Power of 365kVA (292kW). With a fuel consumption of 69.1 L/H at 100% load and a robust 1000L fuel tank (open type), it ensures long-term operational efficiency and a high kWh output for demanding duty cycles in emission-regulated markets.
MPMC power generation delivers reliable economical power solutions with optimal fuel economy and low operating costs, applied in a variety of diverse applications including rental, mining, construction, data center, power plants, telecommunication, etc.
