MPMC GSB Series Hybrid Power Station is a heavy-duty, all-in-one microgrid solution that seamlessly integrates 2550Wp sliding-deployable solar panels , 41–173kWh high-safety LFP energy storage , and authentic Perkins/Yanmar diesel generators . INTELLIGENT ENERGY DISPATCH
How Tri-Power Synergy Works
Smart multi-source energy arbitration autonomously balancing solar intake, battery state-of-charge (SOC), and engine load efficiency.
01
Solar Harvesting Priority
During daylight hours, the 2550 Wp deployable PV array with integrated MPPT controllers channels maximum solar power directly to supply customer loads and recharge the LFP battery system.
02
Silent Battery Supply
Under light-to-medium loads and during nighttime hours, the high-capacity LFP battery bank powers the hybrid inverter/PCS with zero noise, zero emissions, and pure sine-wave stability.
2550 Wp Deployed PV • LFP BESS • Diesel Gen
03
Intelligent Auto-Genset Start
When battery SOC drops below preset thresholds or load surges occur, the Yanmar/Perkins diesel genset automatically starts, running in its peak thermal efficiency sweet spot to power loads and fast-charge the battery.
04
Zero-Downtime Synchronization
DSE7320 / AGC150 controllers govern millisecond seamless changeover between the PCS inverter and the genset, eliminating voltage sags, flickers, and brownouts for mission-critical operations.

Technical Standards & Engineering Remarks
Engine Standard: The diesel engine is fixed with authentic heavy-duty industrial engines from PERKINS / YANMAR .
MPPT & Grid Mode: Integrated MPPT controller comes standard for hybrid inverters. Full On-grid and Off-grid operation supported.
Intelligent Controllers: DSE7320 is standard for genset with hybrid inverters; AGC 150 controller is standard for genset with PCS configurations.
STC Solar Rating: Rated at Standard Test Conditions (STC): Light intensity ≥ 1000 W/m², PV cell temperature ≤ 25℃, Atmospheric air mass AM ≤ 1.5.
