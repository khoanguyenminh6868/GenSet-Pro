MPMC MS320-2 364kVA 60Hz Diesel Generator Set Powered by SCANIA DC09 072A 02-14
Diesel Generator Sets | 60Hz
■ Open-type ■ Silent-type
The MPMC MS320-2 is a reliable 60Hz diesel generator set powered by the SCANIA DC09 072A 02-14 engine. It delivers 400kVA (320kW) standby power and 364kVA (291kW) prime power at 1800rpm, with fuel consumption of 77.2 L/H at 100% load. Available in open type, soundproof SR type, and soundproof SR2 type configurations, it is suitable for industrial facilities, commercial buildings, construction sites, and other applications requiring stable 60Hz power.
MPMC generator sets are renowned for their exceptional durability, low lifecycle costs, and outstanding performance under the most demanding conditions, providing a solid foundation for your operations and ensuring uninterrupted uptime.
