MB3000-2 3300kVA Diesel Generator Set Powered by Baudouin 16M55G3000/6
Diesel Generator Sets | 60Hz
■ Open-type ■ Containerized-type
MPMC MB3000-2 is the flagship ultra-high-power diesel generator set of the MPMC Baudouin series, providing 3750kVA standby power and 3300kVA prime power with standard 3PH 60Hz / 1800rpm for American and Latin American industrial projects. Equipped with a heavy-duty Baudouin 16M55G3000/6 engine and high-performance Leroy Somer alternator, it delivers stable heavy-load output and excellent continuous operation capability.
This 3MW-class genset is specially designed for super-large critical power scenarios, ideal for hyperscale data center clusters, heavy industrial bases, large mining operations, national infrastructure and remote off-grid projects. It serves as reliable emergency backup for zero-outage facilities and stable prime power for harsh, isolated industrial sites.
With intelligent ECU control and strong environmental adaptability, the MB3000-2 offers a dependable high-power power solution for large-scale heavy industrial and key engineering projects worldwide.
