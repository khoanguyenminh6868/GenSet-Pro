MB3300-2 3750kVA Diesel Generator Set Powered by Baudouin 16M55G3300/6
Diesel Generator Sets | 60Hz
■ Open-type ■ Containerized-type
MPMC MB3300-2 flagship high-power diesel generator set offers the top power rating of the series, with 4125kVA (3300kW) standby power and 3750kVA (3000kW) prime power for large standby-only engineering projects, with a standard electrical specification of 3PH 60Hz / 1800rpm. Powered by premium Baudouin 16M55G3300/6 16-cylinder 87.5L large-displacement engine and ECU full intelligent control system, it achieves industry-leading load resistance performance. Matching with high-end Leroy Somer LSA 54.2 M10 alternator, the unit keeps stable power quality under extreme heavy loads. Full-load fuel consumption reaches 794.4L/H, open type noise is 103.06±5dB(A)@7m at 75% load. Designed for national key infrastructure, large power station standby and super industrial park emergency power supply, this flagship model delivers the most reliable and cost-effective high-power power support for top-tier industrial scenarios..
