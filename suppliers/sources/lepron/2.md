Since 1980, the French company LEPRON has been offering generators up to 4,500 kVA for sale or rental (rental available only in France). Discover our range of premium quality generators powered by high-performance biofuel engines . We can provide you with all the services you need (custom design, transport, installation, maintenance, spare parts, etc.). Located in the heart of France, in the Loire Valley, just 2 hours from Paris, we install machines all over the world. LEPRON is an official partner and distributor of KOHLER generators (formerly SDMO). KOHLER rebranded as Rehlko in 2024: as an authorized distributor of Rehlko, our company is very proud to share the brand’s values and contribute to representing them in France.
