Generator Manufacturers & Backup Power Specialists
Your One-Stop Shop for All Things Generators & UPS
Since our establishment, YorPower has emerged as a trusted leader in the back-up power solutions industry, offering a comprehensive range of services for generators and Uninterruptible Power Supplies (UPS) . Our mission is to ensure that our clients have access to reliable, high-quality back-up power solutions, no matter their location or sector.
Read more
Our Offerings
At YorPower, we pride ourselves on being a complete service provider for all your back-up power needs. Our services span from the initial supply and delivery of UPS’s and generators to their installation, commissioning, and maintenance. We also specialize in maintenance and repairs to ensure the longevity and efficiency of your back-up power systems.
YorPower Branded Generators
Our in-house manufactured generators , branded under YorPower, are produced in the UK and shipped worldwide. Known for their reliability and performance, these generators are designed to meet the highest standards and are tailored to suit a variety of applications.
Authorized Dealers for Major Brands
In addition to our own brand, we are official and authorized dealers for some of the most respected names in the industry, including Cummins, Perkins, CAT, and FG Wilson. This allows us to offer a diverse range of generators, ensuring that we have the perfect solution for every client.
UPS Solutions
We are also an authorized dealer and service partner for Riello UPS, providing top-tier reliable back-up power supplies that help to ensure continuous power for critical operations.
Spare Parts
YorPower offers genuine OEM spare parts for generators and UPS systems, ensuring quality and compatibility. Our extensive inventory and expert support help to ensure that your equipment runs smoothly. Enjoy fast delivery, competitive pricing, and reliable parts for all major brands.
Serving Diverse Sectors
Our clients come from a wide range of industries, each with unique power needs. We provide backup power solutions for data centers, healthcare facilities, manufacturing companies, facilities management companies, agricultural operations, sports stadiums, public sector organizations, retail businesses, and many more. Our ability to serve such a diverse clientele is a testament to our versatility and commitment to excellence.
Dedicated Project Management
To support both large and small-scale projects and ensure the seamless installation and commission of our back-up power solutions, we have a dedicated project management team. This team works closely with clients from the initial design phase through to installation, ensuring that every project is executed flawlessly and meets all specifications.
Global Reach
While we are based in Yorkshire, UK, our reach extends far beyond that. We have established a strong presence in international markets, with dealers across Africa, Asia, South America, and Europe. This global network allows us to deliver our high-quality products and services to clients worldwide, maintaining our commitment to reliability and customer satisfaction.
Commitment to Excellence
At YorPower, we are committed to providing our clients with the best possible back-up power solutions. Our trusted team of experts is dedicated to understanding your specific needs and delivering solutions that exceed your expectations. Whether you need a robust backup power system for a healthcare facility or a reliable generator for a remote agricultural site, we have the expertise and resources to meet your requirements.
Contact Us Now
Discover how YorPower can meet your back-up power needs with our extensive range of products and services. Contact Us to discuss your requirements with our knowledgeable team. For trust in your power, trust YorPower.

Who are we?
YorPower is a leading name in the generator and UPS industry. Over the decades, we have built a reputation for trust and reliability, becoming a go-to provider for back-up power solutions.
Read more
Our journey has been marked by significant growth and strategic reorganization, most notably the merger of YorPower, PPSPower (previously The Progress Group & PowerPlant Services), and Glenace in January 2023. This consolidation has enabled us to unify our operations and leverage the strengths of each entity to provide comprehensive back-up power solutions under the YorPower brand.
Stephen Peal, previously Managing Director of PPSPower, now serves as the Group Managing Director, overseeing the integrated operations. This restructuring has enhanced our ability to deliver seamless, end-to-end services, from manufacturing and installation to maintenance and repairs. Our approach is customer-centric, ensuring that we understand and meet the unique needs of each client. This philosophy extends across all our sectors, including facilities management, healthcare, data centers, manufacturing, and public sector services.
Our integrated team brings together decades of expertise and a shared commitment to excellence. With a global presence, we serve clients not only in the UK but across Africa, Asia, South America, and Europe, offering reliable and innovative back-up power solutions tailored to diverse environments and requirements.

What we do
At YorPower, we provide a full spectrum of services for generators and UPS systems, ensuring reliable and efficient power solutions for diverse applications.
Read below more information on our Generator Services, UPS Services and Project Management.

Generator Services:
Our comprehensive generator services cover the entire lifecycle of your equipment:
Manufacture: We produce high-quality YorPower branded generators in the UK, designed to meet rigorous standards and shipped worldwide.
Supply: We offer a wide range of generators, including those from major brands such as Cummins, Perkins, CAT, and FG Wilson.
Delivery: Our logistics network ensures prompt and reliable delivery to any location.
Installation: Our skilled technicians handle the complete installation process, ensuring your generator is set up correctly and efficiently.
Commissioning: We rigorously test and commission your generator to guarantee it meets all operational requirements.
Maintenance: Regular maintenance services keep your generator in optimal condition, minimizing downtime and extending its lifespan.
Repairs: Our expert repair services address any issues quickly and effectively, ensuring minimal disruption to your operations.
Spare Parts: We provide genuine OEM spare parts, ensuring the highest quality and compatibility for your generators.
UPS Services:
Our UPS services ensure continuous power supply for critical systems:
Supply: We offer a range of Riello UPS systems, known for their reliability and performance.
Delivery: Our efficient logistics ensure timely delivery of UPS systems.
Installation: Our team installs UPS systems with precision, ensuring seamless integration with your existing infrastructure.
Commissioning: We conduct thorough commissioning to ensure your UPS system operates flawlessly.
Maintenance: Regular maintenance checks keep your UPS systems running smoothly, preventing unexpected failures.
Repairs: Our technicians are adept at diagnosing and repairing any issues with your UPS systems, ensuring uninterrupted power.
Project Management:
We offer end-to-end project management for large-scale back-up power solutions:
Design: Our expert team works closely with you to design bespoke back-up power solutions tailored to your specific needs.
Implementation: From initial concept to final installation, we manage every aspect of the project, ensuring it is completed on time and within budget.
Support: Our dedicated project management team provides continuous support, ensuring smooth execution and addressing any challenges promptly.
YorPower’s comprehensive services ensure that your power systems are reliable, efficient, and fully supported. Our commitment to excellence and customer satisfaction drives us to deliver the best solutions for your back-up power needs.
