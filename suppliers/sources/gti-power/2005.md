Control Panels
All of our gensets come as standard supplied with a Deep Sea DSE7120 or DSE7220 control panel along with a Deep Sea battery charger and an appropriately sized ABB circuit breaker. Deep Sea Electronics is one of the world’s top manufacturers of generator control and battery charger equipment. All of our control panels are made in the UK and are of excellent quality.
However we are not limited to these and can supply a panel to meet any customer’s requirements… from a basic DSE3110 panel with analogue gauges to a DSE8610 synchronising system panel. Whatever you require we will help you to find a solution.
You also have the option for an automatic transfer switch with all our gensets… in case you need to make sure your backup generator is up and operational as quick as possible after your mains power fails you.
