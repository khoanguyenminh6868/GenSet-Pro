Perkins Mecc Alte
We manufacture Perkins with Meccalte gensets ranging from 10kva – 2,250kVA for 50Hz and 11kVA – 1,700kVA 60Hz. All the Perkins engines used in our gensets are manufactured in either the UK or the USA. The Meccalte alternators are manufactured in the UK.
All gensets are supplied complete with DSE7120/DSE7320 control panel, battery charger, appropriately sized ABB circuit breaker, steel welded baseframe with minimum 8 hour fuel tank, AVMs, batteries, silencers, flex and all other standard accessories so that your genset is ready for use. All you need to add is fuel, oil and coolant.
