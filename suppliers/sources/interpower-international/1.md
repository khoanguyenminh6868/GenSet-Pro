Engineered Power Solutions
Manufacturers of Industrial, Backup, Marine and Bespoke Generators from 2kVA to 4000kVA
Learn More
Interpower are an established British company, experienced in the manufacture of high quality power generation equipment. All products are manufactured in UK factory, and we only use British and European components in our products.
We are specialists in the supply of industrial generators, marine generators, containerised generators, super silent, and emergency backup power generators, for all applications including rental fleets, hospital installations, hotels, airports, and communication networks.
Interpower products and services are installed in high profile installations around the world, including government & ministry buildings, critical military applications, and emergency life support roles. We are approved suppliers to several global oil companies, and have emergency generators serving in harsh and hazardous offshore environments.
With over 30 years of export experience, Interpower has emergency backup generators in over 70 countries, and have a proven ability to supply and support products globally through a network of distributors.
Please contact our experienced team of sales engineers, for assistance with generator sizing, installation specific details and information of our stock generators for sale.
