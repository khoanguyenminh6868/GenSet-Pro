Product Description
Huafeng-Perkins Series diesel generator set can be powered by domestic engines or imported ones(Perkins) and forms a complete set with generators famous at home and abroad. The prime power of the set is from 6.5 to 1800kW.
This series has advantages of low fuel consumption, low noise, low emission and IWS.
