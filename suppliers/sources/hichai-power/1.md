About Us
HICHAI POWER is an export brand owned by TAIZHOU KAIHUA DIESEL GENERATOR SETS CO., LTD., established in 2003.
We provide a full range of diesel generator sets from 15 kW to 3000 kW, suitable for land, marine and gas projects.
Since 2003, we focus on stable and high-efficiency power solutions. We cooperate with internationally renowned brands such as CUMMINS, STAMFORD, MARATHON and WEICHAI to guarantee superior product performance and quality.
We possess strong technical strength throughout design to shipment, supported by professional after-sales service.
Choose HICHAI POWER – your reliable partner for power solutions.
View More
Our greatest strengths lie in our extensive experience of over 20 years since our establishment in 2003. This has enabled us to build a reputation for excellence and reliability. We possess a strong annual production capacity of over 3000 sets, allowing us to meet the demands of both small and large-scale projects promptly. Our products are certified with ISO9001-2008 and other international standards like CE, TLC, CCS, BV, assuring customers of top-notch quality. Moreover, our strategic partnerships with renowned engine OEMs such as Cummins, Perkins, Volvo, MTU, Weichai, and collaborations with leading alternator brands like Stamford, LSA, MECC, Marathon, ensure that our generators deliver outstanding performance and durability.
With rich experience and high-quality products, HiChai Power is your trustworthy choice for power generation solutions.
Our goal is to not only meet but exceed the expectations of our customers, partners, and society at large, leading the way towards a future where power is always available, always dependable.
OUR TEAM
Our team is composed of highly skilled and experienced professionals in the power generation field. Our engineers are proficient in the latest technologies and design principles, ensuring that our generators are at the forefront of innovation. The production team is dedicated to precision and quality, meticulously assembling each unit to meet the highest standards. With a shared passion for excellence and a collaborative spirit, our team is the driving force behind HiChai Power's success and its ability to deliver top-notch power solutions.

Our Service
At HiChai Power, our service is centered around you. We offer comprehensive and customized solutions to address your power needs. Our professional team is dedicated to providing prompt and efficient technical support, ensuring the seamless operation of our power systems. With a focus on reliability and innovation, we strive to exceed your expectations, from installation and maintenance to performance optimization. You can trust HiChai Power to keep your power supply running smoothly and effectively, empowering your business for success.
Pre-Sales Service
Our professional team engages with clients to understand their unique power requirements. We offer detailed consultations, providing expert advice on the most suitable power solutions. Through on-site surveys and in-depth discussions, we ensure that customers make well-informed decisions, tailoring our offerings to match their specific operational and budgetary needs.

Once an order is placed, we maintain close communication with the client. Our efficient logistics ensure timely delivery of products. We also provide installation guidance and support, coordinating with on-site teams to guarantee a smooth and proper setup. After-Sales Service
HiChai Power is committed to long-term customer satisfaction.
