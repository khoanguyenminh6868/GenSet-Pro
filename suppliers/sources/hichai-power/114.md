Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)
Essential information regarding Baudouin generator specifications, operational ratings, and enclosure configurations.
What is the distinction between Standby and Prime kVA?
Prime Power (PRP) represents the continuous power available under variable loads for an unlimited number of annual operating hours. Standby Power (ESP) represents the maximum power output suitable for the duration of a utility power outage with no overload capability.
What electrical standard do these Baudouin units support?
All generator units in this catalog operate under 400V, 3-phase, 1500 RPM, and 50Hz frequency standards, making them fully compliant with industrial grids across Europe, Asia, Africa, and the Middle East.
What alternator brands and series are installed?
The sets are paired with genuine, high-efficiency alternators including Stamford TAL and S-Series models, as well as Leroy-Somer (LSA series) brushless alternators designed for optimal transient load response and voltage stability.
What is the difference between Open Type and Soundproof Canopies?
Open Type sets are engineered for dedicated, indoor plant rooms with built-in ventilation. Soundproof Type enclosures (and 20-foot ISO HQ containers for higher kVA models) offer weather protection, acoustic attenuation, and outdoor installation capability.
How can I get the full technical datasheet for my project?
Direct PDF datasheets can be downloaded by clicking the "Download" links beside each model number in the table. Each document contains exact fuel consumption, wiring schematics, and cooling airflow parameters.
Are Baudouin generators suitable for continuous heavy duty cycles?
Yes, Baudouin marine-heritage diesel engines are engineered for demanding continuous operations, offering proven durability, long overhaul intervals, and high resistance to adverse ambient operating temperatures.
