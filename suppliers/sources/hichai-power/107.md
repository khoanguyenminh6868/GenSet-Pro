5.How often do Deutz diesel generators require maintenance?
Regular maintenance is recommended every 250-500 hours of operation, depending on usage and operating conditions. Key maintenance tasks include oil and filter changes, coolant checks, and inspections of fuel and air systems to ensure optimal performance and longevity.
