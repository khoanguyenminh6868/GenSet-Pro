Company Profile
Anpu Heavy Industry (Shandong) Co., Ltd.
APP Heavy Industry (Shandong) Co., Ltd. is a backbone enterprise in the national machinery industry, with over 10 years of experience in generator set production. It is a AAA-level credit enterprise in Shandong Province. Main products include diesel generator sets, generators, electrical accessories, power distribution control panels, and construction machinery. Diesel generator sets are our leading product. In recent years, our annual production and sales of various diesel generator sets have consistently ranked among the top in the city's industrial production summary and evaluation.
Our company boasts advanced production processes, sophisticated equipment, strong technical capabilities, and comprehensive testing methods. Our diesel generator sets are primarily powered by well-known domestic and international manufacturers (Weichai Power, Yuchai Power, Chongqing Panggu (Chongqing Kok), Shanghai Diesel Engine, Sino-US joint venture Chongqing Cummins, Dongfeng Cummins, Volvo, Kubota, Sino-US joint venture Shanghai Marathon, Lanzhou Electric Machinery Plant, Wuxi Xingnuo, Wuxi Faraday Future, Yangjiang Yingge, Quanyi Electric, Aerospace Kaiweis, Anhui Deco, Sino-French joint venture Leroy-Somer, Sino-US joint venture Wuxi Stamford, Sino-German joint venture Wuxi Siemens, etc.). Production is organized according to the advanced product standard ISO 8528-1993, and the ISO 9002 quality management system is strictly implemented. In 2018, the company successfully transitioned to ISO 2000 certification, receiving unanimous praise from experts. Products are distributed throughout China and Southeast Asia. The company has been designated as a qualified supplier of diesel generator sets by units such as the Jinwen Railway, the Beijing-Kowloon Railway, and the Nanjing Military Region Naval Training Regiment. It has also established good cooperative relationships with the North China Oilfield, Jiangsu Oilfield, railway, and highway departments. In 2019, our factory supplied over 100 sets of 24KW-500KW diesel generator sets for the Beijing-Kowloon Railway, a key national project, totaling over 10 million yuan. In 2022, the Jinwen Railway (Zhejiang section) purchased over 110 sets of our diesel generator sets, totaling over 11 million yuan. In 2023, during the Qinghai-Tibet Railway project, our company's generator sets, ranging from 75KW to 500KW, played a significant role in supporting the development of western China, with over a thousand sets purchased, valued at over 10 million yuan. During the nationwide power rationing in 2020 and 2021, our company's generator sets were a major source of power and energy.
Our company has over 80 employees, including 10 administrative staff, 15 professional technical research personnel, and 15 maintenance and service personnel. Annual production capacity reaches over 3,000 sets of medium-to-high-end generator sets.
Our company produces a full range of diesel generator sets with diverse functions. We have accumulated rich experience in design, manufacturing, and commissioning; they are ideal power sources for factories, mines, institutions, telecommunications companies, railways, oil fields, airports, hospitals, buildings, and more, providing mobile or fixed power and lighting.
Our company adheres to the business philosophy of "Integrity and Trustworthiness, Benefiting the World," and producing responsible products for our users is the unremitting pursuit of all Yinjiang employees.
We sincerely welcome customers to come and discuss business. Our products are your ideal choice.
