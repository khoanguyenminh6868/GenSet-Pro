ONIS VISA Generating Sets – Power Generators Manufacturer Since 1960
Reliable Generating Sets for Every Industrial Sector
Founded in 1960, Visa SpA is one of the leading Italian manufacturers of power generators , with an impressive output of over 5,500 units produced annually and distributed in more than 100 countries . The ONIS VISA range ensures reliability, durability and flexibility for both emergency backup and continuous power supply .
Our industrial generators are fully customizable to meet the critical needs of various sectors: telecommunications , agriculture , construction , events , data centers and healthcare . Every model is Made in Italy with Lean Manufacturing processes and rigorous quality controls.
With over 60 years of expertise, we provide integrated energy solutions: dewatering pumps , BESS (Battery Energy Storage Systems) , lighting towers and global technical support to ensure optimal performance worldwide .

Visa SpA – Italian Manufacturer of Generating Sets, Power Generators and Energy Storage Systems
With over 60 years of experience , Visa SpA is one of the leading global manufacturers of power generators, dewatering motor pumps and battery energy storage systems (BESS) . Thanks to a network of distributors in more than 100 countries worldwide , we deliver reliable power solutions for every industry and application.
Made in Italy Power Generators
Our wide range of ONIS VISA generating sets covers 9 to 3000 kVA , fully designed and manufactured in Italy according to the highest standards of quality, efficiency and durability . Whether for emergency backup, continuous power supply or special projects , our robust and high-performance generators ensure reliable energy in all sectors.
Innovation and Tailored Energy Solutions
Since 1960 , Visa SpA has been a pioneer in the power generation industry , combining decades of expertise with cutting-edge engineering know-how . Our dedicated R&D team develops customized energy systems and advanced solutions for complex applications, ensuring maximum performance and reliable after-sales support .
With proven expertise and Made in Italy excellence , Visa SpA is the trusted partner for businesses worldwide looking for reliability, innovation and high-quality power generators .

Visa Rent - Hire of Generating sets
Visa Rent , "HIRE THE POWER" providing a fleet of 300 recently assembled, sound-attenuated generators, offers a wide range of flexible solutions for the international markets. The units are equipped with synch panels that can be paralleled to reach up to 30 MW. All the gensets are fitted with safety features that comply with the current regulations. In addition to the standard versions, Visa Rent provides units with additional fuel tanks to grant high autonomy, special voltages and frequencies, transformers, resistive loads and power cables for connections.

Nettuno - Irrigation solutions
The recently renewed and evolving Nettuno® Water Division is the result of over 30 years of experience in the irrigation sector manufacturing high quality industrial and agricultural motor pumps and hose reels . Motor pump units for irrigation and pumping of water or fluids can be customized and suited to any liquid or fluid handling needs, even those with solid parts in suspension.
