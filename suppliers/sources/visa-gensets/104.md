DCP Generators Rating
The DCP power rating classification for generator sets is used specifically when selecting a machine for installation at a Data Center facility. According to the Uptime Institute, a Data Center falling within their Tier 3 and Tier 4 categories must have an uninterruptible, independent and dedicated power source. These generators must therefore be available for an unlimited number of operating hours, without limitations on the variable or non-variable average load factor. These generator set classifications must be verified and approved by the Uptime Institute. By using machines in data centers that meet these requirements, it is possible to obtain site certification through the certification issued by the Uptime Institute.

Data Center Power (DCP) declaration
To meet the needs of this market, Visa SpA collaborates with the leading engine manufacturers, who have drawn up a new power rating classification called DCP - Data Center Power (formerly DCC). Both use the same power level according to their PRP ISO 8528-1:018 declaration, but in this case they allow continuous operation with an average load factor of 100%. The new DCP power rating can only be used in data centers in countries with a reliable power grid. Visa SpA therefore offers generator sets corresponding to the highest Uptime Institute classification, Tier III and Tier IV.
