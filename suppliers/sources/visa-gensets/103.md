MOBILE GENERATORS - VM TAILOR MADE (300-650 kVA)
Visa SpA has always focused its attention on the customers’ needs, tailoring production to their requests. Onis Visa generators, with their many variants, are among the most versatile and complete in the market. In this range we focus on custom applications.
Video :
VERSATILE AND RELIABLE SOLUTIONS FOR HIGH-LEVEL POWER PERFORMANCE
We analyze the requirements of each particular client and examine in detail all the aspects of installation (atmospheric conditions, demand, etc.) in order to offer a custom-made power generation solution with optimal performance and functionality.
Our turn-key approach means that we develop all the engineering aspects of the project. Beside designing the genset that corresponds to the customer’s requirements, we also manage the whole project implementation by providing specific support for the logistical aspects and co-operating with customer’s technical team to ensure the best outcome.

STAGE V ENGINES
The VM TAILOR MADE Range is designed in collaboration with the engines manufacturers . The new Stage V engines include a new after-treatment system to guarantee an emissions reduction that is in compliance with EU Regulation 2016/1628 for non-road mobile machinery (NRMM). This means a qualitative leap of stage V engines ensuring a reduction up to 90% of emissions if compared to the previous technology.
LOW CO2 EMISSIONS
All generators manufactured comply with the latest EU standards for exhaust emissions and have high heat exchange performance.
