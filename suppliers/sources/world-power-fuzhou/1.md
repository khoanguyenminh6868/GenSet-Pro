Fuzhou World Power Generation CO.,Ltd (WD POWER) is one of leading professional manufacturer of diesel generators located in China.Supply a comprehensive range of diesel generator sets with outputs ranging from 10kVA to 3000kVA.
Whether it is powering a hospital, residential,hotel, construction or any other industrial business, WD POWER is your full service generator supplier. With the ability to customize the systems needed to meet power generation needs for its customers, WD POWER provides worry-free power year after year. In addition to carrying the premium line of generators, WD POWER can supply a product that is specifically packaged to its customers needs.
