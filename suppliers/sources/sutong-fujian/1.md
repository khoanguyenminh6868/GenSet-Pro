Company Profile
Fujian Sutong Power System Co., Ltd. is committed to the manufacture, R&D, sales and distribution of of diesel generator sets, mobile lighting towers and related spare parts. For decades, we have rich experience in the design, manufacture and maintenance of diesel generator sets and light towers, serving a wide range of industries
