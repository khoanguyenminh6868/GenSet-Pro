RATING DEFINITIONS                                                                                         ``

Prime Power

These ratings are applicable for    supplying continuous electrical power (at   variable load) in lieu of

commercially purchased power.       There is no limitation to the annual hours  of operation and these models

can supply 10% overload power       for 1 hour in 12 hours.

Standby Power

These ratings are applicable for    supplying continuous electrical power (at   variable load) in the event of a

utility power failure. No overload  is permitted on these ratings.
