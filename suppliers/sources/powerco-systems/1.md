Power When You Need It Most
Power Generation Solutions To Meet Your Needs
PowerCo Systems Ltd are independent British manufacturer of a comprehensive range of diesel generator sets with outputs from 5kVA to 3000kVA, 50Hz and 60Hz. Powered by internationally accepted engines such as Perkins, Cummins, and Volvo, coupled to Mecc Alte (UK) Ltd, Cummins Generator Technologies, and Leroy Somer alternators.
Based in the United Kingdom, our team have been manufacturing generator sets since 1995. We pride ourselves on using as many British-sourced components as possible. Our controllers are sourced from Deep Sea Electronics, whose factory is located just a few miles from ours. Perkins engines are manufactured in the UK, Mecc Alte (UK) now manufactures in the UK, and we only use UK-manufactured Newage alternators. A large part of the Cummins range we use is from their UK factories.
All other major components are sourced in the EU or North America to ensure dependable power in any environment.
