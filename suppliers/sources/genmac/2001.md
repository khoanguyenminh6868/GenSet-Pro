The high reliability of GENMAC industrial generating sets
The range of generating sets with diesel engine from 10 to 2000kVA manufactured by Genmac are designed for continuous use, both for production and for emergency use, and are suitable for all applications requiring maximum reliability and durability in any weather condition.
Genmac diesel generating sets are available with different engines brands (Perkins, FPT Iveco, Cummins, John Deere, Deutz, Volvo, Yanmar, Kohler, GM) and water cooled, Mecc Alte, Stamford alternator, and Deepsea and Comap controller.
Genmac generators are also available with equal power, mounting engine in accordance with European Stage 3A emission regulations.
The INDUSTRIAL power generator range also includes a Gas-powered series (Natural Gas/LPG) from 30 to 100kVA and a series with air cooled engine from 30 to 100kVA.
Genmac Industrial range diesel generating sets are used worldwide in different application fields and supply current to Hospitals, Industries, Shopping centres, Hotels, Hen houses, Data centres, Mills, Airports, Fish farms, Events, Resorts, GSM towers, Banks, Universities, Stadiums, and Offices.
