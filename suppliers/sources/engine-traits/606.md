### 1975

Isuzu Motors America, Inc. is established, selling Isuzu engines in the U.S.A.

Isuzu Motors Limited, our parent company, receives an award from the Society of Automotive Engineers of Japan, Inc. (JSAE) for the 6BB1 engine, recognized for its groundbreaking direct-injection technology and cold-start capability — innovations that set new standards in diesel efficiency. The 6BB1 would go on to power a wide range of off-road equipment, including compact loaders, forklifts, generators, and agricultural machinery. This helped solidify Isuzu's product reputation for reliability in demanding industrial environments.

![](https://www.isuzuengines.com/images/1975-engine-6BB1.jpg)

### 1976

Isuzu Motors Limited signs a master distribution agreement with the corporation that later becomes Isuzu Diesel North America, Inc. and ultimately the Isuzu PowerTrain Division for the U.S. and Canadian markets.

![](https://www.isuzuengines.com/images/IsuzuDieselMidwest.jpg)

Isuzu Diesel Midwest is appointed as the first authorized Isuzu engine distributor, establishing coverage in the Midwest. This milestone marked the brand's entry into agricultural and industrial markets. With deep expertise in these sectors, the company played a key role in accelerating regional adoption of Isuzu engines and strengthening customer support.

### 1977

M&L Engine, Inc. joins the network as an authorized Isuzu engine distributor, extending coverage into the Gulf Coast. With expertise in industrial and marine applications, the company helped accelerate adoption of Isuzu engines in the region. This strategic addition reinforced the Isuzu commitment to territory-specific service and customer responsiveness.

![](https://www.isuzuengines.com/images/1977-m_l-engine-logo.png)

### 1981

Husker Power Products, Inc. joins the network as an authorized Isuzu engine distributor to serve Nebraska and the Central Plains. Specializing in irrigation and farming operations, the company enhanced support for agricultural customers. This key appointment underscored the Isuzu dedication to durable, application-specific diesel solutions.

![](https://www.isuzuengines.com/images/1981-husker-power-products-logo.png)

Isuzu Motors Limited, our parent company, introduces the world's first ceramic glow plug which earned a Society of Automotive Engineers of Japan, Inc. (JSAE) award for its zero-preheat capability. This innovation eliminated preheat delays and significantly improved cold-start performance — a breakthrough that strengthened engine reliability in off-road and cold-climate industrial applications.

![](https://www.isuzuengines.com/images/1981-2010-JSAE-Logo.png)

Isuzu Motors Limited introduces the 4BC2 engine—the world's first direct-injection diesel for light-duty trucks. This pioneering technology redefined expectations for fuel efficiency and performance in compact diesel engines. Its successful adaptation for off-road use became a catalyst for Isuzu engines expanding into industrial and off-highway markets, shaping the durable, high-performance diesel platforms that define the brand today.

![](https://www.isuzuengines.com/images/1981-engine-4BC2.png)

![](https://www.isuzuengines.com/images/3KC1.jpg)

### 1983

Isuzu Motors Limited introduces the K-Series engine—the company's first diesel platform designed exclusively for industrial use. This strategic shift away from on-highway applications marked the beginning of a dedicated industrial product line. The K-Series laid the groundwork for the 4L-Series engines distributed today, reinforcing the Isuzu commitment to purpose-built solutions that deliver durability, reliability, and performance across off-road and stationary markets.

### 1984

Hamilton Engine Sales joins the network as an authorized Isuzu engine distributor, expanding coverage into the Pacific Northwest. With strong regional industrial experience, Hamilton contributed to building a nationwide distribution network. This strategic selection reflected the focus on trusted local service and long-term growth.

![](https://www.isuzuengines.com/images/Hamilton_logo_1984.jpg)

Isuzu Motors Limited debuts the 6RA1TC engine—the world's first diesel engine with an electronically-controlled governor. Originally developed for heavy-duty vehicle applications in Japan, this breakthrough in fuel injection technology revolutionized engine responsiveness and fuel management. The innovation set a new benchmark for control systems and influenced future industrial engine designs, reinforcing Isuzu's reputation for engineering leadership.

![](https://www.isuzuengines.com/images/6RB1engine.bmp)

### 1990

Westquip Diesel Sales joins the network as an authorized Isuzu engine distributor, expanding coverage into Western Canada. With expertise in industrial and power generation applications, Westquip strengthened regional support and broadened network reach. This key move supported reliable coverage across North America.

![](https://www.isuzuengines.com/images/1990-westquip-logo.png)

Isuzu Motors Limited introduces the 4H and 6H engine platforms—initially developed for vehicle applications. These advanced platforms featured gear-driven overhead cams, high-pressure fuel systems, and turbocharging technologies that set new standards for performance and reliability. Their successful adaptation for off-road and stationary use laid the foundation for today's industrial engine product line, reinforcing the Isuzu commitment to durable, emissions-compliant diesel solutions.

![](https://www.isuzuengines.com/images/8146DV_4KH.jpg)

![](https://www.isuzuengines.com/images/1991-mack-boring-logo.png)

### 1991

Mack Boring & Parts Company joins the network as an authorized Isuzu engine distributor, expanding coverage into the Northeast and marine sectors. This appointment expanded reach into coastal markets and specialized applications, accelerating adoption of Isuzu engines in the region. This key addition reinforced the Isuzu commitment to reliable performance in demanding environments.

### 1992

Isuzu Motors Limited introduces the 6WA1TC engine—a turbocharged diesel platform engineered for high-demand generator and other off-road applications. Originally designed for heavy-duty cargo vehicles, the 6WA1TC introduced advanced technologies including variable geometry turbocharging and high-pressure fuel control. Its robust performance and emissions-conscious design helped shape future industrial power solutions, influencing the development of future generator-ready platforms.

### 1994

Isuzu Diesel North America merges into Isuzu Motors America, Inc., forming the Isuzu PowerTrain (ISZAPT) Division, also known as Isuzu Diesel. This organizational shift streamlined operations and enhanced market agility. The newly formed ISZAPT Division became the central hub for off-road and stationary engine support, reinforcing the brand's commitment to reliable, regionally supported diesel solutions.

![](https://www.isuzuengines.com/images/IsuzuDiesel.png)

![](https://www.isuzuengines.com/images/1996-engine-6HK1TC.png)

### 1996

Isuzu Motors Limited introduces the 6HK1TC engine, marking a pivotal advancement in diesel technology with the adoption of common-rail fuel injection and turbo-intercooling. Initially developed for on-highway use, this electronically controlled platform laid the foundation for future off-road and stationary engines. Isuzu Diesel supported the integration of this engine architecture into industrial applications—including excavators, wheel loaders, large air compressors, generators, and pumps—contributing to the evolution of Tier 3-compliant solutions and reinforcing the brand's reputation for clean, efficient, and durable power across diverse sectors.

### 2000

Isuzu Motors Limited introduces the 4HL1 hybrid diesel engine, representing an early advancement in fuel-efficient, low-emission diesel technology. Featuring a parallel hybrid system, the 4HL1 achieved up to a 35% improvement in fuel economy and demonstrated the viability of hybridization within commercial diesel platforms. Though first introduced in a commercial context, the 4HL1's engineering principles—particularly its emphasis on emissions control and fuel efficiency—directly influenced the development of the H-Series industrial engines, including the 4HK1. This milestone is a precursor to the clean, electronically controlled engines that would later serve construction and stationary equipment applications, aligning with evolving emissions standards.

![](https://www.isuzuengines.com/images/2000-engine-4HK1.jpg)

![](https://www.isuzuengines.com/images/ADF_Diesel.jpg)

### 2003

ADF Diesel, Inc. joins the network as an authorized Isuzu engine distributor, expanding coverage into Eastern Canada. With strengths in construction, power generation, and industrial applications, ADF brought localized expertise and market reach. This strategic appointment reinforced the Isuzu commitment to reliable regional support.

![](https://www.isuzuengines.com/images/2003-company-building.jpg)

Isuzu Motors America, Inc. relocates to Plymouth, Michigan.

### 2008

Isuzu Motors America, Inc. was merged into Isuzu Motors America, LLC.

![](https://www.isuzuengines.com/images/IsuzuDiesel.png)

![](https://www.isuzuengines.com/images/2010-engine-4JJ1.jpg)

### 2010

The 4JJ1-powered D-MAX earns top honors for fuel efficiency at the Bangkok Motor Show, showcasing the strength of Isuzu engine technology. While this recognition stems from the automotive sector, the 4JJ1 platform continues to serve as the foundation for Isuzu's off-road industrial engine offerings, meeting stringent emissions standards across global markets.

![](https://www.isuzuengines.com/images/1981-2010-JSAE-Logo.png)

The Isuzu 4HK1 engine receives a JSAE award for achieving compliance with some of the world's most stringent emissions regulations—without relying on Selective Catalytic Reduction (SCR). This innovation, driven by a two-stage turbocharger, highlights the advanced engineering behind the 4HK1 platform, which continues to power Isuzu's off-road industrial engine offerings available through the distributor network.

### 2012

SVF International joins the network as an authorized Isuzu engine reseller, representing Isuzu Diesel's presence beyond North America. Operating in Venezuela and Ecuador, this appointment established an Isuzu presence and ensures continued access to trusted Isuzu power solutions within the region.

![](https://www.isuzuengines.com/images/SVF_logo.jpg)

![](https://www.isuzuengines.com/images/Almacenes_logo.png)

### 2014

Almacenes Santa Clara S.A. joins the network as an authorized Isuzu engine reseller in Peru. This appointment provides reliable access to trusted Isuzu power solutions.

### 2015

Isuzu Diesel Mid Canada joins the network as an authorized Isuzu engine distributor, expanding coverage into the province of Manitoba. With a focus on industrial customers across the region, the company helped drive engine adoption and customer-focused service. This targeted appointment effectively met the needs of customers in Manitoba.

![](https://www.isuzuengines.com/images/Isuzu_Diesel_Mid_Canada_logo_1.jpg)

![](https://www.isuzuengines.com/images/2017-technicians.png)

### 2017

Isuzu Diesel opens the Wixom Technical Facility, centralizing engineering, product validation, and application support for off-road industrial engines. This dedicated space enhances OEM integration capabilities and lays the groundwork for expanded service and aftersales support in the years to follow.

### 2022

Hamilton Engine Sales rebrands as Hamilton Power Solutions, continuing its legacy within the Isuzu engine authorized distributor network in the Pacific Northwest and beyond. The new name reflects a broader commitment to power solutions while maintaining dedication to industrial customers. This evolution highlights long-term distributor relationships and enduring regional support.

Isuzu Diesel introduces a new generation of pre-validated, genset-ready power units, beginning with the 4JJ1X model and later expanding to include the 4HK1X and 6HK1X. Designed specifically for generator applications, these units are up to 100% validated and tested in defined configurations, helping OEMs reduce engineering time and costs while accelerating time to market with proven diesel solutions.

![](https://www.isuzuengines.com/images/Hamilton_Power_Solutions_logo_current.jpg)

![](https://www.isuzuengines.com/images/2024-engines.png)

### 2024

Isuzu Diesel expands its genset-ready power unit lineup with the introduction of the 4LE2T and 4LE2X models. These compact, Tier 4 Final-certified units are 100% validated and tested in defined configurations, with over 90% validation on optional setups. Designed for generator applications, they offer OEMs a reliable, pre-engineered solution for seamless integration.

Emergency standby offerings featuring the 2.2L 4LE1T and 4LE2N engines are introduced. Manufactured by Isuzu Motors Limited, these engines are available through Isuzu Diesel both as standalone products and as pre-configured power units. Designed for critical infrastructure applications, these EPA-certified solutions deliver compact reliability and simplified integration for emergency generator systems.

### 2025

Isuzu Diesel celebrates 50 years of Isuzu engines powering the U.S.A.

![](https://www.isuzuengines.com/images/50-years-Isuzu-logo.svg)

Isuzu Diesel introduces the 4J-series compact power unit, expanding the versatility of its industrial engine lineup. Engineered for space-sensitive applications, the 4J-series reflects Isuzu's commitment to innovation, reliability, and customer-focused design. This power unit was awarded with the nationally recognized **_RentalTM_ Editor's Choice Award**, which celebrates the innovation of new and updated products available for equipment rental businesses.

![](https://www.isuzuengines.com/images/2025_4J_Compact_Power_Unit_with_overlay.png)

Isuzu engines mark 50 years throughout North America - a legacy that reflects five decades of innovation, reliability and engineering that puts the customer first.
