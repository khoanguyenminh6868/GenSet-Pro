SDEC is the technical leader in China's internal combustion engine industry. We have developed an extensive product lineup to complement our diesel engines and other main products. Our highest priority is on the customization of products and we are committed to delivering on all customer needs through quality products with outstanding consistency.
When it comes to product development, our objectives have revolved around attaining the highest technical performance while retaining cost effectiveness for our end customer. Our commitment to excellence extends to our industry leading after-sales service and support programs. The spirit of transparency and cooperation is seen in each stage of operation at SDEC. When combined, we provide customers with the highest quality products that are in strict accordance with all of the relevant requirements and specifications and they are maintained through thoughtful services that are sure to deliver on customer satisfaction.
