Rating definitions and conditions

— Continuous power ratings apply to installations where the
   generator set serves as utility. At constant load, the number of
   generator set operating hours is unlimited. A 10% overload
   capacity is available for one hour in twelve. Ratings are in
   accordance with ISO 8528-1, ISO 3046-1, BS 5514 and AS 2789.
   Average load factor: ≤ 100%. Operating hours/year: unlimited.
