6-Cylinder Engine

# TCD 7.8 L6

## Key Features

Water-cooled 6-cylinder inline engine with turbocharging, charge air cooling and cooled external exhaust gas recirculation.

- Engine and exhaust aftertreatment (EAT) are optimised for **highest total system efficiency** and thus ensure lowest consumption and total operating cost.
- The **powerful DEUTZ Common Rail (DCR®) injection system** and the electronic engine control (EMR) with intelligent link to the drive management ensure **optimum engine performance at low fuel consumption**.
- The engines meet the requirements of the EU Stage IV and US EPA Tier 4 with DVERT® selective catalytic reduction (SCR) and particulate filter (DPF). Through the use of the DPF they already comply with the EU Stage V emissions standard expected from 2019.
