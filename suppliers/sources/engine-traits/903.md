## Top benefits of Volvo Penta variable speed marine generator sets

The basic principle is simple: With multiple variable speed gensets connected in a DC grid, you only run the number of engines that the situation demands, whether it’s the power of one genset for low-speed tramming or all gensets at once for full workload.

### Operates at peak efficiency

Each engine operates at peak efficiency without exceeding the optimum rpms for torque and fuel consumption. When more power is needed, the next engine is automatically engaged.

### Extended vessel longevity

With each genset operating part-time and always at optimal load, the vessel’s longevity can be extended, since each engine's running hours are lower than the system’s total running hours.

### Optimized for maximum uptime

The redundant engine setup can be configured to optimize each engine’s service intervals for maximum uptime. In case of service or breakdown of an engine, the other gensets provide enough power to operate the vessel.

### Flexible installation

Volvo Penta’s gensets are compact-sized and can be installed anywhere, independent of the propulsion’s location. This flexible, versatile solution is easy to fit in most types of vessels, regardless of hull design or size.
