## **The Volvo Penta D13 power generation engine is an in-line 6-cylinder, 12.8-liter genset engine with electronic high-pressure fuel injection, overhead camshaft, turbo, and air-cooled exhaust manifold.**

Robust design

### Reliable & powerful

Volvo Penta’s D13 power generation engine is designed for optimal uptime and efficiency. The high performance of this engine is due to its rigid cylinder block, durable crankshaft bearings, air-cooled exhaust manifold, and electronic wastegate turbo.

Enhance performance

### Electronic control system

The D13 power generation engine is equipped with an Engine Management System (EMS), an electronically controlled processing system that optimizes engine performance. It also includes advanced facilities for diagnostics and fault tracing.

EPA Tier 2

### Emissions, fuel and noise

Due to its durable design, the well-balanced D13 power generation engine reduces vibrations and noise, and meets emission control standards. With its advanced injection and charging system and optimized internal efficiency, this genset engine promotes efficient combustion and reduces fuel consumption.

Extended service life

### Enhanced durability

For enhanced durability and extended service life, the D13 power generation engine is equipped with replaceable cylinder liners and valve seats/guides. All service and maintenance points are designed to be easily accessible, even in tight compartments.
