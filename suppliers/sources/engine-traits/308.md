1200 Series
The 1200 Series industrial and electric power engines are designed to operate around the clock, in whatever environmental conditions you choose. They are packed with the latest technology—delivering reduced complexity and cost—whilst also providing the opportunity to downsize and improve productivity. The 1200J products meet EU Stage V/U.S. EPA Tier 4 Final emission standards.
Buy Perkins ® 1200 Series parts today
The Perkins 1200 Series is a modern, high-performance range of 4- and 6-cylinder diesel engines designed for industrial, agricultural, and electric power applications. With displacements from 4.4L to 7.01L and power outputs from 70 kW to 239 kW (93–320 HP), these engines meet the latest global emissions standards including EU Stage V and U.S. EPA Tier 4 Final.
