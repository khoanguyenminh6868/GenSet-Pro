General information
The S12R-PTA engine is designed in Japan and manufactured in France, delivering premium levels of performance, durability and reliability. The engine uses proprietary MHIET mechanical fuel system and turbocharger technology, ensuring economical fuel consumption and maximized power output for a minimized installation footprint. Despite its compact size, the engine layout has been designed to provide easy inspection and maintenance. This strength and reliability are ideally suited for data center, commercial and municipal buildings, manufacturing plant and distributed power applications.
Benefits
Compact in size
Economical fuel consumption
MHI-ET -designed and -manufactured fuel system and turbocharger
Engine design and components layout allows for easy inspection and maintenance
Maintenance activities require a low number of specialised tools
Commonality of parts across the MHIET engine models, simplifies spare part management
