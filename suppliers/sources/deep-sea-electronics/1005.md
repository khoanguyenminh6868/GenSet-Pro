Overview
The DSE8660 MKII is an easy-to-use single or multi-mains (utility) controller with automatic transfer switch capability. Designed to synchronise a
single or multiple DSE8610 MKIIs with single or multiple mains (utility) supplies, the DSE8660 MKII will automatically control the change over from
mains (utility) to generator supply or run generators in sync with the mains (utility) supply to provide no-break, peak lopping and peak shaving power
solutions.
The module can indicate operational status and fault conditions on the LCD screen (multiple languages available), by illuminated LED, audible sounder
and SMS messaging. Comprehensive communications are also available via RS232, RS485 & Ethernet for remote PC control and monitoring to allow
integration into building management systems. The comprehensive event log will record up to 250 events to facilitate maintenance.
An extensive number of fixed and flexible monitoring and protection features are included. Easy alteration of the sequences, timers and alarms can be
made using the DSE PC Configuration Suite Software. Selected configuration is also available via the module’s front panel. With all communication
ports capable of being active at the same time, the DSE86XX MKII series is ideal for a wide variety of demanding load share applications.
