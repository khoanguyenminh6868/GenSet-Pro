APPLICATIONS
We design and manufacture alternators for a wide variety of applications
ALTERNATORS FOR POWER GENERATION
Linz Electric offers high-quality alternators designed for continuous and standby use in the most diverse application areas and environmental conditions. Trust our products to generate electricity reliably.
ALTERNATORS ENABLING THE ENERGY TRANSITION
Discover the best solutions to support the transition to clean and renewable energy. Products specifically designed to be integrated into hybrid systems, microgrids, cogeneration, and biomass systems and to guarantee the highest level of efficiency of the systems.
SPECIAL ALTERNATORS
Linz Electric is a reliable and trusted partner that collaborates with its customers in the design and customization of alternators to obtain the best performance in special industrial applications. Our special alternators are designed to power specific loads on machinery and systems used in the most varied applications.
