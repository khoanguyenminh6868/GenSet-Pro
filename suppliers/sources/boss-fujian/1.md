Introduction
Boss Electrical Machinery Co.,Ltd focuses on Generator and generating set manufacturing, marketing and services.The company is located in Fuzhou City,Fujian Province,China.
BOSS POWERS can meet all kinds of emergency and common power solutions.Products include Stamford Brushless Alternator, ST/STC Brush Generator ,diesel generator sets and so on. Generator sets include open type, silent type, container, trailer type and so on, power covering 3kW-2000kW.
BOSS Products with excellent reliability,safety and durability can meet many condition use. They are exported to Southeast Asia, Middle East, Europe and other areas. We provide the systematic technical support for each customers different needs to seek the best solution.
Our products passed a comprehensive testing , which are in full compliance with industry standards at national and international,got ISO9001, CIQ,CE certification, etc.
