Product Overview
The Perkins 1500 series achieves a perfect balance between low ownership cost, world-class power density, and reliable performance for power demand users. These engines feature a proven core and robust fuel system, delivering superior performance in high-altitude and high-temperature environments, making them an ideal solution for customers operating in harsh climate conditions in unregulated areas.
Performance Overview
1. More Powerful Features
A major feature of this series is that users can easily switch between power supply frequencies of 50-60 Hz. For example, if you run a rental company shipping from Lebanon to Saudi Arabia, you can simply press a switch to meet local standards. No need to contact technicians, making work easier and simpler for you and your operators.
All engines in the 1500 series come with a one-year (unlimited hours) or two-year (up to 500 hours per year) power warranty. Our well-trained global dealer network also provides strong support for your engine, ensuring smooth operation anytime and anywhere.
2. Lower Operating Costs
The 1500 series is specially designed to offer lower ownership costs, easy maintenance, and reduced downtime. The standard oil change interval is 500 hours. Compared to previous models, its outstanding advantage is fuel savings of up to 7%. This engine can significantly reduce ownership and operating costs. In fact, fuel savings at this level over five to seven years are equivalent to the entire cost of the engine.
3. Reliable and Excellent Performance
Our 1500 series engines use a Hydraulic Actuated Unit Injection (HEUI) fuel system to provide world-class power density. This allows the engine to effectively handle variations in fuel quality worldwide and deliver strong performance in any region. These engines are specially designed to provide excellent power even under extreme climate conditions and have demonstrated outstanding performance beyond expectations in environmental tests. Additionally, they offer up to 8% optimization in high-altitude environments, ensuring long-lasting and reliable operation.
