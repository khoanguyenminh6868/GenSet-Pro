Product Overview
Our 1103 series power engines offer an affordable power solution for primary and backup power generation. These engines comply with the EU Stage IIIA/US EPA Tier 3 equivalent emission standards. The compact 3-cylinder 3.3-liter engine provides excellent fuel economy for primary power applications and demonstrates high load endurance in backup power applications.
Performance Overview
1. Easy Maintenance
This series of engines uses naturally aspirated or turbocharged types, providing you with the extra power you need. The engine is equipped with a mechanical fuel injection system, with a maintenance interval of 500 hours, making it easy to maintain.
2. Smooth Transition
Additionally, this series shares common mounting parts with other models in the 1100 series, so when you need to upgrade your application for more power, a smooth transition is easily achievable.
3. Oil and Fuel Filtration
Our 1103 series engines are equipped with high-quality oil and fuel filters to ensure reliable engine performance and durability.
4. Primary Power Generation
The 1103 series power engines can provide engine power ranging from 28 to 68 kWm and generator output up to 30 to 59 kVA.
