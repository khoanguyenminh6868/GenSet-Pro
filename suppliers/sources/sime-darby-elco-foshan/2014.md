Product Overview
The Perkins 4012 series delivers truly outstanding performance, equipped with 12 cylinders and a 46-liter displacement, providing all the power you need. This series of diesel ElectropaK engines can be chosen with both radiator and air filter included, or engine only. They offer generator outputs ranging from 989 to 1600 kVA, with an excellent power-to-weight ratio. The engines feature a compact design, making them easy to transport and install.
Performance Overview
1. Economy
Our engines are economical, reliable, and durable, featuring outstanding power-to-weight ratio and load capacity, with improved emission capabilities.
Interchangeable parts with other engines in the 4000 series make replacing parts or upgrading easy and simple.
2. ElectropaK Diesel
The ElectropaK diesel engines of the 4012 series come equipped with radiators and air filters. The engine models in this series are available in turbocharged and air-to-air charge cooled, as well as turbocharged and air-to-water charge cooled types.
Our 4012 series diesel engines offer engine power from 1250 to 1880 kVA and can provide models to meet all your primary or standby power generation needs.
Engine-only options are also available.
3. Optional Equipment
Optional features for the 4012 series diesel engines include:
• Fuel cooler integrated into the radiator assembly
• Immersion heater with thermostat
