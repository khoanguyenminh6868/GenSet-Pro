Product Overview
The Perkins 2500 series diesel engines provide you with a rated output of 455-624 kVA, meeting your primary and backup power generation needs. The features and advantages of the 2000 series ensure your power generation requirements are met. The flexible six-cylinder engine packages accommodate your available space, and through mechanically operated unit injectors, electronic controls, and carefully matched turbocharging, our 2500 series delivers performance and economy.
Performance Overview
1. Easy to Transport and Install
With an excellent power-to-weight ratio and a compact design, our 2500 series engines are easy to transport and install, providing you with good maintenance access for easy servicing.
Our ElectropaK 2500 series engines come equipped with radiator and air filter systems; you just need to tighten the engine bolts and connect the engine to start generating power.
2. Optimized Economy
Mechanically operated unit injectors, electronic control, and carefully matched turbocharging mean you get optimal fuel atomization and combustion performance, achieving the best economy and lowest emissions.
These engines provide you with reliable power, low fuel consumption, and low wear rates. With their high compression ratio, they ensure clean and quick starts under any conditions.
