Product Overview
The Perkins® 1700 Series is meticulously designed to provide our customers with top-tier performance and maximum competitive advantage. The 1706 is developed using the latest generation 9.3-liter engine core components, offering our customers more powerful capabilities and greater flexibility through simple plug-and-play products.
Advantages:
Achieves a 31% power increase (compared to previous models)
Achieves a 5% improvement in fluid consumption compared to the previous generation
Highly competitive consumption with a 20% reduction in oil usage
Performance Overview
1. Reliable Power
World-class manufacturing capabilities and processes combined with a proven core engine design ensure reliability, quiet operation, and an exceptionally long, efficient service life.
High power density products that combine reliable power and high efficiency. Reduce acquisition costs while improving performance.
2. Flexibility
Radiators adapted for tropical climate conditions are standard equipment.
3. Low-cost advantage throughout the entire lifecycle
Gas fuel consumption is optimized to meet the needs of primary power and continuous operation applications. No additional fluids or additives are required, reducing operating costs.
V-shaped belts and 500-hour oil change intervals help achieve low-cost maintenance.
