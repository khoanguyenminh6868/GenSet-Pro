Product Overview
Our 1104 ElectropaK engine sets the industry standard for primary and standby power generation applications. The 4-cylinder 4.4-liter engine runs smoothly and quietly. They are also electronically controlled, allowing for efficient fuel combustion based on the load applied to the engine. The engine can deliver power ranging from 43.7 to 126.5 kVA, meeting the needs of major power nodes.
Performance Overview
1. Reliable Operation
The 1104 series engine features low fuel and oil consumption, with maintenance intervals of 500 hours, enabling long-term reliable operation.
2. Easy Installation
Universal front and rear ends, connection points, and components across the entire series mean you can easily install different 1100 series engines in your application.
