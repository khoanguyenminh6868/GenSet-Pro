Product Overview
The 6-cylinder Perkins 4006 series offers the versatility you need in today's power generation market. This series of diesel ElectropaK engines/electronics provides power ranging from 750 to 900 kVA, with optional radiator and air filter. This engine has proven its reliability in primary and backup equipment across all key sectors such as manufacturing plants, hospitals, and data centers. The 4006 engine features an excellent power-to-weight ratio and a compact design, making it easy to transport and install.
Performance Overview
1. Reliable Power
• Independent 4-valve cylinder head optimizes airflow
• Single fuel injector ensures outstanding ultra-fine fuel atomization, enabling controlled rapid combustion
• Parts commonality with other engines in the 4000 series helps reduce inventory
• Meets 1/2 TA Luft (1986) emission standards
2. Low Operating Costs
• Standard oil change maintenance interval of 500 hours
• Specially designed to provide lower ownership costs, easy maintenance, and reduced downtime
• Excellent leading warranty service.
