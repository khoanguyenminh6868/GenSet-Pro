Product Overview
The 6-cylinder 7-liter Perkins 1106 series ElectropaK engine provides all the power you need for power generation. This series of engines shares a common platform and offers options for mechanical and common rail fuel systems, allowing the same platform to be used in different emission regions. The engine is reliable, trustworthy, and cost-effective, capable of handling variations in fuel quality across different parts of the world.
Performance Overview
1. Universal Platform
The 1106 engine provides a universal platform, thereby reducing the impact on your business and applications when switching to products designed for higher regulatory regions.
2. Easy Maintenance
With a maintenance interval set at 500 hours, you will find that the 1106 engine can provide the reliable power you need at any time.
3. Fuel Tolerance
Some options are designed to cope with variations in fuel quality across different regions of the world.
