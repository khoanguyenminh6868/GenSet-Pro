Product Overview
As part of our 2000 series, the 6-cylinder Perkins 2200 series diesel engine is perfectly suited for your 350-500 kVA power generation needs, whether as a primary or backup power source. Features and advantages of the 2000 series include flexible packaging to fit your available space, mechanically operated unit injectors, electronic control, and carefully matched turbocharging. You will find that our 2200 series delivers the performance and economy you require.
Performance Overview
1. Compact Solution
With an excellent power-to-weight ratio and a small, compact design, our 2200 series engines are easy to transport and install, providing good maintenance access for easy servicing.
Our ElectropaK 2200 series engines come equipped with radiator and air filter systems; you just need to tighten the engine bolts and connect the engine to start generating power.
2. Reliability and Efficiency
Our 2200 series engines use electronically controlled mechanical unit injectors and carefully matched turbochargers to provide optimal fuel atomization and combustion performance for the best economy. Electronic control of injection achieves low emissions.
The engine delivers the reliable power you need. Low fuel consumption, low wear rate, and high compression ratio mean you get reliable, clean, and fast starts under all conditions.
