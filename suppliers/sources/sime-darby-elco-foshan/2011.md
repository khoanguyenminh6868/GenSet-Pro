Product Overview
The Perkins 2800 series is a technologically mature 6-cylinder, 18-liter inline diesel engine, specifically designed to meet the stringent demands of today's power generation industry. This series of engines is especially suitable for standby power generation. Developed based on mature heavy-duty industrial engines, these engines offer excellent performance and reliability. The 2806A-E18TTAG is a 6-cylinder diesel engine with a capacity of 18 liters, featuring turbocharging and air-to-air charge cooling technology.
It has advanced features that ensure economical and durable operation, low emissions, and advanced overall performance and reliability.
Performance Overview
1. Economic Power
• Uses electronically controlled mechanical unit injectors combined with carefully matched turbocharging to provide excellent fuel atomization and combustion performance, achieving optimal economy
• Electronic control of fuel injection achieves low emissions
2. Reliable Power
• Developed and tested using the latest engineering technology and finite element analysis to achieve high reliability, low oil consumption, and low wear rate
• High compression ratio also ensures clean and quick starting under any conditions
• Perkins global product support is designed to provide an excellent customer experience for customers using Perkins-powered machines. We achieve this through a high-quality distribution network, extensive global coverage, and various OEM collaboration options supported by Perkins. Therefore, whether you are an end user or equipment manufacturer, our engine expertise is an indispensable asset on your path to success
3. Compact, Clean, and Efficient Power
• Excellent power-to-weight ratio and compact size provide optimal power density, easier installation, and cost-effective transportation
• Carefully designed to provide convenient maintenance access for easy servicing
• After launching low-emission technology specifications, environmental impact during operation can be minimized as much as possible, ensuring compliance with US EPA emission regulations. Models with standard technology specifications offer excellent fuel consumption, thereby maximizing engine efficiency
