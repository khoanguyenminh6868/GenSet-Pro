Product Overview
The 8-cylinder Perkins 4008 series offers the versatility you need in today's power generation market. This series of diesel ElectropaK engines/electronic units provides power ranging from 849 to 1022 kVA, with optional radiator and air filter. This engine has proven its reliability in primary and backup equipment across all key sectors such as manufacturing plants, hospitals, and data centers. The 4008 engine features an excellent power-to-weight ratio and a compact design, making it easy to transport and install.
Performance Overview
1. Equipment Selection
Optional equipment for the 4008 series diesel engines includes:
• High-temperature radiator, including water pipes, clamps, hoses, fan, fan guard, and bolts
• Dual-load air filter — paper element with pre-filter
• Conversion lubricating oil filter
• Conversion fuel filter
• Immersion heater with thermostat
• Air starting device
• Instrument panel
2. Reliable Power
The 4008 series engines provide generator output from 884 to 1250 kVA.
The 8-cylinder engines of the 4000 series and the 4008 series engines can be equipped with cooling systems suitable for tropical or temperate environments, meeting your needs anytime, anywhere.
Our engines are affordable, reliable, and durable, featuring excellent power-to-weight ratio and load capacity, with improved gas emission capabilities as well.
