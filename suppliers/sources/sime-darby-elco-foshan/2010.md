Product Overview
Although the Perkins 2800 series diesel engines are suitable for both prime and standby generators, these engines are especially popular in the standby market. They can provide engine power of 514-652 kWm and generator output of 591-750 kVA, featuring mechanically operated unit injectors, electronic control, and carefully matched turbocharging. Moreover, with flexible kits, the Perkins 2800 series fits any available space within your equipment.
Performance Overview
1. Equipment Selection
Optional features for the 2800 series engines include:
• 110V/240V Immersion Heater
• Additional Speed Sensor
• Instrument Temperature and Pressure Sensors
• Hour Meter Electronic Counter
• Air Filter Rain Cover
• Dual Starter/Second Starter Facility
• Tool Kit
2. Reliability and Efficiency
Our 2800 series engines are equipped with mechanically operated unit injectors, electronic control, and carefully matched turbochargers. They provide excellent fuel atomization and combustion performance as well as good fuel economy. The electronic control of injection also helps reduce emissions.
Our 2800 series engines offer you:
• Reliable Power
• Low Oil Consumption
• Low Wear Rate
• High Compression Ratio Ensures Clean and Quick Starting Under Any Condition
Our ElectropaK 2800 series engines come with radiator and air filter systems; you just need to tighten the engine bolts and connect the engine to start generating power.
With an excellent power-to-weight ratio and compact size, the 2800 series engines are easy to transport and install, providing good maintenance access for easy servicing.
