Baudouin Diesel Generator-PG2000-B
The PG2000-B 2000 kVA Baudouin Diesel Generator is engineered to deliver high-capacity prime and standby power for large industrial facilities, data centers, hospitals and critical infrastructure projects where uninterrupted electricity is essential.
Powered by a high-performance Baudouin diesel engine, this generator ensures excellent fuel efficiency, robust performance and long-term operational reliability for demanding power generation applications.
Manufactured in accordance with ISO and CE standards, the PG2000-B can be configured with soundproof canopy, ATS systems, synchronization panels and advanced digital control systems to meet the requirements of complex power installations.
Baudouin engines are globally recognized for their durability and high-power performance in large-scale generator systems. The PG2000-B offers a dependable solution for facilities requiring reliable high-capacity power generation.
This generator is commonly used as a prime or standby power source for data centers, large manufacturing plants, hospitals, airports and major infrastructure developments.
