Perkins Diesel Generator-PG900-P
The PG900-P 900 kVA Perkins Diesel Generator is engineered for large-scale industrial and commercial power requirements. It is widely used in factories, construction projects, hotels, hospitals, and other high-demand facilities requiring stable and continuous energy supply.
Designed for both prime and standby operation, this generator ensures consistent voltage stability, fuel efficiency, and long operational life. Equipped with a globally trusted Perkins engine, it delivers reliable performance under heavy load conditions.
Manufactured under ISO and CE standards, this model can be customized with synchronization panels, ATS systems, and soundproof canopy options according to project requirements.
Powerpack Generator provides direct export to European markets with fast delivery and professional technical support.
