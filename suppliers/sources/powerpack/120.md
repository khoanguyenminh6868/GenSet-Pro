Baudouin Diesel Generator-PG35-B
The PG35-B 35 kVA Baudouin Diesel Generator is designed to provide dependable standby power for small businesses, offices, houses and telecom systems where continuous electricity is essential.
Powered by a reliable Baudouin diesel engine, this generator delivers efficient fuel consumption, stable performance and low operating costs. Its compact design allows easy installation while ensuring consistent power supply during unexpected outages.
Manufactured in accordance with ISO and CE standards, the PG35-B can be supplied with optional soundproof canopy, ATS systems and advanced digital control panels to meet different application requirements.
Baudouin powered generators are known for their durability and reliable power generation performance. The PG35-B offers an excellent balance of efficiency, reliability and cost-effective operation.
This model is commonly used as a backup generator for small businesses, offices, telecom infrastructure and residential applications.
