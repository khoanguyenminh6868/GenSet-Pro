Yangdong Diesel Generator-PG100-Y
The PG100-Y 100 kVA Yangdong Diesel Generator is designed to provide dependable standby power for small businesses, offices, houses and telecom systems. It is an ideal solution for locations that require reliable electricity during unexpected power interruptions.
Powered by a durable Yangdong diesel engine, this generator delivers efficient fuel consumption, stable performance and low operating costs. Its compact structure allows easy installation even in limited spaces while ensuring consistent power supply.
Manufactured in accordance with ISO and CE standards, the PG100-Y can be supplied with optional soundproof canopy, ATS systems and modern digital control panels for automatic power management.
Yangdong powered generators are known for offering cost-effective power solutions. For businesses looking for an affordable diesel generator without compromising reliability, the PG100-Y provides an excellent balance of price, durability and efficiency.
This model is commonly used as a backup generator for offices, small commercial facilities, telecom infrastructure and residential applications.
