Baudouin Diesel Generator-PG250-B
The PG250-B 250 kVA Baudouin Diesel Generator is designed to provide reliable prime and standby power for factories, commercial buildings, hotels and construction projects where uninterrupted electricity is essential.
Powered by a durable Baudouin diesel engine, this generator ensures efficient fuel consumption, stable performance and long service life for demanding industrial applications.
Manufactured in accordance with ISO and CE standards, the PG250-B can be supplied with optional soundproof canopy, ATS systems and advanced digital control panels to meet different project requirements.
Baudouin engines are widely recognized for their durability and high performance in industrial power generation systems. The PG250-B offers an excellent balance of efficiency, reliability and cost-effective operation.
This model is commonly used as a backup generator for factories, hotels, commercial facilities and construction sites requiring dependable standby power.
