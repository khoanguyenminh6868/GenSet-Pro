Baudouin Diesel Generator-PG500-B
The PG500-B 500 kVA Baudouin Diesel Generator is engineered to deliver dependable prime and standby power for large industrial facilities, hospitals, commercial complexes and major construction projects where continuous electricity is essential.
Powered by a high-performance Baudouin diesel engine, this generator ensures efficient fuel consumption, stable operation and long-term reliability for demanding industrial power applications.
Manufactured in accordance with ISO and CE standards, the PG500-B can be configured with soundproof canopy, ATS systems, synchronization panels and advanced digital control systems to meet different project requirements.
Baudouin engines are globally recognized for their durability and high-performance power generation capabilities. The PG500-B provides a reliable solution for facilities requiring high-capacity standby or prime power.
This generator is commonly used as a backup or prime power source for factories, hospitals, data centers, large commercial buildings and infrastructure projects.
