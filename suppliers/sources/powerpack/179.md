Perkins Diesel Generator-PG1125-P
The PG1125-P 1125 kVA Perkins Diesel Generator is designed for heavy-duty industrial and infrastructure projects. Suitable for power plants, large factories, hospitals, data centers, and high-demand construction sites requiring stable and continuous energy supply.
Equipped with globally trusted Perkins engines, this generator ensures optimal performance under demanding load conditions. It provides excellent fuel efficiency, low operating costs, and long service life, even under heavy-duty operations.
Manufactured in compliance with ISO and CE standards, the PG1125-P can be customized with advanced synchronization panels, ATS systems, soundproof canopies, and full project-based configurations.
Powerpack Generator offers direct export to Europe and the Middle East with fast delivery, professional technical support, and turnkey project solutions.
