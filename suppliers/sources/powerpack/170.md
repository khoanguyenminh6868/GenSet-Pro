Kofo Diesel Generator-PG165-K
The PG165-K 165 kVA Kofo Diesel Generator is engineered to deliver dependable standby power for small businesses and commercial environments. It is commonly used in workshops, retail stores, construction sites, offices and telecom infrastructure where uninterrupted electricity is critical.
Equipped with a reliable Kofo diesel engine, the generator provides efficient fuel consumption, stable operation and low maintenance costs. Its compact design enables flexible installation while ensuring consistent performance during unexpected power interruptions.
Manufactured in accordance with ISO and CE standards, the PG165-K can be configured with optional soundproof canopy, ATS systems and modern digital control panels to meet different project requirements.
Kofo powered generators are recognized for offering cost-effective and durable power solutions. For businesses seeking an affordable diesel generator without sacrificing reliability, the PG165-K delivers an excellent balance of performance, efficiency and long service life.
This model is particularly suitable as a backup generator for small enterprises, commercial facilities and light industrial operations that require dependable emergency power.
