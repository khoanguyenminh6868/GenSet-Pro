Yanmar Diesel Generator-PG14-YM
The PG14-YM 14 kVA Yanmar Diesel Generator is designed for small-scale and light commercial power requirements. It is an ideal standby solution for offices, retail stores, workshops, telecom base stations and emergency backup systems.
Powered by the Yanmar 3TNV88-GGEA engine, this model ensures fuel efficiency, low operating cost and dependable service life. Its compact design allows easy installation in limited spaces while maintaining stable and reliable performance during sudden power outages.
Manufactured in compliance with ISO and CE standards, the PG14-YM can be supplied with an optional soundproof canopy and suitable control panel configurations.
