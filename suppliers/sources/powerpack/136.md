Fpt Iveco Diesel Generator-PG135-I
The PG135-I 135 kVA FPT Iveco Diesel Generator provides dependable prime and standby power for medium-sized factories, commercial facilities, hotels, hospitals and construction projects.
Powered by a high-performance FPT Iveco diesel engine, this generator ensures fuel efficiency, low operating costs and stable operation for continuous industrial applications.
Manufactured in compliance with ISO & CE standards, the PG135-I can be supplied with soundproof canopy, ATS systems and advanced digital control panels to meet varied industrial needs.
FPT Iveco generators are recognized for industrial-grade quality, reliability and long service life. The PG135-I is ideal for medium-scale industrial and commercial power requirements.
Powerpack Generator manufactures and exports FPT Iveco powered diesel generator sets for industrial, commercial and standby power applications across Europe, Africa, the Middle East and international markets with fast delivery and professional technical support.
