Fpt Iveco Diesel Generator-PG500-I
The PG500-I 500 kVA FPT Iveco Diesel Generator delivers dependable prime and standby power for large industrial facilities, hospitals, data centers, hotels, and major construction projects where continuous electricity is critical.
Equipped with a powerful FPT Iveco diesel engine, this generator ensures optimal fuel efficiency, robust operation, and long-term reliability for demanding industrial applications.
Manufactured in compliance with ISO & CE standards, the PG500-I can be configured with soundproof canopy, ATS systems, and advanced digital control panels to meet the specific requirements of each project.
FPT Iveco generators are known for their heavy-duty performance and industrial reliability. The PG500-I is ideal for businesses and facilities requiring high-capacity standby and prime power solutions.
