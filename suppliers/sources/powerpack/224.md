Yanmar Diesel Generator-PG46-YM
The PG46-YM 46 kVA Yanmar Diesel Generator is designed for reliable standby and prime power applications in commercial buildings, workshops, construction projects, telecommunications facilities and industrial operations.
Powered by the Yanmar 4TNV98T-GGEA engine, this model combines low fuel consumption, dependable performance and long service life. It delivers 46 kVA (36,8 kW) standby power and 42 kVA (33,6 kW) prime power at 50 Hz.
The generator set can be supplied with an optional soundproof canopy and suitable control panel configurations. Its service-friendly design supports straightforward maintenance and stable operation under demanding conditions.
Powerpack Generator provides direct export to European and international markets with fast delivery and professional after-sales support.
