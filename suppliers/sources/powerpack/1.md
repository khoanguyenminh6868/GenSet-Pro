Diesel Generator Solutions from 10 kVA to 2750 kVA
Powerpack Generator manufactures diesel generator sets from 10 kVA to 2750 kVA for industrial facilities, commercial buildings, construction sites, infrastructure projects and critical power applications. Generator sets are available in open type and soundproof canopy configurations for standby, prime and continuous power requirements. Each project is evaluated according to the required power, operating conditions and installation environment.
Engine and Alternator Options
We offer diesel generator sets powered by globally recognized engine brands including Perkins, Baudouin, FPT Iveco, Hyundai Doosan, Yanmar, Kofo and Yangdong. Depending on generator rating and project requirements, alternator options may include Stamford, Leroy-Somer, Mecc Alte, WEG and Powerpack. This broad component range helps match the generator set to the required output, application and project budget without limiting the customer to a single engine or alternator combination.
Choosing the Right Diesel Generator
Selecting the correct generator requires more than comparing standby kVA values. Prime and standby power, kW output, load profile, motor starting currents, phase, voltage, frequency, daily operating time, ambient temperature, altitude, noise limits and available installation space should all be considered. For construction sites and temporary power projects, transport and site conditions must also be evaluated. Powerpack Generator helps determine the appropriate generator capacity and configuration according to the actual operating requirement.
Generator Configurations and Project Options
Powerpack diesel generators can be configured with ATS systems for automatic mains failure operation, synchronization panels for multiple generator sets or mains parallel operation, advanced control modules and project-specific equipment. Open type sets are suitable for prepared generator rooms, while soundproof canopy models provide weather protection and reduced operating noise for outdoor or noise-sensitive locations. Component selection and equipment options are defined according to the installation and intended duty.
Diesel Generator Applications
Our generator sets support factories, workshops, construction sites, infrastructure projects, hospitals, hotels, commercial buildings, agriculture, mining, telecommunications and other facilities where reliable power is essential. Standby models provide backup power during mains failure, while prime power solutions can serve locations where grid supply is unavailable or insufficient.
Diesel Generator Manufacturer and Exporter
With more than 20 years of experience, export-focused production and fast delivery, Powerpack Generator supplies diesel generator solutions to customers across Europe, Africa, the Middle East and international markets. Share your required power, application, voltage, frequency and operating conditions to receive a project-specific generator selection and quotation.
