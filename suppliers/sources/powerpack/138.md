Fpt Iveco Diesel Generator-PG22-I
The PG22-I 22 kVA FPT Iveco Diesel Generator is engineered to provide dependable prime and standby power for small workshops, light industrial facilities, commercial buildings and backup applications where uninterrupted electricity is essential.
Equipped with a robust FPT Iveco diesel engine, this generator delivers fuel efficiency, stable performance and low operating costs. Its compact design makes installation straightforward while ensuring reliable performance during unexpected power outages.
Manufactured in compliance with ISO and CE standards, the PG22-I can be supplied with optional soundproof canopy, ATS systems and advanced digital control panels to meet varied project requirements.
FPT Iveco powered generators are recognized for their industrial quality and long-term reliability. The PG22-I model is an excellent choice for businesses seeking a reliable diesel generator for light commercial, workshop or backup power needs.
