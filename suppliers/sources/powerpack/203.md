Perkins Diesel Generator-PG66-P
The PG66-P 66 kVA Perkins Diesel Generator is designed for small-scale and light commercial power requirements. It is an ideal standby solution for offices, retail stores, workshops, telecom base stations and emergency backup systems.
Powered by a globally trusted Perkins engine, this model ensures fuel efficiency, low operating cost and long service life. Its compact design allows easy installation in limited spaces while maintaining stable and reliable performance during sudden power outages.
Manufactured in compliance with ISO and CE standards, the PG66-P can be supplied with optional soundproof canopy and advanced control panel configurations.
