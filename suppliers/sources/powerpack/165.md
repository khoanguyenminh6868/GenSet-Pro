Hyundai Doosan Diesel Generator-PG825-D
The PG825-D 825 kVA Hyundai Doosan Diesel Generator is designed to deliver reliable prime and standby power for industrial facilities, hospitals, commercial buildings and major construction projects where uninterrupted electricity is essential.
Powered by a high-performance Hyundai Doosan diesel engine, this generator ensures efficient fuel consumption, stable operation and long service life even in demanding industrial environments.
Manufactured in accordance with ISO and CE standards, the PG825-D can be configured with soundproof canopy, ATS systems, synchronization panels and advanced digital control systems to meet various project requirements.
Hyundai Doosan engines are globally recognized for their durability, efficiency and high performance in industrial power generation systems. The PG825-D offers a dependable solution for facilities requiring reliable standby or prime power.
This model is widely used as a backup or prime power generator for factories, hospitals, shopping centers, data centers and large infrastructure projects.
