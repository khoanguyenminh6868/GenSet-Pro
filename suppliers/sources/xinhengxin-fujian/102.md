3–16kW Open-Frame Diesel Generator Sets – Durable, Portable & Cost-Effective
Built for durability and heavy-duty performance, this 3–16kW open-frame diesel generator series is engineered to handle tough working conditions. The robust steel frame protects the core components, while the forced-air cooling system ensures stable operation during extended runtimes. Equipped with a reliable diesel engine and self-excited alternator, it delivers consistent power output with minimal maintenance. Ideal for remote construction, mining support, agricultural irrigation, and as a standby power source for workshops and small factories. Various output voltages and phase options are available upon request.
