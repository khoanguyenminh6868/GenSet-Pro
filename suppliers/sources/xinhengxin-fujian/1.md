Introduction
Fujian Xinhengxin Motor Co., Ltd. ,founded in 1991, is one of the integrated power generation equipment manufacturing enterprises with a modern production scale of research and development, manufacturing, sales .
Located in Fuan City, Fujian Province, China, the company mainly produces series of generator sets and generators, including 8 series of more than 100 specifications. The product realization process conforms to the ISO9001 international quality management system standard, and has passed the 14001 environmental management system certification and the 18001 occupational health and safety management system certification.
HENSING-Brand diesel water-cooled series generator sets are the companys flagship products, with international famous brand engines, including Cummins, VOLVO, Perkins and so on.They are manufactured with the first-class technology and advanced noise reduction technology to meet the needs of various industries,such as building, hospital, hotel, communication station, database, municipal engineering ,mining and so on. The company production capacity is more than 2000 units with power ranged from 5 kw to 2000kw.
The company produces excitation STC and brushless excitation SLG series generator, with the mature and reliable technology to satisfy the need of power supply for agricultural production, family, breeding, planting and other industries. The products are exported to Europe, southeast Asia, South America and the Middle East and other places .
In recent years, the company has continuously introduced technical personnel, purchased advanced digital production equipment and testing equipment, set up special and research development funds, and established product research and development technology center to develop new products. The technology center will form industrialized production with the R & D achievements by small and medium-sized tests, making the company obtain economic benefits and social recognition. Now, it has obtained more than ten national technology patents. The company will adhere to the development direction of scientific and technological innovation, market-oriented, and walk out of the differentiated development road with its own characteristics.
The company adheres to the enterprise spirit of "cooperation and integrity, practicality and innovation, gratitude and win-win", continues to uphold the management and service concept of "quality first, customer first", and sincerely welcomes customers at home and abroad to visit, guide, negotiate business, and work together to create the future!
