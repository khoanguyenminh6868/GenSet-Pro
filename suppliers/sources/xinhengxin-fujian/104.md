6–8kW Soundproof Diesel Generator Set – Low Noise, Portable & Easy to Maintain
This 6–8kW silent diesel generator series is engineered for quiet, reliable power in noise-sensitive environments. The soundproof canopy effectively reduces operational noise, making it suitable for residential areas, small shops, and office backup. Powered by a direct-injection, air-cooled diesel engine, it delivers stable single-phase or three phase or Dual voltage, 220V output with low fuel consumption and easy starting. The compact design and built-in lifting eyes allow for convenient transport and installation. With overload protection, low-oil shutdown, and a long-running fuel tank, this series offers dependable performance with minimal maintenance.
