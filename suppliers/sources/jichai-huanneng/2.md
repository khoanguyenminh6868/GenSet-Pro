Jichai Huanneng Gas Generating Equipment Co., Ltd (JCHN) is a high-tech sales&service subsidiary company of Jinan Diesel Engine Co., Ltd, specializing in design, supply, erection and maintenance of engine-based power plants. JCHN offers project consultation and design, integrated OEM, equipment leasing, and spare parts supply. As well as this, JCHN provides power plant on-site operation and maintenance services, and power plant investment. JCHN is also capable of providing EPC integrated solutions and turn-key projects of fuel gas comprehensive utilization system, combined heat utilization system (CHP or CCHP), water treatment system, etc.
The generators of JCHN run on natural gas, methane gas, coal mine gas, landfill gas, biomass gas, coke-oven gas, and other combustible gases. Our products have been widely used in more than 30 provinces in China, and exported to more than 50 countries or regions. Sales & Service centers have been established in Kazakhstan, Indonesia, United Emirates, Colombia, and Nigeria respectively to radiate the whole market coverage.
In order to meet the growing demand of international market, Green Power Utility International Limited (GPU) was founded in 2011.
