Introduction
Invested by international and domestic financial groups, Fujian Huatai Power Supply Co.,Ltd. specializes in assembling, vending and maintaining diesel generator sets. With professional experience of designing, configuring and maintaining power generator sets, our products have successfully maintained domestic market of all industries. Also we have gained OEM permission for world-famous engine brands of DEUTZ, PERKINS, JOHN DEERE, CUMMINS and etc. Together with the world famous alternator LEROY SOMER, STAMFORD, MARATHON, we are supplying world class diesel generator sets!
To meet the market's demands, Huatai has devoted into the long-term development of diesel generators. By continuous investments, management improvement and latest technology introduction, we committed ourselves to the market with products of world quality. Our power gensets are now the ideal main and standby power supply for the characteristic of smaller size, lighter weight, lower noises, less consuming and exhausting gases, excellent starting performance and working more steadily and reliably, and have been extensively applied in the industries of telecom, war industry, energy sources, transportation, railway, highway, banking, securities and stocks, real estate companies, factories, hospitals, school and etc.
Huatai Power(HTP) generator sets are manufactured and tested in Fuzhou, the capital of Fujian Province in China. They are complied with or even exceed the standards both at home and abroad: GB/T2820,GB1105,YD/T502,ISO8525-3,ISO3046,ISO8525.Exhaust is complied with Europe iii standard and American EPA and CARB standard.
