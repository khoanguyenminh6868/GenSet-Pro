About Our Company
Fujian Runda Power Machinery Co., Ltd is a manufacturer specialized in providing diesel generator sets power from 10KVA to 2500KVA since 2008. Located in Ningde City, Fujian Province, with 4 modern standardized plants and covering 32000 square meters. Putting an emphasis on quality control. Along with our advanced manufacturing process for the highest standard components, RUNDA POWER delivers products with highly reliable engines,alternators from variously famous brands like Cummins,Kubota,Perkins,Stamford, LeroySomer and Deepsea, Smartgen controllers etc.
We have excellent equipments, technology and R&D team to assure the quality. We have obtained CE, ISO9000,GS,SONCAP,EPA and CARB certificates. Runda power depends on “credibility, efficiency, creation, development” to keep improvement on the quality and supply best services, which obtain the satisfaction and confidence of customers.
