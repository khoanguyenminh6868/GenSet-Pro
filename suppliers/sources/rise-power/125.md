LOVOL Marine Diesel Engine
Description of LovoL Marine Diesel Engine
1. It is a special power platform developed for auxiliary generator for vessel based on many years of experience and combines advanced design concept of engine;
2. Main types include 1004GM, 1004TGM, 1006TGM and 1006TWGM, power from 40-110kw;
3. It is widely used in the auxiliary diesel generator set for carrier, passenger ship, fishing ship and so on.
Advantages of LovoL Marine Diesel Engine
1. With seawater-freshwater dual cooling close circle system, our generating marine diesel engines can alarm and automatically stop when water temperature, oil pressure and rotating speed exceed their limits.
2. The double layer protective structure of the high-pressure fuel pipes ensures its safety and reliability.
3. Gear-driven seawater pump.
4. The high-temperature protective grid in the exhaust system adopts German heat-insulated material.
5. The electric system is safe and stable due to the double-wired structure.
6. Electronic governing system is available.
