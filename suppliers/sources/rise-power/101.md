50—200kW SDEC Marine Generator
Description of SDEC Marine Generator
1. The 50-200kW SDEC marine generator is powered by the SDEC 135, G128 series marine diesel engine (CCS Certificate);
2. Some well-known brushless excitation alternators (Marathon, Stamford, etc.) are equipped;
3. The auto protective device of the diesel generator enables it to control and protect the water temperature, oil temperature, oil pressure, and the speed.
4. Other Marine Classification Society Approval is available, such as BV(France), ABS(USA), etc.
Advantages of SDEC Marine Generator
1. Automatic speed regulation;
2. Automatic voltage regulation;
3. Superior dynamic performance;
4. Stable fluctuation form of voltage;
5. Exceptional efficiency and reliability, long service life, etc;
