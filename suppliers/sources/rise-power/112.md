Perkins Diesel Engine Powered 7—1800kW Industrial Diesel Generator
Powered by UK Perkins diesel engine, 7-1800kW industrial diesel generator is equipped with some famous brushless excitation alternators of Marathon and Stamford, and control panels of DeepSea UK.
With automatic protective device, diesel generator set can automatically control and regulate the water temperature, oil temperature, oil pressure, and speed. It has many advantages such as automatic speed regulation, automatic voltage regulation, good dynamic performance, stable fluctuation of voltage, high efficiency, high reliability, long service life, and more. These generator sets can be combined as a power station group with automatic start system according to customer's requirements.
Perkins is one of the world's leading diesel engine manufacturers since 1932, with sales volume totaling nearly 400,000 units per annum. The engine series of 400, 1100, 1300, 2000 and 4000 feature full specification, reasonable structure, reliable performance, easy maintenance and low exhaust. As a result, they capture a large Chinese market. With high reliability and great durability, Perkins engines are highly sought after by major original equipment manufacturers in the industries of auto maker, construction, and more.
