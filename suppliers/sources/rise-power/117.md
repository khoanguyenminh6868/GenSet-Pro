Mitsubishi Diesel Engine Powered 500—1600kW Mitsubishi Generator
SME was established jointly by Shanghai Diesel Engine Co., Ltd and Mitsubishi Heavy Industries, Ltd.
The Company principally produces various models of engine of S6R2/S12R/S16R series which are mainly applied in land generator set, with the power ranging from 500kW to 2000kW.
As far as the three major series of engines S6R2/S12R/S16R produced by SME are concerned, the technology, production and quality always synchronize with Mitsubishi Heavy Industries Ltd. Benefited from the integration of fuel system and engine development by Mitsubishi Heavy Industries Ltd., SME has realized the optimal performance and ever-reliable high power, high stability and low emission.
