HIPOWER SYSTEMS MOBILE DIESEL TWIN-PACK SERIES
The TWIN-PACK is 2 x 625 kVA (HRVW-1250) or 2 x 685 (HRVW-1375) units in parallel on a single trailer with a distribution panel already installed and part of the
standard package. Our diesel HRVW-1250 & HRVW-1375 include the latest Tier 4 Final engines and are packed with a rich selection of standard features, such as,
a digital control panel, distribution panel, camlock connectors, PMG, buck boost transformer, block heater and many more. They also offer a wide range of optional
features to best meet our customer needs.
