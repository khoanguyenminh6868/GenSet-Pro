Quality You Deserve - Dependability You Can Count On
AURORA Generators designs and builds high-quality, reliable power generators for Home, Industrial, and Off-Grid applications. As a privately owned company, we select only the best components from industry leaders that meet the highest quality standards. Using only quality parts and designing specifically around them, we assemble everything at AURORA Generators, not overseas.
AURORA was formed during the Great Northeast Blackout of 2003. That was when our entire electric grid collapsed. The generators that we depended on to protect us ended up being very noisy, smelling bad, and often failing. Being frustrated with the generators available, we decided to abandon traditional generators and build our own. We grew our business by focusing on meeting and exceeding our customers’ expectations. We do so by listening to what our customers’ needs are, being focused on high quality and providing the best customer support possible
