ABOUT US
SR GENSET focuses on the research and development, production and sales of generator sets. There are 4 production lines with an annual output of 45,000 diesel generators. The power range is from 3kva to 2500kva, covering a variety of appearances and special requirements, and has a number of national patents.
SR GENSET ‘s production department is technically strong, with electrical engineers, process engineers, advanced technology, complete production and testing equipment, a strict quality management system and advanced testing methods to ensure that product quality is 100% qualified. Our company has passed ISO9001 quality system certification, ISO14000 environmental management system certification, CE, TLC certification, CQC and other certifications, which can meet the standards of countries around the world, including Europe, the United States, South America, Australia, Asia and other standards.
