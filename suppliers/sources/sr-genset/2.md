SR GENSET, Generator Expert!
SR GENSET focuses on the research and development, production and sales of generator sets. The power range is from 3kW to 2000kW, covering a variety of appearances and special requirements, and has a number of national patents.
As a professional diesel generator manufacturer, SR GENSET is committed to designing and developing various energy-efficient, green and environmentally friendly power generation system solutions, providing customers with personalized non-standard emergency energy solutions, and continuously providing the society with more green, environmentally friendly, energy-saving and efficient power generation equipment.
Shengrong Power Introduction
Jiangsu Shengrong Power Machinery Co., Ltd.(SR GENSET) is located in Huai’an, Jiangsu, covering an area of 35000 square meters and close to Shanghai. Shengrong Power focuses on the research and development, production, and sales of generator sets, with four production lines and an annual output of 45000 diesel generators.
SR GENSET ‘s production department has strong technical capabilities, including electrical engineers, process engineers, advanced technology, complete production and testing equipment, strict quality management system, and advanced testing methods to ensure 100% qualified product quality. Our company has passed ISO9001 quality system certification, CE, SGS and other certifications, which can meet the standards of countries around the world.
In recent years, we have continuously improved our services, ensured product quality, won customer satisfaction and support, and established deep cooperation with customers from various industries at home and abroad.
Efficient Production
How to effectively shorten the total time from receiving an order to completing delivery. This is not only related to improving customer satisfaction but also directly affects the company’s market competitiveness. SR GENSET ‘s conventional generator delivery time is only 10-15 days, making it one of the fastest delivery companies in the industry.
Large factories, a complete supply chain system, skilled workers, a professional team of engineers, and a fast production and delivery process allow our team to produce the best quality generators at the fastest speed.
Advanced Testing Equipment
At SR GENSET , quality is our top priority. Our dedicated quality control team ensures that each product is thoroughly inspected at every stage of production. From raw materials to final assembly, we follow a rigorous process to guarantee the highest standards of performance, durability, and reliability. This meticulous attention to detail ensures that our products meet the specific requirements of our customers across a variety of industries. Our team works tirelessly to maintain these standards, ensuring that all products are always of the highest quality.
We invest in state-of-the-art testing equipment to accurately evaluate the performance of our diesel generators and related products. These cutting-edge tools help us measure power output, fuel efficiency, noise levels, and other critical parameters to ensure that each unit is operating at its best. Using this precision equipment increases the reliability and lifespan of our products, providing our customers with excellent value.
Overseas Customer Visits
SR GENSET attaches great importance to overseas markets, and 90% of orders are exported to more than 50 different countries. We participate in the Canton Fair, Shanghai GPower Exhibition, Russia, Vietnam, Brazil, Dubai exhibitions, and communicate with customers from all over the world. Many customers visit our factory every year and reach cooperation.
Good generators help customers build a good reputation and occupy a larger market.
