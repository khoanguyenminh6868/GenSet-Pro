FAQ
Are you a diesel generator set manufacturer?
Yes, SRGENSET is a diesel generator manufacturer, and we have our own production plant in Huai’an City, Jiangsu Province, China.
What power rating of diesel generator do you provide?
SRGENSET offers diesel generators with power ranging from 5 to 2000 kW, primarily operating at frequencies of 50 Hz and 60 Hz.
Do you support custom diesel generators?
Yes, SRGENSET supports custom diesel generator services. We have a large stock of frequently customized parts, and you can discuss specific details with our engineers.
Does your factory have certification?
SRGENSET holds numerous certifications, including ISO 9001, ISO 14001, ISO 45001, and other factory certifications, as well as E49, CE, ICG, and other product certifications.
What is the normal delivery time?
SRGENSET maintains a small stock of standard models. If you require a small quantity of these models, we can ship them to you within a week. If you need bulk orders or customized products, we typically ship within 30-40 days.
