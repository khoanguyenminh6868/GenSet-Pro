展商风采 | 普什动力：集大功率燃气发电机组研发制造、燃气电站工程系统集成及运维为一体的高新技术企业
由中国内燃机工业协会、中国内燃机工业协会应急动力装备专委会、中国电器工业协会内燃发电设备分会、隆高展览（上海）有限公司联合主办的“ 第24届上海国际动力设备及发电机组展览会 ”（简称“GPOWER动力展）将于 2025 年 6 月 11 - 13 日在 上海新国际博览中心 召开。
届时， 重庆普什新能源技术有限公司 将重磅亮相展会， 展位号：N1-182 ，诚邀莅临展位参观洽谈。
重庆普什新能源技术有限公司
展位号：N1-182
普什动力·智慧能源
PUSH POWER ·SMART ENERGY
四川省宜宾普什集团有限公司 （以下称“普什集团”）是五粮液集团全资子集团公司，产业涉及新包装、新材料、汽车及动力电池配套、绿色动力及智能制造四个板块，是集现代化、多元化、国际化为一体的高科技企业集团。
重庆普什新能源技术有限公司 （以下称"重普新能源”）是普什集团全资子公司，位于重庆市九龙工业园B区，注册资本5000万元，资产总额超3亿元，专业技术型、生产型人才占比超过三分之二，是一家集大功率燃气发电机组研发制造、燃气电站工程系统集成及运维为一体的高新技术企业。公司具备年产200台大功率燃气机及机组的生产能力， 拥有国家发明专利11项、外观设计专利15项、实用新型专利188项，是重庆市唯一一家燃气内燃机工程技术研究中心。 公司具备专业的研发及技术服务团队，拥有丰富的电站集成经验，在燃气发电行业经营10余年，业务涵盖：天然气、石油伴生气、高低浓度瓦斯、沼气、工业尾气、生物质气、页岩气、混氢、煤炭焦化气等多燃料、复杂应用场景发电项目，能够全面应对电站可能出现的各类技术质量问题，致力于为客户提供“交钥匙”工程。
Yibin Push Group Co., Ltd. (hereinafter referred to as “PushGroup”) is a wholly-owned subsidiary of Wuliangye Group. Its business covers four areas: new packaging, new materials, automotive and power battery accessories, green power, and intelligent manufacturing. It is a high-tech enterprise group integrating modernization, diversification, and internationalization.
Chongqing Push New Energy Technology Co., Ltd. (hereinafter referred to as “Push ”) is a wholly-owned subsidiary of the Push Group, located in Zone B of the Jiulong Industrial Park in Chongqing City. With a registered capital of 50 million yuan and total assets exceeding 300 million yuan, the company employs over two-thirds of its workforce in professional technical and production roles. It is a high-tech enterprise specializing in the research, development, and manufacturing of high-power gas-fired power generation units, as well as the system integration and maintenance of gas-fired power plant engineering projects. The company has an annual production capacity of 200 units of high-power gas engines and generator sets. It holds 11 national invention patents, 15 design patents, and 188 utility model patents, and is the only gas internal combustion engine engineering technology research center in Chongqing. The company has a professional R&D and technical service team with extensive experience in power plant integration. With over a decade of experience in the gas power generation industry, its business scope includes multi-fuel and complex application scenarios such as natural gas, associated petroleum gas, high and low concentration methane, biogas, industrial exhaust gas, biomass gas, shale gas, hydrogen-blended gas, and coal coking gas. It is capable of addressing various technical and quality issues that may arise in power plants and is committed to providing customers with “turnkey” projects.
