About Us
Who is FDK ENERGY – About FuDianKang
FuDianKang Group was founded in 2000 , is one of the earliest companies in China focusing on the field of generators, 25 years + experience in the industry, over the years to complete more than 1,000 domestic and foreign projects and get the customer’s unanimous praise.
We have bonded warehouses , are capable of providing China’s most competitive prices for imported engine generator series. As one of China’s earliest diesel generator manufacturers and exporters, we possess 4 big plants for assembly, repair, and storage.
There are 30+ professional engineers in engineering, electrical, environmental protection and noise abatement, etc. We can provide self-starting, parallel and microcomputer monitoring and other supporting equipments according to customer’s needs, and we can also provide a complete set of commonly used or emergency power solutions, such as anti-sound box and trailer-type generator.
As Cummins’ TOP GOEM authorized manufacturer , we have imported Cummins for fifteen consecutive years the TOP SALES in China. At the same time with the UK Perkins, Japan Mitsubishi, Germany MTU and other top engine companies in-depth cooperation for many years.
