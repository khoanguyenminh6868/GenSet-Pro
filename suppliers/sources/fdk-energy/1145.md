Control System HGM6110 (DETAILED in INSTRUCTION)
HGM6110 is an advanced control module based on micro-processor, containing all necessary functions for protection of the
genset and the breaker control. It can monitor the breaker control and automatically start the engine when the mains are
abnormal. Accurately measure various operational parameters and display all values and alarms information on the LCD. In
addition, the control module can automatically shut down the engine and indicate the engine failure.
