Cummins Power Generation C330D5 330 kVA Generator Specification
The Cummins C330D5 is a 330 kVA Three Phase Auto Start 50Hz Silent Diesel Generator featuring the Cummins QSL9-G5 engine & Stamford HC4D alternator.
Bring 100 years of technical know-how to your diesel generator needs with this rugged, heavy-duty engine. Delivering efficient, cost-effective power, this four-cycle industrial generator - with its perfect mix of reliability and versatility - is perfect for stationary standby and prime power use.
Though available with optional PMG (permanent magnet generator) to enhance motor starting, the base model still boasts a PowerCommand® electronic control system providing auto remote start/stop, status and alarm display. There is also the option of adding a sound attenuating canopy, 25dB(A) or 30/35dB(A) silencers for open sets.
This generator is easy to integrate, designed to operate at ambient temperatures of up to 50 degrees and the integral, set-mounted radiator system simplifies facility design requirements for rejected heat.
Purchasing this Cummins diesel generator will allow you to benefit from the peace of mind that comes from knowing the manufacturer has a wealth of engineering experience and expertise. If you need help deciding which of our extensive catalogue of Cummins Generators is right for you, you can speak to our resident experts via phone or email .
