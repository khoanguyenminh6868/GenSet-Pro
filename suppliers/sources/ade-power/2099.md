Optional Extras:
Additional Fuel Tank
Additional off-the-shelf free-standing fuel tanks can be supplied to act as your generator's primary fuel source or to extend its total possible run time before the need to refuel.
Extended Base Fuel Tank
An extended base fuel tank will be bespoke designed & manufactured for the exact generator model & capacity you require, extending the total possible run time before the need to refuel.
ATS Panel
Investing in a generator for emergency backup power? Add an ATS panel to automatically detect mains failures, start up the generator, and transfer the load. When the panel detects mains power has returned, it will automatically transfer the load back and shut down the generator.
Bespoke Acoustic Enclosure
We specialise in the bespoke design & manufacture of acoustic enclosures for open generator sets. We design to your specific required noise level and fit-out the enclosure with your generator as well as any other required auxiliary equipment.
