Introduction
Fujian Yukun Qiangwei Motor Co., Ltd. is a power generation equipment industry and trade enterprise integrating R & D, production, sales and export. It was founded in May 2010 with a registered capital of 50.6 million yuan and a registered address of No. 2, Jinye Road, Tiehu Village, Chengyang Town,Fuan City, Ningde City, Fujian Province，China. The company invests in Fujian Dacheng Motor Co., Ltd. and Fujian Yidian Power Technology Co., Ltd., with a plant area of 8500 square meters, an annual output value of more than 200 million yuan and an annual export of 20 million US dollars. Fujian Yukun Qiangwei Motor Co., Ltd. has advanced production equipment and has production lines such as laser cutting, sheet metal stamping, metalworking, winding and embedding, plastic spraying, painting and final assembly.
