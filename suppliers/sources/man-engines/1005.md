MAN ENGINE ACADEMY
CUSTOMISED SERVICE CONCEPT
Regular maintenance intervals for marine engines are essential for perfect
functioning and a long service life. The MAN Engine Academy offers
theoretical and practical training in operation, diagnostic strategies and
maintenance. Technically experienced experts train certified service
­centers as well as the service personnel of sales partners and end
customers at the MAN Engine Academy. Course participants have the
opportunity to familiarize themselves with the engine in the Academy’s
modern training environment. The training courses can also be held online
and individually adapted to the respective requirements of the participant.
