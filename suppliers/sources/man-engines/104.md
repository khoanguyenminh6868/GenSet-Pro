MAN Engines offers powerful and compact diesel and gas engines with 6 and 12 cylinders for use as auxiliary engines. With 12.4 l to 29.6 l displacement, they are based on the latest engine platforms and the most cutting-edge high-volume technologies from MAN Truck & Bus SE. All engines comply with the current exhaust gas regulations, which is partially achieved by implementation of the modular exhaust gas aftertreatment from MAN Engines.
The compact design of the MAN engines as well as the modular exhaust gas aftertreatment ensures space-saving installation. Low vibrations and quiet operation of the engines contribute to a pleasant stay on board.
