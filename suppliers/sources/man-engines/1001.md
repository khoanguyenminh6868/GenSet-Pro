MAN ENGINE ACADEMY.
Customised Service Concept
Regular maintenance intervals for marine engines are essential for perfect functioning and a long
service life. The MAN Engine Academy offers theoretical and practical training in operation, diag-

modern training environment. The training courses can also be held online and individually adapted
to the respective requirements of the participant.
