Limit values EU Stage V for inland vessels
For the “EU stage V inland navigation” emission level, the legislator has provided for higher limit values for nitrogen oxides (NO <sub>x</sub> = 1.8 g/kWh) and particulate matter (PM = 0.015 g/kWh) for outputs > 300 kW per engine since 1/1/2021. In addition, the number of particles (PN = 1.0*10 <sup>12</sup> n/kWh) has been limited. For this reason, MAN Engines relies on an exhaust gas aftertreatment system with SCR and diesel particulate filter (DPF) for outputs > 300 kW. For outputs < 300 kW, the legal requirements for nitrogen oxides (NO <sub>x</sub> = 2.1 g/kWh) and particulate matter (PM = 0.1 g/kWh) can be met by using an SCR system without a diesel particulate filter.</p>"}}" id="text-3a6595537f" class="cmp-text">

The modular EGA allows a wide range of installation variants thanks to variably positionable individual components and enables flexible system integration in line with requirements. Modular exhaust gas aftertreatment as technical solution
The basis for meeting the stringent emission requirements is the modular exhaust gas aftertreatment system made by MAN Engines. The modular EGA allows a wide range of installation variants thanks to variably positionable individual components and enables flexible system integration in line with requirements. This advantage is not only evident in tight or even difficult installation situations, but also in the different requirements of emission limits. The modular exhaust gas aftertreatment system made by MAN Engines for workboats was already presented at the International Workboat Show in New Orleans, LA in the US at the end of 2017. The system made by the MAN Engines business unit is made possible by the know-how of MAN Truck & Bus SE, which has been successfully using SCR systems as leading European commercial vehicle manufacturer in the Group’s own trucks in large-scale production since 2006. In addition, MAN Engines benefits from the installation experience gained in the agricultural and industrial sectors, where the technology has already been used for in-line and V engines in series production since 2015.
The MAN D2676 is available for EU Stage V already from 290 kW. For outputs < 300 kWh per engine, an exhaust gas aftertreatment system with SCR only is sufficient in this case.
For outputs above 300 kW, MAN Engines relies on SCR and diesel particulate filters (DPF) to achieve the emission values to comply with EU Stage V.
