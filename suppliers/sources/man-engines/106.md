Important facts about the product range and applications

MAN Engines is the leading manufacturer of diesel engines for off-road applications. With our many years of product experience, we provide a consistent power rating portfolio from 118 to 816 kW (160 to 1,110 HP) with seven engine series. The diesel engines with a displacement of 4.6 to 24.2 litres are based on the latest engine platforms and the most cutting-edge high-volume technologies from MAN Truck & Bus SE – they convince with their compact design and dynamic power delivery.
Thanks to individual adaptation to the relevant application, MAN engines for construction machinery always provide the required charging pressure and the relevant dynamics for demanding tasks. The availability of emission standards ranging from US Tier 4 to EU Stage V ensures that high-volume manufacturers and niche solution providers alike can sell the machines around the world. Furthermore, what really puts these engines at the forefront of their sector is the optimal integration capability in any system environment thanks to MAN Engines interfaces.

Milestones of agricultural technology
As a manufacturer and developer of diesel engines, MAN has been closely associated with agricultural engineering since the early 1920s. The innovative engines were initially installed in the company’s own machines – from motorised ploughs to the company’s tractors – but were later increasingly sold to other manufacturers. Today, MAN engines can be found in agricultural machinery from a wide range of manufacturers and are in particular used in areas with high demands on performance and efficiency.
