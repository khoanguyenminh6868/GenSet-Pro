Diesel engines for Limited Time Power, Prime and Continuous Power
Important facts about the product range and applications
Security of supply in all areas of use: With the MAN D2862 MAN Engines offers a reliable diesel engine for Emergency Standby Power (ESP) and Limited Time Running Power (LTP), Prime Power (PRP) and Continuous Power (COP) mode. In ESP and LTP operation, this engine supplies emergency power during power outages, for instance in facilities prone to failure, and thus impress with their dependable and fast load pick-up.</p>\r\n<p>PRP engines cover short-term peak demands, e.g. at power supply companies and energy-intensive industries. In the same way, the engines in COP operation prove their endurance with operation up to 8,000 hours per year.</p>\r\n<p>Peak load, emergency power, and basic provision – stable running and dynamic, MAN diesel engines are dependable energy suppliers in every segment and guarantee efficient power supply thanks to their sturdy and compact construction and their high economic efficiency.</p>\r\n"}}" id="text-a6b9e00adb" class="cmp-text">
Security of supply in all areas of use: With the MAN D2862 MAN Engines offers a reliable diesel engine for Emergency Standby Power (ESP) and Limited Time Running Power (LTP), Prime Power (PRP) and Continuous Power (COP) mode. In ESP and LTP operation, this engine supplies emergency power during power outages, for instance in facilities prone to failure, and thus impress with their dependable and fast load pick-up.
PRP engines cover short-term peak demands, e.g. at power supply companies and energy-intensive industries. In the same way, the engines in COP operation prove their endurance with operation up to 8,000 hours per year.
Peak load, emergency power, and basic provision – stable running and dynamic, MAN diesel engines are dependable energy suppliers in every segment and guarantee efficient power supply thanks to their sturdy and compact construction and their high economic efficiency.

Emergency Standby Power (ESP)

Average power output is 70 % of the emergency standby power rating.</li>\r\n <li>Typical operation is 50 hours per year with maximum expected usage of 200 hours per year.</li>\r\n <li>Standby power in accordance with ISO 8528.</li>\r\n <li>Fuel stop power in accordance with ISO 3046.</li>\r\n</ul>\r\n<p />"}}" id="text-10f198012d" class="cmp-text">
Power output available with varying load for the duration of an emergency outage. Average power output is 70 % of the emergency standby power rating.
Typical operation is 50 hours per year with maximum expected usage of 200 hours per year.
Standby power in accordance with ISO 8528.
Fuel stop power in accordance with ISO 3046.
