Important facts about the product range and applications
MAN Engines offers engines for operation with natural gas and special gases for pure electrical power generation and combined heat and power. The power range ranges from 37 kW to 750 kW with natural gas and from 68 kW to 750 kW with special gas (biogas, landfill gas, sewage gas). MAN Engines offers engines for operation with natural gas and special gases for pure electrical power generation and combined heat and power. The power range ranges from 37 kW to 750 kW with natural gas and from 68 kW to 750 kW with special gas (biogas, landfill gas, sewage gas). The MAN engines, which have been specially developed for use with natural gas and special gas, are characterised by maximum reliability and achieve the best overall efficiencies of over 90 per cent thanks to their optimum combustion process.
MAN engines for natural gas and special gas (biogas, landfill gas, sewage gas) ensure security of supply – they have been thoroughly tested and optimised in field trials. The engines have proven themselves through their robustness, combined with high operating hours in peak-load and continuous operation, in the agricultural and municipal sectors, in hotels and hospitals as well as in numerous other industries. The long service intervals and high service life of MAN gas engines ensure low total cost of ownership.
