Coming From China, Marketing To The World.
Founded in 2008, Yangzhou Aiweite Electromechanical Co., Ltd. has accumulated extensive experience in the manufacture and installation of generator sets ranging from 1 kVA to 2,500 kVA. Over the years, Aiweite has continued to strengthen its capabilities and market presence, and we are committed to becoming a reliable long-term partner for customers worldwide.
Through continuous innovation, we consistently improve the efficiency, durability, and environmental performance of our products. Advanced manufacturing equipment and strict quality management are applied throughout production to ensure that every generator set meets applicable international standards.
We have established long-term and stable relationships with customers across different markets and place strong emphasis on customer satisfaction. Comprehensive support is provided before, during, and after each project, ensuring that customer requirements are addressed promptly and effectively.
Guided by the principle of “Quality First, Customer First,” we uphold a corporate culture built on integrity, responsibility, innovation, and mutual success. We believe that continuous improvement in product quality and service is essential to earning long-term customer trust and supporting sustainable business growth.
Looking ahead, Aiweite will remain focused on the diesel generator industry. Driven by technological innovation and customer requirements, we will continue to enhance product competitiveness, expand our global presence, and provide dependable power solutions for customers worldwide.

2022 – Present
In 2008, YANGZHOU AIWEITE ELECTROMECHANICAL., LTD was established amidst the booming wave of China’s manufacturing industry, focusing on the R&D and production of land‑use diesel generator sets. In its early years, the company started with basic manufacturing and gradually built a comprehensive production system and strict quality management standards. Over the following decade, it deepened its expertise in the diesel generator field, offering a wide power range from small to medium‑large units, widely used in factories, infrastructure projects, emergency power supply, and more. The company successively obtained ISO 9001 Quality Management System and ISO 14001 Environmental Management System certifications, earning the trust of domestic customers through stable product quality and reliable delivery performance.
In 2022, the company officially launched its foreign trade business, actively expanding into overseas markets. Transitioning from domestic sales to export, it has been proactively integrating into the global diesel generator market. Industry data shows that the global diesel generator market reached approximately USD 19.9 billion in 2025, with the Asia‑Pacific region, driven by strong industrial and infrastructure demand, accounting for nearly 40% of the global share. Keeping pace with industry trends, the company continues to enhance product competitiveness and shift from a single domestic market towards an international footprint. Looking ahead, YANGZHOU AIWEITE ELECTROMECHANICAL CO., LTD remains committed to the development philosophy of "quality first, innovation‑driven," striving to provide reliable power solutions for customers worldwide.
