Product Information
Driven by genuine Swedish Volvo Penta industrial diesel engines, our generator sets adopt classic Nordic precision manufacturing technology. Featuring outstanding operational stability, ultra-low noise, excellent fuel efficiency, and superior low-temperature performance, they deliver reliable premium power solutions for high-standard commercial, industrial, and critical power supply scenarios.
Core Configuration
Engine: Genuine Volvo Penta series industrial diesel engines adopt advanced electronic fuel injection and turbocharged intercooling technology. Featuring a compact rigid structure, precise fuel calibration, and famous cold-start performance, the engines achieve stable startup and continuous operation in -40℃ low-temperature environments. They adapt to high altitude, humid, dusty, and coastal salt-spray conditions, with global standardized after-sales support and long maintenance cycles.
Alternator: Maintenance-free brushless synchronous full-copper alternator with Class H insulation and IP22–IP23 protection grade. It conforms to ISO G3 high power quality standards, featuring ultra-stable voltage and frequency output with low waveform distortion, perfectly matching precision medical equipment, IT systems and sensitive electrical loads.
Control System: Chinese-English LCD intelligent controller with full-range fault detection and protection functions. It supports automatic start-stop, ATS power transfer, 4G remote monitoring, cloud management and multi-unit parallel operation, enabling 24/7 unattended intelligent operation.
Auxiliary System: Equipped with a closed cooling system, triple filtration device, high-efficiency vibration dampers, professional low-noise muffler, and integrated base frame. The modular design simplifies installation and daily maintenance while ensuring long-term stable operation.

Manufactured in accordance with strict Swedish industrial standards, Volvo engines feature a reliable mechanical structure, a low failure rate, and strong continuous load capacity. Fully verified by global high-end projects, the gensets maintain consistent stable output for long-term operation.

Why Volvo Penta Engines Suit Critical Power Projects
Volvo Penta industrial engines use electronic fuel injection and turbocharged intercooling to deliver stable output with low fuel consumption. For projects that need low vibration, low noise and long maintenance intervals, the Volvo platform provides a proven base. We match each engine to an alternator and control system that meets the load profile, then verify the complete assembly on our test bench.
