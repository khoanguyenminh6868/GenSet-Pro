Product Information
Powered by genuine UK Perkins diesel engines, our generator sets feature stable operation, ultra-low noise, high fuel efficiency and simple maintenance, delivering reliable power solutions for harsh working conditions and critical power supply scenarios.
Core Configuration
Engine: Genuine Perkins 400/1100/1300/2000/4000 series engines adopt turbocharged intercooling and electronic fuel injection technology. Featuring low vibration and long service life, the engines operate stably at -40℃~+50℃, 4000m altitude, as well as dusty, humid, and salt-spray corrosive environments.
Alternator: Brushless synchronous full-copper alternator with Class H insulation and IP22–IP23 protection grade. Maintenance-free design ensures stable voltage and frequency output, perfectly compatible with precision equipment and non-linear electrical loads.
Control System: LCD intelligent controller with real-time monitoring and full-range fault protection functions. It supports automatic start/stop, ATS power transfer, remote monitoring and cloud management, realizing 24/7 unattended intelligent operation.
Auxiliary System: Equipped with a professional cooling system, triple filtration device, heavy-duty vibration dampers, high-efficiency muffler, and integrated base frame, ensuring convenient installation and long-term stable operation.
Key Advantages
Ultra-quiet & Eco-friendly: Low-vibration structure ensures silent operation with noise ≤75 dB (A), perfectly suitable for urban areas, hospitals, and residential environments.
High Fuel Efficiency & Low Operation Cost: Optimized fuel injection system realizes sufficient combustion and stable low fuel consumption, effectively reducing long-term operating costs.
Durable & Low Maintenance: Industrial robust structure with long service intervals. Complete global spare parts supply system ensures convenient daily maintenance and stable long-term operation.
Strong Environmental Adaptability: Capable of stable operation in various extreme working conditions. Emissions comply with China Stage III, Euro III, and EPA Tier environmental standards.
Flexible & Versatile Deployment: Open-frame, silent canopy, mobile trailer, container, and high-voltage types are available. The units support multi-unit parallel operation for flexible capacity expansion.
Power Range & Applications
8–100kW: Shops, offices, telecommunication stations, residential standby power, field work, and daily emergency power supply.
100–1000kW: Hospitals, hotels, industrial factories, shopping malls, data centers and mines.
1000–2000kW: Large data centers, industrial parks, ports, offshore projects and oil & gas fields.

Perkins Engine Platforms We Build Around
Perkins has supplied power engines for industrial and electric power applications for decades, with more than 21 million engines produced and a global distributor and service network behind the product. We select the platform by duty, load profile and site conditions rather than by nominal kW alone.
400 series — 403 and 404 families for compact 8–40kW units; naturally aspirated and turbocharged variants suit telecom shelters, small commercial standby and mobile plant.
1100 series — 1103, 1104 and 1106 engines covering roughly 24–200kW; the workhorse band for hospitals, hotels, retail and light industrial standby.
1200 series — 1204 and 1206 six-cylinder units for 100–250kW prime and standby duty with strong block stiffness and low vibration.
1500 / 1700 series — 1506 and 1706 engines for 200–320kW, frequently specified where load steps are large or ambient temperatures are high.
2200 / 2400 / 2500 series — 2206, 2406 and 2506 engines for 280–500kW, common in data centre and industrial park standby systems.
2800 series — 2806 six-cylinder engines for 440–780kW, used in containerised and paralleled installations.

Reading the Model Table
Model designations follow a simple pattern: AWT-P plus the standby rating in kW. AWT-P400 is a 400kW standby set, which corresponds to 364kW prime power and 455kVA standby output at 50Hz. Both figures appear in the table because the correct rating depends on how the set will run.
Standby power — for outage backup under a defined annual hour limit; the rating used for most emergency systems.
Prime power — for continuous supply with variable load, such as remote sites or grid-support duty.
Continuous power — for steady base load at a constant rating, selected where the set is the primary source.
Open-type and silent-type dimensions and weights are listed separately for each model, so structural, transport and foundation planning can proceed before the unit is built. Silent canopies hold noise at or below 75dB(A), which keeps units acceptable in urban, hospital and residential locations.

Emissions, Standards and Compliance
Perkins powered sets in this series are built to comply with ISO 8528 and GB/T 2820 for generator set performance. Engine emissions configurations are available to China Stage III, Euro III and EPA Tier requirements, with the applicable standard matched to the destination market and the engine platform selected.
Where a project requires a specific emissions tier, state it at enquiry stage. The tier determines the engine variant, aftertreatment arrangement and, in some cases, the enclosure layout, so it is treated as a fixed input rather than a late adjustment.
Testing and Acceptance Before Delivery
Each set passes through staged quality control rather than a single final check: incoming inspection of key components, assembly process verification, electrical safety checks and mechanical fitting inspection. Before dispatch, units undergo full-load testing from 0 to 110% of rated capacity, step load response testing, continuous operation endurance testing, voltage and frequency stability testing, and emergency protection system verification.
Test records accompany the unit. For projects with a formal acceptance protocol, we agree the witness test points, load steps and recording format before production so the factory test and the site commissioning test follow the same sequence.
Installation, Interfaces and Site Requirements
The integrated base frame carries the engine, alternator and control panel as one aligned assembly, which reduces foundation work and shortens installation. Planning inputs we confirm with the project team include:
Foundation or plinth dimensions and anti-vibration mounting arrangement
Exhaust routing, silencer position and clearance from combustible surfaces
Combustion and cooling air paths, plus radiator discharge direction

Maintenance, Spare Parts and Serviceability
Perkins engines are supported by a worldwide distributor and service network, and each engine carries its own operation and maintenance manual covering service intervals and procedures. We supply genuine spare parts for engine components, alternator parts, electrical and control system parts, filters and consumables, and provide installation guidance, operation support and technical troubleshooting after commissioning.
Service access is considered at build stage: canopy doors, filter positions and drain points are arranged so routine maintenance can be carried out without dismantling the enclosure.

Frequently Asked Questions
What power range do your Perkins powered sets cover?
The series runs from 8kW to 3400kW in both 50Hz and 60Hz builds. Small sets use 403 and 404 engines, mid-range units use 1100, 1200, 1500 and 1700 series engines, and the largest sets use 2200 through 4016 platforms.
Can you supply a set for a specific emissions tier?
Yes. Emissions configurations are available to China Stage III, Euro III and EPA Tier standards. The destination market and required tier are treated as fixed inputs at enquiry stage because they determine the engine variant and aftertreatment layout.
How is the set tested before it leaves your factory?
Every unit undergoes full-load testing from 0 to 110% of rated capacity, step load response testing, continuous endurance running, voltage and frequency stability checks and emergency protection verification, on top of incoming component inspection and assembly quality control.
Can the generator integrate with our existing monitoring system?
We supply Deep Sea, SmartGen or ComAp controllers, or a control system built to your specified protocol. Remote monitoring and cloud management are supported, along with ATS transfer and multi-unit paralleling where the site needs it.
What enclosure formats are available?
Open-frame, silent canopy, mobile trailer, container and high-voltage formats are all available. Silent canopies hold noise at or below 75dB(A), and container builds fix transport dimensions and lifting points at design stage.
How do we obtain a configuration and quotation?
Send the duty mode, connected load and largest motor, voltage and frequency, site ambient and altitude, enclosure preference, control and ATS interface, emissions tier and acceptance requirements. With those inputs we can fix the engine platform, alternator, enclosure and control architecture and return a complete configuration.
