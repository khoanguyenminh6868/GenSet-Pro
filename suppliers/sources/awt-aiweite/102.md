Product Information
Powered by mature, authentic Yuchai diesel engines, our generator sets deliver stable, fuel-efficient, and cost-effective power solutions for standby, prime, and continuous power applications across industrial and civilian scenarios worldwide.
Core Configuration
Engine: Genuine Yuchai YC series water-cooled diesel engines (4/6/12/20-cylinder) adopt turbocharged intercooling and high-pressure common rail injection. Featuring high torque reserve and strong overload capacity, the engines run stably at extreme temperatures, high altitude, dusty, humid, and salt-spray environments, with easily accessible universal spare parts and reliable global after-sales support.
Alternator: Maintenance-free brushless synchronous full-copper alternator with Class H insulation and IP22–IP23 protection. It provides stable voltage and frequency with low distortion (G3 power quality), fully compatible with precision instruments, medical equipment and non-linear industrial loads.
Control System: Chinese-English LCD intelligent controller with full fault protection. It supports automatic start/stop, ATS transfer, 4G remote monitoring, cloud management and parallel operation, enabling unattended intelligent operation.
Auxiliary System: Equipped with a closed cooling system, triple filtration, vibration dampers, high-efficiency muffler and integrated base frame for simple installation, convenient maintenance and enhanced operational stability.
Key Advantages
Decades of Proven Stable Quality
Backed by 40+ years of manufacturing experience and massive global field verification, Yuchai-powered gensets feature strong heavy-load resistance, low failure rate, and long service life, reliably adapting to diverse harsh industrial working conditions.
Superior Fuel Economy & Low Operating Cost
Precision common rail injection ensures sufficient fuel combustion and low fuel consumption. Universal spare parts and standardized maintenance greatly reduce long-term operational and upkeep costs for higher economic benefits.
Strong Environmental & Working Condition Adaptability
The units maintain stable rated output in high-altitude, low-temperature, dusty, humid, and coastal corrosive environments. Optional cold-start preheating, anti-corrosion and dust-proof customization adapts to extreme climates and special working conditions.
Standard Emission & Low Noise Performance
The gensets meet China National III/IV non-road emission standards. The silent version noise is ≤75dB(A), eco-friendly and suitable for urban, medical and other noise-sensitive areas.
Diversified & Flexible Deployment
Available in open-frame, silent, trailer-mounted, containerized, and high-voltage types. The gensets support parallel operation and personalized customization, fitting both small emergency backup and large-scale industrial power supply scenarios.
Power Range & Applications
10–100kW: Household backup, farms, shops, offices, telecom base stations, small construction and emergency power supply.
100–1000kW: Factories, malls, hotels, hospitals, municipal engineering and medium mines.
1000–4200kW: Industrial parks, large data centers, ports, oil & gas fields and offshore facilities.
Optional Accessories & Customization
Optional configurations: ATS switch, paralleling cabinet, 4G remote monitoring system; silent enclosure, rain canopy, mobile trailer, integrated container power station; cold-start preheater, enlarged fuel tank, explosion-proof and anti-corrosion customization; genuine spare parts and full-lifecycle maintenance services.

Genuine Yuchai Engine Platform
Every Aiweite Yuchai set is assembled around an authentic Yuchai YC-series water-cooled diesel engine. The 4-, 6-, 12- and 20-cylinder families use turbocharged intercooling and high-pressure common rail injection, giving high torque reserve and strong overload capacity. These engines hold rated output in high-altitude, low-temperature, dusty, humid and salt-spray environments.
Because Yuchai maintains a broad global parts and service network, wear components and filters remain easy to source. Our factory builds the complete package — engine, alternator, control system, cooling, exhaust and base frame — so the mechanical and electrical interfaces are matched before shipment.

A closed cooling system, triple filtration, vibration dampers, high-efficiency muffler and integrated base frame complete the package for straightforward installation and maintenance.
