MTU Powered Diesel Generator Sets from Yangzhou Aiweite
Yangzhou Aiweite Electromechanical Co., Ltd. manufactures MTU Powered Diesel Generator Sets for data centers, hospitals, petrochemical plants and national infrastructure where a single outage is unacceptable. We integrate genuine MTU industrial diesel engines with high-precision full-copper brushless alternators and intelligent control systems, covering 20kW to 4000kW in standby, prime and continuous duty.
Every unit is assembled in our Yangzhou factory under ISO 8528 and GB/T 2828 process controls, then full-load tested before dispatch. Our engineering team configures each set around the load profile, site environment and electrical interface you specify.
Why MTU Engines Suit Mission-Critical Power
MTU industrial engines use electronic fuel injection and turbocharged intercooling with an integral rigid block. The result is smooth power delivery, fast load pickup and stable output in cold, hot, high-altitude, humid and salt-spray conditions. Aiweite pairs these engines with maintenance-free brushless synchronous alternators rated Class H insulation and IP22–IP23 protection, meeting ISO G3 power quality for sensitive server, medical and process loads.
Engine: Original German MTU series, electronic fuel injection, turbocharged intercooling.
Alternator: Full-copper brushless synchronous, Class H insulation, IP22–IP23.
Control: Chinese-English LCD intelligent controller with multi-dimensional fault protection.
Auxiliary: Closed circulating cooling, triple filtration, damping absorbers, high-efficiency muffler, integrated base frame.
Available Range and Configuration Options
Our MTU range spans 20kW to 4000kW. Standard builds are available as open-frame, silent canopy (≤75dB(A)), mobile trailer and containerized power stations. Electrical configuration supports 50Hz or 60Hz and global voltage standards, with control systems from Deep Sea, SmartGen, ComAp or a customized platform.

Controls, Automation and Parallel Operation
Our intelligent controllers support automatic start-stop, ATS automatic power switching, 4G remote monitoring and cloud management. Multi-unit parallel operation is available for larger plants, with load sharing and synchronization handled by the control system. The controller interface is configured to match your SCADA, BMS or plant DCS protocol so that alarms, status and metering data reach the operators who need them.
For data centers and hospitals, we typically supply the genset with an integrated paralleling cabinet and ATS, tested as a system before shipment. This reduces on-site interface risk and shortens commissioning time.
Installation, Utilities and Site Requirements
MTU sets are heavy and require a stable foundation, adequate combustion and cooling air, and exhaust routing that meets local code. Our documentation package includes foundation loading data, air and exhaust flow requirements, fuel system schematics and cable entry details. For containerized units, we provide the container outline, lifting points and internal layout so civil works can proceed in parallel with manufacturing.

Quality Control and Acceptance Testing
Every Aiweite MTU set passes incoming inspection of key components, assembly process verification, electrical safety checks and mechanical fitting inspection. Before dispatch, each unit undergoes full-load testing from 0–110% rated capacity, step load response testing, continuous operation endurance testing, voltage and frequency stability testing, and emergency protection system verification.
We can support third-party inspection and provide test reports, wiring diagrams, operation manuals and maintenance schedules as part of the delivery scope. Our factory operates under ISO 9001 and ISO 14001 management systems.
