Product Information
Driven by genuine French Baudouin industrial diesel engines, our generator sets feature premium load capacity, ultra-high stability and long service life. Delivering reliable high-end power solutions, they are widely adopted for critical standby and prime power scenarios worldwide.
Core Configuration
Engine: Genuine Baudouin series heavy-duty diesel engines adopt high-pressure common rail fuel injection and high-efficiency turbocharging intercooling technology. Equipped with replaceable wet cylinder liners and multi-turbo design, the engines support 0–100% full load instantaneous switching. They operate stably under high altitude, extreme temperature, dusty and salt-spray corrosive conditions, with ultra-long overhaul cycle and global standardized after-sales support.
Alternator: Maintenance-free brushless synchronous full-copper alternator with Class H insulation and IP22–IP23 protection rating. Complying with ISO G3 high power quality standard, it delivers ultra-stable voltage and frequency with low waveform distortion, fully adaptable to precision IT equipment, data center loads and non-linear industrial devices.
Control System: Chinese-English dual-language LCD intelligent controller with full-range fault protection. It supports automatic start/stop, ATS power transfer, 4G remote monitoring, cloud management and multi-unit parallel operation, realizing fully unattended intelligent operation.
Auxiliary System: Equipped with a closed-loop cooling system, high-efficiency triple filtration, professional vibration dampers, low-noise muffler, and integrated base frame. The optimized modular structure simplifies installation and daily maintenance while improving overall operational stability.
Key Advantages
Premium Industrial-Grade Durability
Built with European heavy-duty design, Baudouin engines feature a 100,000-hour overhaul lifespan and 15-year service reliability. Proven in global high-standard projects, the gensets have ultra-low failure rates and outstanding heavy-load resistance.
Fast Load Response & High Stability
Featuring low-inertia supercharging and precision fuel control, the units achieve instant full-load pickup with smooth power output. It ensures zero-delay power compensation for critical scenarios such as data center power failure.
Optimized Fuel Economy & Low Lifecycle Cost
Advanced high-pressure common rail technology enables full fuel combustion with 5% lower fuel consumption than conventional models. Long overhaul cycles and universal spare parts effectively reduce long-term operational and maintenance costs.
Strong Extreme Environment Adaptability
The gensets maintain stable rated power with minor attenuation under high altitude, high temperature, severe cold, dust, and coastal corrosion environments. Customized cold-start, dust-proof and anti-corrosion solutions are available for special working conditions.
Eco-Friendly & Flexible Deployment
Meets China National III/IV non-road emission standards and EPA emission requirements. The silent version achieves noise ≤75dB(A). Multiple types, including open-frame, silent canopy, trailer-mounted, containerized, and high-voltage models, are optional, supporting personalized customization and parallel operation.
Power Range & Applications
15–100kW: Commercial buildings, offices, shops, small facilities, and daily emergency backup power.
100–1000kW: Hospitals, malls, hotels, municipal engineering, medium factories and mines.
1000–5000kW: Large data centers, industrial parks, ports, oil & gas fields, offshore islands and core industrial power stations.

Baudouin Powered Diesel Generator Sets Industry knowledge
Yangzhou Aiweite Electromechanical Co., Ltd. manufactures Baudouin Powered Diesel Generator Sets from 15 kW to 3000 kW, integrating genuine Baudouin heavy-duty diesel engines with full-copper brushless alternators and intelligent control systems. Since 2008 we have focused on generator production, system integration and installation, serving industrial, commercial and infrastructure projects worldwide.
The range covers standby, prime and continuous duty, with open-frame, silent canopy, trailer-mounted and containerized builds. Our factory in Yangzhou, China assembles, wires and load-tests each unit before shipment.
What Our Baudouin Powered Range Covers
Baudouin engines are French-designed industrial diesels with high-pressure common rail fuel injection, high-efficiency turbocharging and intercooling, replaceable wet cylinder liners and multi-turbo configurations on larger models. That architecture supports 0–100% load pickup and stable output under high altitude, high temperature, dust and salt-spray conditions.
We build the complete package around the engine: alternator, coupling, base frame, cooling, exhaust, fuel system, control panel and protection devices. Units are configured to ISO 8528 duty classes and tested against the electrical performance the project specifies.

Quality Control and Acceptance Testing
Every Baudouin powered set passes through incoming inspection of key components, assembly process verification, electrical safety checks and mechanical fitting inspection. Before delivery we run full-load testing from 0 to 110% of rated capacity, step load response testing, continuous operation endurance testing, voltage and frequency stability testing, and emergency protection system verification.
Test records, wiring diagrams, operation manuals and commissioning guidance are issued with the shipment. Where the project requires witnessed testing or third-party inspection, we coordinate the schedule and prepare the unit for the agreed acceptance protocol.

Maintenance and Serviceability
Baudouin engines use standardized parts and accessible service points, which keeps routine maintenance straightforward. We supply genuine engine components, alternator parts, control and electrical spares, filters and consumables to support long-term operation and reduce downtime risk.
Typical service intervals cover oil and filter changes, coolant checks, fuel filter replacement, battery and charger inspection, and annual load bank verification. Our support team provides installation guidance, operation support and technical troubleshooting after commissioning.
Frequently Asked Questions
What power range do you build with Baudouin engines?
Our Baudouin powered range runs from 15 kW to 3000 kW in 50 Hz and 60 Hz configurations, covering standby, prime and continuous duty. Larger ratings above roughly 1250 kW are normally supplied as open sets or containerized power stations.
Can the set run in parallel with another unit or the grid?
Yes. We supply paralleling cabinets and controllers that support multi-unit parallel operation, load sharing and synchronization with an existing distribution system. The control platform is selected to match the site protocol.
