Huaquan Generator Set Ordering Process
Submit Your Project Requirements
Clearly define your procurement needs, including the required equipment type (diesel generator set, gas generator set, or energy storage system) and the specific application scenario.
Confirm Layout and Technical Parameters
Based on the design blueprints or site requirements you provide, we will confirm the product's specific power rating, performance parameters, and layout design.
Place Order and Production Scheduling
Upon signing the official contract, a down payment is required. We will then immediately initiate the production process. The estimated production lead time is approximately 15 to 30 working days.
Delivery and Installation
Once manufacturing is complete, you are invited to perform the pre-shipment inspection. Upon your confirmation and final balance payment, we will arrange immediate shipment and provide subsequent on-site installation guidance.
