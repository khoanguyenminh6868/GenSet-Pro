Frequently Asked Questions
What is the fuel consumption of the YC 15kW generator?
The approximate fuel consumption at 75% load is 3.5 L/h of diesel. Actual consumption varies depending on actual load and operating conditions. We recommend using high-quality diesel (sulfur content below 50 ppm) for maximum efficiency.
What is the difference between Prime Power and Standby Power?
Prime Power (15kW) is the maximum power the generator can provide continuously with variable load. Standby Power (16kW) is the maximum power for emergency situations only, limited to 200 operating hours per year.
Is the generator suitable for outdoor use?
Yes, the generator comes with an external steel enclosure with anti-corrosion and UV-resistant coating. We recommend installing the generator under a canopy or roof for protection from direct rain while ensuring adequate ventilation for engine cooling.
How often do I need to service the generator?
We recommend routine maintenance every 250 operating hours (engine oil and oil/fuel filter change), and a comprehensive inspection every 500 hours (spark plugs, fuel injectors, belt tension). Major servicing every 2000 operating hours includes cylinder head inspection and fluid replacement.
Can the voltage and frequency be customized for my country?
Yes, the generator is available at 400/230V 50Hz or 480/277V 60Hz as per your requirement. We can also provide versions at 380V, 415V, or 440V. Please inform the sales team of your voltage and frequency requirements when placing your order.
What is the delivery and shipping time?
Local stock is typically available within 3-5 working days. International shipping takes 15-30 days depending on the destination. We offer sea and air freight with professional packaging and international export standards. All generators undergo a full 4-hour operational test before shipping.
Does the price include Automatic Transfer Switch (ATS)?
The standard price does not include ATS. An ATS panel can be added as an optional extra. We recommend using ATS for applications requiring automatic startup upon grid failure, especially in hospitals and data centers.
What are the warranty terms and after-sales service?
We offer a comprehensive 2-year or 3000 operating hours warranty (whichever comes first) on engine and alternator. Service includes free spare parts replacement during warranty period, remote technical support, and a network of authorized dealers in over 30 countries for local maintenance.
