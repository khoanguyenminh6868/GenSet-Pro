Perkins (7kW - 1800kW)
Perkins, founded in 1932 in Peterborough, England, is one of the world's longest-established diesel engine brands. Now part of Caterpillar's portfolio, Perkins engines are trusted in over 170 countries for their compact footprint, low vibration characteristics, and compliance with stringent global emission standards. Their extensive dealer and service network provides unmatched aftermarket support worldwide.
