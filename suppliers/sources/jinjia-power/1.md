About Jinjia Power
Professional Diesel Generator Manufacturer Since 2011
Company Introduction
Dongguan Jinjia Power Equipment Co., Ltd. was founded in 2011 and is located in Dongguan City, Guangdong Province, China. As a professional diesel generator manufacturer, we specialize in R&D, production, sales and service of diesel generator sets with over 15 years of deep industry experience.
Our factory covers an area of 20,000 square meters with over 200 skilled workers and a complete production line. We produce standard diesel generators, silent generators, container generators, marine generators and parallel control systems. All products undergo strict quality inspection before shipment.
Jinjia Power has been ISO 9001:2015 certified and our products have been exported to over 80 countries and regions across Africa, Southeast Asia, the Middle East, South America and beyond. We are committed to providing reliable power solutions for industrial, commercial, marine and infrastructure projects worldwide.
