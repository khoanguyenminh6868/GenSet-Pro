System Overview
Jinjia Power parallel and grid connection system is engineered for large-scale power supply scenarios requiring multiple generator sets to operate in sync. Featuring advanced DeepSea controllers, the system achieves seamless multi-unit parallel operation with automatic load sharing, ensuring optimal fuel efficiency and even wear across all generator units.
The intelligent control system supports automatic mains failure (AMF) detection, automatic mains restoration (AMR), and seamless transfer between utility power and generator power. Remote monitoring and control via GSM/4G/LAN enables true unattended power station operation, reducing operational costs significantly.
Ideal for factories, data centers, industrial parks, mining operations, hospitals, and large commercial complexes requiring reliable, scalable, and redundant power solutions.
