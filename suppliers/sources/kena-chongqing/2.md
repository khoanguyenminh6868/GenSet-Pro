ABOUT US
Our company insists on survival by quality, market by reputation, development by science and technology, and benefit by management. We adhere to the principle of honesty and self-transcendence, and are willing to join hands with Chinese and foreign enterprises and all walks of life to advance with the times, cooperate and win-win, and become a modern high-tech enterprise. Relying on advanced business philosophy, scientific management, professional talents, excellent technology, high-quality products, and high-quality services, our company continues to grow and develop. We warmly welcome friends from the business circles at home and abroad to come to discuss cooperation, seek common development, and jointly create a more brilliant tomorrow.
Our Vision
Leading brand of low carbon power and intelligent products
Double carbon responsibility, leading green life. To uphold the concept of low carbon and environmental protection, build a full-scene industrial ecology of smart manufacturing, smart operation and smart service, provide people with low-carbon power and smart products for their home travel, and become a brand loved and trusted by global consumers.
Our Mission
Focus on technology and innovation to drive a better life
Create value with technology, lead the future with innovation, and make people's home life and travel experience more convenient, comfortable and enjoyable.
Keeping in mind our original mission, we will continue to strive to drive a beautiful life with technology and innovation, enhance people's well-being, and continuously meet people's aspirations for a better life.
Our Values
A strong country with strong industry, customer first and common prosperity
If the industry is strong, the country is strong; if the industry is prosperous, the nation is prosperous; if you are rich, think of the source, think of the progress; give back to the society and serve the country with industry. Customer first, struggle-based, contribution-oriented, enterprises, employees and partners to co-prosperity, win-win sharing, to achieve common prosperity
