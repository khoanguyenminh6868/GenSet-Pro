COMPANY PROFILE
The division has three engine production bases, one transmission production base, and one axle production base, with a standard production capacity of 362,000 engines, 271,000 transmissions, and 400,000 axles. The Huishan base in Wuxi, a heavy-duty vehicle engine production base, officially commenced production in 2012. Its primary products are the CA6DX, CA6SX, CA6DM, and CA6SM engines. In 2016, the base was selected for the Ministry of Industry and Information Technology's Smart Manufacturing Pilot Demonstration Project. In 2019, it completed the intelligent transformation and upgrade of its production lines, becoming China's first "smart factory" for heavy-duty vehicle engines. In February 2023, it completed the construction of an industry-leading smart manufacturing "super factory."
By 2024, the division will have produced a total of 197,000 engines, 82,000 transmissions, and 380,000 axles, with cumulative sales of 198,000 engines, 80,000 transmissions, and 382,000 axles, achieving total operating revenue of 15.97 billion yuan. From January to March 2025, the division produced a total of 51,000 engines, 27,000 transmissions, and 110,000 axles, with cumulative sales of 53,000 engines, 23,000 transmissions, and 99,000 axles, achieving total operating revenue of 4 billion yuan. Over 66,000 of these engines were exported. To date, the division has exported over 280,000 diesel engines, with sales covering 67 countries and regions worldwide.
