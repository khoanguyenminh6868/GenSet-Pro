Power Generation Solutions designed for every industry
At Kirloskar Oil Engines, we deliver high-performance gensets ranging from 2.8 kW to 3000 kVA, engineered to meet the evolving energy needs of industries. Backed by advanced engineering and continuous research, our generators deliver reliable performance while meeting stringent emission norms and adapting to fluctuating power demands. Our tailor-made, intelligent power solutions partner industries in paving the way for a future where energy systems are cleaner, flexible and reliable. A future that is limitless.
High-Performance, Compact Solution
Delivering high output and durability in a compact footprint, our gensets are designed for efficiency, reliability and ease of integration across diverse applications.
End-to-End Power Solutions
From concept and system design to integration and commissioning, our teams deliver complete power solutions tailored to specific operational needs, ensuring seamless performance and dependable backup.
Intelligent Power Management
Our IoT-enabled systems offer real-time monitoring, energy optimisation and advanced analytics. With capabilities like digital twins, we help businesses improve efficiency, reduce costs and meet emission standards.
Fuel Flexibility for a Sustainable Future
Our fuel-agnostic solutions provide the flexibility to choose energy sources based on operational and environmental needs, enabling lower emissions and improved efficiency.
Future-Ready Technologies
We offer a diverse portfolio that includes microgrids, battery storage, fuel cells, reformers and electrolysers, helping businesses transition to more resilient and sustainable energy systems.
Proven Expertise, Global Presence
With over a million installations worldwide, our solutions are trusted across industries. Our technical expertise and customer-focused approach ensure consistent performance and long-term reliability.
