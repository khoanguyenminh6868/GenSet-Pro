FAQ’s
Is the Kirloskar 400 kVA Diesel Generator affordable?
We offer competitive diesel generator prices, ensuring that you get the best value for your investment. The 400 kVA generator price varies depending on the model and features, but we guarantee you get the highest quality for the cost.
What are the key benefits of a diesel generator?
DG sets are fuel-efficient, consuming less fuel than gas engines, which makes them cost-effective for long-term use. Known for their durability, diesel generators are built to handle heavy loads and perform reliably in demanding conditions. They also require less maintenance due to their robust design and the absence of spark plugs. With a high power output, diesel generators are ideal for applications requiring consistent and strong energy supply. Additionally, diesel fuel is less flammable, which enhances safety by reducing the risk of fire or explosion.
How do I determine the right kVA rating for my needs?
To determine the right kVA rating for your needs, you must first assess the total power requirements of the equipment or systems you intend to run. Start by calculating the total wattage of all devices, then consider factors like starting power surges for motors or machinery, and whether you need the generator for continuous or backup use. Additionally, account for future growth or additional power needs. Consulting a professional can help ensure you select the appropriate kVA rating to match your power demands and avoid under or over-sizing the generator.
How reliable are gensets and what is their expected lifespan?
Our gensets are one of the most reliable products available in the market. They have the best in class fuel efficiency due to an advanced V type cylinder engine, and sophisticated features such as a CRDi fuel injection system, high strength materials, and a high temperature capability to withstand harsh weather conditions. The expected life-span varies depending on the type of genset and daily usage. Our generator sets have 500+ hrs of running capability.
How noisy are gensets and are there noise reduction options?
Noise levels for gensets vary by size. Our gensets come with sound-attenuated enclosures called canopies, which reduce the noise emitted by the engine. We have a silencer placed outside of the canopy which helps reduce noise pollution, along with other external noise reduction options like mufflers and acoustic barriers. We also have the capability of customizing the genset to meet the customer’s specific needs.
