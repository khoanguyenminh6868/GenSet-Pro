Company Profile
Fuzhou Hosem Power Co., Ltd. is one of the most famous diesel generator manufacturers in China. The Hosem brand generator set output range from 3kVA to 3000kVA with frequency at 50Hz or 60Hz, the voltage of the diesel generator from 110kVA to 690V for client options.
The diesel generator powered by Cummins diesel engine, Deutz diesel engine, Perkins diesel engine, Volvo diesel engine, Fawde diesel engine, Yuchai diesel engine, Ricardo diesel engine and other famous brand diesel engine, Coupled with Stamford alternator, Leroy Somer alternator, Marathon alternator, Mecc Alte alternator and Hosem brand alternator for optional.
Hosem Power uses the most famous brand controller for our diesel generator, include Deepsea brand with model DSE7320, DSE6020 and DSE7420 controller, ComAp brand with model MSR10, AMF25, Nano and IC-NT controller, Smartgen brand with HGM6110, HGM6120, HGM7110, HGM7210 and other models.
Hosem Power can supply the open-type industrial generator, marine generator, natural gas generator, biogas generator, soundproof diesel generator, trailer diesel Generator and Ground Power Unit. The Hosem Power diesel generators are widely used in the Island as the continuing power, hospital, highway, airport, factories and big mall.
We supply one year or 1000 working hours warranty for all of our generator set.
