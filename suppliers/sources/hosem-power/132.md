Product Features
Powered by the genuine 4-cylinder FAWDE engine model 4DX21-61D, ensuring strong mechanical durability and low fuel consumption.
Equipped with a standard premium brushless alternator featuring Class H insulation and IP23 protection grade (Stamford and Leroy Somer options available).
Integrated controller panel fitted with standard high-quality Delixi circuit breakers, with ABB or Schneider MCCB available for specific project demands.
Uses a dependable 12V electric starter motor system paired with an included maintenance-free battery for fast emergency ignition.
Built directly on a rigid steel base frame with an integrated fuel tank, providing over 8 hours of continuous runtime.
Every delivery package comes complete with a factory test report, manual, layout drawings, and standard tool kit.
Built-in AVR consistently maintains clean, non-fluctuating power output to safely protect sensitive electronic equipment.
An external emergency stop button is recessed directly on the silent canopy body to protect your genset instantly for safety.
