Introduction
Fuan Huawei Electrical Machinery Co.,Ltd Fujian is the joint venture formalluy established in 1992. Our annual production capacity of various is 600 thousand sets. We specialize in producing and exporting all series of alternator, diesel generator set, electric motor, water pump and gasoline generator set. Now it is the an export base enterprise of Chinese electric and mechanical products, one of the key enterprises in Fujian electric and mechanical companies and one of the key supported enterprises of the Ningde private companies. All our products are exported in the abroad and get a high reputation from our all foreign customers. 　　It covers 12 thousand square meters. With asset RMB30 million, our company has a high quality management team, a well-trained producing team and more than 100 sets of production and test machine. Since its establishment, our company always sticks to the principle "Management by Norm, Existance by Quality and Development by Products" and also insists on the guidline of "Target for Market, Technology as Method, Effeciency as Purpose and Leading by the Advanced Technology". We always insist on improving quality and new items development depending on technology streghthen and advanced management. 　　Our main products include: ST, STC series alternator and SD/SDC series generator&welding electric alternator, GF series diesel generator and low noise generator, HW series 650 watts, 850 watts, 2KW, 3KW, 4KW and 5KW gasonline generator, QB,DB,KF,STP,JET,CPM,SCM,AUJDP etc series electric peripheral water pump, centrifugal water pump and self-sucking water pump, Y,YC,YY,MC,ML etc single phase, three phase electric motor and some specail motors. 　　All above-mentioned products have been tested by Fujian province laboratories and have got the CCC Certificate from China Quality Approval Centre. Our company is the first passed ISO9001(2000 version) Assurance in the same industry. Meanwhile our most products have been approved by CE and GS marks.
