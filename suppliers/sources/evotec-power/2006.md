EVO368 Series Industrial Generator 563/960kVA，400/480V，50/60Hz，2/4/6/8/10Pole
EvoTec's TEVO528 series alternator is a reliable, high-performance solution designed to operate and generate electricity in various applications. With over 67 national patents, this series supports 400V and 480V generators, providing excellent energy conversion efficiency, a well-ventilated structure, and an easy maintenance design concept, reducing operating costs.
The alternator's compact and shockproof design allows for versatile use in various scenarios. At the same time, the additional marine varnish ensures moisture-proof, mold-proof, and anti-fogging properties, greatly extending the service life of this single-phase alternator.
With a rating of 2500/3300kVA and 400/480V output for industrial Gensets, the TCU528 series self-exciting alternator is specifically designed for use with 2500 kVA and 3000 kVA generators.
