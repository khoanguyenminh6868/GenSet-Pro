EVO468 Series Industrial Alternator 1650/2700kVA，400/480V，50/60Hz，2/4/6/8/10Pole
EVO468 series industrial alternator is a high-power self-excitation alternator that provides power from 1650 to 2700kVA (2/4/6/8/10 Pole). The reconnection between single-phase and three-phase is possible. TCU468 series supports 400V / 480V generator, providing excellent energy conversion efficiency, good ventilation structure and easy maintenance design concept, which greatly reduces the use cost.
With a short structure and shockproof design, the EVO468 series is ideal for more conditions, and additional marine varnish ensures it is moisture-proof, mold-proof, and anti-fogging. This will greatly extend the service life of this high-power alternator.
We have more than 67 national patents in alternator design and manufacturing. A reliable and high-performance alternator can run and generate electricity in different applications, bringing the maximum benefits to you.
