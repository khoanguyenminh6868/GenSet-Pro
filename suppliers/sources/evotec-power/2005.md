EVO318 Series Industrial Generator 381/600kVA，400/480V，50/60Hz，2/4/6/8/10Pole
EvoTec offers a reliable and high-performance alternator designed for various applications, delivering maximum benefits to customers. With over 67 national patents, our alternators feature original design and a no-copy appearance, setting us apart in the industry.
The EVO318 series alternator supports 600kVA generators/single-phase AC generators . It provides excellent energy conversion efficiency, a robust ventilation structure, and an easy maintenance design, all of which significantly reduce operating costs.
Its compact structure and shockproof design make it adaptable for a variety of scenarios. With a high protection class (IP21-IP54) and marine varnish coating, the alternator is well-protected against environmental intrusion, ensuring long-lasting durability.

1. 67 nationally authorized patents, including 21 invention patents.

4. Quality materials procured from authoritative sources;
