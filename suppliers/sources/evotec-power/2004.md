EVO288 Series Industrial Generator 175/420kVA，400/480V，50/60Hz，2/4/6/8/10Pole
EvoTec's EVO288 series alternator is a reliable, high-performance solution designed to operate and generate electricity in various applications, bringing maximum customer benefits. With over 67 national patents, this series supports 400V and 480V generators, providing excellent energy conversion efficiency, a well-ventilated structure, and an easy maintenance design concept, reducing operating costs.
The alternator's compact and shockproof design allows for versatile use in various scenarios. At the same time, the additional marine varnish ensures moisture-proof, mold-proof, and anti-fogging properties, greatly extending the service life of this single-phase alternator.
This self-developed industrial generator end (alternator) TCU288 series is rated at 175/420kVA and designed to keep industrial operations running with a 175-400 kVA generator.
Advantages:
1. Shorter dimension design;
a. Better Ventilation and heat dissipation;
b. Substantial cost saving;
2. Unique and Prominent Appearance，High Identification;
a. NO Copy
b. Original and Patented
3. Additional marine varnish, better protection;
4. 67 nationally authorized patents, including 21 invention patents.
