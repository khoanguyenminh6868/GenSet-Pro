Industrial Generator
EvoTec’s industrial alternators are versatile solutions, designed as both land-use AC alternators and three-phase synchronous alternators. These 3-phase alternators are the workhorses of the power generation sector, offering higher power capacity compared to DC generators with better voltage regulation. EvoTec holds independent intellectual property rights and owns a strong brand in the industry. Our alternators for generators cover a wide power range from 6.8 to 6000KW, featuring a compact and space-efficient design, excellent ventilation, and heat dissipation. This design helps maintain low motor temperature rise, ultimately reducing user costs.
These industrial electric generators offer high power generation efficiency, low temperature rise, excellent dynamic performance, low waveform distortion, stable AVR parallel operation, and compatibility with various power systems. EvoTec’s generador industrial products are designed for commercial alternator applications, ensuring reliable performance in a variety of industrial settings.
