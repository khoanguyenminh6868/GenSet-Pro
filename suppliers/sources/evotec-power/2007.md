EVO428 Series Industrial Generator 913/1800kVA，400/480V，50/60Hz，2/4/6/8/10Pole
EvoTec offers a reliable and high-performance alternator that can run and generate electricity across various applications, delivering maximum benefits to customers. With more than 67 national patents, EvoTec stands out with its self-excited AC generators and innovative design.
The EVO428 series alternator supports 400V generators / 480V generators , offering excellent energy conversion efficiency, a well-ventilated structure, and an easy-to-maintain design concept, all of which significantly reduce operational costs.
With its compact structure and shockproof design, this generator is suitable for a variety of scenarios. The additional marine varnish ensures it is moisture-proof, mould-proof, and anti-fogging. These features greatly extend the service life of this single-phase alternator .
