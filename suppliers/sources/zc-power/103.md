Capacity Guide
What Can a 100kW Generator Actually Power?
A 100kW (125 kVA) commercial generator delivers substantial backup capacity for small-to-medium facilities. In real-world terms, this means:
Complete HVAC system for a 15,000-20,000 sq ft commercial building
Full refrigeration and kitchen equipment for a mid-size restaurant
Critical server racks and cooling for a small data center
All medical equipment and climate control for a clinic
Fuel consumption at full load: Diesel models consume approximately 7-9 gallons per hour. At 50% load—the typical operating point for standby applications—consumption drops to roughly half.
Fuel Options
Diesel vs. Natural Gas: Choosing the Right Fuel Type
Your fuel choice affects installation requirements, operating costs, and environmental compliance. Here is how the options compare for 10-100 kW commercial applications:
Diesel Generators: Quick cold-start capability, high power density, widely available fuel globally, long storage life. Best for remote locations without natural gas infrastructure, facilities requiring prime power, and areas with frequent extended outages.
Natural Gas Generators: Cleaner combustion (lower NOx and particulates), no fuel storage required, quieter operation, often lower maintenance costs. Best for urban facilities with strict emissions regulations and standby applications where utility gas is reliable.
Our recommendation: For standby backup applications in urban or suburban settings with reliable natural gas service, natural gas gensets offer simpler compliance and lower total cost of ownership. For remote sites, heavy-duty cycles, or locations without gas infrastructure, diesel remains the proven standard.

FAQ
Frequently Asked Questions About 10-100 kW Commercial Generators
How do I calculate what size generator I need for my business?
Start by listing all equipment that must operate during an outage. Record running watts and starting watts (motors need 2-3x running power to start). Sum the running watts and add the highest single starting surge. Multiply by 1.25 for safety margin. For quick estimation: retail spaces need 50kW base + 10kW per 1,000 sq ft; offices need 50kW base + 5kW per 1,000 sq ft. Our engineers provide complimentary load assessments for verified projects.
What is the total cost of ownership for a 100kW commercial generator?
Beyond equipment cost ($32,000-$58,000), budget for: installation ($3,000-$12,000 depending on site complexity and distance from electrical panel), ATS ($1,500-$5,000), fuel system ($2,000-$8,000 for diesel tanks), and annual maintenance ($800-$1,500). Over 10 years, fuel typically represents 60-70% of total ownership cost—making fuel efficiency and right-sizing critical to ROI.
How loud is a 100kW generator, and will it meet noise regulations?
Unenclosed diesel generators produce 90+ dB at 7 meters—unsuitable for most commercial environments. Our standard sound-attenuated canopies reduce this to 70-75 dB. Upgraded silent enclosures achieve 65-68 dB, comparable to normal conversation levels. Most urban jurisdictions require under 70 dB at property lines. We provide noise calculations and mitigation recommendations for your specific site.
What maintenance does a commercial standby generator require?
Standby generators require minimal maintenance: weekly visual inspections (fuel level, no leaks), monthly exercise runs (automatic via controller), quarterly oil and filter changes (for frequent exercisers), and annual comprehensive service including coolant, fuel filter, air filter, and load bank testing. Natural gas units typically need less frequent service than diesel. We supply maintenance schedules and OEM parts for all units we manufacture.
Can I parallel multiple 10-100 kW generators for redundancy or expansion?
Yes. Paralleling requires generators with compatible digital governors and voltage regulators, plus a paralleling control system. This approach provides N+1 redundancy (if one unit fails, others carry load) and allows phased capacity expansion. We design and supply fully integrated paralleling systems for critical facilities requiring highest reliability.
What certifications do your commercial generators carry?
Standard units carry ISO9001 quality certification and CE marking for European market access. EPA Tier certification available for North American diesel applications. CCC certification for China domestic market. Additional certifications (ISO8528, NFPA 110, UL 2200-equivalent testing) available on request. Full technical documentation including test certificates provided with each shipment.
How long can a standby generator run continuously?
Standby-rated generators are designed for emergency operation during utility outages, not continuous prime power duty. Typical standby ratings allow unlimited annual hours but average load factor must not exceed 70% of standby rating. With adequate fuel supply and maintenance, a well-designed standby genset can operate for weeks during extended outages. For continuous daily operation, specify prime power rated units.

For installation, we provide detailed electrical and mechanical installation drawings, remote technical consultation, and can recommend qualified electrical contractors in your region. Some markets have established installation partnerships—contact our team to discuss options for your specific destination.
