Key Features and Benefits
Copper-Wound AVR Alternator
A 100% copper-wound brushless alternator with Automatic Voltage Regulation continuously monitors output and adjusts excitation current to hold voltage within ±1% of rated — even as load conditions change.
Benefit: Protects computers, POS systems, medical refrigeration, and communication equipment from damaging voltage fluctuations.
Electric Start with Recoil Backup
Standard electric start (key or push-button) with a maintenance-free 12V battery ensures quick, reliable starting. A recoil backup starter is included on most models as a manual fallback.
Benefit: Practical redundancy for generators deployed in remote locations where battery charging facilities may be limited.
Comprehensive Protection Systems
Multiple automatic safeguards come standard: low-oil shutdown prevents catastrophic engine seizure, overload protection trips the breaker if capacity is exceeded, and the control panel monitors voltage and frequency in real time.
Benefit: Protects your investment and minimizes costly repairs through intelligent fault detection.
Global Voltage Configurations
Every generator is configured to your country’s grid standard — 50Hz or 60Hz, single-phase 110-240V or three-phase 380-415V — as a standard part of the order process, not an extra-cost option.
Benefit: Arrives ready to connect, with country-specific outlets and distribution panel layouts available.

Decision Guide
Single Cylinder vs. Twin Cylinder: Making the Right Choice
Choose Single-Cylinder When
Cost, Simplicity & Portability First
You prioritize purchase cost and simplicity. Fewer moving parts means a lower price, reduced spare parts inventory, and maintenance any general mechanic can perform.
Your load profile is relatively stable. Lighting, electronics, and continuous-run appliances are ideal steady-state loads.
You need portability. The ZC-5S (155 kg) and ZC-6.5S (165 kg) are manageable with lift points and an optional wheel kit.
You run intermittently. For standby use under 200 hours per year, the single-cylinder cost advantage far outweighs incremental twin-cylinder efficiency.
Consider Twin-Cylinder When
Heavy Continuous Loads & Smoothness
Your continuous load regularly exceeds 8kW for extended periods
You need to start large motors (3HP+) frequently while other equipment is running
You require the lowest possible vibration for elevated platforms or sensitive structures
Your application demands 24/7 continuous prime power operation
ZC Power offers both configurations — from single-cylinder 5kW portable units to V-twin and multi-cylinder generators up to 4,000kVA. Your engineer will recommend the most cost-effective configuration based on your specific load profile, not the highest-margin option.

FAQ
Frequently Asked Questions
What can a 5kW generator run?
A 5kW silent diesel generator can simultaneously power a refrigerator (200W), lighting (400W), TV and internet equipment (350W), a microwave (1,000W), and a small water pump (1,000W) — with roughly 1,000W of headroom after a 20% safety margin. It will typically not run central air conditioning or large well pumps; for those loads, consider the 8-10kW range.
How quiet are these silent diesel generators?
ZC Power single cylinder silent generators operate at 65-75 dB(A) at 7 meters, depending on model and load. For context: 65 dB is normal conversation; 75 dB is a vacuum cleaner at distance. Open-frame equivalents typically measure 85-95 dB — the silent canopy is roughly 10 to 15 times quieter in perceived loudness.
How long can a single-cylinder diesel generator run continuously?
With the standard 15L tank (25L on the ZC-10S), expect approximately 7.5 to 9 hours at 75% load. Optional 30L or 50L extended tanks are available for longer uninterrupted runtime, and all models can be refueled while running following proper safety procedures.
What is the fuel consumption per hour?
At 75% load: approximately 1.6 L/h (5kW), 2.0 L/h (6.5kW), and 2.8 L/h (10kW). Running at 50% load reduces fuel use by roughly 25-30%. Diesel also stores significantly longer and more safely than gasoline — important for standby units that sit idle for months.
What is the difference between single-cylinder and twin-cylinder?
Single-cylinder engines are simpler, lighter, and more affordable — best for stable loads and intermittent use. Twin-cylinder engines deliver smoother power with less vibration, handle fluctuating loads better, and suit extended continuous operation at higher outputs. In the 5-10kW range, single-cylinder is the cost-effective choice for most standby and portable applications.
Can I get the generator customized for my country’s voltage?
Yes. Every generator is configured to your destination country’s grid — 50Hz or 60Hz, single-phase (110-240V) or three-phase (380-415V) — as a standard part of the order process, not an extra-cost option. Tell us your country and application, and our engineers will configure the correct voltage, frequency, and outlet arrangement.
What does the 1-year warranty cover?
The warranty covers defects in materials and workmanship on the engine, alternator, control system, and canopy for 1 year or 1,000 operating hours from delivery. Claims are processed directly by ZC Power as the manufacturer — no intermediary — with replacement parts shipped by air freight to minimize downtime.
Can these generators connect to an automatic transfer switch (ATS)?
Yes. All models can be configured for ATS compatibility at the time of order. The generator will automatically start, assume the load, and shut down when grid power returns — no manual intervention. Ideal for unattended sites, telecom installations, or homes where the primary user may not be present during an outage.
What shipping options are available?
We ship worldwide from our factory in Jining, Shandong Province, China. Standard shipping is sea freight (FOB or CIF), with transit of 15-40 days depending on destination; air freight is available for urgent orders. All export documentation is prepared for you — the shipment arrives ready for customs clearance.
How do I maintain a single-cylinder diesel generator?
Routine maintenance is straightforward: check oil before each use, change oil and filter every 100-200 hours, replace the air filter as needed (more often in dusty environments), clean the fuel filter every 300 hours, and check battery charge monthly on standby units. The single-cylinder design means fewer components and simpler access than multi-cylinder engines.
