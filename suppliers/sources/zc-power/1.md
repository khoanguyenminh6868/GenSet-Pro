FAQ
Frequently Asked Questions
Find answers to the most common questions about our generator sets, ordering process, and technical specifications.
What is the lead time for a generator set order?
Standard diesel generator sets typically have a lead time of 15–25 working days from order confirmation. Containerized power plants and highly customized units may require 30–45 days. We maintain stock of popular models for urgent orders with delivery in as little as 7 days.
Can you customize the generator to my specific requirements?
Yes. We offer full customization including engine brand selection, alternator specification, canopy design and color, control panel configuration, fuel tank capacity, voltage/frequency (50Hz/60Hz), and parallel operation systems. Our engineering team will work with you to design a solution that meets your exact requirements.
What certifications do your generator sets carry?
All our generator sets carry ISO 9001 and ISO 14001 certification. Standard units are CE marked and SGS verified. For specific applications, we provide DNV GL, Bureau Veritas, ABS, and Lloyd’s Register approvals for marine use, as well as ATEX certification for hazardous area installations.
Do you provide installation and commissioning services?
Yes. We offer turnkey solutions that include site survey, installation supervision, commissioning, load testing, operator training, and ongoing maintenance contracts. For overseas projects, we dispatch experienced engineers to manage on-site installation and ensure everything runs smoothly.
What is the warranty period and what does it cover?
We provide a standard 12-month warranty or 1,000 running hours (whichever comes first) from the date of commissioning. The warranty covers manufacturing defects in the generator set including the engine, alternator, control system, and canopy. Extended warranty packages of up to 5 years are available for purchase.
What is the minimum order quantity (MOQ)?
Our minimum order is 1 unit. We serve customers ranging from small businesses needing a single backup generator to large-scale industrial projects requiring dozens of units. For OEM/ODM partnerships, we discuss volume-based pricing for orders of 10+ units.
