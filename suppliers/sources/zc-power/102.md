Engineering Quality You Can Rely On
Every gasoline power generator that leaves our Shandong facility is built to a single standard: reliable performance when it counts. Our generator sets are powered by 4-stroke, single-cylinder OHV engines with transistor-controlled ignition (TCI), delivering consistent starting, smooth operation, and fuel efficiency across the entire load range. The overhead valve design reduces carbon buildup and extends service intervals compared to side-valve alternatives.
Each engine is paired with a 100% copper-wound brushless alternator regulated by an automatic voltage regulator (AVR), keeping output voltage stable within ±5% of rated value—critical when powering sensitive electronics, variable-speed tools, or equipment with high inrush current.
Frames are heavy-duty tubular steel with engineered vibration-damping mounts and weather-resistant powder coating that resists corrosion in humid, coastal, and tropical environments. Every unit includes low-oil automatic shutdown and resettable circuit breakers on all AC outputs; select models offer optional carbon monoxide detection with automatic engine shutdown.

Frequently Asked Questions
What is a gasoline generator?
A gasoline generator is an engine-driven machine that converts the chemical energy in petrol into electrical power. A 4-stroke internal combustion engine spins a copper-wound alternator, producing AC electricity for tools, appliances, and equipment. Gasoline generators are the most common type of portable power equipment due to their compact size, low acquisition cost, and widespread fuel availability.
How long can a gasoline generator run continuously?
Most portable gasoline generators run 7 to 13 hours on a single tank at 50% load. Gasoline engines are designed for intermittent use—several hours at a time, then cooling down. For continuous 24/7 power, diesel generator sets are the more appropriate choice due to their liquid-cooled design and longer service life.
What size gasoline generator do I need?
Add up your total running wattage plus starting wattage for motor-driven devices. As a guide: 2–3kW covers basic home essentials; 5–7kW supports multiple appliances, a sump pump, and a microwave; 8–10kW runs a small workshop or construction tools. For a detailed calculation, send our engineering team your equipment list.
