FAQ
Frequently Asked Questions About Industrial Generators
How do I determine what size industrial generator I need?
Proper sizing requires analyzing your facility’s total electrical load, including both running watts and starting watts for motors and compressors. Our complimentary load analysis examines your electrical drawings, conducts on-site measurements where feasible, and calculates motor starting surges (typically 3-7 times running current). We also factor in future expansion plans and recommend appropriate capacity margins. Never rely on rule-of-thumb sizing—an undersized generator will fail under load, while an oversized unit wastes capital and fuel.
What is the difference between standby and prime power generators?
Standby generators are designed for emergency backup operation during grid outages, typically running 50-200 hours annually with regular exercise cycles. Prime power generators operate as the primary power source for extended periods—remote sites, off-grid operations, or areas with unreliable grids—running 500+ hours annually. Prime power units feature heavier-duty components, larger cooling systems, and enhanced maintenance access. ZC Power offers both configurations with appropriate warranties and service intervals for each application.
Should I choose diesel or natural gas for my commercial generator?
Diesel generators offer superior reliability, higher power density, and better cold-start performance. They’re ideal for heavy industrial applications, remote sites without natural gas infrastructure, and facilities requiring long runtime on stored fuel. Natural gas generators provide lower emissions, eliminate fuel storage requirements, and often have lower maintenance costs—but depend on utility gas availability. For many critical facilities, we recommend bi-fuel systems that start on diesel and transition to natural gas for extended runtime, combining diesel reliability with gas economics.

Commercial generator costs vary by capacity and configuration. Entry-level commercial units (20-100kW) range from $10,000-$50,000. Mid-range industrial units (100-500kW) cost $50,000-$150,000. Heavy industrial generators (500kW+) range from $150,000-$400,000+. Installation typically adds 15-25% including transfer switches, fuel systems, and electrical integration. ZC Power provides transparent factory-direct pricing and can recommend certified installation partners in your region. Contact our engineering team for a detailed quotation based on your specific requirements.
What maintenance does an industrial generator require?
Industrial generators require preventive maintenance every 250-500 operating hours or annually, whichever comes first. Standard service includes oil and filter changes, coolant inspection, fuel system checks, battery testing, and control system verification. Load bank testing (running the generator at full capacity) should be conducted annually to verify performance and prevent carbon buildup. ZC Power gensets feature accessible maintenance points and our engineering team provides detailed maintenance schedules and training for your technical staff. We also offer preventive maintenance contracts with OEM parts and factory-trained technicians.
