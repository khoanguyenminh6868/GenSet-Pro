Today, BEIDOU Power stands out as a market leader in the provision of power generation and distribution systems, bespoke noise control solutions & containers / enclosures built to exact size or shape requirements meeting both acoustic & environmental restrictions.
BEIDOU POWER
Beidou Power Equipment Yangzhou Co., Ltd. is a leading manufacturer in the field of generator sets, located in the renowned hometown of Yangzhou City. We specialize in the research, development, design, assembly, manufacturing, marketing, and service of generator sets, catering to the diverse needs of our clients.
Beidou is committed to ensuring the highest quality of product development, innovation and service quality. We maintain a close strategic cooperative relationship with renowned domestic enterprises such as Cummins, Perkins, Volvo, Deutz, Benz MTU Series, Shangchai, Yuchai, Weichai, Leroy-Somer, Stamford and Marathon for a long time.
Beidou has a vast network of dealers that extends beyond our domestic market, with a presence in locations such as the Philippines, Kenya, Bangladesh, Nigeria, and other regions. Our unwavering commitment to delivering stable, efficient, and affordable power solutions to our customers and consistently expanding our sales networks is what sets us apart from the competition. Our products cover a wide array of sectors, ranging from industry, agriculture, medical, construction, and more, providing comprehensive solutions to meet the varying needs of our clients. We take pride in our exceptional quality and service, and we are fully committed to providing you with the best possible solution to your.
At Beidou Power Equipment, we have a highly-trained after-sales service team that is dedicated to offering customized and hands-on solutions that cater to each of our client's specific needs. We would be delighted to welcome you to our company and offer our services and expertise to benefit yours.
