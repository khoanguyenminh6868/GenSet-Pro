Product Details
SWT Perkins Series Diesel Generator Sets
As a generator set packager and power system integrator, SWT focuses on complete genset design, matching, control integration, enclosure configuration, factory testing and project-based customization. The product range can be supplied as open type units or containerized silent type solutions, with ATS and multi-unit paralleling systems available according to project requirements.
Wide power coverage from 495kW to 1800kW prime power.
50Hz configurations with 380/400/415V output for international industrial applications.
60Hz configurations with 440/460/480V output for markets and projects requiring 60Hz systems.
Open type and containerized silent type configurations available.
Integrated control system for local/remote start, protection, monitoring and event logging.
Optional ATS for automatic transfer between mains and generator power.
Optional SWT paralleling system for multi-unit operation, load sharing and capacity expansion.
Each generator set is factory-tested before delivery, including load test and protection system inspection.

Operating Conditions
| Item | Description
| Ambient Temperature | <= 40°C under standard rating conditions. Power correction is required when ambient temperature is higher than 40°C.
| Altitude | <= 1000m under standard rating conditions. Power correction is required when altitude is higher than 1000m.
| Humidity | <= 60%. Additional measures may be required in excessive humidity environments.
| Power Factor | 0.8
| Prime Power (PRP) | Suitable for continuous operation under variable load. 10% overload is allowed for 1 hour every 12 hours.
| Standby Power (ESP) | For emergency use at an average load rate of 80%, not exceeding 500 hours per year. No overload is allowed.
Control System
The standard SWT control system is designed for single-unit operation and provides easy operation, monitoring and protection functions. The control system supports LCD display, key operation, multilingual display, local and remote start, event logs and multiple communication interfaces.

ATS Optional
An ATS panel can be provided as an optional configuration for mains/mains or mains/generator applications. The ATS controller combines measurement, display and automatic control functions, supporting fast automatic transfer between two power sources.
Automatic transfer between mains and generator power
LCD display for operating status and control mode
Voltage and frequency measurement
Electrical and mechanical interlock design
Fast switching structure for power source transfer
SWT Paralleling System Optional
For larger power demand or redundant power systems, SWT can provide a paralleling system for genset-to-genset or genset-to-grid applications. The system supports automatic synchronization of up to 32 units and can automatically adjust the number of running units according to load demand.

FAQ
Q: What power range does this Perkins series cover?
A: This product page covers models from 495kW to 1800kW prime power, with both 50Hz and 60Hz configurations available.
Q: Can SWT provide both 50Hz and 60Hz generator sets?
A: Yes. The range includes 50Hz models for 380/400/415V systems and 60Hz models for 440/460/480V systems.
Q: Is a silent or containerized version available?
A: Yes. Open type and containerized silent type configurations are available for different models and project environments.
Q: Can the generator set work with ATS?
A: Yes. ATS is available as an optional configuration for automatic transfer between mains and generator power.
Q: Can multiple units operate in parallel?
A: Yes. SWT can provide a paralleling system for genset-to-genset and genset-to-grid applications, supporting automatic synchronization and load sharing.
Q: What tests are performed before delivery?
A: Each unit is tested at 0%, 25%, 50%, 75%, 100% and 110% load, and the protection devices and control systems are inspected before delivery.
Q: What applications are suitable for this range?
A: Typical applications include industrial power supply, data center backup power, mining, oilfield, infrastructure, public facilities, commercial buildings and emergency standby power systems.
Q: Can SWT customize the configuration?
A: Yes. SWT can customize voltage, frequency, enclosure, fuel system, control system, ATS, communication and paralleling options according to project requirements.
