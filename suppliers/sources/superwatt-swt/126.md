System Configuration
Generator Set Assembly
SWT integrates the engine, alternator, chassis, control box, circuit breaker, industrial muffler, shock absorbers and related accessories into a complete generator set. The focus is on reliable system matching, stable power output, convenient operation and project-ready delivery.
Alternator and Power Output
The alternator is matched for 2000kW / 2500kVA output, 50Hz operation and 380/400/415V voltage requirements. Standard configuration includes Class H insulation, IP23 protection, 3-phase output and low waveform distortion.

FAQ
Q: What is the prime power of this generator set?
A: The prime power is 2000kW / 2500kVA. It is suitable for continuous operation under variable load conditions within the defined PRP rating.
Q: What is the standby power of this generator set?
A: The standby power is 2200kW / 2750kVA. Standby power is intended for emergency use and does not allow overload.
Q: What frequency and voltage does this model support?
A: The standard configuration is 50Hz, 1500r/min, with rated voltage of 380/400/415V.
Q: Is a containerized version available?
A: Yes. The model can be supplied as open type or as a 40ft containerized configuration according to project needs.
Q: Can the generator set work with an ATS panel?
A: Yes. An ATS panel is available as an optional accessory for automatic transfer between mains and generator power.
Q: Can multiple generator sets operate in parallel?
A: Yes. SWT can provide an optional paralleling system for genset-to-genset, genset-to-grid and paralleling-with-ATS applications.
Q: What tests are carried out before delivery?
A: Each unit is load tested at 0%, 25%, 50%, 75%, 100% and 110%, and the protection devices and control systems are inspected before delivery.
Q: What applications is this model suitable for?
A: It is suitable for industrial facilities, mining projects, oil and gas projects, data centers, EPC projects, utilities, infrastructure and emergency standby power applications.
