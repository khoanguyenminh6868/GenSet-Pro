The SWT Cummins series diesel generator sets in the SC688, SC688X and SC715 range are designed for medium-to-large industrial and commercial power applications. With power ratings from 500kW / 625kVA prime power to 572kW / 715kVA standby power, these generator sets can be configured for both 50Hz and 60Hz projects according to site requirements.

Product Details

As a generator set manufacturer and power system integrator, SWT focuses on complete power solutions rather than only component supply. Power Rating Definition
| Item | Specification
| Prime Power (PRP) | The generator set can run continuously for 24 hours under variable load, allowing 10% overload every 12 hours for 1 hour.
| Standby Power (ESP) | Used at an average load factor of 80% for no more than 500 hours per year. No overload is allowed.
System Configuration
Integrated Genset Package
The generator set is supplied as a complete integrated system. Standard components include engine, alternator, chassis, control box, controller for single unit, circuit breaker, industrial muffler, flange of muffler, bellow joint, shock absorber, test report, certificate of quality, schematic diagram, installation manual and operation manual.
Matched Engine Platform
According to the selected model and project requirement, the genset can be matched with Cummins engine platforms such as KTA19-G8A, QSKTAA19-G4 or KT38-G. The product page keeps the focus on complete generator set performance, configuration and project suitability.

ATS Optional
An ATS panel can be provided for applications requiring automatic transfer between mains power and generator power. The ATS controller integrates measurement, display and control functions for mains/mains or mains/generator systems.

Voltage and frequency measurement through LCD screen.
Fast on-load switching between two power sources, with switching time up to ≤80ms depending on configuration.
Electrical and mechanical interlock mechanism to help prevent short circuit between two power sources.
SWT Paralleling System Optional
For larger power demand or redundant power systems, SWT can provide a paralleling system for genset-to-genset, genset-to-grid and paralleling with ATS applications. The system can support automatic synchronizing of up to 32 units.

FAQ
Q1: What power range does this product page cover?
A: It covers SWT Cummins series generator sets including SC688, SC688X and SC715, with prime power from 500kW / 625kVA to 520kW / 650kVA and standby power up to 572kW / 715kVA.
Q2: Are both 50Hz and 60Hz models available?
A: Yes. SC688 and SC715 are 50Hz configurations, while SC688X is a 60Hz configuration for regions requiring 60Hz power systems.
Q3: What configurations are available?
A: Open type and 20-foot containerized configurations are available. Other project-specific requirements can be discussed according to site conditions.
Q4: Can the generator set be used for standby power?
A: Yes. The generator set can be used for standby power applications in factories, commercial buildings, data centers, infrastructure projects and emergency systems.
Q5: Can SWT provide ATS for this generator set?
A: Yes. ATS can be configured for automatic switching between mains power and generator power.
Q6: Can multiple generator sets operate in parallel?
A: Yes. SWT can provide a paralleling system for multi-unit operation, genset-to-genset operation, genset-to-grid operation and paralleling with ATS.
Q7: What tests are carried out before delivery?
A: Each unit passes load tests at 0%, 25%, 50%, 75%, 100% and 110%, and all protection devices and control systems are inspected before delivery.
Q8: What applications are suitable for this power range?
A: Typical applications include industrial power supply, mining, oilfield projects, data centers, construction sites, commercial backup power and distributed power projects.
