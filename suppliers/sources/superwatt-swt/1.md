WHO WE ARE
We specialize in the design and manufacture of power generation systems and mobile lighting towers.
As a leading provider of power solutions in China, Shandong Superwatt Power Tech Co., Ltd. specializes in the design and manufacturing of premium diesel and gaseous generator sets, as well as mobile lighting towers. Our world-class R&D team integrates advanced technology and innovative engineering into every product, delivering exceptional performance and reliability across diverse applications. SWT offers a comprehensive range of precision power equipment, with outputs spanning from 8.3kVA to 3781kVA, ensuring tailored solutions for both standard and specialized requirements.
