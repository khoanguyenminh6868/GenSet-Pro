SWT Kubota Series Small Diesel Generator Set
The SWT Kubota Series small diesel generator set is designed for reliable standby and prime power applications where compact size, stable output, low fuel consumption and easy maintenance are important. Covering 50Hz and 60Hz models from 6kW to 24kW, the series is suitable for residential backup power, small commercial facilities, telecom stations, outdoor worksites, rental power and emergency power supply.

Alternator and Electrical System
The alternator system is configured according to the selected generator set model. Available voltage options include 230V, 240V, 220/127V and 380/400/415V. Frequency options include 50Hz and 60Hz. The alternator configuration supports power factor 0.8, insulation class H, temperature rise class H and IP23 protection.
Frequency: 50Hz or 60Hz, depending on model.
Voltage options: 230V, 240V, 220/127V or 380/400/415V.
Phase options: single-phase and three-phase.
Insulation class: H.
Temperature rise class: H.
Protection class: IP23.
Waveform distortion (THD): <5%.
Telephone interference factor (THF): <2%.
SWT Control System – Standard
The standard SWT single-unit control system is designed for convenient operation and generator set protection. It supports LCD display, multilingual interface, local and remote start, engine and alternator parameter monitoring, event logs and optional communication interfaces.
LCD screen and key operation.

Local and remote start.
Control and protection functions.

Multiple communication interfaces available.

ATS Optional
An ATS panel is available as an optional accessory for mains-to-generator or mains-to-mains applications. The ATS controller combines measurement, display and automatic control functions, helping realize automatic transfer when utility power fails.
Microcomputer control and digital technology.
LCD display for ATS operating status and controller mode.
Voltage and frequency measurement through LCD screen.
Fast on-load switching of two power sources, with switching time up to <=80ms according to specification.
Electrical and mechanical double interlock design to prevent short circuit between two power sources.
SWT Paralleling System Optional
For larger power demand or flexible load management, SWT can provide a paralleling system for genset-to-genset or genset-to-grid applications. The system can support automatic synchronising of up to 32 units and automatic unit loading/unloading according to load demand.
Automatic synchronising for multiple generator sets.
Genset-to-genset and genset-to-grid applications.
PLC system and touch screen available upon request.
Automatic load-based unit start/stop to improve fuel economy.
Easy future capacity expansion.

FAQ
Q1: What power range does the SWT Kubota Series cover?
A: The series covers 50Hz and 60Hz models from 6kW to 24kW, with standby ratings up to 32.5kVA depending on model configuration.
Q2: Are both 50Hz and 60Hz models available?
A: Yes. 50Hz models include SK8.3-1P, SK16.5-1P, SK25-1P and SK32.5. 60Hz models include SK8.8X-1P, SK16.5X-1P, SK30X and SK30X-1P.
Q3: Can SWT provide single-phase models?
A: Yes. Single-phase models are available for 230V or 240V applications, including SK8.3-1P, SK16.5-1P, SK25-1P, SK8.8X-1P, SK16.5X-1P and SK30X-1P.
Q4: Are three-phase models available?
A: Yes. Three-phase models are available, such as SK32.5 for 380/400/415V and SK30X for 220/127V applications.
Q5: What enclosure types are available?
A: Open type, silent type and super silent type configurations are available according to site requirements.
Q6: Can the generator set work with ATS?
A: Yes. ATS panel is available as an optional accessory for automatic switching between mains and generator power.
Q7: What tests are carried out before delivery?
A: Each unit is tested at 0%, 25%, 50%, 75%, 100% and 110% load, and the protection devices and control systems are checked before delivery.
Q8: What applications are suitable for this series?
A: Typical applications include residential backup power, telecom stations, small commercial buildings, construction sites, farms, rental power and emergency standby power.
