Cummins Series (1000)kW (1250)kVA Diesel Generator Set
The SC1250 series diesel generator set is designed for high performance, providing reliable and efficient power for industrial, commercial, and residential applications. Equipped with advanced Cummins engines and robust components, it ensures optimal fuel efficiency and long-term stable operation.

Product Details
Key Features
High Power Output :
Prime Power : 908kW/1135kVA
Standby Power : 1000kW/1250kVA
Suitable for large-scale energy demands, offering flexibility to accommodate variable loads.
Fuel Efficiency : Powered by a Cummins 12-cylinder, 37.8L turbocharged engine, providing excellent fuel efficiency, with consumption rates of 235L/h at 100% prime power and 271L/h at standby power.
Durable Engine : The engine features a compression ratio of 15:1, electronic fuel injection, and a water-cooling system, ensuring reliable performance in harsh environments.
Continuous Operation : Capable of running continuously for 24 hours under variable loads, with a 10% overload capacity for 1 hour every 12 hours, ensuring flexibility.
Advanced Control System : Equipped with an LCD display, multilingual support, remote start options, real-time monitoring of engine and alternator parameters. The system is IP65-rated for protection against dust and water.
Scalable Power : Supports parallel operation with up to 32 units, allowing for easy expansion of power capacity to meet growing demands.
High Protection : The alternator has an IP23 protection rating, and the control system is IP65-rated, ensuring reliable operation in harsh conditions.
