ABOUT SWT
SWT, the leading power equipment manufacturer in China, was founded in 1993. with over 30 years of manufacturing experience and development, we have emerged as an exceptional manufacturer and trusted integrated solution provider for power plants and equipment globally. Our extensive product range includes diesel generators, mobile lighting towers, and gas generator sets.
With over a decade of research experience in Mobile Lighting Tower technology, we proudly introduced the world’s first LED lighting tower, establishing ourselves as pioneers in this field. In recent years, we have also made significant investments in the development of Vehicle Power Stations and Gas Generator Sets, earning a strong reputation for delivering products of outstanding quality and performance.
In 2005, a team of United Nations experts conducted a comprehensive assessment of SWT’s manufacturing systems, intelligent production process management, quality control, and delivery capabilities for silent container-type generator sets. The team expressed high confidence in SWT’s operational excellence, leading to the award of a US$30 million contract. SWT successfully delivered the order, with all units passing rigorous performance tests before being deployed to United Nations peacekeeping force bases in Chad, Sudan, Liberia, and Lebanon, where they have since served with high reliability.
SWT’s high-performance generator sets have gained a global footprint, serving customers in more than 100 countries across Southeast Asia, Australia, the Middle East, Africa, the Americas, and Europe. Domestically, we have built a robust distribution network through partnerships with over 40 authorized distributors, ensuring comprehensive market coverage and responsive after-sales support. We have also established successful collaborations with leading enterprises and institutions—including Vanke, China Huaneng Group, Peking Union Medical College Hospital, China Unicom, and Zijin Mining Group—as well as with organizations in broadcasting, healthcare, finance, and railway sectors. These partnerships reflect the market’s trust in SWT and have laid a solid foundation for our continued growth and innovation.
