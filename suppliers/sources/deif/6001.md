Land Power
Power Management Systems
DEIF land power products, such as our generator controllers , protection relays and electrical switchboard , are dedicated to energy efficiency that reduces fuel consumption, cut emissions, and costly maintenance intervals.
Our land power management systems are integrated solutions that include logic functions such as blackout start, load-dependent start/stop, heavy consumer control, load shedding, priority control, and plant operational modes. These system solutions operate without additional equipment such as external PLCs.
