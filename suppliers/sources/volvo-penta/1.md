Industrial power generation solutions
Power generation engine range
As an independent supplier, Volvo Penta offers reliable and flexible industrial power generation engines. Designed for fuel efficiency, easy installation, and engineered to deliver optimal load performance, our industrial power generation solutions aim to support your operational efficiency.
Paving the way forward
Emission technology
Volvo Penta power generation engines feature advanced emission technologies – helping you meet the requirements of Stage II/Tier 2 and higher – while balancing performance and efficiency. Our innovative SCR technology has been part of our engine offering since Stage IIIB.
Industrial emission technology
Renewable fuels
All Volvo Penta power generation engines are compatible with HVO fuel, and do not require additional type approval or certification for use. Our D8 dual-fuel engine can also operate on another renewable fuel – hydrogen. When hydrogen is not available, traditional fuel can be used.
Alternative fuels
Diversifying into BESS
As the energy landscape evolves, Volvo Penta is dedicated to supporting you in navigating the energy transition. Our subsystems for Battery Energy Storage Systems (BESS) are purpose-built to enable OEMs in building transportable, high-performance BESS solutions for industries where energy density is essential.

Power generation applications
Water pumps and irrigation
Volvo Penta supplies engines for powering irrigation systems in agriculture and farming, as well as municipal and industrial water management systems.
Construction and infrastructure
Rental gensets with Volvo Penta engines are widely utilized to provide temporary power for construction sites and equipment used in building and roadworks.
Disaster recovery and emergency
Volvo Penta-powered gensets can provide electric power for emergency shelters, hospitals and pumps during floods, earthquakes, and other disasters.
Mining and quarrying
Volvo Penta builds engines designed to power heavy machinery and pumps for dewatering and ventilation in underground mines and quarries.
Data centers
An energy-dense BESS ensures data center uptime during outages, providing compact backup power and saving space.
Manufacturing and process plants
Volvo Penta engines deliver stable power to critical equipment in textile, food processing and chemical production industries.
Port handling
Generators with Volvo Penta engines supply power for port operations where grid access may be limited or inconsistent.
Events
For short-term, mobile power needs at live events like outdoor concerts and festivals, Volvo Penta-powered gensets offer a practical solution.

Power generation ratings
Standby power
The standby power generation rating is referring to the engine’s ability to supply standby electrical power at variable load in areas with well-established electrical networks, in the event of normal utility power failures.
Prime power
The ISO standard rating for prime power generator engines is referring to the engine’s ability to supply electrical power at a variable load with 70% load factor for an unlimited number of hours. A 10% overload capability is available with this rating.
Continuous power
The rating for continuous power generator engines is referring to the maximum power a generating set can deliver continuously while supplying a constant electrical load for an unlimited number of hours.
Data center power
The rating for data center power solutions is referring to the maximum power a generating set can deliver while supplying a variable or constant electrical load for an unlimited number of hours.
