density.                                                              • Service interval 1000 hours
It features a proven combustion technology with Common
Rail injection system, resulting in high fuel efficiency and low
exhaust emission levels.

The engine also features a compact and low weight design
that is well-balanced, providing smooth operation with low
noise. It´s designed for easily accessible service points.

A wide range of options are available, cooling package with
the option of fixed or visco controlled fan and air-filter that will
suit a variety of installations.
