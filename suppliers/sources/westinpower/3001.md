Recent Project
Zhuhai Jinwan Offshore Wind Farm Zhuhai Jinwan Offshore Wind Farm is currently the largest offshore wind farm in the Guangdong-Hong Kong-Macao Greater Bay Area. With 5,300,000,000RMB of investment, the wind farm equips 55 wind generating set, with total power capacity up to 300,000kW, the expected annual power production to the grid is 729,000,000kWh.
Power Station in Indonesia The power station is located in Tebing Tinggi, Kepulauan Meranti, Indonesia. By combining multiple generator sets, the power station is established and then connected to the power grid to provide electricity to surrounding cities.

The generator set equipped in the data center applies TC QSK60-G21 engine, DSE8610 synchronizing & load sharing control module, and DSE8004 graphical remote overview display.
