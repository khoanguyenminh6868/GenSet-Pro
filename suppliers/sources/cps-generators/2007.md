Cummins Generators
Cummins G-Drive engines have manufactured power generation engines for over 80 years, providing vital power for various business sectors such as healthcare, industrial, commercial, data centres and telecommunications to name a few.
CPS Generators manufacture a comprehensive range of Diesel generators, using Cummins G Drive engines. Using Cummins G-Drive engines, we can produce a bespoke generator solution for your application.
We design and manufacturers all our generators in North Yorkshire, UK. Our quality generators, associated products and services cover the whole of the UK, and our products are exported worldwide. Our services include site surveys, bespoke generator builds, generator & power equipment installation, generator servicing packages and generator commissioning services.
