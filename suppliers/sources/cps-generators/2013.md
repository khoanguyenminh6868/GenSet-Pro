Scania Generators
Scania Diesel Generators by Constant Power Solutions - Made in the UK.
Our Scania Powered Diesel gensets are amongst the most popular, due to their relliability and efficiency. Our AS Series diesel generators powered by Scania offer a power range of 250 kva to 700 kva. Scania engines are renowned for contributing constant, trouble free power. The engines are suitable for both prime and standby applications.
Scania engines are renowned for contributing constant, trouble-free power delivery. From prime power at remote sites and entertainment venues through standby and back up power to large power requirements, Scania is the engine of choice.
