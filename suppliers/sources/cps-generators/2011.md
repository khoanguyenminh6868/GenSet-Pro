Volvo Generators
Volvo Penta engines are ideal for various business sectors such as healthcare, industrial, commercial, data centres and telecommunications to name a few. Volvo Generators are constructed with reliable efficiency and unmatched load acceptance at their core.
CPS Generators manufacture a comprehensive range of Diesel generators, using Volvo Penta Diesel engines.
Using Volvo Penta engines, CPS can produce a bespoke generator solution for your application.
Our quality generators, associated products and services cover the whole of the UK, and our products are exported worldwide. Our services include site surveys, bespoke generator builds, generator & power equipment installation, generator servicing packages and generator commissioning services.
