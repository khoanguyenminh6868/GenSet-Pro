Perkins Generators
Perkins Diesel Generator Sets by Constant Power Solutions.
Perkins are a globaly renowned and recognised engine manufacturer. Generators powered by Perkins engine are ideal for various business sectors such as healthcare, industrial, commercial, data centres and telecommunications to name a few.
CPS Generators manufacture a comprehensive range of Diesel generators, using Perkins Diesel engines.
CPS generators, using Perkins engines, can produce a bespoke generator solution for your application.
Our quality generators, associated products and services cover the whole of the UK, and our products are exported worldwide. Our services include site surveys, bespoke generator builds, generator & power equipment installation, generator servicing packages and generator commissioning services.
