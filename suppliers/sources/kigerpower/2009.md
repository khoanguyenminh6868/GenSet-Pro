Frequently Asked Questions
What is the difference between rated and maximum power on the KG9900LiE? Rated power (10kW) is the output the set is designed to deliver continuously and is the figure to size a load against. Maximum power (10.5kW) is the short-duration ceiling for motor starting and inrush. Buy on the rated figure.
What voltage configurations are available? The catalog lists 110V / 220V / 230V / 380V / 400V with power factor cos 1 / 0.8. Tell us the supply standard in your destination country when you order and we will configure the unit to match.
Which engine is fitted and what does it displace? A KG1100F diesel engine of 660cc. It is a single cylinder vertical, 4-stroke, air-cooled design producing 85dB at 7 metres.
How is it started, and how noisy is it? Starting is electrical. Sound pressure is 85dB measured at 7 metres, so allow for that distance when positioning the set next to occupied rooms or neighbours.
How long does it run on a tank? The fuel tank holds 12.5L. Running time depends on load, so ask us for the figure at your expected duty for this model.
