Frequently Asked Questions
What is the difference between rated and maximum power on the KG15000SiE? Rated power (12kW) is the output the set is designed to deliver continuously and is the figure to size a load against. Maximum power (13kW) is the short-duration ceiling for motor starting and inrush. Buy on the rated figure.
What voltage configurations are available? The catalog lists 110V / 220V / 230V / 380V / 400V with power factor cos 1. Tell us the supply standard in your destination country when you order and we will configure the unit to match.
Which engine is fitted and what does it displace? A 2V95F diesel engine of 1247cc. It is a air-cooled, direct injection / two-cylinder, v-twin design producing 86dB at 7 metres.
How is it started, and how noisy is it? Starting is by electric. Sound pressure is 86dB measured at 7 metres, so allow for that distance when positioning the set next to occupied rooms or neighbours.
How long does it run on a tank? The fuel tank holds 24L. Running time depends on load, so ask us for the figure at your expected duty for this model.
