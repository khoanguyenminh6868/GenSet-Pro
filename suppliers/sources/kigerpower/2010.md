Ideal for camping, picnics and any other place where power is needed, with stable AC output and a low fault rate. It is rated at 10000W, 50Hz. The unit weighs 134 kg and measures 97 × 60 × 78 cm. Frequently Asked Questions
What size tools and appliances can this portable gasoline generator power? Equipped with a reliable 4-stroke OHV engine and AVR voltage regulation, it powers household emergency essentials (refrigerators, lighting, Wi-Fi, televisions) as well as job-site tools (drills, circular saws, air compressors). Check running and starting wattage requirements before connecting.
How does the automatic low-oil shutoff protect the engine? The built-in oil level sensor immediately grounds the ignition circuit and halts the engine when oil drops below the safe operating threshold, preventing cylinder scoring and catastrophic bearing seizure.
What is the fuel tank runtime and fuel consumption rate? High-capacity steel fuel tanks provide 8 to 12 hours of continuous operation at 50% load. The efficient OHV combustion chamber ensures low specific fuel consumption under variable commercial and residential loads.
What customization options are supported for wholesale export orders? We support custom frame tube diameters, wheel kits with folding handles, digital VFT meters (Volts, Frequency, Time), localized socket standards (Schuko, BS, US, NEMA), and private-label cartons with CE and SONCAP certifications.
