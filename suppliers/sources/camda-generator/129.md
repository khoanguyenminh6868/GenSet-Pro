What Is an 825 KVA Standby Diesel Generator?
An 825 KVA Standby Diesel Generator is a backup power system designed to automatically supply electricity during utility power failures. Unlike prime power generators that serve as the primary power source, standby generators remain on standby until an outage is detected.
Once utility power is interrupted, the generator automatically starts and transfers electrical loads through an Automatic Transfer Switch (ATS). This process typically occurs within a few seconds, minimizing operational disruption.
At a standard power factor of 0.8, an 825 KVA standby generator can provide approximately 660 kW of usable electrical power output.
| Generator Rating | Power Factor | Equivalent Output | Typical Usage
| 825 KVA Standby | 0.8 | 660 kW | Emergency Backup Power
| 750 KVA Prime | 0.8 | 600 kW | Continuous Operation
Source: IEC 60034 Generator Standards and ISO 8528 Generator Rating Guidelines.
This power capacity makes the generator suitable for medium and large industrial facilities, office complexes, healthcare institutions, warehouses, telecommunications facilities, and infrastructure projects requiring dependable emergency power.
How Does the 825 KVA Standby Diesel Generator Work?
The working principle behind a standby diesel generator is straightforward but highly effective. During normal operation, utility power supplies electricity to the facility while the generator remains in standby mode.
An Automatic Transfer Switch continuously monitors incoming power quality. If a power outage occurs, the ATS immediately sends a startup command to the diesel generator.
The engine starts automatically and reaches operating speed within seconds. The alternator then begins producing electricity. Once the generator achieves stable voltage and frequency levels, the ATS transfers the electrical load from the utility source to the generator.
Utility power interruption detected
ATS sends startup signal
Diesel engine starts automatically
Alternator generates electricity
ATS transfers facility load
Generator supplies emergency power
Utility power returns
ATS switches load back to utility source
Generator enters cool-down cycle
Automatic shutdown occurs
This fully automated process helps maintain business continuity without requiring operator intervention.
