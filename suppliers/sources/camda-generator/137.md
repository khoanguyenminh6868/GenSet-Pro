When I talk to customers about open-type power systems, I usually start with a very direct idea: not every project needs a covered, silent structure. In many industrial cases, what people really need is simple, strong, and easy-to-maintain power equipment.
That’s exactly where the Cummins Powered Open Diesel Generator fits in.
At Camda New Energy Equipment Co. Ltd., I’ve worked on many projects where open-type generators are still the best choice—especially in factories, mining areas, and construction sites where ventilation and easy servicing matter more than sound insulation.
1. What This Open Diesel Generator Is Designed For
This generator is designed for reliability, simplicity, and heavy-duty use. The open frame structure makes it easier to access the engine, alternator, and control system for maintenance and inspection.
I often describe it like this: it is a strong working machine without extra cover. What you see is what you maintain.

2. How the Open Diesel Generator Works
The working principle is very straightforward. There are four main parts: engine, alternator, control system, and base frame.
I usually explain it in simple terms: fuel goes into the engine, the engine creates mechanical energy, and the alternator turns it into electricity.
When power failure happens, the ATS system automatically starts the generator and transfers the load without manual operation.

8. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)
Q1: What is an open type generator?
It is a generator without a soundproof enclosure, designed for easy maintenance and industrial use.
Q2: Is it suitable for outdoor use?
Yes, but it should be installed under a shelter for protection against rain and dust.
Q3: Does it support automatic start?
Yes, it works with ATS for automatic start and stop.
Q4: What fuel does it use?
It uses diesel fuel optimized for Cummins engines.

Usually 15–35 days depending on configuration.
