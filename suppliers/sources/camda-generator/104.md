Introduction to 120 KW Gas Generator
A 120 KW Gas Generator is a medium-capacity power generation system designed to produce stable electrical output using gaseous fuels such as natural gas, biogas, or LPG. It is widely used in industrial facilities, commercial buildings, construction sites, agricultural operations, and emergency backup systems.
As global demand for cleaner and more efficient energy increases, the 120 KW Gas Generator system has become a preferred solution due to its balance of power output, fuel efficiency, and lower emissions compared to traditional diesel generators.
This article provides a complete overview of the 120 KW Gas Generator , including working principles, technical specifications, advantages, applications, installation requirements, and maintenance guidelines.
What is a 120 KW Gas Generator?
A 120 KW gas-powered generator is an engine-driven electrical power system that converts chemical energy from gas fuel into mechanical energy, which is then transformed into electrical energy through an alternator.
Key Concept:
Fuel (natural gas / biogas / LPG) → Engine combustion → Mechanical rotation → Electrical energy output (120 KW)
The system is engineered for:
Continuous power supply (Prime Power)
Emergency backup power (Standby Power)
Peak shaving in industrial energy systems
How a 120 KW Gas Generator Works
A 120 KW Gas Generator Set operates through four main stages:
1. Fuel Supply System
Gas is delivered through pipelines or storage tanks. Pressure regulators ensure stable fuel flow.
2. Gas Combustion Engine
The gas-air mixture is compressed and ignited inside the engine cylinders, generating controlled explosions that drive pistons.
3. Mechanical Energy Conversion
The piston motion rotates the crankshaft, producing mechanical energy.
4. Alternator Conversion
The alternator converts mechanical energy into electrical power with stable voltage and frequency output.
