2. How the Cummins Gas Generator System Works
Let me explain it in a simple way without heavy engineering talk.
Gas enters the engine through a controlled pipeline system.
Air mixes with gas in a precise ratio.
Combustion happens inside cylinders, pushing pistons.
Rotation is created in the crankshaft.
Alternator converts energy into electricity.
This process runs continuously as long as fuel is supplied and the cooling system works properly.
