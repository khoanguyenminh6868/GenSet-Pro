What Is a 1000 KVA Standby Diesel Generator?
A 1000 KVA Standby Diesel Generator is a large-capacity backup power system designed to automatically start and supply electricity when utility power becomes unavailable. The generator remains in standby mode during normal operation and activates within seconds after detecting a power failure.
The term "1000 KVA" refers to the apparent power output capability of the generator. Under a typical power factor of 0.8, a 1000 KVA diesel generator can provide approximately 800 kW of real power output.

How Does a 1000 KVA Standby Diesel Generator Work?
Many customers ask us how a standby generator can restore power so quickly. The process is actually straightforward.
When utility power is available, the generator remains idle while continuously monitoring the incoming electrical supply through an Automatic Transfer Switch (ATS).
If a power outage occurs:
The ATS detects utility failure.
The control system sends a start signal to the diesel engine.
The engine reaches operating speed.
The alternator begins producing electricity.
The ATS transfers electrical loads from the utility to the generator.
The facility continues operating with minimal interruption.
Once utility power is restored and stabilized, the ATS automatically switches the load back to the grid. The generator then enters a cooling cycle before shutting down.
This automatic process often takes less than 15 seconds, depending on site configuration and local requirements.
