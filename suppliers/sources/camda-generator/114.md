How the 150 KVA Standby Diesel Generator Works
The operation is fully automatic when connected to an ATS (Automatic Transfer Switch).
Under normal conditions, utility power supplies electricity. The generator stays in standby mode.
When power fails, the ATS detects the interruption and sends a start signal. The diesel engine starts immediately, reaches rated speed, and begins generating electricity through the alternator.
The load is transferred from the grid to the generator within seconds.
When utility power returns, the system switches back automatically and shuts down safely.
This process ensures minimal interruption for critical operations.
