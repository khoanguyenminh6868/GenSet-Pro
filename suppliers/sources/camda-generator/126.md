How to clean the cooling system:
◇Use eau douce to clean the cooling system;
◇If there is a drainage valve, close it or connect the coolant pipe;
Note: In order to avoid producing air pocket, and the speed of intake coolant could not be over 19 L/min.
◇Inject the mixture of water and cleaner into cooling system. Consult the manufacture 's direct, close the cover;
◇Start the engine, running the genset without any load for 30 minites, and wait the coolant temperature reach to 82 ℃(180℉).
◇Stop the engine, after it has been cold, loosen the cover of cooling system and depress its pressure. Take off the cover, open the drainage valve (if there is), or take off the coolant pipe at the bottom, so that the coolant can leak out from cooling system, and use eau douce to clean the cooling system, then close the valve or connect the coolant pipe.
How to add coolant:
◇Inject the right coolant to the cooling system via the injector, but don't cover the injector.
◇Start the engine, and keep idle running for1 minute so as to discharge all the air among the gaps, and then stop it.
◇Check the coolant level and keep it equal the bottom of the injector.
◇Clean the cover of the injector, and check its gasket. If the gasket gets out of work, the cover needs to be replaced. If the gasket can work, please use right PRESSURE INSPETING TOOL to inspect the injector cover and check if it meets the required pressure marked on the cover. If it can't meet, please replace to new injector cover.
◇Start the engine, check the cooling system whether any leak occurred, and whether the runing and temperature on good condition.
Protective equipment:
The safe and protective equipment must be installed when install Gas Generator Set , to protect the personal and matterssafety. The protective panel should be installed for revolving parts or moving parts. The heat insulating equipment should be installed for high temperature parts to ensure the safety of operator.
Crankcase breather:
All engines are designed with crankcase breather and exhaust pipe to discharge the gas of crankcase, lower the pressure of crankcase. Please check if the installing position of crankcase breather and exhaust pipe are right or faulse before genset operation.
Cooling system clutch and connection:
When the engine is still operating or water temperature is still very high, please don't loosen the clutch. Because the pressure of cooling system is very high, if the clutch is loosen, high temperature water will be sprayed out and cause seriously scald. After the engine and water cool, heat exchanger, thermometer box and radiator clutch can be open. In order to ensure the person and genset safety, please change the problematic and aged pipes timely.

What Is a 650 KVA Standby Diesel Generator (1500 RPM / 1800 RPM)?
A standby diesel generator is a backup power system that only runs when the main power fails. It sits quietly in standby mode, always ready to start automatically.
When a power outage happens, the system starts within seconds and takes over the electrical load. Once the grid returns, it switches back automatically.

Source: IEC 60034 & ISO 8528 Generator Rating Standards
How the System Works in Real Applications
The system is built around an Automatic Transfer Switch (ATS). It constantly monitors the utility power supply.
Normal condition: load powered by grid
Grid failure detected: ATS sends start signal
Engine starts automatically
Voltage and frequency stabilize
Load is transferred to generator
Power continues without interruption
Grid returns: system switches back automatically
The whole process usually happens within 10–15 seconds. No manual operation is needed.
