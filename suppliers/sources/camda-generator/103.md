² When the generator set is on the normal working hours, gas engine water temperature and oil temperature is not more than 95℃, oil pressure should be in the range of 0.25-0.35 Mpa. ² Be ready to check the pipeline of gas engine, such as leakage, which should be repaired promptly.
² Observe the gas engine exhaust gas color. When gas engine running at full capacity under normal circumstances, the exhaust fumosoroseus have slightly green gray.

Frequent malfunction and disposal measures:
Please stop the generator quickly once encounter the problem as follows during the running, increased and reduced the power load. (Turn off the key, cut the gas supply and iginition power supply)
1. Oil pressure lower than 0.15 Mpa
2. Water temperature higher than 90℃
3. Frequency continuously exceed 30 seconds and higher than 52Hz
4. Battery voltage lower than 24V.
Problem Processing Method:
1. Check the oil level to see if it needs filling
2. Check the water tank to see if it is enough
3. Check the load increased and decreased whether according to the requirement of generator
4. Battery should be charged for 24 hours, if it can’t reach 24V, it should be cahnged.
If the generator set can’t start normally, please check the items as follows:
u Whether electric-key start too long (over than 15 seconds) but not pressing the start button
u Check if the gas connecting joint connects closely
u Check if the outside circuit connecting is liable
u Check if the battery voltage is normal
u Check if the high voltage winding is damaged and connected normally
u Check if the gas pipe explosion-proof is closed
u Check if the mixer and electronic speed governor actuator connecting is liable
Problem Processing Method:
u Cut off the battery power supply and connect it again
u Reset the protection device
u Charging or replace the battery

150 KW Gas Generator – Complete Technical & Industry Guide
1. Introduction to 1 50 KW Gas Generator
The 150 KW Gas Generator is a medium-capacity power generation system widely used in industrial, commercial, and emergency backup applications. It converts gaseous fuels such as natural gas, LPG (liquefied petroleum gas), or biogas into reliable electrical energy through an internal combustion engine connected to an alternator.
In modern energy systems, the 150 KW Gas Generator is considered an optimal balance between power output, fuel efficiency, and environmental performance. It is commonly installed in factories, hospitals, data centers, agricultural facilities, and commercial buildings requiring stable and continuous power supply.
Compared with diesel generators, gas-powered systems provide:
Lower emissions
Reduced operational cost
Quieter operation
Longer engine lifespan
Higher sustainability performance
As global industries shift toward cleaner energy, the 150 KW Gas Generator system has become a key component in distributed energy networks and hybrid microgrid systems.
2. What is a 150 KW Gas Generator?
A 150 KW Gas Generator is an internal combustion power system that generates 150 kilowatts of electrical output by burning gas fuel.
Basic Definition:
It is a gas-fueled engine generator set designed to produce stable AC power for continuous or standby use.
Core Energy Conversion Process:
Gas fuel → combustion energy → mechanical rotation → electrical power generation
Output Characteristics:
Rated Power: 150 KW
Voltage Range: 400V / 480V (customizable)
Frequency: 50Hz / 60Hz
Phase: Three-phase output system
3. Working Principle of 150 KW Gas Generator
The 150 KW Gas Generator system operates based on the four-stroke internal combustion cycle, which ensures continuous and efficient energy conversion.
Step 1: Gas Intake Process
A controlled air-fuel mixture enters the combustion chamber through the intake valve.
Step 2: Compression Process
The piston compresses the mixture to increase energy density and combustion efficiency.
Step 3: Power (Combustion) Stroke
A spark plug ignites the compressed mixture, producing high-pressure gas expansion that drives the piston downward.
Step 4: Exhaust Process
Burned gases are expelled through the exhaust valve, completing the cycle.
The mechanical motion is transferred to the alternator, which converts it into electrical energy.
4. Key Components of 150 KW Gas Generator
A complete 150 KW Gas Generator system includes several integrated subsystems:
4.1 Gas Engine Unit
The primary combustion engine that drives mechanical rotation.
4.2 Alternator
Converts mechanical energy into electrical energy.
4.3 Control System
Digital or PLC-based controller for monitoring voltage, frequency, load, and alarms.
4.4 Gas Supply System
Includes gas filter, regulator, solenoid valve, and pressure control components.
4.5 Cooling System
Water-cooled or radiator-based system to maintain stable engine temperature.
4.6 Lubrication System
Ensures engine components operate smoothly with reduced friction.
4.7 Exhaust System
Removes combustion gases safely and efficiently.
4.8 Base Frame
Heavy-duty steel structure supporting the entire generator set.
