What Is a 100 KVA Standby Diesel Generator?
A 100 KVA standby diesel generator is a backup power generation system designed to automatically supply electricity when the utility grid fails. The unit remains in standby mode during normal operation and starts automatically when a power interruption is detected.
The 100 KVA power rating makes this generator suitable for medium-sized facilities that require reliable emergency electricity. It can support critical loads such as lighting systems, elevators, pumps, servers, HVAC equipment, communication devices, and industrial machinery.
Unlike portable generators, standby generators are permanently installed and integrated with automatic transfer switches (ATS), enabling seamless transition between utility power and generator power.
How Does the 100 KVA Standby Diesel Generator Work?
The operating principle is straightforward yet highly reliable.
The utility power supplies electricity under normal conditions.
The automatic transfer switch continuously monitors the grid.
When a power outage occurs, the ATS sends a start signal to the generator.
The Cummins diesel engine starts within seconds.
The alternator converts mechanical energy into electrical energy.
The ATS transfers the load to the generator.
When utility power returns, the ATS switches back and the generator enters standby mode.
This automatic process minimizes downtime and protects critical operations from unexpected power interruptions.
