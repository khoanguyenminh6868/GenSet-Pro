What Is a 600 KVA Standby Diesel Generator?
A 600 KVA Standby Diesel Generator is a backup power system that does not run all the time. Instead, it stays in standby mode and activates automatically when the main power supply fails.
When a power outage occurs, the generator starts within seconds and supplies electricity to connected systems. Once utility power returns, it switches back automatically.
At a standard power factor of 0.8, the generator provides approximately 480 kW of usable output power.
| Generator Type | Power Factor | Output Power | Application
| 600 KVA Standby | 0.8 | 480 kW | Emergency Backup
| 550 KVA Prime | 0.8 | 440 kW | Continuous Operation
Source: IEC 60034 & ISO 8528 Generator Standards
How the System Works in Real Life
The system is connected to an Automatic Transfer Switch (ATS), which constantly monitors grid power.
Normal operation: utility power supplies load
Power failure detected: ATS sends start signal
Diesel engine starts automatically
Voltage and frequency stabilize
Load is transferred to generator
Power is restored within seconds
Once grid power returns, the system switches back automatically and the generator shuts down after a cooling cycle.
