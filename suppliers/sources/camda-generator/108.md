I’ve been working around diesel generator systems for years, and if there is one thing I learned early, it is this: standby power is not about comfort—it is about control during chaos.
When the grid drops, everything depends on whether the generator starts fast, holds stable voltage, and keeps the load alive.
This is where the 35 KVA Standby Diesel Generator Open type or Super silent type becomes a practical and reliable solution.
At Camda New Energy Equipment Co. Ltd., we build generator sets not just for specifications on paper, but for real working environments—factories, telecom stations, hospitals, and construction sites where power interruption is simply not acceptable.
1. What This 35 KVA Standby Generator Is Designed For
The 35 KVA standby diesel generator is designed to automatically supply backup power when the main grid fails. It can be configured in two structures: open type for industrial environments and super silent type for noise-sensitive locations.
I often explain it in a simple way: open type is like a strong outdoor worker—easy to access and maintain. Super silent type is like a quiet indoor assistant—working without disturbing the environment.

2. Working Principle in Simple Terms
Inside every diesel generator system, there are three core parts: engine, alternator, and controller. I usually describe them like this:
The engine is the muscle that burns fuel
The alternator is the heart that creates electricity
The controller is the brain that manages everything
When a power outage happens, the automatic transfer switch (ATS) detects it instantly. The generator starts automatically and takes over the electrical load in seconds.

3. Open Type vs Super Silent Type Design Difference
One of the most common questions I get is: which type should I choose? The answer depends on installation environment and noise requirements.
Open type is easier to maintain and more cost-efficient. Super silent type includes acoustic insulation and a weatherproof canopy to reduce noise significantly.

| Open Type | Easy maintenance, lower cost | Construction sites, factories | Camda Engineering Data
| Super Silent Type | Low noise operation (65–75 dB) | Hospitals, hotels, residential areas | ISO 3744 Acoustic Standard
