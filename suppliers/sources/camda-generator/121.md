1. What Is a 400 KVA Standby Diesel Generator?
A 400 KVA standby diesel generator is a backup power system that automatically starts when the main grid fails. It does not run all the time. Instead, it stays in standby mode, ready to act instantly when needed.
At Camda, we combine Cummins engine technology, Stamford or Leroy Somer alternators, and intelligent control systems to make sure the generator responds quickly and safely during emergencies.

2. How the Generator Works – Simple Explanation
I usually explain this in a very simple way: it is like a “backup guard” for your electricity system.
When power goes out, the Automatic Transfer Switch (ATS) detects the problem immediately. It sends a signal to start the Cummins engine. The engine burns diesel fuel and produces mechanical energy. The alternator converts that energy into electricity.
Once grid power returns, the system switches back automatically and the generator shuts down after cooling.
ATS detects power failure instantly
Engine starts automatically
Alternator produces electricity
Load is transferred safely
System shuts down after recovery
