What Is a 550 KVA Standby Diesel Generator?
A standby diesel generator is an emergency power system designed to operate automatically when utility power fails. Unlike prime power generators that run continuously, standby generators remain idle until an outage occurs.
When connected to an Automatic Transfer Switch (ATS), the generator continuously monitors incoming utility power. If an interruption is detected, the generator starts automatically and supplies electricity to the facility within seconds.
A 550 KVA Standby Diesel Generator provides approximately 440 kW of usable power output at a standard power factor of 0.8.

Source: ISO 8528 Generator Rating Standard and IEC 60034 Electrical Machine Standards.
How the 550 KVA Standby Diesel Generator Works
The operating principle is straightforward and highly reliable. During normal conditions, utility power supplies electricity to the facility while the generator remains on standby.
Utility power failure occurs.
ATS detects the interruption.
Controller sends an automatic start command.
Cummins diesel engine starts.
Alternator generates stable voltage and frequency.
ATS transfers facility load to the generator.
Generator powers the facility.
Utility power returns.
ATS transfers load back to utility supply.
Generator cools down and shuts off automatically.
The entire process usually takes only a few seconds, helping businesses avoid costly downtime.
