Understanding the 888 KVA Standby Diesel Generator
A standby diesel generator serves as an emergency power source that automatically starts when utility power fails. Unlike prime power generators that operate as a primary source of electricity, standby generators remain idle until an outage occurs. Once the power interruption is detected, the generator starts automatically and transfers the electrical load within seconds.
The 888 KVA Standby Diesel Generator provides substantial backup power capacity suitable for medium to large-scale facilities. With a standard power factor of 0.8, the generator can deliver approximately 710 kW of usable electrical power.

How the 888 KVA Standby Diesel Generator Works
Many customers ask us how standby generators respond so quickly during a power outage. The process is simple but highly effective.
When utility power is available, the generator remains in standby mode. An Automatic Transfer Switch (ATS) continuously monitors incoming electrical power. The moment a power interruption occurs, the ATS sends a signal to the generator controller.
The diesel engine immediately starts, reaches operating speed, and begins driving the alternator. Once the generator output reaches the required voltage and frequency, the ATS transfers the electrical load from the utility source to the generator.
The entire process typically takes between 10 and 15 seconds, ensuring minimal disruption to business operations.

This fully automatic operation eliminates the need for manual intervention and provides reliable emergency power around the clock.
