1. What Is a 60 KVA Standby Diesel Generator?
A 60 KVA Standby Diesel Generator is a backup power system designed to automatically supply electricity when the main grid fails. In simple words, it “kicks in” when your normal power goes down. I usually explain it like this to customers: it is like a spare engine for your whole building.
At Camda New Energy Equipment Co. Ltd., we design this generator for stable performance, low fuel consumption, and long running hours. It is powered by a diesel engine, often paired with well-known brands such as Cummins-based systems, ensuring durability and strong output under load.

2. How the Generator Actually Works (Simple Explanation)
Many customers ask me how a standby generator works. I keep it simple. Inside the system, there are three key parts: the engine, the alternator, and the control system.
Engine: Burns diesel fuel to create mechanical energy.
Alternator: Converts mechanical energy into electricity.
Control Panel: Detects power loss and starts the generator automatically.
When grid power fails, the automatic transfer switch (ATS) sends a signal. Within seconds, the generator starts and takes over the load. This is why we call it a standby system—it waits quietly until it is needed.
