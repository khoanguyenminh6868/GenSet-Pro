Understanding the 500 KVA Standby Diesel Generator
A standby diesel generator is designed to provide emergency electrical power when the primary utility source fails. Unlike prime generators that operate continuously, standby generators remain ready for immediate activation when required.
The 500 KVA Standby Diesel Generator typically delivers approximately 400 kW of usable electrical power at a standard power factor of 0.8. This output is sufficient to support medium and large facilities during power interruptions.

How a 500 KVA Standby Diesel Generator Works
The operating process is simple but highly effective. The generator remains connected to an Automatic Transfer Switch (ATS), which constantly monitors utility power.
Utility power operates normally.
The ATS continuously monitors incoming voltage.
A power outage occurs.
The ATS detects the failure.
The controller sends a start command.
The diesel engine starts automatically.
The alternator produces stable electrical power.
The ATS transfers the facility load to the generator.
Operations continue with minimal interruption.
When utility power returns, the ATS reconnects the facility to the grid.
The generator completes a cooling cycle and shuts down.
This entire process usually takes only a few seconds, helping protect equipment, production schedules, and critical business operations.
