Noise control measures:
1 sound insulation measures
In order to ensure that the noise of the equipment room is not transmitted from the channel, it is necessary to install a fireproof soundproof door at the location of the channel. The sound insulation door is designed according to the national standard J649-M1021. The door body is a double-layer steel plate composite structure, filled with high-quality sound-absorbing rock wool, and the frame is covered with felt at the joint of the door seam to play an effective sealing role.
2 wall sound absorption measures
According to the noise sound pressure level generated by the generator during operation and the situation of the machine room, it is determined whether the sound absorption processing is performed on the surrounding walls and the top surface of the machine room, and the project needs to perform sound absorption processing.
The wall and ceiling of the generator room are made of sound-absorbing rock wool, and the micro-perforated galvanized sheet is placed on the outside. The sound absorption body is as follows:
The angle iron is used as the keel, the angle iron mesh is 500×500mm, the galvanized sheet surface is 0.8mm thick, the perforation rate is >20%, and the bulk density of the glass fiber cloth and the galvanized iron wire mesh wrapped on the protective panel is 25kg/m3. The sound-absorbing rock wool with a thickness of 50 mm and the perforated plate for the outer wall serve as a sound-absorbing body shield.
3 air intake, exhaust air noise treatment
The noise of the inlet and outlet of the fan is also quite large, and it is not well managed to meet the relevant environmental requirements. In order to effectively reduce the aerodynamic noise of the inlet and outlet air, it is required to meet the requirements of the Class III standard, and it is intended to eliminate the noise of the inlet and exhaust vents. This project needs to do the air intake and exhaust air treatment.
4 secondary muffler
A muffler and a secondary muffler are installed separately for the exhaust gas pipe of the generator set. One of the muffler is self-contained for the generator set, and the secondary muffler is an impedance composite muffler with a noise reduction of more than 30 dB(A).
5 Other treatments
Through the wall pipe, the cable trench is the same as the sound insulation pipe, and the cable is filled with ultra-fine glass wool through the wall to implement soft contact to prevent the propagation of solid noise waves.
After adopting the above series of measures, the impact of the noise source of the equipment room on the outside world can meet the relevant environmental protection regulations.

How the 250 KVA Standby Diesel Generator Works
One of the most common questions I receive is how quickly a standby diesel generator can respond when utility power fails.
The process is completely automatic when the generator is connected to an Automatic Transfer Switch (ATS).
Once the ATS detects a utility power interruption, it immediately sends a start signal to the diesel engine. The engine reaches operating speed within seconds and begins driving the alternator.
The alternator converts mechanical energy into electrical energy and supplies power to the connected facility.
When utility electricity returns and stabilizes, the ATS automatically transfers the electrical load back to the grid and safely shuts down the generator.
This process occurs quickly and efficiently, minimizing operational disruptions.
