How the 200 KVA Standby Diesel Generator Works
The operating principle is simple but highly effective.
Under normal circumstances, utility electricity supplies power to the facility. The standby generator remains in ready mode.
When a power outage occurs, the Automatic Transfer Switch (ATS) immediately detects the loss of utility power and sends a signal to start the generator.
Within seconds, the Cummins engine reaches operating speed and drives the alternator. The alternator converts mechanical energy into electrical energy and supplies power to the connected loads.
The ATS automatically transfers the electrical load from the utility grid to the generator.
Once utility power returns and stabilizes, the ATS transfers the load back and safely shuts down the generator.
This automatic process helps ensure uninterrupted operation of critical systems.
