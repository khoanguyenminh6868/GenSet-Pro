What Is a 50/60 Hz 275 kVA Standby Diesel Generator?
A standby diesel generator is designed to automatically supply electricity when the primary utility power source fails. Unlike prime power generators that operate continuously, standby units remain ready for immediate operation during emergencies.

How the Generator Operates During a Power Failure
Many customers ask me how quickly the generator responds when utility power is interrupted.
The answer is simple: very quickly.
When utility electricity fails, the Automatic Transfer Switch (ATS) immediately detects the outage. The ATS then sends a signal to start the diesel engine.
Within seconds, the engine reaches operating speed and drives the alternator to generate electricity. The ATS automatically transfers the electrical load from the utility supply to the generator.
Once utility power returns and stabilizes, the ATS safely transfers the load back and shuts down the generator.
This process helps facilities avoid:
Production downtime
Equipment shutdowns
Data loss
Communication failures
Safety risks
Financial losses
For critical facilities, this automatic response can significantly reduce operational disruptions.
