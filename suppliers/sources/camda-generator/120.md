How the Generator Works During a Power Outage
Many customers ask me what actually happens when utility power fails. The process is surprisingly fast and fully automatic when an Automatic Transfer Switch (ATS) is installed.
When the ATS detects a power interruption, it immediately sends a start signal to the diesel engine. Within seconds, the engine reaches operating speed and drives the alternator to generate electricity.
The generated power is then transferred to the facility's electrical system. Critical equipment continues operating with minimal interruption. Once utility power returns and stabilizes, the ATS transfers the load back to the main power source and safely shuts down the generator.
This automatic sequence helps businesses avoid:
Production downtime
Data loss
Equipment damage
Safety risks
Revenue loss
Operational interruptions
For hospitals, manufacturing plants, and data centers, this rapid response can make a significant difference.
