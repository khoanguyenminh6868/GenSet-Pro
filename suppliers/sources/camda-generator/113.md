How the 135 KVA Standby Diesel Generator Works
The working process is simple but highly reliable.
Under normal conditions, utility power supplies electricity. The generator remains in standby mode.
When a power failure occurs, the Automatic Transfer Switch (ATS) detects the outage and sends a signal to start the generator.
The diesel engine starts immediately, reaches rated speed, and drives the alternator to produce electricity.
The load is transferred from the grid to the generator within seconds, ensuring uninterrupted operation.
When utility power returns, the system automatically switches back and shuts down safely.
This automatic process ensures continuous protection for critical systems.
