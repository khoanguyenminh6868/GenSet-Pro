When I work with customers on backup power planning, I always start from a simple reality: electricity failure never gives warning. It just happens.
That’s why a small but stable system like the 27 KVA standby diesel generator with CE and ISO verified is often the most practical solution for many businesses.
At Camda New Energy Equipment Co. Ltd., we don’t treat generators as simple machines. I see them more like a safety layer for business operations—quiet when everything is fine, but absolutely critical when things go wrong.
1. What This 27 KVA Standby Generator Is Built For
This 27 KVA diesel generator is designed for standby use. It stays idle during normal grid power and automatically starts when electricity fails. The CE and ISO verification ensures that the system follows international safety and manufacturing standards.
I usually explain it in simple terms: it’s like a backup worker who never sleeps, always ready to step in when needed.

2. Working Principle in Simple Language
Inside the generator system, there are three main parts: diesel engine, alternator, and controller. I always explain it in a way that even non-technical users can understand.
Engine = produces mechanical energy
Alternator = converts energy into electricity
Controller = manages start, stop, and protection
When power outage happens, the automatic transfer switch (ATS) detects it immediately and starts the generator within seconds. The load is transferred smoothly without manual operation.
