camda New Energy Equipment Co.,Ltd. is a dedicated manufacturer of generator set fueled by diesel, natural gas, biogas, oil gas, landfill gas and coal bed methane(CBM). With production capacity of 3000 units annually, Camda is one of the leading diesel genertor & Gas Generator Set manufacturer and supplier in China now.
Camda was previously known as Camda Generator Work Co. Ltd which was established in 1998, mainly producing Diesel Generator Set s. Later the name-changing marked a milestone in the company’s pursuit from conventional power solutions to renewable energies by building gas power division.
Through cooperation with China Cummins, Camda has developed its own brand of gas engines to power 30kWe to 500kWe Gas Generator sets, which are very popular in both domestic and overseas markets. Due to its outstanding achievements in gas power generation, Camda has been authorized as an exclusive distributor for GE gas engines (Jenbacher) since 2012.
Now we are working closely with worldwide brands, including GE Jenbacher, Cummins, Perkins, Yuchai, Stamford, Leroy Somer, Schneider, ABB, etc.
Camda has been widely accepted and respected nationwide and overseas due to its strict quality management and all-around expertise. ISO14001:2015 Environmental Management System is strictly followed in gensets manufacturing. ISO9001:2008 is strictly conducted to standardized business and manufacture operation to ensure quality control. OHSAS18001:2007 Occupational Health & Safety Management System is also highly observed to guarantee safety work. By the way, all of diesel and gas generator sets are CE certified by SGS.
Depending on our own manufacturing facilities, engineering & service team, Camda can provide global services in complete solution from design, manufacturing, construction, installation, to operation and maintenance. We always keep “service one, satisfy one” as our principal, continue to improve technology innovation, develop cost-effective products, devote to be a pioneer in new energy and energy saving field.
