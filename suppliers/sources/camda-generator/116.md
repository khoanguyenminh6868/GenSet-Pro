How the AC Three Phase 225 KVA Standby Diesel Generator Set Works
The operating principle is straightforward but highly effective.
Under normal conditions, utility electricity powers the facility. The standby generator remains ready for operation.
When utility power fails, an Automatic Transfer Switch (ATS) immediately detects the interruption and sends a signal to start the generator.
Within seconds, the Cummins diesel engine reaches operating speed and drives the alternator to generate electricity. The ATS transfers the electrical load from the utility grid to the generator.
Once utility power is restored and stable, the ATS automatically switches the load back and safely shuts down the generator.
This fully automated process helps maintain business continuity with minimal disruption.
