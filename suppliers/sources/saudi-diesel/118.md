Product Description
OVERVIEW
| | Heading | Description
| Overview | The Bobcat PG770W is a 60 Hz-only large generator, listed by Bobcat Middle East & Africa as non-current after a 2024–2025 production run. As a 60 Hz-only model it matches 60 Hz networks such as Saudi Arabia’s, and it delivers 800 kVA standby and 744 kVA prime from a Hyundai DP222LA engine and a Leroy Somer TAL-A49-B1 alternator. The enclosed unit measures 6,060 × 2,440 × 2,590 mm, weighs 6,485 kg dry and runs for about 7 hours at 75% load, with a DSE 7320 MkII controller. This page is kept as a reference for PG770W units in operation; SDEC can help with service and parts enquiries and current Bobcat alternatives.
