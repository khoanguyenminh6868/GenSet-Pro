Product Description
OVERVIEW
| | Heading | Description
| Overview | The Bobcat PG450W is a non-current large generator, listed by Bobcat Middle East & Africa with production from 2016 to 2018. It produces 450 kVA standby and 405 kVA prime at 50 Hz, or 495 / 445 kVA at 60 Hz, from a Hyundai P158LE engine coupled to a Leroy Somer TAL-A47-A alternator. A 950-litre tank provides around 13 hours at 75% load, and the enclosed unit measures 5,260 × 2,000 × 2,130 mm. Control is by a Deep Sea Electronics DSE 7320 panel. This page is maintained as a technical reference for PG450W units still in service; SDEC can help with service and parts enquiries and advise on current Bobcat models.
