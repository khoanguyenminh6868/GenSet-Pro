Product Description
OVERVIEW
| | Heading | Description
| Overview | The Bobcat PG60W is the largest model in Bobcat’s small generator range and has its own Bobcat 60 kVA listing, with a different engine and alternator from the PG50W. It delivers 63 kVA standby and 58 kVA prime at 50 Hz (65 / 59 kVA at 60 Hz) from a turbocharged four-cylinder Yanmar 4TNV106T engine paired with a Stamford S1L2-Y1 alternator. A 135-litre tank and a Deep Sea Electronics DSE 4520 controller are fitted as standard, with 400/230 V at 50 Hz or 380/220 V at 60 Hz output. It suits larger site compounds, workshops and commercial standby duties. SDEC can confirm the configuration for your application and support the unit with parts and service in Saudi Arabia.
