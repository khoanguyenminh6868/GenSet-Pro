Mitsubishi MGS-R Diesel Generators – C Models
The Mitsubishi MGS-R Series is the diesel generator set line from Mitsubishi Heavy Industries, developed for facilities where backup power cannot fail. Mitsubishi states that the series is aimed primarily at data centres across Asian and Middle Eastern markets and also serves as a reliable backup power solution for commercial facilities such as shopping malls and office buildings.
This page presents the Mitsubishi MGS-R models listed on this page with the C suffix. Each model page lists the standby and prime ratings and the Mitsubishi engine fitted, with a brochure to download, so you can shortlist a Mitsubishi generator against your load profile.
For data-centre projects, Mitsubishi states that MGS-R generator sets satisfy the Tier III and Tier IV standards of the Uptime Institute, alongside the ISO Class G3 standard for generator sets. That makes the series a strong candidate wherever the specification calls for a mission-critical standby diesel generator.
Selecting a power generator at this level involves more than the rating: frequency, load steps, enclosure, paralleling and fuel system all need to match the facility design. SDEC’s power team can work with your consultant to confirm the correct MGS-R configuration and scope of supply.
SDEC has served the Kingdom since 1978 and supports Mitsubishi generator sets with spare parts and maintenance services.
Contact SDEC to confirm current availability and to request technical data and a quotation for the MGS-R models with the C suffix.
