Mitsubishi MGS-R Diesel Generators – L Models
When a data hall, a shopping mall or an office tower loses mains power, the generator sets behind it have seconds to take the load. The Mitsubishi MGS-R diesel generator sets on this page are built for exactly this kind of standby duty.
The MGS-R Series was launched by Mitsubishi Heavy Industries as its current diesel generator set line. Mitsubishi positions it primarily for data centres in Asian and Middle Eastern markets and as a reliable backup power solution for large commercial facilities. According to Mitsubishi, the sets satisfy the Uptime Institute’s Tier III and Tier IV standards and the ISO Class G3 standard.
This page shows the MGS-R models listed on this page with the L suffix. On each model page you will find the standby and prime ratings and the Mitsubishi engine model, together with a brochure, so that your consultant can compare generator sets and plan single or multiple-set installations.
Large standby plants are engineered systems. Our power team can support you from the first load list through to delivery, helping to confirm the MGS-R configuration that suits the electrical design and the site.
After commissioning, SDEC keeps your Mitsubishi power generator ready with spare parts and maintenance services delivered through its branches across the Kingdom.
Contact SDEC to confirm current availability and to request technical data and a quotation for the MGS-R models with the L suffix.
