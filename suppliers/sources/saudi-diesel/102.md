Bobcat Medium Generators
Bobcat Medium Generators from SDEC cover commercial and industrial power requirements above the small-generator range while remaining below Bobcat’s large-generator class. The models listed on this page are PG90W, PG110W and PG165W, all of which fall within Bobcat’s official Medium classification.
Verified Bobcat ratings include 80 kVA prime / 88 kVA standby for PG90W, 100 / 110 kVA for PG110W, and 150 / 165 kVA for PG165W. This makes the category relevant for customers comparing a 100 kVA generator or other medium diesel generator sizes for prime or standby power.
Generator selection should be based on the real electrical load, starting current, duty cycle and required reserve rather than the headline kVA figure alone. The right sizing decision helps avoid both an undersized set and unnecessary excess capacity.
SDEC supplies Bobcat generators in Saudi Arabia and supports customers with sizing guidance, spare parts and after-sales service. Review the model pages for the available specifications and contact SDEC to confirm current availability and request a quotation.
