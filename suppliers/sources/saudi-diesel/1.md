About Us
TRUSTED INDUSTRIAL EQUIPMENT PARTNER IN SAUDI ARABIA SINCE 1978
Saudi Diesel Equipment Company (SDEC) is a leading provider of industrial equipment, heavy machinery, and power solutions in Saudi Arabia. Since 1978, SDEC has supported construction, infrastructure, industrial, logistics, energy, and other key sectors with reliable equipment, global brands, and comprehensive after-sales support across the Kingdom.
