Product Description
OVERVIEW
| | Heading | Description
| Model overview | Mitsubishi MGS0800R-C is a 60 Hz diesel generator set supplied by Saudi Diesel Equipment Company (SDEC) for standby and prime power planning on commercial and industrial projects. The MHIESA specification sheet rates it at 787.5 kVA / 630 kW standby, with prime power of 700 kVA / 560 kW at 380 V or 712.5 kVA / 570 kW at 480 V (outputs at 40 °C, 1,000 m ASL). It is powered by the Mitsubishi S6R-PTAR engine with an S54F (380 V) or MG-S5F (480 V) alternator; the set measures 3550 × 1800 × 1940 mm with a dry weight of 5500–5650 kg.
| Applications & enquiry | For standby and prime power planning for commercial and industrial projects, compare the exact model variant, installation or working constraints, power and load requirements, and service documentation with the project brief. Contact SDEC to confirm the correct MGS0800R-C configuration, current supply status and relevant documentation for Saudi Arabia.
