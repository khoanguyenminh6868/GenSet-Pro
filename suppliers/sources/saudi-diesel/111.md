Product Description
OVERVIEW
| | Heading | Description
| Overview | The Bobcat PG330W is a non-current large generator listed by Bobcat Middle East & Africa with 2024 production. It is rated at 330 kVA standby and 299 kVA prime at 50 Hz, with notably higher output at 60 Hz (380 / 345 kVA), which made it a strong option for 60 Hz networks such as Saudi Arabia’s. It uses a Hyundai P126TI-II engine with a Leroy Somer TAL-A46-H alternator and a DSE 7320 MkII controller, running about 6.5 hours on the standard 340-litre tank or 10 hours on the 530-litre extended tank. This page serves as a reference for existing PG330W units; contact SDEC for service, parts enquiries and current Bobcat alternatives.
