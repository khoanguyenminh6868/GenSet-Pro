Bobcat Large Generators
Bobcat large generators are diesel generator sets built for sites that need dependable power at scale. Bobcat describes its largest generators as offering outstanding power for the most demanding applications, built on a robust design with proven components.
Saudi Diesel Equipment Company (SDEC) supplies Bobcat power generators in Saudi Arabia for prime and standby duty. The large-range units listed on this page are equipped with Hyundai engines. Each model page shows its standby and prime ratings and a downloadable brochure, so you can compare generator sets against your load requirement.
Industrial generators of this size are typically used as standby power for facilities, to keep operations running during outages and emergencies, and as temporary power for events and worksites.
Choosing a diesel generator is about more than the kVA figure. Our team can help you size the set, select the right configuration and plan the installation. After delivery, SDEC supports your generator with spare parts and maintenance services through our branches across the Kingdom.
Model availability changes over time. Contact SDEC to confirm the current Bobcat generator range and to request a quotation.
