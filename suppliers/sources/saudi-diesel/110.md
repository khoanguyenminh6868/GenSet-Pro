Product Description
OVERVIEW
| | Heading | Description
| Overview | The Bobcat PG300W is a large mobile diesel generator that Bobcat Middle East & Africa now lists as a non-current model (produced 2015–2024). It is rated at 303 kVA standby and 272 kVA prime at 50 Hz (327 / 304 kVA at 60 Hz), with a Hyundai P126TI-series engine, a Leroy Somer TAL-A46 alternator and a Deep Sea Electronics DSE 7320 MkII controller. With the standard 340-litre tank it runs for around 7 hours at 75% load, or 11 hours with the 530-litre extended tank, in a 4,400 × 1,370 × 2,100 mm enclosure. This page is kept as a technical reference for existing PG300W owners; SDEC can support these units with service and parts enquiries and advise on current Bobcat alternatives.
