Bobcat Small Generators
Bobcat Small Generators from SDEC provide compact diesel generator options for customers who need dependable prime or standby power in Saudi Arabia. The SDEC range on this page includes PG20W, PG30W, PG50W and PG60W, covering Bobcat’s small-generator class from roughly 20 to 60 kVA.
Official Bobcat data confirms 50 Hz prime and standby ratings across this range, with the listed models stepping from compact output levels up to the PG60W. Available generator options can include control-panel upgrades, extended fuel tanks, circuit breakers, 60 Hz configurations and other factory options depending on the model and project requirement.
A small diesel generator is useful when the required load does not justify a larger industrial set, but correct sizing still matters. Starting currents, continuous load, standby duty and future load growth should all be considered before selecting a generator.
SDEC supplies Bobcat generators in the Kingdom and supports customers with product selection, spare parts and after-sales service. Review the model pages for available specifications, then contact SDEC to confirm the current Bobcat small generator range and request a quotation.
