| Overview | The Bobcat PG165W is a diesel generator set designed for prime and standby power on construction sites, industrial facilities, commercial buildings and remote projects. The main operating figures for this model are listed in the specification section on this page, together with the product images and, where available, the brochure. Saudi Diesel Equipment Company (SDEC) supplies this equipment in Saudi Arabia and can help you choose the right configuration for your application, arrange delivery, and support the machine with genuine spare parts and after-sales service through its branches across the Kingdom.
