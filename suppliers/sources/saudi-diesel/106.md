DEVELON Wheel Loaders
DEVELON wheel loaders from SDEC provide productive loading and material-handling capability for construction, infrastructure, quarry and heavy-site applications in Saudi Arabia. The range covers different machine sizes so customers can match the wheel loader to the material, bucket requirement and working conditions.
Verified DEVELON data for machines represented in this category includes models for infrastructure construction as well as mining, quarry and general construction work. For example, the DL420A-7M is listed at 22.595 t operating weight with a 3.9–4.1 m³ bucket and 218 kW engine power. Other verified models in the range cover smaller operating weights and bucket sizes.
The right wheel loader depends on more than machine size. Material density, target bucket capacity, loading height, travel distance and site conditions all affect productivity and machine selection.
SDEC supplies DEVELON construction equipment in the Kingdom with spare parts and after-sales service. Contact us with your material, bucket and site requirements to identify the right wheel loader and request a quotation.
