Product Description
OVERVIEW
| | Heading | Description
| Overview | The Bobcat PG750W is a non-current large generator that Bobcat Middle East & Africa lists with production from 2015 to 2025. It produces 746 kVA standby and 679 kVA prime at 50 Hz, rising to 878 / 794 kVA at 60 Hz, from a Hyundai DP222LB engine with a Leroy Somer TAL-A49 alternator (TAL-A49B at 50 Hz, TAL-A49-C at 60 Hz). As an enclosed unit it measures 6,060 × 2,440 × 2,590 mm, weighs 6,485 kg dry and runs for about 7.5 hours at 75% load. This page remains as a reference for PG750W generators in the field; SDEC can support service and parts enquiries and advise on current Bobcat high-output models.
