Product Description
OVERVIEW
| | Heading | Description
| Overview | The Bobcat PG20W is the entry model of Bobcat’s small mobile generator range, rated at 19 kVA standby and 18 kVA prime at 50 Hz, or 24 / 21 kVA at 60 Hz. It pairs a naturally aspirated Yanmar 4TNV88 diesel engine with a Stamford S0L2-G1 alternator and a 135-litre fuel tank, giving up to 36 hours of running at 75% prime load on 50 Hz. With a dry weight of 861 kg and a silenced canopy as standard, it is a practical choice for site cabins, small workshops, lighting and temporary power where a compact unit is needed. SDEC can advise on configuration, quotation, genuine parts and after-sales support in Saudi Arabia.
