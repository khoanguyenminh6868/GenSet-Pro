Product Description
OVERVIEW
| | Heading | Description
| Overview | The Bobcat PG90W is the Volvo-powered 88 kVA model in Bobcat’s 70–110 kVA medium generator range, with 88 kVA standby and 80 kVA prime at 50 Hz, or 94 / 89 kVA at 60 Hz. It is built around a Volvo TAD530GE engine (Stage II / Tier 2) and a Leroy Somer TAL-44-B alternator, controlled by a Deep Sea Electronics DSE 4520 panel. A 300-litre tank provides around 18 hours at 75% prime load, and the silenced package measures 1,870 × 1,004 × 1,870 mm with a dry weight of 1,900 kg. It is well suited to construction sites, commercial buildings and events needing reliable mid-range power. SDEC can size the unit for your load and provide quotation, parts and service in Saudi Arabia.
