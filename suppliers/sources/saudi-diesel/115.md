Product Description
OVERVIEW
| | Heading | Description
| Overview | The Bobcat PG610W is a non-current large generator, produced from 2015 to 2021 according to Bobcat Middle East & Africa. It is rated at 605 kVA standby and 550 kVA prime at 50 Hz (660 / 600 kVA at 60 Hz) and uses a Hyundai DP180LA engine, with a Leroy Somer TAL-A47-D alternator for 50 Hz builds and TAL-A47-F for 60 Hz. In the 5,260 × 2,000 × 2,130 mm enclosure its 950-litre extended tank provides about 10 hours at 75% load. This page documents the PG610W for owners and service teams; SDEC can help with service and parts enquiries and advise on current Bobcat large generators.
