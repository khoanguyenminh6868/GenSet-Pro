Product Description
OVERVIEW
| | Heading | Description
| Overview | The Bobcat PG550W is a non-current large generator, produced between 2015 and 2024 according to Bobcat Middle East & Africa. It delivers 550 kVA standby and 500 kVA prime at 50 Hz, increasing to 615 / 560 kVA at 60 Hz, and is driven by a Hyundai DP158LD engine with a Leroy Somer TAL-A47-C alternator. Sharing the 5,260 × 2,000 × 2,130 mm enclosure and 950-litre tank of the PG450W, it runs for around 10 hours at 75% load under DSE 7320 control. This page is kept as a reference for existing PG550W generators; SDEC can assist with service and parts enquiries and recommend current Bobcat equivalents.
