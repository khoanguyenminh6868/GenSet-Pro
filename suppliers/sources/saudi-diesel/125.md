Product Description
OVERVIEW
| | Heading | Description
| Overview | The Bobcat PG50W delivers 46 kVA standby and 41 kVA prime at 50 Hz, rising to 48 / 43 kVA at 60 Hz, which makes it the mid-point of Bobcat’s small generator line. It uses a turbocharged Yanmar 4TNV98T engine with a Stamford S1L2-N1 alternator and offers up to 19 hours of running at 75% prime load at 50 Hz. The unit shares the 2,200 × 1,040 × 1,630 mm silenced package of the PG30W but weighs 1,000 kg dry. It suits medium site offices, workshops, pumps and standby power for small commercial buildings. SDEC can confirm the right rating for your load and support the unit with parts and service in Saudi Arabia.
