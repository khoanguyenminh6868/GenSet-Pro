Product Description
OVERVIEW
| | Heading | Description
| Overview | The Bobcat PG30W is a small silenced diesel generator rated at 32 kVA standby and 30 kVA prime at 50 Hz (31 / 29 kVA at 60 Hz). It is powered by a Yanmar 4TNV98 engine driving a Stamford S0L2-P1 alternator, and Bobcat quotes up to 24 hours of running at 75% prime load at 50 Hz. The unit measures 2,200 × 1,040 × 1,630 mm and weighs 921 kg dry, so it moves easily between sites. Typical uses include small construction sites, events, shops and backup for light commercial loads. SDEC can help size the generator for your load, prepare a quotation and provide parts and service in Saudi Arabia.
