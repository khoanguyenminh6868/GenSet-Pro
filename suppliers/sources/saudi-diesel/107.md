DISD Wheel Loaders
DISD wheel loaders from SDEC cover small and mid-sized loading requirements for construction and material-handling work. DEVELON’s official regional site describes the SD wheel-loader line as specially designed for small and mid-sized wheel loader applications.
The verified machines represented in this range span from the SD200N at 10.3 t operating weight and 1.7 m³ bucket capacity up to the SD380-2 at 19 t and 3.7 m³. Verified model applications include general and infrastructure construction as well as mining and quarry work, depending on the machine.
For customers comparing a small wheel loader or mid-sized loader, the right choice depends on material density, bucket requirement, loading height, travel pattern and ground conditions. Matching these factors to the machine size is more useful than selecting by operating weight alone.
SDEC supplies and supports the DISD/SD wheel-loader range in Saudi Arabia with product guidance, spare parts and after-sales service. Contact the team to confirm current model availability, specifications and quotation.
