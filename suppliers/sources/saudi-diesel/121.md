Product Description
OVERVIEW
| | Heading | Description
| Overview | The Bobcat PG110W is the Volvo-powered 110 kVA model in Bobcat’s 70–110 kVA medium range, producing 110 kVA standby and 100 kVA prime at 50 Hz, or 118 / 105 kVA at 60 Hz. It uses a Volvo TAD531GE engine (Stage II / Tier 2) with a Leroy Somer TAL-44-D alternator in the same compact 1,870 × 1,004 × 1,870 mm silenced package as the PG90W, so it delivers more power without a larger footprint. The 300-litre tank gives about 13 hours at 75% prime load, and dry weight is 1,900 kg. It is a strong fit for commercial buildings, larger site compounds and rental fleets. SDEC can confirm the configuration, prepare a quotation and support the unit with parts and service in Saudi Arabia.
