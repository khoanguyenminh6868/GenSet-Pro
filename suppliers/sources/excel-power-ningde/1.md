Introduction
Excel Power devotes itself to researching, manufacturing, and maintenance of generator sets and power systems, provides comprehensive, specialized, scale and systematic power system solutions. Excel Power has absorbed advantage for the global first-level generator sets design and manufacture technologies and advanced Processing skills. Products cover varied diesel, gasoline, natural gas generator sets, mobile power station, brushless alternators, ST & STC alternators, etc. Power range from 5Kw to 2000Kw.
Excel Power holds the concept of "safe, advanced, environmental" as the core value, with excellent quality and service, Excel Power has built good reputation in the area of generator sets. Excel Powerhas certificated with
the ISO9001:2008, ISO4001, CE, products are exported to more than 90 countries and regions including Europe, Americas, Middle East, Africa, Asia, etc. The products are widely used in many fields such as agriculture and
forestry, mining, farms, hospitals, telecommunication system, buildings, armies, hotels, airports, etc. Meanwhile, Excel Power has established global service systems with strategic partners all over the world.
