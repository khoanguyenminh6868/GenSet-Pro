Description & Specifications
Product Overview
The Brushless Synchronous Generator YH/YHD series is an industrial-grade 4-pole AC brushless alternator engineered and manufactured by FUAN KELIANG ELECTRICAL MACHINERY CO.,LTD. (established in 1991). Compliant with international standards including IEC 60034-1, ISO 8528, and GB755, this series delivers ultra-stable electric power, rapid transient recovery, and exceptional operating efficiency for integration with premier diesel and gas engines worldwide.
Key Engineering Features
Advanced Brushless Excitation with AVR: Equipped with a high-reliability solid-state Automatic Voltage Regulator (SX460 / AS440) maintaining voltage stability within ±1.0% under fluctuating loads, with superior motor starting surge capacity.
12-Lead Reconnectable Stator Winding: Features 12 reconnectable terminal leads allowing versatile voltage configurations: Series Star (380V-415V-440V-480V), Parallel Star (190V-208V-240V), and Series Delta (220V-240V-277V).
2/3 Pitch Winding Design: Effectively suppresses excessive neutral current circulation and third harmonic waveform distortion (THD Class H Insulation & 100% Pure Copper: High-grade Class H (180°C) thermal insulation with vacuum pressure impregnation (VPI) across all stator and rotor windings, engineered for continuous operation (125K temperature rise) and standby emergency peak load (163K temperature rise).
Dynamic Rotor Balancing: Rotor assembly is dynamically balanced to Grade G2.5 per ISO 1940 standards, paired with premium sealed-for-life bearings for smooth, low-vibration performance.
Standard SAE Engine Interfacing: Available in both single-bearing flexible disc coupling (SAE 3, SAE 4, SAE 5 / flywheels 6.5, 7.5, 8, 10, 11.5) and double-bearing foot-mounted designs compatible with all major engine brands.
