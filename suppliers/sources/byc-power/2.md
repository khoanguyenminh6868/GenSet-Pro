COMPANY PROFILE
Fuan Boyuan Power Machinery Co.,LTD. ( abbreviation: BYC Power) devotes itself to manufacuring and maintenance of diesel generator, AC alternator and power systems, provides specialized, scale and systematic power system solutions. BYC Power, which aims to find the most suitable solutions for your valuable customers' needs and to provide the best service, has been continuing its way with the same principles on quality control.
Main products cover diesel generator sets and AC alternator. Power range from 5KVA to 2500KVA. All products are strictly manufactured in accordance with the ISO9001：2015 production standard to assure best quality, and are complied with CE certificate. Base on good control at quality, delivery time and service, BYC POWER set up good cooperation relationship with business partners from more than 50 countries and regions including Americas, Europe, Middle East, Africa, Asia, etc. The annual production are growing year by year steady.
