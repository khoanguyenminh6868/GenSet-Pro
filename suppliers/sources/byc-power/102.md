Product Description
BYC Power's self-starting 600kW containerized generator provides a powerful power solution for large-scale operations. Rated at 16/18 kW, it is ideal for a variety of applications that require a reliable power supply. The generator can run at 50/60 Hz with 1500/1800 rpm options to meet different industrial needs.
The generator provides a wide voltage range of 220V-480V and supports single-phase and three-phase operation. It has different design options including silent, open, trailer, canopy and containerized configurations to meet specific installation requirements.
The engine model used is 4Z2.3-G12, with a power of 21/23kW, a displacement of 2.3L, a lubricating oil capacity of 10L, and a fuel consumption of 3.65L/h, which can ensure long-term operation and high fuel utilization.
The generator is a single-bearing, brushless, self-excited alternator with a choice of top brands including Stanford, Leroy-Somer, Mecc Alte, Marathon and Faraday. The alternator has a power factor of 0.8 and can regulate the voltage by 25% for stable performance. It features H-class insulation and IP23 protection to ensure durability in challenging environments.
For easy operation and control, the generators can be equipped with various controller options from DeepSea, DEIF, ComAp or SmartGen.
