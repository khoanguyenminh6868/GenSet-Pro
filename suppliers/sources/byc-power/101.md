Product Description
Introducing the high performance 10 kVA open diesel generator for mining. This generator is designed for heavy duty applications. It has a rated output of 10 kW.
The generator operates at 50/60Hz and has a maximum output of 10.5kW, ensuring reliable power. The rated voltage can be set to 220V or as required.
It is available in single or three phase options. Power factor is 1, ensuring efficient operation. Insulation class F, for durability.
The generator adopts self-excited constant voltage (AVR) excitation system. It has a standard control panel for easy use. There are two sockets available for use, which is convenient for use.
The engine type is a single cylinder, vertical, 4-stroke design. The displacement is 498cc and the compression ratio is 19:1. The fuel type is diesel, which is suitable for a variety of conditions.
It has an oil capacity of 1.65 liters and a fuel tank capacity of 13.5 liters. This generator is ideal for mining operations and can provide stable power in harsh environments.
