PT Traktor Nusantara is an Exclusive Perkins Engine Distributor in Indonesia and also fabricating Generator Set (Genset) since 1976.
Since 2020, PT Traktor Nusantara have two types operations which are classified with Traknus Generator Set (Genset) and Traknus S-Series
Traknus Genset S-Series itself classify the product into 2 types:
Open Type ( 9 – 1000 kVA) - Perkins Engine with Traknus Alternator
Silent Type ( 9 – 1000 kVA) - Perkins Engine with Traknus Alternator
