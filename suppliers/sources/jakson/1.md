Frequently Asked Questions
What services does JAKSON Group offer?
We offer a comprehensive range of energy solutions, including diesel generators, solar modules, solar rooftop systems, battery energy storage systems (BESS), and microgrid solutions. Our portfolio also includes green hydrogen production, infrastructure services (EPC), renewable EPC, renewable IPP, biofuels , and various other green energy solutions. We also provide specialized solutions for defense applications and metro rail electrification projects.
What social impact initiatives is JAKSON Group involved in?
Jakson drives impactful social change through CSR initiatives in three key focus areas. Our Education initiatives include Early Learning Centers, providing quality education to underprivileged children. We promote Health and Well-being through health camps with Project Baala and sanitation facilities in rural communities. Our Community Empowerment programs develop sustainable model villages in Balaghat and Phaltan with a focus on water management, agriculture enhancement, and creating income generation avenues for villagers.
What are the goals of the JAKSON Group?
We intend to become a Rs 15,000 crore company soon with 15 GW+ RE assets by 2030, having global leadership in solar and green hydrogen space , transmission assets across the country, and driving India’s renewable energy future with large-scale investments and innovation.
How does JAKSON Group stay ahead of global trends in renewable technology?
We stay ahead of global energy solution trends through strategic diversification into emerging sectors like green hydrogen, participation in global climate initiatives like COP28, and continuous innovation in our manufacturing facilities. Our international presence across India, Europe, MEA, CIS, and APAC regions allows us to adapt to regional renewable trends while maintaining partnerships with industry leaders worldwide.
What innovative technologies is JAKSON Group investing in for future energy solutions?
We are investing in cutting-edge technologies, including green hydrogen and ammonia production, electrolyzer manufacturing, battery energy storage systems, biofuels, waste-to-energy solutions, and utility-scale energy storage. We are also pioneering projects like India’s first CO2-to-methanol plant and hydrogen systems for urban mobility applications.
What makes JAKSON Group different from other energy solution providers?
With over 77 years of expertise, we distinguish ourselves through our commitment to 24/7 reliability, trust , sustainability focus, comprehensive offerings, and state-of-the-art manufacturing facilities. Our turnkey solutions span from concept to commissioning, and we maintain a unique dual role as both a power producer and infrastructure provider. This gives us an exceptional understanding of risk-return dynamics.
How can I stay updated with JAKSON Group's latest news and innovations?
You can follow us through our official website, social media platforms, industry publications, and by subscribing to our newsletter. We also participate in major industry events like RE Invest, REI Expo etc. where we showcase our latest innovations and commitments to sustainable energy solutions.
What is the history of the JAKSON Group?
We were founded in 1947 as a distributor of electrical goods. In 1979, we started manufacturing gensets, partnered with Cummins in 1982, and pioneered acoustic enclosures in 1998. We diversified into infrastructure solutions in 2012, commissioned our first solar IPP plant in 2014, launched PV module manufacturing in 2016, and recently established biofuels manufacturing and electrolyzer production facilities.
How does JAKSON Group ensure energy efficiency in its solutions?
We ensure energy efficiency through our fuel-efficient CPCB IV+ diesel generators, innovative solar solutions that optimize sunlight conversion, and microgrid systems with ACC battery technology. Our hybrid energy solutions combine renewable sources with power backup systems for optimal efficiency.
