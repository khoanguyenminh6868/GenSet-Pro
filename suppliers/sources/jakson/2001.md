Power That Never Lets Your Industry Down
In industrial operations, downtime is not just inconvenient – it is costly. Jakson, an authorised OEM partner of Cummins India, manufactures CPCB IV+ compliant silent diesel gensets from 7.5 kVA to 3750 kVA, built to keep your plant, hospital, data centre, or infrastructure running without interruption.
Why Industries Choose Jakson Generators
Cummins-powered reliability, advanced combustion, digital controls, and proven engine technology for long, stable service life.
Complete kVA range - 7.5 kVA to 3750 kVA, one brand, every load.
Built for Indian industries, for high-temperature operation, dusty environment, grid fluctuations, and heavy starting loads.
Silent and CPCB IV+ compliant, factory-fitted enclosures no need for retrofits.
Pan-India support with trained service engineers, genuine spares, and fast response.

Key Benefits of Indsutrial Diesel Genset
Instant Switchover
AMF panels detect grid failure and immediately auto-start the genset. Motors, compressors, and CNC machines keep running continuously.
Fuel Efficiency
Cummins engines deliver more kW per litre. Operating at 50-75% load keeps fuel costs lean and lifecycle costs lower.
Heavy Load Handling
Designed to absorb high inrush currents from motors, cranes, and pumps without voltage dips or frequency swings.
Silent and Compliant
Acoustic enclosures and CPCB IV+ engines keep your plant within noise and emission norms - no extras required.
Low Maintenance
Extended service intervals, durable components, and Jakson AMC plans minimize downtime and operating costs.
