Company Profile
Jiangsu Nenjo Generator Technology Co., Ltd. started in 1995, formerly known as the state-owned Jiangdu Mindu Generator Factory (a subsidiary unit of the Environmental Protection Bureau) is one of the professional high-tech private enterprises engaged in generator research and development, manufacturing and sales , service for more than 20 years. At present, it has developed into an innovative enterprise with a registered capital of 58 million Yuan. Our company has a beautiful environment and covers an area of nearly 50,000 square meters. It is a garden-style factory with a standardized workshop of more than 30,000 square meters. Now there are 96 employees, including 37 senior technical workers in our company.
Our company introduced and digested foreign advanced brushless generator manufacturing technology, strictly in accordance with GB/T2820.3-2009 and GB755 national standards. Our NENJO brand alternators have EU CE certification and North American CSA certification, it can be connected with all engines with SAE standard interfaces in the world. NENJO alternators have the characteristics of sufficient power, long life, light weight, and beautiful appearance. A number of alternators have been well received and appreciated by the majority of users for a long time.
Our company has excellent process equipment, strong technical force, and various technical indicators are in the leading position in China.
Our company have ISO9001 international quality management system certification standard, ISO14001 Environmental management system certification, ISO45001 Occupational Health and Safety Management System Certification and Marine use alternator CCS( China Classification Society) certification. our company's products have been exported to North Korea, Vietnam, Russia, Japan, Uzbekistan, Pakistan, New Zealand, United Arab Emirates, Australia, Ghana and more than 30 countries and regions. Our company always aim to the principle of quality first and customer foremost to provide you with comprehensive services.
