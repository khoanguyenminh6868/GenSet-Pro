Tianjin Sinogas Repower Energy Co., Ltd was established in 2009, is located in Tianjin, China. After years development, the company has developed into a professional natural gas and hydrogen gas industry equipment supplier and technical solution provider.
We are specialist to supply full value chain equipment & solution for natural gas industry including: upstream gas treatment solution, midstream gas storage and transportation solutions, down stream utilization solutions.
Our company mission is to offer best products and services to globle business partners !
Our company belief :
Production of innovative, reliable, high quality natual gas products and provide highly satisfactory after service to our customers.
To train professional and dedicated engineering team to provide customers with professional solution and service for customers' CNG and LNG business.
Always for the perspective of clients' interests and response to customer requirements at first time.
Provide strong support for our partners domestic and abroad, both at technical and commercial aspects.
For offer full range of natural gas industry value chain equipment and technical solutions:
1) Oil and gas field: gas purification equipment, gas field power supply solution, flame gas recycling and transportation packages, de-hydrogen skid;
2) Gas treatment: Mini LNG plant, large capacity gas compressor, city gas gate station, pressure regulation unit, CNG mother station for gas distribution network, LNG refilling station, mobile CNG/LNG station;
3) Gas transportation: Mini-LNG carrier, marine CNG carrier, CNG ship, CNG ISO container, CNG trailer, large capacity mining purpose CNG container, railway transportation CNG container, LNG ISO tank container, LNG semi-trailer, H2 gas trailer, hydrogen gas storage unit;
4) Power generator: gas generator from 300-4000KW, gas power plant, off-grid power generation solution from natural gas fuel, bio-gas power generation solution, power plant gas storage and peak-shaving solution，energy-saving solution;
5) Bi-fuel engine and power solution for vehicle, ship.
For our partner around the world, Sinogas always can provide you with reliable natural gas application solutions and technical support; With support from our group engineering team, we are cooperating with customers from more than 30 countries around the world, we provide complete services and solutions for gas station owner, virtual gas pipeline operators, gas transportation business operators and customers, we always meet and exceed customer needs and expectations.
Clean energy makes our world better, we always look forward to cooperate with more friends around the world, to promote our business together, to help the energy market, to help the people and our environment !
