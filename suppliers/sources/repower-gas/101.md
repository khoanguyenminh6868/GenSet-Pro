Skid Mounted Gas Dryer
Skid Mounted Gas Dryer
1. We have BWD rear natural gas dryer (720-3000NM3/h) and FWD pre-dehydration natural gas dryer(720-20000NM3/h)
2. The main structure adopts the whole armored double adsorption tower and fully automatic (semi-automatic) control mode.
3. The adsorption tower is filled with high-quality molecular sieve, and the porous molecular sieve has strong affinity for water to remove water in the natural gas
4. The heating regeneration method is used to remove the water in the saturated adsorption tower, so that the molecular sieve in the adsorption tower can regain water absorption capacity.
