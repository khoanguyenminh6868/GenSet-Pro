Frequently asked questions
What is a marine genset and why is it important?
A marine genset, a generating set, or a generator engine is an auxiliary engine that provides electrical power for ship systems and diesel-electric propulsion. It ensures reliable onboard energy supply for essential operations.
What power ranges do Wärtsilä gensets cover?
W&auml;rtsil&auml; gensets are available in power ranges from approximately 0,74 MW to 10,4 MW, covering requirements for small ferries to large merchant vessels.
Are Wärtsilä gensets suitable for diesel-electric propulsion?
Yes. W&auml;rtsil&auml; gensets are designed for both auxiliary power and diesel-electric propulsion applications, providing flexibility for different vessel designs.
How compact are Wärtsilä gensets compared to alternatives?
W&auml;rtsil&auml; gensets feature a compact footprint for easier integration and space savings on board, without compromising power output or efficiency. Dimensions are listed on this page for each genset model. Detailed performance data is available through the W&auml;rtsil&auml; Engine Configurator .
Can Wärtsilä gensets operate on alternative and future fuels?
Yes. W&auml;rtsil&auml; gensets are designed for fuel flexibility, including LNG, ammonia and methanol. A modular engine design ensures that W&auml;rtsil&auml; gensets are easy to convert to future fuels for most efficient operations throughout a vessel’s lifetime. Please contact us if you don’t find a genset model for a specific fuel you are looking for.
