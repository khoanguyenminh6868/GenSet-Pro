ATS
The ATS Cabinet is an intelligent power system integrating diesel generators to form a robust emergency supply, keeping critical operations running during outages.
Core Advantages:
1. Instant Failover
Detects power interruptions in milliseconds and switches to backup generators immediately, ensuring zero downtime for emergency systems like lighting, security, and fire protection.
2. Smart Restoration
Automatically reconnects to main power when it resumes—no manual input needed—optimizing energy efficiency.
3. 24/7 Reliability
Engineered for fast, precise switching that meets international safety standards, guaranteeing continuous power.
4. Dual Monitoring
Advanced sensors track primary and backup power quality, enabling proactive management to prevent cascading failures.
