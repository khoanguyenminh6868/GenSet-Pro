产品概述
The 20M55 series is a new generation of high-end engines independently developed by Weichai, featuring a compact structure, high reliability, and excellent load response; The 20M55 diesel engine offers a power output range of 3550-4320 kWm, compliant with Non-emission/EPA Tier II standards, and suitable for 3300-4000 kWe generator sets.
产品优势
Emissions: Non-emission/EPA Tier II
High reliability: Compacted graphite iron (CGI) cylinder block and cylinder head, alloy steel crankshaft, high structural strength, and long design service life
Superior economy: Electronic high-pressure common rail (HPCR) fuel system ensures optimal fuel atomization, high power density, and energy efficiency
Superior power performance: Specifically designed to meet the rigorous requirements of modern data centers, featuring flexible calibration of the proprietary ECU and excellent load-bearing capacity
Premium configuration: Supporting the sustainable development of global data centers and providing high-end customized solutions for the digital infrastructure sector
