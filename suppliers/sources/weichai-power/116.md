产品概述
The 16M33 series land-based standard generator set is a high-performance power product with fully independent design and research for the engine, alternator, and control system.
产品优势
High-Efficiency Eco-friendly Power: Equipped with an intelligent ECU (Engine Control Unit) fuel system, achieving ultra-low fuel consumption through precise control of fuel injection and air-fuel ratio, with economy comparable to top international brands. Complies with EPA II emission standards, balancing power output with environmental compliance
All-Weather Operational Adaptability: Features military-grade protection design, turbocharged intercooling system, and low-temperature heating device, ensuring stable operation in extreme temperatures from -30℃ to 55℃, altitudes up to 3,000 meters, and 95% humidity, effortlessly handling harsh environments such as mining sites and islands
Intelligent Control Platform: Integrated with a digital smart controller, supporting millisecond-level dynamic response, meeting G3 transient performance standards, and enabling cloud-based energy efficiency management via IoT modules
Long-Term Reliable Guarantee: The generator sets have undergone more than thirty durability and performance tests, ensuring long-lasting durability and superior reliability
Modular Service System: Based on a platform-based development architecture, major components are interchangeable across models, significantly reducing maintenance downtime; Equipped with an intelligent diagnostic interface and supported by Weichai's global emergency service network, significantly reducing lifecycle operation and maintenance costs
