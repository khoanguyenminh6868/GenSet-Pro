产品概述
The 6M33NG series is a high-speed engine independently developed by Weichai, featuring a compact structure, reliable operation, strong power output, and excellent economy; The 6M33NG gas engine has a power output of 480 kWm, suitable for 400 kWe generator sets.
产品优势
Gas System: High injection pressure ensures high engine power and superior economy; specifically developed electronic control functions for generator set operating conditions meet various control and speed regulation requirements
Air Intake System: High-efficiency turbocharger for optimized air intake and more efficient combustion
Superior NVH Performance: Utilizes pilot injection technology and a high-precision meshing gear train to achieve low engine vibration and noise
