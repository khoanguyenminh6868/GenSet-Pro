产品概述
The 12M33NG series is a new generation of high power density engines independently developed by Weichai, featuring a compact structure and superior economy; The 12M33 gas engine has a power range of 550–900 kWm, suitable for 500–858 kWe generator sets.
产品优势
Combustion System: Utilizes open-chamber spark plug technology, resulting in high engine thermal efficiency
Air Intake System: High-efficiency turbocharger for optimized air intake and more efficient combustion
Superior NVH Performance: Utilizes a high-precision meshing gear train to achieve low engine vibration and noise
