产品概述
The 6M33 series land-based generator sets are Weichais premium inline 6-cylinder generator sets, featuring stable power, high reliability, and a wide range of applications; The 6M33 series land-based generator sets offer a power output range of 560-660 kW.
产品优势
High Reliability: Derived from over a century of European engine technology, the generator sets have undergone more than thirty durability and performance tests, ensuring long-lasting durability and superior reliability
High Adaptability: Capable of operating in complex environments such as high temperatures, extreme cold, high altitudes, high humidity, and dusty conditions, while maintaining performance with varying fuel qualities
High-Efficiency Power: Features proprietary transient response calibration to ensure robust power output and stability
High Economy: Matched with turbocharging and intercooling to increase air intake and optimize the combustion process, delivering significant fuel savings
