产品概述
The 12M33 series diesel generator set is a new generation of high power density generator sets independently developed by Weichai, featuring a compact structure, superior fuel economy, and broad application prospects; Power output ranges from 900 to 1300 kW.
产品优势
Fuel System: High injection pressure ensures high engine power and excellent fuel atomization for superior fuel economy; specifically developed electronic control functions for generator set operating conditions meet various control and speed regulation requirements.
Air Intake System: High-efficiency turbocharger for optimized air intake and more efficient combustion
