Under the theme “Green and Intelligent Coexistence, Connecting the Blue Ocean”, Weichai presented its full lineup of marine propulsion engines, oceangoing gensets, silent power units, and new energy batteries, offering green, smart, integrated powertrain solutions and demonstrating its formidable marine engineering capabilities.

Weichai Powers Indonesia"s First Dedicated LNG Container Vessel
The container vessel Digul Mas, powered by the Weichai 6M33NG marine gas engine, recently completed its successful maiden commercial voyage in Indonesia. Built and invested by PT Temas Tbk, a leading Indonesian container liner and integrated logistics operator, the ship marks Indonesia"s first commercially operating electric-propulsion container vessel powered solely by LNG, delivering an innovative green propulsion solution to the regional maritime sector.

Wang Zhijian, Chairman of Shandong Heavy Industry Group, led the exhibition delegation and attended the new product launch event.
