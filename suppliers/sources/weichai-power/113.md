产品概述
Designed for medium-to-high power continuous power supply and complex load scenarios, suitable for prime or standby power requirements in data centers, factory production lines, infrastructure projects, and port logistics.
产品优势
Intelligent Control: Supports CAN bus communication and is compatible with mainstream ATS and remote cloud monitoring platforms, featuring remote start/stop, parameter monitoring, and fault alarming
High Adaptability: Capable of operating in complex environments including high temperatures, extreme cold, high altitudes, high humidity, and dusty conditions, while maintaining performance with varying fuel qualities
High Reliability: The products have undergone over thirty durability and performance tests, ensuring long-lasting durability and superior reliability of the generator sets
High Economy: Matched with high-pressure ratio turbochargers to increase air intake and optimize combustion for significant fuel savings
