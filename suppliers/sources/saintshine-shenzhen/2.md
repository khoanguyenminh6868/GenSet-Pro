One Stop Power Solution Provider
Shenzhen Saintshine Power Co., Ltd. is a company specializing in the technical research and development, manufacturing, and
sales of electrical equipment and power system consulting. We provide integrated services including power system consulting,
equipment selection, training, installation, commissioning, operation, and maintenance.
At Saintshine Power, we are capable of providing a comprehensive range of generator sets, including air-cooled or water-cooled gensets, diesel and gas gensets, plateau type gensets, silent type gensets, trailer type gensets, container type gensets, remote cooling type gensets, marine gensets, highvoltage gensets, and heavy oil gensets. Our single genset power scope covers a range from 8kW to 4000kW, and we cansynchronize several units of gensets to create power plants ranging from 2MW to 50MW.
