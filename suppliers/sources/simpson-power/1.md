About Simpson
Originated in BC, Canada, Simpson has been serving industrial and commercial customers for more than 46 years. With Simpson (China) Ltd established in 2006 and overseas factory in Indonesia in the same year, Simpson started a new era in the 21st century, providing reliable power generation products and services to both domestic market and Asia-Pacific region.

History
Founded in Vancouver, BC, Canada in 1969, SIMPSON was the largest generator manufacturer in Canada and one of the top five of generator producers in North America. With headquarter located in Alberta, Canada, SIMPSON was specialized in the products of diesel and natural gas generator sets, vehicle lights and industrial air compressors for decades.
SIMPSON kept adopting new technologies in its design, manufacturing and service of power products. Inspired by its legend cooperate culture of “international quality, superior service, market orientation and business continuity”, SIMPSON supplied many emergency generators and energy solutions to its global customers through its three manufacture plants which are located in Canada, Indonesia and China respectively to meet customer’s requirement in various custom designs and applications. SIMIPOWER also devoted to society with better power supply equipment which are more environmental friendly and better fuel economy.
SIMPSON (China) Ltd was established in July, 2006, it started a new era in SIMPSON history, it also laid great foundation for further development of the SIMPSON brand in China and Asia-Pacific region. Simpson Indonesia factory was also set up in the same year to provide a reliable supply of high-power units and services to both domestic market and Asia-Pacific region. SIMPSON factories in Asia-Pacific region inherit the excellent quality and production management model of the Canadian SIMPSON with more than forty years experiences. In addition, the factories strictly follow the standard of ISO 9001 management system and test all the generator sets per the ISO8525 standard to ensure the quality and technical specification as the same as Canadian SIMPSON.
SIMPSON has received ISO9001 Certificate, ISO14000 Certificate, OHSAS 18001 Certificate as well as the domestic telecommunications industry certificate issued by TLC test center. SIMPSON products are widely recognized by the major telecom operators such as China Mobile, China Telecom, China Unicom and China Tower. SIMPSON has become one of the registered and preferable brands of equipment procurement by Chinese communication industry .
SIMPSON has established and maintained a friendly cooperation relationships with the major engine OEMs in North America, Europe and Asia, such as MITSUBISHI, PERKINS, MTU/DDC, DOOSAN· DAEWOO, DEUTZ, CUMMINS, FOTON LOVOL etc; generator OEMs like MARATHON, STAMFORD, LEROY SOMER, KATO, ENGGA etc; control system OEMs such as THOMSON TECHNOLOGY in Canada and deep-sea in British, to ensure an excellent product quality and leading-edge technology in SIMPSON products. Moreover, SIMPSON lines include air-cooled, water-cooled, gasoline, diesel and natural gas units with 50HZ or 60HZ standard output power, ranging from 50kW to 5500kW (voltage 220V to 13.8kV).
As the professional manufacturer of generator sets, SIMPSON has the aim to meet customers’ need and actively commit to design and produce various generator systems. With years of efforts, SIMPSON has achieved great success to be an international professional company, capable of doing engineering design, manufacturing, installation and site commissioning. The products include the gasoline and diesel powered generator sets, high voltage generator sets, DC inverter generators, intelligent generator sets, container type generator sets, silent drag car generator sets, intelligent paralleling control system, ATS intelligent conversion system, oil machine intelligent management system, trailer units, mobile power systems, sound attenuation system and custom design system per customers’ special requirements.
