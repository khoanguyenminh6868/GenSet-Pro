Main Features
Lower fuel consumption
Same design and manufacture workmanship as US Cummins, it has lower fuel consumption among the manufacturers of this range.
Outstanding reliability
The design of key components reserve adequate safety margin. The strength exceeds the similar engines by 30%, longer engine life span.
Lower service and maintenance cost
Less amount of accessories and lower failure rate ensure the general service and maintenance cost at the minimum. The service cost can be saved by up to 50% in comparison to manufacturers of the same range engine.
Excellent cold start capability
The air intake preheating device and the high power starter improve the cold start performance of engine.
Excellent performance and design advantages
The advanced design maximize the engine power output up to 27.0kW/L(small specific weight ratio).
