SIMPSON Cummins series sound proof enclosure Genset
>
Simpson enclosure generator sets, designed and manufactured as per international standards, are equipped with heavy-duty generators to supply reliable and stable power source for the outdoor applications.
1、The water-proof and weather-proof capability of the enclosure have been dramatically improved by adopting super-efficient anti-corrosion materials.
2、Long-distance transportation and hoisting become much easier thanks to the high strength structure design and the use of the standardized normal containers
3、High efficient seismic isolators in use to ensure the stable operation
4、The noise level is at 70～80dB（A）(LP1m)
5、The enclosures are accommodated with control room, fuel supply system, lighting system and extra service space.
