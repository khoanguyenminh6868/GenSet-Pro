Advanced design, simple structure,small footprint andlight weight.It eliminated the overheating failure possibility caused by the water leak, frozen coolant anddebris (which are common in water cooled engines), maintenance are significantly minimum.
2、Environment adaptability, high reliability and wide applications
Air-cooled diesel engine has stable thermal state. It is less affected by the environment and can operate with high reliability u nder temperature range of -40 。 C to 50 。 C
3、Slight vibration, low noise level, excellent low-temperature start-up
With advanced sound attenuation and balance technology, the air-cooled engine has lower noise and vibration level than water- cooled engines. With built-in air heater the Deutzengine has superior cold startcapability.

The emission of air-cooled engine meets the international emission standards.
