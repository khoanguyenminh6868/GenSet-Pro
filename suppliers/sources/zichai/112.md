Product Details
M32 backpressure turbine is single stage,middle-temperature,and middle-pressure backpressure turbine.Its rotor uses a velocity-compounded stage impeller.Nozzle pitch diameter is 800mm.Its speed regulating system can use KOEI electronic speed governing,and also can use woodward TG17 or TG13 mechanical governor and TG17R+2301A controller electronic governing system,forced lubrication.Its speed of main shaft is 1500-4500r/min,and it can achieve gear shaft outpit to installation of gearbox according to user requirements.The main feature of models is the use of middle-temperature,middle-pressure steam as steam source dragging revolving mechanical doing work to realize the energy stave using for energy conservation and economize on raw materials.
