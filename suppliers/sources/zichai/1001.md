■ Company Profile
Zicai Power Co., Ltd. is a wholly-owned subsidiary of China Agricultural Development
Group Co., Ltd., a professional manufacturer of high-power medium-speed diesel engines, with a
production history of more than 50 years. The company is headquartered in Zichai Industrial Park,
Zibo High-tech Zone, with an annual output of more than 10,000 sets and a total power of more
than 6 million kilowatts. There are three subsidiaries: Qingdao Zichai Boyang Diesel Engine Co.,
Ltd., Zibo Zichai New Energy Co., Ltd. and Zicai Machinery Co., Ltd.
Leading products: N350, N330, 300, N28, ZC270, N26, L250, L230, 210, ZC200, Z180,
Z170, V170, EY170, L160, Z150 16 series 100～5880kW "Zichai" brand marine diesel engine,
LNG / Diesel, LPG/diesel, methanol/diesel dual-fuel engine and LNG gas engine; 120-5000kW
diesel, heavy oil, dual-fuel, biomass gas engine, automatic unit, etc.
The products are widely used in fishing vessels, passenger and freighter transportation, inland
shipping, port tugboats, engineering vessels, law enforcement vessels, military vessels, marine
generator sets, land generator sets, various power stations and pumping station units. "Zichai"
products and sales service outlets cover more than 30 provinces, municipalities and autonomous
regions across the country, and are also exported to more than a dozen countries and regions such
as Southeast Asia, South America, Africa, North Korea, and West Asia.
