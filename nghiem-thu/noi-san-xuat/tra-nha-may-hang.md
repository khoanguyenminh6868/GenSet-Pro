**Marathon (Marathon Generators / Marathon Electric)**
- **Ownership:** sold by Regal Rexnord to WEG; the Marathon, Cemp and Rotor brands were included. The WEG release was 403 when I tried it. Only third-party sources give the closing date (30 Apr 2024), so that date is tier C.
  - A WEG news title in search results says Marathon is "a company acquired by WEG in 2024". The page itself (https://www.weg.net/institutional/CN/en/news/products-and-solutions/weg-boosts-argentina-portfolio-with-technology-from-marathon-acquisition) was 403, so this is a search snippet only.
- **Plants:**
  - Search snippets of marathonelectric.com/about (403 when fetched, not verified) mention Wausau WI, Lima OH ("an important addition to our generator production") and a Monterrey facility "dedicated to producing motors and generators".
  - The Marathon 60 Hz rating guide (https://literature.marathonelectric.com/.../MG-CT24015E-R22-SB0073E-Marathon-Generator-Rating-Guide-60Hz-8gbrgz.pdf) says, per the search snippet, that generators are "manufactured and inventoried worldwide". I could not extract the PDF text to confirm.
  - A 2025 Marathon "BABA compliance" flyer appears to be about motors and could not be read.
- **Tier C:** job listings for "WEG Marathon" in Wausau.
- **Single-country production stated by maker:** unclear. No product-to-plant mapping was verified.

Notes on the controller table:
- **DSE:** the April 2023 article covers its new "Controls and Automation Business Group" with MOTORTECH. It does not name a parent company.
- **Woodward, tier C / unverified:**
  - A search summary said the easYgen-800 manual (https://www.woodward.com/products/publication-asset/b37692a/) states "designed in Germany and Poland; manufactured in China". The PDF could not be read, so this is not verified. It matters because it would contradict the Kraków statement for that model.
  - The Woodward Poland news item (13 Jun 2025) says only "Center of Excellence in Industrial electronics manufacturing".
- **SICES, tier C:** a DirectIndustry-hosted SICES catalogue says "designed and made in Italy".
- **Lovato:**
  - The company page says products are "designed, industrialized, tested and built by LOVATO Electric", and lists the Czech Republic and Croatia without stating production for genset controllers.
  - Tier C: a 2022 Lovato brochure (lovatoelectric.com PDF, Italian) was reported in a search summary as listing genset controllers among products of the Gorle plant. I did not fetch it.
- **DynaGen, tier C:** dealer pages say "Manufactured In Canada" (TG410/TG350). These may predate Cattron.
- **SmartGen, tier C:** Zhengzhou Daily (Aug 2023) reports that the company's industrial park in the Zhengzhou high-tech zone went into use in Jan 2023. Third-party sources give the address as No.28 Jinsuo Road, Zhengzhou.
